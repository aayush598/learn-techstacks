# 00 — Market Terms Glossary

## Purpose
Define the market terminology used throughout this resource so readers share a
precise vocabulary.

## Price & data
- **OHLCV**: Open, High, Low, Close, Volume of a bar.
- **Bid / Ask**: best buy / best sell price in the book.
- **Spread**: ask − bid.
- **Mid**: (bid + ask) / 2.
- **Slippage**: difference between expected and actual fill price.
- **Gap**: jump in price across a period boundary.
- **Tick**: single trade or quote update.

## Orders
- **Market order**: fill immediately at best available price.
- **Limit order**: fill only at limit price or better.
- **Stop order**: becomes market/limit when triggered.
- **Bracket**: entry + stop + target as one unit.
- **OCO**: one-cancels-other order linkage.
- **Taker / maker**: crosses the spread / rests on the book.

## Market structure
- **Liquidity**: ability to trade without moving price much.
- **Depth**: size available in the order book.
- **Market impact**: price movement caused by your own order.
- **Volatility**: variability of price over time.
- **ATR**: average true range — a volatility measure.
- **VWAP**: volume-weighted average price.
- **POC**: point of control — highest-volume price level.
- **Session phases**: open / morning / midday / afternoon / close.

## Strategy concepts
- **Edge**: expected advantage (after costs).
- **Expectancy**: average profit per trade.
- **Payoff ratio**: average win / average loss.
- **Win rate**: fraction of winning trades.
- **Mean reversion**: betting price returns to fair value.
- **Momentum/breakout**: betting a move continues.
- **Regime**: the market's current character (trend/range, high/low vol).
