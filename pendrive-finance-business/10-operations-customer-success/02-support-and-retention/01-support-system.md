# 01 — Support System & Retention (Keep Every Customer, Get Renewals)

Support is not a cost — it's what makes renewals and referrals. Build a simple, reliable support system.

## The Support Promise

> "I'll reply within one business day and help you solve your problem. If it's a bug, I'll fix it. If it's data, I'll help you recover from backup."

## Support Channels (keep it simple)

- **WhatsApp** (primary — most customers are comfortable here).
- **Phone calls** for urgent/data issues.
- **Remote** (screen share) for walkthroughs.
- **On-site** only for serious/paid add-on (travel fee).

## The "Support Tier" Model (tie to annual license)

| Tier | Respond | Included | Extra |
|------|---------|----------|-------|
| Included (annual license) | within 1 business day | usage help, bug fixes, minor updates | on-site = paid add-on |
| Priority (premium/paid) | same-day | faster, priority + more sessions | a higher annual add-on |

## Support Ticket Log (Part 14 template)

| Date | Customer | Issue | Severity | Resolution | Status |
|------|----------|-------|----------|------------|--------|
| | | | High/Med/Low | | Open/Done |

- Track all issues → you see patterns to fix at the source (reduce future support).
- **Fix root causes**, not just symptoms: if "stock wrong" happens a lot, improve the template.

## Handling Support Issues (SOP)

1. **Acknowledge fast** ("Got it, let me help.").
2. **Diagnose** — reproduce the issue.
3. **Priority:**
   - Data loss/urgent → handle immediately, recover from backup.
   - Blocking bug → quick fix/workaround.
   - Minor → schedule/fix in update.
4. **Communicate** clearly, in plain language.
5. **Verify** the fix with the customer.
6. **Log** + learn.

## Retention / Renewal Playbook (target ≥80%)

- **Don't disappear** between sales. Schedule periodic check-ins.
- **Deliver value all year**:
  - Yearly check-in (report "this is what you've tracked").
  - Send a **small update/release** occasionally (shows software improving).
  - Remind them their **backup is safe** with you.
- **Renewal sequence** (from Part 04):
  - 60 days: "Welcome, your license renews soon."
  - 30 days: remind + prepay discount offer.
  - Due: invoice.
  - 15–30 days after: follow-up.
- **Make renewal frictionless** — one UPI/tap payment.

## Reducing Support Load (so you scale)

- **Great onboarding** (fewer questions later).
- **Self-help** — a short FAQ/video (Part 14) for common questions.
- **Solid templates** that rarely break.
- **Backups** — prevent data-loss panics.
- Batch support to fixed hours (Part 15) instead of answering all day.

## Red Flags (a customer about to churn)

- ⚠️ Not using it (no logins) → re-engage with a check-in.
- ⚠️ Repeated annoyance about a missing feature → decide: add it (if valuable) or set expectations.
- ⚠️ Complaints not resolved → fix fast before they leave.
- ⚠️ At renewal, silence/stalling → push the value + prepay discount.

## "Save the Customer" Bests

- Apologize + fix fast (speed beats politeness).
- Never argue over who's at fault — solve it.
- A goodwill gesture (extra support/week, discount) on a bad experience buys loyalty.
