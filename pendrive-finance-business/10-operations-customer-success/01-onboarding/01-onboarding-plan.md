# 01 — Onboarding & Customer Success (Turn Buyers Into Lifelong Customers)

Onboarding done well = happy customer, renewal, and referrals. It's where you earn your recurring business.

## The First 2 Weeks (make or break)

**Day 0 (Delivery):**
- Deliver pendrive, walk through the basics, confirm works on their machine.
- Collect balance (if not already), give support contact + renewal date.

**Day 1–2 (Set up together):**
- 30-min session: log in, add first products/customers, make first invoice.
- Confirm their data imports correctly.
- Leave the cheat-sheet + demo video.

**Day 7 (Check-in):**
- Ask: "How's it going? Any friction?" — fix concerns early.
- Ensure they've actually used it (adoption).

**Day 14 (Consolidation):**
- Confirm routine (billing, stock, backup) is being used.
- Collect feedback; fix minor issues.

## The Onboarding Checklist (Part 14 template)

- [ ] Pendrive works on their PC.
- [ ] Login + users set up.
- [ ] Products/customers imported & verified.
- [ ] They made a test invoice.
- [ ] They know how to check reports & backup.
- [ ] Support contact shared; renewal date noted.

## Boosting Adoption (customers who use it renew)

- **Make it their daily routine** — connect to their workflow ("bill every sale, don't wait").
- **Simple cheat-sheet** beside the machine (Part 14).
- **One-window support** — they know exactly where to reach you.
- **Monthly value reminder** — "Here's what we saved/tracked this month" (Part 04: year-in-review builds renewal).

## Customer Success = Recurring Revenue

Happy, actively-using customers:
- **Renew** their annual license (recurring income).
- **Buy add-ons** (higher ARPC).
- **Refer others** (free lead generation).
This is the flywheel of your business. Invest in it deliberately.

## Measure Success (Weekly)

- Active users (== do they log in / use it).
- Support tickets/issues (down is good, but fix root causes).
- Renewal conversions.
- Referrals from each customer.

> A customer you "sell and forget" will churn. A customer you onboard and support becomes your best salesperson.
