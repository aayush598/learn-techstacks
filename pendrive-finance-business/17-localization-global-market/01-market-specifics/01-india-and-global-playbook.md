# 01 — Market-Specific Playbooks: India (Primary) & Global (Secondary)

Localize your sales, pricing, compliance, and language per market. This chapter supports your home market (India assumed) and how to go global.

## INDIA — Primary Market Playbook

**Who to sell to:** kirana/retail, pharmacies, clinics, restaurants, salons, coaching, small service firms — across your city + online.

**Pricing (India, ₹):**
- Starter: ₹3–5k setup + ₹1.5–2k/yr license (Part 04).
- Complete: ₹8–15k setup + ₹3–5k/yr.
- Premium: ₹20–40k setup + ₹8–15k/yr.
*(Localize to your region's income levels.)*

**Payments:** UPI (primary), cash, NEFT/IMPS, cheque (wait to clear).

**Compliance (India):**
- Sole Proprietorship → MSME/Udyam registration (easy, recommend early).
- PAN; GST when turnover crosses threshold (services ~₹20L/goods ~₹40L — check current).
- Income tax filing; advance tax if applicable (Part 07).
- Data: DPDP Act (privacy) (Part 07).

**GST angle:** Your software's **GST-ready reporting + e-invoice-friendly output** is a strong selling point to Indian small businesses (tax-season pain).

**Languages:** Offer local-language UI (Hindi, regional) via the platform's i18n — huge differentiator. A shop owner who sees the software in their own language trusts it more.

**Channels (India):**
- WhatsApp Business (huge in India), WhatsApp Status marketing.
- Google Business Profile + local SEO ("billing software near me").
- Local walk-ins, shopkeeper WhatsApp groups, local directories (JustDial/IndiaMART/Sulekha).
- Referrals from CAs and IT/computer shops (Part 10).

## Key English/Global English-Speaking Markets (secondary)

**Pricing (USD):**
- Starter: $40–70 + $20–30/yr
- Complete: $110–200 + $45–70/yr
- Premium: $270–550 + $110–200/yr
*(Localize per country's buying power.)*

**Payments:** PayPal, Stripe, bank transfer, Wise, local digital wallets.

**Compliance (general):**
- Register locally (US: Sole Prop/LLC; UK: Sole Trader/Ltd; EU: local + VAT + GDPR).
- VAT/GST/sales-tax per country; invoice accordingly.
- GDPR (EU), CCPA (California), DPDP (India) privacy rules (Part 07).

## High-Potential Global Niche Opportunities

- **Emerging markets (Philippines, Indonesia, Vietnam, Nigeria, Kenya, LATAM):** huge micro-business base, similar pain (cost, offline, non-technical), lower competition.
- **Western markets:** charge premium; emphasize "offline + privacy + no subscription" (many want to escape SaaS).
- Sell **"desktop/offline accounting + inventory"** to businesses and freelancers who dislike subscription software.

> Since your product is **offline + portable**, you can deliver **anywhere** by shipping a pendrive + remote/video setup. Geography is not a barrier.
