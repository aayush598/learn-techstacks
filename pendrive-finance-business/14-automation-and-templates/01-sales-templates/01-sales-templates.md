## B. Cold WhatsApp Message (Part 05 has variants; shortened)

> Hi [Name]! I build offline finance software for [niche] businesses — it runs from a pen drive, no monthly subscription. I help owners with [billing speed / stock control / clear profit]. Open to a quick 5-min chat this week? No pressure. 🙂

## C. Demo Follow-Up

> Hi [Name], as promised — that demo showed [1 fast billing + GST invoice, 2 stock that reorders itself, 3 one-click profit report], all offline on your pen drive. Would you like me to send a proposal for the [Complete] package? I can start this week.

## D. Proposal Summary (short version)

> **Proposal — [Business]**
> What you get: [list scope/modules/reports/invoice/migration/training].
> Not included: [exclusions — add-ons priced separately].
> Price: Setup ₹____ + Annual license ₹____ (covers support + fixes).
> Payment: 50% deposit to start, balance on delivery. Money-back if I miss the agreed delivery timeline.
> Start date: [date]. Delivery ~[date].
> Reply "yes" to confirm. Valid 14 days.

## E. Referral Ask (to happy customer)

> Hi [Name], thanks again for trusting me! If anyone you know in [niche] has billing/stock/profit headaches, happy to help them too — and I'll credit you ₹____/free months on your renewal for every successful referral. Who should I reach out to?

## F. Renewal Reminder (Part 04/10)

> Hi [Name], a friendly heads-up your annual license renews on [date]. It covers [support + fixes + backups]. Renew now and get [prepay discount]. Payment: [UPI/invoice link]. Thank you for being a customer! 🙏

## G. Repeat "Let Me Think" Response

> Of course — is it price, fit, or trust you're unsure about? Happy to address it directly so you can decide comfortably.

## H. Post-Delivery Testimonial Request

> Hi [Name], hope everything's working well! If you're happy, would you share a 1-line review + Google review? It really helps other [niche] owners find me. Example: "Set up billing/stock software on a pen drive — fast, offline, mine." 🙏
