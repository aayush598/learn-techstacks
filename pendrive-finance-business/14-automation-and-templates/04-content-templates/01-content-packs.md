# 01 — Content & Support Packs (Reusable Material)

Create these once, reuse everywhere. They save you time and build trust.

## A. Customer Cheat-Sheet (give with every delivery)

```
          [Business] — Quick Guide (your software)
1. SELLING:  Open > Sales > New Bill > add items > Save > Print.
2. STOCK:    Check Stock screen; reorder when alert shows.
3. PROFIT:   Reports > Profit & Loss — view daily/weekly/monthly.
4. BACKUP:   Menu > Backup (do this weekly) — I keep a copy too.
5. HELP:     WhatsApp [number] — I reply within a business day.
Renewal date: [date].  Support covered by your annual license.
```

## B. FAQ (Support Self-Help — reduces your load)

- **Q: How do I back up?** → Menu > Backup. I also keep a secure copy.
- **Q: Lost my pen drive?** → Contact me; I'll restore from backup to a new drive.
- **Q: The internet is down — will it work?** → Yes, fully offline by design.
- **Q: How do I add a product/customer?** → [simple steps].
- **Q: Something looks wrong in a report?** → Send a screenshot; I'll check and fix.
- **Q: Can staff use it?** → Yes — different roles/permissions as set up.

## C. Social/Status Content Pack (rotate weekly)

1. **Tip:** "3 silent ways shops lose money (and how to fix them)."
2. **Demo:** 30-sec offline billing clip.
3. **Result/testimonial:** "Set up billing+stock on a pen drive — offline, mine."
4. **Offer:** Founding price for the first 3 customers.
5. **Objection-buster:** "Afraid offline = losing data? Here's how it auto-backs-up."

## D. Demo Recording Leave-Behind

- Record a 3–5 min walkthrough for the niche; send as WhatsApp video after demos.
- Include: billing, stock, profit report, offline note, "built for you."

## E. Workshop / Free-Demo Slide Deck Outline

1. Problem (billing mistakes, stock loss, no profit clarity).
2. The offline/ownership idea.
3. Live 5-min demo on their niche.
4. The 3 offers + price (Part 04).
5. Risk reversal + how to start (deposit, timeline).

## F. Partner One-Pager (for CAs/IT shops)

- Who you are, what you do (offline customized finance software).
- Benefits for their clients (ownership, offline, affordable).
- Referral reward plan (Part 10).
- Contact + "let's partner."

## G. Closing/Onboarding Videos

- A 60-sec "welcome + how to start" video for each new customer.
- Reduces repeat questions; feels personal.

## Content Rules

- Make once, reuse everywhere (Part 06).
- Keep it simple and in the customer's language.
- Get **permission** for any customer name/photo/video (Part 06).
- Batch-create content in 1 hour/week (Part 10/15).
