# 01 — Tracker Spreadsheets & Tools (The Systems That Run You)

Use spreadsheets (Google Sheets — free + mobile) for all trackers. Here are the columns for each. Create one tab per tracker in a single "Business Tracker" sheet.

## 1. Lead / Pipeline Tracker

| Lead | Niche match | Source | Pain | Stage | Last contact | Next action | Est value | Warmth (1-5) |
|------|------------|--------|------|-------|--------------|-------------|-----------|--------------|

**Stages:** New → Contacted → Qualified → Demo → Proposal → Closed → Won/ Lost

## 2. Project / Delivery Tracker

| Customer | Scope/plan | Deposit | Balance | Due date | Stage | Next step | Notes |
|----------|-----------|---------|---------|----------|-------|-----------|-------|

**Stage:** Required → Build → Test → Migrate → Backup → Train → Deliver → Support

## 3. Customer & Renewal Tracker (your asset)

| Customer | Business type | Plan | Setup fee | Annual license | Renewal date | Last contact | Status/notes |
|----------|---------------|------|-----------|----------------|--------------|--------------|--------------|

Sort by **renewal date** — this drives your renewal calendar (Part 04/10).

## 4. Cash Flow Tracker (monthly)

| Date | Description | Type (IN/OUT) | Category | Amount | Balance |
|------|-------------|---------------|----------|--------|---------|
| | | IN | setup fee / license / add-on | | |

Weekly: review closing balance (Part 08).

## 5. Income / Expense Summary (per month, for tax)

| Month | Total IN | Total OUT | Categories breakdown | Tax set-aside |
|-------|----------|-----------|----------------------|---------------|

## 6. Support Ticket Log

| Date | Customer | Issue | Severity | Resolution | Status |
|------|----------|-------|----------|------------|--------|

## 7. Testimonial / Referral Tracker

| Customer | Delivered | Testimonial? | Google review? | Referrals asked | Names given | Rewarded? |
|----------|-----------|--------------|----------------|-----------------|-------------|-----------|

## Recommended Free Tools

- **Google Sheets** — all trackers (mobile-friendly).
- **Google Calendar / reminders** — renewals, follow-ups, deadlines.
- **WhatsApp Business** — catalog + Status marketing (Part 06).
- **Google Business Profile** — local SEO (Part 06).
- **Canva** — logo, flyer, marketing sheet (Part 14).
- **Screen recorder (phone/app)** — demo clips (Part 06).
- **Simple note app** — capture ideas/quick logs.

## Anti-Overkill Rule

Don't build 15 elaborate spreadsheets. **Start with 3:** Lead tracker, Customer/Renewal tracker, Cash Flow tracker. Add Project + Support when you have volume. Simplicity = you'll actually use them.

## Back These Up

- Keep your **Business Tracker** backed up (cloud) — it's your brain and your **asset** (Part 13).
