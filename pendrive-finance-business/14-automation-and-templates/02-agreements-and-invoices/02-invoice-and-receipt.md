# 02 — Invoice & Receipt Templates (Look Professional, Get Paid)

Send a proper invoice/receipt for every payment — it's professional and legally clean.

## A. Simple Invoice Template

```
                      [YOUR BUSINESS NAME]
                "Your business software. On your pen drive."
          [Address] | [Phone] | [WhatsApp] | [GST/PAN/Reg No.]

                        TAX INVOICE
Invoice No: INV-____        Date: [DATE]
Client: [Name/Business]      Client ID: ____

DESCRIPTION                                   AMOUNT
--------------------------------------------  -----
Starter/Complete/Premium setup [specify]      ₹____
Data migration (add-on)                      ₹____
Training (add-on)                            ₹____
Annual license (year 1)                      ₹____
--------------------------------------------  -----
Subtotal                                     ₹____
GST @ [rate]% (if registered)                ₹____
--------------------------------------------  -----
TOTAL DUE                                    ₹____

PAYMENT:
- Deposit received (50%):                    ₹____
- Balance due on delivery:                   ₹____

Payment methods: UPI / Cash / Bank Transfer.
Terms: Deposit on signing; balance before final handover.
```

## B. Payment Receipt (for when you receive payment)

```
                      [YOUR BUSINESS NAME]
                      PAYMENT RECEIPT
Receipt No: RCP-____      Date: [DATE]
Received from: [Name/Business]
Amount: ₹____  (in words: ____________________)
For: [setup / annual license / add-on / renewal]
Method: [UPI/Cash/Bank]   Ref: [txn no.]
Signature: ______________
```

## C. Renewal Invoice Template

```
Invoice No: INV-____   Date: [DATE]
Annual License Renewal — [Business Name]
Period: [Start] to [End] (12 months)
Coverage: Support + bug fixes + minor updates + backup assistance.
Amount: ₹____   (prepay discount applied: [X]%)
Payment due: [DATE].  Method: [UPI/invoice link]
```

## Invoice Tips

- **Number consecutively** (INV-0021, etc.) — looks organized, aids accounting.
- **Same business identity** on every invoice (name + reg no.).
- Send **immediately** when agreed; resend on renewal.
- Store copies for tax (Part 07) — PDF them into a folder.
- Include **payment terms** clearly (deposit/balance).
