# 01 — Service Agreement Template (Copy & Localize; Get It Signed)

Adapt this to your country/laws. Keep it plain and fair. Read Part 07 for why each clause matters.

---

# SERVICE AGREEMENT

**This Agreement is made on [DATE] between:**

**Provider:** [Your Name / Business Name], [Address], [Contact]
**(referred to as "Provider")**

**and**

**Client:** [Client/Business Name], [Address], [Contact]
**(referred to as "Client")**

## 1. Services
Provider shall deliver customized offline finance software for the Client's business, defined by the attached Scope Sheet ("Deliverables"), which may include: [billing/inventory/accounting/reports/data migration/training] as selected.

## 2. Scope & Exclusions
Only the items listed in the Scope Sheet are included. Additional requests beyond the Scope Sheet are priced separately as add-ons and are not included unless agreed in writing.

## 3. Deliverables & Format
Deliverables are provided on a USB drive(s). The software runs on Windows computers and works offline. Provider keeps the software code/rights; Client owns the data entered into the software.

## 4. Timeline
Estimated start date: [DATE]. Estimated delivery: [DATE]. Provider will communicate promptly on any delay.

## 5. Fees & Payment
- Setup/Customization Fee: [AMOUNT]
- Annual License (Support + Fixes): [AMOUNT]/year
- Payment: [50]% deposit of the Setup Fee on signing; balance of the Setup Fee on delivery, before final handover of the drive.
- Add-ons are billed separately and due on order.

## 6. Annual License / Renewal
The Annual License covers: support (phone/WhatsApp), bug fixes, minor updates, and reasonable usage help, renewable annually. It does not include: major new modules, on-site visits (fee applies), or large data migration (fee applies).

## 7. Warranty & Support
Provider will reasonably support the Deliverables per the Annual License. There is no obligation for work outside the Scope.

## 8. Data, Backup & Confidentiality
- Provider will keep a secure backup of the Client's configured software/data during the engagement.
- Provider will keep all Client data confidential and will not share it.
- Client is responsible for safeguarding its own pen drive(s) and following backup guidance. Provider is not liable for data loss on a drive physically damaged or lost by the Client beyond the backups retained.

## 9. Limitation of Liability
Provider's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid by the Client. Provider is not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential losses (including lost profits or business interruption).

## 10. Termination
Either party may terminate with 14 days written notice. On cancellation before delivery, Client is entitled to a refund of the deposit less reasonable work done to date. Refund of the setup fee applies if Provider fails to meet the agreed delivery timeline without cause.

## 11. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of [Region/Jurisdiction].

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the parties have signed on the date above.

Provider: ______________________  Date: ______
Client: ______________________   Date: ______
