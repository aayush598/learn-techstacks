# 01 — Failure & Recovery: What To Do If Things Go Wrong

Even great businesses hit rough patches. The difference is a **plan** for recovery. Here's yours.

## "If This Happens, Do This" Response Playbook

| Situation | Immediate Action | Recovery Path |
|-----------|------------------|---------------|
| **No sales after 4–6 weeks** | Don't panic; diagnose | Fix offer/message/channel (Part 05/12); talk to 10 more businesses; adjust niche/price |
| **Data loss incident** | Recover from backup now | Tell customer, fix cause, goodwill, document (Part 11 ch. data) |
| **Customer angry / threatening** | Listen, apologize, fix fast | Over-deliver on fix; honor agreement; often rebuilt trust |
| **Cash running low** | Cut costs + quick-cash (Part 12) | Get a starter sale/training/payment; consider bridging income (Part 08) |
| **Tech problem I can't fix** | Be honest, offer workaround | Get help (forum/freelance dev), set expectations, fix when possible |
| **Overwhelmed / burnout** | Protect rest; prioritize | Raise prices, batch work, cut non-revenue tasks, consider help (Part 13) |
| **Competitor undercuts me** | Don't panic-price | Re-emphasize ownership/customization/support (Part 01) |
| **Legal issue (tax/contract)** | Consult a professional (CA/lawyer) | Resolve properly; add protections (Part 07) |

## The 3Fs of Recovery (stay composed)

1. **Face it** — acknowledge the problem honestly (no hiding).
2. **Fix it** — act on the #1 root cause immediately.
3. **Forward** — prevent recurrence + move on (don't spiral).

## The "Honest Reassessment" Rule

If something isn't working after real effort:
- **Was the offer wrong?** → change it (Part 04/12).
- **Was the niche wrong?** → switch niche (Part 03).
- **Was the channel wrong?** → switch channel (Part 05/06).
- **Was the price wrong?** → adjust (Part 04).
- **Do I lack a skill?** → get help/training.

> Don't keep doing the same thing expecting different results. Data > ego.

## Protect Your Confidence

- **Paper wins** — keep a list of every small win (first customer, first renewal, good review) to read when discouraged.
- **Perspective** — businesses take time; steady deposits compound.
- **Community** — connect with other entrepreneurs for support/ideas.
- **Health** — sleep, movement, food; burnout is a real risk (Part 10).

## Your Personal "Never Give Up" Triggers

Re-assess *only* if **all** of these are true after ~6 months of real effort:
- No paying customer at all.
- No traction/leads from multiple channels.
- Cash is exhausting your runway with no path.
- You've genuinely addressed niche/offer/message.

Then pivot (try a different niche/offer) — not necessarily quit. There's demand somewhere; find your fit.
