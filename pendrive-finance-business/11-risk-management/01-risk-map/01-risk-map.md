## The "Biggest, Most Honest" Risks (top priority)

1. **Data loss** (impact very high) → mitigate with **backups + test restore**, always.
2. **Running out of cash before first sales** → mitigate with **low burn + quick-cash + partial income** (Part 08/12).
3. **Time wasted on non-revenue/scope-creep work** → mitigate with **deposits, scope sheets, focus on one niche**.
4. **Reputation from one bad delivery** → mitigate with **testing + fast fix + backing guarantees** (Part 09/10).

## The Risk-Minimizing Strategy (principle)

- **Don't build everything upfront.** Validate with a deposit-test before heavy build (Part 03/12).
- **Get paid before/early.** Deposits + progress payments = cash risk near-zero.
- **Keep costs variable & low.** Little fixed overhead = little downside.
- **Keep your data honesty.** Protect customers' data = protect your reputation and from liability.
- **Never put all eggs in one customer/niche** until proven.

## Risk "Tripwires" (know when to act)

- ⚠️ Cash balance falling 3+ weeks → quick-cash + cut costs (Part 08/12).
- ⚠️ 3+ support issues on a feature → fix root cause now (Part 10).
- ⚠️ No new business 2–3 weeks → change offer/message/channel (Part 05/06).
- ⚠️ One customer > 40% of revenue → diversify (Part 13).
- ⚠️ You're sleeping poorly / overwhelmed → delegate/raise prices/rest (Part 13).

## Your Personal Risk "Balance"

The **lowest-risk way to build wealth quickly here** = keep your day-job/freelance income (reduce pressure), build the business on deposits, keep buffers, and grow stepwise. That's the honest "fast + low-risk" path — not overnight millions.
