# 01 — Reputation & Data Risk Protection (Your Two Most Valuable Assets)

Your **reputation** and **customer trust** are literally your business. One data disaster or bad delivery can undo months of work. Protect both ruthlessly.

## Reputation Risk (Protecting Your Name)

### What Can Hurt It
- Delivering broken/buggy software.
- Over-promising and under-delivering.
- Ignoring support / slow replies.
- Dishonest marketing or testimonials.
- Arguing with customers over blame.

### Protection System
1. **Never over-promise** — only sell features that work (Part 09 testing).
2. **Test everything** before delivery (Part 09 checklist).
3. **Fast, honest support** — reply in 1 business day (Part 10).
4. **Over-deliver slightly** — a small bonus builds advocates (Part 02 standards).
5. **Handle mistakes gracefully** — apologize + fix fast + goodwill (Part 02/10).
6. **Honest testimonials & marketing** only (Part 06).
7. **Under-promise, then surprise** them with more.

### If Reputation Is Hit (recovery)
- Own it publicly & privately (apologize).
- Fix the issue completely.
- Follow up with goodwill.
- Learn; add a rule to prevent recurrence.
- One great recovery can rebuild trust (people forgive honest effort).

## Data Risk (Protecting Customer Data & Yourself)

You'll be trusted with customers' financial records. Treat it as a sacred duty.

### Protection System (from Part 07 — reassert)
1. **Always keep a backup** of every customer's app + data (verified restore).
2. **Encryption always on** (platform AES-256).
3. **Confidentiality** — never share/discuss customer data.
4. **Clean copies** — no other-customer/test data in deliveries.
5. **Access control** — restricted keys/copies.
6. **Contract liability cap** (Part 07) limits your financial exposure.
7. **Incident protocol** (Part 07): tell customer, recover from backup, fix, apologize.

### Data Loss Prevention Checklist (per delivery)
- [ ] Backup created + stored offsite (2 copies minimum).
- [ ] Restore tested once.
- [ ] No stray data in delivered copy.
- [ ] Encryption active.
- [ ] Passwords/access documented & stored safely (Part 14).
- [ ] Drive tested on clean machine (Part 09).

## The "Worst Case" Playbook (data loss incident)

1. **Don't panic; act fast.**
2. Recover from **backup** immediately.
3. **Tell the customer** honestly & transparently.
4. **Fix the cause** so it won't repeat.
5. **Apologize + goodwill** (extra support/free month).
6. **Document** to prevent again.

## Written Acknowledgement

Consider a simple clause/customer acknowledgment that they received the backup and understand backup ownership — reduces disputes. (Full liability cap in contract, Part 07.)

## Review Rhythm

Quarterly: re-read all risk chapters (esp. Part 11), update your checklist. Complacency is the silent killer.
