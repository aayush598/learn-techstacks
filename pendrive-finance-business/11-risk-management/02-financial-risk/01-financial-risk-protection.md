# 01 — Financial Risk Protection (Protect Your Cash From Every Angle)

Financial risk can kill the business. Here's the full protection system.

## Risk: Non-Payment / Late Payment
**Mitigations:**
- **Deposit-first (50%)** before any work (Part 04).
- **Signed agreement** with payment terms (Part 07).
- **Balance before full handover** — never deliver completely before paid.
- Track every payment promise (Part 14 tracker).
- Pause support/updates for chronic non-payment (per agreement).

## Risk: Running Out of Cash (lumpy sales)
**Mitigations:**
- **Business cash buffer** (1–3 months) (Part 08).
- **Stay low-burn** — keep fixed costs minimal.
- **Prepay discount** on renewals to pull cash forward.
- **Quick-cash routes** ready (Part 12).
- **Partial income** (job/freelance) until business pays (Part 08).

## Risk: Over-investing / Spending Profit
**Mitigations:**
- Reinvest only a % (Part 08); pay yourself a salary.
- Set-aside **tax + savings** first, always.
- Don't buy bulk inventory ahead (buy pendrives in small batches).

## Risk: Debt & Interest Traps
**Mitigations:**
- Avoid high-interest loans/credit-card debt for marketing.
- Bootstrap with deposits — reinvest profit, not borrowed money.
- If you need capital, prefer a small secured/family loan over predatory lenders — with a clear repayment plan.

## Risk: Financial Errors (bad bookkeeping)
**Mitigations:**
- Separate business/personal accounts (Part 07).
- Monthly reconciliation (Part 08).
- Simple cash-flow + income trackers (Part 14).
- When growing, use a CA/accountant (Part 07).

## Risk: Tax Penalties
**Mitigations:**
- Set aside ~20–30% tax from every payment (Part 07).
- Register + file on time (Part 07 calendar).
- Keep receipts for deductions.
- Use a CA for tricky matters.

## Risk: One Customer = Too Much Revenue
**Mitigations:**
- Diversify across customers; cap any one customer's share.
- Grow your recurring/license base (Part 04) so no single client matters.

## The "Money Safety" Golden Rules (summary)

1. Pay yourself last (after buffers, costs, tax) but **consistently**.
2. Track every rupee in/out monthly.
3. Deposit-first, always.
4. Never spend what you haven't confirmed.
5. Buffers before spending.

> Cash discipline is what lets you survive the lean weeks and thrive in the good ones. This is the #1 solo survival skill.
