# 01 — Closing Techniques: Turning Interest Into Payment

Closing = getting the **deposit + signed agreement** now, not "let me think." Here's how to convert reliably.

## The #1 Rule

> **Ask directly for the sale and set the next step before the conversation ends.**

Weak closing: "So... would you be interested?" (invites "maybe")
Strong closing: "Great, I can start your setup this week. I need a 50% deposit of ₹____ via UPI/cash and we'll begin. Shall we do that now?"

## 7 Proven Closers

### 1. The "Which One" Close (assumptive)
Give 2 positive options:
> "Would you like the Complete package or the Premium one? Most owners start with Complete."

### 2. The "When" Close (time-based)
> "I can slot your setup in this week. Would Wednesday work to start?"

### 3. The "Money-Back" Close (risk reversal)
> "If I don't deliver what we agreed on time, I'll refund your setup fee. So you have zero risk in starting today."

### 4. The "Scarcity" Close (honest)
> "I take on only 3 new setups per month to keep quality high. I have 1 slot left this month. Want me to hold it for you?"

### 5. The "Value Recap" Close (remind the win)
> "For ₹____ you get fast billing, stock control, and clear profit — plus your data is yours. Over a year this saves you far more. Shall we get started?"

### 6. The "Small Commitment" Close (reduce the ask)
> "Let's start with the Starter plan at just ₹____ to prove it works for you. Once you see it, we can upgrade later."

### 7. The "Referral-Proven" Close (social proof)
> "Another [shop/clinic] owner nearby had the exact same problem and now [result]. They referred me because they were so happy. Can I set yours up?"

## Handling "Let Me Think" (the #1 stall)

1. Agree: "Of course, happy to."
2. Ask the real reason: "Just so I can help you decide — what's the main thing you're unsure about? Price, fit, or trust?"
3. Address it directly (use Part 03 objection table).
4. Then re-close: "If I can [address objection], would you be ready to start this week?"

## Handling "Too Expensive"

1. Don't immediately discount.
2. Reframe value: "Compared to paying ₹X/month forever, mine is one-time + a tiny yearly fee — you own it. Over 2 years you save ₹____."
3. Offer a **cheaper bundle** (drop add-ons) rather than cutting the core price.
4. Offer **split payment** (deposit + balance on delivery) if truly needed.

## The Close Script (Step-by-Step)

1. **Summarize** the deal (what they get, price, timeline).
2. **Confirm value** ("This solves your stock problem and shows clear profit — right?").
3. **Ask for commitment** ("Shall we start? I'll take the ₹____ deposit now.").
4. **Handle any objection** the same moment.
5. **Get the deposit + signature** (agreement). Set delivery date.
6. **Thank + next step** (remind what happens next: "I'll send the proposal and start your customization.")

## After the Close (don't disappear)

- Send the **signed agreement + invoice** immediately (Part 14).
- Confirm the deposit received.
- Book the first touchpoint (requirements call).
- Add to your delivery/project tracker (Part 09).

## Mindset

- Closing is **helping** the customer solve a real problem — it's not manipulation.
- A sincere, clear close with a money-back safety = genuine service.
- If they say no, stay gracious — you may win them later or get referrals.
