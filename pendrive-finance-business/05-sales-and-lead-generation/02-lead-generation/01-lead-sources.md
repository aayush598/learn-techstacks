# 01 — Lead Sources: Where To Find Buyers (Ranked by Speed & Cost)

As a solo starter, prioritize the **fastest, cheapest, highest-converting** sources. Local + referral beats broad online ads for your first money.

## Ranked Lead-Source Playbook

### 1. Your Warm Network (FASTEST)
- Friends, family, ex-colleagues, neighbors who own/run businesses.
- Ask: "Who do you know with a shop/clinic/etc. that's tired of billing/stock problems?"
- Warm introduction converts far higher than cold outreach.

### 2. Walk-In Direct Sales (FAST & HIGH converting)
- Visit shops in your niche in your area. Short, respectful pitch + leave a one-page flyer + WhatsApp contact.
- Best hours: mid-morning / mid-afternoon (owners are free; not rush hour).

### 3. Referrals (CHEAPEST & highest quality)
- Every happy customer → ask for 2 referrals.
- Partner referral: CA/accountants, computer/IT shops, stationery shops, local business associations.

### 4. Google Maps / Google Business (BEST for local online)
- Search your niche on Google Maps, note businesses, contact via WhatsApp/phone.
- Claim your own **Google Business Profile** so "billing software near me" finds YOU.

### 5. WhatsApp / Telegram Business Groups
- Join local shopkeeper/business groups (with admin approval).
- Be helpful (tips), not spammy. Run polls/surveys → generate interest.

### 6. Social Media (local + niche)
- Instagram/Facebook: local business pages, comment helpfully, post short demo videos.
- WhatsApp Status: share a 30-sec demo clip to your network daily.

### 7. Local Directories (JustDial, IndiaMART-style, classifieds, OLX, local listings)
- Post a free business listing: "Customized offline billing/inventory software on pen drive."

### 8. Local Print/Wall Ads (optional, small budget)
- Small flyer at shop counters, notice boards, local newspaper one-liner — only after you have a proven offer.

### 9. Online Freelance/Remote (ADDITIONAL, later)
- Upwork, Fiverr, LinkedIn, IndiaMART for remote customers wanting offline/desktop accounting software.
- Great for scaling beyond your city later (Part 13).

## Your "Lead Engine" Setup (Week 1)

Set up these 3 things immediately:
1. **WhatsApp Business account** (professional profile, catalog of your offers).
2. **Google Business Profile** (free, high local value).
3. **Lead tracker spreadsheet** (Part 14 template).

## Lead Quality Score (skip the bad ones)

Score each lead 1–5; pursue 3+:
- Real pain (billing/stock/profit) — 5 if strong
- Willing to pay (deposit test) — 5 if yes
- Reachable & responsive — 5 if quick replies
- In your niche — 5 if perfect fit

> Focus ALL energy on high-score leads. A great lead is worth 10 average ones.

## Daily Outreach Targets (from Part 15)

- **Cold outreach:** 10–20 businesses/day (WhatsApp + walk-ins).
- **Follow-ups:** 5–10/day to warm/engaged leads.
- **Referral asks:** 1–2/day.
