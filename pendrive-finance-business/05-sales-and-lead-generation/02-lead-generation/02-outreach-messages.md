# 02 — Outreach Messages That Get Replies (Use These Templates)

Copy-paste, personalize with the business name, and send. Adapt tone to your market/language.

## A. WhatsApp Cold Outreach (to a specific business)

> Hi [Name/Business]! 👋
> My name is [YourName]. I build finance software for [niche] businesses that works fully OFFLINE from a pen drive — no monthly subscription.
>
> I noticed you manage [their business type]. I help owners like you with **[biggest 1–2 pains: e.g., faster billing, stock control, clear profit, GST reports]** — everything saved on YOUR pen drive, not some online server.
>
> No pressure at all. Would you be open to a quick 5-minute chat this week? I can show how it works on a shop like yours. 🙂
>
> Thanks!

## B. Walk-In Script (Face to Face)

> "Hi, I'm [Name]. I'm helping shop owners in this area move to simple offline software. Before I take your time — how do you currently handle your [billing/stock/accounts]? ... [listen] ... What's the part that's most annoying/costs you most? ... Would you like to see a quick demo of software built exactly for a place like yours — runs offline, data on your pen drive, no monthly fees?"

## C. Referral Ask (to existing/friend customer)

> "Thanks for trusting me with your software! If you know any other [niche] owner facing the same billing/stock problems, I'd love to help them too. For every successful referral, I'll give you [reward: e.g., a month free on your next renewal / ₹__]. Who should I reach out to?"

## D. Partner / CA / IT-Shop Outreach

> "Hi [Name], I build offline customized finance software for small businesses on pen drives. I'm looking for [finance/IT] professionals whose clients could benefit — I can send you a commission for successful referrals. Would you be open to a quick chat to see if it's a good fit?"

## E. Follow-Up (3 different touches — most sales need this)

**Touch 1 (Day 2 after no reply):**
> "Hi [Name], just bumping this — I know you're busy. Quick question: are you currently using any billing/inventory software, or still on a notebook/Excel? Curious what would help you most."

**Touch 2 (Day 5):**
> "Hi [Name], sharing a quick idea — many owners lose real money to stock/running out and unclear profit. I can fix that on a pen drive, fully offline, customized to you. Worth a 5-min call?"

**Touch 3 (Day 12):**
> "Hi [Name], I understand if now isn't the time. I'll keep your details and can reach out again soon if that's fine. If you ever want a free demo, just ping me. 🙂"

## F. Demo Invitation

> "Hi [Name]! Great news — I can show you a live demo on YOUR kind of business. It's 15 minutes, no obligation. I'll show: [1] instant billing with GST invoice, [2] stock that tells you when to reorder, [3] one-click profit report — all offline on your pen drive. When suits you best this week — Tue or Thu?"

## Golden Rules For Messages

1. **Personalize** (name + their situation) — never copy-paste blindly.
2. **One clear pain** — don't list 10 features.
3. **Low pressure** — "no obligation," "5 minutes," "curious."
4. **Clear next step** — the ask.
5. **Follow up** — kitty about 60–80% of replies coming after the 1st message.
6. **Send at good times** — late morning/afternoon on weekdays; avoid Sunday mornings/rush hours.

## Do Not Do

- ❌ Mass-spam generic "I have software for you" to everyone.
- ❌ Lie about features.
- ❌ Be pushy or rude when told no.
