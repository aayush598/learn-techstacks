# 01 — The Sales Process: A Repeatable Pipeline That Makes You Money

Selling is a **system**, not luck. Follow this pipeline every time. As a solo founder, you ARE the sales force — so make it simple and repeatable.

## The Sales Funnel (Your Pipeline)

```
Lead → Qualify → Demo → Proposal → Close → Deliver → Upsell/Referral
```

## Stage 1: Lead Generation (Part 05 chapter 02)
Get names/contacts of businesses that match your niche.

## Stage 2: Qualify (is this worth your time?)
- Do they have the pain we solve? (billing/stock/profit)
- Can they afford the Starter at least? (deposit test)
- Will they pay for customization, or do they want everything free?

**Drop** low-quality leads early — your time is precious. Focus on qualified ones.

## Stage 3: Demo (Part 05 chapter 04)
- Show a LIVE demo on THEIR type of business (their niche template).
- Focus on their jobs (Part 03), not features.
- Keep it 15–20 minutes, then ask.

## Stage 4: Proposal (Part 05 chapter 04)
- Give a clear written proposal: what's included, price, timeline, payment terms.

## Stage 5: Close (Part 05 chapter 03)
- Get the **deposit + signed agreement** at the close. Don't leave without it.

## Stage 6: Deliver (Part 09, Part 10)
- Deliver on time, get testimonial, collect balance.

## Stage 7: Upsell & Referral (Part 10)
- Upsell add-ons/renewal. Ask for referrals. Get testimonials.

## Sales Rules For Solo Founders

1. **Follow up relentlessly** — most sales happen on follow-up #3–5, not the first call.
2. **Have a next step** in every conversation ("Shall I set up the demo for Tuesday?").
3. **Track every lead** (Part 14 template) — never lose a name.
4. **Ask for the sale** — state clearly what you want them to do.
5. **Be honest** — never over-promise features you can't deliver (protects reputation).
6. **Time-box selling** — block focused hours for outreach every day (Part 15 daily plan).

## Target: 10 Qualified Proposals → 3 Closes

A healthy solo ratio: reach out to many, demo the good ones, close 10–30% of demos. Don't be discouraged by "no" — each no is data.
