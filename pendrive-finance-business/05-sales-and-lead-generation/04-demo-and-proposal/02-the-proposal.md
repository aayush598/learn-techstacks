# 02 — The Written Proposal: Turn a "Maybe" Into a "Yes"

A clear written proposal makes you look professional and removes ambiguity. Send one after every demo. Use Part 14's proposal template.

## What A Good Proposal Contains

1. **Your intro line** — who you are, what they asked for.
2. **Their situation/pain** (show you listened): "You mentioned stock keeps running out and you don't have clear monthly profit."
3. **The solution** — what you'll build/deliver (their chosen plan + modules).
4. **What's included** — deliverables list, timeline, support included.
5. **What's NOT included** (scope control).
6. **Price & payment terms** (50% upfront etc.).
7. **Guarantee** — money-back on delivery terms.
8. **Next steps** — what happens after they accept + sign line.
9. **Validity** — "This quote is valid for 14 days."

## The "3-Step Forward Motion" Proposal Closing

End with a simple, low-effort acceptance:
> "To start, just reply 'yes' to confirm the Complete package. I'll send the agreement, you pay the 50% deposit, and I begin your customization by [date]."

## Keep It Simple (Length Rule)

- 1 page for Starter, 1–2 pages for Complete/Premium.
- Plain language, bullet points, bold the price.
- Attach your **agreement** (Part 14) alongside.

## Scope Control (crucial)

List clearly what ISN'T included:
- ❌ Unlimited revisions beyond what's specified.
- ❌ Add-ons not listed.
- ❌ Features outside finance/business operations.
- ❌ On-site visits beyond a stated distance (or state travel fee).

Scope control = no surprise free-work demands later.

## Pricing Presentation (layout)

```
Starting Order:
3. Premium — ₹____ (with X, Y, Z + priority support)
2. Complete — ₹____  ← RECOMMENDED
1. Starter — ₹____ (with A, B)

Add-ons (optional):
- Data migration ₹____
- Extra training ₹____
- Spare pendrive ₹____
```

Always recommend ONE and say why.

## After Sending the Proposal

- **Follow up in 24–48 hrs** if no reply.
- Use a closing technique on the follow-up.
- Update your lead tracker (stage = "Proposal sent").
- If they stall >2 weeks, move them to a "nurture" list and follow up monthly (don't spam).

## Proposal = Trust Signal

A clean proposal builds confidence and separates you from shady sellers. Send it within **24 hours** of the demo, every time.
