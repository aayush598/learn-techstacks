# 01 — The Demo Playbook: The 15-Minute Demo That Wins Business

The live demo is your single most powerful sales tool. Nail it and most demos close. Do it on THEIR business type.

## Before the Demo (prep 15 min)

- Confirm time/place (prefer in-person at their shop, or a video call).
- Have their **niche template** ready and tailored with a few realistic sample records.
- Prepare to show their **top 3 jobs** (Part 03).
- Have a laptop or their shop computer ready to plug the pendrive.

## The Demo Structure (15–20 minutes max)

### 1. Open: connect (1 min)
- "Thanks for your time. I'll show how your [billing/stock/profit] can be simple, offline, and on your own pen drive."

### 2. Show their #1 pain being solved (5 min)
- Open the app, show a realistic invoice being made (their business).
- Show stock automatically updating + a reorder alert.
- "This is your pen drive. No internet, no monthly fee. It's yours."

### 3. Show #2: profit/money clarity (3 min)
- One-click daily/weekly profit report, customer ledger.

### 4. Show #3: the "data is yours" difference (3 min)
- Point out it runs offline and keeps data encrypted on the pen drive.
- Contrast: "Other apps keep your data on their server; this stays in your pocket."

### 5. The flexibility (2 min)
- "And because it's customized, I build it around YOU — your fields, your reports, your format."

### 6. Close (1–2 min)
- Summarize value + price, then use a closing technique (Part 03).

## Demo "Do's"

- Let them **touch it** — hand over the mouse/phone; let them make an invoice.
- Speak in **their words** (jobs, not features).
- Use a **realistic scenario** ("Here's you selling 5 items to a repeat customer...").
- Acknowledge their **nodding cues** and lock in agreement ("That's useful, right?").
- Keep it **short** — end while they still want more.

## Demo "Don'ts"

- ❌ Don't show every module (overwhelming).
- ❌ Don't get lost in technical detail.
- ❌ Don't ignore their objections — address each as it comes.
- ❌ Don't go over 20 minutes without a clear ask.

## Handling Demo Objections (live)

| They say | You say |
|----------|---------|
| "Only one computer?" | "You can use it on any Windows PC — just plug the pen drive in. And it never loses your data." |
| "Can my staff use it?" | "Yes — roles/permissions let you control what each person sees. I set that up for you." |
| "What about warranty?" | "Annual license covers support + fixes. If something breaks, I help." |
| "Too much to learn?" | "It takes ~30 minutes of my training to get comfortable. I'm with you till it clicks." |

## After the Demo

- **Immediately** send a written **proposal** (Part 04) with price, scope, payment terms.
- Set a **follow-up date** (never leave it open-ended).
- Record notes in your lead tracker (what they loved/objected to).

## Virtual Demo Notes (for remote customers)

- Use screen share; drive the demo yourself.
- Send a **recorded demo video** after (Part 06) as leave-behind.
- Share via WhatsApp so they can re-watch.
