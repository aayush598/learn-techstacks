# 01 — Data Privacy, Security & Liability (Protect Your Customers AND Yourself)

Your entire value proposition is "your data is safe with you." You MUST actually protect data — and protect yourself from liability. This is part of your brand.

## Your Dual Duty

1. **Protect customer data** (your #1 promise).
2. **Protect yourself** from blame/liability (contract + backups + honest practice).

## Data Protection Practices (implement)

- **Encrypted storage** — platform already uses AES-256; always use it.
- **Backups, backups, backups** — keep a secure backup of every customer's data (in addition to their pen drive). Store backups safely (local + optionally cloud, encrypted).
- **Confidentiality** — never discuss/share a customer's financial data.
- **Clean devices** — ensure delivered app has no leftover test/other-customer data.
- **Access control** — you control who has the master/encryption keys.
- **Disposal** — wipe/write-protect leftover drives properly (Part 11).

## Data Privacy (Follow Local Laws: India DPDP Act, EU GDPR, etc.)

General best practices regardless of law (localize details):
- You only collect what's needed (business name, contact, data for their app).
- State how data is used/stored (contract clause).
- Get consent for data you hold (e.g., storing their data for backup).
- Honor deletion requests reasonably (with the above backups as exception).
- Use **consent** wording in your agreement/onboarding.

> Your customers' financial data belongs to them. You are a licensed caretaker. This is both ethical and the key legal risk to manage.

## Limitation of Liability (protect yourself financially)

- In the contract, cap your liability to the fees paid (Part 03 clause).
- Clearly state you're not liable for **indirect/consequential losses** (e.g., a business losing customers due to downtime).
- This does NOT exempt you from gross negligence or data-loss-without-backup — so always keep backups (backups are your main defense).

## Your "Security Promise" To Customers (use in marketing)

> "Your data is encrypted on your pen drive, backed up securely, and never shared. We keep your business data confidential."

## Handling a Data Incident (worst case)

1. **Stay calm, act fast.**
2. **Tell customer immediately** & transparently (hiding it destroys trust).
3. **Recover from backup** before anything else.
4. **Fix the cause** before it recurs.
5. **Apologize + goodwill** (extra support/free month).
6. Document + learn (Part 11).

## Incident Prevention Checklist (before each delivery)

- [ ] Backups created and verified (restore tested once).
- [ ] No other customers' data in the delivered copy.
- [ ] Encryption active.
- [ ] Passwords/access documented in a safe place (Part 09).
- [ ] Delivery tested offline from the actual pendrive.

## Compliance Notes By Market (localize)

- **India:** DPDP Act — data privacy law; GST; Shops & Establishment.
- **EU:** GDPR — strong consent/rights rules.
- **US:** state data laws (CCPA/CPA etc.); none nationwide sweeping yet.
- **UK/elsewhere:** local equivalents.

> When operating internationally, at minimum follow the strictest/a reasonable privacy baseline + your contract terms. Consult local counsel if you scale.
