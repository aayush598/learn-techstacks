# 01 — Setting Up & Registering Your Business

You can start selling quickly and legally. Here's the path in most countries (localize: India shown as primary example, note for global).

## Start Simple, Formalize As You Grow (RECOMMENDED)

**Phase 1 (Today, ~0–2 weeks):** Operate as a **Sole Proprietorship**. Cheapest, fastest, least paperwork. You're the business. Open a **separate bank account** (even a current/savings account in your name used ONLY for business) and a **separate business UPI** so money is trackable.

**Phase 2 (When revenue grows — e.g., ₹5–10L/yr or $7–15k):** Upgrade to a formal entity (below). This is optional at low volume but gives liability protection + tax benefits + professionalism.

## Registration Options (India-primary)

| Option | What It Is | When To Choose |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| **Sole Proprietorship** | You run it personally | Start now (simplest) |
| **One Person Company (OPC)** | Single-owner Pvt Ltd | When you want liability shield + credibility but stay solo |
| **Private Limited (Pvt Ltd)** | Company with liability protection | When you hire/raise/grow significantly |
| **LLP** | Limited Liability Partnership | Similar to OPC; professional image |

**Recommended:** Start as Sole Proprietor. Upgrade to **OPC or Pvt Ltd** when your revenue/risk justifies it (see Part 08 money thresholds). For global users: US = LLC/Sole Proprietor; UK = Sole Trader/Ltd; etc. — same principle: start simple, register when growing.

## Practical Setup Checklist (start now)

- [ ] Separate **business bank account** (or a clearly-separated sub-account).
- [ ] Separate **business UPI/phone** for income (updates: link to the account).
- [ ] Get any simple local license/registration you're required to have (e.g., **Shops & Establishment** / **MSME/Udyam** registration in India — usually quick, sometimes free) — check your local rules.
- [ ] Choose business name (Part 06).
- [ ] Optional: Business phone number (second SIM or WhatsApp Business on same number is fine early).
- [ ] Bookkeeping system set up (Part 08).

## MSME / Udyam Registration (India, recommended early)

- Register as an MSME (Udyam) — free/quick online.
- Benefits: formal status, sometimes easier loans, government procurement eligibility.
- Signals you're a real, registered business (adds trust).

## PAN / Tax Registration (India)

- **PAN** (Personal) — you need for taxes; get/browse first.
- **GST registration** — required when your **annual turnover crosses threshold** (e.g., ₹20–40L for services/goods in India). Until then you may not need it, but check your rules.
- When you register for GST, you can charge/show GST and claim input credit — relevant for the "GST ready" finance software you sell.

## Business Identity Kit (for trust)

- Name card (cheap, print 100).
- A simple one-page website or LinkedIn + Google Business Profile (Part 06).
- Invoice/receipt template with your business name + PAN/reg no. (Part 14).

## Key Filing/Compliance Dates (keep a calendar)

- Income tax return filing (local deadline; India ~July 31 for individuals).
- GST returns (if registered) — monthly/quarterly.
- Any annual renewal of licenses.

> **Warning:** Do not neglect tax and registration — it's a growing legal risk. Part 11 covers compliance risk. When in doubt, ask a local CA/accountant (they're also a potential referral partner).
