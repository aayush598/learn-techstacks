# 01 — Taxes & Accounting Basics (Keep It Simple, Stay Legal)

You don't need to be an expert — you need a simple, honest system and the discipline to follow it. When in doubt, hire a local accountant/CA.

## The Golden Rule

> **Track every rupee/dollar in and out. Separate business money from personal money. Set aside your taxes in advance.**

## Accounting Model (Simple Cash Basis)

**Cash basis** — record income when you receive it, expense when you pay it. Perfect for a solo service business. (It's simple and low-volume-friendly.)

### What To Track Each Month

1. **Income** — all deposits (setup fees, licenses, add-ons) → record source.
2. **Expenses** — pendrives, travel, marketing, software/license fees (e.g., a CA if hired), bank fees, small equipment.
3. **Set-asides** — tax money (see below), savings.

## Your Quarterly/Annual Tax Steps (India-primary, generalize)

1. **Tax on your income** — estimate taxable profit (income − allowable expenses) and **set aside ~20–30%** of profits in a separate account for tax.
2. **GST** — once you cross the threshold, register and charge GST on invoices; file returns and pay collected GST. (You become a GST business — exactly the kind of thing your software helps your clients with.)
3. **Advance tax** — if your tax liability is above the limit, pay advance tax in installments to avoid penalties.
4. **File income tax return** before the deadline.

> For your country: check local income tax, sales/GST/VAT, and business registration rules. A one-hour consult with a local accountant saves you from costly mistakes. Many accountants will also refer businesses to you — a win-win.

## DIY Bookkeeping Setup (free tools)

- Use a **spreadsheet** (Part 14 income/expense template) or a free accounting app.
- **Reconcile monthly** — UPI/bank statement vs your records.
- Keep **receipts/invoices** for all deductions (electronically stored).

## Common Deductible Expenses (lower your tax, legally)

- Pendrives & hardware you buy & resell (these are COGS/direct costs).
- Travel to clients.
- Marketing & advertising.
- Office/utility portion (if home-based, a reasonable portion).
- Software/tools/subscriptions you use.
- Business phone/internet portion.
- Accountant/CA fees.

> Keep records to justify every deduction. Don't inflate — be honest.

## "Set Aside" Discipline (protect your profit)

Open a **separate savings account**. Each month move:
- **Tax %** (e.g., 20–30% of profit).
- **Savings/emergency %** (e.g., 10–20% — Part 08).
The rest is your living money. Never spend the tax set-aside.

## When To Hire a Professional

When things grow (multi-country, GST complexities, company registration) **hire a CA/accountant**. Their fee is often small vs the risk/effort saved — and they become a referral source for your software.
