# 01 — Contracts & Agreements: Protect Yourself (Never Skip)

Verbal deals lead to disputes and unpaid work. A **simple written agreement** protects you, sets expectations, and looks professional. Use the template in Part 14, localize as needed.

## Minimum Agreement Contents (for every paid project)

1. **Parties** — your business name + customer business/name.
2. **Scope of work** — exactly what you'll deliver (plan/modules, deliverables list).
3. **What's NOT included** — exclusions (scope control).
4. **Deliverables & format** — pendrive, apps, reports.
5. **Timeline** — start date, estimated delivery date.
6. **Price & payment terms** — deposit %, balance, payment method, installments if any.
7. **License/ownership** — the software remains licensed (customer gets rights to use; you retain rights). Data belongs to the customer.
8. **Support & warranty** — what the annual license covers (support, fixes) and what's paid add-on.
9. **Data/backup** — who keeps backups, disclaimer on data loss beyond backups.
10. **Privacy/confidentiality** — you won't share their data.
11. **Termination** — what happens if either side stops (refund terms).
12. **Limitation of liability** — you're not liable for indirect loss (business interruption) beyond your fees; recommended for risk.
13. **Governing law / jurisdiction** — which rules apply.
14. **Signatures + date.**

## Keep It Short & Plain

- 1–2 pages, plain language, bullet points. Not a scary legal wall.
- The act of *signing* makes it a real commitment for both sides.

## Key Clauses Explained (why they matter)

| Clause | Why It Matters |
|--------|----------------|
| Scope + exclusions | Prevents endless "can you also..." free work |
| Payment terms (deposit first) | Guarantees your cash; filters time-wasters |
| License/ownership split | You keep the software IP; customer owns their data |
| Backup disclaimer | Protects you if a pendrive physically fails (you also keep backups) |
| Limitation of liability | Caps your exposure at your fees — critical legal protection |

## The Deposit + Agreement Rule

- **Collect the deposit BEFORE starting** work.
- **Have the signed agreement BEFORE** you begin.
- Send a copy to the customer and keep your copy.

## Deliverables Checklist With Agreement

Tie the agreement to a **scope checklist** (Part 09) so there are no surprises at delivery:
- [ ] All agreed modules working
- [ ] Data migrated (if paid)
- [ ] Training done (if paid)
- [ ] Backup created + handed/stored
- [ ] Balance collected before handing pendrive

## Renewal & AMC Agreement Note

For the annual license, your base agreement can state renewals + the renewal amount, OR you send a short **renewal invoice + terms** each year. Keep it simple.

## If Something Goes Wrong (contract first)

- Point to the agreement calmly.
- Offer a reasonable fix (bug = fix free; scope not included = quote separately).
- Only escalate (legal) as a last resort — reputation matters more for a solo founder.
