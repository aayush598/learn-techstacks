# 01 — DAYS 1–30: Foundation & First Money (Your Launch Sprint)

**Goal of this phase:** Get your **first paying customer** and prove the model. Set up the basics, do research, build a sellable MVP for ONE niche, and get a first deposit.

## Weeks 1–2: Setup, Research, and Sellable MVP

### Week 1 — Foundation Setup
- [ ] Read Parts 00–01 (this resource's map + strategy).
- [ ] Set up **3 trackers** (Lead, Customer/Renewal, Cash Flow) (Part 14).
- [ ] Open **separate business bank account + UPI** (Part 07).
- [ ] Register business basics (start sole proprietorship; MSME/reg as needed) (Part 07).
- [ ] Choose **brand name + tagline + logo**; set up WhatsApp Business + Google Business Profile (Part 06).
- [ ] Set **prices** using the calculator (Part 04) + build the 3-pack offers.
- [ ] Prepare templates: agreement, invoice, outreach messages, marketing sheet (Part 14).
- [ ] Create your **one-page marketing sheet** (Part 14).

### Week 2 — Research + Pick Niche + First MVP
- [ ] Do **20 walk-in interviews** (Part 03) to confirm pain + price range.
- [ ] **Pick ONE niche** (Part 03 — retail recommended).
- [ ] Build your **niche Sellable MVP** (Part 09): billing + stock + profit + offline + backup.
- [ ] Test it on a **clean Windows PC** (Part 09).
- [ ] Create your **warm list** of 20 contacts who run businesses (Part 12).

## Weeks 3–4: First Sales Push

### Week 3 — Launch Outreach
- [ ] Message/walk-in your **warm list** (highest conversion first) (Part 12/05).
- [ ] Offer the **founding pre-sale** (Part 12) to first 3 customers.
- [ ] Run **live demos** (Part 05) — perfect the 15-min demo.
- [ ] Aim: **1+ deposit** by end of week 3–4 (the #1 goal).
- [ ] Post weekly content (starter tip + demo clip) (Part 06).

### Week 4 — Close + Deliver First Customer
- [ ] Close the first customer: signed agreement + 50% deposit (Part 05/04).
- [ ] Deliver in days (Part 09), collect balance, hand over pendrive + backup.
- [ ] **Get testimonials + Google review** (Part 06/10).
- [ ] Ask for **2–3 referrals** (Part 10).
- [ ] Set up the annual-renewal reminder (Part 04/10).

## Phase 1 Checklist (aim to complete by Day 30)

- [ ] All foundation items above done.
- [ ] 20+ interviews done; niche chosen and confirmed.
- [ ] Sellable MVP built + tested.
- [ ] **≥1 paying customer with deposit** (funded).
- [ ] First testimonial + referral flow started.
- [ ] Prices/offers/templates finalized.

## Phase 1 Warning Signs (fix fast)

- ⚠️ No deposit by end of week 4 → you haven't talked to enough real businesses OR your offer/price is off. **Do more face-to-face/warm outreach NOW** (Part 12), and re-check price (Part 04). Don't keep doing the same.

## Phase 1 Wins To Celebrate

- First paid customer / first renewal — a real milestone. Write it in your wins list (Part 11).
