# 01 — The Daily Playbook (Your Repeatable Day-in/Day-out Machine)

This is the day-to-day execution script. Follow it every working day so you never wonder "what should I do now?" — it feeds the money machine (Part 12 weekly cash targets).

## The Daily Blocks (typical solo working day)

| Time (adapt) | Block | What To Do |
|--------------|-------|------------|
| Morning | **Deep Work (build/deliver)** | Customize, test, migrate, prep deliveries (Part 09). Phone on silent. |
| Mid-morning break | **Outreach block 1** | 10+ touches: warm leads, follow-ups, referrals (Part 05). |
| Afternoon | **Demos / calls** | Run demos (Part 05), requirements calls, close (deposit + agreement). |
| Late afternoon | **Support + admin** | Batch-answer support (Part 10), update trackers, send invoices. |
| Evening | **Bonus outreach / content** | A few more touches, post 1 piece of content (Part 06). |
| Evening close | **Daily close-on** | 5-min review (below). |

## The 5-Minute Daily Close-On (every evening)

1. [ ] Money in/out today updated in Cash Flow tracker.
2. [ ] Any unresponded support handled.
3. [ ] 3+ leads touched today (outreach/follow-up).
4. [ ] At least one project advanced.
5. [ ] Tomorrow's #1 priority noted.

## Daily Money-Making Priorities (in order)

1. **Book/get a deposit** (highest priority when pipeline is light).
2. **Deliver signed work** → collect balance (turn work into cash).
3. **Follow up every engaged lead** (multitouch — Part 05).
4. **Ask for referrals** on every happy customer.
5. **Renewals/follow-ups** (Part 04/10).

> Rule: When pipeline is light, spend the day **outreaching**, not polishing. When signed work exists, **deliver fast** to get cash + free your hands for more sales.

## The Weekly Rhythm (map onto the weekly cash playbook)

| Day | Focus |
|-----|-------|
| Mon | Outreach push + pipeline update |
| Tue | Delivery/build |
| Wed | Demos + close |
| Thu | Delivery/support + renewals |
| Fri | Admin: cash flow, invoices, scorecard, next week plan |
| Sat | Content/marketing + outreach catch-up |
| Sun | Rest |

## Anti-Procrastination Rules

- **Start with the scariest/important task** first (usually outreach or closing).
- **Time-box** — set a timer for 45–60 min blocks; do that one thing.
- **Batch** — don't context-switch (outreach hour, then deep work hour).
- **Phone off** during deep work.
- **Small wins** — finish a tiny task to build momentum.

## Daily Discipline Checklist (your non-negotiables)

- [ ] **Deposits first, always** — never do unpaid work.
- [ ] **Update trackers** — no "I think I'll remember."
- [ ] **Reply to support within 1 business day.**
- [ ] **Back up customer data** continuously.
- [ ] **Rest** — protect your rest day; burnout kills solo businesses (Part 10/11).

## Crisis-Mode Daily (lean weeks)

When cash is tight:
1. Cut non-essential costs.
2. Spend the day on **quick-cash** (Part 12): warm outreach, founding pre-sale, renewals, training/rescue service.
3. Keep at least part-time bridging income if needed (Part 08/11).
4. Track everything; adjust offer/message if no traction.

## Remember Your North Star

> **Every day moves toward: deposits now + renewals later + a sellable business.** Follow the blocks, hit the touches, protect your rest, and let the money machine compound.
