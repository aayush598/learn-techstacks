# 03 — DAYS 61–90: Momentum, Renewals, and Proof (Set Up Compound Growth)

**Goal:** Reach a healthy customer base, get first renewals flowing, and set the business up to compound beyond day 90.

## Weeks 9–13: Momentum + Compound Growth

### Week 9 — Sharpen the Funnel
- [ ] Track your numbers (Part 08 metrics): leads → demos → proposals → closes → deposits.
- [ ] **Double down channel that works** (referrals/local/online) — drop what doesn't (Part 05/06).
- [ ] Improve the demo + close script with real data (Part 05).

### Week 10 — Grow Recurring
- [ ] Push **annual prepay / renewals** (Part 04/10).
- [ ] Offer add-ons (migration, backup service, priority support) to raise ARPC (Part 02/04).
- [ ] Set up **auto-renewal** where possible (with consent) (Part 04).

### Week 11 — Productize + Partner
- [ ] Package your niche template as a **repeatable product** (Part 12/13).
- [ ] Formalize **2–3 partner referral channels** (CA/IT shop/association) (Part 10).
- [ ] Consider listing on **online/freelance channels** to reach beyond city (Part 13).

### Week 12–13 — Consolidate & Plan
- [ ] Review **monthly scorecard** (Part 08) — revenue, renewals, ARPC, cash.
- [ ] Build a **90-day-forward plan** (the next quarter) with your wins + learnings.
- [ ] Decide: raise prices / add a 2nd niche / get help (Part 13) based on data.

## End-of-90-Day Targets (honest, achievable)

- [ ] **5–10 customers** (vary with niche/price).
- [ ] **Repeatable delivery** (≤ days per customization).
- [ ] **First annual renewals** structured & beginning.
- [ ] **Referral engine + partners** active.
- [ ] **Cash positive** with business buffer + some renewals.
- [ ] **Testimonials + Google reviews** banked.
- [ ] Prices raised (if warranted) for new customers.

## The Quarterly Review (do at Day 90)

Use Part 08 metrics to answer:
1. What made money? (do more)
2. What wasted time/money? (stop)
3. What's my best channel? (double down)
4. Am I profitable after salary? (yes = real business)
5. What's the next 90-day target?

Update your **master tracker** and set the next quarter's weekly cash target (Part 12).

## Beyond Day 90

You've proven the model. Now follow **Part 13 (Growth & Scaling)** to compound: productize, partner, go remote, add niches, possibly get help — building toward real recurring wealth.

## Celebrating The Win

A paying, recurring customer business after 90 days = you've beaten the hardest part (starting + proving). Protect momentum, keep the systems, and scale.
