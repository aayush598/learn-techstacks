# 01 — Pendrive Selection & Fulfillment (Reliable Hardware = Reputation)

Choosing reliable hardware and prepping it correctly prevents data-loss disasters (your biggest risk).

## Pendrive Selection (buy quality, not cheap)

| Spec | Recommendation |
|------|----------------|
| Type | USB 3.0 or higher (faster, reliable) |
| Capacity | 16–64 GB (plenty for software + data) |
| Brand | Reputable: Sandisk, Kingston, Crucial, Samsung |
| Why | Cheap no-name drives fail and lose data → reputation killer |

> Rule: Never buy the absolute cheapest. A ₹100 drive that fails costs you a customer + trust. A reliable ₹300–500 drive is worth it. (You can mark it up as hardware cost.)

## Fulfillment Prep (before handing over)

1. **Fresh, tested drive** — check it works, no errors (quick format/verify).
2. **Load the configured app + data.**
3. **Lock/protect** the data safety (enable write-protect if the platform supports it — optional).
4. **Label** the drive (business name or "Backup") for clarity.
5. **Seal/package** professionally (with your business card / QR to demo).
6. **Include backup** — hand a second copy OR point to stored backup + explain.

## Backup Policy (your #1 protection against data loss)

- **Always keep an offsite/secure backup** of every customer's app + data (your house/cloud, encrypted).
- Hand the customer a **spare backup drive** (or explain where theirs is).
- Test that restore works (Part 03).
- Rule: **Two copies at minimum** — one with customer, one with you.

## Multiple Drives / Multi-User Setup

- For multi-computer, each machine may need its own pendrive (or a shared one they hand around — but recommend individual drives for safety).
- Document which drive goes where (Part 14 tracker).

## Handling Drive Failure (worst case, professionally)

- Your **backup** saves you. Rebuild from backup fast.
- Never blame the customer; just fix it quickly (and to their relief).
- This is exactly why you keep backups + charge an annual license that covers recovery help.

## Keep A "Fulfillment Kit"

Have ready: a stock of reliable pendrives, labels, small packaging, a card with support/renewal info. This makes each delivery fast and professional.
