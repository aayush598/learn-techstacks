# 02 — Scoping & Requirements (Get It Right Before You Build)

Clear scope = no surprise free-work, faster delivery, happier customer. Spend effort here.

## The Requirements Call (before quoting/building)

Ask the customer these 15 questions (adjust per niche):

### Business Basics
1. What type of business exactly? How many staff/counters?
2. Rough monthly sales/customers/stock lines?
3. What do you sell & how (billing, cash/credit, home delivery)?

### Current State
4. How do you track today (paper/Excel/app/nothing)?
5. What's the most painful part / wastes most time?
6. Have you used software before? What did you hate?

### Wants (jobs)
7. What MUST the software do for you (top 3)?
8. Do you need GST/invoice printing? In what language/format?
9. Multi-user or single user? One shop or multiple?
10. Do you need customer credit/ledger tracking?

### Data
11. What data do you already have (Excel/list) to import?
12. How important is backup/offline to you?

### Logistics
13. Who will use it daily (owner/staff)? Tech comfort?
14. Where will you run it (one computer, which OS — Windows?).
15. Budget range? Preferred payment (UPI/cash/split)?

## Build The "Scope Sheet" (Part 14 template)

From answers, produce a **scope checklist**:
- Modules included (from Part 02 module library).
- Forms/tables to customize.
- Reports/invoice format.
- Users/permissions.
- Data migration scope.
- Training sessions.
- Deliverables (pendrives, backup).
- **Exclusions** (what's NOT included).

> The scope sheet = the "what's included" list in your agreement (Part 07). Both sign it.

## Managing Scope Creep (the #1 killer)

- Any request **outside the signed scope** → quote it as an **add-on** (don't silently absorb).
- Politely: "That's not in the current scope. I can add it as an add-on for ₹____ if you'd like."
- Keep the scope sheet **visible** during the project ("we agreed on X").
- Reserve a small "buffer" for definition-of-done tightening, but hold the line beyond it.

## Confirm Payment Before Building

- Get **signed scope + agreement + 50% deposit** before you begin (Part 04/07).
- This locks scope and funds the project.

## "Definition of Done" (agree upfront)

Agree what "done" means:
- Which modules/workflows tested.
- Data migrated.
- Training delivered.
- Backup handed over.
- Balance paid; pendrive delivered.
Agreeing "done" avoids an endless "one more change" loop.
