# 01 — Financial Growth Plan: From First Rupee to Real Money

A realistic path from your first sale to a serious income. Follow the stages; don't skip.

## Stage 0 — Survival (Months 0–3) [Goal: first cash + prove demand]

- Objective: get your **first 1–3 paying customers** with a deposit-first model.
- Keep costs near-zero. Use quick-cash routes (Part 12).
- Use Starter/launch offers to build proof fast.
- **Milestone:** First ₹15–25k / $200–350 received + 2–3 testimonials.

## Stage 1 — Foundation (Months 3–6) [Goal: repeatable delivery]

- Sell consistently to your one niche. Nail the template + delivery (Part 09).
- Grow to **5–10 customers**.
- Start collecting **annual renewals** (recurring base begins).
- Get referrals flowing (Part 10).
- **Milestone:** ~₹50–80k / $700–1,100 cumulative; first renewals due.

## Stage 2 — Cash Flowing (Months 6–12) [Goal: replace side income]

- Bucket hours: outreach + delivery + support as a routine (Part 15).
- Raise prices once you have proof (Part 04).
- Add add-ons / premium to raise ARPC.
- **Milestone:** 15–30 active customers; ~₹3–8L / $4–10k year-1 revenue; recurring base covers costs.

## Stage 3 — Scaling (Year 2+) [Goal: real business value]

- Go online/remote to new regions (Part 13).
- Build templates library; speed up delivery (Part 09).
- Consider formal entity + possibly first hire/assistant (Part 07/13).
- Add more templates/verticals (Part 13).
- **Milestone:** recurring revenue ~₹1L+/mo eventually; ARPC up; assets (templates, client base) building toward a sellable business (Part 13).

## Reinvestment Priorities (as money grows, in order)

1. **Better pendrives/hardware** (reliability = reputation).
2. **Marketing** (local ads once proof exists).
3. **Tools/software** (accounting, video, website).
4. **Training/books** (skills = better delivery).
5. **First help** (assistant/template builder) when your time is the bottleneck.

## The Compounding Rule

**Do NOT spend 100% of profit.** Pay yourself a sustainable salary, keep buffers, and reinvest the rest into what creates more customers (marketing, capacity, templates). This is how solo becomes scalable.

## Red Flags In Growth (check quarterly)

- ⚠️ You're turning away work due to lack of time → time to raise prices OR get help.
- ⚠️ Support is eating all your time → improve templates/testing + self-help docs (Part 10).
- ⚠️ Renewals flat/low → your value/support isn't sticky enough (Part 10).
