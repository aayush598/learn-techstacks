# 02 — Emergency Funds & Buffers (Your Safety Net)

Buffers are what let you survive rainy weeks and avoid desperate/bad decisions.

## The Three Buffers You Maintain

| Buffer | Purpose | Size Target |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| **1. Personal Emergency Fund** | Your life if income stops | 3–6 months × living cost |
| **2. Business Cash Buffer** | Smooth lumpy business income | 1–3 months × business costs |
| **3. Tax Set-Aside** | Pay taxes without stress | ~20–30% of profit (Part 07) |

## How To Build Buffers (even with little money)

- **Auto-transfer** a fixed amount/percent to each account the moment money arrives (build habit over amount).
- **Start small**: even ₹500–1,000 / $10 per project adds up fast.
- **Windfall rule**: any unexpected big payment → put 50% into buffers.
- **Never raid the emergency fund** for non-emergencies (impulse buys, "good deals").

## Order of Building (priority)

1. **Survive** → pay living costs.
2. **Business buffer** → so lean weeks don't kill you.
3. **Emergency fund** → build to 3–6 months over time.
4. **Tax set-aside** → always running in parallel.

## When Buffers Are "Enough"

- Business buffer covers 1–3 lean months.
- Personal emergency 3–6 months.
- You can shrug at a slow month without panic = you've made it.

## Using Buffers Correctly

- **Business buffer** = for business survival (replace lost income, buy essential stock) + reinvestment with discipline.
- **Emergency fund** = ONLY genuine emergencies (medical, major repair) — replace it after using.

## Common Money Mistakes (avoid)

- ❌ Spending profit before setting aside tax/savings.
- ❌ Mixing personal & business money.
- ❌ Buying a "nice new setup" before the business pays for itself.
- ❌ Taking on high-interest debt for marketing.
- ❌ Chasing get-rich-quick schemes with your buffer.

## The Mental Benefit

Money buffers = **freedom to say no** to bad deals, to wait for good customers, and to work calmly. That calm is worth more than the cash itself.
