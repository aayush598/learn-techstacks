# 01 — Cash Flow Management (Cash Is King — Never Run Dry)

Profit on paper means nothing if cash runs out. Manage **cash flow** obsessively, especially in the early irregular months.

## The Cash Flow Rule

> **Money IN (deposits/sales) must consistently arrive before/early enough to cover money OUT (costs + your life). Get paid upfront. Convert to recurring quickly.**

## Cash Flow Reality of This Business

**The good news:** Setup-fee-first gets you **upfront cash**. 
**The risk:** Sales can be lumpy (some lean weeks). So you MUST:
1. Collect **deposits** (cash now).
2. Build **recurring renewals** (predictable base).
3. Keep costs **low & variable**.
4. Watch **leads → cash** pipeline (Part 05) so you know when cash is coming.

## The "Cash Cushion" (business buffer)

Keep **1–3 months of business+living costs** as a business cash buffer. This smooths lumpy sales weeks and lets you say no to bad deals.

## Cash Flow Strategies

1. **Deposit everything** — 50% upfront = half the cash before you work (Part 04).
2. **Preference for prepaid**: annual license prepay discount pulls cash forward.
3. **Don't buy inventory ahead** — buy pendrives in small batches as you sell (avoid cash lock).
4. **Negotiate payment** — if a client stalls balance, slow down delivery until paid.
5. **Recurring base** — aim to grow annual renewals so fixed costs are always covered.

## Handling a Cash Squeeze (lean weeks)

- **A: Bridge with quick service** — do low-cost training/migration (fast cash) or a Starter setup.
- **B: Ask for referral sales** — cheap high-quality leads.
- **C: Reduce costs** — trim marketing/travel, work from home.
- **D: Prepay discount push** — get renewals in ahead.
- **E: Temporary side work** — freelance/tuition until business fills. (Not shameful; it protects you.)
- **Avoid:** high-interest loans, credit-card debt, borrowing from family with no plan.

## Cash Flow Early Warning Signs (act fast)

- ⚠️ Closing balance trending down 3+ weeks.
- ⚠️ Deposits not covering upcoming costs.
- ⚠️ Too much time on unpaid/non-revenue work.

> Fix: immediately pivot to revenue-generating activity (Part 12 quick-cash).

## Never Mix Personal & Business Cash

Pay yourself a fixed amount; keep the rest in the business buffer. This preserves clean accounting (Part 07) and cash discipline.
