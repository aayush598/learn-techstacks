# 01 — Business Finance: The 8 Numbers You Must Know

Track these 8 numbers monthly. They tell you if you're winning. Most are simple to compute from your tracker (Part 14).

## The 8 Key Business Metrics

### 1. Total Revenue (IN)
All money received: setup fees + licenses + add-ons. **Target: growing month-over-month over time.**

### 2. Total Costs (OUT)
All business spending. Keep it **variable & low** early on.

### 3. Gross Margin (per project)
Setup fee minus direct costs (pendrive, travel) = margin per customer.
> Your setup should have a **healthy margin (~60–80%)** after direct costs + a buffer.

### 4. Net Profit (monthly)
Revenue − costs − your salary = profit. **Positive net profit with a salary = real business.**

### 5. Recurring Revenue (Monthly/Annual Recurring)
Your running annual licenses. **This is your "sleep income" and business VALUE.** Grow it relentlessly.

### 6. Customer Count & Renewal Rate
- Active customers: ____
- Renewal rate: licenses renewed / due (%).
- **Target: ≥ 80% renewal.**

### 7. Average Revenue Per Customer (ARPC)
Total revenue ÷ active customers. **Raise it via add-ons/upsells and premium plans.**

### 8. Lead → Cash Time
Average days from first contact to payment. **Shorten it** (deposits, faster demos).

## Reading Your Numbers (what to do)

| If you see… | Do this |
|-------------|---------|
| Revenue up, costs flat | 🔥 double down (more marketing/sales) |
| Costs rising faster than revenue | Trim/reduce; focus on higher-margin add-ons |
| Renewal rate falling | Fix support/value (Part 10) — renewals are cheap revenue |
| No new customers for 2–3 weeks | Increase outreach + fix offer (Part 05/12) |
| Cash falling 3+ weeks | Quick-cash routes (Part 12) + cut costs |

## Reinvestment & "Pay Yourself" Policy

- **Pay yourself a fixed salary** once income allows (e.g., 30–50% of profit).
- **Reinvest 20–40%** into growth (better pendrives, local ads, more outreach hours, better laptop).
- **Keep the rest** as profit/buffer.

## Break-Even Number (know it cold)

**Monthly break-even = (your salary + business costs + tax) per month.**
- How many customers/licenses cover it? 
- Example: needs ₹40k/mo → ~5 Complete setups/yr + renewals, or ~8 Starter/year.
- Knowing break-even = you know exactly how many sales you need each month. Write your number here:
  **My monthly break-even revenue = ₹/$ ____ ( ≈ ____ customers or ____ renewals ).**

## The Real Money Engine (long-term)

1. Your **one-time setup fees** = immediate cash.
2. Your **annual renewals** = compounding baseline (10 renewals year 1 → 10 + new in year 2, etc.).
3. Your **add-ons & premium** = higher ARPC.
**All three compound → by year 2–3 you have real, semi-passive recurring income.**
