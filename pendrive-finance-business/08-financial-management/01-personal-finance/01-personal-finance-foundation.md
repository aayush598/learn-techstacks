# 01 — Personal Finance Foundation (You Cannot Build on Empty Stomach)

A solo business fails fast when personal finances collapse. Protect your personal runway first — it buys you time to make the business work.

## The 3 Personal Money Accounts (Set Up Today)

1. **Living Account** — your monthly expenses (rent, food, bills).
2. **Emergency Fund (separate savings)** — 3–6 months of living expenses you NEVER touch except real emergencies. Non-negotiable.
3. **Business Account** — separate from personal (Part 07).

> Rule: Business money and personal money NEVER mix. Pay yourself a **fixed salary/allowance** from the business each month once it earns.

## Your Personal Budget (Know Your Number)

### Monthly Living Cost (L)

List: rent, food, transport, utilities, phone/internet, EMIs/loans, family support, insurance, misc.

**L = ₹/$ ____ per month.**

### Minimum Personal Runway (E)

**E = L × 3 (better: × 6)** = Emergency Fund target.

> If you have little savings, this business buys you **time** — protect it. Start lean.

## Personal Runway for Starting (how long can you survive?)

- **Runway (months) = (Savings) ÷ L**
- If runway < 3 months, prioritize **quick cash** (Part 12 starter/first-sale routes) and keep costs near-zero.
- Consider a **part-time day job or freelance** until the business pays your bill genuinely (bridging income — very smart, not a failure).

## The Order of Your Money (every rupee that comes in)

1. Cover **living costs** (you survive).
2. Pay **business costs** (pendrives, marketing) — reinvest only what's needed.
3. **Set aside tax** (Part 07).
4. Emergency fund buffer.
5. Profit/savings + growth reinvestment.

## Reducing Personal Burn (fast money is about your L being low)

- Keep your fixed costs low so you don't need huge income to break even.
- At L of ₹30–50k, you break even with just 1–2 projects/a couple of licenses — very achievable.
- **Lower L = less pressure = better decisions** (you won't accept bad deals out of desperation).

## Insurance (cheap, essential risk protection)

- **Health insurance** — protect yourself from a medical bill wiping out the business.
- Consider **term life** if you have dependents (small monthly cost).
- Business indemnity/liability — optional but consider as you grow (Part 07/11).

## Monthly Personal Money Ritual (30 min)

1. Check Living account balance.
2. Check Emergency fund is intact (don't raid it).
3. Record this month's income & living spend (Part 14 template).
4. Move tax + savings set-asides (Part 07/08).
5. Decide what you can reinvest into the business.
