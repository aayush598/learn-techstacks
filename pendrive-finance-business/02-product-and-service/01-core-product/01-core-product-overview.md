# 01 — The Core Product: Customized Offline Finance Software on a USB Drive

## What the Customer Actually Receives

A **USB pendrive** containing a complete, customized business finance application that:

- Runs on any **Windows computer** — no installation, no admin rights needed (portable).
- Stores **all data encrypted** (AES-256) on the pendrive itself — data is on their drive, not the cloud.
- Works **fully offline**.
- Is **customized** to their business — their forms, fields, reports, and workflow.
- Keeps **no traces** on the host computer (privacy).

## What the Platform Includes (the "engine")

Powered by the Pendrive Finance Platform (see `~/aayush/projects/pendrive_finance/software/`) — a metadata-driven, no-code runtime + visual designer. Key capabilities:

- Visual **Form / Table / Menu / Report / Dashboard** designers — you customize without coding.
- Core finance modules: **Accounting, Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Expenses, Income, Cash/Bank, Taxation/GST, Payroll, Fixed Assets, Loans, Budgeting, CRM/Vendor/Product management**.
- **Security**: user login, roles/permissions (RBAC), audit trail, encryption.
- **Backup & restore**, import/export (Excel), printing, document management.
- **Multi-company / multi-language** for larger clients.
- **Lua scripting** for custom workflow logic.
- **AI assistant** for form generation & suggestions (advanced).

## The "Customer-Facing" Simple Description (what you tell them)

> "You get a pen drive. Plug it into any computer. Open your finance software. It looks and works exactly for your business — your bills, your inventory, your reports. Everything is saved securely on the pen drive. No internet needed. No monthly subscription. Your data is yours."

## The 5 Core Value Props You Always Lead With

1. **It's yours** — data on your drive, no one else can access it, no vendor lock.
2. **It fits your business** — customized to exactly how you work.
3. **It works offline** — never stops because the internet is down.
4. **It's affordable long-term** — small setup + low annual fee, not endless subscription.
5. **It's simple** — built for non-technical owners; you help set it up.

## Important: Platform Maturity Note

The platform source exists and is under active development (see `software/PLAN.md` phases). **Before selling at scale, have a working, tested deliverable.** Part 09 gives you the fast-track path to a sellable product. Do not over-promise features that aren't built/tested yet.

## Hardware & Fulfillment Basics

- Recommended: **USB 3.0+ pendrive, 16–64 GB**, reliable brand (SanDisk/Kingston/Crucial).
- Pre-load runtime + customization, then seal/deliver with a **protective cover**.
- Keep **spare/backup copies** of every customer's data.
- Optionally brand the pendrive with the business name for a professional feel.
