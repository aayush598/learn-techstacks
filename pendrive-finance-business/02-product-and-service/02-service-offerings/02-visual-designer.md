# 02 — Product: The Visual Designer (How You Customize Fast)

## Why the Designer Is Your Superpower

The platform's **Visual Designer** lets you create and customize complete applications **without writing code**:

- **Form Designer** — drag & drop fields (text, number, date, dropdown, file, etc.)
- **Table Designer** — create tables/fields, relationships, keys
- **Menu Designer** — build navigation (sidebar, ribbon, tree)
- **Report Designer** — build print-ready reports, invoices, summaries
- **Dashboard Designer** — KPI cards, charts, gauges/widgets
- **Theme Designer** — colors/fonts to match the business brand
- **Workflow Designer** — visual flowchart approval/automation
- **Permission system** — role & field-level access

**Result:** You can build a niche template once, then customize each customer's copy in hours-to-days, not weeks-to-months.

## The "Template + Customize" Method (YOUR core delivery engine)

1. **Build one master template** for your chosen niche (e.g., Retail) with all standard tables/forms/reports.
2. For each customer, **copy the template**, then **customize**:
   - Business name, logo, theme colors
   - Their specific fields (e.g., a shop's "purchase from wholesaler" vs "purchase from farmer")
   - Their reports / invoice format
   - Their users & permissions
3. **Test** on the pendrive, then deliver.

This is what makes the business **fast, repeatable, and profitable**.

## Quality Gates Before Delivery (never skip)

- [ ] Data entry forms work end-to-end (add/edit/delete/save).
- [ ] Reports & invoices print correctly with business name/logo.
- [ ] Login/permissions work as configured.
- [ ] Offline run tested on a clean Windows machine from the pendrive.
- [ ] **Backup created** and stored separately.
- [ ] No confidential test data left in the delivered copy.

## Using the AI Assistant (advanced accelerant)

The platform's AI assistant can help generate forms from a text description and suggest fields/captions. Use it to speed up initial template builds — but always test the output before delivery (Part 11 risk).
