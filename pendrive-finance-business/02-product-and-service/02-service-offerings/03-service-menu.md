# 03 — Service Offerings: The Full Menu of What You Sell

Beyond the core product, you sell a menu of services. This is where recurring and higher revenue comes from.

## The Four Revenue Pillars

1. **Setup & Customization Fee** (one-time) — the main upfront cash.
2. **Annual License / Maintenance** (recurring) — your recurring income engine.
3. **Add-on Services** (per project) — training, data migration, custom reports, extra modules.
4. **Hardware + Accessories** (per unit) — pendrives, backup drives, printer config.

## Service Menu (price each in Part 04)

| # | Service | Type | Description |
|---|---------|------|-------------|
| 1 | **Starter Custom Setup** | One-time | Standard niche template + setup on 1 pendrive + basic training |
| 2 | **Full Customization** | One-time | Deep tailoring: custom forms, reports, fields, workflow for the exact business |
| 3 | **Data Migration** | Add-on | Move data from Excel/paper/old software into the new system |
| 4 | **Training & Onboarding** | Add-on | Show owner/staff how to use it (1–3 sessions) |
| 5 | **Custom Report / Invoice Design** | Add-on | Branded invoice, special reports |
| 6 | **Extra Modules** | Add-on | Payroll, fixed assets, branch management, etc. |
| 7 | **Annual License / AMC** | Recurring | Support + updates + backup assistance for 12 months |
| 8 | **Priority Support** | Recurring | Faster/on-site support |
| 9 | **Backup Service** | Recurring | You keep secure backups of their data |
| 10 | **Second Pendrive / Copy** | One-time | Spare drive for safety |
| 11 | **Multi-User / Multi-Branch** | One-time | Advanced multi-company/multi-branch setup |
| 12 | **Ongoing Data Entry / Assist** | Recurring | Optional outsourced data help (advanced) |

## Recommended "Offer Ladder" (Guide Customers Up)

Sell from lowest-risk to highest-value:

| Level | Offer | Target |
|-------|-------|--------|
| **Entry (Low risk)** | Starter Setup — low price to get first customers & testimonials | New/cold leads |
| **Core (Recommended)** | Full Custom Setup + annual license | Most customers |
| **Premium** | Full Custom + Migration + Training + Priority support + backup | Established businesses |
| **Enterprise/Partner** | Multi-branch, multi-user, ongoing support contract | Growing clients |

## Bundling Recommendation

**Start with 3 clean offers** (from Part 04). Don't overwhelm yourself or customers with 12 options. Offer the rest as "add-ons available."

## Don't-Sell List (Scope Control)

- ❌ Building custom software from scratch for each customer (too slow).
- ❌ Features far outside finance/business operations (web/game/apps).
- ❌ Unlimited free lifetime support.
- ❌ Working without a signed agreement or upfront deposit.
- ❌ On-site support visits beyond agreed distance.
