# 01 — The Module & Feature Library (What You Can Sell)

This is a map of every module/feature you can customize and sell. Use it to scope a customer's project and to build niche templates.

## Core Finance & Operations Modules

| Module | What It Does | Typical Customers |
|--------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Company Management** | Business profile, logo, fiscal settings | All |
| **User Management** | Users, roles, passwords, permissions | All multi-user |
| **Sales / POS** | Bills, invoices, quotations, POS billing | Retail, restaurants, services |
| **Purchase** | Purchase orders, supplier bills, stock-in | Retail, manufacturing, restaurants |
| **Product / Inventory** | Items, categories, stock levels, reorder alerts | Retail, pharmacy, restaurant |
| **Accounting** | Ledger, journal entries, double-entry, P&L, balance sheet | All |
| **Cash & Bank** | Cash register, bank accounts, reconciliations | All |
| **Expense Management** | Record & categorize expenses, petty cash | All |
| **Income Management** | Track income streams, receipts | All |
| **Taxation / GST** | GST rates, tax invoices, returns/reports | India & GST regions |
| **Payroll** | Salary, attendance, payslips, deductions | Firms, clinics, salons with staff |
| **Fixed Assets** | Asset register, depreciation | Larger businesses |
| **Loan Management** | Loan tracking, EMI, interest | Businesses with loans |
| **Budgeting** | Budget vs actual tracking | Growth businesses |
| **CRM** | Customer contacts, follow-ups, history | Service, B2B |
| **Vendor Management** | Supplier records, ledgers, payments | Retail, manufacturing |
| **Document Management** | Attach invoices, agreements, scans | All |

## Reporting & Intelligence Modules

- Standard reports: **Sales, Purchase, Stock, Profit & Loss, Cash Flow, Customer/Vendor ledgers**
- **Dashboard/KPI** cards, charts, gauges
- **Search** across records
- **Notifications & alerts** (low stock, pending payments, birthdays)
- **Business Analytics** (trends, best sellers)

## System / Platform Modules

- **Security** — encryption, RBAC, audit trail, tamper detection
- **Portable USB** — auto-run, no traces
- **Backup & Restore** — automatic, one-click
- **Import / Export** — Excel in/out, CSV
- **Printing** — invoices, thermal printer support, PDF export
- **Multi-Currency** — for exporters/multi-region
- **Multi-Language / i18n** — local language support
- **Multi-Company / Multi-Branch** — enterprise
- **Workflow Engine** — approvals, automation
- **AI Assistant** — form generation, suggestions, anomaly detection
- **Plugin System / Marketplace** — extensions (advanced, ecosystem)

## How To Use This Library

1. When scoping a customer, **pick the modules they need** from this list → this becomes their "module checklist."
2. When building a niche template, **include the default module set** for that niche + a couple of add-ons to upsell.
3. When adding features later, **add modules to the library** and to your pricing menu.

## Upsell Ideas (from modules)

- A Retail client → upsell **Payroll**, **Fixed Assets**, **Multi-Branch**, **Backup Service**.
- A Clinic → upsell **Document Management**, **Appointment reminders**, **Priority support**.
