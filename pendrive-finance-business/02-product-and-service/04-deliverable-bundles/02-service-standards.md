# 02 — Service Standards & Quality Promise

Define your standard of excellence so every delivery is consistent and earns referrals. A solo business lives or dies by reputation.

## Your Service Promise (say this to every customer)

> "I will set up software that fits your business, keep your data safe, and be there when you need help. If something isn't right, I'll fix it."

## 10 Service Standards

1. **Deliver on time** — give a clear date, then hit it (or communicate early if delayed).
2. **Reply within one business day** to support messages.
3. **Test before deliver** — never hand over untested software.
4. **Always keep a backup** of customer data.
5. **Explain simply** — no jargon; make the owner confident.
6. **Protect confidentiality** — never share a customer's data.
7. **Under-promise, over-deliver** — surprise them with a little extra.
8. **Ask for feedback** after every project and act on it.
9. **Have clear agreements** — scope, price, and what's included in writing.
10. **Respect their money** — fair pricing, honest invoices, no surprises.

## Tracking Quality (simple)

Keep a simple log (spreadsheet or notebook):

| Customer | Delivered on time? | Issues reported | Support response time | Would they refer? |
|----------|--------------------|------------------|------------------------|--------------------|

Aim: **delivered on time = 100%**, **would-refer = 90%+**.

## Handling Mistakes

- Acknowledge quickly, apologize sincerely, fix fast.
- If a bug appears, provide a **workaround immediately** + permanent fix soon.
- Offer a goodwill gesture (extra support, small discount) if it caused real trouble — this builds massive loyalty.

## Quality → Referrals → Growth

Referrals are your cheapest, highest-converting revenue. Consistently great service is the #1 referral driver. See Part 10 for the referral system.
