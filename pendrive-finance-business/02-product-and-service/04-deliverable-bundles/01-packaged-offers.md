# 01 — Packaged Offers (Your 3 Clean Offers)

Never present a confusing menu. Present **3 clear packages** and position add-ons separately. These become your sales sheet (Part 05) and marketing core (Part 06).

> Prices shown are **starting points** — final numbers are set in Part 04. Always collect half upfront.

## Offer 1 — "Starter" (For First Customers & Low Friction)

**Best for:** Cold leads, price-sensitive, or your first testimonials.

- One niche template configured for their business
- 1 pendrive
- Basic data-entry forms + essential reports + invoice printing
- 30-day support (WhatsApp)
- Annual license included in first year

**Price:** Lowest setup fee (see Part 04) — designed to say "yes" and build trust.

---

## Offer 2 — "Complete Custom" (RECOMMENDED — sell this most)

**Best for:** Most businesses that want it to actually fit.

- Full customization of forms, fields, reports, invoices, workflow
- Choose modules from the library (retail, inventory, accounting, etc.)
- 1–2 pendrives + backup setup
- Data migration from Excel/paper
- 2 training sessions + 30–90 day support
- Annual license for year 1

**Price:** Mid setup fee + annual license (see Part 04).

---

## Offer 3 — "Premium / Business Partner" (High Value)

**Best for:** Established or multi-branch businesses.

- Everything in Complete Custom
- Multi-user / multi-branch
- Priority + on-site support
- Manual/automated backup service
- Custom reports & advanced modules (payroll, fixed assets, analytics)
- Extended support year

**Price:** Higher setup fee + annual retainer (see Part 04).

## Bundling Rules

1. **Always quote at least "Complete Custom"** as the recommended middle option (anchoring makes Starter look cheap and Premium look premium).
2. **Bundle support + backup** into Premium to raise value (recurring service revenue).
3. **Never give unlimited free support** — cap support days per package.
4. **Collect 50% (or a minimum deposit) upfront** before starting any work.

## "Add-On Builder" Technique

When a customer says "just the basic," gently offer 1–2 relevant add-ons that save them real time (e.g., "Would you like me to also import your existing 500 customers from Excel so you don't retype them?"). This naturally raises order value.
