# 01 — The Price Calculator (Build Your Actual Prices)

Use this simple calculator to derive prices that (a) cover your time, (b) cover costs, (c) leave profit. Fill in your numbers.

## Step 1: Know Your Hourly "Minimum" Rate

Total monthly personal expenses (E) = ₹/$
Hours you can work on client work per month (H) = hours

**Minimum hourly rate = E ÷ H**
Example: E=₹50,000, H=60 → ₹833/hr.

This is your **floor** — never work below it on "Complete/Premium" work.

## Step 3: Calculate Your Setup Fee

```
Setup Fee = (Total hours × your rate)
          + (Direct costs × markup)
          + (Profit/buffer)
```

- Direct costs = pendrive(s) ₹/$, packaging, travel.
- Add a **30–50% buffer** for surprises (customizations always take longer).
- Add **profit** on top so you're not just breaking even.

## Step 4: Set Your Annual License

**Annual License ≈ 20–40% of the Setup Fee** (or based on value delivered).
- Starter year-1 license often included to lower friction.
- Renewal must be **high enough to justify support** but **low enough to renew easily**.

## Validate Prices Against Market (Part 01/03)

- Compare with competitor prices (Part 01) — are you within reason?
- Compare with customer research (Part 03) — are you inside their tolerated range?

## Sanity Checks

- [ ] Setup fee covers your hours + costs + profit (not just costs).
- [ ] You could deliver 2–3 of these per month without burnout.
- [ ] Annual license is worth emailing customers to renew next year.
- [ ] Prices are not so high you have zero buyers, nor so low you lose money.

## Raising Prices Rule

Once you have **5 happy proof points/testimonials**, raise prices 15–25%. Repeat every few months. Existing customers keep old price (grandfather) or renew at new — your call, but always honor commitments.
