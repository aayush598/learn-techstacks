# 01 — Collecting Payments (Cash Flow Is Survival)

Getting paid reliably and *upfront* is **the** difference between thriving and failing. This is a discipline, not an afterthought.

## The Golden Rule

> **Never start work without a deposit. Never hand over the final product without full (or agreed balance) payment.**

## Recommended Payment Terms

| Milestone | % to collect |
|-----------|--------------|
| On signing agreement | 50% (minimum) deposit |
| On delivery (before handing pendrive) | Remaining 50% (or 40% + small on use) |
| Premium/large jobs | 30/40/30 (signature / mid / delivery) |

> For repeat/trusted customers you may do 50/50 but still always get a deposit.

## Payment Methods You MUST Accept (India-first, add local)

1. **UPI** (fastest, most common) — scan code.
2. **Cash** (many shop owners prefer cash) — issue receipt.
3. **Bank transfer/NEFT/IMPS** — for larger amounts.
4. **Cheque** (with caution — wait to clear).
5. **Card/tap** (optional, via a UPI QR that also takes cards: e.g., business QR).

For international/global customers: **PayPal, Stripe, Wise, bank transfer** as applicable to your region.

## The Payment Conversation (be direct & comfortable)

- State price clearly and confidently. Don't sound apologetic.
- Get **written agreement** (Part 14 template) with price + payment terms + scope.
- Collect deposit **at signing**, in front of them.
- Send a simple **invoice/receipt** (Part 14 template) — makes it official and professional.

## Collecting the Balance (from the deposit)

- Agree the balance-date at signing.
- A few days before delivery, remind: "Ready to deliver [date]. Balance is ₹____ — will you have it ready?"
- Deliver **only after** balance is received (or an agreed payment plan you trust).
- For larger balances, a short **post-dated payment plan** is fine — but only with a signed agreement.

## Late Payment Handling (firm but kind)

1. Send a friendly reminder at due date.
2. Follow up in 3–5 days.
3. Escalate politely with clear terms ("I need X by Y to continue").
4. As a last resort, pause support/updates (per your agreement) until paid.
5. Keep records of all payment promises (Part 14).

## Tracking Money (simplify)

Use a simple spreadsheet (Part 14 template):
- Customer name, total price, deposit received, balance, balance date, renewals due.

**Rule:** Update it the moment money comes in. Zero "I think they paid."

## Deposit = Filter

Collected deposits **filter time-wasters** and **commit serious buyers**. If a person refuses to pay a small deposit, they were unlikely to be a profitable customer anyway.
