# 01 — Pricing Strategy: The Principles That Make You Money Fast

Pricing is the #1 lever for "fastest money." These principles are battle-tested for a solo service product business.

## Principle 1: Charge for the SETUP, Not Just the Product

Your time is your asset. The **setup/customization fee** is what funds your life. Never sell the platform "for free" hoping for future money — charge an upfront setup fee that reflects your work.

## Principle 2: Use "Setup Fee + Small Annual License" (RECOMMENDED)

Why this model is ideal for solo + fastest cash:
- **Setup fee = instant cash** when you need it (funds you now).
- **Annual license = recurring income** that grows without new sales.
- **Low annual = easy "yes"** vs SaaS's big recurring bill.
- **Low risk** for the customer → easier to sell.

Alternative models (know them, don't default):
- Pure one-time sale → no recurring income (you re-sell forever).
- Pure SaaS subscription → harder to sell to offline/cost-conscious customers; more delivery burden.
- Pure hourly/custom-dev → slow, low margin, not scalable.

## Principle 3: Price on VALUE, Not on Hours

Charge for the **outcome** (saved time, fewer mistakes, clear profit) — not just your effort. A customer who loses ₹10k/month to stock mistakes will happily pay ₹8k once + ₹3k/yr to fix it.

## Principle 4: The "3-Pack" Anchoring

Always show **3 prices** (Starter / Complete / Premium). Most customers pick the **middle** one — that's by design. Never show a single price.

## Principle 5: Collect Money Upfront

- **50% (or a minimum deposit) before you start** the customization.
- **Balance before/on delivery**.
- Never do the full work before being paid.

## Principle 6: Raise Prices Over Time (don't stay cheap)

Start slightly lower to win first testimonials, then **raise prices** every 3–6 months as you get proof/expertise. Your first customers got a "launch discount"; new ones pay full price.

## Principle 7: Small Add-Ons, Big Margin

Add-ons (training, migration, spare pendrive, priority support) are high-margin and easy to upsell. Always mention 1–2 relevant add-ons.

## Principle 8: Bundles Over Freebies

Never give things free forever. Bundle support/backup into a paid Premium plan so you're compensated for your ongoing time.

## Your Pricing Mindset

- You are a **problem-solver with a customized product**, not a low-cost commodity.
- Discounting is a **scarcity tool** ("launch offer"), not a habit.
- The customer who undervalues you wastes your time — price filters serious buyers.
