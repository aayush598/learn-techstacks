# 01 — The Annual License & Recurring Revenue System

Recurring revenue is what turns this from "odd jobs" into a **real business**. Build this system from day one.

## Why Recurring Revenue Matters

- Predictable income without finding new customers every month.
- Compounds: 30 customers × renewal = real baseline.
- Makes your business **valuable/sellable** later (Part 13).
- Reduces stress: you're not always hunting for the next sale.

## What the Annual License Covers (be clear, avoid disputes)

Recommended scope for the annual license:
- **Support** (phone/WhatsApp) — reasonable help using the software.
- **Bug fixes** — fixes to any issue found in your software.
- **Minor updates** — small feature/version improvements.
- **Backup assistance** (or included backup service).
- Renewed for **another year of license validity** (like a subscription-maintenance).

NOT included (these are paid add-ons): major new modules, on-site visits, large data migration, extensive new customization.

## Pricing the Annual License

- Rule of thumb: **20–40% of setup fee**, or a flat local amount (₹3,000–15,000 / $45–200 depending on plan).
- Offer **annual prepay discount** to encourage one-shot renewal.
- Some plans bundle support+backup into a higher license (Premium).

## The Renewal Calendar (automate reminders)

Build a simple calendar (spreadsheet + reminders) of **all renewals due**:

| Month | Renewal due | Customer | Amount | Status |
|-------|-------------|----------|--------|--------|
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |

**Renewal reminder cadence:**
- **60 days before** — notify "your license renews soon."
- **30 days before** — remind + offer prepay discount.
- **Due date** — invoice.
- **15–30 days after** — follow up.

> See Part 10 for the full customer-retention/renewal playbook and Part 14 for templates.

## Boosting Renewal Rate (target ≥ 80%)

1. **Deliver value all year** — don't disappear between sales. Send a yearly check-in.
2. **Keep data safe** — remind them you hold backup (give them a reason to stay).
3. **Send a "year in review"** (X invoices, Y customers, Z saved) — shows the value.
4. **Add new features** occasionally — renewals feel worth it.
5. **Prepay discount** — reward early payment.

## Add-On Recurring Services (extra recurring income)

- **Backup service (annual)** — you keep secure offsite backups.
- **Priority support (annual)** — faster/on-site priority.
- **Auto-renewal** — with consent, charge & renew automatically for zero-friction income.

| 6 | 8 | ~₹32k / $430 |

*(Recurring is on TOP of one-time setup fees.)*

## Make Recurring Automatic & Effortless

- Use one **renewal tracker** and set reminders (Part 14).
- Standardize license terms in your agreement template.
- Have a **simple one-click renewal** (pay again via UPI).
