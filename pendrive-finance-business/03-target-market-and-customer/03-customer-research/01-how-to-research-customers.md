# 01 — How To Research Your Customers (Low-Cost, Fast)

You don't need expensive market research. You need **real conversations with real businesses**. Do this in your first weeks.

## Method 1: 20 Walk-In Interviews (Do this first)

Walk into 20 businesses matching your niche in your area. Be friendly and respectful.

**Opening script (adapt):**
> "Hi, I'm [Name]. I build finance software for [niche] shops. I'm doing a quick 5-minute chat to understand how shops like yours track sales and stock, so I can serve them better. No obligation. Can I ask you a couple of questions?"

**Ask these 5 questions:**
1. How do you currently track your sales, stock, and money? (paper / Excel / app / nothing)
2. What's the most annoying or time-consuming part of that today?
3. Have you tried any software? What did you like/dislike? Why did you stop (if any)?
4. If software could do everything offline and was customized to your shop, what would you want most?
5. How much would you be willing to pay to fix this problem (one-time + small yearly)?

**Record:** note answers anonymously. Look for repeated pains and price signals.

## Method 2: Survey via WhatsApp/Telegram Groups

- Join 3–5 local business/shopkeeper groups (validated by admin).
- Post a short 3-question poll (respect group rules):
  - "How do you track sales/stock? (poll) Paper / Excel / App / Nothing"
  - "What's your biggest billing pain? (poll)"
  - "Would you pay once + small yearly for offline software that fits your shop? Yes / No / Maybe"

## Method 3: Observe Competitors & Their Reviews

- Look at 5–10 local competitors (billing apps). Read their **negative reviews** — these reveal pain & unmet needs.
- Look at **positive reviews** — reveal what buyers value.

## Method 4: Secondary Research (web)

- Search "[your niche] billing software complaints 2026", "small business data loss cloud", "GST filing small shop problems".
- Collect 5 buyer objections and 5 motivations to add to your notes.

## Method 5: Hypothetical "Pre-Sell" Test (highest value)

Before building the full product:
1. Tell 5 businesses about your **Starter offer** (Part 04).
2. Ask "if I offered this for ₹[price], would you buy this month?"
3. **Collect 3+ "yes" with a small booking deposit** before you build the template.

> This is the ultimate validation: real money = real demand. If no one pays a deposit, don't build yet — refine the offer/niche.

## What To Record After Research

- Top 3 pains (with quotes).
- Top 3 desired outcomes.
- Price range people tolerate.
- Objections (so you can prepare answers).
- 5 warm leads (name + contact) for your first sales (Part 05).

## Research Output = Your "Pain Board"

Write on a board/notebook:
> "My customer's #1 pain is ________. They'd pay ₹____ to fix it. They care about [cost/offline/ownership/simplicity]. Their #1 objection is ______."
