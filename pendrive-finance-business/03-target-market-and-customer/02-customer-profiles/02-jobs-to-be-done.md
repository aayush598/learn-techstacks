# 02 — Jobs-To-Be-Done (What Customers "Hire" Software For)

Stop thinking in features. Think in **jobs** your customer needs done. This reframes your pitch.

## The 8 Core "Jobs" Your Product Does For Them

| Job | Plain-Language Need | Your Feature That Does It |
|-----|---------------------|---------------------------|
| **Bill fast & right** | Not lose money to billing mistakes or slow queues | POS/billing + GST invoice printing |
| **Know their stock** | Avoid running out / over-buying / theft | Inventory + reorder alerts |
| **Know their profit** | No clue if they're actually making money | Sales/purchase reports, P&L |
| **Track who owes them** | Customers/suppliers on credit | Customer/vendor ledgers |
| **Stay tax-clean** | GST/returns without panic | Tax module + reports |
| **Manage staff** | Payroll, attendance, who sells what | Payroll + user permissions |
| **Keep records safe** | Not lose the notebook / not risk data | Encrypted storage + backup |
| **Run offline** | Internet is unreliable in their area | Fully offline runtime |

## Reframe Your Pitch Around Jobs (not features)

**Weak pitch (features):** "I have inventory, billing, GST, reports, and payroll."
**Strong pitch (jobs):** "Never lose money to billing mistakes, always know your stock, and finally see your real profit — all on a pen drive that works even when the internet is down."

> Rule: Talk about the **outcome** (job done), not the **tool**.

## "When…" Scenarios (trigger moments)

Use these to open conversations:
- **"When tax season comes…"** → they panic about records → you offer clean reports.
- **"When a customer argues about a bill…"** → you offer a printed, accurate invoice.
- **"When stock runs out at rush hour…"** → you offer reorder alerts.
- **"When they wonder if they're profitable…"** → you offer a one-click profit report.

Each is a doorway into a demo.
