# 01 — Buyer Personas (Who You're Selling To)

A persona is a detailed description of your ideal customer. It sharpens every sales and marketing decision. Fill in with your real local data after research (Part 03).

## Persona 1: "The Busy Shop Owner" (RETAIL — PRIMARY)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Who | Owner-manager of a kirana/grocery/general store |
| Age | 35–60 |
| Tech comfort | Low–medium; uses a phone for WhatsApp, may use Excel or a notebook |
| Current system | Notebook/register, maybe a cheap billing app |
| Main pain | Stock miscalculation, cash mistakes, slow billing, no clear profit picture, tax India/GST stress |
| Wants | Covers billing, stock, and profit; fast; easy; cheap over the long run |
| Buying fear | "It's too technical," "It'll be expensive," "What if I lose my data?" |
| Triggers to buy | Live demo on their shop, tax-season pain, a trusted referral, low risk guarantee |
| Where to reach | WhatsApp, walk-in the shop, local shopkeepers' groups, referrals |
| Money | Will pay for saving time & mistakes; price-sensitive but values ownership |

## Persona 2: "The Growing Service Owner" (SALON/CLINIC/COACHING — SECONDARY)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Who | Owner of a salon, small clinic, or coaching center |
| Age | 28–50 |
| Tech comfort | Medium |
| Current | Manual appointments/registers, maybe a simple app |
| Pain | No appointment/fee tracking, missed payments, staff payroll, no reports |
| Wants | Bookings/billing, staff & fee tracking, simple reports, low cost |
| Buying fear | Setup disruption, complexity |
| Triggers | Save admin time, referrals, live demo |
| Money | Will pay for time saved and professionalism |

## Persona 3: "The Accountancy/Professional" (LATER)

- CA/accountant or firm manager
- Needs ledgers, GST, reports, client files
- Higher budget, more demanding — aim for this only after you've proven delivery in Tier 1.
