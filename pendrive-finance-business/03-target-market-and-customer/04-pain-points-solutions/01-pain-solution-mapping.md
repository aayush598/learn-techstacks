# 01 — Pain-Point → Solution Mapping (Your Pitch Cheat Sheet)

Use this table to instantly convert any customer pain into your solution message. Print this and keep it with you when selling.

## Common Pains → Your Messaging

| Customer Pain | Your Solution Message |
|---------------|------------------------|
| "Billing takes too long / mistakes cost me money" | "You get fast, accurate billing with printed invoices — mistakes cut down, lines move fast." |
| "I don't know my real profit" | "A one-click profit & loss report tells you exactly what you earn each day/month." |
| "Stock keeps running out / I over-buy" | "Inventory with reorder alerts tells you when to buy, so you never run out — or over-buy." |
| "Customers on credit forget to pay" | "A customer ledger tracks who owes you, with payment reminders." |
| "Tax season is a nightmare" | "Clean, tax-ready reports and GST invoices so filing is easy, not stressful." |
| "SaaS charges me every month forever" | "You own it on a pen drive. Small setup fee + tiny yearly license — not a lifelong subscription." |
| "I worry about my data / internet goes down" | "Everything is on YOUR pen drive, offline. No cloud, no outage stops you, no one else has your data." |
| "Software is too complicated" | "Built for you and set up by me. I show your family and staff how to use it simply." |
| "I don't trust computers" | "My system is tested every time, backed up, and I'm here to help. Start small/low-risk." |

## The 4-Step Pitch Formula (memorize)

1. **Empathize** — name their pain ("Billing mistakes must be stressful and costly.").
2. **Diagnose** — explain cost of not fixing it ("That adds up across a month/year.").
3. **Offer** — your customized offline solution (jobs, not features).
4. **Reduce risk** — low entry, money-back, support, ownership ("You can try the Starter plan; your data is on the pen drive you own.").

## Objection Handling (their "no" and your response)

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Too expensive" | "Compared to paying ₹X every month, mine is a small one-time setup plus a tiny yearly fee — over 2–3 years you save a lot and you own it." |
| "Too technical for me" | "You don't need to learn anything technical. I set it all up and train you. It's as simple as a phone app." |
| "I'll keep my notebook" | "Of course — but notebooks cost you unknown profit, mistakes, and tax-time stress. Let me show you a 5-minute live demo on a shop like yours." |
| "What if I lose my data?" | "Automatic backup + I keep a secure copy. Your data is safer than a notebook." |
| "I'll think about it" | "Sure. What's the main thing you're unsure about? Let me address it now." |
| "Other software is cheaper" | "They charge monthly and keep your data. Mine is customized to your business and you own it. Over a year, I'm usually cheaper." |
| "No internet, will it work?" | "Yes — that's the point. It's fully offline." |

## The Money-Back / Low-Risk Guarantee

**Recommended guarantee:** "If I don't deliver what we agreed within the timeline, I'll refund your setup fee." This removes the biggest fear and dramatically raises conversions. Protect boundary: only honor for genuine delivery failure, not endless free changes.
