# 01 — Resources & Continuous Learning (Sharpen Your Edge)

A curated list of resources to keep improving as a solo founder. Learn deliberately, but prioritize **action** (Part 15) — learning serves the business, not the other way around.

## This Business's Home

- **Product source:** `~/aayush/projects/pendrive_finance/software/` (PLAN.md, README.md).

## Business / Marketing Learning (free first)

- YouTube: small-business marketing, local SEO, WhatsApp marketing, Indian SMB software demos.
- Google Skillshop (free): digital marketing fundamentals, Google Business.
- Blogs/forums: small-business owner communities, local entrepreneur groups.

## Sales Skills

- Practice the demo + outreach scripts (Part 05/14) on real businesses — **experience beats theory**.
- Record your demos/role-play; review what worked (Part 05).

## Financial Skills

- Basic accounting (Part 07/08) + a free course if needed.
- Your own books are the lab — practice daily (Part 08).

## Platform / Technical Skills

- To deliver well you only need the **Designer** (no-code). Learn the Designer deeply (Part 02/09).
- For modifying the platform itself, follow the technical roadmap (Part 19) and the source docs.
- Communities: Qt/C++ and SQLite resources only if you customize the engine.

## Templates & Libraries (in this resource)

## "Learn, Then Act" Rule (anti-procrastination)

- **80/20:** Spend 80% of time DOING (outreach, delivery), 20% learning.
- Pick **one skill** to improve per month (topic = your current bottleneck).
- Whenever you learn something, **apply it in the next session** — don't just collect.

## Recommended Monthly Rituals

- **Month review:** Part 08 scorecard + weekly cash (Part 12).
- **Quarterly review:** Part 15 ch. 3 + Part 13 scaling decision.
- **Skill improvement:** one topic per month tied to a real gap.

## Final Note On Resources

The most valuable resource is your **own trackers + testimonials + fail/win log**. Trust real data over generic advice. This resource + your real customer results = everything you need.
