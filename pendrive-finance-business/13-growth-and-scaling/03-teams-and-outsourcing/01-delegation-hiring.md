# 01 — Getting Help: Outsourcing & Building a Micro-Team (Scale Past Your Hours)

Solo is the start, not necessarily forever. When your time becomes the bottleneck, add help. Do it smart and low-risk.

## When To Get Help (the triggers)

- ⚠️ You're turning away/waiting on work because you lack time.
- ⚠️ Support is eating hours you should spend on sales.
- ⚠️ Your business is growing but your delivery is slowing.
- ⚠️ You consistently work 60+ hours or burnout signs appear (Part 10/11).

## The Order of Adding Help (cheapest first)

1. **Systems & automation** (Part 14) — replace your repetitive work first (usually enough early on).
2. **Outsourced one-offs** — freelance a specific task (template build, video editing, admin, logo) on Upwork/Fiverr/local.
3. **Part-time assistant (virtual)** — data/admin/support handling; low cost.
4. **Contract specialist** — someone to build templates/modules (if you lack a skill).
5. **Partner/first hire** — a person who can sell AND deliver (when demand justifies salary).

## What To Delegate (and NOT delegate)

**Delegate (low-risk, time-consuming):**
- Data entry / migration prep.
- Basic admin (invoicing, tracking, scheduling).
- Support triage (first response).
- Template pieces / repetitive builds (with your specs).
- Marketing content creation (drafts you approve).

**Keep (core, high-risk):**
- Setting prices & deals.
- Final quality/testing approval.
- Customer relationships & key promises.
- Data/backup security decisions.
- Reputation-critical decisions.

## Managing Help (so it adds value, not chaos)

- **Give clear specs** — templates/checklists (Part 14) so work is repeatable and correct.
- **Test/approve everything** they produce (never ship unverified work).
- **Simple communication** — WhatsApp/Telegram + a shared task list.
- **Pay for results** — agree scope + fee upfront; milestone payments.
- **Protect data** — non-disclosure, no customer data shared unnecessarily.

## Paying Help Affordably (solo-friendly)

- **Pay per task/result**, not salary early on.
- Use **commission/referral** for sales help (they're paid on success).
- Start with **hourly freelance**; scale to part-time only when the numbers justify.

## Building a "Dream/Systems File"

Document your processes (Part 14) so anyone you bring in can follow them without you explaining everything. This is what makes you able to delegate at all.

## The Caution

- Adding help before demand = burned cash. **Help should reduce your bottleneck, not create cost.**
- Grow help **incrementally** and tied to proven revenue.
