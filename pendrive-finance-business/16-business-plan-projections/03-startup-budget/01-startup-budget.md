## "I Have Very Little Money" Starting Kit

You can begin with **just:** a laptop (or borrowing one), a phone with WhatsApp, a couple of pen drives, and your time. Minimum viable starting cost is often under ~₹5–10k / $70–150 (registration + a few drives + small branding). Everything else can be earned and reinvested.

## Where The Money COMES From Early (fund the growth)

1. **First deposit(s)** — your first customer funds the next step.
2. **Founding pre-sale** — first 3 customers prepay, giving cash.
3. **Reinvestment rule** — don't spend profit on non-essentials; reinvest into drives/marketing (Part 08).

## The "Do It For Free First" Option (zero budget)

- Use **free** tools (Part 18): Google Sheets/Drive, WhatsApp Business, Google Business Profile, Canva, free screen recorder.
- Deliver your **first setup to a willing customer** even at a low/founding price, or do 1 free in exchange for testimonial + referral (Part 12).
- Reinvest that first money into better drives + marketing.

## Avoid These Cost Traps (startup)

- ❌ Fancy office / expensive equipment before you earn.
- ❌ Bulk-buying pen drives before you have sales.
- ❌ Expensive ads before proof + testimonials.
- ❌ Paying for expensive branding/website early.
- ❌ High-interest loans to "fund growth" (Part 08/11).

## Your Reinvestment Ladder (as income grows)

1. Better/reliable pendrives + backups (reputation).
2. Marketing (local + small ads, once proof) (Part 06).
3. Longer reach (online/remote channels) (Part 13).
4. Time-saving tools/hardware.
5. First helper if time is the bottleneck (Part 13).
