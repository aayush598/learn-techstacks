## What Is This Business?

You sell **customized, secure, offline-first business finance software** that runs from a USB pendrive — no installation, no internet dependency, no data traces left on host computers, bank-grade AES-256 encryption. Each customer gets their own **customized** finance system (billing, inventory, accounting, GST, payroll, CRM, reports) tailored to *their* business, delivered on a portable device they plug into any Windows computer.

This is a **"SaaS-lite but on-device"** model: you charge a setup/customization fee + annual license, and each pendrive is a recurring, high-margin, low-recurring-cost product.

**Your business model in one line:**
> Build it once (the platform), customize per customer (fast, no-code), deliver on a pendrive, and collect setup fees + annual license renewals.

## The Fastest Money Summary (Do This First)

If you want money **fastest, easiest, and lowest-risk**, follow this loop from Day 1:

1. **Pick ONE niche** (e.g., `retail shops` or `small clinics`) — don't sell to everyone. (Part 03)
2. **Build ONE template** for that niche in the Designer (not the full platform). (Part 09)
3. **Validate by selling** — approach 20 real businesses in your city that week. (Part 05, Part 12)
4. **Sell a "Starter Setup"** for low risk: setup fee + annual license. Get paid upfront. (Part 04)
5. **Deliver fast** using the template + 1–2 days of customization. (Part 09, Part 10)
6. **Get testimonials** and referrals from every happy customer. (Part 10)
7. **Reinvest** profits into marketing and better templates. (Part 08, Part 13)

**First money targets (realistic, solo, offline/online combined):**
- Month 1–3: First 1–3 paying customers (₹15k–₹60k / $200–$800 earned)
- Month 4–6: 5–10 customers, building recurring license revenue
- Month 7–12: 15–40 customers + referral engine running
