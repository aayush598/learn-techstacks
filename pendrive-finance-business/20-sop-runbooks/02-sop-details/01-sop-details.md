# 01 — SOP: Sales & Onboarding Runbook (Lead → Paid Customer)

**Trigger:** When you have a new lead or prospect.
**Goal:** Convert a qualified lead into a signed, deposit-paid customer, smoothly.

## Steps

1. **Log the lead** in the Lead tracker (Part 14) with source + pain + warmth score.
2. **Qualify** (Part 05): real pain? affordability? niche match? If not, keep on nurture list, don't chase.
3. **Outreach** — send the message, follow the multitouch cadence (Part 05/14) until engaged or clearly declined.
4. **Book a demo** (Part 05) — prefer in-person at their business, or video.
5. **Run the 15-min demo** on their niche template (Part 05) focusing on their jobs.
6. **Present the 3-pack proposal** (Part 04/05) with recommendation + price + payment terms.
7. **Handle objections** (Part 03) using the objection table.
8. **Close** — ask for the deposit + signed agreement (Part 05/14). Get **50% upfront**.
9. **Send agreement + invoice** immediately; confirm deposit received (Part 14).
10. **Book the requirements call** (SOP S4) and set the delivery date.

**Done-when:** Signed agreement + deposit received + requirements call booked.
**Escalation:** If lead stalls >2 weeks, move to monthly nurture (Part 05).

## Key Numbers To Hit

- Leads logged: 10–20/week (Part 12).
- Touches before a yes/no: 3–5.
- Close rate on demos: 20–30% healthy.

## SOP: Scoping & Agreement Runbook (S4)

**Trigger:** After deposit/agreement — before building.
**Goal:** Lock scope + expectations + payment.

## Steps

1. Run the **requirements call** (15 questions, Part 09).
2. Fill the **scope sheet** (Part 14): modules, forms, reports, migration, training, exclusions.
3. Estimate **hours → price** (Part 04 calculator).
4. Confirm **price + timeline + payment terms** with the customer.
5. Both **sign the agreement** (Part 14).
6. Add the project to the **Project tracker** (Part 14).

**Done-when:** Closed scope, signed agreement, agreed timeline, deposit logged.

---

## SOP: Delivery & Onboarding Runbook (S6)

**Trigger:** Project is built and passing QA.
**Goal:** Deliver reliably, get paid fully, start support + referrals.

## Steps

1. **Test** the configured app on a clean Windows PC from the pen drive (Part 09 checklist).
2. Create + verify **backup** (Part 09); store a secure copy.
3. **Migrate** data (if paid) + verify import (Part 09).
4. **Train** the customer (1–3 sessions, simple, cheat-sheet — Part 14).
5. **Deliver** the pendrive; collect the **balance** before final handover (Part 04).
6. Hand over: app drive, backup, cheat-sheet, support contact, renewal date (Part 10).
7. **Follow up** in a few days (adoption check).
8. **Ask for testimonial + Google review + 2–3 referrals** (Part 10).
9. Set the **renewal reminder** (Part 04/10).

**Done-when:** Delivered, balance paid, onboarding done, testimonial/referral asked.

---

## SOP: Support Runbook (S7)

**Trigger:** Customer reports an issue.
**Goal:** Resolve fast, keep them happy, renewing, referring.

## Steps

1. Acknowledge within **1 business day** (Part 02 standard).
2. Triage severity: Data/urgent → fix now; Blocking bug → workaround + fix; Minor → schedule.
3. Diagnose (reproduce), then fix (backup-recover if data issue) (Part 11).
4. Verify the fix with the customer.
5. Log in the **support ticket tracker** + learn the root cause (Part 10/19).
6. Update the customer's renewal case (a well-handled support = renew).

**Done-when:** Issue resolved + logged; customer satisfied.

---

## SOP: Renewal Runbook (S8)

**Trigger:** 60 days before an annual license due date (Part 04).
**Goal:** Renew ≥80% of licenses with low effort.

## Steps

1. **60 days:** notify "your license renews soon," highlight value (Part 04/10).
2. **30 days:** remind + offer prepay discount (Part 04).
3. **Due date:** send renewal invoice (Part 14).
4. **Due 15–30 days:** follow up once/twice kindly.
5. **Collect** via UPI/tap; confirm + thank.
6. Log renewal in Customer/Renewal tracker.

**Done-when:** Renewed or clearly declined (then evaluate why — Part 10).

---

## SOP: Referral & Testimonial Runbook (S9)

**Trigger:** After a happy delivery or a satisfied support experience.
**Goal:** Turn every happy customer into referrals + proof.

## Steps

1. Ask for **2–3 names** of owners with the same pain (Part 10).
2. Offer a **simple forwardable intro** (Part 14).
3. **Reward** the referrer when the referral buys (Part 04/10).
4. Ask for a **testimonial + Google review** (Part 06 + Part 14).
5. Log in the Referral/Testimonial tracker.

**Never:** fabricate testimonials, or pressure.

**Done-when:** Names gathered + rewarded when converted; testimonial requested.

---

## SOP: Money & Admin Runbook (S10)

**Trigger:** Weekly + monthly.
**Goal:** Cash flow healthy, books clean, tax set aside.

## Steps

1. **Weekly:** update Cash Flow tracker, send invoices due, log payments (Part 08/14).
2. **Monthly:** reconcile bank/UPI vs records; review scorecard (Part 08); move tax + savings set-asides (Part 07/08); review pipeline numbers (Part 12).
3. **Quarterly:** full review (Part 15 ch. 3), update projections (Part 16), review SOPs.

**Done-when:** Books current, tax aside, cash position known.
