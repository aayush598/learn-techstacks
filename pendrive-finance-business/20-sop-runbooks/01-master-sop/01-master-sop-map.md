## SOP Format (use consistently)

Every detailed SOP follows this template:
1. **Trigger** — when to run it.
2. **Goal** — the outcome.
3. **Steps** — numbered, copy-paste actionable.
4. **Tool/checklist** — which tracker/template to use.
5. **Done-when** — exit criteria.
6. **Escalation** — what to do if something fails.

## How SOPs Make You Money & Lower Risk

- **Consistency** → every customer gets the same great experience → referrals (Part 10).
- **Speed** → you don't re-decide each time → deliver faster (Part 09).
- **Delegation** → hand an SOP to a helper and it works (Part 13).
- **Reputation** → fewer mistakes → trust (Part 11).

## SOP Library Growth Habit

- Whenever you do something important twice, **write it as an SOP** (10 min).
- Keep all SOPs in one folder, cross-referenced to relevant parts.
- **Review quarterly** (Part 15 ch. 3) and update with learnings.
