## Business Model Canvas

| Block | Your Answer (fill this in / recommended default) |
|-------|---------------------------------------------------|
| **1. Customer Segments** | Small & micro businesses: retail shops, clinics, pharmacies, restaurants, salons, CA/accounts firms, coaching institutes, service businesses. Start with ONE niche (Part 03). |
| **2. Value Propositions** | Customized, secure, offline-first finance software on a USB pendrive. One-time setup + low annual fee. No monthly SaaS bleed. Data stays with the customer. Fully tailored to how they work. |
| **3. Channels** | Direct outreach (walk-ins, WhatsApp, calls), referrals, local business partnerships, Google Business Profile, local SEO, YouTube/WhatsApp demo videos. (Part 05, Part 06) |
| **4. Customer Relationships** | High-touch personalized onboarding (solo = your edge), annual license renewal, support via WhatsApp/phone, referral rewards. |
| **5. Revenue Streams** | (1) Setup/Customization fee (one-time upfront), (2) Annual license/renewal, (3) Add-ons (extra modules, report packs, training), (4) Optional hardware markup on pendrives. (Part 04) |
| **6. Key Resources** | The Pendrive Finance Platform, your time, one laptop, USB pendrives, client data/templates, your reputation & testimonials. |
| **7. Key Activities** | Product customization (Designer), sales & outreach, delivery & installation, support & renewals, building niche templates. |
| **8. Key Partnerships** | Computer/IT shops (referrals), CA firms / accountants (referrals), stationery/office suppliers, business associations/chambers of commerce, WhatsApp group admin networks. |
| **9. Cost Structure** | Pendrive hardware, your time, marketing (nearly zero initially), business registration fees, software tools, minor travel costs. Low fixed costs = low risk. |
| **10. Risk Factors** | Time investment before first sale, customer churn at renewal, technical bugs, data-privacy liability, competitor SaaS prices. (Mitigated in Part 11.) |
