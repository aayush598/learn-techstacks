# 03 — SWOT Analysis + Key Strategic Assumptions

Understand your Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats — then confirm the assumptions this business relies on.

## Strengths (internal, help you)

- **Offline-first, portable** product — unique vs cloud SaaS (which fails when offline / when the vendor stops paying hosting).
- **Customization** — you tailor to each business; giant SaaS can't.
- **Affordability** — one-time + low annual fee beats ₹5k–20k/month SaaS.
- **No recurring heavy infra cost** — data is on the customer's pendrive, not your server.
- **Solo = high-touch, fast, personal** service.

## Weaknesses (internal, hold you back)

- **Solo bandwidth** — limited capacity to serve many customers at once.
- **No big brand recognition** yet.
- **Product may still be in development** (see Part 09 for a fast-track delivery plan).
- **Renewal collection** depends on you staying in touch with customers.

## Opportunities (external, help you)

- **Huge underserviced market**: millions of small shops/clinics/firms that use paper or Excel because SaaS is too expensive/confusing.
- **Subscription fatigue** — businesses are actively looking to cut recurring costs.
- **Data-privacy awareness** — businesses wary of cloud after data leaks and outages.
- **Niche verticals** with no good cheap software (some trades, local services).
- **Repeat revenue** via annual license renewals and add-ons.

## Threats (external, hold you back)

- **Cheap SaaS alternatives** (Free/₹299-mo apps) — counter with "data is yours, offline, customized."
- **Platform bugs** could hurt reputation if not tested (mitigate with testing, Part 09).
- **Data-loss liability** if a pendrive fails (mitigate with auto backup, Part 09/10).
- **Competitors copying** your niche playbooks (mitigate with speed + relationships).
- **Economic slowdown** reducing small-business spending (mitigate with low-price starter plan).

## Key Strategic Assumptions (confirm these are true)

1. There is a large population of small businesses **not using good software** because of cost or complexity. → Verify in Part 03.
2. Businesses value **"data is mine, offline, secure"** enough to pay. → Test in Part 12 validation calls.
3. You can **customize fast enough** to deliver profitably. → Prove in Part 09.
4. Annual license **renewals** will provide growing recurring revenue. → Build renewal system in Part 10.

> Technique: Don't bet the farm on any single assumption. Mitigate by starting small (one niche, low-cost starter plan) per Part 12.
