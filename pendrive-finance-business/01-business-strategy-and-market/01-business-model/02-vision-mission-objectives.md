# 02 — Business Vision, Mission & Objectives

Your strategic foundation. Short, clear, and easy to remember.

## Vision (where you're going)

> To become the most trusted provider of **affordable, customized, offline, secure business software** for small and micro enterprises — delivered with zero subscription burden.

## Mission (what you do every day)

> Help every small business afford and use software that is **customized to them, secure, and completely theirs** — on a device they own.

## Your Core Values (how you behave)

1. **Customer-first** — every business is unique; default templates, but never "one-size-fits-all" service.
2. **Trust & Security** — protect customer data as if it were your own.
3. **Speed** — deliver fast; respect the customer's time and money.
4. **Simplicity** — make software feel easy even for non-technical owners.
5. **Honesty** — never oversell; only promise what you can deliver.

## Why a Personal Mission Matters for a Solo Business

- Your **reputation is your brand**. Every interaction builds or hurts trust.
- **Speed + honesty** = referrals, which are the cheapest and highest-converting source of revenue for a solo founder.
- You cannot out-advertise bigger SaaS — so you **out-care and out-customize**.
