# 03 — Competitor & Pricing Landscape

## Who Are Your Competitors?

| Competitor Type | Examples | Their Weakness You Exploit |
|-----------------|----------|------------------------------|
| Cheap retail POS apps (free/₹299-mo) | Generic billing apps | Not customized; recurring cost; no data ownership; weak reporting; generic UX |
| Mid-scale SaaS ERPs | Large cloud ERPs | Expensive (₹15k+/mo); complex; internet-dependent; not local |
| Big-brand accounting (Tally, etc.) | Tally, QuickBooks | Recurring/costly licensing; complex; not personalized to niche |
| Excel/paper (the "do nothing" option) | Manual registers | Error-prone, slow, no reports, no security — your cheapest-to-beat competitor |
| Local IT shops reselling software | Small tech vendors | Often poor support, outdated tech, template-only (not truly customized) |

## Your Unique Position (The "Blue Ocean" Angle)

You are NOT fighting on price or features alone. Your differentiators:

1. **Truly customized** — built around the exact business, not a config template.
2. **Offline + portable + local data** — the "data is yours" message.
3. **Low total cost** — one-time setup + small annual license vs endless subscription.
4. **Human, local, high-touch support** — a real person who answers.

**Message that beats every competitor:**
> "Other software charges you forever and keeps your data on their server. Mine lives on YOUR pen drive, is built for YOUR business, works offline, and costs a fraction in the long run."

## How Competitors Price (So You Can Position)

| Competitor | Price Model | Approx Cost |
|------------|-------------|-------------|
| Generic retail billing app | Monthly/Yearly subscription | ₹2,000–6,000/year or ₹299–999/mo |
| Mid SaaS ERP | Monthly per feature | ₹30k–2 lakh/year |
| Tally / accounting suites | Perpetual + AMC | ₹18k–50k one-time + annual AMC |
| Cheap apps | Freemium/ad-based | Free but ad-heavy, no ownership |

## Your Pricing Strategy vs Competition (Summary)

You position as **"premium-custom, low-total-cost"**:
- Charge a **setup/customization fee** that funds your time (Part 04).
- Keep the **annual license low** (much lower than SaaS) to win the "save money" story.
- Sell **add-ons** for extra revenue.

> Rule: Do NOT compete on being cheapest. Compete on **ownership + customization + offline + support**. The setup fee separates serious customers from time-wasters.

## Positioning Statement (memorize & repeat)

> **"For [target/business type] who want software that actually fits and keeps data safe, we provide [customized offline finance software on a USB drive]. Unlike [cheap subscriptions/generic ERPs], our solution is [fully yours, offline, personalized, low long-term cost]."**

## Watch-Your-Back Notes

- If a **big free app** dominates your niche, don't fight free — emphasize **ownership, offline, customization, and reports a free app can't give**.
- If competitors **drop prices**, don't panic — your customization + data-ownership story holds.
- Keep **collecting testimonials** — social proof is your best defense against price cutters.
