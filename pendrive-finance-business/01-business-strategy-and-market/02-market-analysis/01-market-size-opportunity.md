# 01 — Market Size & Opportunity Assessment

## The Big Picture (2026 Context)

The "small and micro business software" market is enormous and dramatically **underserviced**:

- The vast majority of retail shops, clinics, pharmacies, restaurants, salons, cafés, coaching centers, and service firms worldwide still run on **paper registers, notebooks, or Excel**.
- Big cloud SaaS products target mid-size and larger businesses. Their pricing and complexity put off micro-businesses.
- There is rising **subscription fatigue** and **cloud/data-privacy distrust** after high-profile outages and data breaches.

### Why "Pendrive/Offline" Is a Genuine Advantage (Not Just a Gimmick)

| Aspect | Cloud SaaS | Your Pendrive Product |
|--------|-----------|------------------------|
| Recurring cost | ₹5k–20k/month forever | Setup fee + low annual fee |
| Works offline (no internet) | ✗ Mostly requires internet | ✓ Fully offline |
| Data location | On someone else's server | On the customer's USB drive |
| Customization | Limited / config-level | Fully customized to customer |
| Data ownership | Vendor/platform | Customer owns it |
| Tech skill needed | Moderate | Very low / visual |

**Segment most likely to buy:** businesses that are (a) non-technical, (b) cost-conscious, (c) worried about data, and (d) currently using paper/Excel.

## Sizing The Opportunity (Ballpark)

Do your own local count — this is the most reliable number:

```
Your local opportunity = (Number of target businesses in your city/area)
                      × (Estimated revenue per customer per year)
```

**Example (illustrative):**
- City area with ~2,000 small businesses matching your niche.
- Only 5% might ever buy (*realistic* with effort) = 100 potential customers.
- At ₹8,000 first-year revenue each = **₹8,00,000** addressable potential in just your local area.
- Plus online/remote customers across the country/world = multiply further.

**Renewal leverage:** even 50 active customers at ₹3,000 renewal = ₹1.5 lakh/year recurring *before* any new sales. Recurring revenue compounds your earnings.

## Assess the 3 Ts (Total, Target, Obtainable)

| Level | Definition | Your estimate |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| **Total Addressable** | Everyone who could use finance software | Millions |
| **Target (serviceable)** | Businesses in your niche + region you can realistically reach | ~____ |
| **Obtainable (SOM)** | Those you can realistically win & serve solo | ~____ (start with 1–3) |

> For a solo founder, focus 100% on **Obtainable** — win 1–3 customers, then grow the channel.

## Proceed/Don't-Proceed Checklist

Check ALL before investing serious time:

- [ ] At least 10 businesses in your area already spend money on billing/inventory software OR say the pain is real.
- [ ] At least 5 businesses said they'd consider going offline/USB if it saved money & kept data local.
- [ ] You can build one niche template in ≤ a few weeks (Part 09).
- [ ] You're comfortable doing face-to-face + WhatsApp sales (Part 05).
- [ ] You have a low-cost starter offer (Part 04) to reduce buyer risk.

If all are true → **proceed**. If unsure → spend a few days on Part 03 research before anything else.
