# 01 — Digital Marketing Blueprint (Near-Zero Budget, High Local Impact)

As a solo starter, spend **almost nothing** on ads. Use free, high-ROI local channels first. This is about consistency, not budget.

## Channel Priority (for fastest local money)

### 1. Google Business Profile (MUST — free & powerful)
- Create it, verify, complete profile (services, photos, hours, offers).
- Choose keywords: "billing software near me", "inventory software for shops", "GST billing software".
- Post updates/offers occasionally.
- Politely ask customers for **5-star Google reviews** (huge for local trust).

### 2. WhatsApp Business (MUST)
- Set up **catalog** with your 3 offers + add-ons + prices.
- Use **WhatsApp Status daily** (30-sec demo clip, tip, testimonial).
- Create/join local business groups (get rep approval; be helpful).

### 3. Social Media (Instagram/Facebook) — local + niche
- Post 2–3x/week: 30-sec demo videos, before/after ("Notebook → app"), client success, tips.
- Use local hashtags + location tags.
- Comment helpfully on local business pages (not spam).
- Facebook local business groups.

### 4. Local Directories (free listings)
- JustDial, IndiaMART, Sulekha, OLX classifieds, local directories.
- Consistent NAP (Name/Address/Phone).

### 5. YouTube (organic long-term)
- Record simple demo/tutorial videos (phone is fine).
- Titles like: "How a [niche] shop manages billing & stock offline on a pen drive".
- Embed/share via WhatsApp to warm leads.

### 6. Referral Engine (Part 10) — the real growth driver
Your best "channel" is happy customers + partners referring you.

## Paid Ads (ONLY later — after proof)

Once you have testimonials + a proven offer (month 3+), try **small local ads**:
- Facebook/Instagram local ads targeting your niche + city (₹100–300/day).
- Google local services ads.
- Set a **hard monthly budget cap** (e.g., ₹2–5k / $30–70) and track leads.
- Never outspend your earnings.

## The "Local SEO" Fast Win

People search "billing software [your area]". Make sure:
- Google Business Profile says [your area] + services.
- Your website (if any, even a simple one-page) has [your area] keywords.
- You have some Google reviews with these words.

## Content Calendar (simple, 1 hour/week)

| Day | Content |
|-----|---------|
| Mon | Tip post (e.g., "3 signs your shop is losing money silently") |
| Wed | 30-sec demo clip |
| Fri | Testimonial / result story |
| Weekend | Offer / demo invite |

## Metrics To Watch (not vanity — money metrics)

- Leads generated per week (from marketing).
- Demos booked.
- Close rate.
- Cost per lead (should be ≈ ₹0 for organic).

> Rule: If a channel gives **zero leads in 4 weeks**, change the message or drop it. Double down on what works.

## Never Do

- ❌ Spamming groups (gets you banned + bad reputation).
- ❌ Buying fake followers/reviews.
- ❌ Over-promising in ads.
