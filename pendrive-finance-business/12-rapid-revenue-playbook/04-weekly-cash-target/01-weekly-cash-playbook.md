# 01 — The Weekly Cash Target Playbook (Make Money a Habit)

Put a **number on your week** and hit it. This turns vague effort into a money-making machine.

## Set Your Weekly Cash Target

**Weekly target = (your monthly living + monthly break-even) ÷ 4.**

Example: monthly need ₹40k → weekly need ≈ ₹10k (≈ 1 Complete setup, or 1 deposit + a renewal + add-ons).

**My weekly cash target = ₹/$ ____.**

> Even in slow weeks, aim for **at least one deposit** — momentum + cash both matter.

## The Weekly Revenue Equation (what produces the money)

```
Deposits/Sales = Leads contacted × conversion
```

To hit your weekly number, you need a known number of touches:
- **Touches needed ≈ Target ÷ (avg deal × close rate).**
- Example: avg deal ₹8k deposit, close 20% → you need ~6 qualified leads/week → ~20–30 touches/week.

## Your Week-by-Week Cash Plan

| Day | Cash-Making Actions |
|-----|---------------------|
| Mon | 20 outreach messages / walk-ins + follow-ups (Part 05) |
| Tue | Delivery/build (turn signed work into cash) |
| Wed | Demos booked + run → close (Part 05) |
| Thu | Renewal/prepay push + follow-ups (Part 10) |
| Fri | Invoices sent, deposits tracked, cash-flow update (Part 08) |

## Chasing the Number Without Burnout

- **Leading indicator** = touches/week (in your control). Hits = outcome.
- If you're short, **increase touches** (more messages/demos), not panic.
- **Quality wins over volume** — warm leads and referrals convert best (Part 05/10).

## Weekly Cash Review (Friday, 15 min)

- [ ] Money in this week: ____
- [ ] Money out: ____
- [ ] Net: ____
- [ ] Deposits booked for next week: ____
- [ ] What worked? Do more of it.
- [ ] What didn't? Change/stop.

## Monthly Cash Milestone Habit

Each month, review against Part 08 metrics:
- Revenue in → up/down?
- Renewals due → collected?
- Recurring stack → growing?
- Adjust next month's weekly target.

## The "Always Booked" Anti-Lull Habit

- **Never finish a week with an empty pipeline.** Always have next week's demos/deposits booked.
- If pipeline's empty → the problem is **touches**, fix it now (more outreach).

## Quick Reminder (your honest fast-path)

- **Fastest:** warm/first sale + founding pre-sale (Part 12 route A/D).
- **Easy:** local niche + referral (Part 05/06/10).
- **Low-risk:** deposit-first + buffers + low burn (Part 04/08/11).

> Money is a **numbers game you control.** Weekly targets + daily touches = results.
