# 01 — Quick Cash Tactics (Every Low-Risk Way to Get Paid This Month)

A toolkit of fast revenue moves — pick the ones that fit your situation.

## Tactic 1: The "Founding Pre-Sale" (cash now, goodwill forever)
Offer your **first 3–5 customers** a founding price with a **lifetime price lock** as long as they stay. Creates urgency + cash now + loyal references (Part 04).

## Tactic 2: Annual-Prepay Discount Push
Offer existing/renewing customers a discount if they **pay the full annual license now** instead of monthly. Pulls cash forward (Part 04/10).

## Tactic 3: "Data Rescue / Migration" Bundle
For businesses drowning in Excel/notebook: charge a fee to **clean, structure, and import** their data into software (Part 12 route B). Easy cash, huge value.

## Tactic 4: Invoice-Format / Report Services (quick add-ons)
Businesses want **professional GST invoices + GST-ready reports** (tax season). Offer invoice design + tax-report setup as a quick paid add-on (Parts 02/06).

## Tactic 5: Referral Bonanza (activate your network this week)
Message all warm contacts: "I'm setting up small businesses with offline finance software — do you or anyone you know need better billing/stock/profit tracking? I'll help referrals too." Plant the seed everywhere (Part 05/10).

## Tactic 6: Local Workshop / Demo Event (small paid sessions)
Host a **paid 1-day workshop** or a **free demo + signup** for local niche owners (rent a small hall or do it online): "Give me one morning, I'll show you how to stop losing money to billing/stock — and get a free look at software built for your business." Collect signups, then close setups. (Starter version = fast cash if you charge.)

## Tactic 7: Become the "Go-To Local Expert" (free lead magnet)
Post daily 30-sec demos + tips (Part 06). People will start asking "how do I get this?" → your demo funnel fills your pipeline (Part 05).

## Tactic 8: "No-Risk Pilot" (risk reversal to get first sales)
"Try it for a month. If it's not working, I'll refund your setup fee and you keep training." This removes fear and converts the hesitant (Part 03/04). Only if you're confident in delivery.

## Tactic 9: Bundle With Tax Season / Year-End (seasonal spike)
Around tax/return time, offer a **"tax-season readiness" package** (clean books, GST reports, invoices). Seasonal targeting = timing beats effort.

## Tactic 10: Upsell Add-Ons On Every Delivery
Always mention 1–2 relevant add-ons (migration, spare pendrive, extra training, priority support). Small add-ons raise each order's value (Part 02/04).

## "Do-After-You-Have-Proof" Cash Routes (month 2+)
- **Online/remote customers** via freelance platforms (Upwork/Fiverr/IndiaMART) for offline desktop accounting software — sells beyond your city (Part 13).
- **Template packs / digital product** — sell pre-built niche templates (Part 13) for passive-ish income.
- **Referral/affiliate network** with CAs and IT shops (Part 10) — recurring partner income.

## Rules For Quick Cash
- **Don't discount to everyone** — use founding/prepay scarcity, not habit (Part 04).
- **Deposit first** on any setup work.
- **Focus** — pick 2–3 tactics and do them hard this month, not all half-heartedly.

## Weekly Cash Check (Part 04/08 rhythm)
- Weekly aim: book at least 1 deposit/sale.
- Track: leads → demos → proposals → deposits (Part 05 pipeline).
