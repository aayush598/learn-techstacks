# Astrophysics — Photon Ring Signatures Interview Questions and Answers

## Q1: What is the photon ring?
**A:** The sharp, magnified image of the black hole's unstable photon sphere - a thin annulus just outside the shadow boundary produced by photon orbits that wind around the hole.

## Q2: What is its mathematical nature?
**A:** A caustic/leak region: rays with impact parameter in a narrow band around b_crit wind many times and pile up in the image - an exponential-approach bright edge.

## Q3: How thin/bright is it?
**A:** The ring's width shrinks and intensity grows toward the critical orbit (self-similar sub-rings); at high resolution the photon ring is a few % of the shadow width.

## Q4: How does it differ from the disk image's outer ring?
**A:** The disk's own Doppler-brightened ring is the accretion flow's image; the PHOTON ring is the geometry's caustic - the two can co-locate visually but have distinct scalings.

## Q5: What is the sub-ring (demagnification) ladder?
**A:** Null geodesics winding n half-turns appear as n faint copies demagnified by ~e^{-pi} per half-turn - the 'onion' near the shadow boundary.

## Q6: How is the photon ring measured?
**A:** Its diameter and fractional width (relative to the shadow) are the robust fingerprints; ring width is spin/inclination-dependent (smaller-ratio at high a).

## Q7: Why is the photon ring great for tests of gravity?
**A:** Its radius is mostly determined by the geometry (M, a) rather than emission details - a deviation from GR in the ring is a clean probe of exotic-gravity theories.

## Q8: How does the ring respond to spin?
**A:** Prograde ring shifts inward, retrograde outward -> an asymmetric ring width; measuring the ring's asymmetry pins a approximately.

## Q9: What does the renderer output for the ring?
**A:** A radial intensity cut across the image produces the ring-profile map - the diagnostics (radius, width, asymmetry) that feed model comparisons.

## Q10: What is the ring's time behavior?
**A:** The ring brightness flickers as the disk's inner emission varies, but its RADIUS stays fixed - separating geometry (stable) from emission (variable).

## Q11: What is the summary?
**A:** The photon ring is a geometric caustic signature - sharp, self-similar, spin-sensitive, and emission-robust; it is the precision observable of horizon-scale images.

## Q12: Why is photon ring signatures central to interpreting black-hole images?
**A:** Observables like the ring, shadow, and Doppler asymmetry trace directly to photon ring signatures, so understanding it lets you read images as physics.
