## Why a Retainer?

A retainer is the **highest-leverage engagement model** for both of us:

- **For you:** Guaranteed availability, institutional knowledge retention, faster response times, predictable billing
- **For me:** Stable income, deeper context, better work quality

Compared to ad-hoc hourly work, retainers save you **30-50% on effective hourly rates** and eliminate onboarding overhead for each new project.
