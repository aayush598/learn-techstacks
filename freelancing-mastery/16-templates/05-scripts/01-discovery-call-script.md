# Discovery Call Script

**Duration:** 30 minutes
**Goal:** Understand the client's problem, qualify the opportunity, and get agreement to move to proposal

---

## Pre-Call Preparation (15 minutes)

- [ ] Research the company (website, LinkedIn, recent news, funding)
- [ ] Research the prospect (LinkedIn profile, posts, shared connections)
- [ ] Identify 1-2 specific observations about their current setup
- [ ] Prepare 3-5 targeted questions
- [ ] Set up call recording (with permission) and note-taking
- [ ] Test audio/video

---

## Script Structure

---

### 0:00 — Opening (2 minutes)

> "Hi [Name], thanks for jumping on today. I know you're busy, so I'll be respectful of your time.
>
> **Here's my plan for our 30 minutes:**
> 1. Understand what you're working on and where you're at (15 min)
> 2. Share some relevant experience on my end (10 min)
> 3. Decide if it makes sense to move forward with a proposal (5 min)
>
> Sound good?"

**Set the stage:** Listen 80%, talk 20%. This call is about them.

---

### 0:02 — Background & Context (5 minutes)

> "First, could you give me a quick overview of [Company] and your role there?"

> "What prompted you to look into [project type / service] now?"

> "What's changed recently that makes this a priority?"

**Listen for:**
- ✅ Clear problem definition
- ✅ Urgency or deadline
- ✅ Budget discussion or approval process
- ❌ Vague needs, no timeline, no budget

---

### 0:07 — Problem Deep Dive (8 minutes)

> "Tell me more about the current situation. What's working, and what's not?"

> "What specific problems are you running into with [current system/process]?"

**Diagnostic questions (pick 2-3):**

**If it's a technical project:**
> "What does your current tech stack look like?"
> "What's the biggest bottleneck right now?"
> "Have you tried solving this before? What happened?"

**If it's a strategy/consulting engagement:**
> "What decisions are hanging on this work?"
> "Who needs to align on the approach?"
> "What would success look like 6 months from now?"

**If it's ongoing support/retainer:**
> "What does your current support situation look like?"
> "What's your biggest pain point day-to-day?"
> "How much does downtime cost you?"

**If the prospect is vague:**
> "If you had to pick the single most important thing to solve, what would it be?"

---

### 0:15 — Impact & Quantification (5 minutes)

> "What's the impact of this problem on your business?"
> - "How much revenue is it costing you?"
> - "How many hours per week is your team spending on workarounds?"
> - "What opportunities are you missing because of this?"

> "What's the cost of doing nothing for the next [X months]?"

> "If we solve this, what changes? How do you measure that?"

**Quantify if possible:**
- $X lost revenue per month
- Y hours wasted per week → $Z in labor cost
- X% conversion rate improvement potential

---

### 0:20 — Budget & Decision Process (5 minutes)

> "Do you have a budget range in mind for this?"

**If yes:** "That's helpful. Can you tell me how you arrived at that number?"
**If no:** "No problem at all. If we put together a solution that fits, what kind of budget would be realistic?"

> "What's the approval process like? Is there anyone else who needs to be involved in this decision?"

> "What's your timeline for making a decision?"

> "Are there any competing priorities or projects that might delay this?"

**Qualify:**
- [ ] Budget exists or is obtainable
- [ ] Decision maker(s) identified
- [ ] Clear timeline
- [ ] Authority to move forward

---

### 0:25 — Positioning & Next Steps (5 minutes)

> "Based on what you've shared, here's what I'm hearing: [1-2 sentence summary of problem]."
>
> "I've helped [X] clients with similar challenges. For example, [Client Name] had [similar problem], and we [specific result]."
>
> "Here's what I'd recommend as a next step:"
> 1. "I'll put together a proposal that outlines the approach, timeline, and investment."
> 2. "We can schedule a follow-up to walk through it together."
> 3. "If everything aligns, we can kick off as early as [date]."
>
> "Does that sound like a good plan?"

> "Is there anything I missed or anything else you'd like to discuss?"

**Get commitment:**
> "Great, I'll send the proposal by [day]. Would [day] work for a 30-minute walkthrough?"

**Confirm details:**
- [ ] Decision maker contact info
- [ ] Any stakeholders to include in proposal walkthrough
- [ ] Key dates/deadlines

---

### 0:30 — Close

> "Thanks again, [Name]. I appreciate the conversation and I'll get that proposal to you by [day].
>
> In the meantime, if you have any other questions, feel free to email me."

---

## Post-Call Actions (15 minutes)

- [ ] Send thank-you email within 2 hours (see email template)
- [ ] Update CRM with notes
- [ ] Tag lead: [Qualified / Unqualified / Needs Nurture]
- [ ] Draft proposal if qualified
- [ ] Add follow-up tasks to calendar

## Call Scoring Rubric

| Factor | Green (Proceed) | Yellow (Caution) | Red (Pass) |
|--------|----------------|------------------|------------|
| Problem clarity | Clear, specific pain | Vague need | No clear problem |
| Budget | Has budget / budget process | "Not sure" | No budget / expects free |
| Timeline | < 30 days | 30-90 days | "Someday" / no timeline |
| Authority | Is decision maker | Influencer but not DM | No access to DM |
| Fit | Exact match for my services | Partial match | Wrong niche |
| Value potential | $5k+ | $1-5k | < $1k |

**If 3+ red flags → don't pursue. Thank them and move on.**

## Advanced Branches

### Branch A: Prospect is Technical (CTO / Lead Dev)

> "What's your current architecture look like? Monolith, microservices, serverless?"
> "How do you handle CI/CD and deployments?"
> "What's your testing coverage like?"
> "What's your biggest technical debt item right now?"

### Branch B: Prospect is Non-Technical (Founder / CEO)

> "What outcome are you looking for from this project?"
> "How will you measure success?"
> "Who on your team will be working with me on this?"
> "What does your ideal timeline look like?"

### Branch C: Prospect is a Team Lead / Manager

> "How many engineers do you have on your team currently?"
> "What's the biggest bottleneck for your team right now?"
> "Are you looking to augment your team or outsource entirely?"
> "How do you handle knowledge transfer and documentation?"

### Branch D: Prospect is Evaluating Multiple Freelancers

> "Who else are you considering for this?"
> "What criteria are you using to make your decision?"
> "Is there anything specific you're looking for that you haven't seen yet?"
> "What would make the decision easy for you?"

### Branch E: The "Just Looking" Prospect

> "What information would help you make a decision?"
> "What's the trigger that would make you move forward?"
> "What's the best way for us to stay in touch?"
> "Would it be helpful if I shared some case studies in [area]?"

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Talking too much about yourself | Ask more questions; don't pitch until invited |
| Accepting vague answers | Ask "why" and "what does that look like" |
| Not quantifying impact | Push for numbers: "What does that cost you?" |
| Ignoring non-DM stakeholders | Ask "who else needs to be involved?" |
| Not establishing next steps | Always end with a clear commitment |
| Overpromising on timeline | Be realistic — under-promise, over-deliver |
| Not handling objections in real-time | Acknowledge, explore, address |
