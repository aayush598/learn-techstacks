# Follow-Up Email Sequences

**Pattern:** Follow up 3 times over 2 weeks, then move to nurture.

Do not send all at once. Wait 3-5 days between each email. If they respond at any point, stop the sequence and engage naturally.

## Follow-Up Best Practices

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Wait 3-5 days between emails | Send daily or within 24 hours |
| Add value in every email | Just say "checking in" |
| Keep each email under 100 words | Write novels |
| Vary the subject line | Reuse the exact same subject |
| Have a clear CTA | Be vague about next steps |
| Respect "no" gracefully | Keep emailing after a clear "not interested" |
| Track opens and responses | Blindly blast without tracking |
