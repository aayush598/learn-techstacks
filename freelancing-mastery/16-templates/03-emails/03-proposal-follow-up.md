# Post-Proposal Follow-Up Email Templates

**Goal:** Convert proposal into signed agreement. Timing is critical — send immediately after sending the proposal, then follow up strategically.

---

## Template 1: Standard Proposal Follow-Up (Sent Immediately)

**Subject:** Proposal enclosed — [Project Name]

Hi [Name],

As promised, here's the proposal for [Project Name]:

**[Link to proposal or PDF attachment]**

**Quick summary:**
- **Timeline:** [X] weeks (start [Date])
- **Investment:** $[X]
- **Next step:** Sign + [X]% deposit to kick off

I'm available to walk through any questions — want to schedule 20 minutes this [day] or [day]?

Best,
[Your Name]

---

## Template 2: Soft Follow-Up (Day 2-3 — No Response)

**Subject:** Quick thoughts on [Project Name] proposal

Hi [Name],

Just checking in — did the proposal for [Project Name] make sense?

One thing I wanted to highlight: **[key differentiator or risk-reduction guarantee]**.

A couple of clients have told me this was the deciding factor for them, so I wanted to make sure it didn't get overlooked in the document.

If you have 10 minutes this week, I'm happy to walk through it live. Otherwise, feel free to reply with any questions.

Best,
[Your Name]

---

## Template 3: Scarcity / Urgency (Day 5-7)

**Subject:** Capacity update — [Project Name]

Hi [Name],

Following up on the [Project Name] proposal.

I wanted to give you a heads-up that I currently have [X] other proposals under review, and I'll be allocating my bandwidth for the next [timeframe] by [decision deadline].

The proposed start date of [Date] is still available, but I can't guarantee it past [Day].

If you'd like to move forward, just reply to this email with "I approve" and I'll send the signature link.

Best,
[Your Name]

---

## Template 4: Budget Adjustment Offer (If You Can Flex)

**Subject:** Option to adjust the [Project Name] scope

Hi [Name],

I know [Project Name] is a significant investment. I've been thinking about how to make this work for you, and I put together two alternative approaches:

**Option A — Full scope:** $[X] (original proposal)
**Option B — Phased approach:** $[X] upfront for Phase 1, then Phase 2 at $[X] (start with core features)
**Option C — Reduced scope:** $[X] for a [X]-week MVP with [core features only]

Would any of these work better for your budget?

Happy to jump on a call to discuss what makes the most sense.

Best,
[Your Name]

Hi [Name],

I don't want to pile on, but I thought this might help your decision:

We recently completed a very similar project for [Client Name] in [Industry]. Here's what they said:

> "[Testimonial quote from similar client — include specific metric]"

[Link to case study if applicable]

I'd love to get the same results for [Company]. Let me know if you'd like to discuss further.

Best,
[Your Name]

## Template 6: The "Breakup" (Day 10-14 — Final Follow)

**Subject:** Closing the loop on [Project Name]

Hi [Name],

I haven't heard back on the proposal, and I respect that you're busy or the timing might not be right.

I'll close out this proposal on my end and free up the [start date] slot for another client.

If priorities change, feel free to reach out. The proposal is always here, and I'd be happy to revisit when the time is right.

Last thing — I'm sharing [useful resource / insight] that might be valuable regardless. [Link]

Wishing you all the best.

Best,
[Your Name]

I'll add a reminder on my end to circle back in [timeframe they mentioned]. In the meantime, I'll occasionally share relevant content in case it's useful.

If anything changes sooner, my inbox is open.

Best,
[Your Name]

Hi [Name],

Great question about [topic]. Here's my thinking:

[Clear, concise answer addressing their specific concern. If adjusting scope/price, specify exactly what changes.]

**Revised:**
- [What changed]
- [New cost if applicable]
- [Impact on timeline]

Does this address your concern? Happy to refine further.

Best,
[Your Name]

## Follow-Up Sequence Timing

| Email | When | Goal |
|-------|------|------|
| Proposal sent | Immediately | Deliver + summary + CTA |
| Soft follow-up | Day 2-3 | Open conversation |
| Value / scarcity | Day 5-7 | Create urgency |
| Budget option | Day 7-10 (if stalled on price) | Remove price objection |
| Social proof | Day 7-10 (alternative) | Build confidence |
| Breakup | Day 10-14 | Close or move on |
| Re-engagement | Month 3-6 | Rekindle if timing was off |
