# Cold Outreach Email Templates

Use these templates to generate leads. **Personalize every email.** Research the recipient, reference something specific about their company, and always include a clear call to action.

---

## Template 1: Technical Problem-Solution (Highest Conversion)

**Best for:** Developers/CTOs when you've identified a specific technical gap

**Subject:** Quick question about [Their Site/Tool]'s [specific issue]

Hi [Name],

I was looking at [Company]'s [page/product/tool] and noticed that [specific observation — e.g., "the checkout flow drops users on step 3" / "the page takes 6 seconds to load"].

We recently helped [Similar Client] solve the same problem and their [metric improved by X% — e.g., "conversion rate went up 22%"].

If you're open to it, I'd love to share how we approached it. No pitch — just the playbook.

Are you free for a 15-minute call this [day] or [day]?

Best,
[Your Name]
[Your Website]
[Your LinkedIn]

---

## Template 2: Value-Add Resource (Warm Lead)

**Best for:** Prospects in your network or industry who haven't engaged yet

**Subject:** Resource: [Content Title]

Hi [Name],

I've been following [Company]'s work in [industry/space] — really impressed with [specific thing they did].

I recently put together [a guide / a case study / a tool] on [relevant topic] that I thought might be valuable for your team given your focus on [area].

[Link to resource]

If it resonates and you'd like to discuss how we've applied this for similar companies, I'd be happy to set up a quick call.

Thanks,
[Your Name]

## Template 3: Referral / Introduction

**Best for:** Warm leads through mutual connections

**Subject:** [Mutual Contact] suggested we connect

Hi [Name],

[Mutual Contact] suggested I reach out — they thought our work on [topic] could be valuable for what you're building at [Company].

Quick background: I help [target client type] with [primary service offering]. Most recently, we [specific result for similar client].

If you're open to it, I'd love to grab 15 minutes to learn about what you're working on and see if there's a fit.

Best,
[Your Name]

## Template 4: The "Ideal Client" Direct Pitch

**Best for:** When you have a very specific ICP and know exactly what you can do for them

**Subject:** [X] clients in [Industry] — all had the same problem

Hi [Name],

Over the past [X] months, I've worked with [X] companies in [Industry/niche]. Every single one had the same challenge: [specific problem].

They were all struggling with [pain point], and they all had the same blind spot — [insight].

I built a [solution type] that solves this in [timeframe], and the results have been [specific metric].

I'm looking for [X] more clients in [Industry] this month. If [Company] is dealing with [pain point], I'd love to show you what we've done.

Call this [day] or [day]?

Best,
[Your Name]

---

## Cold Outreach Checklist

- [ ] Researched recipient's role and recent work
- [ ] Referenced something specific about their company
- [ ] Mentioned a similar client result with metrics
- [ ] Keep it under 150 words
- [ ] Single clear CTA (one link or one question)
- [ ] No attachments (they trigger spam filters)
- [ ] Sent from a professional email address
- [ ] Followed up 3-5 days later (use follow-up templates)
- [ ] Tracked opens/clicks (optional, use HubSpot, Mixmax, or Mailtrack)
