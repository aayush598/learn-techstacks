# Post-Meeting Thank You & Next Steps Emails

**Timing:** Send within 2 hours of the meeting. Strike while the conversation is fresh.

---

## Template 1: Initial Discovery Call Thank You

**Subject:** Great meeting you — next steps

Hi [Name],

Thanks so much for your time today. I really enjoyed learning about [Company] and what you're building in [area].

**My understanding of your priorities:**
1. **[Priority 1]** — [Brief recap]
2. **[Priority 2]** — [Brief recap]
3. **[Priority 3]** — [Brief recap]

**Next steps on my end:**
- [Action 1 — e.g., "I'll put together a proposal by [Day]"]
- [Action 2 — e.g., "I'll research [specific tech/tool] and include my recommendations"]

**Action needed from you:**
- [Action for client — e.g., "Please share access to [tool/system]"]
- [Action for client — e.g., "Please introduce me to [stakeholder name]"]

I'll have something for you by [date/time]. In the meantime, feel free to reach out if anything else comes to mind.

Best,
[Your Name]

## Thank You Email Best Practices

1. **Send within 2 hours** — strike while iron is hot
2. **Recap their words** — show you were listening
3. **Include clear next steps** — both sides know what's expected
4. **Add value** — link a relevant article or resource
5. **Keep it concise** — 3-5 paragraphs max
6. **Close with a CTA** — what happens next

---

- [ ] Sent thank-you within 2 hours

- [ ] Added follow-up reminder to my calendar
