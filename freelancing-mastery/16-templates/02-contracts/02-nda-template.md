## 1. Definition of Confidential Information

1.1 **"Confidential Information"** means any non-public information, in any form (written, oral, electronic, or visual), disclosed by one party to the other in connection with the Purpose, including but not limited to:

- Business plans, strategies, and forecasts
- Customer and supplier lists
- Financial data and pricing information
- Source code, algorithms, and technical specifications
- Product designs, prototypes, and roadmaps
- Trade secrets and proprietary processes
- Employee and personnel information
- Marketing plans and sales data
- Any information marked "Confidential" or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential

1.2 Confidential Information includes information disclosed directly or indirectly, orally or in writing, before or after the date of this Agreement.

---

## 2. Exclusions

Confidential Information does **not** include information that:

a) Is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement by Receiving Party
b) Was known to Receiving Party prior to disclosure (with written records)
c) Is independently developed by Receiving Party without use of Confidential Information
d) Is rightfully received from a third party without restriction
e) Is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order (with prompt notice to Disclosing Party)

---

## 3. Obligations of Receiving Party

Receiving Party agrees to:

3.1 **Maintain Confidentiality:** Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence using at least the same degree of care used to protect its own similar information, but in no event less than reasonable care.

3.2 **Limit Access:** Disclose Confidential Information only to employees, contractors, or advisors who:
   - Have a legitimate need to know for the Purpose
   - Are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as this Agreement

3.3 **Restrict Use:** Use Confidential Information **solely for the Purpose** and for no other reason.

3.4 **No Reverse Engineering:** Not reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble any Confidential Information.

3.5 **No Copying:** Not copy or reproduce Confidential Information except as necessary for the Purpose.

3.6 **Protection Measures:** Implement reasonable security measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or use.

---

## 4. Term & Termination

4.1 This Agreement begins on the date above and continues for **[X] years** (the "Term").

4.2 Confidential Information disclosed during the Term remains protected for **[X] years** from the date of disclosure.

4.3 **Trade secrets** remain protected as long as they maintain trade secret status under applicable law.

4.4 Upon written request, each party shall:
   - Return all Confidential Information in tangible form
   - Permanently delete all electronic copies
   - Certify in writing that this has been done

## 5. No License or Warranty

5.1 Nothing in this Agreement grants either party any intellectual property rights, licenses, or ownership in the other's Confidential Information.

5.2 All Confidential Information is provided **"AS IS"** without warranty of any kind.

---

## 6. Remedy for Breach

6.1 Both parties acknowledge that monetary damages may be inadequate for a breach of this Agreement.

6.2 The non-breaching party is entitled to seek **injunctive relief** and any other remedies available at law or equity.

6.3 The breaching party shall pay all reasonable legal fees and costs incurred by the non-breaching party in enforcing this Agreement.

---

7.2 **Amendments:** Must be in writing and signed by both parties.

7.3 **Governing Law:** **[State/Country]** law governs this Agreement.

7.4 **Jurisdiction:** Disputes resolved exclusively in **[County, State]** courts.

7.5 **Severability:** If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder remains in effect.

7.6 **Waiver:** Failure to enforce any provision is not a waiver.

7.7 **No Assignment:** Neither party may assign this Agreement without written consent.

7.8 **Export Control:** Each party shall comply with applicable export control laws.
