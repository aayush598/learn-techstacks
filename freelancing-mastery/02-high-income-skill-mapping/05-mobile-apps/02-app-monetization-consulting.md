# App Monetization Consulting: Helping Clients Make Money From Apps

## Overview

Most developers know how to BUILD apps. Few know how to make money from them. App monetization consulting bridges that gap — you help clients turn their app from a cost center into a revenue generator.

This is a high-value, under-served niche. Every app developer needs monetization strategy, but most focus on features and design. You'll charge premium rates for revenue expertise.

## Why Monetization Consulting Pays

1. **Direct revenue impact**: Your work directly increases client revenue (easy to measure ROI)
2. **Under-served niche**: Most consultants focus on development, not monetization
3. **All app types need it**: Free apps need ads, paid apps need pricing strategy, SaaS needs subscription optimization
4. **Continuous need**: Monetization strategy evolves with market conditions

## Service Offerings

### Service 1: App Monetization Audit

**What you do**: Review the client's app and identify revenue opportunities.

**Audit scope**:

**Business model assessment**:
- Current monetization approach (paid, freemium, subscription, ads, in-app purchases)
- Competitor pricing analysis
- Market positioning
- User willingness to pay analysis

**User experience review**:
- Onboarding and value demonstration
- Paywall placement and design
- Purchase flow friction analysis
- Retention mechanics

**Pricing analysis**:
- Current pricing vs market benchmarks
- Price elasticity estimation
- Tier structure optimization
- Promotional pricing strategy

**Revenue operations**:
- Analytics implementation (are they measuring revenue correctly?)
- Funnel analysis (conversion rate, drop-off points)
- Churn analysis (for subscription apps)
- LTV calculation accuracy

**Deliverables**:
- Current monetization scorecard
- Revenue opportunity analysis (e.g., "You're leaving $50K/month on the table")
- Prioritized recommendations with expected impact
- Implementation roadmap

**Pricing**: $8-20K depending on app complexity

### Service 2: Subscription Strategy and Optimization

**What you do**: Design or optimize subscription pricing and packaging.

**This is the highest-value monetization service.** Subscription apps are the majority of App Store revenue.

**Strategy components**:

**Tier structure design** ($10-25K)
- How many tiers? (research shows 3 is optimal for most apps)
- What features in each tier?
- Pricing per tier (based on value, competition, and willingness to pay)
- Free vs paid feature analysis

**Free trial optimization** ($5-15K)
- Trial length (3-day, 7-day, 14-day, 30-day — which converts best?)
- Trial experience (what users see during trial)
- Trial conversion optimization
- Post-trial win-back strategies

**Annual vs monthly pricing** ($3-8K)
- Discount percentage for annual (20-40% is standard)
- How to present the annual option
- Introductory pricing strategies

**Promotion and retention** ($5-15K)
- Introductory offers (free trial, pay-as-you-go, discounted first period)
- Promotional offers (discount for returning users)
- Win-back strategies for churned users
- Loyalty pricing (grandfathering)

### Service 3: In-App Purchase (IAP) Strategy

**What you do**: Design consumable and non-consumable IAP strategies.

**IAP types**:
- **Consumables**: Coins, gems, points, boosts, extra lives
- **Non-consumables**: Remove ads, premium features, pro version
- **Auto-renewable subscriptions**: Ongoing access (most revenue for most apps)
- **Non-renewing subscriptions**: Time-limited access (rarely optimal)

**Strategy components**:
- IAP pricing (psychological pricing: $0.99, $4.99, $9.99, $19.99)
- IAP placement (when to offer, how to present)
- Bundle strategy (value packs, starter packs)
- Limited-time offers (countdown timers, seasonal)

**Pricing**: $5-20K depending on IAP complexity

### Service 4: Ad Revenue Optimization

**What you do**: Maximize ad revenue without destroying user experience.

**This is critical for free apps and freemium apps.**

**Ad formats and CPM benchmarks** (2025-2026):

| Ad Format | Avg CPM | User Impact | Best For |
|-----------|---------|-------------|----------|
| Banner | $1-5 | Low | Utility apps, content apps |
| Interstitial | $5-20 | Medium | Games, casual apps |
| Rewarded Video | $15-30 | Low (opt-in) | Games, any app with currency |
| Native | $5-15 | Low-Medium | Content apps, social apps |
| Offerwall | $20-100+ | High | Games, utility apps |
| App Open | $8-20 | Low | Any app |

**Optimization services**:
- Ad network mediation setup (AdMob, AppLovin, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network)
- Ad placement strategy (where, when, how often)
- Frequency capping (prevent ad fatigue)
- A/B testing ad formats
- Ad revenue vs user retention analysis

**Pricing**: $8-20K for full ad monetization implementation

### Service 5: App Store Optimization (ASO) for Revenue

**What you do**: Optimize app store listings to drive more downloads (which drives more revenue).

**ASO services**:
- Keyword research and optimization
- Title and subtitle optimization
- Icon, screenshot, and preview video optimization
- Rating and review management
- Localization strategy
- Conversion rate optimization (impression to install)

**Pricing**: $5-15K for initial ASO + $2-5K/month for ongoing

### Service 6: Revenue Analytics and Dashboard

**What you do**: Set up proper revenue tracking so the client can make data-driven decisions.

**What most apps are missing**:
- Accurate LTV calculation
- Cohort analysis (how do different user groups perform?)
- Funnel analysis (where do users drop off?)
- Segmentation (high-value vs low-value users)
- Subscription analytics (trial conversion, churn, expansion)

**Tools**: RevenueCat, Adapty, Qonversion (subscription analytics), Mixpanel/Amplitude (product analytics), Adjust/Branch (attribution)

**Deliverables**:
- Analytics implementation (RevenueCat, Mixpanel, etc.)
- Revenue dashboard (key metrics at a glance)
- Regular reporting cadence

**Pricing**: $5-15K setup + $2-5K/month for ongoing analysis

## Client Acquisition

### Where Monetization Clients Come From

**1. App development agencies** (40%)
- They build apps but don't know monetization
- They refer you to their clients (or hire you directly)
- **Build**: Network with mobile dev agencies

**2. Indie app developers** (30%)
- Solo developers who built an app but aren't making money
- **Build**: Be active in indie dev communities (Indie Hackers, r/iOSProgramming)

**3. Founders/Product managers** (20%)
- SaaS founders who added a mobile app but don't know how to monetize
- **Build**: Content marketing about app monetization

**4. Venture studios** (10%)
- Companies that build and launch apps for equity
- They NEED monetization expertise to make their business model work

### Ideal Client Profile

**Best clients**:
- App with 10K+ downloads but low revenue (room for improvement)
- Subscription app with <5% trial conversion (fixable)
- Free app with ads but low eCPM (optimizable)
- App preparing to launch monetization (build it right from the start)
- Indie developer who built a great app but doesn't understand business

**Geography**: US and Western Europe apps earn 3-5x more per user than other regions. Focus on apps targeting these markets.

- **App Store Connect**: Subscriptions, IAPs, pricing, promotions

### A/B Testing

- **App Store Connect Product Page Optimization**: A/B test icons, screenshots

- **AppLovin MAX** (strong mediation platform)
