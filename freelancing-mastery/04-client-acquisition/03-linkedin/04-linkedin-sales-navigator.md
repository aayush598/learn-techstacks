## 2. Advanced Search Filters

**Geo-filtering:**
- Target specific countries, cities, or regions
- Use this to focus on high-paying markets (US, UK, EU, Australia)
- Combine with "Company HQ location" for enterprise targeting

**Industry Filtering:**
- Be specific: "Computer Software" > "Technology"
- Combine 2-3 related industries for better reach
- Exclude industries you don't serve

- 11-50: Growing startups (need specialists)
- 51-200: Scale-ups (budget for quality)

## 3. Saved Search Strategy

### Creating Your First Saved Search

**Step 1: Define Your ICP (Ideal Client Profile)**

Example:
```
Role: CTO, VP Engineering, Technical Founder
Industry: Computer Software, Internet, IT Services
Company Size: 11-200 employees
Location: United States, United Kingdom, Canada
Seniority: Owner, CXO, VP, Director
```

**Step 2: Add Keywords**

```
"building" "scaling" "migrating" "looking for" "hiring" "developer"
```

These keywords find people actively discussing their needs.

**Step 3: Exclude Irrelevant Results**

```
Exclude: Recruiters, Staffing Agencies, Freelancers, Students
```

**Step 4: Save and Name the Search**

```
"CTOs US SaaS 11-200 employees"
```

### Search Ideas by Client Type

**Startup Founders:**
```
Title: Co-Founder, CTO, CEO
Company Size: 1-10
Industry: Computer Software
Keywords: "MVP" "building" "launch" "looking for technical"
```

**Growing Companies:**
```
Title: VP Engineering, Head of Engineering, Engineering Manager
Company Size: 11-200
Industry: Computer Software, Internet
Keywords: "scaling" "hiring" "migrating" "technical debt"
```

**Enterprise (long game):**
```
Title: Director of Engineering, CTO, VP Product
Company Size: 200-5000
Industry: Financial Services, Healthcare, Technology
Keywords: "digital transformation" "modernization" "migration"
```

### Advanced Search Techniques

**Boolean Search:**
```
("CTO" OR "VP Engineering" OR "Head of Engineering") AND ("React" OR "Next.js" OR "TypeScript")
```

This gives you decision-makers who are technical and use relevant technologies.

**Exclusion Searches:**
```
NOT "recruiter" NOT "talent acquisition" NOT "HR"
```

Removes irrelevant profiles.

**Company Type Filter:**
- Focus on private companies (public companies have more red tape)
- Exclude subsidiaries (decisions made at parent level)
- Include recently funded companies (they have budget)

### Saved Search Cadence

**Active searches (check daily):**
- Leads matching your exact ICP
- Recent job changes (people in new roles need help)
- Content engagers (people who engaged with relevant content)

**Passive searches (check weekly):**
- Broader industry searches
- Competitor mentions
- New companies in your target space

**Monitoring searches (check monthly):**
- Saved lead updates
- Company news/events
- Industry changes

## 5. InMail Strategy

### When to Use InMail vs Connection Request

**Use InMail when:**
- You've exhausted connection requests for the week
- You want to message an existing 2nd-degree connection
- You're targeting C-suite at large companies
- You need to send a longer message
- You have InMail credits available

**Use Connection Request when:**
- You have room in your weekly connection limit
- You want to start a conversation naturally
- The prospect is mid-level or below
- You prefer the "soft" approach

### InMail Templates That Convert

**Template 1: Problem-Aware**

```
Subject: [Their Company]'s [specific challenge]

Hi [Name],

I noticed that [their company] is [doing something specific].

Many [industry] companies I work with face [specific challenge]. One common approach that works is [specific strategy].

I've helped [similar company type] achieve [result] with [approach].

Would you be open to a 15-minute discussion about whether this could work for [their company]?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

**Template 2: Value-First**

```
Subject: Quick idea for [their company]

Hi [Name],

I've been following [their company]'s growth. Impressive work with [specific achievement].

I have an idea that could help with [specific challenge they likely face]. Based on my work with [similar client], we were able to [result].

Would you be open to a brief call to explore this?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

**Template 3: Social Proof**

```
Subject: How I helped [similar company] achieve [result]

Hi [Name],

I recently helped [similar company] solve [specific problem] and achieve [specific result].

Given your work at [their company], I think a similar approach could be valuable.

I've attached a brief case study. Would you be open to a call to discuss?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### InMail Best Practices

- Keep subject lines under 10 words
- First sentence must hook (they see it in preview)
- Under 300 words total
- One clear call to action
- Never include links (reduces deliverability)
- Send during business hours (Tue-Thu, 9-11 AM)

### InMail Response Rates

| Type | Average Response Rate |
|---|---|
| Cold InMail (no prior contact) | 10-15% |
| InMail after prospect viewed your profile | 25-35% |
| InMail to existing connection | 40-50% |
| InMail with mutual connection mentioned | 30-40% |

## 6. Account-Based Marketing (ABM)

### The ABM Approach for Freelancers

Instead of targeting individuals, target specific companies:

1. **Identify 20 target companies** (ideal clients)
2. **Find 3-5 decision-makers** at each company
3. **Engage with all of them** (content, comments, connections)
4. **Coordinate your outreach** across the account

### Company Targeting Search

```
Company Name: [Your target companies]
Function: Engineering, Product, C-Suite
Seniority: Director, VP, CXO, Owner
```

### Multi-Contact Strategy

When targeting a company:

| Contact | Role | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Contact A | CTO/VP Eng | Technical conversation |
| Contact B | Product Manager | Value/ROI conversation |
| Contact C | Engineering Lead | Implementation conversation |

### Company Alerts

Set up alerts for your target accounts:
- New hires (someone new joined — good timing)
- Job changes (someone left — gap they need to fill)
- Funding announcements (they have budget)
- Product launches (they need support)
- Leadership changes (new priorities)
