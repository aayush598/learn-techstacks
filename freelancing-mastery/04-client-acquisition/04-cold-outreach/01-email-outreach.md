# Cold Email Outreach

## Why Cold Email Still Works

Cold email is the most scalable client acquisition channel. Unlike Upwork or LinkedIn, you're not limited by platform algorithms. You can reach any decision-maker at any company with a well-crafted email.

The key numbers:
- Average open rate: 20-40% (with good subject lines)
- Average reply rate: 5-15% (with good copy)
- Average conversion to client: 1-3% (from email → call → client)

At 3% conversion, sending 1,000 emails = 30 clients. That's $150K+ in revenue for most service offerings.

**Consulting Services:**
- CEO
- CTO
- VP of Product
- Head of Strategy
- Founder

### Decision-Maker Sourcing Workflow

1. **Start with the company type** (11-200 employees, funded, your target industry)
2. **Find the company** on LinkedIn or Crunchbase
3. **Search for the decision-maker** role at that company
4. **Find their email** using Apollo or Hunter
5. **Verify the email** before sending
6. **Add to your sequence**

### The "5 Prospects Per Company" Strategy

For each target company, find:
1. Primary decision-maker (CTO, etc.)
2. Secondary decision-maker (VP, etc.)
3. Potential champion (engineer, PM who could advocate)
4. CEO/Founder (for smaller companies)
5. Referral source (someone who could introduce you)

## 3. Email Sequencing

### The 4-Email Sequence

**Email 1: Value-First Introduction**
```
Subject: [Company Name] / [Specific Insight]

Hi [Name],

I've been following [Company Name]'s work in [industry]. [Specific compliment about their recent work].

I help [target client type] achieve [specific result] by [your service].

One specific way I could help [Company Name]: [specific idea relevant to them].

Would you be open to a 15-minute call to discuss?

Best,
[Your Name]
[Link to portfolio/site]
```

**Email 2: Case Study (Day 3)**
```
Subject: How [Similar Company] [Achieved Result]

Hi [Name],

I recently helped [Similar Company] solve [similar problem] and achieve [specific result].

Here's what we did:
- [Specific action 1]
- [Specific action 2]
- [Specific action 3]

The result: [Specific outcome with metrics]

I think a similar approach could work for [Company Name]. Happy to share more details.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

**Email 3: Problem Deep Dive (Day 6)**
```
Subject: [Specific Problem] at [Company Name]

Hi [Name],

I've been thinking about the [specific problem] that companies like yours face.

Some signs this might be affecting [Company Name]:
- [Sign 1]
- [Sign 2]
- [Sign 3]

If this resonates, I'd love to share how I've helped others solve it.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

**Email 4: Breakup (Day 10)**
```
Subject: Re: [Company Name]

Hi [Name],

I understand you're busy and this might not be a priority right now.

I won't follow up again, but if [specific problem] ever becomes a challenge, I'd be happy to help.

Wishing you and [Company Name] all the best.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### The 7-Email Sequence (High-Value Targets)

For $5K+ clients, use a longer sequence:

1. **Email 1 (Day 1):** Value-first introduction
2. **Email 2 (Day 3):** Case study from similar client
3. **Email 3 (Day 5):** LinkedIn connection request
4. **Email 4 (Day 8):** Specific insight about their business
5. **Email 5 (Day 12):** Social proof (testimonial)
6. **Email 6 (Day 16):** Direct offer with specific scope
7. **Email 7 (Day 21):** Final breakup

## 4. Subject Lines That Get Opened

### The Best Subject Line Formulas

**"Their Name" Personalization:**
```
[Name], quick question about your stack
[Name], noticed something about [Company Name]
```

**Problem-Based:**
```
Your [specific problem]
Scaling [specific technology] at scale?
Fixing [specific issue] before it costs you
```

**Curiosity Gap:**
```
A faster way to [specific outcome]
The opposite approach to [common practice]
You're probably not doing [specific thing]
```

**Straightforward:**
```
Quick idea for [Company Name]
Introduction — [Service] for [Company Name]
[Company Name] / [Specific benefit]
```

**Case Study/Proof:**
```
How [Similar Company] achieved [Result]
[Specific Result] for [Related Company]
[Number]% improvement in [Metric]

```

### Subject Line Rules

- Keep under 50 characters (mobile-friendly)
- No ALL CAPS (screams spam)
- No exclamation marks (screams spam)
- No dollar signs (screams spam)
- Personalize with their name or company
- Avoid spam trigger words: free, guarantee, act now
- Test 3-5 variations per campaign

### A/B Testing Subject Lines

```
Campaign A: "[Name], quick question"
Campaign B: "Idea for [Company]"
Campaign C: "Your [specific problem]"
```

Send 20% of your list to each variant. The winner goes to the remaining 40%.

## 5. Email Copy Formulas

### The AIDA Formula

**Attention:** Grab them with a specific observation
**Interest:** Show you understand their problem
**Desire:** Present your solution with proof
**Action:** Clear call to action

### The "4 Sentences" Email

The shortest emails often perform best:

```
Subject: [Specific insight about their company]

Hi [Name],

[Specific observation about their company/work].

[Specific way I can help, with proof from similar client].

[Clear call to action — 15 min call?]

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### The "PAS" Formula

**Problem:** State their specific problem
**Agitate:** Explain why it matters
**Solution:** How you solve it

```
I noticed [Company Name] is [specific problem].

This typically leads to [negative consequences] and costs [amount] in [lost revenue/wasted time].

I've helped [similar companies] solve this through [your approach]. [Specific result].
```

### The "Before-After-Bridge" Formula

**Before:** The current pain
**After:** The ideal state
**Bridge:** How you get them there

```
Right now, your [specific system] is [specific problem].

Imagine if [specific ideal outcome] instead.

I've helped [similar company] achieve this by [your specific approach]. Here's how it worked: [case study].
```

## 6. Personalization Strategies

### The Minimum Viable Personalization

For every email, personalize:
1. **Their first name** (in the greeting)
2. **Company name** (in the body)
3. **One specific observation** (their recent post, funding, product launch)

### The "Gold Standard" Personalization

```
I noticed [Company Name] just [raised funding / launched product / hired key person].

Your [specific thing they did] suggests you're focused on [specific goal].

I've been helping [similar companies] achieve [related outcome] through [your service].

Here's a specific idea for [Company Name]: [personalized suggestion].
```

### Personalization at Scale

Use merge tags for the basics:
```
{{first_name}}
{{company_name}}
{{industry}}
{{role}}
{{personalized_observation}}
```

For "personalized_observation," use Apollo's data fields or manually add for your top prospects.

### The 80/20 Personalization Rule

80% of effect comes from 20% of personalization:
- Name + company (covers 80% of "personalized feel")
- One specific observation (the 20% that makes them feel special)

Don't spend 30 minutes on each email. Spend 30 seconds. The volume + decent personalization beats perfect personalization at low volume.

## 7. Deliverability Best Practices

### Domain Setup

1. Buy a separate domain (yourname.io, yourname.co)
2. Set up Google Workspace or similar (yourname@yourname.co)
3. Configure SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records
4. Warm up the domain for 2-4 weeks before sending cold emails

### SPF, DKIM, DMARC

**SPF (Sender Policy Framework):** Authorized servers that can send from your domain
**DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail):** Digital signature verifying email authenticity
**DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication):** Policy for unauthenticated emails

Your email tool will provide setup instructions. Follow them exactly.

### Domain Warmup Schedule

Use Instantly or Warmbox's built-in warmup:

| Week | Emails Sent/Day | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | 5-10 | Warmup only |
| Week 2 | 10-20 | Warmup + 5 cold |
| Week 3 | 20-40 | Warmup + 10-20 cold |
| Week 4 | 40-60 | Warmup + 20-40 cold |
| Week 5+ | 60-80 | Warmup + 40-60 cold |

### Sending Limits

Per email address per day:
- 30-50 emails max (cold)
- 5-10 warmup emails
- 40-60 total

Per domain:
- 2-3 email addresses
- 100-150 total emails/day

### Bounce Management

- Never send to unverified emails (bounce rate > 3% kills deliverability)
- Remove bounces immediately
- Use a verification tool before every campaign
- Clean your list monthly

## 10. Legal Compliance

### CAN-SPAM Act (US)

- Don't use false or misleading headers
- Don't use deceptive subject lines
- Identify the message as an advertisement
- Include your valid physical postal address
- Include a clear opt-out mechanism
- Honor opt-out requests promptly

### GDPR (EU)

- Requires consent for emailing individuals
- Must provide opt-out in every email
- Must identify yourself legally
- Must process data fairly
- Higher penalties for violations ($20M+ or 4% of revenue)

### Practical Compliance

- Include an unsubscribe link in every email
- Include your physical address or PO box
- Use a legitimate sending domain
- Don't scrape or buy email lists
- Remove unsubscribes immediately
- Use a consent-based model when possible

## Final Truth

Cold email is a numbers game that rewards precision. Send 100 poorly targeted emails and get 0 replies. Send 100 well-researched emails to the right people with good copy and get 10+ replies.

The difference isn't luck — it's preparation. Invest in the right tools, write good copy, target the right prospects, and track everything. Cold email becomes a predictable client machine.

Most freelancers stop after 50 emails with no replies. The ones who send 5,000+ with continuous optimization build agencies.
