| AWS/Azure/GCP | $50-$200 per signup | $100 | 100 |
| DigitalOcean | $25 per $25 spend | $100 | 100 |
| Cloudflare | Varies | $50-$200 | 50-200 |
| Sentry | $50 per account | $100 | 100 |
| Vercel | 5% of revenue | $100-$500 | 20-100 |
| GitHub Copilot | $10/month recurring | $120 | 84 |
| Stripe | $100 + 0.5% for 2 years | $500+ | 20 |

**The affiliate flywheel:** Each referral can earn 12-24 months of commissions. Refer 5 developers per month, and after 12 months you have 60+ active referrals generating monthly passive income.

## The Affiliate Content Strategy

### High-Converting Content Types

**1. Comparison Pages (30% conversion rate)**
"Vercel vs Netlify vs Cloudflare Pages: Which is best in 2024?"
Include affiliate links to all three. Let the comparison do the selling.

**2. "Tech Stack" Posts (25% conversion)**
"My 2024 SaaS Tech Stack" - list every tool you use with affiliate links
Include: Hosting, database, auth, monitoring, email, payments, analytics

**3. Deployment Tutorials (20% conversion)**
"Deploy a Next.js app to [hosting] in 5 minutes"
Step-by-step with screenshots. Link to hosting in step 1.

**4. "How to Build [X]" Tutorials (15% conversion)**
"Build a SaaS with Next.js, Stripe, and Supabase"
Include affiliate links for each tool in the stack.

**5. Tool Review Posts (10% conversion)**
"Better Stack vs Sentry: Which error monitoring tool should you use?"
Honest review with affiliate links.

**6. "Why I Switched" Posts (35% conversion)**
"Why I moved from Netlify to Vercel and saved $200/month"
Most powerful because it includes before/after value.

**7. Coupon/Discount Posts (40% conversion)**
"[Tool] Promo Code: Save 20% on your first month"
Highest converting because people are already looking to buy.

## Strategic Affiliate Promotion

### The "Stack" Strategy

Don't promote one tool. Promote a stack. Create a recommended tech stack and earn commissions on every tool:

**Example: The Modern SaaS Stack**
- Hosting: Vercel (affiliate)
- Auth: Clerk (affiliate)
- Database: Supabase (affiliate)
- Payments: Stripe (affiliate)
- Email: Resend (affiliate)
- Monitoring: Sentry (affiliate)
- DNS: Cloudflare (affiliate)

One post recommending the stack = up to 7 affiliate commissions per developer.

### The "Integrate Your Products" Strategy

**You have a boilerplate or starter kit?** Include affiliate links in the code:

```
.env.example:
VERCEL_URL=
CLERK_SECRET_KEY=[Sign up at clerk.com?ref=yourlink]
SUPABASE_URL=[Sign up at supabase.com?ref=yourlink]
STRIPE_SECRET_KEY=[Sign up at stripe.com?ref=yourlink]
SENTRY_DSN=[Sign up at sentry.io?ref=yourlink]
```

Every developer who uses your boilerplate needs to sign up for these tools. If 500 people download your boilerplate, even 10% using your affiliate links = 50 commissions.

### The "Naturally in Your Workflow" Strategy

The most authentic promotion is when you're genuinely using the tool:

- In your coding videos/screenshots, your terminal shows your tools
- In your deployment tutorials, you're using your host of choice
- In your stack posts, you're listing what you actually use
- When someone asks "what monitoring do you use?" you have a genuine answer and a link

### The "Discount Code" Strategy

Many affiliate programs offer unique discount codes. These convert 2-3x better than generic links:

"[Tool] is what I use for [purpose]. Use code YOURCODE for 20% off your first month."

**Why it works:**
- Feels like a favor, not an ad
- Creates urgency (limited time)
- Makes the user feel smart for finding a deal

## Legal and Ethical Guidelines

### Disclosures

**Required by FTC (US) and similar laws globally:**
- "This post contains affiliate links. If you purchase through these links, I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you."
- Make disclosure clear and prominent (not hidden in footer)
- Disclose on every page/post with affiliate links
- Disclose in video description AND verbally in video

### Ethical Affiliate Marketing

**Rules to live by:**
1. Only promote tools you actually use and believe in
2. Never recommend a tool just for the commission
3. Be honest about pros and cons
4. If you'd recommend it without the commission, it's fine to have commission
5. If you wouldn't, don't promote it

**The trust equation:**
"Your audience trust is worth more than any single affiliate commission. One bad recommendation costs you 10 future commissions."

## The Golden Rules of Developer Tool Affiliates

1. **Authenticity beats optimization every time.** Recommend what you use.
2. **Comparisons convert best.** Developers research before buying.
3. **Tutorials are evergreen.** Write once, earn for years.
4. **Recurring > one-time.** Prioritize programs with recurring commissions.
5. **Build an asset, not just links.** Your content and audience are the real value.
6. **Disclosure builds trust.** Hidden affiliate links destroy credibility.
7. **Catch people early.** Beginners convert better than experienced devs.
8. **Free tools attract.** Give away value, monetize through recommendations.
9. **SEO compounds.** Every article is an asset that earns over time.
10. **Diversify.** Don't rely on one affiliate program or traffic source.
