# General Consulting / Strategy Proposal Template

## How to Use This Template

Consulting proposals sell thinking, not doing. You're not selling hours — you're selling clarity, direction, and confidence in decision-making. The deliverables are documents, frameworks, and recommendations. The value is in the quality of your analysis and the ROI of your recommendations.

## Consulting Engagement Red Flags

| Red Flag | Risk | Mitigation |
|----------|------|------------|
| "We need a strategy, but we already know what we want" | You're being hired to validate, not advise | Clarify upfront: are they open to being wrong? |
| Team can't agree on the problem | Scope will expand as you mediate | Build alignment work into scope, charge for it |
| No data available | Analysis will be shallow | Set expectations: "Without data, recommendations will be based on interviews and benchmarks" |
| Decision-maker won't participate | Recommendations won't be adopted | Require CEO/decision-maker availability as a condition |
| "We need this in 2 days" | Rushed = low quality, high risk | Push back on timeline, or narrow scope severely |
| Competitor already hired you | Conflict of interest | Disclose immediately, offer to recuse or use firewall |

## Differentiating Solo Consulting

When competing against McKinsey, BCG, Bain, or Deloitte:

| Factor | Big Firm | Solo Consultant |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| Cost | $500K-$2M for typical strategy | $15K-$100K |
| Team | Partner + manager + 2-3 analysts | Just me (you get the expert) |
| Timeline | 3-6 months | 3-8 weeks |
| Depth | Broad, may lack niche expertise | Deep, specialized |
| Politics | May hesitate to give difficult advice | Complete candor |
| Follow-through | Engagement ends, new team may implement | I can implement what I recommend |
| Chemistry | Relationship with partner, not the team | Direct relationship with doer |
