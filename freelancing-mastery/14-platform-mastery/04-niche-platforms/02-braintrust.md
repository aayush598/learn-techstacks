# Braintrust: The Decentralized Talent Network

## Platform Overview

Braintrust (BTRST) is a user-owned talent network that connects freelance developers, designers, and other technical experts with enterprise clients. It is the first decentralized freelancing platform, using blockchain technology and a token-based economy.

**Why Braintrust Matters:**
- Zero platform fees for freelancers (0% — no joke)
- Clients pay a 5-10% fee, but freelancers keep 100% of their rate
- Average developer rate: $80-$180+/hour
- Typical project size: $10K-$150K+
- Client base: NASA, Porsche, Nestle, Deloitte, Goldman Sachs, and 500+ others
- Token-based governance and incentives
- No bidding — projects are matched or negotiated

**The Braintrust Difference:**
- 0% freelancer fee (keep every dollar you earn)
- Decentralized governance (BTRST token holders vote on platform decisions)
- Referral-based growth (earn BTRST tokens for referring clients and freelancers)
- Enterprise clients only (no low-budget projects)
- Focus on long-term, complex engagements

## Getting Started on Braintrust

### Application Process

Braintrust is invite-only but open to qualified professionals. The application process is selective but less competitive than gun.io or Toptal.

**Step 1: Apply**
- Visit braintrust.com/apply
- Connect your wallet (MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet)
- Complete your profile with experience, skills, and rate expectations
- Link LinkedIn and GitHub

**Step 2: Profile Review**
- Braintrust reviews your profile (24-72 hours)
- They verify your experience through LinkedIn and portfolio
- They assess skills alignment with client demand
- Most qualified professionals are accepted

**Step 3: Complete Your Profile**
- Add detailed work history
- Upload portfolio projects
- Set your availability and preferred rate
- Complete skills assessments (if available)
- Connect your payment method (US bank account, crypto wallet)

**Step 4: Start Getting Matched**
- You appear in Braintrust's talent pool
- Clients invite you to projects
- You can also apply to posted opportunities
- Negotiate directly with clients

**Profile Photo:**
- Professional headshot

**Headline:**
- "Senior Full-Stack Developer | React, Node.js, Python | 10+ Years Enterprise Experience"
- Include "Enterprise" in your headline (Braintrust clients are enterprise)
- Specific technologies and years of experience

**Bio Structure:**

Your bio should emphasize enterprise experience and complex problem-solving.

"Senior full-stack developer with 10+ years of experience delivering enterprise-scale applications. I specialize in React, Node.js, Python, and cloud infrastructure (AWS/GCP). I have deep experience in fintech, healthcare, and e-commerce sectors, having delivered 15+ enterprise projects for Fortune 500 companies."

"Throughout my career, I have led development teams, architected complex systems, and delivered solutions that handle millions of users and terabytes of data. I work best in collaborative environments with clear objectives and direct communication."

**Skills:**
- List 10-15 relevant skills
- Enterprise-focused technologies (Java, .NET, AWS, Azure)
- Modern stack skills (React, Node.js, TypeScript, Python)
- Soft skills (team leadership, architecture, client management)
- Industry-specific expertise (fintech, healthcare, etc.)

**Portfolio:**
- Enterprise projects preferred
- Detail your role, team size, and project scale
- Include measurable outcomes
- Client testimonials (especially from enterprise clients)

## The Token-Based Economy

### BTRST Token Overview

Braintrust uses the BTRST token as its native cryptocurrency. Understanding the token is essential for maximizing your Braintrust experience.

**Token Functions:**
- **Governance:** Token holders vote on platform decisions
- **Referral Rewards:** Earn BTRST for referring clients and freelancers
- **Staking:** Stake BTRST to access premium features and opportunities
- **Incentives:** Complete actions to earn BTRST (profile completion, skills tests, etc.)

**Token Value:**
- BTRST is traded on major exchanges (Coinbase, Binance, Kraken)
- Market price fluctuates based on demand and platform growth
- Tokens can be held, sold, or used within the platform
- Some freelancers hold tokens as investment while using the platform

### Earning BTRST Tokens

**Referral Bonuses:**
- Refer a client: Earn 10% of their first year's platform fees in BTRST
- Refer a freelancer: Earn a one-time BTRST bonus (typically $100-$500 worth)
- If you refer a client who spends $100K on the platform, you earn $10K in BTRST
- Referral rewards are paid in BTRST tokens

**Staking Rewards:**
- Stake BTRST to earn additional rewards
- Staking also improves your profile visibility
- Higher stakes = more visibility = more opportunities
- Staking requirements vary by role and experience

**Incentives:**
- Complete your profile: 25 BTRST (one-time)
- Pass skills assessments: 10-50 BTRST per assessment
- Complete your first project: 100-500 BTRST bonus
- Maintain high ratings: Recurring BTRST bonuses
- Participate in governance: Vote on proposals for token rewards

### Using BTRST Tokens

**Hold:** Treat tokens as an investment in the platform's growth
**Sell:** Convert to USD for immediate income
**Stake:** Earn passive income and improve visibility
**Vote:** Participate in platform governance decisions
**Refer:** Use tokens to incentivize referrals

### Token Strategy for Freelancers

- **Beginner:** Complete profile and assessments to earn initial tokens
- **Active:** Earn tokens through referrals and project completion
- **Advanced:** Stake tokens for visibility and governance participation
- **Expert:** Hold tokens as long-term investment in platform growth

**Tax Considerations:**
- BTRST tokens are taxable income when earned
- Track the USD value at time of receipt
- Staking rewards are also taxable
- Consult a crypto-savvy tax professional
- Use crypto tax software (Koinly, CoinTracker)

## Finding and Winning Projects

### How Projects Work on Braintrust

**Client Posts a Project:**
- Enterprise client defines scope, timeline, and budget range
- Braintrust reviews and approves the project
- Project is posted to the talent pool

**Talent Matching:**
- Braintrust's algorithm matches freelancers to projects
- Clients can also search for specific skills
- You receive notifications for matching projects

**Applying/Expressing Interest:**
- Express interest in projects that fit your skills
- Clients review your profile and portfolio
- If interested, they reach out for an interview

**Interviewing:**
- 1-2 video calls with the client
- Discuss project requirements and your approach
- Negotiate rate and timeline
- If both agree, the engagement begins

**Engagement:**
- Contract is signed on Braintrust
- Time tracking (built-in or via integration)
- Weekly or monthly invoicing
- Payment released upon approval

### Proposal Strategy

**When You Receive a Project Invitation:**

1. **Respond within 24 hours** (speed signals interest)
2. **Read the project description carefully**
3. **Express genuine interest** with specific context
4. **Highlight relevant experience** from your portfolio
5. **Ask 1-2 smart questions** about the project
6. **Propose a brief call** to discuss further

**Template Response:**
"Hi [Client Name], thank you for the invitation. I've reviewed the project and I'm very interested. I have [X years] of experience with [technologies], including a similar project for [similar client] where I [specific achievement]. I'd love to discuss the project in more detail. Would you be available for a 30-minute call this week?"

**How to Stand Out:**
- Reference specific details from the project description
- Share a similar case study from your portfolio
- Propose a specific approach or solution
- Demonstrate understanding of their industry
- Be enthusiastic but professional

### Client Communication on Braintrust

**Initial Communication:**
- Use Braintrust's messaging system
- Be professional and responsive
- Answer questions thoroughly
- Share relevant portfolio links

**During Interviews:**
- Prepare specific examples of past work
- Discuss your process and methodology
- Understand their goals and constraints
- Ask about team structure and culture
- Clarify expectations for communication and reporting

**After Winning a Project:**
- Send a welcome message with project plan
- Set up communication channels
- Schedule regular check-ins
- Provide weekly status updates
- Document everything

## Payment and the Fee Structure

### The 0% Fee Explained

Braintrust's zero-fee model is its biggest advantage for freelancers.

**How It Works:**
- Client pays your full rate (e.g., $100/hr)
- Braintrust charges the client a 5-10% platform fee (separate from your rate)
- You receive 100% of your rate
- No withholding, no deductions, no surprises

**Comparison with Other Platforms:**

| Platform | Freelancer Fee | Take-Home on $100/hr |
|----------|---------------|---------------------|
| Braintrust | 0% | $100.00 |
| Upwork | 10-20% | $80-$90 |
| Toptal | 0% | $100.00 |
| Fiverr | 20% | $80.00 |
| Freelancer.com | 10% | $90.00 |
| Guru | 8-12% | $88-$92 |

**Annual Impact:**
At $100/hr, 40 hrs/week, 50 weeks/year = $200,000 gross
- Braintrust: $200,000 take-home
- Upwork (20%): $160,000 take-home
- Difference: $40,000/year in your pocket

### Payment Methods

**US Bank Account:**
- Direct deposit (ACH)
- 1-3 business day settlement
- No additional fees
- Best for US-based freelancers

**International Wire:**
- Available for non-US freelancers
- 3-5 business day settlement
- Wire transfer fees apply (varies by bank)
- Best for high-value payments

**Crypto (USDC/USD Coin):**
- Stablecoin (pegged to USD)
- Instant settlement
- Low transaction fees
- Available globally
- Best for international freelancers and crypto users

**PayPal (limited):**
- Higher fees than ACH
- Slower settlement
- Available in select countries

### Invoicing and Taxes

**Invoicing:**
- Braintrust generates invoices automatically
- Weekly or monthly billing cycles
- Invoice includes your rate, hours, and total
- Client approves and payment is released
- Download invoices for your records

**Tax Considerations:**
- Braintrust provides 1099 forms for US freelancers
- International freelancers are responsible for local tax compliance
- All income is reportable (including BTRST tokens)
- No tax withholding (you are responsible for estimated taxes)
- Set aside 25-40% of income for taxes (depending on location)

## Building Your Reputation

### Ratings and Reviews

**Braintrust's Review System:**
- Clients rate freelancers after each project
- Ratings are public on your profile
- 5-star system with written reviews
- Both parties can leave reviews

**Maintaining 5-Star Rating:**
- Communicate proactively (weekly updates)
- Deliver on time, every time
- Exceed expectations when possible
- Resolve issues immediately
- Ask for feedback during the project (don't wait for the end)

**Recovering from a Bad Review:**
- Respond to the review professionally
- Explain what happened objectively
- Show what you learned
- Move on and focus on your next project
- One bad review among many 5-star reviews is fine

### Referrals on Braintrust

Referrals are the highest-leverage activity on Braintrust. The referral system is designed to incentivize network growth.

**Client Referrals:**
- Refer a company that becomes a Braintrust client
- Earn 10% of their platform fees for the first 12 months
- If they spend $1M on the platform, you earn $100K in BTRST
- No cap on referral earnings
- Referrals can generate passive income for years

**Finding Clients to Refer:**
- Past clients from other platforms
- Companies you've worked with before
- Companies in your network
- Companies posting in freelance communities
- Your current employer (if they hire freelancers)

**Freelancer Referrals:**
- Refer qualified developers to Braintrust
- Earn a one-time BTRST bonus (typically $100-$500 worth)
- The referred freelancer must complete a project for you to earn
- Refer your freelance friends and colleagues
