# Guru and PeoplePerHour Strategy Guide

## Platform Overviews

### Guru

Guru has 3M+ members and processes $250M+ in annual transactions. It's particularly strong for:
- Web and software development
- Design and multimedia
- Writing and translation
- Administrative support
- Engineering and manufacturing

**Key Features:**
- Fixed-price and hourly projects
- SafePay escrow system
- Workroom for project collaboration
- Flexible billing options
- Lower competition than Upwork/Freelancer
- Better for long-term contracts

### PeoplePerHour

PeoplePerHour is UK-based with 2M+ freelancers and 1M+ clients. It's particularly strong for:
- Web development and design
- SEO and digital marketing
- Content writing
- Video production
- Business consulting

**Key Features:**
- Hourlies (predefined service packages)
- Fixed-price and hourly projects
- Escrow payment protection
- UK and European client base (less competition from US-focused freelancers)
- Strong in SEO and marketing categories

**Why These Platforms Together:**
Guru + PeoplePerHour gives you access to clients who aren't on Upwork or Freelancer. Combined, they can generate $3K-$10K/month with the right strategy. Many top freelancers use all three platforms simultaneously.

## Guru Profile Optimization

### Profile Structure

**Profile Photo:**
- Professional headshot on clean background
- Business casual or formal
- High resolution, well-lit
- Consistent across all platforms

**Headline:**
- Formula: Senior [Role] | [X] Years | [Specialty 1], [Specialty 2], [Specialty 3]
- Example: "Senior Full-Stack Web Developer | 10+ Years | PHP, Python, React, Laravel"
- Include primary keywords for your niche

**Overview (Bio):**

Section 1 - Introduction:
- "I'm a senior full-stack developer with 10+ years of experience building complex web applications for startups and enterprises worldwide."

Section 2 - Expertise (bullet points):
- 8+ years of PHP/Laravel development
- 6+ years of React and Vue.js frontend
- 4+ years of AWS cloud architecture
- Expert in REST API design and integration
- Database design (MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB)

Section 3 - Process:
- "My workflow: Requirements analysis -> Technical architecture -> Agile development with weekly demos -> Comprehensive testing -> Smooth deployment."

Section 4 - Guarantee:
- "I guarantee: on-time delivery, clean code with documentation, 30 days of post-launch support, and 100% satisfaction or your money back."

**Skills:**
- Add 20-30 relevant skills
- Focus on your highest-value technical skills
- Verify skills with Guru tests where available
- Update quarterly

**Portfolio:**
- Minimum 10 portfolio items
- Format: Title, description, technologies, link
- Include measurable results
- Client testimonials embedded in portfolio
- Video case studies if possible

**Certifications:**
- Industry certifications (AWS, Google, Microsoft)
- Programming language certifications
- Project management certifications
- Guru skills tests passed

**Employment History:**
- Only relevant positions (tech, leadership)
- Emphasize client-facing and project management roles
- Include freelance work as "Self-Employed"

## Guru Bidding Strategy

### The Guru Bid Structure

**Subject:**
- Direct and focused on their project
- Example: "Laravel Expert - 8 Years Experience - Let's Build Your E-commerce Platform"

**Bid Amount:**
- Research the market rate for similar projects
- Bid slightly above midpoint (not lowest, not highest)
- For long-term projects, bid a fixed monthly rate
- Always include what the price covers

**Bid Message:**

**1. Opening (2-3 sentences):**
- Show you read the brief
- "I reviewed your project requirements for building a multi-vendor marketplace. I have 3 years of experience building similar platforms."

**2. Credibility (2-3 sentences):**
- "I've delivered 5+ multi-vendor marketplaces using Laravel and Vue.js. My most recent project handled 50K+ products and 2K+ vendors."

**3. Approach (3-4 sentences):**
- "My approach includes: 1) Requirements workshop to finalize scope, 2) Database and architecture design, 3) Sprint-based development with weekly deliverables, 4) QA testing and deployment. You'll have full visibility through Trello/Asana."

**4. Timeline and Budget (2-3 sentences):**
- "I estimate 6-8 weeks for full delivery. Budget is $5,000-$8,000 depending on final feature scope. I'm flexible on phasing to match your budget."

**5. Call to Action (1-2 sentences):**
- "Would you like to schedule a 20-minute call to review the technical approach? I'm available this week."

### When to Bid on Guru

**Optimal Times:**
- Monday-Thursday, 9am-12pm EST
- New projects get the most views in first 2 hours
- Bid within first hour for maximum visibility

**Project Selection:**
- Budget over $500 (under $500 = high competition, low value)
- Client has verified SafePay (payment method confirmed)
- Client has a hiring history (successfully hired before)
- Project description is detailed (50+ words)
- Fewer than 10 bids already placed
- Client responds to bids (check their history)

**Project Types to Target:**
- Web development (highest volume on Guru)
- API integration and development
- Bug fixing and maintenance for existing sites
- Database design and optimization
- Cloud migration and setup

**Project Types to Avoid:**
- Data entry (low rates, high competition)
- Virtual assistant (under $10/hr)
- Copy-paste work (not skilled freelancing)
- Projects without a clear description
- Clients with no history or payment verification

## PeoplePerHour Profile Strategy

### The "Hourlie" System

PeoplePerHour's unique feature is "Hourlies" - predefined service packages. This is the highest-leverage feature on the platform.

**Hourlie Structure:**

1. Title: Clear, specific, keyword-rich
   - "I will build a responsive WordPress website with e-commerce functionality"
   - "I will develop a custom React.js dashboard with API integration"

2. Category and Subcategory: Choose the most specific option
   - Web Development > Web Programming
   - Design > Web Design

3. Description:
   - What you deliver (specific deliverables)
   - Who this is for (target client)
   - Technical requirements (what client needs to provide)
   - Timeline (3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks)
   - Your process (step by step)
   - Why choose you (credentials, experience)

4. Pricing:
   - Basic: $500 (core service)
   - Standard: $1,000 (core + extras like mobile responsive, SEO)
   - Premium: $2,000 (full service including content, analytics, training)
   - Extras: Add-on services at additional cost

5. Extras:
   - Additional page at $50 each
   - E-commerce setup $200
   - SEO optimization $150
   - Content upload $100
   - Priority delivery +50%

6. Gallery/Portfolio:
   - 5-10 images of past work
   - Before/after comparisons
   - Screenshots with annotations
   - Client logos (if allowed)

7. FAQ Section:
   - Common questions about your service
   - Delivery timeline
   - Revision policy
   - Technical requirements

**Hourlie Optimization:**

- Create 5-10 Hourlies covering your main services
- Update pricing quarterly (increase as you get more reviews)
- Test different price points (start lower, raise as reviews come in)
- A/B test titles and descriptions
- Monitor which Hourlies get the most views and conversions

**Hourlie Pricing Strategy:**

|-------------|-------|----------|---------|
| WordPress Site | $500 | $1,000 | $2,000 |
| React Dashboard | $1,000 | $2,000 | $4,000 |

## PeoplePerHour Bidding Strategy

### Sending Proposals

PeoplePerHour uses a system where clients send you "invitations to bid" AND you can send proposals.

**Proposal Structure:**

**Subject Line:**
- "Available to start immediately - [Your Specialty] Expert"

**Body:**

- "I've reviewed your project for [project name/type]. I'm confident I can deliver exactly what you need."

- "With 10+ years of experience in [your field], including 25+ projects similar to yours, I have the expertise and track record to deliver exceptional results."

- "My process: [2-3 sentence process overview]. I deliver work that exceeds expectations, on time and on budget."

- "I can start [immediate/next week] and deliver within [timeline]. My rate is [amount] for the full scope. This includes [deliverables list]."

- "Would you like to discuss the project in more detail? I'm available for a call.'

**Key Differences from Guru:**
- PeoplePerHour clients prefer shorter, more direct proposals
- Emphasize speed and reliability
- UK clients value politeness and professionalism
- Name-drop relevant UK brands if applicable (helps with trust)

### Hourlie Requests

When clients request a specific Hourlie:
- Respond within 1 hour (speed matters)
- Confirm you can deliver exactly what they need
- Offer to customize the Hourlie if needed
- Suggest the appropriate tier
- Offer extras based on their specific needs

**Conversion Rate Optimization:**
- Reply to 100% of Hourlie requests within 2 hours
- Follow up if no response in 48 hours
- Offer a 10% discount for first-time clients
- Offer a satisfaction guarantee

## Escrow and Payment Systems

### Guru SafePay

**How SafePay Works:**
1. Client deposits funds into SafePay
2. Funds are held by Guru
3. You work on the project
4. Client approves the work
5. Funds are released to you
6. Guru deducts their fee (8.75% for members, varies by membership)

**Important SafePay Rules:**
- Never start work without SafePay funded
- The funded amount is what you'll be paid (no scope creep without new SafePay)
- Use milestones for projects over $500
- Document everything in the Workroom
- Disputes are resolved by Guru based on evidence

**Milestone Best Practices:**

| Project Value | Milestones | Structure |
|--------------|-----------|-----------|
| $100-$500 | 1 milestone | 100% on completion |
| $500-$2,000 | 2 milestones | 50% on design approval, 50% on completion |
| $2,000-$10,000 | 3 milestones | 33% each for milestones |
| $10,000+ | 4-5 milestones | 25% each for phases |

**Releasing Milestones:**
- Only release work after client confirms satisfaction
- Keep proof of delivery (screenshots, deployment links)
- For code: Deliver through repository, not just zip files
- Always release the final milestone (last payment) last

### PeoplePerHour Escrow

PeoplePerHour uses a similar escrow system:
- Client pays into escrow before work starts
- Funds released upon client approval
- Platform fee: 20% for first $500 with a client, then 5%
- Fee structure incentivizes repeat business

**Fee Optimization:**
- First project with client: 20% fee (painful)
- Second project: 5% fee (much better)
- Strategy: Win first project at slightly lower rate, then raise rates for repeat work
- Always try to get repeat business after the first project

## Building Reputation on Guru

**Getting Started:**
- Complete verification (ID verification, skills tests)
- Start with 3-5 small projects (under $500) to build reviews
- Offer exceptional quality and quick turnaround
- Ask for detailed reviews (guide them on what to say)

**Review Request Template:**
"Thank you for the opportunity to work on your project. If you're satisfied with the results, I would really appreciate a review on Guru. Specifically mentioning [specific deliverable] and [specific result] would be incredibly helpful for my profile. Thank you!"

**Maintaining 5-Star Rating:**
- Communicate proactively (weekly updates)
- Deliver ahead of schedule when possible
- Offer post-delivery support (30 days)
- Fix issues immediately and without argument
- Never leave a client unhappy

**Recovering from a Bad Review:**
- Contact the client privately
- Offer to fix the issue at no charge
- If resolved, politely ask if they'd update the review
- If not resolved, respond professionally to the review
- Learn from it and improve

## Building Reputation on PeoplePerHour

**The PeoplePerHour Review System:**
- Reviews are critical for Hourlie visibility
- First 5 reviews determine your initial ranking
- 4.5+ stars = featured in search results
- 4.8+ stars = "Top Quality" badge

**Getting Reviews:**
- Deliver faster than promised (3-day delivery in 2 days)
- Over-communicate (send progress updates daily)
- Add unexpected value (bonus deliverable, extra support)
- Ask for review immediately after delivery
- Follow up if no review after 3 days

**The Review Velocity Factor:**
- New reviews boost your ranking temporarily
- Getting 2-3 reviews per week is optimal for ranking
- If work is slow, temporarily reduce prices to get volume
- More reviews = higher ranking = more clients = higher prices

## Client Acquisition and Retention

### Finding Clients on Guru

**Guru Search Tips:**
- Use advanced search filters (budget >$500, verified payment)
- Search for projects posted in the last 24 hours
- Bookmark recurring clients (they post regularly)
- Set up email alerts for relevant keywords
- Check the "Favorite Projects" section for repeat clients

**Niche Categories on Guru:**
Guru has specialized categories where competition is lower:
- Engineering and Manufacturing (high budget, few freelancers)
- Legal (high budget, specialized)
- Finance and Accounting (recurring work)
- Network and System Administration (long-term)
- Databases and ERP (high value)

### Finding Clients on PeoplePerHour

**PeoplePerHour Search:**
- Set up alerts for relevant keywords
- Check new projects every morning
- Apply to "Buyer Requests" (clients who posted a project)
- Respond to Hourlie requests immediately

**UK/European Client Advantage:**
- Fewer freelancers target UK clients (compared to US)
- UK clients often have higher budgets
- European clients value quality over price
- Time zone advantage (if in Europe/Africa)
- GDPR knowledge is a differentiator

### Converting to Repeat Clients

**The Repeat Client System:**
1. Deliver exceptional work on project 1
2. Offer a maintenance package after delivery
3. Check in monthly with value-added suggestions
4. Offer a retainer for ongoing work
5. Ask for referrals after 3 months

**Retainer Pricing Example:**
- Monthly maintenance: $500-$2,000/month
- Includes: bug fixes, updates, backup, security patches
- Excludes: new features (separate project)
- Commitment: 3-month minimum, 30-day cancellation

## Cross-Platform Strategy

### Guru + PeoplePerHour + Other Platforms

**The Multi-Platform Approach:**
- Guru: Long-term contracts, milestone projects
- PeoplePerHour: Hourlies, quick projects
- Upwork/Freelancer: Volume bidding

**Time Allocation:**
- 30% of time: Guru (high-value projects)
- 30% of time: PeoplePerHour (Hourlies and quick projects)
- 30% of time: Bidding and proposals
- 10% of time: Portfolio and profile maintenance

**Income Goals:**
- Month 1-3: $1K-$2K/month combined
- Month 4-6: $2K-$5K/month combined
- Month 7-12: $5K-$10K/month combined
- Year 2+: $10K-$20K+/month from all platforms
