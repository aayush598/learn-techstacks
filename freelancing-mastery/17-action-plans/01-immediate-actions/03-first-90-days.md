# First 90 Days: Freelancing Growth Plan

**Goal:** $10,000+/month consistent revenue, repeatable client acquisition system, strong reputation
**Phases:** Build (Month 1) → Scale (Month 2) → Stabilize (Month 3)

---

## Month 1: Build Foundation (Days 1-30)

*See 02-first-30-days.md for detailed week-by-week plan.*

**Primary Goal:** First paying client & repeatable outreach system
**Revenue Target:** $3,000-$5,000 in signed contracts
**Time Split:** 80% sales, 20% delivery

### Month 1 Milestones

| Week | Focus | Key Metric | Target |
|------|-------|-----------|--------|
| Week 1 | Launch & first outreach | Messages sent | 30 |
| Week 2 | Pipeline building | Calls booked | 5 |
| Week 3 | First closes | Deals signed | 1-2 |
| Week 4 | Systematization | Systems documented | All core |

### Month 1 Deliverables

- [ ] Niche defined, profiles live, offer clear
- [ ] 100+ outreach messages sent
- [ ] 10+ discovery calls completed
- [ ] 5+ proposals sent
- [ ] 2+ clients signed ($3,000+ total)
- [ ] First client delivery completed
- [ ] Outreach system documented
- [ ] CRM with 50+ prospects
- [ ] Proposal + contract templates ready

## Month 2: Scale & Execute (Days 31-60)

**Primary Goal:** Multiple clients, first referrals, cash flow positive
**Revenue Target:** $5,000-$8,000
**Time Split:** 50% sales, 50% delivery

### Month 2 Strategy

**Week 5-6: Stabilize Delivery**
- [ ] Deliver exceptional work for Month 1 clients
- [ ] Collect testimonials immediately upon completion
- [ ] Ask every client for 1-2 referrals
- [ ] Send referral request email to 20 contacts
- [ ] Create case studies from completed work

**Week 7-8: Scale Outreach**
- [ ] Increase rates by 15-25% for new prospects
- [ ] Test 2 outreach channels (LinkedIn, email, communities)
- [ ] Optimize best-performing message templates
- [ ] Reduce total outreach volume, increase targeting quality
- [ ] Start building retainer conversations

### Month 2 Weekly Targets

| Activity | Weekly Target |
|----------|--------------|
| Outreach messages | 30 |
| Discovery calls | 3-5 |
| Proposals sent | 3 |
| Deals closed | 1-2 |
| Client delivery hours | 15-20 |
| Testimonials collected | 1 |

### Month 2 Key Actions

**Sales:**
- [ ] Create a referral script and start using it on every call
- [ ] Follow up with all Month 1 prospects who didn't close
- [ ] Post on LinkedIn 3x/week with client results (anonymized)
- [ ] Join and engage in 3 niche communities

**Delivery:**
- [ ] Create project templates for repeatable work
- [ ] Set up client communication cadence (daily Slack, weekly calls)
- [ ] Start tracking time on all work (even fixed-price)
- [ ] Over-deliver on at least one project

**Business:**
- [ ] Open a separate business bank account
- [ ] Set aside 25-30% of all income for taxes
- [ ] Review rate — are you leaving money on the table?
- [ ] Identify your most profitable service type

### Month 2 Milestones

| Week | Focus | Key Metric | Target |
|------|-------|-----------|--------|
| Week 5 | First referrals | Referrals requested | 5 |
| Week 6 | Delivery excellence | Testimonials | 2 |
| Week 7 | Rate optimization | New rate locked | 15%+ increase |
| Week 8 | Retainer groundwork | Retainer conversations | 2 |

## Month 3: Stabilize & Grow (Days 61-90)

**Primary Goal:** $10,000 month, predictable pipeline, systems running without you
**Revenue Target:** $8,000-$12,000
**Time Split:** 30% sales, 60% delivery, 10% optimization

### Month 3 Strategy

**Week 9-10: Build Predictability**
- [ ] Convert 1 client to monthly retainer
- [ ] Build a repeatable sales process (documented)
- [ ] Create a standardized onboarding process
- [ ] Implement project management system (Trello/Asana/Linear)
- [ ] Build a simple referral partnership with 2-3 complementary freelancers

**Week 11-12: Optimize & Systematize**
- [ ] Review all metrics — what's your actual effective hourly rate?
- [ ] Prune low-value activities (admin, low-quality leads)
- [ ] Raise prices again if demand > capacity
- [ ] Hire or outsource first non-core task (bookkeeping, admin)
- [ ] Build Month 4+ growth plan

### Month 3 Weekly Targets

| Activity | Weekly Target |
|----------|--------------|
| Outreach messages | 15-20 (quality > quantity now) |
| Discovery calls | 2-3 |
| Proposals sent | 2 |
| Deals closed | 1-2 |
| Client delivery hours | 25-30 |
| Retainer conversations | 1-2 |

### Month 3 Key Actions

**Sales:**
- [ ] Shift from cold outreach to referral/inbound majority
- [ ] Update portfolio with Month 1-2 case studies
- [ ] Create a standard proposal template for your most common project
- [ ] Develop 2-3 packaged offerings (e.g., "Website Audit $500", "MVP in 2 Weeks $5k")

**Delivery:**
- [ ] Build templates for recurring deliverables
- [ ] Create SOPs for your most common project type
- [ ] Start tracking client satisfaction (NPS or simple survey)
- [ ] Build a "client portal" (simple Notion page or shared drive)

**Business:**
- [ ] Register as LLC or business entity (consult with accountant)
- [ ] Set up retirement account (SEP IRA or Solo 401k)
- [ ] Get professional liability insurance
- [ ] Hire a bookkeeper or use accounting software

## Month 4+ Trajectory

**If you hit $10k+/month:**

| Month | Target | Strategy |
|-------|--------|----------|
| Month 4 | $12k-15k | Raise rates, add retainer clients |
| Month 5 | $15k-18k | Drop low-value clients, focus on premium |
| Month 6 | $18k-22k | Consider first hire or sub-contractor |
| Month 9 | $25k-30k | Agency model or high-ticket consulting |
| Month 12 | $35k-50k | Multi-person operation or niche authority |

**If you're below $10k:**

| Issue | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| Not enough leads | Double outreach, try new channels, build referral system |
| Low close rate | Improve discovery call, reduce price, offer guarantee |
| Low rates | Raise rates 25% and replace lost clients |
| Bad retention | Improve delivery quality, set expectations better |
| Wrong niche | Pivot to higher-demand, higher-budget market |
| Poor time management | Block scheduling, focus on highest-value activity |
