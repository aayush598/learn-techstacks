# First 30 Days: Freelancing Launch Plan

**Goal:** $3,000-$5,000 in signed contracts, repeatable outreach system, first delivery completed
**Weekly commitment:** 20-30 hours (or full-time if possible)

---

## Week 1: Foundation & First Revenue (Days 1-7)

*See 01-first-7-days.md for day-by-day breakdown.*

**Week 1 Objectives:**
- [ ] Niche defined, profiles live, outreach templates ready
- [ ] 30+ outreach messages sent
- [ ] 3+ discovery calls booked
- [ ] 1+ proposal sent
- [ ] Systems: CRM, booking, payment processing set up

**Key Metric:** Outreach messages sent
**Success Signal:** At least 1 discovery call or response

---

## Week 2: Pipeline Building (Days 8-14)

### Daily Routine

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 8:00-9:00 | Outreach: Send 10 personalized messages |
| 9:00-10:00 | Discovery calls (if booked) |
| 10:00-12:00 | Client work (if you have it) or skill building |
| 12:00-13:00 | Lunch + LinkedIn engagement |
| 13:00-14:00 | Follow-ups on previous outreach |
| 14:00-15:00 | Content creation (post, article, case study) |
| 15:00-16:00 | Admin: CRM, invoicing, proposal refinement |
| 16:00-17:00 | Network: Communities, referrals, partnerships |

### Weekly Goals

- [ ] **Outreach:** 50 messages sent (10/day, Mon-Fri)
- [ ] **Follow-ups:** Follow up with every non-response from Week 1
- [ ] **Calls:** 5 discovery calls booked
- [ ] **Proposals:** 3 proposals sent
- [ ] **Close:** At least 1 signed deal
- [ ] **Content:** 3 LinkedIn posts, 1 article
- [ ] **Network:** Join 2 communities, engage daily
- [ ] **Referrals:** Ask 3 people for referrals

### Key Actions

**Monday:**
- [ ] Follow up with all Week 1 outreach that got no response
- [ ] Send 10 new outreach messages
- [ ] Post on LinkedIn

**Tuesday:**
- [ ] Send 10 new outreach messages
- [ ] Handle any discovery calls
- [ ] Work on proposals for warm leads

**Wednesday:**
- [ ] Send 10 new outreach messages
- [ ] Content: Write and publish 1 article/post
- [ ] Review pipeline — rank leads by probability

**Thursday:**
- [ ] Send 10 new outreach messages
- [ ] Follow up on proposals (use proposal follow-up templates)
- [ ] Network: Engage in 2 communities

**Friday:**
- [ ] Send 10 new outreach messages
- [ ] End-of-week outreach to close strong
- [ ] Week review: wins, losses, lessons

**Weekend:**
- [ ] Catch up on client work if needed
- [ ] Plan Week 3
- [ ] Rest (avoid burnout)

## Week 3: Scaling & Delivery (Days 15-21)

### Focus Areas

1. **50/50 split:** 50% time on sales, 50% on delivery
2. **Refine your pitch:** Based on what's working
3. **First delivery excellence:** Over-deliver for your first client(s)
4. **Testimonials:** Collect them immediately

### Weekly Goals

- [ ] **Outreach:** 50 messages sent
- [ ] **Follow-ups:** Follow up on Week 2 outreach + pending proposals
- [ ] **Calls:** 5 discovery calls
- [ ] **Proposals:** 3 proposals sent
- [ ] **Close:** 1+ signed deal
- [ ] **Delivery:** First milestone complete for signed client
- [ ] **Testimonial:** Request testimonial upon delivery
- [ ] **Referral:** Ask first client for 1 referral

### Key Actions

**If you have paying work:**
- [ ] Deliver early or above scope — blow them away
- [ ] Send daily progress updates
- [ ] At delivery: ask for testimonial + referral

**If you still have no paying work:**
- [ ] Increase outreach volume to 20/day
- [ ] Re-evaluate your niche (too broad? wrong market?)
- [ ] Lower your initial rate to get first client (then raise)
- [ ] Offer a small free audit or consultation to build trust

**Proposal Follow-Up Sequence:**
- [ ] Day 0: Send proposal (immediately after call)
- [ ] Day 3: Soft follow-up — "Any questions?"
- [ ] Day 7: Scarcity — "Capacity filling up"
- [ ] Day 10: Breakup — "Closing this out, reach out when ready"

## Week 4: Systematization & Growth (Days 22-30)

### Focus Areas

1. **Document everything:** Create repeatable systems
2. **Rate increase:** If you discounted for first client, raise for next
3. **Referral system:** Formalize referral requests
4. **Content engine:** Schedule 2 weeks of content
5. **Financial review:** First month revenue vs. goals

### Weekly Goals

- [ ] **Outreach:** 30 messages (you'll have referral pipeline starting)
- [ ] **Follow-ups:** Systematized follow-up sequences
- [ ] **Calls:** 3-5 discovery calls
- [ ] **Proposals:** 2-3 proposals
- [ ] **Close:** 1-2 signed deals
- [ ] **Referral requests:** Send to all past clients and contacts
- [ ] **Systems:** Document your repeatable processes

### Key Actions

**Refine Your Offer:**
- [ ] Analyze which outreach messages got responses — double down
- [ ] Analyze which objections you faced most — prepare better responses
- [ ] Package your most requested service into a standard offer

**Build for Month 2:**
- [ ] Create a referral partner program (affiliate or commission)
- [ ] Build a lead magnet (checklist, guide, assessment)
- [ ] Set up a simple email capture on your site
- [ ] Plan a small paid ad test (LinkedIn or Google)

## End of Month 1 Assessment

### Pass (On Track)
- [ ] $3,000+ in signed contracts
- [ ] Repeatable outreach system running
- [ ] First client delivery complete or in progress
- [ ] Clear niche and offer defined

### Fail (Need Adjustment)
- [ ] Under $1,000 in signed contracts
- [ ] Outreach feels like a grind with no responses
- [ ] No discovery calls happening
- [ ] Unclear what's not working

### Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| No responses to outreach | Change target, change message, increase volume |
| Calls but no proposals | Build more value on calls, clarify pain points |
| Proposals but no closes | Reduce price, add urgency, improve follow-up |
| Clients but no profit | Raise rates for next client, reduce scope creep |
| Overwhelmed | Systematize, drop low-value activities, focus on 1 channel |
| Not enough time | Block 2-hour focus sessions, reduce distractions |
