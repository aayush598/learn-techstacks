# First 7 Days: Freelancing Launch Sprint

**Goal:** Go from zero to first paying client in 7 days.
**Mindset:** Speed over perfection. Done is better than perfect.
**Daily commitment:** 4-6 focused hours (evenings/weekends if you have a job).

---

## Day 1: Foundation Setup (Sunday)

### Morning (2 hours)

**Task 1: Define Your Niche (45 min)**
- [ ] Pick ONE specific service you'll offer
- [ ] Pick ONE specific industry or client type
- [ ] Write your "elevator pitch" in one sentence:
  > "I help [specific client] achieve [specific outcome] by [your service]."

**Task 2: Set Your Rate (15 min)**
- [ ] Research market rates for your niche
- [ ] Set your **minimum viable rate** (the lowest you'll accept)
- [ ] Set your **target rate** (what you'll quote first)
- [ ] Set your **stretch rate** (what you'll quote established clients)

> **Quick rate formula:** (Desired annual income / 1,000) = hourly rate minimum.
> Example: $100k / 1,000 = $100/hr minimum. Target $150/hr.

### Afternoon (2 hours)

**Task 3: Create Your Profile (1 hour)**
- [ ] Update LinkedIn headline to reflect your niche
- [ ] Update LinkedIn "About" section with your elevator pitch + CTA
- [ ] Create/update profile on 2 platforms: [Upwork, Contra, Toptal, or niche job board]

**Task 4: Build Your Portfolio Page (1 hour)**
- [ ] Create a simple landing page (use Carrd, Notion, or your own site)
- [ ] Include: who you help, what you do, past results, CTA to book a call
- [ ] Add 2-3 case studies or project descriptions (even personal projects)

### Evening (1 hour)

**Task 5: Identify 20 Prospects (1 hour)**
- [ ] List 20 companies that match your ideal client profile
- [ ] Find the decision maker's name and email/LinkedIn for each
- [ ] Save to a tracker (spreadsheet or CRM)

---

## Day 2: Outreach Setup (Monday)

### Morning (2 hours)

**Task 1: Write Your Outreach Templates (1 hour)**
- [ ] Write a cold outreach email template (use the template in 16-templates)
- [ ] Write a LinkedIn message version (shortened)
- [ ] Write a follow-up template

**Task 2: Set Up Your Sales Stack (1 hour)**
- [ ] Create a simple CRM (HubSpot free, Notion, or spreadsheet)
- [ ] Set up email tracking (Mixmax free or HubSpot free)
- [ ] Create a Calendly or similar booking link for discovery calls

### Afternoon (2 hours)

**Task 3: Send First 10 Outreach Messages (1.5 hours)**
- [ ] Personalize each message (reference something specific)
- [ ] Send to 10 prospects from your list
- [ ] Track each in your CRM

**Task 4: Post on LinkedIn (30 min)**
- [ ] Write a post about a problem you solve (not a pitch — value)
- [ ] Example: "3 signs your [system] needs an upgrade" or "How we fixed [problem] for [client]"

### Evening (1 hour)

**Task 5: Expand Your Prospect List (1 hour)**
- [ ] Find 10 more prospects
- [ ] Search job boards for freelance contracts matching your niche

---

## Day 3: Pipeline Building (Tuesday)

### Morning (2 hours)

**Task 1: Send Next 10 Outreach Messages (1 hour)**
- [ ] Same process as Day 2 — personalize and send

**Task 2: Respond to 3-5 Job Postings (1 hour)**
- [ ] Find relevant listings on Upwork, We Work Remotely, or niche boards
- [ ] Send tailored proposals (not copy-paste)

### Afternoon (2 hours)

**Task 3: Create Your Proposal Template (1.5 hours)**
- [ ] Use the technical project proposal template from this guide
- [ ] Pre-fill your standard sections (about you, process, terms)
- [ ] Save as a template you can customize per client

**Task 4: Prepare Your Discovery Call Script (30 min)**
- [ ] Review the discovery call script from this guide
- [ ] Practice it once out loud

### Evening (1 hour)

**Task 5: Network (1 hour)**
- [ ] Join 2 relevant Slack/Discord communities in your niche
- [ ] Introduce yourself (not a pitch — "hey, I specialize in X, happy to help")
- [ ] Engage with 3 posts from potential clients on LinkedIn

---

## Day 4: Lead Conversion (Wednesday)

### Morning (2 hours)

**Task 1: Follow Up on Outreach (1 hour)**
- [ ] Follow up with anyone who opened but didn't reply (use follow-up templates)
- [ ] Send LinkedIn connection requests to prospects

**Task 2: Prospect Research (1 hour)**
- [ ] Deep-dive research on your 3 warmest leads
- [ ] Find 1-2 specific insights you can use in follow-up

### Afternoon (2 hours)

**Task 3: Prepare For & Handle Discovery Calls (2 hours)**
- [ ] If you have calls scheduled — prep for each
- [ ] If no calls yet — send another 5 outreach messages
- [ ] Record your discovery call (with permission) for self-review

### Evening (1 hour)

**Task 4: Content Marketing (1 hour)**
- [ ] Write a short article or thread on a problem you solve
- [ ] Publish on LinkedIn or Twitter/X
- [ ] Engage with comments

---

## Day 5: First Proposal (Thursday)

### Morning (2 hours)

**Task 1: Follow Up (1 hour)**
- [ ] Follow up on any unanswered outreach
- [ ] Respond to any inbound interest

**Task 2: Write a Proposal for a Qualified Lead (1 hour)**
- [ ] If you have a qualified prospect — write their proposal
- [ ] Use the technical project proposal template
- [ ] Send it by end of day

### Afternoon (2 hours)

**Task 3: Build Credibility Content (1 hour)**
- [ ] Create a simple case study from a past project (or personal project)
- [ ] Format: Problem → Solution → Result (with numbers)

**Task 4: Pipeline Review (1 hour)**
- [ ] Review your CRM/tracker
- [ ] Categorize leads: Hot / Warm / Cold / Dead
- [ ] Plan next actions for each

### Evening (1 hour)

**Task 5: Upskill (1 hour)**
- [ ] Identify 1 skill gap that's blocking sales
- [ ] Spend 1 hour closing that gap (tutorial, documentation, practice)

---

## Day 6: Close & Deliver (Friday)

### Morning (2 hours)

**Task 1: Follow Up on Proposals (1 hour)**
- [ ] Follow up on any proposals you've sent
- [ ] Handle objections using the objection handling scripts

**Task 2: Continue Outreach (1 hour)**
- [ ] Send 5 more personalized outreach messages
- [ ] Keep pipeline full

### Afternoon (2 hours)

**Task 3: Pre-Close Activities (1 hour)**
- [ ] Prepare your contract template (use the freelance contract template)
- [ ] Prepare your invoice template
- [ ] Set up your payment processing (Stripe, PayPal, Wise, or bank)

**Task 4: Send Follow-Ups (1 hour)**
- [ ] Follow up on LinkedIn connections
- [ ] Follow up on any pending conversations
- [ ] Thank anyone who gave you time this week

### Evening (1 hour)

**Task 5: Week 1 Review (1 hour)**
- [ ] Review what worked and what didn't
- [ ] Count: Outreach sent / Responses / Calls booked / Proposals sent / Deals closed
- [ ] Adjust strategy for Week 2

---

## Day 7: Systems & Optimization (Saturday)

### Morning (2 hours)

**Task 1: Process Documentation (1 hour)**
- [ ] Document your client intake process
- [ ] Document your delivery process
- [ ] Create checklists for repeatable tasks

**Task 2: Automate Where Possible (1 hour)**
- [ ] Set up email auto-responders or templates
- [ ] Create proposal template variations
- [ ] Set up bookkeeping/tracking system

### Afternoon (2 hours)

**Task 3: Content Batch (1 hour)**
- [ ] Write 5 LinkedIn posts you can schedule over the next 2 weeks
- [ ] Create 1 piece of lead magnet content (checklist, guide, template)

**Task 4: Network Expansion (1 hour)**
- [ ] Identify 5 more potential referral sources
- [ ] Schedule 1 coffee chat / informational interview per week moving forward

### Evening (1 hour)

**Task 5: Plan Week 2 (1 hour)**
- [ ] Set targets for Week 2: [#] outreaches, [#] calls, [#] proposals
- [ ] Block time on calendar for outreach, delivery, and admin
- [ ] Identify your biggest bottleneck and plan to address it

## If You Get No Responses (Troubleshooting)

**Problem: No replies to outreach**
- [ ] Refine your targeting (too broad?)
- [ ] Improve your subject line (test different formats)
- [ ] Shorten your message (under 100 words)
- [ ] Add more specific value (reference their specific situation)
- [ ] Increase volume (send 20/day instead of 10)

**Problem: Calls but no proposals**
- [ ] You're not establishing enough value on the call
- [ ] You're not identifying the real pain point
- [ ] Review your discovery call technique

**Problem: Proposals but no closes**
- [ ] Your price may be too high (or too low = perceived low quality)
- [ ] Your proposal may lack specificity
- [ ] You're not handling objections effectively
- [ ] Follow up more persistently (but respectfully)
