## Phase 1: Build the Agency Infrastructure (Days 1-60)

### You're not a freelancer anymore. You're building a business.

**Week 1-2: Legal & Financial Structure**

- [ ] Form LLC or S-Corp (consult with accountant on best structure)
- [ ] Open business bank accounts and credit cards
- [ ] Set up payroll system (Gusto, ADP, or Rippling)
- [ ] Get business insurance ($2M general liability + errors & omissions)
- [ ] Create accounting system with COA (chart of accounts)
- [ ] Establish contractor agreements and W-9 processes
- [ ] Set up 401k or profit-sharing plan

**Week 3-4: Brand & Positioning**

- [ ] Rebrand from personal name to company name
- [ ] Build agency website (case studies, team, services, process)
- [ ] Create sales deck for enterprise clients
- [ ] Develop ROI calculator and value framework
- [ ] Create standard pricing for all service lines

## Enterprise Sales Playbook

### How to Win $50k+ Deals

**The buying committee includes:**
- Economic buyer (CFO/CEO — cares about ROI)
- Technical buyer (CTO/VPE — cares about quality/stability)
- User buyer (Product/Eng manager — cares about delivery)
- Champion (your internal advocate)

**Key differences from $5k/$10k sales:**

| $10k Deal | $50k Deal |
|-----------|-----------|
| CTO decides alone | 3-5 stakeholders involved |
| 1-2 week sales cycle | 4-12 week sales cycle |
| Send proposal → close | RFP → demos → references → negotiation |
| Price-based objections | Risk-based objections |
| Single email follow-up | Multi-touch campaign |
| You sell | You + sales team sell |

**Enterprise sales process:**
1. **Identify** — Target account research + warm intro
2. **Connect** — Initial conversation with champion
3. **Discover** — Deep discovery with all stakeholders
4. **Solution design** — Custom proposal + technical validation
5. **Proposal** — Formal proposal + pricing
6. **Negotiation** — Legal review, security review, references
7. **Close** — Contract signed, kickoff scheduled

### RFP Responses

- [ ] Create RFP response template library
- [ ] Maintain security/compliance documentation package
- [ ] Have case studies for each industry vertical
- [ ] Prepare reference list (with permission)
- [ ] Develop pricing model that's competitive but profitable

## Common Pitfalls at $50k Level

| Pitfall | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Not delegating enough | You should be 0% on delivery by now. If not, fix immediately. |
| Cash flow crunch | 50% upfront, milestone payments, net 15, chase late payers |
| Wrong hires | Hire slow, fire fast. 30-60-90 day performance plans. |
| Client concentration | No single client > 25% of revenue |
| Margin erosion | Track utilization weekly. Drop low-margin work. |
| Sales dependency on you | Hire salesperson. Build inbound system. |
| Brand too personal | Transition to company brand. You're the founder, not the product. |
| Neglecting culture | With 5+ people, culture matters. Invest in it. |

## $50k → $100k+ Transition Triggers

You're ready to scale beyond $50k when:

- [ ] Sales system runs without you
- [ ] Delivery system runs without you
- [ ] You have 3+ enterprise clients
- [ ] You have a leadership team (PM, sales lead, delivery lead)
- [ ] Margins are consistently 50%+
- [ ] You have a clear differentiator in the market

**Scaling options:**
1. **New service lines** — Add complementary services
2. **New markets** — Geographic expansion or new verticals
3. **Acquisitions** — Buy a small agency for talent/contracts
4. **Product/SAAS** — Build a product from your agency work
5. **Franchise/network** — Replicate your model in other markets
