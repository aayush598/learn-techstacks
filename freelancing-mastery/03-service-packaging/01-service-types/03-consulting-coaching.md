# Consulting & Coaching Engagements

## Overview

High-ticket consulting and coaching is the fastest path to $500-$5,000+ per session with minimal delivery time. You sell your expertise, judgement, and frameworks — not your execution hours. A single consulting engagement can net $10k-$50k for 10-20 hours of work. This is the highest-leverage service model in freelancing.

## Consulting vs Coaching vs Advisory

| Model | What You Sell | Output | Typical Fee |
|-------|--------------|--------|-------------|
| Consulting | Your expertise + recommendations | Report, strategy, plan, assessment | $2k-$20k |
| Coaching | Your guidance + accountability | Sessions, frameworks, feedback | $500-$5k/session |
| Advisory | Your judgement + network | Ongoing strategic counsel | $3k-$15k/mo |
| Mentoring | Your experience + wisdom | Structured learning, career guidance | $200-$1k/session |

## The High-Ticket Consulting Model

### What You Charge
- **Strategy session (60-90 min):** $500-$2,000
- **Deep-dive consulting (half-day):** $2,000-$5,000
- **Full consulting engagement (multi-session):** $5,000-$25,000
- **Annual tech advisory: $10,000-$50,000/year**

### Why Clients Pay These Rates
They're not paying for your time. They're paying for:
- **Speed** — You solve in 2 hours what would take them 2 weeks
- **Experience** — You've made the mistakes so they don't have to
- **Network** — You know who to talk to, what tools to use
- **Objectivity** — You see the forest, not just the trees
- **Confidence** — Your recommendation de-risks their decision
- **Accountability** — You hold them to action, not just intention

## Consulting Offer Types

### 1. Tech Strategy Consulting
**For:** Founders, CTOs, product leaders
**Problem:** "What tech stack should we use?" "Should we build or buy?" "Why is our system falling apart?"

**Deliverable:** Technology strategy document + presentation
**Structure:**
- Pre-work: audit current systems, stakeholder interviews
- Session: findings presentation, recommendations
- Post-work: written strategy document, roadmap

**Fee:** $5k-$15k
**Example outcome:** "We recommended migrating from a monolith to microservices, saved the client $200k/year in infrastructure costs."

### 2. Architecture Review
**For:** Engineering teams, startups raising Series A
**Problem:** "Is our architecture going to scale?" "We keep having production outages."

**Deliverable:** Architecture review report with prioritized findings
**Structure:**
- Codebase review (async)
- Infrastructure review
- Interview with engineering team
- Written report with findings + remediation plan
- Presentation to stakeholders

**Fee:** $3k-$10k
**Example outcome:** "Identified 5 critical bottlenecks. Client fixed them in 2 months, scaled to 10x traffic without issues."

### 3. Code Audit / Security Review
**For:** Companies before funding, acquisition, or launch
**Problem:** "Is our code secure?" "Are we shipping quality?"

**Deliverable:** Audit report with severity-rated findings
**Structure:**
- Static analysis + manual review
- Vulnerability assessment
- Quality metrics
- Report with remediation priorities

**Fee:** $2k-$8k (depending on codebase size)

### 4. Fractional Executive Consulting
**For:** Pre-seed to Series A startups
**Problem:** "We need technical leadership but can't afford full-time CTO."

**Deliverable:** Monthly strategic guidance + execution oversight
**Structure:**
- Weekly 1:1 strategy call
- Monthly board/ investor update
- Slack/email access
- Hands-on for critical decisions

**Fee:** $5k-$15k/month (see retainer guide for details)

### 5. Technical Due Diligence
**For:** VCs, PE firms, acquirers
**Problem:** "Is this company's tech worth the valuation?"

**Deliverable:** Due diligence report
**Structure:**
- Codebase and architecture review
- Team assessment
- Technical debt evaluation
- Risk assessment
- Written report + presentation

**Fee:** $10k-$50k

### 6. AI/ML Strategy Consulting
**For:** Companies exploring AI adoption
**Problem:** "How do we use AI in our business?" "What should we build vs buy?"

**Deliverable:** AI opportunity assessment + roadmap
**Structure:**
- Business process audit
- AI opportunity identification
- Feasibility assessment
- Implementation roadmap
- ROI projections

**Fee:** $5k-$20k

### 7. Performance Optimization Consulting
**For:** SaaS companies with performance issues
**Problem:** "Our app is slow, users are churning."

**Deliverable:** Performance audit + optimization strategy
**Structure:**
- Load testing
- Bottleneck identification
- Optimization recommendations
- Implementation support (optional)

**Fee:** $3k-$8k

## How to Get Consulting Clients

### 1. The "Problem First" Approach
Identify a specific problem, create content about it, offer the fix.

```
Content: "5 Signs Your Startup Has Technical Debt That 
         Will Kill Your Series A"
CTA: "Book a 30-min Tech Debt Assessment: $500"
```

### 2. The Audit-to-Engagement Funnel
- Free/cheap audit (30-min call, $0-$500)
- Low-ticket assessment ($500-$2k)
- Full consulting engagement ($5k-$25k)

```
Step 1: Free 30-min call → Identify pain point
Step 2: Paid $500 Tech Assessment → Deliverable with findings
Step 3: $10k Strategy Engagement → Full solution
```

### 3. The Referral Engine
After every consulting engagement:
- Ask for 3 referrals
- Offer a referral fee (15-20%)
- Send a "done-for-you" referral email they can forward

### 4. The "I Know Someone Who..." Pattern
When you help a client, they tell their VC, their peers, their network. Be so good that people can't stop talking about you.

### 5. Content Marketing for High-Ticket
- Write deep technical articles (not surface-level)
- Publish case studies with real numbers
- Speak at conferences / meetups
- Host workshops (free for qualified leads)
- Build a newsletter (500+ warm subscribers)

## Pricing Consulting Engagements

### Value-Based Pricing Formula
```
Your fee = % of value delivered
         = 20-30% of projected value
         = $X,XXX to $XX,XXX

Example:
- Your recommendation saves client $100k/year
- Your fee: $20k-30k
- ROI: 3x-5x for the client
```

### Retainer-Based Consulting
```
Monthly: $5k-$15k/month
Initial commitment: 3-6 months
Renewal: month-to-month after initial term
```

### Project-Based Consulting
```
Fixed fee for defined deliverable:
- Strategy Document: $5k
- Architecture Review: $8k
- Due Diligence: $15k
- Full AI Strategy: $20k
```

### Session-Based Coaching
```
Single session: $500-$2k
Package of 4: $1.5k-$6k (10% discount)
Package of 12: $4k-$18k (20% discount)
VIP Day (4-6 hours): $3k-$10k
```

## The Consulting Engagement Flow

### Step 1: Discovery Call (Free, 30 min)
Goal: Qualify, build rapport, identify pain
- What's going on? Why now?
- What have you tried?
- What would solving this mean for you/your business?
- What's the cost of NOT solving it?
- Budget range: "Have you allocated budget for this?"
- Decision process: "Who else needs to be involved?"

### Step 2: Proposal (Paid if possible, $0-$500 for the proposal)
- Summary of understanding
- Proposed approach
- Deliverables
- Investment
- Timeline
- Guarantee

### Step 3: Engagement
- Pre-work (information gathering)
- Core sessions (2-6 sessions over 2-8 weeks)
- Between-session work (client does homework)
- Final deliverable

### Step 4: Follow-through
- Implementation support (optional, additional fee)
- Check-in at 30/60/90 days
- Upsell to retainer or ongoing advisory

## The Guarantee Strategy

High-ticket consulting needs strong guarantees to overcome risk objection.

### The "Double Your Money" Guarantee
```
"If you implement my recommendations and don't see 
at least 2x ROI within 90 days, I'll refund 100% 
of your fee."

Requirements: 
- You must implement all recommendations
- You track results honestly
- You allow me to use as case study
```

### The "Satisfaction or Keep Working" Guarantee
```
"If you're not satisfied after our engagement, 
I'll keep working with you for free until you are."
```

### The "No Questions Asked" Guarantee
```
"30-day money-back guarantee. No questions asked."
```

## The Consultative Sales Process

High-ticket consulting = high-ticket sales. You must sell consultatively.

### Week 1: Build Curiosity
- Share valuable content about their problem
- Engage with their content
- Offer a free resource

### Week 2: Offer a Low-Risk Entry
- "I noticed you're dealing with [problem]. Would you be open to a 15-minute brainstorm?"

### Week 3: The "Consultative Call"
Don't pitch. Diagnose.
- Ask better questions than they've heard
- Show understanding they haven't seen
- Share a relevant case study
- If fit, present your engagement

### Week 4: Close or Nurture
- If yes: send proposal, follow up every 2-3 days
- If no: "No problem. Here's a free resource. Let's check in next quarter."
- If maybe: Offer a paid mini-engagement ($500-$1k) to start

## Handling Objections

### "That's expensive"
Response: "You're right, it's an investment. The question is — what's the cost of NOT solving this? You mentioned it's costing you $X/month in [lost revenue / wasted time / stress]. My engagement eliminates that cost. In 3 months, this pays for itself."

### "I need to think about it"
Response: "Absolutely. What specifically gives you hesitation? Is it the investment, the timing, or something else?"

Then ask: "On a scale of 1-10, how likely are you to go ahead with this?"

### "Can you do it for less?"
Response: "I have three pricing tiers. The one I recommended gives you [specific outcomes]. If we reduce scope to [reduced deliverable], I can do it for [reduced price]. Which approach works better for you?"

### "I can get someone cheaper on Upwork"
Response: "You absolutely can. And they'll probably give you good advice too. The difference is I've done this for [X clients] and my recommendations typically generate [Y% ROI]. If cheaper works, go for it. But if you want certainty and speed, I'm here."

## Summary Rules

1. Never sell by the hour — sell by the outcome
2. Discovery calls are for diagnosing, not pitching
3. Always offer a guarantee (removes risk)
4. Create a framework (gives you authority)
5. Package into programs, not hourly sessions
6. Case studies are your #1 sales tool
7. Higher prices attract better clients
8. Referral engine must be built from day one
9. Over-deliver on every engagement
10. Raise prices after every 3-5 engagements
11. Write every day (content builds authority)
12. Get on podcasts (trust transfers fast)
