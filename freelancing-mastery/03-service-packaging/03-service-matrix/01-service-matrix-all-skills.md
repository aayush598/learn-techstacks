# Complete Service Matrix — All Skill Areas

## How to Use This Matrix

Complete pricing tiers for every major skill area in tech freelancing. Each row has three pricing tiers (Basic/Pro/Premium). Adjust ±30% for your market and experience.

Pricing notes:
- **T1 (Basic):** Entry-level, template-based, minimal customization
- **T2 (Pro):** Custom work, moderate complexity, priority delivery
- **T3 (Premium):** Full customization, white-glove, ongoing value
- Enterprise clients: multiply by 1.5-2x

## Pricing Adjustment Guidelines

### By Client Type
| Client | Multiplier | Example |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| Startup (under $1M) | 0.7x | $5k to $3.5k |
| Small business ($1-10M) | 1.0x | Base price |
| Mid-market ($10-100M) | 1.5x | $5k to $7.5k |
| Enterprise ($100M+) | 2.0-3.0x | $5k to $10-15k |
| Agency (reselling) | 1.2-1.5x | $5k to $6-7.5k |

### By Project Complexity
| Factor | Adjustment |
|--------|-----------|
| Well-defined scope | Base price |
| Ambiguous requirements | +30% |
| Multiple stakeholders | +20% |
| Tight deadline (under 50% normal) | +50% |
| Legacy system integration | +30% |
| Mission-critical system | +50% |

## Summary Rules

1. These are starting points — adjust for your market and experience
2. Always offer three tiers (Good/Better/Best)
3. Premium tier anchors value perception for lower tiers
4. Enterprise clients get 1.5-2x pricing
5. Adjust based on complexity, timeline, and your demand
6. Track win rates per tier and optimize pricing monthly
7. When fully booked, raise all tiers by 20%
8. Develop case studies at each tier to justify pricing
9. Retainers are 2-5x more valuable than one-off projects
10. Use the retainer model to smooth revenue and build relationships
