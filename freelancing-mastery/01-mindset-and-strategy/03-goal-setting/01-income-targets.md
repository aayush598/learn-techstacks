# Income Targets: Setting and Achieving Financial Goals

## The Target-Setting Framework

### Step 1: Calculate Your Minimum Viable Income (MVI)
```
Monthly Expenses: $X
Savings Goal: $Y
Taxes (25-35%): $Z
MVI = X + Y + Z
```

### Step 2: Set Income Tiers

| Tier | Monthly Income | Timeline | Strategy |
|------|---------------|----------|----------|
| Survival | Covers expenses | 30-60 days | Take any decent project |
| Comfortable | 2x expenses | 60-90 days | Be selective, increase rates |
| Thriving | 5x expenses | 90-180 days | Premium clients only |
| Freedom | 10x+ expenses | 180-365 days | Agency/retainers/products |

### Step 3: Break Down to Daily Actions

**Example: $10,000/month target**
- At $5,000/project → Need 2 projects/month = 1 project every 2 weeks
- At $200/hr → Need 50 billable hours/month = ~12.5 hrs/week
- Each project requires: 10 proposals → 5 responses → 3 calls → 1-2 closes
- Daily: Send 3-5 proposals + follow up on previous ones

## Adjusting Targets

- If you're not hitting outreach numbers → block time, remove distractions
- If outreach works but calls don't convert → improve qualification, fix pitch
- If calls convert but at low prices → implement value-based pricing
- If projects deliver but no repeat work → add upsell and retainer offers

## The Income Escalation Path

| Year | Monthly Target | Hourly Rate | Project Size | Client Type |
|------|---------------|-------------|--------------|-------------|
| Year 1 | $3k-$8k | $50-$100 | $1k-$5k | Small businesses, startups |
| Year 2 | $8k-$20k | $100-$200 | $5k-$20k | Growing startups, agencies |
| Year 3 | $20k-$50k+ | $200-$500 | $20k-$100k+ | Enterprises, funded startups |

## Key Principle

Set targets that scare you but are achievable. A $10k/month target is ~$500/day for 20 days. If you can't find someone to pay you $500 for your expertise, you're either in the wrong niche or not communicating value effectively.
