# Tracking Metrics: Measure What Matters

## The 7 Metrics That Predict Your Income

### 1. Outreach Volume
**What:** Number of proposals sent, emails sent, DMs sent per week
**Target:** 50+ per week minimum
**Why:** This is your top-of-funnel. Volume covers for imperfect targeting.

### 2. Response Rate
**What:** % of outreach that gets a response
**Target:** 20-40% (varies by channel)
**Why:** Measures message quality and targeting accuracy

### 3. Call Booking Rate
**What:** % of responses that turn into discovery calls
**Target:** 30-50%
**Why:** Shows if your initial conversation is compelling enough

### 4. Call-to-Close Rate
**What:** % of discovery calls that result in a project
**Target:** 30-50% for qualified leads
**Why:** Measures sales skills and client qualification

### 5. Average Project Value (APV)
**What:** Average $ per project
**Target:** Increase by 20%+ per quarter
**Why:** This is the fastest lever to pull for income growth

### 6. Client Acquisition Cost (CAC)
**What:** Time/money spent to acquire one client
**Target:** Reduce by 50% per quarter
**Why:** Efficiency determines scalability

### 7. Effective Hourly Rate
**What:** Total revenue / total hours worked (including non-billable)
**Target:** $100+ first 3 months, $200+ first year
**Why:** True measure of your earning efficiency

## Weekly Review Process

Every Sunday, review:
1. **Outreach:** Did I hit 50+ touches?
2. **Pipeline:** How many active deals? Total pipeline value?
3. **Conversion:** Where is my biggest leak?
4. **Revenue:** Actual vs target
5. **Rate:** Effective hourly rate trending up?

## Leading vs Lagging Indicators

| Leading (Predict Future) | Lagging (Show Past) |
|-------------------------|-------------------|
| Outreach volume | Revenue |
| Responses received | Projects closed |
| Calls booked | Profit margin |
| Pipeline value | Client satisfaction |

Focus on leading indicators. They tell you if you'll hit your targets before it's too late.

## The 80/20 of Metrics

If you can only track 3 things:
1. Proposals sent this week
2. Pipeline value ($)
3. Effective hourly rate

## Common Metric Mistakes

- **Tracking hours instead of outcomes** → Leads to hourly thinking
- **Not tracking non-billable time** → Underestimates true cost
- **Vanity metrics (profile views, likes)** → Don't correlate with income
- **Inconsistent tracking** → Can't improve what you don't measure
