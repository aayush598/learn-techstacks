# 90-Day Plan: From Zero to $5k+ Month Freelancing

## Overview

This 90-day plan is designed for someone who already has technical skills but needs a freelancing business system. Each phase builds on the previous one.

## Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1-30)

### Week 1: Setup & Positioning
- Day 1-2: Define your niche (use 02-skill-stacking to combine 2+ skills)
- Day 3-4: Create your service packages (3 tiers: Good/Better/Best)
- Day 5-7: Build your portfolio website and update LinkedIn

### Week 2: Platform Setup
- Day 8-10: Create/optimize Upwork profile with niche focus
- Day 11-12: Create profiles on 2 additional platforms (Toptal, gun.io, etc.)
- Day 13-14: Prepare 5 case studies from past work or personal projects

### Week 3: Outreach Systems
- Day 15-17: Set up CRM (HubSpot free tier or similar)
- Day 18-20: Write 5 outreach templates (email + LinkedIn)
- Day 21: Create lead list of 100 target clients

### Week 4: First Proposals
- Day 22-24: Send 20 proposals on Upwork
- Day 25-27: Send 30 cold emails/LinkedIn DMs
- Day 28-30: Follow up on all sent proposals

## Phase 2: Momentum (Days 31-60)

### Week 5-6: Refine & Scale Outreach
- Analyze what's working (which proposals convert, which emails get replies)
- Double down on best-performing channel
- Send 50+ proposals/outreaches per week
- A/B test subject lines and opening hooks

### Week 7-8: Close First Clients
- Focus on discovery calls and demos
- Use the pricing frameworks to avoid undervaluing yourself
- Get first 2-3 clients at $2k-$5k per project minimum

### Week 9: Delivery Excellence
- Over-deliver on first projects
- Set up communication rhythms (weekly updates, transparent progress)
- Collect testimonials mid-project, not just at end

## Phase 3: Acceleration (Days 61-90)

### Week 10-11: Raise Rates
- Increase rates by 25-50% for new prospects
- Convert first project clients to retainers
- Get referrals from happy clients

### Week 12: Systematize
- Document SOPs for everything
- Start looking for subcontractors if overbooked
- Plan next 90 days targeting $10k/month

## Key Metrics to Track

| Metric | Week 1-4 Target | Week 5-8 Target | Week 9-12 Target |
|--------|----------------|----------------|-----------------|
| Proposals sent | 50 | 100 | 150 |
| Response rate | 20%+ | 30%+ | 40%+ |
| Discovery calls | 5 | 15 | 25 |
| Projects closed | 0-1 | 2-4 | 5-8 |
| Monthly revenue | $0 | $2k-$5k | $5k-$10k |
| Average project value | N/A | $2k+ | $5k+ |

## Daily Non-Negotiables

- 2 hours of outreach/proposal work (morning)
- 1 hour of skill/content development
- Track EVERYTHING (leads, outreach, responses, calls)
- End each day knowing your #1 priority for tomorrow
