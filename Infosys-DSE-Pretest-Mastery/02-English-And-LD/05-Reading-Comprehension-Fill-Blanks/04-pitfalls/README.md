## Quick rules

- Name the trap before you reject the option.
- Reject universals and quantifier upgrades.
- Under-claim rather than over-claim.

## Common traps

- Being persuaded by a fluent, vocabulary-matching option.
- Missing a quantifier swap.
- Spending 3 minutes on one item.
