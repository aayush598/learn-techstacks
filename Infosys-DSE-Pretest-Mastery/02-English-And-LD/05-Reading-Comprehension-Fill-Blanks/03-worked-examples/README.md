## In one sentence
**Three complete passages solved end to end — workplace, general knowledge, and a paragraph-completion cloze — with every option judged.**

## Why the pre-test tests this

- Seeing the evidence line identified for every answer teaches the habit, not just the verdict.
- The third passage shows the most dangerous distractor shape: a topic-jump that reuses the passage's own words.

## Quick rules

- Locate, then eliminate, then confirm.
- Reference and tone beat topic in paragraph completion.
- An option reusing the passage's vocabulary may still be a trap.

## Common traps

- Choosing the punchy "conclusion" option in a cloze.
- Accepting a plausible-in-life answer that the passage never states.
