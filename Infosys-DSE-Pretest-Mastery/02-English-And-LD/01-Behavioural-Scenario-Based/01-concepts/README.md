## In one sentence
**These three files give you the mental model: the four option archetypes, the 5-step professional response framework, and how best/least and motivational questions are scored.**

## Why the pre-test tests this

- The behavioural sub-type is 5–8 questions of the 15 in Section 2 — the largest single block of the section.
- These files are the theory. Read them once, then drill in [`03-worked-examples`](../03-worked-examples/README.md).

## Quick rules

- Read the four-archetype table until you can recite it without looking.
- Every professional answer = recipient + action + closure.
- "Least appropriate" = the option that creates a new problem.

## Common traps

- Skipping the concept files and drilling only the examples — you will pattern-match on the story, not the shape.

## Checklist

- [ ] I can name all four archetypes in under 30 seconds.
- [ ] I know the 5 framework steps in order.
- [ ] I know the difference between "least professional" and "least appropriate".
