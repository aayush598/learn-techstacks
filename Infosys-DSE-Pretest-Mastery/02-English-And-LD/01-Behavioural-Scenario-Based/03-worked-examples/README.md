## Quick rules

- Attempt each question with the timer running before reading the solution.
- Check your answer against the archetype, not against your instinct.

## Common traps

- Reading the answer first and recognising your own reasoning in it — that is fluency, not skill.

## Checklist

- [ ] I answered all 10 before opening any solution.
- [ ] I scored at least 8/10 timed.
- [ ] I can explain why each rejected option fails, out loud.
