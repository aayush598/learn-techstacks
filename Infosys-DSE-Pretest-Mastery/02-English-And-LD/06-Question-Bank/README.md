# Question Bank — Section 2 (English & L&D)

> Section 2 · English & L&D · Question Bank ·  _64 questions · ~105 min_

**What this is:** 64 practice questions across the five sub-types of Section 2, in the format the pre-test uses.
Work them on paper, under a timer, before you touch anything else.

## In one sentence
**Section 2 is 15 questions in 22 minutes, and this bank gives you 64 questions with real options, definite answers, and a one-line reason for every answer.**

## Why the pre-test tests this

- The pre-test does not reward reading. It rewards **answering under a 90-second cap**.
- 39 of 60 questions is the pass mark, so Section 2's 15 questions are roughly 4 marks of headroom. Getting
  11 of 15 in Section 2 is usually enough if the other sections are strong.
- Every practice question you do without a timer is a question you have not actually practised.

**Note on the mix:** the paper gives roughly 40% behavioural and 30% reading. This bank gives you 37%
behavioural, 16% etiquette, 31% error correction + vocabulary, and 16% reading. Do the banks in order, then
return to the reading bank — reading is the sub-type that decays fastest without repetition.

## How to use this bank

```
ROUND 1 — untimed, closed book
  Read each question, answer it, and check the key immediately.
  Do NOT look at the key before answering. Guessing counts as wrong.
  Note the question numbers you got wrong.

ROUND 2 — same questions, 90-second cap, closed book
  Re-do only the ones you missed, with a timer.
  You will discover that "I knew the answer" and "I answered in 40 seconds" are different skills.

ROUND 3 — mixed, 90-second cap
  Do one full bank in order, timed end to end.
  This is the round that matches the paper.
```

## The four rules that decide most of these marks

1. 🔥 **Behavioural:** pick the option that names the fact, states what you will do, and closes the loop. It is
   usually the **most specific** option, not the most emotional one.
2. 🔥 **Error correction:** exactly one segment breaks a rule. Test the **verb-bearing segments first**.
3. 🔥 **Vocabulary:** the **sentence** decides the meaning, not the dictionary.
4. 🔥 **Reading:** locate the evidence line **before** you read any option.

## The 90-second rule

| If you are at… | Do this |
|----------------|---------|
| 30 seconds | Re-read the question stem and identify the question type |
| 60 seconds | Eliminate two options on a stated rule, not a feeling |
| 80 seconds | Make the best choice, mark it, move on |
| 90 seconds | Hard stop — flag it and return in the final review |
