## In one sentence
**Two files: the 26 confused word pairs with a distinguishing test each, and the synonym/antonym/one-word-substitution vocabulary pool.**

## Why the pre-test tests this

- Confused pairs are the most reliable vocabulary marks in Indian pre-tests. The list is finite.
- The word lists are pure recall — no reasoning required, so the payoff is high for the time spent.

## Files

| File | Contents |

## Quick rules

- Learn each pair **with its test**.
- The sentence decides; the dictionary does not.
- ensure → thing, assure → person, insure → money.

## Common traps

- Picking the spelling twin of the target word.
- Answering from the abstract definition.

## Checklist

- [ ] I can recite the 6 highest-frequency pairs.
- [ ] I know the idiom rule (literal meaning).
