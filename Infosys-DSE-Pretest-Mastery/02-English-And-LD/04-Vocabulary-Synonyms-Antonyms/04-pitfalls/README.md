## Quick rules

- "Is this option a synonym of the target?" — ask it first on antonym items.
- Is it the FULL opposite, or a milder version?
- Never pick the spelling twin.

## Common traps

- Choosing a synonym on an antonym item.
- Confusing the axis of opposition (speed vs care).
