## In one sentence
**Two execution files: the full 8-step scan with a time budget for every step, and the 45-second fast scan for when you are behind schedule.**

## Why the pre-test tests this

- Section 2 is 15 questions in 22 minutes. Error correction has to be 45 seconds, not 90.
- A fixed order (agreement before articles) is what makes the scan fast.

## Quick rules

- Sentence first, options second. Always.
- Verb first, then agreement, then tense.
- 45-second budget; flag and move at 60 seconds.

## Common traps

- Re-reading the sentence instead of scanning it.
- Stopping after the fast scan when it finds nothing.
