# 04 - Test Day Strategy

> Section 00 · Exam Blueprint · Test Day Strategy · _Estimated reading time: 11 min_
> **Prerequisite:** [../README.md](../README.md)

## In one sentence
**On test day, your method — not your knowledge — decides the outcome: apply the 90-second rule, flag and review, and never let one question own your time.**

## Why the pre-test tests this
- **Pressure:** 60 questions in 90 minutes rewards procedure over improvisation.
- **Flagging:** lets you defer hard questions without losing them.
- **Stuck management:** prevents the "one more step" cascade.
- **Consistency:** a fixed plan removes day-of indecision.

## Core concept

### The test-day loop
```
READ STEM → 90-SECOND RULE → BANK CHEAP MARKS → FLAG HARD → FINAL-15 REVIEW
```

### What to have decided before you sit
| Decision | Recommended choice |
|----------|-------------------|
| Rule | 90-second rule (know ≤45s, eliminate 45-70s, guess+flag 70-90s, never >90s) |
| Order | Cheap marks across the paper first (if order allows) |
| Flagging | Flag only genuinely uncertain questions |
| Review | Final 15 min, flagged only |
| Guessing | Match the live negative-marking policy |

### The three failure modes this folder prevents
| Failure | Prevention |
|---------|-----------|
| Over-running one question | 90-second rule |
| Forgetting uncertain questions | Flagging |
| Freezing when stuck | Pre-decided unstick procedure |

## Worked example
**Meera's test day:** she reads the instructions, notes there is no negative marking, and fills every question. She flags 6 hard ones, banks DBMS/SQL and behavioural English early, protects Java time, and reviews flags in the last 12 minutes. Result: 41 → clears. Compare her earlier mock: 37 from the same knowledge, lost to unplanned pacing.

## Quick rules
- **Follow the 90-second rule.**
- **Bank cheap marks early.**
- **Flag, don't freeze.**
- **Review flagged only, in the final 15 min.**
- **Match live guessing policy.**

## Common traps
- **Improvising order under pressure.**
- **Not using flags.**
- **Using final minutes on answered questions.**
- **Ignoring the live guessing policy.**

## Checklist
- [ ] You've decided your rule, order, and flagging approach.
- [ ] You've practiced the stuck procedure.
- [ ] You know the final-15 review plan.
- [ ] You know your guess rule for the live policy.
- [ ] You've run at least one full 90-minute mock.
