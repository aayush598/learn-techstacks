# 02 - Sectional Analysis

> Section 00 · Exam Blueprint · Sectional Analysis · _Estimated reading time: 11 min_
> **Prerequisite:** [../README.md](../README.md)

## In one sentence
**Each section has a different difficulty distribution and cost-per-mark** — analyze where the cheapest 39 points actually live before you sit.

## Why the pre-test tests this
- **Section asymmetry:** 25 Java questions vs 10 Aptitude; return per minute differs.
- **Cheapest marks:** English & L&D and DBMS/SQL JOIns are often faster points than Aptitude DI.
- **Score model:** you can build 39/60 from specific section contributions.
- **Weakness targeting:** knowing the weight distribution tells you what to fix.

## Core concept

### Section weight → score share
| Section | Q | Share of paper | Marks if fully correct |
|---------|---|----------------|------------------------|
| Aptitude | 10 | 16.7% | 10 |
| English & L&D | 15 | 25.0% | 15 |
| DBMS/SQL | 10 | 16.7% | 10 |
| Java problem-solving | 25 | 41.7% | 25 |
| **Total** | **60** | **100%** | **60** |

### Cheapest-marks map (general guidance)
| Section | Cheapest archetypes | Expensive archetypes |
|---------|---------------------|----------------------|
| Aptitude | data sufficiency, percentages, ranking | long data interpretation, some number series |
| English & L&D | behavioural/professionalism (intended answer), quick error correction | dense grammar nuance |
| DBMS/SQL | ACID, basic theory, simple joins | multi-join query writing |
| Java | compile/execute ID, common output patterns | convoluted inner-class/polymorphism output |

### Source of the 39
| Section | Target correct | Reasoning |
|---------|----------------|-----------|
| Java | 15-16 | ~60% of the biggest pool |
| English & L&D | 10-11 | ~70%, mostly fast |
| DBMS/SQL | 7-8 | ~75%, predictable |
| Aptitude | 5-6 | ~55%, pick easy archetypes |
| **Sum** | **37-41** | Margins safely above 39 |

## Worked example
**Meera (DBMS-strong):** targets Java 13, English 11, DBMS 9, Aptitude 6 = 39. Her risk is Aptitude (weak) and time (Java). Plan: protect DBMS (cheap for her), drill English to bank time, accept 13/25 Java rather than chase 20. This meets the gate with the least wasted effort.

## Quick rules
- **Java is the biggest pool (41.7%).**
- **English & L&D is the fastest section (25%).**
- **DBMS/SQL is the most predictable (16.7%).**
- **Aptitude is the smallest (16.7%): don't over-invest.**
- **Build 39 from section targets, not one section.**

## Common traps
- **Chasing 100% in Aptitude:** low ROI.
- **Neglecting Java:** 25 questions can carry or sink you.
- **Ignoring English:** it's the quickest 15 points if you know intended answers.
- **Assuming equal difficulty across sections.**

## Checklist
- [ ] You know each section's share of the paper.
- [ ] You have a target correct-count per section.
- [ ] You know your cheapest-mark archetypes.
- [ ] You have a plan for your weakest section.
- [ ] Your section targets sum above 39.
