# Score Model: Building 39 From The Sections

> Section 00 · Exam Blueprint · Sectional Analysis · _Estimated reading time: 18 min_
> **Prerequisite:** [../README.md](../README.md)

## In one sentence
**You do not need to be good at everything — you need a section-by-section target that sums to at least 39, and then you defend it.**

## Why the pre-test tests this
- **Threshold math:** 39/60 is the gate, and the model shows the easiest path to it for any profile.
- **Resource allocation:** it tells you where to spend study hours and which sections to protect on test day.
- **Personality fit:** different profiles — Ananya, Rahul, Meera — reach 39 in genuinely different ways.
- **Margin thinking:** targets aim above 39 so that one slip does not fail you.

## Core concept

### The base target model (balanced)
| Section | Q | Target correct | Target % | Marks |
|---------|---|----------------|----------|-------|
| Java | 25 | 15 | 60% | 15 |
| English & L&D | 15 | 11 | 73% | 11 |
| DBMS / SQL | 10 | 8 | 80% | 8 |
| Aptitude | 10 | 6 | 60% | 6 |
| **Total** | **60** | **40** | **67%** | **40** |

**Margin: +1 over the 39 gate.** One mistake is absorbed.

### Profile-fit models
| Profile | Java | English | DBMS | Aptitude | Total | Path |
|---------|------|---------|------|----------|-------|------|
| Rahul (Java-strong, English-weak) | 18 | 9 | 7 | 5 | 39 | Lean on Java; scrape English |
| Meera (DBMS-strong, time-panic) | 13 | 11 | 9 | 6 | 39 | Bank DBMS, manage time |
| Ananya (average aptitude) | 14 | 11 | 8 | 7 | 40 | Balanced, aptitude slight edge |
| Data-Eng aspirant | 12 | 10 | 10 | 7 | 39 | Max the DBMS section |

Notice that all four profiles reach the gate with **completely different** section mixes. There is no single "correct" path.

### Scoring sensitivity: which loss hurts most
| If you lose... | Impact | Recovery difficulty |
|----------------|--------|---------------------|
| 3 Java marks | Need +3 elsewhere | Hardest — the pool is large but each mark is contested |
| 3 English marks | Need +3 | Easy — English is recoverable with judgment |
| 3 DBMS marks | Need +3 | Easiest — theory marks are the fastest to relearn |
| 3 Aptitude marks | Need +3 | Medium — recover from other sections |

### Why the model is not "study everything"
If you study uniformly, you spend equal hours on a 10% section and a 41.7% section. The model forces you to weight hours by marks at stake and by how cheaply you can earn them.

## Worked example
**Rahul's reconstruction.**

Rahul is strong in Java (targets 18) and weak in English (targets 9). Start the arithmetic:
- `18 + 9 = 27`. He needs `39 − 27 = 12` more from DBMS + Aptitude.
- He decides to secure DBMS theory — ACID, normalization, keys — which is worth roughly 5 easy marks. That takes him to 32.
- He then needs 7 from Aptitude, but targets only 5 (he is average). That is 37.
- Gap: 2 marks. He closes it by raising his English target from 9 to 11 using the behavioural/etiquette rule (which needs no grammar skill).

**Final split:** Java 18 + English 11 + DBMS 5 + Aptitude 5 = 39. The model told him exactly which 12 marks he had to chase, and that 2 of them were cheap English judgment items, not hard grammar.

### Re-run after every mock
| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Record actual correct per section |
| 2 | Compare to target |
| 3 | Identify the largest gap |
| 4 | Adjust the target or the plan |
| 5 | Re-sum and confirm ≥ 40 |

## Quick rules
- **Every profile reaches 39 differently** — find your two anchor sections.
- **Aim for 40, not 39.**
- **Recover lost Java marks from English or DBMS, not vainly from Java.**
- **DBMS theory is the fastest section to relearn.**
- **Re-run the model after every mock.**
- **Weight study hours by marks at stake.**

## Common traps
- **Ignoring the model and just "studying everything".**
- **Aiming for exactly 39,** so a single slip fails you.
- **Over-targeting weak sections** instead of anchoring strong ones.
- **Forgetting section weight when allocating study hours.**

## Checklist
- [ ] You have a target correct count for each section.
- [ ] Your section targets sum to at least 40.
- [ ] You know your two anchor sections.
- [ ] You know which section recovers a shortfall fastest.
- [ ] You update the model after each mock.
- [ ] You weight study hours by marks at stake.
