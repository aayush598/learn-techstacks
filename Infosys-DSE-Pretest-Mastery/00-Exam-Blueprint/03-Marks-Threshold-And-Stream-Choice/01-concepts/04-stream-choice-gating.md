# Stream Choice Gating

> Section 00 · Exam Blueprint · Marks Threshold And Stream Choice · _Estimated reading time: 15 min_
> **Prerequisite:** [01-threshold-arithmetic.md](01-threshold-arithmetic.md)

## In one sentence
**The pre-test is the gate: clear 39/60 and the stream-selection form unlocks with five options; fail and none is offered.**

## Why the pre-test tests this
- **The stakes:** the pre-test is not the final interview — it is the key to the stream-selection form.
- **Strength mapping:** your section strengths predict which stream fits you best.
- **Expectation setting:** stream seat availability may vary, so clearing is necessary but not always sufficient.
- **Motivation:** knowing what comes after sharpens the study goal.

## Core concept

### Gating logic
```
Pre-test score >= 39/60  ->  stream selection unlocked  ->  choose from 5 streams
Pre-test score <  39/60  ->  no stream selection offered
```

### Streams and their pre-test signal
| Stream | Highest-signal section | What to be strong at |
|--------|------------------------|----------------------|
| Java + Angular | Java (25) | Java OOP, collections, exceptions |
| Data Science & Agentic AI | Aptitude + DBMS | Logic, data, reading simulation |
| UI Stack | English & L&D | Communication, web fundamentals |
| Data Engineering | DBMS / SQL | SQL, joins, normalization |
| Microsoft Azure + .NET | Java + DBMS | OOP transferable to .NET, data |

### Reported caveat
Stream seat availability and allocation rules can vary by batch and location. **Clearing the gate makes you eligible; it does not guarantee your first-choice stream.** Treat any stream advice as guidance, not entitlement.

### What "unlock" means in practice
| Before clearing | After clearing |
|-----------------|----------------|
| No stream form | Stream form with five options |
| No training track assigned | A DSE training track begins from your stream |
| Pre-test is the whole task | Stream choice becomes the decision |

## Worked example
**Meera (DBMS-strong) clears with 41.**

1. The form offers five streams.
2. Her strongest signal (DBMS) points to Data Engineering.
3. Her weak time-management warns against Java + Angular's heavier coding load.
4. She also has decent English and some logic, so Data Science & Agentic AI is a plausible backup.

She ranks: Data Engineering (primary), Data Science & Agentic AI (backup), UI Stack, Java + Angular, Microsoft Azure + .NET. Because she ranked all five, seat variability on her first choice does not leave her stuck.

## Quick rules
- **39/60 unlocks the form.**
- **Five streams are offered.**
- **Your section strengths map to streams.**
- **Clearing is necessary, not sufficient, for first choice.**
- **Choose where your signal is strongest.**
- **Rank all five before the form opens.**

## Common traps
- **Treating the pre-test as the final selection.**
- **Assuming your first-choice stream is guaranteed.**
- **Ignoring your own section strengths when choosing.**
- **Assuming stream rules are identical across batches.**

## Checklist
- [ ] You know the gate is 39/60.
- [ ] You know the five streams.
- [ ] You have mapped your strengths to a stream.
- [ ] You know clearing is necessary but not sufficient.
- [ ] You will read the live stream rules.
- [ ] You have ranked all five streams.
