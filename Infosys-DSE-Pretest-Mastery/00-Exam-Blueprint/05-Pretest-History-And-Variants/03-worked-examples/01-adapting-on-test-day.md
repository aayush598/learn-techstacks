## Worked example
**What Karthik would change.**

1. **Guessing discipline:** his 12 wrong included ~4 blind guesses. If he guessed only with eliminations, his wrongs drop to ~7.
   - Net = `40 − 7 × 0.25 = 38.25`.
2. **DBMS budget:** he under-budgeted DBMS at 14 minutes because he was used to a 10-question section. Allocating by count, not habit, would have caught the missing theory marks.
   - Bank two more DBMS theory marks → net ≈ 40.25.

| Change | Net score | Verdict |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| Original | 37 | ❌ below |
| Elimination-only guessing | 38.25 | ⚠️ marginal |
| Elimination + 2 extra DBMS marks | 40.25 | ✅ clears |

**The lesson:** under penalties, elimination discipline and net tracking decide pass/fail — and budgets must follow the live counts, not your habits.

## Quick rules
- **Prepare topics, not counts.**
- **Do live config in two minutes.**
- **Re-budget to actual counts.**
- **Under a penalty, guess only with eliminations.**
- **Track net, not raw.**
- **Do not under-budget a section because it "used to be small".**

## Common traps
- **Doing the expected order reflexively.**
- **Guessing blindly under a penalty.**
- **Under-budgeting DBMS because "it used to be bigger".**
- **Reading raw correct as the score.**

## Checklist
- [ ] You will do live config.
- [ ] You will re-budget to actual counts.
- [ ] You will guess only with eliminations under a penalty.
- [ ] You will track net, not raw.
- [ ] You prepare topics, not counts.
- [ ] You do not budget by old habits.
