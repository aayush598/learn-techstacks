# Answer Key, Index and Scorecard

> Section 1 · Aptitude · Question Bank · _Estimated reading time: 5 min_
> **Prerequisite:** [Part A](../03-worked-examples/01-question-bank-part-a-foundations.md)

## In one sentence

All 72 answers with a one-line reason each, plus the scorecard that tells you what to re-drill next.

## Why the pre-test tests this

- A score without a breakdown is just a number. The scorecard below converts a score into a **plan**.
- The one-line reasons exist so you can check *your* reasoning, not just your letter.

## Core concept

### How to use the scorecard

1. Attempt one part without the answers.
2. Mark it. Count the correct answers **per archetype**, not just in total.
3. Look up every wrong answer's reason and write your own reason next to it.
4. If your reason differs from the printed reason, you misread the question — that is a bigger
   problem than the arithmetic, and it is the one to fix.
5. Re-drill only the blocks where you scored below 70%.

## Quick rules

- Mark per archetype, not just in total.
- A wrong answer with the **right reason** is luck; fix it.
- A right answer with the **wrong reason** is worse — it will cost you in the exam.
- Redo only the blocks below 70%.
- Do not re-read the whole bank; re-drill the specific archetype.

## Common traps

| Trap | Why it hurts | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Scoring only the total | You do not know what to fix | Use the archetype table |
| Ignoring the reason column | You repeat the same misread | Write your own reason first |
| Re-doing the whole bank | You waste hours on topics you own | Re-drill blocks below 70% |
| Timing only some attempts | The pace breaks in the real paper | Always attempt at least one timed part |
| Not re-attempting | Errors stay uncorrected | Re-attempt every wrong block after 48 hours |
