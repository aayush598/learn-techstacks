# Question Bank — Topic Guide

> Section 1 · Aptitude · Question Bank · _Estimated reading time: 60 min per full pass_

## What the pre-test asks in this topic

The aptitude section of the Infosys DSE pre-test has **10 questions in roughly 15 minutes**. This
bank holds **72 questions** across all nine archetypes, with a complete answer key, a scorecard, an
error log and eight timed drills.

| Part | Questions | Content |
|---|---|---|
| A | Q1–Q24 | 10 data-sufficiency questions + 14 foundation arithmetic questions |
| B | Q25–Q48 | 24 quantitative-core questions: mixtures, percentages, profit/loss, ratios, work, speed |
| C | Q49–Q72 | 24 mixed questions: interpretation, ranking, inequalities, series, speed |

## Learning objectives

After this topic you should be able to:

- **Name the archetype** of any aptitude question from its first line, in under 20 seconds.
- Finish any question inside its **time box**: 55 s for data sufficiency, 60 s for ranking,
  inequalities and series, 90 s for computation.
- Use the **five-option data-sufficiency procedure** without hesitating on the letter.
- Hold the **solute constant** in any mixture question, and run **alligation** for any ratio.
- Convert **km/h ↔ m/s** without thinking, and pick the right crossing formula from the stem.
- Score **per archetype**, so your revision targets the 20% of the syllabus that is costing you
  marks.
- Log every error with a **named signature**, and re-attempt it 48 hours later.

## How to use this bank

1. **Attempt untimed, one part at a time.** Write your reasoning next to every answer — including
   the right ones.
2. **Score per archetype**, using the self-assessment table in the answer key.
3. **Log every error** in the [error log](04-pitfalls/01-question-bank-error-log.md) with a named
   signature.
4. **Re-attempt after 48 hours**, untimed, and check the log again.
5. **Then go timed.** Part A in 27 minutes, then the 90-second drills, then a full mock.

## Key takeaways

- ✅ **Name the archetype first.** The verb in the stem decides the method.
- ✅ **Time-box by archetype:** 55 s DS, 60 s series/ranking/inequality, 90 s computation.
- ✅ **Skip and return.** A skipped question is worth more than a half-finished one.
- ✅ **Score per archetype**, not in total. A total score hides where you lose marks.
- ✅ **Log the error, not the feeling.** "Careless" is not a signature.
- ✅ **Re-attempt after 48 hours.** Unfixed errors are worth zero.
- ⚠️ **Meera's warning:** she reads every question fully before deciding what it is, and the last
  three questions of her paper are guesses. Read the first line, name it, then read the rest.
