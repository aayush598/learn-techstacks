# Question Bank — Part C: Mixed Archetype Drill (Q49–Q72)

> Section 1 · Aptitude · Question Bank · _Estimated reading time: 35 min_
> **Prerequisite:** [Data Interpretation](../../03-Data-Interpretation/README.md)

## In one sentence

Twenty-four questions that mix every archetype — interpretation, ranking, inequalities, series and
speed — so you practise switching between them under time pressure.

## Why the pre-test tests this

- The real pre-test does not sort questions by archetype. It mixes them deliberately, so you must
  **switch** cleanly between "find the rule" and "compute the answer" questions.
- Part C is the endurance drill: 24 mixed questions in 27 minutes, without the comfort of a block
  heading that tells you the method.
- The ranking and inequality questions here are the highest-yield marks in the section: the
  reasoning is short, and the distractors are systematic.

## Worked example

### C1 — Data interpretation

**Q49.** A pie chart shows the preferences of 800 applicants: 35% opted for CSE, 30% for IT, 20% for
ECE and the rest for other branches. How many applicants opted for ECE?

A. 120
B. 140
C. 160
D. 180

**Answer: C.** 20% of 800 = 160.

---

**Q50.** A bar chart shows monthly sales (in thousands of rupees) of 40, 55, 60, 75 and 85. What
percentage of the total sales came from the highest month?

A. 25%
B. 27%
C. 30%
D. 32%

**Answer: B.** Total = 315 and the top month is 85 → 85/315 ≈ 26.98% ≈ 27%.

---

**Q51.** The average of 5 numbers is 24. If one of the numbers, 16, is replaced by 34, what is the new
average?

A. 27.6
B. 26.4
C. 27.2
D. 28

**Answer: A.** Total was 120; it becomes 138 and the average is 138 ÷ 5 = 27.6.

---

**Q52.** A table gives module completions and module sizes: A 40 of 80, B 60 of 100, C 50 of 100, D 30
of 120, E 45 of 90. Which module has the highest completion percentage?

A. Module A
B. Module B
C. Module C
D. Module D

**Answer: B.** A = 50%, B = 60%, C = 50%, D = 25%, E = 50% — Module B is highest.

### C2 — Ranking and ordering

**Q53.** In a row of 40 students, Meera is 12th from the left. What is her position from the right?

A. 27
B. 28
C. 29
D. 30

**Answer: C.** 40 − 12 + 1 = 29th from the right.

---

**Q54.** Suresh is 5th from the top and 21st from the bottom of a class. How many students are in the
class?

A. 24
B. 25
C. 26
D. 20

**Answer: B.** 5 + 21 − 1 = 25.

---

**Q55.** A is faster than B, C is slower than B, D is the slowest of the four, and E is faster than A.
Who is the fastest?

A. A
B. B
C. D
D. E

**Answer: D.** E > A > B > C > D, so E is the fastest.

---

**Q56.** Five friends sit in a row. Ananya is 2nd from the left, Meera is 4th from the left, and
Rahul sits immediately to the right of Meera. Who is at the extreme right?

A. Ananya
B. Meera
C. Rahul
D. Karthik

**Answer: C.** Meera is 4th, so the person immediately to her right is 5th — the extreme right — and
that is Rahul.

### C3 — Inequalities and relations

**Q57.** If x ≤ 8 and x ≥ 3, which of the following is definitely true?

A. x ≥ 3
B. x > 8
C. x = 5
D. x < 3

**Answer: A.** The range is 3 ≤ x ≤ 8; only "x ≥ 3" holds for every value in it.

---

**Q58.** If 2x > 10, then which of the following must be true?

A. x > 3
B. x > 4
C. x > 5
D. x > 6

**Answer: C.** Dividing by 2 gives x > 5.

---

**Q59.** If a < b and b < c, which of the following is definitely true?

A. a > c
B. a < c
C. a = c
D. c < a

**Answer: B.** Chaining the two relations gives a < b < c, so a < c.

### C4 — Number series

**Q60.** Find the next number: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?

A. 40
B. 42
C. 44
D. 48

**Answer: B.** Differences 4, 6, 8, 10 → the next difference is 12 → 30 + 12 = 42.

---

**Q61.** Find the next number: 1, 8, 27, 64, ?

A. 100
B. 125
C. 128
D. 144

**Answer: B.** The cubes 1³, 2³, 3³, 4³ → 5³ = 125.

---

**Q62.** Find the next number: 4, 9, 16, 25, ?

A. 30
B. 35
C. 36
D. 40

**Answer: C.** The squares 2², 3², 4², 5² → 6² = 36.

---

**Q63.** Find the next number: 5, 20, 19, 76, ?

A. 75
B. 300
C. 304
D. 76

**Answer: A.** The operations alternate: ×4, −1, ×4, −1 → 76 − 1 = 75.

### C5 — Speed, distance, trains, boats and work

**Q64.** A car covers 180 km in 2.5 hours. What is its speed?

A. 60 km/h
B. 68 km/h
C. 72 km/h
D. 75 km/h

**Answer: C.** 180 ÷ 2.5 = 72 km/h.

---

**Q65.** What is 45 m/s in km/h?

A. 150 km/h
B. 160 km/h
C. 162 km/h
D. 180 km/h

**Answer: C.** 45 × 18/5 = 162 km/h.

---

**Q66.** Two trains of lengths 200 m and 150 m travel in the **same direction** at 36 km/h and 54 km/h.
How long does the faster train take to overtake the slower one completely?

A. 14 seconds
B. 35 seconds
C. 55 seconds
D. 70 seconds

**Answer: D.** 10 m/s and 15 m/s → relative speed 5 m/s; distance 350 m → 70 seconds.

---

**Q67.** A boat travels downstream at 15 km/h and upstream at 9 km/h. What is the speed of the boat in
still water?

A. 9 km/h
B. 11 km/h
C. 12 km/h
D. 15 km/h

**Answer: C.** b = (15 + 9)/2 = 12 km/h (and the stream is 3 km/h).

---

**Q68.** A cyclist covers 30 km at 10 km/h and the next 30 km at 30 km/h. What is the average speed for
the whole ride?

A. 12 km/h
B. 15 km/h
C. 18 km/h
D. 20 km/h

**Answer: B.** 3 hours + 1 hour = 4 hours for 60 km → 60 ÷ 4 = 15 km/h.

---

**Q69.** A can finish a job in 20 days and B in 30 days. They work together for 4 days and then A
leaves. How many more days does B need to finish the job?

A. 16 days
B. 18 days
C. 20 days
D. 24 days

**Answer: C.** In 60 units of work, together they do 5 units a day → 20 units in 4 days, 40 units
remain, and B does 2 units a day → 20 days.

### C6 — Mixed ratio and percentage

**Q70.** The ratio of A to B is 3 : 5 and the ratio of B to C is 5 : 7. What is the ratio of A to C?

A. 3 : 7
B. 3 : 5
C. 5 : 7
D. 9 : 7

**Answer: A.** B is already 5 in both ratios, so A : C = 3 : 7.

---

**Q71.** A shopkeeper marks an item 40% above the cost price and allows a 10% discount. What is the
profit percent?

A. 24%
B. 26%
C. 30%
D. 32%

**Answer: B.** On a cost of 100, marked price = 140 and selling price = 140 × 0.9 = 126 → profit 26%.

---

**Q72.** In a class, 40% of the students are girls. If 20% of the girls and 30% of the boys leave the
class, what percentage of the class remains?

A. 65%
B. 70%
C. 74%
D. 75%

**Answer: C.** Out of 100 students: 40 girls (8 leave) and 60 boys (18 leave) → 26 leave and 74 remain.

## Quick rules

- DI: read the question first, then find only the data you need.
- Ranking: positions add with a −1; "immediately" means adjacent.
- Inequalities: find the option true in **every** case.
- Series: difference row, then ratio row; one step only.
- Speed: convert once; add or subtract speeds by direction.
- Work: add rates; drains subtract.
- Percent: multiply in sequence; the base is the original.

## Common traps

| Trap | Where it bites | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Reading the wrong bar or slice | Q49, Q50 | Match the label, not the position |
| Forgetting the +1 in positions | Q53, Q54 | Total = top + bottom − 1 |
| Pick "sometimes true" | Q57, Q59 | Test every value in the range |
| Cubes vs squares | Q61, Q62 | Check two terms |
| Adding speeds in the same direction | Q66 | Subtract |
| Arithmetic mean of speeds | Q68 | Total distance ÷ total time |
| Summing days instead of rates | Q69 | Add rates |
| Adding successive percentages | Q71 | Multiply |
