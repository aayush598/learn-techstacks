# Question Bank — Part B: Quantitative Core (Q25–Q48)

> Section 1 · Aptitude · Question Bank · _Estimated reading time: 35 min_
> **Prerequisite:** [Mixtures & Alligation](../../07-Mixtures-Alligation/README.md)

## In one sentence

Twenty-four questions on mixtures, percentages, profit and loss, ratios, time and work — the
quantitative core that carries most of the marks in the aptitude section.

## Why the pre-test tests this

- These are the archetypes where a **misread** costs a mark: "of the mixture" vs "of the water",
  percentage base, add vs remove.
- Part B is where candidates who "know the formulas" still lose marks, because the formulas were
  applied to the wrong situation.
- Solve it timed: **27 minutes for 24 questions** (about 68 seconds each).

## Worked example

### B1 — Mixtures and alligation

**Q25.** In what ratio must milk and water be mixed so that the mixture contains milk and water in
the ratio 2 : 3?

A. 2 : 3
B. 3 : 2
C. 4 : 1
D. 1 : 2

**Answer: A.** Alligation on milk as the solute: mean = 2/5 = 40%, cheaper = water (0%), dearer = milk
(100%) → water : milk = (100 − 40) : (40 − 0) = 3 : 2, so milk : water = 2 : 3.

---

**Q26.** A vessel contains 40 litres of a mixture of milk and water in the ratio 3 : 2. How much water
must be added so that the ratio becomes 3 : 4?

A. 12 litres
B. 16 litres
C. 20 litres
D. 24 litres

**Answer: B.** Milk = 24 litres stays fixed; 24 = 3 parts → water must become 32 litres, and 16 is
already there → add 16 litres.

---

**Q27.** A container holds 50 litres of a mixture of acid and water containing 20% acid. 10 litres of
water is removed. What is the concentration of acid now?

A. 20%
B. 22%
C. 25%
D. 12.5%

**Answer: C.** The 10 litres of acid is unchanged and the total falls to 40 litres → 10/40 = 25%.

---

**Q28.** In what ratio must a 20% salt solution and a 50% salt solution be mixed to obtain a 30%
solution?

A. 1 : 2
B. 2 : 1
C. 3 : 1
D. 1 : 3

**Answer: B.** Alligation: (50 − 30) : (30 − 20) = 20 : 10 = 2 : 1, cheaper solution first.

---

**Q29.** A shopkeeper mixes 30 kg of tea worth ₹120 per kg with 20 kg of tea worth ₹90 per kg. What
is the price per kg of the mixture?

A. ₹105
B. ₹108
C. ₹110
D. ₹112

**Answer: B.** (30 × 120 + 20 × 90)/50 = 5400/50 = ₹108 — a weighted average, not the ₹105 midpoint.

### B2 — Percentages

**Q30.** What is 35% of 240?

A. 72
B. 80
C. 84
D. 96

**Answer: C.** 0.35 × 240 = 84.

---

**Q31.** A number increased by 20% becomes 480. What was the number?

A. 384
B. 400
C. 396
D. 420

**Answer: B.** 480 ÷ 1.20 = 400.

---

**Q32.** A price is increased by 10% and then by 20%. What is the net percentage increase?

A. 30%
B. 32%
C. 33%
D. 33.2%

**Answer: B.** 1.10 × 1.20 = 1.32 → 32%.

---

**Q33.** A student scores 35% of the maximum marks and fails by 45 marks. The passing mark is 50% of
the maximum. What is the maximum mark?

A. 270
B. 300
C. 360
D. 450

**Answer: B.** The gap 50% − 35% = 15% equals 45 marks → the maximum is 45 ÷ 0.15 = 300.

---

**Q34.** The population of a town is 25,000. It increases by 12% and then decreases by 10%. What is
the final population?

A. 25,000
B. 25,200
C. 25,500
D. 28,000

**Answer: B.** 25,000 × 1.12 = 28,000, then × 0.90 = 25,200.

### B3 — Profit, loss and discount

**Q35.** An article is bought for ₹1,500 and marked 20% above the cost. It is sold at a 15% discount
on the marked price. What is the profit percent?

A. 2%
B. 5%
C. 6%
D. 0%

**Answer: A.** Marked price = ₹1,800, selling price = 1800 × 0.85 = ₹1,530, profit = ₹30 → 30/1500
= 2%.

---

**Q36.** Two articles are sold for the same price. On one there is a 20% profit and on the other a
20% loss. What is the overall profit or loss percent?

A. 0%
B. 5%
C. 10%
D. 2%

**Answer: A.** Cost prices ₹100 and ₹100 give selling prices ₹120 and ₹80 → total cost ₹200, total
selling ₹200 → 0%.

---

**Q37.** A ₹1,000 item is sold at a 20% discount, and then a 10% surcharge is added to the discounted
price. What is the final price?

A. ₹800
B. ₹880
C. ₹900
D. ₹920

**Answer: B.** 1000 × 0.80 × 1.10 = ₹880.

---

**Q38.** After two successive discounts of 20% and 10%, what is the single equivalent discount?

A. 25%
B. 28%
C. 30%
D. 32%

**Answer: B.** 0.80 × 0.90 = 0.72 → 28%.

### B4 — Ratio and proportion

**Q39.** The ratio of A to B is 4 : 7 and the ratio of B to C is 3 : 4. What is the ratio of A to C?

A. 3 : 7
B. 4 : 7
C. 7 : 3
D. 3 : 4

**Answer: A.** Make B equal: A : B = 12 : 21 and B : C = 21 : 28, so A : C = 12 : 28 = 3 : 7.

---

**Q40.** A bag has 45 marbles in the ratio red : blue = 4 : 5. If 5 red marbles are added, what is the
new ratio of red to blue?

A. 1 : 1
B. 5 : 6
C. 25 : 24
D. 9 : 10

**Answer: A.** Red = 20, blue = 25; adding 5 red gives 25 : 25 = 1 : 1.

### B5 — Time, work, pipes and speed

**Q41.** A and B together finish a job in 6 days. A alone finishes it in 10 days. In how many days
does B alone finish it?

A. 10 days
B. 12 days
C. 15 days
D. 16 days

**Answer: C.** 1/6 − 1/10 = 2/30 = 1/15 → 15 days.

---

**Q42.** A worker completes 40% of a job in 12 days. At the same rate, how long does the remaining
60% take?

A. 16 days
B. 18 days
C. 20 days
D. 24 days

**Answer: B.** 60% is 1.5 times 40%, so 1.5 × 12 = 18 days.

---

**Q43.** Pipe A fills a tank in 15 minutes and pipe B in 30 minutes. If both are open, how long does
the tank take to fill?

A. 8 minutes
B. 10 minutes
C. 12 minutes
D. 15 minutes

**Answer: B.** 1/15 + 1/30 = 3/30 = 1/10 → 10 minutes.

---

**Q44.** A train 180 m long crosses a platform 270 m long at 54 km/h. How long does the crossing take?

A. 12 seconds
B. 15 seconds
C. 24 seconds
D. 30 seconds

**Answer: D.** 54 km/h = 15 m/s and the distance is 180 + 270 = 450 m → 450 ÷ 15 = 30 seconds.

---

**Q45.** A cyclist rides from A to B at 12 km/h and returns along the same road at 4 km/h. What is the
average speed for the whole trip?

A. 5 km/h
B. 6 km/h
C. 8 km/h
D. 7.5 km/h

**Answer: B.** Equal distances → 2 × 12 × 4/(12 + 4) = 96/16 = 6 km/h.

---

**Q46.** A car is 120 m behind a bus. The bus travels at 36 km/h and the car at 72 km/h. How long
does the car take to catch up?

A. 8 seconds
B. 10 seconds
C. 12 seconds
D. 15 seconds

**Answer: C.** Relative speed = 72 − 36 = 36 km/h = 10 m/s → 120 ÷ 10 = 12 seconds.

### B6 — Number series

**Q47.** Find the next number: 3, 6, 12, 24, ?

A. 36
B. 42
C. 48
D. 54

**Answer: C.** The ratio is constant at 2 → 24 × 2 = 48.

---

**Q48.** Find the wrong term: 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 52.

A. 16
B. 25
C. 36
D. 52

**Answer: D.** The series is the squares 2², 3², 4², 5², 6², 7² = 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49 — so 52 is the
wrong term and the correct value is 49.

## Quick rules

- Mixtures: hold the solute constant; alligation for ratios; cheaper mixture first.
- Percentages multiply in sequence; the base is the original quantity.
- Profit percent is measured on the **cost price**; discount percent on the **marked price**.
- Ratio of ratios: make the common term equal.
- Time and work: add rates; drains subtract.
- Speed: convert units once; add or subtract speeds according to direction.
- Series: difference row, then ratio row.

## Common traps

| Trap | Where it bites | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Arithmetic mean of speeds | Q45 | `2xy/(x+y)` |
| Simple average for a tea mixture | Q29 | Weighted average |
| Adding successive percentages | Q32 | Multiply |
| Percent change on the wrong base | Q31, Q33 | Original value is the base |
| Adding the percentages of a discount | Q38 | Multiply |
| Times added instead of rates | Q41 | Add rates |
| Pole instead of platform | Q44 | Length + platform |
