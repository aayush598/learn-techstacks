# Question Bank Archetype Map

> Section 1 · Aptitude · Question Bank · _Estimated reading time: 6 min_
> **Prerequisite:** [Question bank topic](../README.md)

## In one sentence

Nine archetypes cover every aptitude question; once you can name the archetype from the stem, the
method is already chosen.

## Why the pre-test tests this

- The pre-test does not label its questions. Your first 20 seconds go to naming the archetype — and
  that is exactly where candidates lose marks they had the knowledge to earn.
- This map turns "a maths question" into "a mixture question with a replacement verb", which is
  actionable.

## Core concept

### The nine archetypes

| # | Archetype | Stem signature | Method in one line | Time | Bank questions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Data sufficiency** | "What is X? I. … II. …" | I alone → II alone → together → compare | 55 s | Q1–Q10 |
| 2 | **Percentages** | "What is x% of y?", "increased by x%", "failed by n marks" | Multiply for a part; divide by the base for a change | 65 s | Q11–Q13, Q30–Q34 |
| 3 | **Profit, loss, discount** | "bought for", "sold for", "marked price", "discount" | Percent of **cost** for profit; percent of **marked** for discount | 70 s | Q14–Q16, Q35–Q38, Q71 |
| 4 | **Data interpretation** | A chart, table, graph, or a "total and ratio" pair | Read the question, find only the data you need, then compute | 80 s | Q49–Q52 |
| 5 | **Ranking and ordering** | "from the left/right", "immediately", "faster than" | Positions add with −1; "immediately" means adjacent | 60 s | Q53–Q56 |
| 6 | **Inequalities and relations** | "if x > y and y > z", "which is definitely true" | Build the chain; pick the option true in every case | 60 s | Q24, Q57–Q59 |
| 7 | **Number series** | A list of 4–6 numbers, "next number" or "wrong term" | Difference row, then ratio row; extend one step | 60 s | Q23, Q47–Q48, Q60–Q63 |
| 8 | **Mixtures and alligation** | "milk and water", "acid", "salt", "how much to add/remove" | Hold the solute constant; alligation for ratios | 70 s | Q25–Q29, Q72 |
| 9 | **Ratio, speed, distance, work** | "km/h", "train", "crosses", "days", "pipes" | Convert units once; name the situation; apply one formula | 75 s | Q17–Q22, Q41–Q46, Q64–Q70 |

### The three-second sub-naming inside speed and work

| The stem says | Sub-type | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| "crosses a pole" | Pole | `L/v` |
| "crosses a platform" | Platform | `(L + P)/v` |
| "in opposite directions" | Opposite | Add speeds |
| "in the same direction" / "overtakes" | Same direction | Subtract speeds |
| "downstream" / "upstream" | Boat | `b ± s` |
| "together" | Combined work | Add rates |
| "and then A leaves" | One leaves | Work done, then the rest |

### The three-second sub-naming inside mixtures

| The stem says | Sub-type | Move |
|---|---|---|
| "in what ratio" | Alligation | `Q_cheap : Q_dear = (C_dear − C_mean) : (C_mean − C_cheap)` |
| "how much water must be added" | Add solvent | Solute constant |
| "X litres of water is removed" | Remove solvent | Solute constant, volume down |
| "X litres of the mixture is drawn out" | Remove mixture | Concentration unchanged |
| "is replaced by" | Replacement | Remove, then add, then restore the volume |

### The three-second sub-naming inside series

| The stem says | Sub-type | Move |
|---|---|---|
| "find the next number" | Extend | Difference row → ratio row → one step |
| "find the wrong term" | Correct it | Find the rule, recompute the term |
| "is the odd one out" | Pattern break | Compare all terms against the rule |

### Time budget for the whole section

| Section | Questions | Budget | Per question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data sufficiency block | 2 | 2 min | 55 s |
| Computation block | 8 | 12 min | 90 s |
| **Total** | **10** | **14 min** | — |

Leave 1 minute of the 15 for review. If you are still on question 7 at minute 12, you are going too
slow on the computation block — skip, mark and return.

## Worked example

**Q. A vessel contains 60 litres of a mixture of milk and water in the ratio 3 : 2. 12 litres of the
mixture is drawn out and the vessel is filled with milk. What is the ratio of milk and water now?**

**Naming drill**: "mixture", "milk and water" → **archetype 8 (mixtures)**. Sub-naming: "drawn out"
+ "filled with" → **replacement**.

**Method**: remove first (12 × 3/5 = 7.2 milk, 12 × 2/5 = 4.8 water), then add 12 litres of milk,
then check the volume is still 60.

**Answer: 17 : 8** (40.8 : 19.2).

**Why the naming drill was worth 20 seconds**: without it, "drawn out" looks like "remove pure
water" and you compute a concentration instead of a ratio — and then you cannot even choose an
option.

## Quick rules

- Name the archetype before reading the numbers.
- Sub-name it from the verb (add / remove / replace / mix / together / crosses / immediately).
- Then apply exactly one formula.
- Time-box by archetype, not per question: DS 55 s, computation 90 s.
- Two minutes of thinking on a question that should take 90 seconds is a loss of three future
  questions.

## Common traps

| Trap | Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Reading the whole stem before deciding the archetype | 3 minutes gone on one question | Read the first line only, name it, then read the rest |
| Guessing the method from the numbers | Wrong formula applied confidently | The verb decides, not the numbers |
| Treating a DS question as a computation | Lost time, no learning | The DS procedure decides, not the arithmetic |
| Mixing up "of the mixture" and "of the water" | Wrong formula | Sub-name the removal |
| Applying a formula without converting units | Wrong answer, right method | Convert once, early |
| Spending 3 minutes on one series | Section unfinished | 60 s cap for series |

## Checklist

- [ ] I can name the archetype from the first line of a stem in under 20 seconds.
- [ ] I can sub-name mixtures and speed questions from the verb.
- [ ] I know the per-archetype time budget.
- [ ] I skipped and returned rather than burning 3 minutes.
- [ ] I left 1 minute for review in a timed paper.
