# 90-Second Drills — The Pace Trainer

> Section 1 · Aptitude · Question Bank · _Estimated reading time: 6 min_
> **Prerequisite:** [Archetype map](../01-concepts/01-question-bank-archetype-map.md)

## In one sentence

Eight short timed drills that train the one skill the pre-test actually measures — finishing each
question inside its time box.

## Why the pre-test tests this

- You do not lose marks in the Infosys DSE pre-test because you cannot do arithmetic. You lose them
  because 3 of 10 questions were never attempted.
- 90 seconds is not an arbitrary number: 10 questions × 90 s = 15 minutes, which is the whole
  aptitude budget with nothing left over.
- The drills below take 5–10 minutes each and can be done on a phone between classes.

## Core concept

### The time box

| Archetype | Box | If you are at 90 s |
|---|---|---|
| Data sufficiency | 55 s | Mark your best guess and move |
| Percentages, profit, discount | 90 s | Skip to the next question |
| Data interpretation | 90 s | Re-read the question in 20 s, then move |
| Ranking, inequalities | 60 s | Guess and move |
| Number series | 60 s | Guess and move |
| Mixtures | 90 s | Skip and move |
| Speed, distance, work | 90 s | Skip and move |

### Drill 1 — Unit conversion sprint (3 minutes)

Write the answers only. Target: 18 of 20 in 3 minutes.

| # | Convert | # | Convert |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 72 km/h → m/s | 11 | 15 m/s → km/h |
| 2 | 54 km/h → m/s | 12 | 20 m/s → km/h |
| 3 | 36 km/h → m/s | 13 | 45 minutes → seconds |
| 4 | 90 km/h → m/s | 14 | 3 hours → minutes |
| 5 | 108 km/h → m/s | 15 | 90 km/h → km/min |
| 6 | 18 km/h → m/s | 16 | 1.5 km/min → km/h |
| 7 | 120 km/h → m/s | 17 | 2.5 hours → seconds |
| 8 | 45 km/h → m/s | 18 | 12 m/s → km/min |
| 9 | 30 km/h → m/s | 19 | 60 km/h → km/min |
| 10 | 72 m/s → km/h | 20 | 36 km/h → m/min |

**Answers:** 1 = 20 m/s, 2 = 15, 3 = 10, 4 = 25, 5 = 30, 6 = 5, 7 = 33.33, 8 = 12.5, 9 = 8.33,
10 = 259.2 km/h, 11 = 54 km/h, 12 = 72, 13 = 2,700, 14 = 180, 15 = 1.5 km/min, 16 = 90 km/h,
17 = 9,000, 18 = 0.72 km/min, 19 = 1 km/min, 20 = 0.6 km/min.

**Trap in the set:** number 10 gives km/h and the multiplier is `18/5`, not 18. If you wrote 1,296,
you multiplied by 18 instead of 3.6.

### Drill 2 — Alligation in 10 seconds each (4 minutes)

Give the ratio of the **first** listed solution to the **second**, to obtain the third.

| # | Mix | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20% and 50% → 30% | 2 : 1 |
| 2 | 30% and 60% → 50% | 1 : 2 |
| 3 | 10% and 40% → 20% | 2 : 1 |
| 4 | 25% and 45% → 35% | 1 : 1 |
| 5 | 20% and 60% → 32% | 7 : 3 |
| 6 | 40% and 70% → 60% | 1 : 2 |
| 7 | 15% and 35% → 20% | 3 : 1 |
| 8 | 30% and 50% → 38% | 3 : 2 |
| 9 | 12% and 48% → 30% | 1 : 1 |
| 10 | 20% and 80% → 56% | 2 : 3 |

**Worked check (5):** (60 − 32) : (32 − 20) = 28 : 12 = 7 : 3 — the **first** solution takes the
difference between the second and the mean.

**Trap in the set:** #2 and #6 look like 1 : 1 because the mean is exactly in the middle of the two
concentrations. It is not: (60 − 50) : (50 − 30) = 10 : 20 = 1 : 2.

### Drill 3 — Average speed in 20 seconds (3 minutes)

Equal distances each way. Give the average speed.

| # | Speeds | Answer | # | Speeds | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 40 and 60 | 48 | 6 | 30 and 45 | 36 |
| 2 | 20 and 60 | 30 | 7 | 12 and 24 | 16 |
| 3 | 30 and 50 | 37.5 | 8 | 15 and 45 | 22.5 |
| 4 | 60 and 90 | 72 | 9 | 24 and 40 | 30 |
| 5 | 45 and 75 | 56.25 | 10 | 18 and 27 | 21.6 |

**Check 1:** 2 × 40 × 60/100 = 48 ✅ **Check 3:** 2 × 30 × 50/80 = 37.5 ✅ **Check 5:** 2 × 45 × 75/120
= 56.25 ✅ **Check 8:** 2 × 15 × 45/60 = 22.5 ✅ **Check 10:** 2 × 18 × 27/45 = 21.6 ✅

**Trap check:** the arithmetic means are 50, 40, 40, 75, 60, 37.5, 18, 30, 32 and 22.5. None of them
is the answer to that line — if you wrote one of them, you used the equal-**times** formula. (Note
that #3's correct answer, 37.5, is #6's arithmetic mean: do not confuse the two lines.)

### Drill 4 — Time and work in 20 seconds (3 minutes)

| # | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A in 6 days, B in 3 days, together | 2 days |
| 2 | A in 12, B in 18, together | 7.2 days |
| 3 | A in 20, B in 30, together | 12 days |
| 4 | A in 15, B in 10, together | 6 days |
| 5 | A in 8, B in 24, together | 6 days |
| 6 | Together 6 days, A alone 10, B alone | 15 days |
| 7 | Together 12 days, A alone 20, B alone | 30 days |
| 8 | A in 10 days does what B does in 15 → efficiency A : B | 3 : 2 |
| 9 | Fill 20 min, fill 30 min, together | 12 min |
| 10 | Fill 20 min, drain 30 min, together | 60 min |

**Worked check (5):** 8 × 24/(8 + 24) = 192/32 = 6 days.

**Trap in the set:** #6 and #7 ask for the **single** worker's time. Subtract rates, never divide
times.

| 4 | 80% of 150 | 120 |
| 5 | 45% of 200 | 90 |

### Drill 7 — Data sufficiency decisions (5 minutes)

Give the letter only (A = both together; B = I alone; C = II alone; D = either alone; E = not even
both).

| # | Stem | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Find x. I. x + 4 = 10. II. 3x = 18 | D |
| 2 | Find y. I. y = 2x. II. x = 7 | A |
| 3 | Find n. I. n is 5 more than m. II. m = 4 | A |
| 4 | Find the SP. I. CP = 500, profit 20%. II. Profit = 100 | B |
| 5 | Find the speed. I. 200 km in 4 h. II. 300 km in 5 h | D |
| 6 | Find the ratio A : C. I. A:B = 2:3. II. B:C = 3:4 | A |
| 7 | Find x. I. x is 12. II. x = 9 | E |
| 8 | Find the total. I. 30% is 15. II. Total = 50 | C |
| 9 | Find the days. I. 4 workers in 6 days. II. 8 workers, same job | B |
| 10 | Find the area. I. Length = 10, breadth = 6. II. Perimeter = 32 | D |

**Worked checks (4, 8, 9 and 10).**

- #4: I alone gives 25% profit → selling price ₹600; II alone gives ₹100 profit with no cost
  price → insufficient. Answer **B**.
- #8: II alone states the total, which is what the stem asks for → sufficient. Answer **C**.
- #9: I alone gives 24 worker-days, so 8 workers take 3 days; II alone gives no rate → answer **B**.
- #10: I alone gives the area (10 × 6 = 60); II alone gives length + breadth = 16 with no product →
  insufficient. Together they give area 60, and both agree → answer **D**.

### Drill 8 — Ranking in 20 seconds (3 minutes)

| # | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 40 students, Meera 12th from the left → from the right | 29 |
| 2 | 5th from top, 21st from bottom → total | 25 |
| 3 | 30 students, 18th from the right → from the left | 13 |
| 4 | 45 students, 25th from the left → from the right | 21 |
| 5 | 12th from top, 30th from bottom → total | 41 |
| 6 | E faster than A, A faster than B, B faster than C → fastest | E |
| 7 | A slower than B, C slower than D, D slower than B → fastest | B |
| 8 | Five in a row, Ananya 2nd from left, Meera 4th from left, Rahul immediately right of Meera → extreme right | Rahul |
| 9 | Three on a podium: gold > silver, bronze < silver → who is last | bronze |
| 10 | 8 cars in a race; Rahul is 3rd, Ananya is 1st → who is 2nd if Meera is 4th | Not determinable |

**Worked checks (1, 3, 4, 5 and 9).**

- #1: 40 − 12 + 1 = 29 ✅
- #3: 30 − 18 + 1 = 13 ✅
- #4: 45 − 25 + 1 = 21 ✅
- #5: 12 + 30 − 1 = 41 ✅
- #9: gold > silver > bronze, so bronze is last ✅

## Worked example

**Drill 3, line 1.** A car travels from A to B at 40 km/h and returns along the same road at 60 km/h.
What is the average speed for the whole trip?

**Step 1 — read the stem for what is equal.** Out and back over the same road → the two distances are
equal. So the equal-**times** formula `(x + y)/2` does not apply.

**Step 2 — apply the equal-distances formula.**

```
avg = 2xy/(x + y) = 2 × 40 × 60 / (40 + 60) = 4800/100 = 48 km/h
```

**Step 3 — prove it by construction.** Take each leg as 120 km: 120 ÷ 40 = 3 h out, 120 ÷ 60 = 2 h
back. Total 240 km in 5 h → 240 ÷ 5 = **48 km/h** ✅

**Step 4 — reject the arithmetic mean.** `(40 + 60)/2 = 50` is the equal-times answer. It is the most
common wrong answer in this topic, and it appears as an option on purpose.

**Time: 25 seconds.** Name the archetype (average speed), ask "equal what?", apply one formula, stop.

## Quick rules

- Drill on a timer, every time. An untimed drill teaches nothing about pacing.
- Score per drill; re-drill anything below 90%.
- Do one drill a day, not all eight in one sitting.
- Each drill should take 3–5 minutes. If it takes longer, you are calculating, not drilling.
- After a drill, note the error **type**, not the question number.

## Common traps

| Trap | Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Untimed drilling | Feels good, tests nothing | Timer always on |
| Mixing units in a drill | 72 m/s answer | Say the pair: 5/18 and 18/5 |
| Using the equal-times formula for average speed | 50 instead of 48 | Ask "equal what?" |
| Adding times for work | 30 days | Add rates |
| Guessing DS letters by pattern | E too often | Run the four tests in order |
| Skipping the drill you fail | Slow progress | Re-drill below 90% next day |

## Checklist

- [ ] I do one drill a day, timed.
- [ ] I record the score for each drill.
- [ ] I re-drill any drill below 90%.
- [ ] I note the error type, not just the question number.
- [ ] I keep each drill under 5 minutes.
