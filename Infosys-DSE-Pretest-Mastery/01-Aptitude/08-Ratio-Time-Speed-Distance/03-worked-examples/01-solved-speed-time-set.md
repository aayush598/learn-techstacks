## In one sentence

Eleven questions covering every speed, distance and work shape the pre-test uses — each one solved
in four lines with a breakdown of why every wrong option is wrong.

## Why the pre-test tests this

- Speed–distance is the biggest single source of marks in the aptitude section.
- Each question below maps to a situation word in the stem: **pole, platform, opposite, same
  direction, downstream, upstream, equal distances, together, one leaves early, fill and drain**.
- Learn to spot the situation word, and the formula follows.

## Worked example

### Q1. A car travels at 72 km/h. What is its speed in metres per second?

A. 18 m/s
B. 20 m/s
C. 25.6 m/s
D. 36 m/s

**Solution**

```
72 × 5/18 = (72/18) × 5 = 4 × 5 = 20 m/s
```

**Answer: B (20 m/s).**

**Why the distractors fail**

| Option | Why it is wrong |
|---|---|
| 18 m/s | Used `÷ 4` or `× 1/4` |
| 25.6 m/s | Used `÷ 2.8`, i.e. inverted `5/18` incorrectly |
| 36 m/s | Halved 72 — confused km/h with m/min |

**Check.** `20 m/s × 3600 s/h = 72000 m/h = 72 km/h` ✅

---

### Q2. A car travels from A to B at 40 km/h and returns along the same road at 60 km/h. What is the
average speed for the whole trip?

A. 45 km/h
B. 48 km/h
C. 50 km/h
D. 52.5 km/h

**Solution — equal distances, so harmonic mean**

```
avg = 2 × 40 × 60 / (40 + 60) = 4800 / 100 = 48 km/h
```

**Answer: B (48 km/h).**

**Why the distractors fail**

| Option | Why it is wrong |
|---|---|
| 45 km/h | Subtracted 15 from 60 — not an average method at all |
| 50 km/h | The arithmetic mean — correct only for **equal times**, not equal distances |
| 52.5 km/h | Added 15% to 45 for no reason |

**Proof that 48 is right.** Let each leg be 120 km. Outbound: 120 ÷ 40 = 3 h. Return: 120 ÷ 60 = 2 h.
Total 240 km in 5 h → `240 ÷ 5 = 48 km/h` ✅

---

### Q3. A can complete a piece of work in 12 days and B in 18 days. How long will they take working
together?

A. 6 days
B. 7.2 days
C. 7.5 days
D. 30 days

**Solution — add rates**

```
A's rate = 1/12 = 3/36
B's rate = 1/18 = 2/36
Together  = 5/36 per day
Time      = 36/5 = 7.2 days
```

**Answer: B (7.2 days).**

**Why the distractors fail**

| Option | Why it is wrong |
|---|---|
| 6 days | Each works half the job in half the time — ignores the rates |
| 7.5 days | The average of 12 and 18, i.e. you averaged the **times** |
| 30 days | Added the times |

---

### Q4. A boat travels downstream at 12 km/h and upstream at 8 km/h. What is the speed of the boat in
still water?

A. 8 km/h
B. 10 km/h
C. 12 km/h
D. 20 km/h

**Solution**

```
b = (D + U)/2 = (12 + 8)/2 = 10 km/h
s = (D − U)/2 = (12 − 8)/2 = 2 km/h
```

**Answer: B (10 km/h).**

**Why the distractors fail**

| Option | Why it is wrong |
|---|---|
| 8 km/h | The upstream speed only |
| 12 km/h | The downstream speed only |
| 20 km/h | Added the two speeds instead of averaging |

**Check.** Downstream `10 + 2` = 12 ✅, upstream `10 − 2` = 8 ✅

---

### Q5. A train 200 m long runs at 72 km/h. How long does it take to cross a platform 300 m long?

A. 10 seconds
B. 15 seconds
C. 20 seconds
D. 25 seconds

**Solution**

| Step | Working |
|---|---|
| Speed in m/s | `72 × 5/18` = 20 m/s |
| Distance | `200 + 300` = 500 m |
| Time | `500 ÷ 20` = **25 seconds** |

**Answer: D (25 seconds).**

**Why the distractors fail**

| Option | Why it is wrong |
|---|---|
| 10 seconds | Crossing a **pole**: `200 ÷ 20` — you left out the platform |
| 15 seconds | The platform only: `300 ÷ 20` |
| 20 seconds | `400 ÷ 20` — the lengths added wrongly |

---

### Q6. Two trains, 150 m and 100 m long, move in opposite directions at 54 km/h and 36 km/h. How
long do they take to cross each other?

A. 6 seconds
B. 10 seconds
C. 16.7 seconds
D. 50 seconds

**Solution**

| Step | Working |
|---|---|
| Convert | 54 km/h = 15 m/s; 36 km/h = 10 m/s |
| Relative speed (opposite → add) | 25 m/s |
| Distance | `150 + 100` = 250 m |
| Time | `250 ÷ 25` = **10 seconds** |

**Answer: B (10 seconds).**

**Why the distractors fail**

| Option | Why it is wrong |
|---|---|
| 6 seconds | Counted only the longer train's length: `150 ÷ 25` |
| 16.7 seconds | Used only the faster train's speed: `250 ÷ 15` |
| 50 seconds | Subtracted the speeds (the same-direction formula): `250 ÷ 5` |

---

### Q7. The ratio of milk to water in a 48-litre mixture is 5 : 3. How many litres of water does the
mixture contain?

A. 18 litres
B. 20 litres
C. 24 litres
D. 30 litres

**Solution**

| Step | Working |
|---|---|
| Total parts | `5 + 3` = 8 |
| One part | `48 ÷ 8` = 6 litres |
| Water | `3 × 6` = **18 litres** |

**Answer: A (18 litres).**

**Why the distractors fail**

| Option | Why it is wrong |
|---|---|
| 20 litres | Treated 48 as 8 parts of 6 and mis-multiplied |
| 24 litres | Half the total — assumed an equal split |
| 30 litres | The milk amount (`5 × 6`) |

---

### Q8. A and B can build a wall in 12 and 18 days respectively. A works alone for the first 4 days,
then B works alone to finish it. How many days does the whole job take?

A. 14 days
B. 15 days
C. 16 days
D. 18 days

**Solution — work in 36 units**

| Step | Working |
|---|---|
| A's 1-day work | `36 ÷ 12` = 3 units |
| B's 1-day work | `36 ÷ 18` = 2 units |
| A's 4 days | `4 × 3` = 12 units |
| Remaining | `36 − 12` = 24 units |
| B's time | `24 ÷ 2` = 12 days |
| **Total** | `4 + 12` = **16 days** |

**Answer: C (16 days).**

**Why the distractors fail**

| Option | Why it is wrong |
|---|---|
| 14 days | You assumed they worked together after day 4 (together time would be `4 + 12 × 2/5 = 8.8` days) |
| 15 days | B's time only, without adding A's first 4 days |
| 18 days | B's time alone for the entire job |

**Cross-check with fractions.** A does `4/12 = 1/3` of the work. The remaining `2/3` takes B
`2/3 × 18 = 12` days. Total `4 + 12` = 16 ✅

---

### Q9. A tank can be filled by one pipe in 20 minutes and emptied by another in 30 minutes. If both
are open, how long does the tank take to fill?

A. 45 minutes
B. 50 minutes
C. 60 minutes
D. 12 minutes

**Solution — the drain's rate is negative**

```
Net rate = 1/20 − 1/30 = (3 − 2)/60 = 1/60 per minute
Time = 60 minutes
```

**Answer: C (60 minutes).**

**Why the distractors fail**

| Option | Why it is wrong |
|---|---|
| 45 minutes | Simple average of 20 and 30 |
| 50 minutes | Added 20 and 30 |
| 12 minutes | Added the rates — that is the "both fill" case, not fill-and-drain |

**Note.** 60 minutes is longer than the 20-minute fill time, which makes sense: the drain fights the
fill the whole time.

### Q10. A train 180 m long overtakes a man walking at 6 km/h in the same direction in 15 seconds.
What is the speed of the train?

A. 43.2 km/h
B. 45 km/h
C. 49.2 km/h
D. 55.2 km/h

**Solution**

| Step | Working |
|---|---|
| Relative speed | `180 m ÷ 15 s` = 12 m/s = `12 × 18/5` = 43.2 km/h |
| Train speed | `43.2 + 6` = **49.2 km/h** |

**Answer: C (49.2 km/h).**

**Why the distractors fail**

| Option | Why it is wrong |
|---|---|
| 43.2 km/h | The **relative** speed — you forgot to add the man's speed back |
| 45 km/h | Rounded 43.2 + 6 wrongly |
| 55.2 km/h | Added the man's speed twice |

**Sanity check.** 49.2 km/h = 13.67 m/s; relative speed = 13.67 − 1.67 = 12 m/s; `180 ÷ 12` = 15 s ✅

---

### Q11. A car covers 240 km in 4 hours. If it increases its speed by 20 km/h, how long does it take
to cover the same distance?

A. 2.5 hours
B. 3 hours
C. 3.5 hours
D. 4 hours

**Solution**

| Step | Working |
|---|---|
| Original speed | `240 ÷ 4` = 60 km/h |
| New speed | `60 + 20` = 80 km/h |
| New time | `240 ÷ 80` = **3 hours** |

**Answer: B (3 hours).**

**Why the distractors fail**

| Option | Why it is wrong |
|---|---|
| 2.5 hours | `240 ÷ 96` — you increased the speed by 60%, not 20 km/h |
| 3.5 hours | Used 68.6 km/h — averaged the two speeds instead of adding 20 |
| 4 hours | The original time |

## Quick rules

- Convert units once.
- Pole → `L`; platform/bridge → `L + P`.
- Opposite → add speeds; same direction → subtract.
- Downstream `b + s`, upstream `b − s`.
- Average speed = total distance ÷ total time; equal distances → `2xy/(x+y)`.
- Time and work: add rates; together `T = ab/(a+b)`.
- Drain pipes subtract.
- Efficiency ratio = inverse of time ratio.
- 90 seconds.

## Common traps

| Trap | Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Arithmetic mean of speeds | 50 km/h | Use `2xy/(x+y)` |
| Wrong conversion direction | 259 m/s | `× 5/18` |
| Pole vs platform | 10 s | Add the platform length |
| Same-direction formula | 50 s | Opposite → add |
| Times added | 7.5 days | Add rates |
| Rate ratio inverted | A/B swapped | Inverse of time |
| One train's length only | 6 s | Add both lengths |

## Checklist

- [ ] I converted the speed before computing and used it consistently.
- [ ] I identified the situation from the stem's wording.
- [ ] I used the harmonic mean for equal-distance average speed.
- [ ] I added rates for combined work and subtracted for drains.
- [ ] I sanity-checked the final answer against the input range.
