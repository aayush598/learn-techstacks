### Distance / speed / time

| Formula | |
|---|---|
| Distance | `= S × T` |

### Crossing formulas

| Situation | Time |
|---|---|
| Train crosses a pole | `L / v` |
| Train crosses a platform of P | `(L + P) / v` |
| Train crosses a bridge of B | `(L + B) / v` |
| Two trains cross, opposite | `(L1 + L2)/(v1 + v2)` |
| Two trains cross, same direction | `(L1 + L2)/(v1 − v2)` |
| Train overtakes a man | `L/(v_train − v_man)` |
| Train overtakes a train | `(L1 + L2)/(v1 − v2)` |

### Boat and stream

| Quantity | Formula |
|---|---|
| Downstream | `b + s` |
| Upstream | `b − s` |
| Boat in still water | `b = (D + U)/2` |
| Stream speed | `s = (D − U)/2` |

### Average speed

| Situation | Formula |
|---|---|
| General | `total distance / total time` |
| Equal distances | `2xy/(x + y)` |
| Equal times | `(x + y)/2` |

### Time and work

| Situation | Formula |
|---|---|
| A in `a` days, B in `b` days, together | `ab/(a+b)` |
| Rate of A | `1/a` of the work per day |
| A does in 6 days what B does in 10 days | `A : B = 10 : 6` |
| A works `x` days, then B alone finishes | `x + b(1 − x/a)` |
| A is `k` times as efficient, together in `T` | A alone `kT/(k+1)`, B alone `T/(k+1)` |
| Fill in `m`, drain in `n`, both open | `1/m − 1/n` |
| Fill in `m`, fill in `n`, both open | `1/m + 1/n` |

### Ratio and proportion

| Situation | Method |
|---|---|
| Reduce a ratio | Divide both by the GCD |
| One part of a total `T` | `T/(a+b)` |
| Share of one term | `a × T/(a+b)` |
| New ratio after adding `x` | Convert `x` into parts, then add |

## Worked example

**Q. A train 200 m long runs at 72 km/h. How long to cross a 300 m platform?**

```
speed = 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s
distance = 200 + 300 = 500 m
time = 500 ÷ 20 = 25 seconds
```

**Answer: 25 seconds.**

**Second check.** `20 m/s × 25 s = 500 m` ✅ and `500 = 200 (train) + 300 (platform)` ✅

## Quick rules

- `× 5/18` and `× 18/5`, in that order, every time.
- Total distance ÷ total time.
- Length + platform.
- Add opposite, subtract same.
- Add rates, subtract drains.
- Inverse ratio for efficiency.
- 90 seconds.

## Common traps

| Trap | Fix |
|---|---|
| Mean of speeds | `2xy/(x+y)` |
| Inverted conversion | Check the magnitude |
| Pole vs platform | Add the platform |
| Same-direction formula | Match to direction |
| Times added | Add rates |
| Inverted efficiency ratio | Invert |
| Drain as fill | Subtract |
