# Ratio, Time, Speed & Distance — Topic Guide

> Section 1 · Aptitude · Ratio, Time, Speed & Distance · _Estimated reading time: 35 min for the full topic_

## What the pre-test asks in this topic

Speed–distance is the **largest single source of marks** in the aptitude section. Expect 2–3
questions from these shapes:

1. **Unit conversion** — "72 km/h in m/s?"
2. **Average speed** — a round trip at two speeds.
3. **Train** — crossing a pole, a platform, or another train.
4. **Boat and stream** — downstream and upstream.
5. **Ratio/proportion** — a ratio applied to a total, or a share of a total.
6. **Time and work** — two people together, or one person leaving early.
7. **Pipes** — filling with a drain open.

## Learning objectives

After this topic you should be able to:

- Convert **km/h ↔ m/s** and every other common unit pair instantly (`× 5/18`, `× 18/5`).
- Compute **average speed** as total distance ÷ total time, and use the harmonic mean `2xy/(x+y)`
  for equal distances.
- Distinguish **pole, platform and bridge** crossings and the right distance in each.
- Apply **relative speed**: add for opposite directions, subtract for the same direction.
- Use `b = (D + U)/2` and `s = (D − U)/2` for boats and streams.
- Solve **ratio and proportion** questions by parts.
- Solve **time and work** questions by adding rates, using LCM work units.
- Handle the "**A works x days, then B finishes**" variant.
- Finish a speed question in **90 seconds**.

## Quick solving procedure

1. **Convert units once** (15 s) — m/s for trains and boats, km/h for cars, and stay there.
2. **Name the situation** (10 s) — pole / platform / opposite / same direction / downstream /
   upstream / equal distances / together / one leaves early / fill-and-drain.
3. **Apply the one formula** (40 s).
4. **Sanity check** (15 s) — range, units, and (for fill-and-drain) the answer must exceed the
   fill time alone.

## Key takeaways

- ✅ **`× 5/18`** for km/h → m/s. Say the pair, not the arithmetic.
- ✅ **Average speed = total distance ÷ total time.** Never average the two speeds for a round trip.
- ✅ **Pole → `L`; platform → `L + P`.** The train covers its own length plus the obstacle.
- ✅ **Opposite → add; same direction → subtract.**
- ✅ **`b = (D + U)/2`** for the boat in still water.
- ✅ **Add rates for work, not times.** `T = ab/(a+b)`.
- ✅ **Efficiency ratio is the inverse of the time ratio.**
- ✅ **A drain pipe's rate is negative.**
- ✅ **90 seconds, then move.**
- ⚠️ **Rahul's warning:** "I knew the formula but chose the pole answer because I read fast." Read
  the situation word before the numbers.
