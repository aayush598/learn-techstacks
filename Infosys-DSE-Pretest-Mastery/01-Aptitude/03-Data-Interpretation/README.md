# Data Interpretation — Topic Guide

> Section 1 · Aptitude · Data Interpretation · _Estimated reading time: 30 min for the full topic_

## What the pre-test asks in this topic

The DSE pre-test shows a **chart or table** — bar, pie, table or line graph — and asks one or two
questions about it. The most common shapes you will meet:

1. **Direct read** — "how many applicants came through referrals?"
2. **Missing value** — "what is the value of the blank cell?"
3. **Percentage** — "what % of the total chose X?"
4. **Ratio-based** — "what is the ratio of A to B?"
5. **Approximate** — "approximately how much…?" with options 5–10 apart.

The same data set is often shared by two or more questions in the paper, so the total you compute for
the first question is worth reusing.

## Learning objectives

After this topic you should be able to:

- Recognise the four chart types and the fastest way to read each one.
- Name the five DI question archetypes and the single move that solves each.
- Find the **total** row or column before starting any calculation.
- Convert between ₹ thousand, lakh and crore without hesitation.
- Convert a chart percentage into a count, and a count into a percentage, using the total.
- Solve a missing-value problem by subtracting the knowns from the correct total.
- Build a ratio in **parts** rather than from raw numbers.
- Read a line graph for max, min, trend and biggest change without reading every point.
- Decide, from the **option spacing**, whether an exact or an approximate answer is expected.
- Finish a DI question in **70 seconds** or less.

## Quick 3-step solving procedure

1. **Read the question and the options first.** The options tell you the required precision and often
   tell you which two numbers you need.
2. **Find the total and normalise the units.** Most DI questions reduce to "value ÷ total" or
   "total − knowns".
3. **Compute once, sanity check, mark.** If the answer is not near any option, you have read the
   wrong cell — go back to the headers, not to the arithmetic.

## Key takeaways

- ✅ **Question first.** Most DI time is lost reading data you never needed.
- ✅ **Total first.** It powers most DI questions and turns a missing cell into one subtraction.
- ✅ **One unit.** ₹ thousand vs lakh vs crore is the classic silent killer.
- ✅ **% in chart ≠ count in answer.** Convert with the total, both directions.
- ✅ **Ratio in parts.** Divide everything by a common factor, then simplify.
- ✅ **Option spacing tells you the precision.** 5 or 10 apart → approximate.
- ✅ **Line graphs: max, min, trend, endpoints.** Not every point.
- ✅ **Bank the easiest question first** when a data set feeds several questions.
- ⚠️ **Meera's warning:** she starts at the top of the chart and works down. She loses 90 seconds
  before she knows which number the question wants.
