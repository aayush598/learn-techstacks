# Mixtures & Alligation — Topic Guide

> Section 1 · Aptitude · Mixtures & Alligation · _Estimated reading time: 30 min for the full topic_

## What the pre-test asks in this topic

The aptitude section regularly includes **1 mixture or alligation question**. The shapes that appear:

1. **Ratio** — "In what ratio must milk and water be mixed so that the ratio is 2 : 3?"
2. **Add a liquid** — "How much water must be added so that the ratio becomes 3 : 4?"
3. **Remove a liquid** — "If 10 litres of water is removed, what is the new concentration?"
4. **Replace part of the mixture** — "12 litres is drawn out and refilled with milk."
5. **Weighted average** — "30 kg at ₹120/kg and 20 kg at ₹90/kg — find the mixture price."

## Learning objectives

After this topic you should be able to:

- State the **alligation rule** in the right direction: `Q_cheap : Q_dear = (C_dear − C_mean) : (C_mean − C_cheap)`.
- Convert a **milk : water ratio** into a percentage and use alligation on it.
- Solve any **add/remove** question by holding the **solute amount constant**.
- Explain why **removing part of the mixture leaves the concentration unchanged**, and why removing
  **pure water** raises it.
- Perform a **replacement in two steps** (remove, then add) and verify the volume is restored.
- Compute a **weighted average** and reject the simple-average distractor.
- Recognise the **five shapes** of mixture question from the stem's verb.
- Finish a mixture question in **70 seconds**.

## Quick solving procedure

1. **Name the shape** (5 s): ratio / add / remove / replace / weighted average.
2. **Set up the two quantities** (10 s): solute amount S and total volume V.
3. **Pick the method** (10 s): alligation for ratios, constant solute for add/remove, two steps for
   replacement, weighted mean for the reverse direction.
4. **Sanity-check** (10 s): is the answer between the inputs? Is the volume restored? Did I use
   percentages for alligation?

## Key takeaways

- ✅ **Solute and volume are the only two things you track.** Everything else follows from them.
- ✅ **Alligation: cheaper mixture first.** The bigger difference pairs with the weaker mixture.
- ✅ **Percentages, never ratios**, inside alligation.
- ✅ **Removing part of the mixture keeps the concentration unchanged.**
- ✅ **Removing pure water raises the concentration** — the solute stays.
- ✅ **Replacement is two steps**: remove, then add. Then check the volume.
- ✅ **Weighted average**, not simple average, when quantities differ.
- ✅ **70 seconds, then move.**
- ⚠️ **Meera's warning:** she read "10 litres of water" as "10 litres of the mixture" and lost a
  mark. Read the verb and the noun.
