# Infosys Specific — Company Culture Interview Questions and Answers

## Q1: Why do you fit Infosys's culture?
**A:** Infosys values client value, integrity, and continuous learning — I model continuous learning (open source, internships), communicate transparently (early risk-raising), and take ownership of outcomes, all of which match.

## Q2: How do you feel about working in a large organisation?
**A:** Productively: large orgs mean strong processes, diverse projects, and mentorship I'd use well; I'm organised enough to thrive amid the process, not fight it.

## Q3: What is your understanding of Infosys's 'Client Value' principle?
**A:** That output means nothing unless it moves the client's business — I bring that by asking 'what does this deliver?' and wiring data work back to business outcomes.

## Q4: How would you handle a situation where a client request conflicts with Infosys policies?
**A:** Raise it with the account lead with a professional explanation, document the concern, and follow the escalation path — integrity is a deliverable too.

## Q5: How do you work with global, distributed teams?
**A:** Written clarity over verbal shortcuts: documented decisions, timezone-aware async communication, and clear ownership — I've worked asynchronously on open source and remote internships.

## Q6: What do you know about Infosys's sustainability efforts?
**A:** Infosys has been carbon-neutral in its operations for years and runs 'Infosys Zero' initiatives — mentioning it shows research depth and value alignment beyond money.

## Q7: How do you view teamwork across cultures?
**A:** As a skill: different timezones and communication norms are just another interface to engineer — I ask, assume good intent, and write things down so alignment holds.

## Q8: What work environment do you prefer?
**A:** One with feedback loops and ownership: clear scope, honest reviews, and the autonomy to debug my own failures — that's what Infosys's project teams provide.

## Q9: How do you handle a senior colleague's differing work style?
**A:** Adapt my interface to theirs (detail vs big-picture, sync vs async) without changing my technical standards — working styles are constraints, not threats.

## Q10: What does integrity mean to you in software?
**A:** Saying 'I don't know', logging what actually happened, not misrepresenting progress, and owning mistakes — the technical translation of Infosys's integrity value.

## Q11: Tell me about a time you demonstrated 05 company culture (STAR method).
**A:** STAR: Situation (set context), Task (your goal), Action (your specific steps + decisions), Result (measurable outcome + learning). Keep it under 90 seconds, focus on YOUR actions, and end with what you learned.

## Q12: How does 05 company culture show up in a typical IT services project at Infosys?
**A:** Every client project runs on shared processes, teams and deadlines; 05 company culture determines how smoothly collaboration and delivery go, so interviewers probe it via past behaviour with hypotheses like this.

## Q13: What is your honest personal strength and a related weakness around 05 company culture?
**A:** Pick a genuine strength backed by a concrete example, then a real weakness you are actively improving, with the improvement plan — never a disguised strength, never something disqualifying.

## Q14: Give a self-introduction that showcases 05 company culture.
**A:** Structure: who you are, education, relevant internships/projects (with tech + result), achievements, and why this role — in that order, 60-90 seconds, ending on why you fit the SP/DSE profile.

## Q15: How do you behave when a teammate does not exhibit 05 company culture?
**A:** Talk to them privately, understand their constraints first, offer help or clear expectations aligned to the deadline, escalate only if needed — leadership by influence, not authority.

## Q16: Describe a failure caused by lack of 05 company culture and your corrective action.
**A:** Own the failure without blaming others, quantify what went wrong, state the exact corrective action and the prevention mechanism now in place, and close on the lesson retained.

## Q17: Why should we hire you? Base the answer on 05 company culture.
**A:** Differentiate with evidence: internship projects, contributions (merged PRs, hackathon finalist, IEEE publication), breadth across Python/AI/full-stack, and a clear plan for the DSE role — numbers and facts over adjectives.

## Q18: How do you handle pressure and deadlines in relation to 05 company culture?
**A:** Break the work into milestones, prioritise by impact, communicate early on blockers, and protect quality gates; give a real project example where this worked under a tight deadline.

## Q19: What questions would you ask the panel that also show 05 company culture?
**A:** Ask about the tech stack you will work on, the training roadmap, project exposure in the first year, and how growth to senior roles is structured — shows seriousness and long-term commitment.

**A:** Pick a number on a 5/10 scale, justify with concrete evidence, and immediately connect to what you are doing to improve — confident, honest and growth-oriented tones win.
