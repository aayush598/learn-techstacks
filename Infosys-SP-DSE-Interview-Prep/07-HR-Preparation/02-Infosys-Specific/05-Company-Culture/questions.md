**100 Interview Q&A — Infosys Specialist Programmer (SP) / Digital Specialist Engineer (DSE)**

1. **Why do you fit Infosys's culture?**
   - Infosys values client value, integrity, and continuous learning — I model continuous learning (open source, internships), communicate transparently (early risk-raising), and take ownership of outcomes, all of which match.

2. **How do you feel about working in a large organisation?**
   - Productively: large orgs mean strong processes, diverse projects, and mentorship I'd use well; I'm organised enough to thrive amid the process, not fight it.

3. **What is your understanding of Infosys's 'Client Value' principle?**
   - That output means nothing unless it moves the client's business — I bring that by asking 'what does this deliver?' and wiring data work back to business outcomes.

4. **How would you handle a situation where a client request conflicts with Infosys policies?**
   - Raise it with the account lead with a professional explanation, document the concern, and follow the escalation path — integrity is a deliverable too.

5. **How do you work with global, distributed teams?**
   - Written clarity over verbal shortcuts: documented decisions, timezone-aware async communication, and clear ownership — I've worked asynchronously on open source and remote internships.

6. **What do you know about Infosys's sustainability efforts?**
   - Infosys has been carbon-neutral in its operations for years and runs 'Infosys Zero' initiatives — mentioning it shows research depth and value alignment beyond money.

7. **How do you view teamwork across cultures?**
   - As a skill: different timezones and communication norms are just another interface to engineer — I ask, assume good intent, and write things down so alignment holds.

8. **What work environment do you prefer?**
   - One with feedback loops and ownership: clear scope, honest reviews, and the autonomy to debug my own failures — that's what Infosys's project teams provide.

9. **How do you handle a senior colleague's differing work style?**
   - Adapt my interface to theirs (detail vs big-picture, sync vs async) without changing my technical standards — working styles are constraints, not threats.

10. **What does integrity mean to you in software?**
   - Saying 'I don't know', logging what actually happened, not misrepresenting progress, and owning mistakes — the technical translation of Infosys's integrity value.
