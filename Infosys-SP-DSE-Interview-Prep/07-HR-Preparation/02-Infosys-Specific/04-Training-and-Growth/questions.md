# Infosys Specific — Training And Growth Interview Questions and Answers

## Q1: How do you feel about the initial training period?
**A:** I see training as an investment I'd optimise: I'd go beyond the curriculum — practise extra problems, learn the tools deeply, and help peers — and enter the project phase stronger than the minimum.

## Q2: What do you expect from Infosys's training programme?
**A:** Structured foundations in the stack (Data Engineering tooling, DSA, databases), mentoring, and real project simulation — I expect to absorb the discipline as much as the content.

## Q3: How do you handle learning a new technology quickly?
**A:** My method: official quickstart for 30 minutes, build a tiny end-to-end example, then a real-ish task with tests — I've ramped on LangChain, LangGraph, Streamlit, and FastAPI exactly this way.

## Q4: Are you willing to relocate/work from any Infosys location?
**A:** Yes — flexibility is part of the deal and I'm eager to work from Infosys campuses and client sites: I've prepared for relocation as part of the specialist onboarding.

## Q5: How do you plan your growth in the first 3 years?
**A:** Specialist track: master the DSE craft (pipelines, databases, quality), earn internal certifications, take on harder modules, and eventually mentor juniors — growth by deepening, not hopscotching.

## Q6: What certifications are you willing to pursue?
**A:** Cloud (AWS/azure data certs), database (PostgreSQL), and data engineering certs — relevant, employer-aligned, and compatible with project work.

## Q7: How do you handle a role that starts simpler than your expectations?
**A:** With humility and intent: every production codebase has depth — I'd master the simplest module completely before asking for more; credibility is built before ambition is granted.

## Q8: What does career growth look like at Infosys?
**A:** From Specialist to Specialist Lead/Architect tracks via performance, certifications, and increasingly complex ownership — I've researched the SP path so my answer shows I'm planning, not just hoping.

## Q9: How open are you to switching domains within Infosys later?
**A:** Open — data engineering, analytics, or AI consulting later; the common thread is the data/AI direction, and I'd follow the work that challenges me most.

## Q10: What will make you successful in a consulting environment?
**A:** Consulting is delivery discipline: understanding the client's business driver, coding to the contract, communicating progress early — the exact behaviours I've built shipping products against deadlines.

## Q11: Tell me about a time you demonstrated 04 training and growth (STAR method).
**A:** STAR: Situation (set context), Task (your goal), Action (your specific steps + decisions), Result (measurable outcome + learning). Keep it under 90 seconds, focus on YOUR actions, and end with what you learned.
