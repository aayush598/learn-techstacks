1. **How do you feel about the initial training period?**
   - I see training as an investment I'd optimise: I'd go beyond the curriculum — practise extra problems, learn the tools deeply, and help peers — and enter the project phase stronger than the minimum.

2. **What do you expect from Infosys's training programme?**
   - Structured foundations in the stack (Data Engineering tooling, DSA, databases), mentoring, and real project simulation — I expect to absorb the discipline as much as the content.

3. **How do you handle learning a new technology quickly?**
   - My method: official quickstart for 30 minutes, build a tiny end-to-end example, then a real-ish task with tests — I've ramped on LangChain, LangGraph, Streamlit, and FastAPI exactly this way.

4. **Are you willing to relocate/work from any Infosys location?**
   - Yes — flexibility is part of the deal and I'm eager to work from Infosys campuses and client sites: I've prepared for relocation as part of the specialist onboarding.

5. **How do you plan your growth in the first 3 years?**
   - Specialist track: master the DSE craft (pipelines, databases, quality), earn internal certifications, take on harder modules, and eventually mentor juniors — growth by deepening, not hopscotching.

6. **What certifications are you willing to pursue?**
   - Cloud (AWS/azure data certs), database (PostgreSQL), and data engineering certs — relevant, employer-aligned, and compatible with project work.

7. **How do you handle a role that starts simpler than your expectations?**
   - With humility and intent: every production codebase has depth — I'd master the simplest module completely before asking for more; credibility is built before ambition is granted.

8. **What does career growth look like at Infosys?**
   - From Specialist to Specialist Lead/Architect tracks via performance, certifications, and increasingly complex ownership — I've researched the SP path so my answer shows I'm planning, not just hoping.

9. **How open are you to switching domains within Infosys later?**
   - Open — data engineering, analytics, or AI consulting later; the common thread is the data/AI direction, and I'd follow the work that challenges me most.

10. **What will make you successful in a consulting environment?**
   - Consulting is delivery discipline: understanding the client's business driver, coding to the contract, communicating progress early — the exact behaviours I've built shipping products against deadlines.
