# Infosys Specific — Why Infosys Interview Questions and Answers

## Q1: Why do you want to join Infosys?
**A:** Three specific reasons: (1) Infosys's data & AI scale gives SP DSE exposure to industrial datasets and pipelines I can't see anywhere else, (2) the structured specialist growth path matches my plan to deepen into data engineering + ML, (3) a company investing in Topaz/AI means my GenAI skills meet real enterprise problems immediately.

## Q2: Why Infosys and not another IT services company?
**A:** Compare deliberately, not dismissively: Infosys's Topaz AI-first push, its training depth for freshers, and its data practice breadth fit my AI/ML profile better than a more general services or product role.

## Q3: Why should we hire you over other candidates?
**A:** Because I bring shipped AI work, not just coursework: three internships in agentic/DS, projects like ScriptVector and MigratorGen, merged open-source PRs to Agno, and SIH finalist discipline — plus the Python/DSA/DBMS fundamentals SP DSE needs.

## Q4: What attracts you to the SP DSE role specifically?
**A:** It combines exactly what I like: solving real data problems (DSA + SQL + data pipelines) in a specialist stream with Infosys's training and project variety — a structured path from data structures to data engineering.

## Q5: How do you align with Infosys's transformation to an AI-first company?
**A:** I've lived the AI lifecycle — RAG, agentic frameworks, evaluation — and I can bring that into data engineering work; joining a company explicitly investing in Topaz means my skills are a current asset, not a future one.

## Q6: What is your 2-year vision at Infosys?
**A:** Year 1: master the SP DSE core — DSA, databases, data engineering tools — on real client projects. Year 2: specialise into data engineering/AI pipelines and start contributing to internal engineering IP and knowledge-sharing.

## Q7: Why are you choosing services over a product company?
**A:** Services offer breadth: different domains, datasets, and techniques on a fast cycle — early-career breadth I value more than product depth right now; I can always deepen later.

## Q8: What do you know about Infosys's specialist (SP) path?
**A:** Infosys Specialist Programme tracks distinguish top freshers into specialised streams (Data Engineering/System/Digital etc.) with dedicated training, mentors, and faster growth — SP DSE means I'm committing to a technical stream, not a generic bench.

## Q9: Why DSE — why not System or other specialist tracks?
**A:** DSE is the intersection of my two loves — data structures and applied AI: I want to build the pipelines and platforms that make AI real, and DSE is the direct route to that craft.

## Q10: Are you open to roles different from what you applied for?
**A:** Yes — my fundamentals (Python, DSA, SQL) transfer; I'd say I'm flexible on role as long as it's in the data/AI direction, and I'll bring the same energy to whatever the business needs.
