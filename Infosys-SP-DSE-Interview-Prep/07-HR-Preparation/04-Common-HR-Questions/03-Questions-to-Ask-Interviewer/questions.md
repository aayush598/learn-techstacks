**100 Interview Q&A — Infosys Specialist Programmer (SP) / Digital Specialist Engineer (DSE)**

1. **What would a typical first 6 months look like for a new DSE?**
   - Show intent to onboard well: training scope, first-project assignment, and mentorship structure — also signals you're planning, not just landing.

2. **What does success look like in this role after one year?**
   - You're asking about measurable expectations — the strongest single question to align your effort with the team's definition of 'good'.

3. **What is the team's current data/AI focus and biggest challenge?**
   - Data practice at Infosys is investing hard in AI — this question shows you see the big picture and are already thinking about contribution.

4. **How does the SP DSE path progress into a specialist lead/architect?**
   - Shows you understand the specialist programme and are career-planning within the organisation, not hopping at the first chance.

5. **What are the growth opportunities for someone with an AI/ML background?**
   - Positions you to be placed where your strengths fit, and shows confidence in your differentiation — while staying in the data track.

6. **What are the current technical challenges the data team faces?**
   - A team-solving question: you're asking to be useful, not just employed — interviewers remember candidates who think about work before joining.

7. **What would make a candidate stand out in this team's eyes?**
   - A direct, low-risk way to hear how they'd want you to perform — and any answer you get becomes your onboarding roadmap.

8. **How is feedback and performance review structured here?**
   - Shows you want to improve measurably (matches Infosys's development culture) and are comfortable with honest evaluation.

9. **What technologies does the data practice currently standardise on?**
   - Evidence of real interest in the craft — and it lets you map your skills (Python, SQL, pipelines) onto their stack immediately.

10. **What advice would you give someone starting here?**
   - Respectful, open-ended, and relationship-building — and it hands them the chance to mentor you before you've even started.

11. **Tell me about a time you demonstrated 03 questions to ask interviewer (STAR method).**
   - STAR: Situation (set context), Task (your goal), Action (your specific steps + decisions), Result (measurable outcome + learning). Keep it under 90 seconds, focus on YOUR actions, and end with what you learned.
