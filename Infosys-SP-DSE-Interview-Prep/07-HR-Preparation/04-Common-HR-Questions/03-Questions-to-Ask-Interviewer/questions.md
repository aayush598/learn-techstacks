# Common Hr Questions — Questions To Ask Interviewer Interview Questions and Answers

## Q1: What would a typical first 6 months look like for a new DSE?
**A:** Show intent to onboard well: training scope, first-project assignment, and mentorship structure — also signals you're planning, not just landing.

## Q2: What does success look like in this role after one year?
**A:** You're asking about measurable expectations — the strongest single question to align your effort with the team's definition of 'good'.

## Q3: What is the team's current data/AI focus and biggest challenge?
**A:** Data practice at Infosys is investing hard in AI — this question shows you see the big picture and are already thinking about contribution.

## Q4: How does the SP DSE path progress into a specialist lead/architect?
**A:** Shows you understand the specialist programme and are career-planning within the organisation, not hopping at the first chance.

## Q5: What are the growth opportunities for someone with an AI/ML background?
**A:** Positions you to be placed where your strengths fit, and shows confidence in your differentiation — while staying in the data track.

## Q6: What are the current technical challenges the data team faces?
**A:** A team-solving question: you're asking to be useful, not just employed — interviewers remember candidates who think about work before joining.

## Q7: What would make a candidate stand out in this team's eyes?
**A:** A direct, low-risk way to hear how they'd want you to perform — and any answer you get becomes your onboarding roadmap.

## Q8: How is feedback and performance review structured here?
**A:** Shows you want to improve measurably (matches Infosys's development culture) and are comfortable with honest evaluation.

## Q9: What technologies does the data practice currently standardise on?
**A:** Evidence of real interest in the craft — and it lets you map your skills (Python, SQL, pipelines) onto their stack immediately.

## Q10: What advice would you give someone starting here?
**A:** Respectful, open-ended, and relationship-building — and it hands them the chance to mentor you before you've even started.

## Q11: Tell me about a time you demonstrated 03 questions to ask interviewer (STAR method).
**A:** STAR: Situation (set context), Task (your goal), Action (your specific steps + decisions), Result (measurable outcome + learning). Keep it under 90 seconds, focus on YOUR actions, and end with what you learned.

## Q12: How does 03 questions to ask interviewer show up in a typical IT services project at Infosys?
**A:** Every client project runs on shared processes, teams and deadlines; 03 questions to ask interviewer determines how smoothly collaboration and delivery go, so interviewers probe it via past behaviour with hypotheses like this.

## Q13: What is your honest personal strength and a related weakness around 03 questions to ask interviewer?
**A:** Pick a genuine strength backed by a concrete example, then a real weakness you are actively improving, with the improvement plan — never a disguised strength, never something disqualifying.

## Q14: Give a self-introduction that showcases 03 questions to ask interviewer.
**A:** Structure: who you are, education, relevant internships/projects (with tech + result), achievements, and why this role — in that order, 60-90 seconds, ending on why you fit the SP/DSE profile.

## Q15: How do you behave when a teammate does not exhibit 03 questions to ask interviewer?
**A:** Talk to them privately, understand their constraints first, offer help or clear expectations aligned to the deadline, escalate only if needed — leadership by influence, not authority.

## Q16: Describe a failure caused by lack of 03 questions to ask interviewer and your corrective action.
**A:** Own the failure without blaming others, quantify what went wrong, state the exact corrective action and the prevention mechanism now in place, and close on the lesson retained.

## Q17: Why should we hire you? Base the answer on 03 questions to ask interviewer.
**A:** Differentiate with evidence: internship projects, contributions (merged PRs, hackathon finalist, IEEE publication), breadth across Python/AI/full-stack, and a clear plan for the DSE role — numbers and facts over adjectives.

## Q18: How do you handle pressure and deadlines in relation to 03 questions to ask interviewer?
**A:** Break the work into milestones, prioritise by impact, communicate early on blockers, and protect quality gates; give a real project example where this worked under a tight deadline.

## Q19: What questions would you ask the panel that also show 03 questions to ask interviewer?
**A:** Ask about the tech stack you will work on, the training roadmap, project exposure in the first year, and how growth to senior roles is structured — shows seriousness and long-term commitment.

## Q20: Rate yourself on 03 questions to ask interviewer and justify it.
**A:** Pick a number on a 5/10 scale, justify with concrete evidence, and immediately connect to what you are doing to improve — confident, honest and growth-oriented tones win.
