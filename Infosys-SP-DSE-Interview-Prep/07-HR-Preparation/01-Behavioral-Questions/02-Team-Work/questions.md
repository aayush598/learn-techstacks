# Behavioral Questions — Team Work Interview Questions and Answers

## Q1: Describe a time you worked in a team.
**A:** Use my SIH 2024 finalist project: a 4-member team built a full solution from ideation to demo under a deadline — my role covered the AI/backend, and we soured by merging modules daily (Git), not at the end.

## Q2: How do you handle disagreements in a team?
**A:** I separate ego from evidence: discuss the constraint (time vs correctness vs cost) rather than the person, write down both options' trade-offs, and if it's not urgent, prototype the cheaper option to decide with data — e.g., choosing between prompt engineering vs fine-tuning for a demo.

## Q3: How do you handle a teammate who isn't pulling their weight?
**A:** First: clarify if it's a blocker or motivation (ask, don't accuse). Next: reassign scope to their strengths and offer pair sessions. If it persists, escalate with facts — a professional, non-personal approach interviewers want to hear.

## Q4: What role do you usually take in a group?
**A:** I'm naturally the systems person — I build the integration layer (APIs, data flow, CI) that makes everyone else's work composable; but I've also learned to step back and let specialists lead their part to avoid being a bottleneck.

## Q5: How do you divide tasks in a project?
**A:** By ownership boundaries, not arbitrary files: each person owns a module with a defined interface, integrations happen on a shared branch with CI tests — the git workflow is itself the coordination mechanism.

## Q6: Tell me about a team achievement you're proud of.
**A:** ScriptVector shipped end-to-end by turning the 'suggest relevant snippets' idea into a RAG pipeline (Gemini embeddings + Agno + SQLite) — the team contribution was my embedding/index design that made it fast enough to feel usable.

## Q7: How do you give constructive feedback?
**A:** Situation-Behaviour-Impact: specific example, the behaviour, the consequence — then a concrete suggestion. Framed as 'let's improve the outcome', never as blame.

## Q8: How do you onboard a new team member?
**A:** A short architecture walkthrough, a starter issue with a clear contract, and pairing on the first merge — I've done this informally at internships and it accelerates everyone.

## Q9: How do you keep the team motivated under crunch?
**A:** Visible progress: a shared board with small wins, slicing tasks into shippable chunks, and celebrating checkpoints — momentum beats coercion on a deadline.

## Q10: Have you led a group? How?
**A:** I've led technical workstreams — I set the architecture, distributed tasks by strengths, and unblocked failures — while remaining hands-on; leadership for me is coordination + accountability, not authority.

## Q11: Tell me about a time you demonstrated 02 team work (STAR method).
**A:** STAR: Situation (set context), Task (your goal), Action (your specific steps + decisions), Result (measurable outcome + learning). Keep it under 90 seconds, focus on YOUR actions, and end with what you learned.

## Q12: How does 02 team work show up in a typical IT services project at Infosys?

## Q14: Give a self-introduction that showcases 02 team work.

## Q15: How do you behave when a teammate does not exhibit 02 team work?
**A:** Talk to them privately, understand their constraints first, offer help or clear expectations aligned to the deadline, escalate only if needed — leadership by influence, not authority.

## Q16: Describe a failure caused by lack of 02 team work and your corrective action.
