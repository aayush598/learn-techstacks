1. **Introduce yourself.**
   - I'm Aayush Gid, a final-year B.Tech ECE student (2022-26) with a strong software engineering profile: I build AI/ML applications on Python — agentic AI at my internships, RAG systems like ScriptVector, and open-source contributions to Agno — and I'm applying because I love turning complex problems into reliable systems, which is what the SP DSE role is about.

2. **Tell me about yourself in one minute.**
   - Script it in 3 beats: (1) education + current status, (2) the two things that show depth — my AI/MLE work (Krip AI, Clone Futura, NullClass) and my shipped projects (MigratorGen, ScriptVector, OpenRTL ai), (3) why Infosys SP DSE (scale, data engineering challenges, structured growth). Practice it out loud until it's 55-60 seconds.

3. **Walk me through your resume.**
   - Group by time: internships (Krip AI — Agentic AI; Clone Futura — AI Agent Developer; NullClass — Data Science), then projects (MigratorGen, ScriptVector, OpenRTL ai), then achievements (SIH 2024 finalist, Techfest top-5, IEEE conference paper). For each: what problem, what you built, what result.

4. **What are your strengths?**
   - Deep, applied engineering: I can take a GenAI concept (RAG, agents, evaluations) and ship a working, testable API on it (FastAPI + Docker + CI/CD), which I proved across three internships and open-source PRs to Agno.

5. **What are your weaknesses?**
   - Pick a truthful, non-fatal one and show remediation: e.g., 'I sometimes dive deep into implementation before clarifying requirements — I now write a one-page spec and get it reviewed before coding, which saved rework in my script-migration project.'

6. **What is your biggest achievement?**
   - Being an SIH 2024 national finalist and publishing an IEEE conference paper — but frame your favourites: the IEEE paper shows research rigour, the finalist shows working under pressure on a real problem.

7. **Where do you see yourself in five years?**
   - Growing from a strong engineer into a technical lead on data/AI platforms — I want to master industrial-scale data engineering and ML systems first, which the SP DSE role's breadth enables at Infosys.

8. **Tell me about your family background.**
   - Be brief and positive — family support for your engineering journey, values of discipline and learning; keep it to 3-4 sentences; interviewers ask to gauge personality, not personal data.

9. **What is your favourite subject and why?**
   - Data structures and machine learning — because both reward clean reasoning: DSA gives provable efficiency, ML gives systems that learn from evidence; my DSA fundamentals and AI internships both trace back to that.

10. **What are your hobbies/interests?**
   - Open-source contribution (I've had PRs merged into Agno) and building side projects — frame hobbies as evidence of self-driven learning rather than just recreation.

11. **Describe your daily routine / time management.**
   - I block my day: deep work in the morning (projects/learning), internship/collab work in the afternoon, and evenings open-source/reading — consistent habits, not just during exam season.

12. **How did you prepare for this interview?**
   - Three tracks: (1) DSA revision on arrays/DP/graphs (SP DSE's known focus), (2) a deep-dive on my ML/GenAI projects so I can explain architecture and trade-offs, (3) Infosys research — its Data & Analytics practice to speak the company's language.
