# Ml Fundamentals — Unsupervised Learning Interview Questions and Answers

## Q1: What is unsupervised learning?
**A:** Finding structure in unlabelled data: clustering (grouping), dimensionality reduction (compression), and anomaly detection — no labels to predict.

## Q2: What is k-means clustering?
**A:** Partitions points into k clusters by iterating assign-to-nearest-centroid then recompute-centroid; sensitive to initialisation and k; use k-means++ and elbow/silhouette.

## Q3: What is hierarchical clustering?
**A:** Agglomerate (bottom-up merge) or divide (top-down split) producing a dendrogram — no k required upfront; good for small-to-medium data.

## Q4: What is DBSCAN?
**A:** Density-based clustering: clusters are dense regions separated by sparse areas; finds arbitrary shapes, robust to outliers, needs a min-samples/epsilon setting.

## Q5: What is PCA (Principal Component Analysis)?
**A:** A dimensionality-reduction technique projecting data onto the orthogonal directions of maximum variance — decorrelating features, denoising, visualising high-D data.

## Q6: What is the difference between PCA and t-SNE/UMAP?
**A:** PCA: linear, global variance, good for pre-processing. t-SNE/UMAP: non-linear, preserve local structure, built for visualisation — different objectives entirely.

## Q7: What is anomaly/outlier detection?
**A:** Detecting points unlike the bulk: statistical (z-score/IQR), isolation forest, one-class SVM; typical for fraud/network monitoring.

## Q8: What is association rule mining?
**A:** Finding co-occurrence rules (A -> B) with support/confidence/lift, e.g., Apriori for market-basket analysis.

## Q9: How do you evaluate clustering?
**A:** Internal metrics: silhouette score, inertia; external (when labels exist): Rand index, mutual information — no single ground-truth measure.

## Q10: What is a Gaussian Mixture Model?
**A:** Data as a mixture of k Gaussian components each with own mean/covariance; EM estimates parameters — soft clustering with probability memberships.

## Q11: What is dimensionality reduction vs feature selection?
**A:** Reduction transforms features into a compressed space (PCA); selection picks a subset of original features (removes irrelevant/redundant columns) — select for interpretability.
