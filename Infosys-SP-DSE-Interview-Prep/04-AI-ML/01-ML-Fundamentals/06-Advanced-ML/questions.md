1. **What is ensemble learning?**
   - Combining several models to beat a single one: bagging (parallel, reduces variance), boosting (sequential, reduces bias), stacking (meta-learner).

2. **What is bagging vs boosting?**
   - Bagging trains models on bootstrap samples and averages (Random Forest); boosting fits each model to residual errors of the prior (AdaBoost, XGBoost) — variance vs bias reduction.

3. **What is XGBoost vs Random Forest?**
   - XGBoost is gradient-boosted trees with regularisation, native missing-value handling, and depth-wise growth — often the tabular-data winner after tuning.

4. **What is gradient boosting?**
   - Sequentially adding trees trained on the negative gradient of the loss w.r.t. predictions — the generalisation of AdaBoost powering GBDT family.

5. **How do you handle class imbalance beyond metrics?**
   - Resampling (SMOTE, undersample), class weights in the loss, focal loss for hard examples, and adjusted thresholds — combined, not just one.

6. **What is a data generator / pipeline for training?**
   - Tensor slicing into batches with augmentation and shuffling, iterated over epochs — the framework-agnostic training loop concept.

7. **What is hyperparameter tuning by grid/random/Bayesian search?**
   - Grid exhausts the product space; random samples it; Bayesian (Optuna) models the objective to guide searches — random beats grid at equal budgets, Bayesian beats both with time.

8. **What is the trade-off between model interpretability and performance?**
   - Linear/trees are explainable but weaker; deep models excel but need SHAP/LIME to justify — regulated domains demand the former, scale allows the latter.

9. **What is transfer learning at the ML feature level?**
   - Reusing embeddings/pre-trained representations as frozen features on a new task — smaller labelled data needed, faster convergence.

10. **What is a time-series forecasting nuance?**
   - Order matters: no random shuffling, walk-forward validation, lag/recursive features, and stationarity checks — supervised framing is possible but leak-prone.
