# Llm And Genai — Advanced Genai Interview Questions and Answers

## Q1: What is multi-modal GenAI?
**A:** Models handling text+image+audio (GPT-4o, Gemini) — one encoder space for mixed inputs; tasks like image-QA and visual reasoning.

## Q2: What is fine-tuning an LLM vs prompting (when to fine-tune)?
**A:** Fine-tune when style/domain/format must be baked in, cost of prompt engineering exceeds training, or latency/vocab demands; evidence: prompt-first, fine-tune-last.

## Q3: What is a completion guardrail system?
**A:** Input filtering (prompt injection), output moderation, and retry loops — layered safety around raw generation.

## Q4: What is evaluation of generative outputs?
**A:** LLM-as-judge, ROUGE/BLEU for similarity, groundedness/faithfulness checks, and human raters — no single metric; combine with task-specific scoring.

## Q5: What is the hallucination-reduction toolkit?
**A:** RAG grounding, low temperature, constrained decoding, self-verification (ask the model to produce evidence), and retrieval feedback loops.

## Q6: What is tool-use / chaining for reliability?
**A:** Delegating computation to deterministic functions (calculators, APIs, DBs) instead of hoping the LLM computes — the reliability pattern behind agents.

## Q7: What are the safety and bias concerns in GenAI?
**A:** Harmful content, copyright, data leakage, and stereotyping — model-level alignment plus app-level filters and logging of audits.

## Q8: What is a RAG-as-a-service architecture?
**A:** Pick embeddings, vector DB, chunker, reranker, and LLM as separable services — the components map directly to what you built in ScriptVector.

## Q9: What is an agentic loop added to generation?
**A:** Generate -> observe tool result -> plan -> act, looped until done — generation alone becomes a component; orchestration becomes the product.

## Q10: What are the latency/cost levers for production GenAI?
**A:** Smaller models for simple paths, caching, streaming, batching, quantisation, and routing between model tiers — the engineering optimisation discussion.
