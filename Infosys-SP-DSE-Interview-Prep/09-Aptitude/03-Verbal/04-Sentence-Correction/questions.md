1. **What is a sentence-correction question?**
   - Spotting the grammatical/usage error in a sentence and choosing the corrected version — testing agreement, tense, parallelism, idioms, and modifiers.

2. **How do you spot subject-verb agreement errors?**
   - Find the verb, find its subject (skipping prepositional phrases), and match number — 'The group of players run' → 'runs' (subject 'group').

3. **What is parallelism and how do you fix it?**
   - Listed items must match grammatical form: 'to swim, jogging, and to cycle' → 'to swim, to jog, to cycle' — the parallel structure rule.

4. **What is a dangling modifier?**
   - A modifier with no clear subject: 'Running to the bus, my shoe fell off' → the shoe didn't run; rewrite to 'As I ran...' — attach modifiers to their subject.

5. **How do you use the correct tense in a sentence?**
   - Match the time context: 'She said she will come' → 'would come' (backshift); 'By next year, I will have graduated' (future perfect) — sequence signals the tense.

6. **What is the difference between 'between' and 'among'?**
   - between for two items; among for three or more — the classic preposition rule; 'among the three of us' vs 'between you and me'.

7. **What is an idiom error?**
   - Fixed phrases used wrongly: 'different than' → 'different from'; 'comply to' → 'comply with' — recognise the standard-preposition pairs.

8. **What is the fastest way to correct a sentence?**
   - Identify the error TYPE first (agreement, tense, modifier, parallelism, idiom), then eliminate options that don't fix it — pattern diagnosis beats reading options as sentences.
