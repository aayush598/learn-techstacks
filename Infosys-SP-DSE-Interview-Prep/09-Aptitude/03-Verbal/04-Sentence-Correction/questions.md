# Verbal — Sentence Correction Interview Questions and Answers

## Q1: What is a sentence-correction question?
**A:** Spotting the grammatical/usage error in a sentence and choosing the corrected version — testing agreement, tense, parallelism, idioms, and modifiers.

## Q2: How do you spot subject-verb agreement errors?
**A:** Find the verb, find its subject (skipping prepositional phrases), and match number — 'The group of players run' → 'runs' (subject 'group').

## Q3: What is parallelism and how do you fix it?
**A:** Listed items must match grammatical form: 'to swim, jogging, and to cycle' → 'to swim, to jog, to cycle' — the parallel structure rule.

## Q4: What is a dangling modifier?
**A:** A modifier with no clear subject: 'Running to the bus, my shoe fell off' → the shoe didn't run; rewrite to 'As I ran...' — attach modifiers to their subject.

## Q5: How do you use the correct tense in a sentence?
**A:** Match the time context: 'She said she will come' → 'would come' (backshift); 'By next year, I will have graduated' (future perfect) — sequence signals the tense.

## Q6: What is the difference between 'between' and 'among'?
**A:** between for two items; among for three or more — the classic preposition rule; 'among the three of us' vs 'between you and me'.

## Q7: What is an idiom error?
**A:** Fixed phrases used wrongly: 'different than' → 'different from'; 'comply to' → 'comply with' — recognise the standard-preposition pairs.

## Q8: What is the fastest way to correct a sentence?
**A:** Identify the error TYPE first (agreement, tense, modifier, parallelism, idiom), then eliminate options that don't fix it — pattern diagnosis beats reading options as sentences.

## Q9: State the formula used to solve 04 sentence correction problems quickly in aptitude tests.
**A:** Every aptitude topic rests on a small set of formulas and unit-adjustments; write the formula, plug the numbers, and always sanity-check the units (hours/minutes, percent, ratio) before finalising — most errors are unit errors.

## Q10: Describe a shortcut technique for 04 sentence correction.
**A:** Look for symmetric/divisible structure, cancellation in fractions, or base-value anchoring (e.g., assume a convenient total like 100 or LCM). Shortcuts reduce arithmetic but should not skip the conceptual check.

## Q12: What are the typical mistakes students make in 04 sentence correction?
