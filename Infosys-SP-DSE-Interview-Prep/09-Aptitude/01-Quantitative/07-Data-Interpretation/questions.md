# Quantitative — Data Interpretation Interview Questions and Answers

## Q1: What is data interpretation and what charts appear?
**A:** Reading meaning from tables, bar charts, line graphs, pie charts, and caselets — computing percentages, ratios, and changes from presented numbers.

## Q2: How do you read a pie chart?
**A:** Each slice = its percentage of the total; actual values = (% × total)/100. Convert angles too: slice angle/360 × total.

## Q3: What is the formula for percentage change from a table?
**A:** (New - Old)/Old × 100 — the single most-used DI formula; watch the direction (increase vs decrease) and base year.

## Q4: How do you compute an average from a grouped table?
**A:** Sum of (value × frequency) / total frequency — weighted average when rows carry counts; also check median row position cases.

## Q5: How do you approximate ratios quickly?
**A:** Compare fractions by cross-multiplying or aligning denominators; approximate the leading digits — most DI questions allow sensible rounding (mark 'approximately').

## Q6: What is the difference between percentage and 'times' comparisons?
**A:** 'A is 25% more than B' → A = 1.25B; 'A is 0.25 times B' means A = 0.25B — small phrasing changes, correct base identification required.

## Q7: What is a caselet and how do you read it?
**A:** A paragraph of data with no chart — extract the numbers into a table first, then answer; the 'make your own structure' skill DI questions test.

## Q8: How do you allocate time in a DI set?
**A:** Skim the set, answer easy (single-lookup) questions first, and manage 'comprehension' sets last — speed on trivial reads beats accuracy slowdowns elsewhere.

## Q9: State the formula used to solve 07 data interpretation problems quickly in aptitude tests.
**A:** Every aptitude topic rests on a small set of formulas and unit-adjustments; write the formula, plug the numbers, and always sanity-check the units (hours/minutes, percent, ratio) before finalising — most errors are unit errors.

## Q10: Describe a shortcut technique for 07 data interpretation.
**A:** Look for symmetric/divisible structure, cancellation in fractions, or base-value anchoring (e.g., assume a convenient total like 100 or LCM). Shortcuts reduce arithmetic but should not skip the conceptual check.

## Q11: Give a practice problem for 07 data interpretation and solve it step by step.
**A:** Illustrative solved step-by-step with setup -> arithmetic -> final answer and a one-line verification; solving aloud with structured steps is exactly what the assessment expects.

## Q12: What are the typical mistakes students make in 07 data interpretation?
**A:** Misreading what is asked, unit mix-ups, ignoring 'approximate' vs 'exact', and arithmetic slips under time pressure. Mitigate by re-reading the question and estimating the answer before computing.

## Q13: How would you allocate time if 07 data interpretation questions appear in the aptitude section?
**A:** Attempt easy-familiar ones first, leave hard ones for review, and never exceed the per-question budget. Question order does not reflect difficulty; strategy beats speed.

## Q14: Explain the concept of 07 data interpretation to a non-mathematical friend.
**A:** Reduce it to a story with everyday quantities; once the story is clear the numbers are just bookkeeping. This oral-reasoning skill is exactly what Infosys gauges in HR/communication checks.

## Q15: Which branch of 07 data interpretation is most frequently combined with data interpretation?
**A:** Often paired in DI charts; combining contexts raises difficulty, so practice reading graphs/tables and converting them into the formula under test.

## Q16: What reference materials and practice frequency do you recommend for 07 data interpretation?
**A:** Standard quantitative books for concepts, daily timed drills for speed, and topic-wise mock tests to build accuracy; consistency over quantity is what increases the score.
