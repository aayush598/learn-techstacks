1. **What are the area formulas for common shapes?**
   - Square s², rectangle l×b, triangle ½×b×h, circle πr², trapezium ½(a+b)h, circumference 2πr — the six formulas aptitude lean on.

2. **What is the Pythagorean theorem?**
   - a² + b² = c² for right triangles — and the triples (3,4,5), (5,12,13), (8,15,17) are worth recognising instantly.

3. **What is the difference between perimeter and area on a rectangle?**
   - Perimeter 2(l+b); area l×b — units length vs area; doubling the sides ×4 the area but only ×2 the perimeter — the scaling relationship.

4. **What is the area of an equilateral triangle?**
   - (√3/4)s²; its height is (√3/2)s. Recognise 30-60-90 and 45-45-90 side ratios for faster solves.

5. **What is the volume of a cylinder, cube, sphere?**
   - Cylinder πr²h, cube s³, cuboid l×b×h, sphere (4/3)πr³, cone (1/3)πr²h — the volume set for mensuration questions.

6. **What is the circumference-radius relationship?**
   - C = 2πr; if the circumference doubles the radius doubles; circles' area grows with r² — scaling relationships recur in geometry.

7. **What is the angle sum of a triangle and an n-gon?**
   - Triangle sum 180°; general n-gon interior sum (n-2)×180° — the base for any polygon-angle breakdown.

8. **What is the area of a triangle from coordinates?**
   - Area = ½|x1(y2-y3) + x2(y3-y1) + x3(y1-y2)| — the shoelace formula for coordinate geometry problems.
