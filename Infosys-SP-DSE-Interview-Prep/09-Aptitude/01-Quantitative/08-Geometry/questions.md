# Quantitative — Geometry Interview Questions and Answers

## Q1: What are the area formulas for common shapes?
**A:** Square s², rectangle l×b, triangle ½×b×h, circle πr², trapezium ½(a+b)h, circumference 2πr — the six formulas aptitude lean on.

## Q2: What is the Pythagorean theorem?
**A:** a² + b² = c² for right triangles — and the triples (3,4,5), (5,12,13), (8,15,17) are worth recognising instantly.

## Q3: What is the difference between perimeter and area on a rectangle?
**A:** Perimeter 2(l+b); area l×b — units length vs area; doubling the sides ×4 the area but only ×2 the perimeter — the scaling relationship.

## Q4: What is the area of an equilateral triangle?
**A:** (√3/4)s²; its height is (√3/2)s. Recognise 30-60-90 and 45-45-90 side ratios for faster solves.

## Q5: What is the volume of a cylinder, cube, sphere?
**A:** Cylinder πr²h, cube s³, cuboid l×b×h, sphere (4/3)πr³, cone (1/3)πr²h — the volume set for mensuration questions.

## Q6: What is the circumference-radius relationship?
**A:** C = 2πr; if the circumference doubles the radius doubles; circles' area grows with r² — scaling relationships recur in geometry.

## Q7: What is the angle sum of a triangle and an n-gon?
**A:** Triangle sum 180°; general n-gon interior sum (n-2)×180° — the base for any polygon-angle breakdown.

## Q8: What is the area of a triangle from coordinates?
**A:** Area = ½|x1(y2-y3) + x2(y3-y1) + x3(y1-y2)| — the shoelace formula for coordinate geometry problems.

## Q9: State the formula used to solve 08 geometry problems quickly in aptitude tests.
**A:** Every aptitude topic rests on a small set of formulas and unit-adjustments; write the formula, plug the numbers, and always sanity-check the units (hours/minutes, percent, ratio) before finalising — most errors are unit errors.

## Q10: Describe a shortcut technique for 08 geometry.
**A:** Look for symmetric/divisible structure, cancellation in fractions, or base-value anchoring (e.g., assume a convenient total like 100 or LCM). Shortcuts reduce arithmetic but should not skip the conceptual check.

## Q11: Give a practice problem for 08 geometry and solve it step by step.
**A:** Illustrative solved step-by-step with setup -> arithmetic -> final answer and a one-line verification; solving aloud with structured steps is exactly what the assessment expects.

## Q12: What are the typical mistakes students make in 08 geometry?
**A:** Misreading what is asked, unit mix-ups, ignoring 'approximate' vs 'exact', and arithmetic slips under time pressure. Mitigate by re-reading the question and estimating the answer before computing.

## Q13: How would you allocate time if 08 geometry questions appear in the aptitude section?
**A:** Attempt easy-familiar ones first, leave hard ones for review, and never exceed the per-question budget. Question order does not reflect difficulty; strategy beats speed.

## Q14: Explain the concept of 08 geometry to a non-mathematical friend.

## Q15: Which branch of 08 geometry is most frequently combined with data interpretation?

## Q16: What reference materials and practice frequency do you recommend for 08 geometry?
