# Quantitative — Permutations And Combinations Interview Questions and Answers

## Q1: What is the difference between permutations and combinations?
**A:** Permutations count ORDERED arrangements (P = n!/(n-r)!); combinations count UNORDERED selections (C = n!/[r!(n-r)!]) — order decides which formula.

## Q2: How many ways to arrange letters of 'APPLE'?
**A:** 5! / 2! = 60 (the two P's are interchangeable) — divide by factorials of repeated letters.

## Q3: In how many ways can a committee of 3 be chosen from 8 people?
**A:** C(8,3) = 8!/(3!5!) = 56. Order doesn't matter for a committee — combination, not permutation.

## Q4: How many 3-digit numbers can be formed from 1-9 without repetition?
**A:** 9×8×7 = 504 (arrange from the hundreds digit down). Use the slot-method when positions impose constraints.

## Q5: What is the formula for circular arrangement?
**A:** Circular arrangements of n distinct items = (n-1)! — fixing one item removes rotational symmetry; some variants also divide by 2 for flip symmetry.

## Q6: How many ways to arrange 3 boys and 2 girls with girls together?
**A:** Treat the 2 girls as one block: arrange 4 units (3 boys + block) = 4!, then arrange inside the block = 2! → 4!×2! = 48.

## Q7: What is the difference between 'at least one' and 'exactly one'?
**A:** 'At least one' = total - none; 'exactly one' = pick it and choose the rest with none more. Translate phrases to the complement or fixed-selection method.

## Q8: If nP2 = 30, find n.
**A:** n(n-1) = 30 → n^2 - n - 30 = 0 → n = 6. Recognise the quadratic hidden in permutation variable questions.
