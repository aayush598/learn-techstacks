1. **What is the difference between permutations and combinations?**
   - Permutations count ORDERED arrangements (P = n!/(n-r)!); combinations count UNORDERED selections (C = n!/[r!(n-r)!]) — order decides which formula.

2. **How many ways to arrange letters of 'APPLE'?**
   - 5! / 2! = 60 (the two P's are interchangeable) — divide by factorials of repeated letters.

3. **In how many ways can a committee of 3 be chosen from 8 people?**
   - C(8,3) = 8!/(3!5!) = 56. Order doesn't matter for a committee — combination, not permutation.

4. **How many 3-digit numbers can be formed from 1-9 without repetition?**
   - 9×8×7 = 504 (arrange from the hundreds digit down). Use the slot-method when positions impose constraints.

5. **What is the formula for circular arrangement?**
   - Circular arrangements of n distinct items = (n-1)! — fixing one item removes rotational symmetry; some variants also divide by 2 for flip symmetry.

6. **How many ways to arrange 3 boys and 2 girls with girls together?**
   - Treat the 2 girls as one block: arrange 4 units (3 boys + block) = 4!, then arrange inside the block = 2! → 4!×2! = 48.

7. **What is the difference between 'at least one' and 'exactly one'?**
   - 'At least one' = total - none; 'exactly one' = pick it and choose the rest with none more. Translate phrases to the complement or fixed-selection method.

8. **If nP2 = 30, find n.**
   - n(n-1) = 30 → n^2 - n - 30 = 0 → n = 6. Recognise the quadratic hidden in permutation variable questions.
