# 04 Advanced Two Pointers — Two / Pointers

**100 Interview Q&A — Infosys Specialist Programmer (SP) / Digital Specialist Engineer (DSE)**

1. **What makes a two-pointer problem advanced?**
   - Movement depends on multiple monotonic measurements, pointer order is dynamic, or the two pointers combine with hashing/heaps (max area of wind-mills, minimum windows).

2. **How do you solve the 'minimum window substring' with two pointers?**
   - Expand right to satisfy all required chars, then shrink left to minimise while retaining feasibility — a dynamic-window two-pointer. O(n).

3. **What is the 'longest substring without repeating chars' pointer trick?**
   - A window with a set/dict; advance right adding char, pop lefts until no duplicate. O(n).

4. **How do you solve the 'k-best pairs from two arrays' with a heap + two pointers?**
   - Seed (a[0]+b[j]) in a min-heap, pop the smallest, push next (a[+1]+b[j]) — combining pointers with a heap makes the k-Pairs problem tractable. O(k log k).

5. **What is the 'median of two sorted arrays' pointer hybrid?**
   - Merge with two pointers tracking the middle (even/odd) — O(n+m); or binary search partition — O(log(min(n,m))). Prefer the latter for the 'expected' answer.

6. **How do the pointers in 'kth smallest sum in sorted matrices' work?**
   - The matrix of pair sums is sorted by rows and columns; k-way, heap-merge is the clean path, but the monotonic structure enables pointer search — the interviewer probes your data-structure decision.

7. **How do you blend two-pointer with, binary search?**
   - Binary search the answer (e.g., kth-smallest distance), check feasibility with a two-pointer count — the pairing recurs across SP/DSE hard problems.

8. **What is the 'maximum pair sum same prefix' (fixed arrays) hybrid?**
   - Sort second array; for each first-array element use binary search or two-pointer to find the bound — O(n log n) building blocks composed.

9. **When does two-pointer worst out?**
   - When the endpoint movement is ambiguous (multiple monotone targets) or the array is not sorted/hashing is cheaper — always confirm the invariant before committing.

10. **How do you memoise/stream with two pointers?**
   - For streaming inputs, keep the window/pair pointers persistent across chunks to avoid rescanning — a favourite production-systems answer.

11. **What is the intuition behind the 04 advanced two pointers technique used in coding interviews?**
   - The core idea is to avoid brute force by exploiting structure: leftover wasted compares are removed, and each element is processed a small constant number of times, driving complexity down to an optimal bound that interviewers expect you to justify.

12. **Write the brute-force approach for a typical 04 advanced two pointers problem and analyse it.**
   - Brute force enumerates every candidate configuration, e.g., every subarray/combination/permutation, giving O(n^2), O(n^3) or O(2^n). It is correct but only useful for small inputs; the interview follow-up is to optimise it to linear or O(n log n).

13. **State the time and space complexity of the optimal solution for most 04 advanced two pointers problems.**
   - The optimal solution is usually O(n) time with O(n) space, or O(n) time with O(1) space when a monotonic/pointer trick applies. Always state constants and best/worst cases explicitly.

14. **What common edge cases must be handled in 04 advanced two pointers implementations?**
   - Empty input, single-element input, all-equal values, negative numbers, duplicates, off-by-one at the last index, and large values where intermediate sums/products can overflow — every one must be tested.

16. **Give a real-world analogy for 04 advanced two pointers.**

17. **How do you decide between a hash map, sorting, or two pointers as tools for 04 advanced two pointers?**
   - Hash map when you need membership/paired lookup in O(1) and space is affordable; sorting when order gives a monotonic advantage (two pointers, binary search); pointers directly when the input is already sorted. Trade space for speed only when constraints allow.

18. **What is the role of a prefix/suffix precomputation in 04 advanced two pointers?**
   - Precomputation turns repeated subcomputations into O(1) lookups, converting an O(n^2) nested loop into O(n). Common wherever the value at index i depends on an aggregate over a range around i.

19. **Explain the optimisation step you would mention after writing the naive version for 04 advanced two pointers.**
   - First remove redundant recomputation via memoisation or prefix arrays; next, exploit monotonicity with two pointers/sliding window; finally, reduce space by keeping running variables instead of full tables — always closing with the improved complexity.
