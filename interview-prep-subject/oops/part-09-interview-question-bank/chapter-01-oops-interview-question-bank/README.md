## One-paragraph narrative connecting all sections
The chapter moves from *recall* to *application* to *revision*. Section 01 builds recall: 100 questions covering every concept, ordered easy → medium → hard, each with a 1-4 line answer plus the source part to revisit. Section 02 applies that recall to the whiteboard: full OOP coding problems (Tic-Tac-Toe, LRU Cache, Vending Machine) with the modeling steps and complete Java solutions. Section 03 takes it to system scale: the LLD interview process (requirements → objects → relationships → design → code), with a fully worked Parking Lot and the evaluation rubric interviewers use. Section 04 personalizes it: which companies ask what, with heatmap tables. Section 05 is the compression: the entire resource in 30 minutes of bullets and tables for the day before.

## Common interview trap in this chapter
Candidates study questions in isolation and freeze when the interviewer *follows up*. Every answer in this chapter includes the likely follow-up and the SOLID/pattern check the interviewer is probing for. Second trap: solving LLD by jumping straight to code — interviewers grade the *process* (requirements, boundary conditions, class design, trade-offs), not just the code. Always state your assumptions (e.g., "single parking lot, multiple floors") before designing.
