# AC Steady-State Analysis - Practice Questions

## Instructions
Each question has one correct option. Use RMS phasors unless peak values are explicitly stated.

## Questions

### Q1. Rectangular to polar form [Easy]
Convert `3+j4` Ω to polar form.

A. `5∠0°` Ω  
B. `5∠53.13°` Ω  
C. `7∠36.87°` Ω  
D. `12∠90°` Ω

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** `|Z|=√(3²+4²)=5` and `θ=tan⁻¹(4/3)=53.13°`.

### Q2. Series impedance [Easy]
A `4 Ω` resistor is in series with a coil whose impedance is `j3 Ω`. The total impedance is:

A. `j7 Ω`  
B. `4+j3 Ω`  
C. `7+j4 Ω`  
D. `5+j0 Ω`

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Series impedances add directly: `Z=4+j3 Ω`.

### Q3. RL impedance magnitude [Easy]
A 100 Ω resistor and 0.1 H inductor operate at 50 Hz. The inductive reactance is approximately:

A. 3.14 Ω  
B. 31.4 Ω  
C. 314 Ω  
D. 3,140 Ω

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** `X_L=2πfL=2π×50×0.1=31.4 Ω`; therefore `|Z|=√(100²+31.4²)≈104.8 Ω`.

### Q4. Average resistor power [Easy]
An RMS voltage of 10 V is applied to a 20 Ω resistor. The average power is:

A. 2 W  
B. 5 W  
C. 10 W  
D. 20 W

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** `P=V_rms²/R=10²/20=5 W`.

### Q5. Pure inductive power [Easy]
A pure inductor is connected to an AC source. Its average power and power factor are:

A. Positive; unity  
B. Zero; unity  
C. Zero; zero lagging  
D. Positive; zero lagging

**Answer:** C  
**Explanation:** An ideal inductor consumes zero average real power, and current lags voltage by 90°.

### Q6. Maximum-power condition [Medium]
For maximum average power transfer from a source to a purely resistive load:

A. `R_L=R_s/2`  
B. `R_L=R_s`  
C. `R_L=2R_s`  
D. `R_L=0`

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** The maximum-power theorem requires the conjugate match. For two real resistances, this reduces to `R_L=R_s`.

### Q7. Frequency from phase [Medium]
For `Z=10/(1+jωτ)`, the impedance phase is 45° lagging when `ωτ=1`. If `τ=10 ms`, the angular frequency is:

A. 10 rad/s  
B. 100 rad/s  
C. 200 rad/s  
D. 1,000 rad/s

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** `ω=1/τ=1/0.01=100 rad/s`.

### Q8. Three-power calculation [Medium]
A load carries 10 A RMS with power factor 0.8 lagging and resistance `R=8 Ω`. Its real and reactive powers are:

A. `P=640 W`, `Q=480 var`  
B. `P=640 W`, `Q=600 var`  
C. `P=800 W`, `Q=600 var`  
D. `P=1,000 W`, `Q=600 var`

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** `S=10²×8=800 VA`; `P=800×0.8=640 W`; `Q=√(800²−640²)=480 var` lagging.

### Q9. RMS and peak values [Easy]
A sinusoidal voltage has a peak value of `10√2` V. Its RMS value is:

A. 5 V  
B. 10 V  
C. `10√2` V  
D. 20 V

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** `V_rms=V_peak/√2=10 V`.

### Q10. Complex power [Medium]
A load has `S=500+j300 VA`. The magnitude of apparent power and power factor are:

A. `583 VA`, 0.856  
B. `600 VA`, 0.833  
C. `800 VA`, 0.625  
D. `300 VA`, 0.600

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** `|S|=√(500²+300²)=583.1 VA`; `PF=500/583.1=0.857` lagging.
