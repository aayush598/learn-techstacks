# Kirchhoff's Laws - Practice Questions

## Instructions
Each question has one correct option. For Part A preparation, treat a wrong answer as −0.33 mark.

## Questions

### Q1. Current through a series combination [Easy]
A 12 V source is connected across three series resistors whose total resistance is 3 Ω. What is the circuit current?

A. 1 A  
B. 3 A  
C. 4 A  
D. 9 A

**Answer:** C  
**Explanation:** `I = V/R = 12/3 = 4 A`.

### Q2. Applying KCL [Easy]
At a node, 2 A and 3 A enter while 1 A and 1 A leave. What additional current must leave the node?

A. 2 A  
B. 3 A  
C. 4 A  
D. 5 A

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Total current entering is `2 + 3 = 5 A`; the existing 2 A leaving leaves 3 A to leave.

### Q3. Applying KVL [Easy]
In one closed loop, a 12 V source supplies three resistor drops of 5 V, 4 V, and 3 V. Is the loop consistent with KVL?

A. No, the drops are 11 V  
B. No, the drops are 12 V  
C. Yes, because the drops sum to 12 V  
D. Yes, only if the current is zero

**Answer:** C  
**Explanation:** `5 + 4 + 3 = 12 V`, so the algebraic sum of rises and drops around the loop is zero.

### Q4. Parallel resistance [Easy]
Resistors of 6 Ω and 3 Ω are connected in parallel. Their equivalent resistance is:

A. 1 Ω  
B. 2 Ω  
C. 3 Ω  
D. 9 Ω

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** `R_eq = 6×3/(6+3) = 2 Ω`.

### Q5. Voltage division [Medium]
A 10 V source supplies a series pair of 4 Ω and 6 Ω resistors. What is the voltage across the 4 Ω resistor?

A. 2.5 V  
B. 4 V  
C. 6 V  
D. 10 V

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** `V_4 = 10×4/(4+6) = 4 V`.

### Q6. Current-source node voltage [Medium]
A 2 mA current source feeds a node connected to ground by 1 kΩ and 2 kΩ resistors in parallel. The node voltage is:

A. 0.67 V  
B. 1.33 V  
C. 2.00 V  
D. 4.00 V

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** `R_eq = 1 kΩ ∥ 2 kΩ = 0.667 kΩ`; hence `V = 2 mA × 0.667 kΩ = 1.33 V`.

### Q7. Nodal equation [Medium]
A node has a 1 kΩ branch to a 10 V source and a 2 kΩ branch to ground. The node voltage is found from `(V−10)/1 kΩ + V/2 kΩ = 0`. Find `V`.

A. 3.33 V  
B. 5.00 V  
C. 6.67 V  
D. 10.0 V

**Answer:** C  
**Explanation:** Multiplying by 2 kΩ gives `2(V−10)+V=0`, so `3V=20` and `V=6.67 V`.

### Q8. Ideal voltage source [Medium]
An ideal 12 V source is connected in parallel with a 4 kΩ resistor. What voltage appears across the resistor?

A. 0 V  
B. 4 V  
C. 6 V  
D. 12 V

**Answer:** D  
**Explanation:** Every branch connected across an ideal voltage source has the same 12 V terminal voltage. The resistor draws 3 mA; the source supplies the current.

### Q9. Delivered power sign [Medium]
A 12 V source supplies 2 A to an external network. Under the passive sign convention, the power absorbed by the source is:

A. +24 W  
B. −24 W  
C. +6 W  
D. −6 W

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Current leaving the positive terminal means the source delivers power: `P_abs = 12×(−2) = −24 W`.

### Q10. Opposing ideal sources [Medium]
Two ideal 5 V sources are connected in series opposition across a 1 kΩ resistor. What is the resistor current?

A. 0 A  
B. 2.5 mA  
C. 5.0 mA  
D. 10.0 mA

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** The net source voltage is `5−5=0 V`, so the resistor current is zero.
