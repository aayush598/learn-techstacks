# Coupled Circuits and Transformers - Practice Questions

## Instructions
Each question has one correct option. For an ideal transformer, define `n=N₂/N₁`.

## Questions

### Q1. Mutual inductance [Easy]
Two inductors have `L₁=4 H`, `L₂=9 H`, and coupling coefficient `k=0.5`. Their mutual inductance is:

A. 1.5 H  
B. 2.0 H  
C. 3.0 H  
D. 6.0 H

**Answer:** C  
**Explanation:** `M=k√(L₁L₂)=0.5√36=3 H`.

### Q2. Coupling coefficient [Easy]
If `L₁=4 H`, `L₂=9 H`, and `M=3 H`, the coupling coefficient is:

A. 0.25  
B. 0.50  
C. 0.75  
D. 1.00

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** `k=M/√(L₁L₂)=3/6=0.5`.

### Q3. Series-aiding inductance [Medium]
For `L₁=4 H`, `L₂=9 H`, and `M=3 H`, the series-aiding equivalent inductance is:

A. 7 H  
B. 13 H  
C. 19 H  
D. 25 H

**Answer:** C  
**Explanation:** `L_eq=L₁+L₂+2M=4+9+6=19 H`.

### Q4. Series-opposing inductance [Medium]
Using the same values, the series-opposing equivalent inductance is:

A. 7 H  
B. 13 H  
C. 19 H  
D. 1 H

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** `L_eq=L₁+L₂−2M=4+9−6=7 H`.

### Q5. Dot convention [Easy]
If current enters the dotted terminal of one coil and leaves the dotted terminal of the other, the mutual induced voltages are:

A. Aiding  
B. Opposing  
C. Always zero  
D. Dependent only on resistance

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Opposite current directions at the dotted terminals make the mutual terms subtract in the loop voltage equation.

### Q6. 1:1 transformer impedance [Easy]
An ideal 1:1 transformer has an 8 Ω load on its secondary. The load resistance seen at the primary is:

A. 2 Ω  
B. 4 Ω  
C. 8 Ω  
D. 16 Ω

**Answer:** C  
**Explanation:** With `n=N₂/N₁=1`, `Z_in=Z_L/n²=8 Ω`.

### Q7. Step-down transformer [Medium]
A 10:1 step-down transformer has a 4 Ω secondary load. The load reflected to the primary is:

A. 0.4 Ω  
B. 4 Ω  
C. 40 Ω  
D. 400 Ω

**Answer:** D  
**Explanation:** `n=1/10=0.1`; `Z_in=4/(0.1)²=400 Ω`.

### Q8. Ideal-transformer current [Medium]
An ideal transformer has `N₂/N₁=0.5` and primary current `I₁=2 A`. The secondary current magnitude is:

A. 0.5 A  
B. 1 A  
C. 2 A  
D. 4 A

**Answer:** D  
**Explanation:** `I₁=nI₂`; therefore `I₂=I₁/n=2/0.5=4 A`.

### Q9. Source impedance reflection [Medium]
A 10 Ω source impedance is on the primary of an ideal transformer with `n=0.5`. Its value referred to the secondary is:

A. 2.5 Ω  
B. 5 Ω  
C. 20 Ω  
D. 40 Ω

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Referring a primary impedance to the secondary multiplies it by `n²`: `Z'_s=10×0.25=2.5 Ω`.

### Q10. Shorted secondary [Medium]
If the secondary of a coupled-coil pair is shorted, the effective primary inductance is:

A. `L₁+M`  
B. `L₁−M`  
C. `L₁−M²/L₂`  
D. `L₁+M²/L₂`

**Answer:** C  
**Explanation:** The shorted secondary produces its opposing flux, giving `L_sc=L₁−M²/L₂`.
