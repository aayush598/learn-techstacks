# Trend Analysis - ISRO CBT ECE (2023-2025)

## Overall Trends

---

## 1. Subject Weightage Trends

### Consistently High Priority (10%+ of paper):
```
Analog Circuits:           9 -> 10  (Average: 9.5, ~12%)
Communication Systems:    10 -> 9   (Average: 9.5, ~12%)
EM/Microwaves/Antennas:    9 -> 10  (Average: 9.5, ~12%)
Digital Circuits & HDL:    9 -> 8   (Average: 8.5, ~11%)
Signals & Systems/DSP:     8 -> 9   (Average: 8.5, ~11%)
```

These 5 subjects together account for approximately **55-57%** of Part A.

### Medium Priority (7-9% of paper):
```
Network Theory:            6 -> 8   (Average: 7, ~9%)
Electronic Devices:        6 -> 7   (Average: 6.5, ~8%)
Control Systems:           7 -> 6   (Average: 6.5, ~8%)
Power Electronics:         7 -> 5   (Average: 6, ~7.5%)
```

### Lower Priority (5-6% of paper):
```
Engineering Mathematics:   5 -> 4   (Average: 4.5, ~5.5%)
Measurements:              4 -> 4   (Average: 4, ~5%)
```

---

## 2. Question Type Trends

### Direct/Recall Questions (~35%):
- Formula substitution
- Property identification
- Definition-based
- Example: "What is max efficiency of Class B amplifier?"

### Moderate Calculation (~45%):
- 1-2 step numerical
- Formula application with given values
- Example: "Given R1, R2, calculate Schmitt trigger UTP"

### Tricky/Complex (~20%):
- Multi-concept integration
- Non-standard circuit analysis
- Example: "Cascade of amplifier stages with noise figure"

---

## 3. Non-GATE Subject Importance

### ISRO includes subjects NOT in standard GATE ECE:
```
Power Electronics: 5-7 questions per year
Measurements:      4 questions per year
Electrical Machines: 0-2 questions per year
VHDL/Verilog:       2-3 questions per year
```

**Total Non-GATE contribution: 11-16 questions (14-20% of Part A)**

## 5. Time Management Implications

```
Total time: 120 minutes
Part A (80 questions): ~90 minutes (67.5 sec/question)
Part B (15 questions): ~30 minutes (2 min/question)

Recommended allocation:
- Easy questions (30): 30 * 45 sec = 22.5 min
- Moderate (36): 36 * 75 sec = 45 min
- Hard (14): 14 * 90 sec = 21 min
Total Part A: ~88.5 min
Part B: ~30 min
Review: ~1.5 min
```

## 8. Year-over-Year Changes

### 2023 -> 2025 Shifts:
- **More** Network Theory (+2 questions)
- **More** Analog Circuits (+1 question)
- **Fewer** Power Electronics (-2 questions)
- **Fewer** Control Systems (-1 question)
- **More** conceptual questions overall
- **More** VHDL/HDL emphasis

### Predicted Future Trends:
- Continued emphasis on Analog, Communications, EM
- Growing Network Theory importance
- Possible increase in VHDL/system design questions
- Non-GATE subjects remain stable (5-7 questions combined)
