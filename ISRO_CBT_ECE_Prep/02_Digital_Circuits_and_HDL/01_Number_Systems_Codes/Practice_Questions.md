# Number Systems and Codes - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) Binary 1010 in decimal:
(A) 10  (B) 8  (C) 12  (D) 5

**Solution:** 8+0+2+0 = 10
**Answer: (A) 10**

---

### Q2. (Easy) Binary 1111 in hex:
(A) F  (B) E  (C) 15 (dec too)  (D) 0F as hex F
**Answer: (A) F**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) 2's complement of 1001 (4-bit):
(A) 0111  (B) 1101  (C) 1000  (D) 0110

**Solution:** invert 1001 -> 0110, +1 -> 0111
**Answer: (A) 0111**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) 2's complement range for N bits:
(A) -2^(N-1) to +2^(N-1)-1  (B) 0 to 2^N  (C) symmetric  (D) None

**Answer: (A) -2^(N-1) ... +2^(N-1)-1 (e.g., 8-bit: -128 to +127)**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) BCD 8421 of decimal 5:
(A) 0101  (B) 101  (C) 010 binary  (D) 1000

**Answer: (A) 0101**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) Gray code characteristic:
(A) Only 1 bit changes between adjacent  (B) Adjacent differ  (C) Binary  (D) Random

**Answer: (A) Adjacent codes differ in exactly one bit (Gray)**

---

### Q7. (Moderate) Excess-3 code:
(A) BCD + 3  (B) BCD - 3  (C) Gray  (D) 2's comp

**Answer: (A) Excess-3 = (BCD) + 3, self-complementing**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) XOR of all bits detects:
(A) Parity (even/odd)  (B) Error location  (C) Only value  (D) Nothing

**Answer: (A) Parity - XOR gives parity bit (odd count of 1s)**

---

### Q9. (Moderate) Octal 17 to decimal:
(A) 15  (B) 17  (C) 23  (D) 8

**Solution:** 1*8 + 7 = 15
**Answer: (A) 15**

---

### Q10. (Moderate) Largest 4-bit unsigned number:
(A) 15  (B) 7  (C) 16  (D) 8

**Answer: (A) 15 (=1111, 2^4-1)**
