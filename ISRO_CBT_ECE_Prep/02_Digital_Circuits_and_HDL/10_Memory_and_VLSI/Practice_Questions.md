# Memory and VLSI - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) DRAM cell contains:
(A) 6 transistors  (B) 1 transistor + capacitor  (C) Only capacitor  (D) 2 transistors

**Answer: (B) 1 transistor + 1 capacitor (needs refresh)**

---

### Q2. (Easy) SRAM is used as:
(A) Main memory  (B) Cache  (C) Secondary  (D) Hard disk

**Answer: (B) Cache - fast but low density**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) Memory 64K x 8 has:
(A) 16 address lines  (B) 8 address lines  (C) 64 address lines  (D) 4 address lines

**Solution:** 64K = 2^16, so 16 address lines
**Answer: (A) 16 address lines**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) DRAM needs refresh because:
(A) Capacitor charge leaks  (B) Slow  (C) Hot  (D) Cheap

**Answer: (A) Stored charge (capacitor) leaks and must be refreshed periodically**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) CMOS dynamic power:
(A) C V^2 f  (B) C V f  (C) V/f  (D) I V^2

**Answer: (A) P = C * V^2 * f**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) EEPROM differs from EPROM:
(A) Cheaper  (B) Electrically erasable (no UV)  (C) Faster only  (D) Non-volatile

**Answer: (B) Electrically erasable/rewritable (EPROM needs UV)**

---

### Q7. (Moderate) Cache relies on:
(A) Randomness  (B) Locality of reference  (C) Large size  (D) Slow speed

**Answer: (B) Locality of reference (temporal/spatial)**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) Number of 8Kx8 chips to build 64Kx8 memory:
(A) 8  (B) 4  (C) 16  (D) 2

**Solution:** 64K/8K = 8 chips
**Answer: (A) 8**
