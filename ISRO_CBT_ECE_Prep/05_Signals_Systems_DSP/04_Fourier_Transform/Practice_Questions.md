# Fourier Transform - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) Fourier transform applies to:
(A) Aperiodic signals  (B) Only periodic  (C) Only periodic  (D) Only DC

**Answer: (A) Aperiodic (continuous) signals - extension to non-periodic**

---

### Q2. (Moderate) FT of delta function:
(A) 1 (constant)  (B) 0  (C) delta  (D) rect

**Answer: (A) FT of δ(t) = 1 (flat spectrum)**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) FT of constant 1:
(A) 2π δ(w)  (B) 1  (C) 0  (D) sinc

**Answer: (A) FT of 1 = 2π δ(ω) (DC)**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) FT of cos(w0 t):
(A) Pair of deltas at ±w0  (B) one delta  (C) sinc  (D) rect

**Answer: (A) FT of cos(w0t) = π[δ(w-w0)+δ(w+w0)]**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) Time shift property:
(A) x(t-T) <-> X(w) e^{-jwT}  (B) X(w)/T  (C) X(w)+T  (D) no change

**Answer: (A) Delay by T -> multiply by e^{-jwT} (linear phase)**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) Convolution theorem:
(A) x1*x2 <-> X1 X2  (B) X1+X2  (C) X1-X2  (D) X1/X2

**Answer: (A) Time convolution = frequency multiplication**

---

### Q7. (Moderate) Rectangular pulse FT:
(A) sinc function  (B) delta  (C) rect  (D) exp

**Answer: (A) FT of rect = sinc (sampling/rect pulse)**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) Scaling property x(at):
(A) (1/|a|) X(w/a)  (B) X(aw)  (C) a X(w)  (D) X(w/a)

**Answer: (A) x(at) <-> (1/|a|) X(ω/a)**

---

### Q9. (Moderate) Differentiation property: dx/dt:
(A) jw X(w)  (B) X(w)/jw  (C) X(w)/w  (D) j X(w)

**Answer: (A) d/dt <-> jω X(ω)**

---

### Q10. (Moderate) Duality/symmetry: rect <-> sinc shows:
(A) Sinc <-> rect (interchanged)  (B) Only noise  (C) No relation  (D) Only DC

**Answer: (A) Sinc has rect FT (duality / reciprocity of time-freq)**
