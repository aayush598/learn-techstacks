# Fourier Series - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) Fourier series represents a:
(A) Periodic signal (in terms of sinusoids)  (B) Aperiodic  (C) Only sine  (D) Only DC

**Answer: (A) Periodic signals as sum of harmonically related sinusoids**

---

### Q2. (Moderate) Fundamental frequency f0 related to period T:
(A) f0 = 1/T  (B) f0 = T  (C) f0 = 2π/T  (D) f0 = T/2

**Answer: (A) f0 = 1/T (fundamental), omega0 = 2π/T**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) An even signal has:
(A) Only cosine (and DC) terms, no sine  (B) Only sine  (C) No DC  (D) Only odd harmonics

**Answer: (A) Even function -> cosine series only (b_n = 0)**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) An odd signal has:
(A) Only sine terms, no DC  (B) Only cosine  (C) Cos + DC  (D) Even only

**Answer: (A) Odd -> sine terms only, a0 = 0**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) Coefficients a0 represents:
(A) DC value (average)  (B) Fundamental  (C) Harmonics  (D) None

**Answer: (A) a0 = DC/average value**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) Square wave symmetry (half-wave) has:
(A) Only odd harmonics  (B) Only even  (C) All  (D) None

**Answer: (A) Half-wave symmetric square wave -> odd harmonics only**

---

### Q7. (Moderate) Gibbs phenomenon is:
(A) Overshoot near discontinuities (finite terms)  (B) Aliasing  (C) Leakage  (D) Folding

**Answer: (A) Overshoot (~9%) at discontinuities when truncated Fourier series**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) Dirichlet conditions ensure:
(A) Convergence of Fourier series  (B) No convergence  (C) Complex  (D) None

**Answer: (A) Conditions (finite discontinuities, integrable) guarantee convergence**

---

### Q9. (Moderate) Parseval's theorem relates average power:
(A) to sum of squared magnitude coefficients  (B) to phase  (C) to freq only  (D) none

**Answer: (A) Power = sum of |C_n|^2 (Parseval)**

---

### Q10. (Moderate) Exponential Fourier series coefficient C_n for real even signal:
(A) Real (symmetric)  (B) Imaginary  (C) Complex random  (D) Zero

**Answer: (A) Real coefficients for real even signal**
