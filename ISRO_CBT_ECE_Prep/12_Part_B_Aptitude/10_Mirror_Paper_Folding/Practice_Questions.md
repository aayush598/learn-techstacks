## Question 1: Vertical Mirror — Letter

What is the mirror image of the letter "R" when reflected in a vertical mirror placed on the right side?

(A) R
(B) ꟻ (horizontally reversed R)
(C) P
(D) Я (Cyrillic-looking reversed R)

**Solution:**
Vertical mirror swaps left-right:
- R has a vertical stem on the left and a curve on the right
- Mirror image: vertical stem on the right, curve on the left
- This is the reversed/horizontally flipped R

**Answer: (D)** — Я (horizontally reversed R).

## Question 2: Vertical Mirror — Word

What is the mirror image of the word "ISRO" when reflected in a vertical mirror?

(A) ISRO
(B) ORSI
(C) ORIS
(D) ISOR

**Solution:**
Vertical mirror reverses left-right:
I-S-R-O → O-R-S-I

The word becomes "ORSI" when read left to right in the mirror image.

**Answer: (B)** — ORSI.

## Question 3: Horizontal Mirror — Letter M

What is the water image (horizontal mirror) of the letter "M"?

(A) M
(B) W
(C) Σ
(D) E

**Solution:**
Horizontal mirror flips top-bottom:
- M has peaks pointing up
- When flipped upside down, the peaks point down
- M becomes W

**Answer: (B)** — W.

## Question 4: Horizontal Mirror — Number

What is the water image of the number "3"?

(A) 3
(B) E
(C) Reversed 3 (flipped upside down)
(D) 8

**Solution:**
Horizontal mirror flips top-bottom:
- 3 has a top curve and bottom curve
- When flipped: the top curve goes to bottom and vice versa
- The result looks like a reversed 3 (not the same as original)

**Answer: (C)** — Reversed 3 (flipped upside down).

## Question 5: Paper Folding — Single Horizontal Fold

A rectangular paper is folded once from top to bottom. A circular hole is punched at the center of the folded paper. How many holes appear when unfolded?

(A) 1
(B) 2
(C) 3
(D) 4

**Solution:**
- 1 fold → 2 layers
- 1 hole through 2 layers = 2 holes
- The hole is at the center, and the fold is horizontal through center
- Since the hole is on the fold line, it becomes 1 hole? No — the center of the folded piece is at the fold line.

Actually: the center of the folded piece (after folding top to bottom) is at the horizontal fold line. A hole at the fold line remains as 1 hole when unfolded.

**Answer: (A)** — 1 hole (the hole is on the fold line).

## Question 6: Paper Folding — Single Vertical Fold

A square paper is folded from left to right. A hole is punched at the top-right corner of the folded piece. How many holes appear when unfolded, and where?

(A) 1 hole at the top-right corner
(B) 2 holes — one at top-right, one at top-left
(C) 2 holes — both on the right half
(D) 4 holes

**Solution:**
- Fold left to right → the left half overlaps the right half
- Hole punched at top-right corner of folded piece
- The top-right corner of the folded piece corresponds to the top-right of the right half AND the top-left of the left half (which is folded over)
- When unfolded: 2 holes, one at top-right and one at top-left, symmetric about the vertical fold line

**Answer: (B)** — 2 holes, symmetric about the fold line.

## Question 7: Paper Folding — Double Fold

A square paper is folded in half horizontally, then in half vertically. A single hole is punched at the corner where both fold lines meet (the center of the original paper). How many holes when unfolded?

(A) 1
(B) 2
(C) 4
(D) 8

**Solution:**
- Fold 1 (horizontal): 2 layers
- Fold 2 (vertical): 4 layers
- Hole at the corner where folds meet = center of original paper
- This point is on BOTH fold lines
- A hole on a fold line stays as 1 hole for that fold
- Since the hole is at the intersection of both fold lines, it remains as 1 hole

**Answer: (A)** — 1 hole (at the center, on both fold lines).

## Question 8: Paper Folding — Two Holes

A paper is folded once (left to right). Two holes are punched: one at the top and one at the bottom of the folded piece. How many holes when unfolded?

(A) 2
(B) 3
(C) 4
(D) 6

**Solution:**
- 1 fold → 2 layers
- 2 holes × 2 layers = 4 holes
- The top hole mirrors across the fold → 2 holes (top area)
- The bottom hole mirrors across the fold → 2 holes (bottom area)

**Answer: (C)** — 4 holes.

## Question 9: Mirror Image — Asymmetric Figure

A figure shows an arrow pointing to the right (→) with a small circle at its tail. What is its vertical mirror image?

(A) → with circle at tail
(B) ← with circle at head
(C) ← with circle at tail
(D) → with circle at head

**Solution:**
Vertical mirror swaps left-right:
- Arrow pointing right → arrow pointing left
- Circle at tail (left side) → circle at the NEW left side (which was the right side)
- Wait: in the mirror, the tail is on the right and head on the left
- Original: circle at left (tail), arrow points right
- Mirror: circle at right (now tail), arrow points left

The circle is at the tail of the reversed arrow. The tail is on the right side of the mirror image.

So the mirror image is ← with circle at the tail (which is now on the right).

**Answer: (C)** — ← with circle at tail (reversed arrow, circle follows the tail).

## Question 10: Paper Folding — Diagonal Fold

A square paper is folded along the diagonal (bottom-left to top-right). A hole is punched at the midpoint of the top edge of the folded triangle. How many holes when unfolded?

(A) 1
(B) 2
(C) 3
(D) 4

**Solution:**
- Fold along diagonal → creates a triangle
- The top edge of the triangle is NOT the fold line (the fold is the diagonal)
- Hole at midpoint of top edge (not on fold line) → will be mirrored across the diagonal
- 1 fold → 2 layers → 2 holes

The hole and its mirror image across the diagonal.

**Answer: (B)** — 2 holes.

## Question 11: Mirror Image — Complex Figure

A figure shows the letter "E" with a horizontal line extending from the middle bar to the right. What is its vertical mirror image?

(A) E with line extending left from middle bar
(B) Reversed E with line extending right from middle bar
(C) Reversed E with line extending left from middle bar
(D) E with line extending left from middle bar

**Solution:**
Vertical mirror:
- E becomes reversed E (ꟻ)
- The horizontal line extends to the RIGHT in original → in mirror it extends to the LEFT
- The line is at the middle bar of the reversed E

Result: reversed E (ꟻ) with line extending left from the middle bar.

Wait — "extending left from middle bar" of the reversed E would be extending toward the original E's direction. Let me reclarify:

Original: E with line extending RIGHT from middle bar
Mirror: reversed E with line extending LEFT from middle bar

**Answer: (C)** — Reversed E with line extending left from middle bar.

## Question 12: Paper Folding — Three Folds

A paper is folded three times: left-to-right, top-to-bottom, then left-to-right again. One hole is punched. How many holes when unfolded?

(A) 4
(B) 6
(C) 8
(D) 16

**Solution:**
Each fold doubles the layers:
- After fold 1: 2 layers
- After fold 2: 4 layers
- After fold 3: 8 layers

1 hole × 8 layers = 8 holes

**Answer: (C)** — 8 holes.

## Question 13: Mirror Image — Clock

What is the mirror image (vertical mirror) of a clock showing 3:00?

(A) 3:00
(B) 9:00
(C) 12:00
(D) 6:00

**Solution:**
At 3:00, the hour hand is at 3 (right) and minute hand at 12 (top).
Mirror image (vertical): hour hand moves to 9 (left), minute hand stays at 12 (top).

Mirror of 3:00 = 9:00

**Answer: (B)** — 9:00.

## Question 14: Paper Folding — Triangular Cut

A square paper is folded in half (top to bottom), then in half again (left to right). A triangular cut is made at the bottom-right corner of the folded piece. What does the unfolded paper look like?

(A) 4 triangular cuts, one in each corner
(B) 2 triangular cuts on the right side
(C) 4 triangular cuts, symmetric about both fold lines
(D) 1 triangular cut at the center

**Solution:**
- 2 folds → 4 layers
- Triangular cut at bottom-right corner of folded piece
- The bottom-right corner of the folded piece corresponds to:
  - After fold 1 (top to bottom): the bottom-right of the folded piece is at the bottom of the original
  - After fold 2 (left to right): the bottom-right is at the bottom-right of the folded piece

Unfolding:
- Across vertical fold: triangular cut mirrors to the left → 2 cuts on the bottom
- Across horizontal fold: each cut mirrors to the top → 4 cuts total

The 4 cuts are in the 4 corners of the original paper, symmetric about both fold lines.

**Answer: (C)** — 4 triangular cuts, symmetric about both fold lines.

## Question 15: Mirror Image — Number

What is the vertical mirror image of the number "1234"?

(A) 4321
(B) 1234
(C) Reversed digits: 4̅3̅2̅1̅ (each digit also flipped)
(D) 1̅2̅3̅4̅ (digits flipped but same order)

**Solution:**
Vertical mirror reverses left-right:
- The ORDER reverses: 1234 → 4321
- Each INDIVIDUAL digit is also horizontally flipped
- So the result is the reversed sequence with each digit mirrored

1→reversed 1, 2→reversed 2, 3→reversed 3, 4→reversed 4
And the order reverses.

**Answer: (C)** — 4̅3̅2̅1̅ (reversed order with each digit flipped).
