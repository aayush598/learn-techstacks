## Question 1: Shape Completion

A square has a pattern with a missing quarter. The square is divided into 4 quadrants. Top-left has a diagonal line from top-left to bottom-right with a small circle at center. Top-right has a diagonal from top-right to bottom-left with a small circle at center. Bottom-left is empty. Bottom-right has the missing portion.

**Which of the following completes the square?**

(A) Diagonal from top-left to bottom-right with circle at center
(B) Diagonal from top-right to bottom-left with circle at center
(C) Both diagonals crossing with a circle at center
(D) A horizontal line with a circle at center

**Solution:**
The square exhibits 4-fold rotational symmetry. Each quadrant contains a diagonal from the outer corner toward the center with a circle at the intersection point. The missing bottom-right quadrant should have a diagonal from bottom-right to top-left (going toward center) with a circle at center.

**Answer: (A)** — The diagonal runs from the outer corner (bottom-right) toward the center with a circle, completing the rotational symmetry.

## Question 2: Odd One Out

Which figure does NOT belong?

Figures described:
- Figure A: A square with all four diagonals drawn (X pattern)
- Figure B: A square with horizontal and vertical midlines (+ pattern)
- Figure C: A square with both diagonals AND midlines (union of A and B)
- Figure D: A triangle with medians drawn from each vertex

**Solution:**
- Figures A, B, C are all modifications of a SQUARE
- Figure D is based on a TRIANGLE
- Figures A, B, C all involve lines connecting midpoints or corners of a 4-sided figure
- Figure D involves medians of a 3-sided figure

Additionally: A and B are subsets of C. D is structurally different.

**Answer: (D)** — The triangle is the only non-quadrilateral figure.

## Question 3: Figure Series Completion

Sequence of 4 figures:
1. A single straight line pointing right (→)
2. Two lines forming an L shape (right angle)
3. Three lines forming a U shape (right angle at bottom)
4. Four lines forming a square (complete box)

**What comes next?**

(A) A pentagon (5 lines)
(B) A square with one diagonal (5 lines)
(C) A square with a dot in center
(D) Two concentric squares

**Solution:**
- Figure 1: 1 line
- Figure 2: 2 lines
- Figure 3: 3 lines
- Figure 4: 4 lines
- The pattern is: each step adds one line to form a new closed/semi-closed shape
- The progression is 1→2→3→4 lines, so next should have 5 lines
- Option A (pentagon) has 5 lines but is a different shape family
- Option B (square + diagonal) has 5 lines and continues building on the square

Looking more carefully: the figures build progressively — each line connects to form increasingly complete shapes. After completing the square (4 lines), the natural next step is adding a diagonal (5th line).

**Answer: (B)** — Square with one diagonal, continuing the line count progression (5 lines).

## Question 4: Visual Analogy

Figure pair relationship:
- A (small filled circle) is to B (large empty circle)
- As C (small filled square) is to ?

(A) Large filled square
(B) Large empty square
(C) Small empty square
(D) Large empty circle

**Solution:**
From A to B:
- Size changes: small → large
- Fill changes: filled → empty
- Shape remains: circle → circle

Apply same changes to C (small filled square):
- Size: small → large
- Fill: filled → empty
- Shape: square → square

Result: Large empty square

**Answer: (B)** — Large empty square.

## Question 5: Mirror Image

Find the mirror image of the following arrangement (vertical mirror on the right side):

```
  ↑
P Q R
  ↓
```

(A) R Q P (with arrows reversed)
(B) R Q P (with arrows in same direction)
(C) P Q R (with arrows reversed)
(D) R P Q (with arrows reversed)

**Solution:**
Vertical mirror reflects left-right:
- The order R Q P reverses to P Q R → R Q P (rightmost becomes leftmost)
- Arrows: ↑ becomes ↑ (unchanged in vertical mirror for vertical arrows)
- ↓ becomes ↓ (unchanged)

Wait — the mirror image reverses left-right. So "P Q R" becomes "R Q P".
Arrows ↑ and ↓ are vertical, so they remain unchanged in a vertical mirror.

**Answer: (B)** — R Q P with arrows in the same direction (↑ on top, ↓ on bottom).

## Question 6: Embedded Figure

Target figure: A small equilateral triangle pointing upward (△)

Which of the following complex figures contains this triangle hidden within?

(A) A six-pointed star (Star of David) — contains two overlapping triangles
(B) A figure with parallel horizontal lines
(C) A spiral pattern
(D) Concentric circles

**Solution:**
- A six-pointed star is formed by two overlapping equilateral triangles
- One triangle points up (△) and one points down (▽)
- The target triangle △ is clearly embedded in the star
- Options B, C, D have no triangular elements

**Answer: (A)** — The six-pointed star contains the upward-pointing triangle.

## Question 7: Figure Series — Rotation

Sequence:
1. Arrow pointing UP
2. Arrow pointing RIGHT
3. Arrow pointing DOWN
4. Arrow pointing LEFT

**What comes next?**

(A) Arrow pointing UP
(B) Arrow pointing RIGHT
(C) Arrow pointing DOWN
(D) Arrow pointing diagonally UP-RIGHT

**Solution:**
- Figure 1→2: 90° clockwise rotation
- Figure 2→3: 90° clockwise rotation
- Figure 3→4: 90° clockwise rotation
- Rule: Rotate 90° clockwise each step
- Next: Arrow pointing LEFT rotated 90° CW = Arrow pointing UP

**Answer: (A)** — Arrow pointing UP.

## Question 8: Odd One Out — Shading

Figures: Four circles, each divided into quadrants with different shading
- A: Top-left and bottom-right shaded
- B: Top-right and bottom-left shaded
- C: Top half shaded
- D: Top-left and bottom-right shaded

**Solution:**
- A and D are identical (both have top-left and bottom-right shaded)
- B is the mirror image of A
- C has different shading — top half shaded (adjacent quadrants)
- A, B, D all have DIAGONALLY opposite quadrants shaded
- C has ADJACENT quadrants shaded

**Answer: (C)** — C has adjacent quadrants shaded while others have diagonally opposite.

A is to B as C is to ?

(A) Square divided into 8 equal parts with alternating shading
(B) Square divided into 4 equal parts with same shading
(C) Circle divided into 8 parts
(D) Square divided into 16 parts

**Solution:**
From A to B: Number of divisions doubles (4 → 8), same alternating pattern maintained.
From C: Square with 4 divisions → double divisions → 8 divisions, alternating shading.

## Question 10: Mirror Image — Complex Figure

A letter "F" is shown. What is its mirror image in a vertical mirror?

(A) F
(B) ꟻ (reversed F)
(C) E
(D) L

**Solution:**
Vertical mirror flips left-right. The letter F has:
- A vertical stem on the left
- Two horizontal bars extending right

Mirror image: vertical stem on the right, horizontal bars extending left.
This is the reversed F (ꟻ).

**Answer: (B)** — The horizontally flipped version of F.

## Question 11: Shape Completion — Pattern

A 3×3 grid has patterns. The first row has: circle, square, circle. Second row has: square, circle, square. Third row has: circle, square, ?. The missing element is:

(A) Circle
(B) Square
(C) Triangle
(D) Diamond

**Solution:**
This follows a checkerboard pattern:
- Row 1: C, S, C
- Row 2: S, C, S
- Row 3: C, S, ?

Each row alternates, and the pattern is consistent:
- Position (1,1)=C, (1,2)=S, (1,3)=C
- Position (2,1)=S, (2,2)=C, (2,3)=S
- Position (3,1)=C, (3,2)=S, (3,3)=C

**Answer: (A)** — Circle completes the checkerboard pattern.

A dot moves around a square:
- Figure 1: Dot at top-left corner

**What comes next?**

**Solution:**

## Question 13: Odd One Out — Lines and Curves

- Figure A: Made entirely of straight lines (a triangle)
- Figure B: Made entirely of straight lines (a pentagon)
- Figure C: Made of straight lines and curves (a semicircle)
- Figure D: Made entirely of straight lines (a hexagon)

**Solution:**
- A, B, D: all figures composed entirely of straight line segments

**Answer: (C)** — The semicircle is the only figure containing a curve.

## Question 14: Visual Analogy — Clock Analogy

If 3 o'clock is to 9 o'clock, then 12 o'clock is to:

(A) 6 o'clock
(B) 3 o'clock
(C) 12 o'clock
(D) 9 o'clock

**Solution:**
- 3 o'clock and 9 o'clock are 180° apart (opposite positions)
- The relationship is: rotate 180°
- 12 o'clock rotated 180° = 6 o'clock

**Answer: (A)** — 6 o'clock is diametrically opposite to 12 o'clock.

## Question 15: Embedded Figure — Complex

Target: A letter "T" shape

Which arrangement embeds a "T"?

(A) A horizontal line with three vertical lines below it at left, center, and right
(B) Three concentric circles
(C) A grid of horizontal and vertical lines forming a 4×4 grid
(D) A zigzag pattern

**Solution:**
- Option A: Has a horizontal line with vertical lines — the center vertical line meeting the horizontal line forms a "T"
- Option B: No straight lines forming "T"
- Option C: A 4×4 grid contains many "T" junctions at intersections
- Option D: No "T" junction

Both A and C contain "T" shapes. However, in a 4×4 grid, every internal intersection of a horizontal and vertical line creates a "T" (or "+"). In option A, the center vertical meeting the horizontal creates exactly a "T".

The most unambiguous embedding is in option A where one specific "T" is clearly visible.

**Answer: (A)** — The arrangement with horizontal line and center vertical line forms a clear "T".
