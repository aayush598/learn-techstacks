## Question 1: Percentage

A student scores 180 marks out of 300. What is the percentage?

(A) 55%
(B) 60%
(C) 65%
(D) 70%

**Solution:**
Percentage = (180/300) × 100 = 60%

**Answer: (B)** — 60%.

## Question 2: Successive Percentage Change

A price increases by 20% and then decreases by 20%. What is the net change?

(A) 4% increase
(B) 4% decrease
(C) No change
(D) 2% decrease

**Solution:**
Net change = x + y + xy/100
= 20 + (-20) + (20 × -20)/100
= 20 - 20 - 4
= -4%

Net change = 4% decrease.

Alternatively: Let original = 100
After +20%: 120
After -20%: 120 × 0.8 = 96
Net change = 96 - 100 = -4 (4% decrease)

**Answer: (B)** — 4% decrease.

## Question 3: Profit and Loss

A shopkeeper buys an article for ₹500 and sells it at 15% profit. What is the selling price?

(A) ₹550
(B) ₹575
(C) ₹600
(D) ₹525

**Solution:**
SP = CP × (1 + Profit%/100)
= 500 × (1 + 15/100)
= 500 × 1.15
= ₹575

**Answer: (B)** — ₹575.

## Question 4: Simple Interest

Find the simple interest on ₹2000 at 5% per annum for 3 years.

(A) ₹250
(B) ₹300
(C) ₹350
(D) ₹400

**Solution:**
SI = P × R × T / 100
= 2000 × 5 × 3 / 100
= 30000 / 100
= ₹300

**Answer: (B)** — ₹300.

## Question 5: Compound Interest

Find the compound interest on ₹1000 at 10% per annum for 2 years.

(A) ₹200
(B) ₹210
(C) ₹220
(D) ₹190

**Solution:**
A = P(1 + R/100)^T
= 1000 × (1 + 10/100)^2
= 1000 × (1.1)^2
= 1000 × 1.21
= ₹1210

CI = A - P = 1210 - 1000 = ₹210

**Answer: (B)** — ₹210.

## Question 6: Average Speed

A car travels from A to B at 60 km/h and returns at 40 km/h. What is the average speed?

(A) 48 km/h
(B) 50 km/h
(C) 52 km/h
(D) 55 km/h

**Solution:**
For equal distances with speeds v₁ and v₂:
Average speed = 2v₁v₂/(v₁ + v₂)
= 2 × 60 × 40 / (60 + 40)
= 4800 / 100
= 48 km/h

**Answer: (A)** — 48 km/h.

## Question 7: Time, Speed, Distance

A train 200 m long crosses a pole in 20 seconds. What is its speed in km/h?

(A) 36 km/h
(B) 40 km/h
(C) 45 km/h
(D) 30 km/h

**Solution:**
Speed = Distance / Time = 200 m / 20 s = 10 m/s
Convert to km/h: 10 × 18/5 = 36 km/h

**Answer: (A)** — 36 km/h.

## Question 8: Ratio and Proportion

Divide ₹1200 in the ratio 3:5.

(A) ₹450 and ₹750
(B) ₹400 and ₹800
(C) ₹500 and ₹700
(D) ₹350 and ₹850

**Solution:**
Total parts = 3 + 5 = 8
First part = 3/8 × 1200 = ₹450
Second part = 5/8 × 1200 = ₹750

**Answer: (A)** — ₹450 and ₹750.

## Question 9: Average

The average of 5 numbers is 20. If one number is removed, the average becomes 18. What is the removed number?

(A) 24
(B) 26
(C) 28
(D) 30

**Solution:**
Sum of 5 numbers = 5 × 20 = 100
Sum of 4 numbers = 4 × 18 = 72
Removed number = 100 - 72 = 28

**Answer: (C)** — 28.

## Question 10: Work and Time

A can complete a work in 12 days, B can complete it in 15 days. In how many days can they complete it together?

(A) 6 days
(B) 6.67 days
(C) 7 days
(D) 8 days

**Solution:**
A's rate = 1/12 per day
B's rate = 1/15 per day
Combined rate = 1/12 + 1/15 = 5/60 + 4/60 = 9/60 = 3/20 per day

Time = 1 / (3/20) = 20/3 = 6.67 days

**Answer: (B)** — 6.67 days (or 6 days 16 hours).

## Question 11: Profit/Loss with Discount

A shopkeeper marks an article at ₹800 and gives a 25% discount. If he makes a 20% profit, what is the cost price?

(A) ₹500
(B) ₹533.33
(C) ₹550
(D) ₹480

**Solution:**
SP = MP × (1 - Discount%/100) = 800 × 0.75 = ₹600
SP = CP × (1 + Profit%/100)
600 = CP × 1.2
CP = 600 / 1.2 = ₹500

**Answer: (A)** — ₹500.

## Question 12: Compound Interest — Difference

What is the difference between CI and SI on ₹5000 at 10% per annum for 2 years?

(A) ₹40
(B) ₹50
(C) ₹60
(D) ₹45

**Solution:**
For 2 years: CI - SI = P × (R/100)^2
= 5000 × (10/100)^2
= 5000 × 0.01
= ₹50

**Answer: (B)** — ₹50.

## Question 13: Mixture — Alligation

Sugar worth ₹40/kg is mixed with sugar worth ₹55/kg in the ratio 3:2. What is the price of the mixture per kg?

(A) ₹45
(B) ₹46
(C) ₹47
(D) ₹48

**Solution:**
Weighted average = (3 × 40 + 2 × 55) / (3 + 2)
= (120 + 110) / 5
= 230 / 5
= ₹46 per kg

**Answer: (B)** — ₹46/kg.

## Question 14: Time and Work — Pipes

Pipe A fills a tank in 6 hours, Pipe B fills it in 8 hours. If both are opened together, how long to fill the tank?

(A) 3.43 hours
(B) 4 hours
(C) 7 hours
(D) 14 hours

**Solution:**
A's rate = 1/6 per hour
B's rate = 1/8 per hour
Combined = 1/6 + 1/8 = 4/24 + 3/24 = 7/24 per hour

Time = 24/7 ≈ 3.43 hours

**Answer: (A)** — 3.43 hours (3 hours 26 minutes).

## Question 15: Percentage — Reverse

If A is 20% more than B, by what percentage is B less than A?

(A) 16.67%
(B) 20%
(C) 25%
(D) 15%

**Solution:**
A = B × 1.2 (A is 20% more than B)
B = A / 1.2

B less than A by: (A - B)/A × 100
= (A - A/1.2)/A × 100
= (1 - 1/1.2) × 100
= (0.2/1.2) × 100
= 16.67%

**Answer: (A)** — 16.67%.

## Question 16: LCM/HCF

Find the LCM of 12, 15, and 20.

(A) 30
(B) 45
(C) 60
(D) 120

**Solution:**
Prime factorization:
12 = 2² × 3
15 = 3 × 5
20 = 2² × 5

LCM = 2² × 3 × 5 = 60

**Answer: (C)** — 60.

## Question 17: CI — Half-Yearly Compounding

Find CI on ₹10000 at 10% per annum compounded half-yearly for 1 year.

(A) ₹1025
(B) ₹1050
(C) ₹1075
(D) ₹1100

**Solution:**
Half-yearly rate = 10/2 = 5%
Number of half-years = 2

A = 10000 × (1 + 5/100)^2
= 10000 × (1.05)^2
= 10000 × 1.1025
= ₹11025

CI = 11025 - 10000 = ₹1025

**Answer: (A)** — ₹1025.

## Question 18: Successive Discounts

A shopkeeper offers two successive discounts of 10% and 20%. What is the equivalent single discount?

(A) 28%
(B) 30%
(C) 25%
(D) 32%

**Solution:**
Net discount = d₁ + d₂ - d₁d₂/100
= 10 + 20 - (10 × 20)/100
= 30 - 2
= 28%

**Answer: (A)** — 28%.
