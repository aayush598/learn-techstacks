# Study Plan - 8 Month Detailed Breakdown

## Month-by-Month Curriculum Map

---

## Month 1: Mathematics + Network Theory Foundation

### Week 1: Linear Algebra + Basic Circuits
| Day | Morning (3h) | Afternoon (2h) | Evening (1h) |
|-----|-------------|----------------|--------------|
| Mon | Matrix operations, determinants | KCL and KVL practice | Formula review |
| Tue | Eigenvalues and eigenvectors | Nodal analysis problems | Formula review |
| Wed | Singular matrices, rank | Mesh analysis problems | Formula review |
| Thu | Cayley-Hamilton theorem | Thevenin/Norton problems | Formula review |
| Fri | System of linear equations | Superposition theorem | Formula review |
| Sat | Practice 20 math questions | Practice 20 network questions | Review mistakes |
| Sun | REST or light revision | - | - |

### Week 2: Calculus + Transients
- Differentiation rules, chain rule
- Integration techniques
- First-order RC and RL circuits
- Time constant calculations
- Steady-state and transient response
- t=0+ initial conditions

### Week 3: Differential Equations + Second-Order Circuits
- 1st order ODEs (separable, linear)
- 2nd order linear ODEs
- RLC series/parallel transients
- Over-damped, under-damped, critically-damped
- Neper and resonant frequencies

### Week 4: Probability + RLC Resonance
- Probability distributions (Poisson, uniform, normal)
- Bayes theorem
- RLC series and parallel resonance
- Q-factor and bandwidth
- Half-power frequencies

---

## Month 2: Electronic Devices + Analog Circuits

### Week 5: Semiconductor Physics + Op-Amp Basics
- Energy bands, intrinsic/extrinsic semiconductors
- PN junction physics, V-I characteristics
- Ideal op-amp properties (virtual ground/short)
- Inverting and non-inverting amplifier
- Gain formulas

### Week 6: BJT/MOSFET + Op-Amp Linear Circuits
- BJT: alpha-beta relations, biasing
- MOSFET: enhancement/depletion, drain current
- Summing amplifier, difference amplifier
- T-network feedback
- Instrumentation amplifier

### Week 7: Special Diodes + Op-Amp Non-Linear Circuits
- Zener, tunnel, varactor, Schottky diodes
- Comparators
- Schmitt triggers (UTP, LTP, hysteresis)
- Zero-crossing detector
- Window comparator

### Week 8: Small-Signal Models + Feedback + Power Amplifiers
- BJT hybrid-pi model
- MOSFET small-signal model
- Feedback topologies (4 types)
- Effects on gain, BW, impedance
- Class A/B/AB/C/D/F amplifiers
- Efficiency formulas

---

## Month 3: Digital Electronics + Communication Systems

### Week 9: Number Systems + Boolean Algebra
- Binary, octal, hex conversions
- BCD, Gray code, 1s/2s complement
- Boolean theorems, De Morgan's laws
- K-maps (2,3,4 variable)
- Don't care conditions

### Week 10: Combinational + Sequential Circuits
- Half/full adders and subtractors
- MUX, DEMUX, decoders, encoders
- Flip-flops (SR, JK, D, T)
- Excitation tables, characteristic equations
- Counters and shift registers

### Week 11: FSMs + VHDL + AM Modulation
- Moore vs Mealy machines
- Sequence detector design
- VHDL entity/architecture, data types
- Signal vs variable
- Standard AM, DSB-SC, SSB
- Modulation index, power relations

### Week 12: FM + Digital Modulation + PCM
- FM vs PM, beta, Carson's rule
- BPSK, QPSK, 16-QAM
- PCM (sampling, quantization, encoding)
- SQNR formula
- Companding (A-law, mu-law)

---

## Month 4: Electromagnetics + Antennas + Microwaves

### Week 13: Vector Analysis + Maxwell's Equations
- Gradient, divergence, curl
- Stokes and Gauss divergence theorems
- 4 Maxwell's equations (differential and integral)
- Boundary conditions

### Week 14: Plane Waves + Transmission Lines
- Intrinsic impedance, Poynting vector
- Polarization, skin depth
- Brewster angle, total internal reflection
- Characteristic impedance, VSWR
- Input impedance, reflection coefficient

### Week 15: Smith Chart + Impedance Matching + Waveguides
- Smith chart construction and usage
- Single stub matching
- Quarter-wave transformer
- TE/TM/TEM modes
- Cutoff frequency, dominant mode
- Phase and group velocity

### Week 16: Microwave Devices + Antenna Fundamentals
- Gunn diode, IMPATT, TWT, HEMT
- S-parameters, S-matrix properties
- Directional coupler, Magic tee
- Radiation resistance, directivity, gain
- Friis transmission equation
- EIRP calculation

---

## Month 5: Control Systems + DSP

### Week 17: System Modeling + Routh-Hurwitz + Root Locus
- Transfer function derivation
- Block diagram reduction
- Routh array construction
- Special cases (row of zeros)
- Root locus construction rules

### Week 18: Bode Plots + Nyquist + State Space
- Magnitude and phase plots
- Corner frequencies
- Nyquist encirclement criterion
- Gain and phase margins
- State-space A, B, C, D matrices
- Controllability and observability

### Week 19: Compensators + PID + Fourier Transforms
- Lead compensator (phase lead)
- Lag compensator (gain improvement)
- PID characteristics
- Ziegler-Nichols tuning
- Fourier Series and Transform
- Properties and standard pairs

### Week 20: Laplace/Z-transform + DTFT + FFT
- ROC determination
- Inverse transforms (partial fractions)
- Initial/final value theorem
- Z-transform properties
- DTFT definition and properties
- FFT DIT butterfly, log2(N) stages

---

## Month 6: Non-GATE Subjects + First Revision

### Week 21: Power Electronics
- SCR characteristics, two-transistor model
- Single-phase rectifiers (half-wave, full-wave, bridge)
- Vdc formulas
- DC-DC converters (Buck, Boost, Buck-Boost)
- Duty cycle relationships

### Week 22: Power Electronics (contd) + Measurements
- Inverters, choppers, commutation
- PWM techniques
- SMPS vs linear regulators
- AC bridges (Wien, Maxwell, Schering)
- LVDT, strain gauge

### Week 23: Measurements + Electrical Machines
- Voltmeter loading effects
- PMMC instruments
- CRO operations
- DC motor basics
- Induction motor slip

### Week 24: First Complete Revision - All Subjects

- Identify weak areas
- Practice 50 questions per day
