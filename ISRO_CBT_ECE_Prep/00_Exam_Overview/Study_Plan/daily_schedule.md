## Effective Study Techniques

### Active Recall Method
1. Read a concept
2. Close the book
3. Write down everything you remember
4. Check and fill gaps
5. Repeat after 1 day, 3 days, 7 days

### Spaced Repetition Schedule
| Review Cycle | Timing | What to Review |
|-------------|--------|----------------|
| 1st review | Same day evening | Today's new formulas |
| 2nd review | Next morning | Yesterday's formulas |
| 3rd review | After 3 days | Day before yesterday's formulas |
| 4th review | After 1 week | Last week's formulas |
| 5th review | After 1 month | Monthly comprehensive review |

### Practice Question Strategy
1. **First pass:** Attempt all questions without time limit
2. **Second pass:** Note down incorrect and unattempted questions
3. **Third pass:** Re-study the concept from Concepts.md
4. **Fourth pass:** Solve similar questions from another source
5. **Timed practice:** Solve 20 questions in 25 minutes (75 sec/question)

### Formula Memorization Technique
1. Write each formula 3 times
2. Say it aloud while writing
3. Create a mnemonic for complex formulas
4. Test yourself before sleep
5. Review immediately after waking up
