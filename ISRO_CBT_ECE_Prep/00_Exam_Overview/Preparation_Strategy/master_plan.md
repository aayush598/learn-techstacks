# ISRO Scientist/Engineer 'SC' (ECE) - Master Preparation Plan

## Overview
- **Target:** ISRO ICRB Electronics (ECE) CBT Exam
- **Duration:** 8 months comprehensive preparation
- **Daily Commitment:** 8-10 hours (dedicated aspirants) / 5-6 hours (working professionals)
- **Goal:** Score 70+ out of 95 questions (top percentile)

## Phase 2: Core Technical Subjects (Month 3-5)

### Month 3: Digital Electronics + Communication Systems
**Week 9-10: Digital Circuits**
- Number systems, Boolean algebra, K-maps
- Combinational circuits (MUX, decoders, adders)
- Sequential circuits (flip-flops, counters, FSMs)
- VHDL fundamentals, metastability/MTBF

**Week 11-12: Communication Systems**
- AM modulation (standard, DSB-SC, SSB, VSB)
- FM modulation, Carson's rule
- Digital modulation (PSK, QAM, FSK)
- PCM, TDM, sampling theorem

### Month 4: Electromagnetics + Antennas + Microwaves
**Week 13-14: Electromagnetics**
- Maxwell's equations, plane wave propagation
- Transmission lines, impedance matching
- Smith chart usage

**Week 15-16: Microwaves + Antennas**
- Waveguides (TE, TM, cutoff frequencies)
- Microwave devices (Gunn, IMPATT, TWT, HEMT)
- S-parameters, microwave components
- Antenna fundamentals, Friis equation, EIRP

### Month 5: Control Systems + Filters/DSP
**Week 17-18: Control Systems**
- Transfer functions, block diagram reduction
- Routh-Hurwitz stability
- Root locus, Bode plots, Nyquist criterion
- State-space analysis, compensators, PID

**Week 19-20: DSP + Filter Design**
- DTFT, DFT, FFT algorithms
- Digital filter structures (IIR, FIR)
- Filter design methods (Butterworth, Chebyshev)

## Key Success Principles
1. **Formula First:** For ISRO, knowing formulas saves time (75 sec/question)
2. **Non-GATE Subjects Matter:** 20-25% of paper is unique to ISRO
3. **Speed over Depth:** Quick recall > lengthy derivations
4. **Mock Tests are Essential:** Build exam temperament
5. **Part B is Free Marks:** No negative marking - attempt ALL 15 questions
6. **Consistency beats Intensity:** 6 hours daily > 14 hours on weekends
