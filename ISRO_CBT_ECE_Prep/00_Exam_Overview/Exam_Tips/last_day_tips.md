# Last Day Preparation Tips

## The Day Before ISRO CBT Exam

---

## DO NOT DO
- Do NOT start any new topic
- Do NOT solve very difficult problems
- Do NOT stay up late studying
- Do NOT discuss exam difficulty with others
- Do NOT skip meals
- Do NOT panic about syllabus completion

## What TO Do

### Morning (Light Revision Only)
- [ ] Glance through formula handbook (30 min max)
- [ ] Review quick reference cards (15 min)
- [ ] Read through ISRO shortcuts file (10 min)

### Afternoon (Logistics + Relaxation)
- [ ] Check exam center location and distance
- [ ] Plan route - account for traffic
- [ ] Keep admit card, ID proof ready
- [ ] Pack: pen, pencil, eraser, watch (non-smart)
- [ ] Check exam timing and reporting time

### Evening (Complete Relaxation)
- [ ] Light walk or exercise (30 min)
- [ ] Watch something relaxing
- [ ] Talk to family/friends (positive conversations only)
- [ ] No studying after 6 PM

### Night (Sleep Preparation)
- [ ] Sleep by 9:30-10:00 PM
- [ ] Set 2 alarms (30 min apart)
- [ ] Keep clothes ready for tomorrow
- [ ] Light dinner (no heavy food)
- [ ] No screen time 30 min before sleep

## Quick Formula Glance (5-Minute Morning-of-Exam)

### High-Yield Formulas to Recall
```
Analog: LTP = Vsat * R1/(R1+R2), f_osc = 1/(2piRC) [Wien]
Digital: MTBF = (1/fclk) * e^(Tsetup/tau)
EM: Z0 = sqrt(L/C), VSWR = (1+|Gamma|)/(1-|Gamma|)
Comm: SQNR = 6.02n + 1.76 dB, C = B*log2(1+SNR)
Signals: Convolution identity: x(t) * delta(t) = x(t)
Network: tau = RC (first order), f0 = 1/(2pi*sqrt(LC))
EDC: kT/q = 26mV at room temp
Control: Gain margin, Phase margin from Bode
Power: Vdc_buck = D*V_in, Vdc_boost = V_in/(1-D)
Math: det(AB) = det(A)*det(B)
```

## Mental Preparation

### Confidence Boosters
1. You have prepared for 8 months
2. You know the exam pattern thoroughly
3. You have practiced hundreds of questions
4. Part B has no negative marking - guaranteed marks
5. ISRO questions are formula-based and direct
6. Many questions (~35%) are easy recall type

### Stress Management
- Deep breathing: 4 counts in, 7 hold, 8 out
- Positive self-talk: "I am prepared and ready"
- Remember: It is just an exam, not life or death
- Many attempts possible - this is not your last chance

### Time Anxiety Prevention
- Trust your preparation
- If stuck for more than 90 seconds, move on
- Mark and return to skipped questions
- Part B is easier - save time for it
- Last 10 minutes: attempt any remaining questions
