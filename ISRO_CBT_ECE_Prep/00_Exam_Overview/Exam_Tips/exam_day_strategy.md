| Arrive | 30-45 minutes before reporting time |

### After Seating
- Verify your roll number and seat
- Check computer/screen is working
- Note exam duration: 120 minutes
- Wait for instructions

## Exam Execution Strategy

### Phase 1: Quick Scan (First 5 minutes)
1. Open the exam paper
2. Quickly scan all 95 questions
3. Mentally categorize:
   - **GREEN:** Can solve immediately (< 30 sec)
   - **YELLOW:** Need some thinking (30-90 sec)
   - **RED:** Unsure or lengthy (> 90 sec)

### Phase 2: Easy Questions First (Minutes 5-60)
- Solve all GREEN questions first across Part A and Part B
- These are direct formula recalls (~35% of paper)
- Expected: 25-30 questions in 55 minutes
- Do NOT get stuck on any single question

### Phase 3: Moderate Questions (Minutes 60-95)
- Now attempt YELLOW questions
- These require 1-2 step calculations (~45% of paper)
- Expected: 25-30 questions in 35 minutes
- If stuck for > 90 seconds, mark and move on

### Phase 4: Difficult Questions (Minutes 95-110)
- Attempt RED questions if time permits
- These are multi-step or tricky (~20% of paper)
- Use elimination to narrow choices
- Even partial guessing with elimination improves odds

### Phase 5: Final Review (Last 10 minutes)
- Verify all questions are attempted
- Check Part B: ALL 15 questions must be answered (no negative marking!)
- Review any flagged questions
- Do NOT change answers unless you're sure

## Subject-wise Attempt Order for Part A

### Recommended Order (Based on Speed + Accuracy)
1. **Communication Systems** (Quick formulas, high weightage)
2. **Analog Circuits** (Direct op-amp, amplifier problems)
3. **Digital Circuits** (K-maps, MUX, VHDL are fast)
4. **Signals and DSP** (Convolution, transform properties)
5. **Network Theory** (t=0+ conditions, resonance)
6. **Electronic Devices** (Formula-based, quick)
7. **Control Systems** (Routh, Bode - needs some calculation)
8. **Electromagnetics** (Waveguide, antenna - some lengthy)
9. **Power Electronics** (Converter formulas)
10. **Engineering Math** (Quick if you know the formula)
11. **Measurements** (Bridge balance, LVDT - quick)

### Why This Order?
- Start with high-confidence, high-weightage subjects
- Build momentum with easy questions
- Save time-consuming calculations for later
- Part B is independent - do it whenever convenient

## Part B Strategy (Aptitude - 15 Questions, No Negative Marking)

### Golden Rules
1. **Attempt ALL 15 questions** - No negative marking!
2. Spend maximum 2 minutes per question (30 min total)
3. Visual/abstract reasoning: Trust your first instinct
4. Syllogisms: Draw Venn diagrams
5. Quantitative: Estimate before calculating
6. If unsure, eliminate 2 choices and pick from remaining

### Time Allocation for Part B
- 15 questions x 2 minutes = 30 minutes
- OR: Do it in two sittings (7-8 questions each)
- Best time: After completing 60+ Part A questions

---

## Negative Marking Strategy

### Part A: -0.33 for Wrong Answer
- **Expected Value Analysis:**
  - If you can eliminate 1 option out of 4: Attempt (EV positive)
  - If you can eliminate 2 options: Definitely attempt
  - If completely random guess: Do NOT attempt (EV negative)
  - If 50% sure: Attempt (EV = 0.5*1 - 0.5*0.33 = +0.335)

### Decision Framework
| Confidence Level | Action |
|-----------------|--------|
| > 80% sure | Answer immediately |
| 50-80% sure | Answer (positive EV) |
| 30-50% sure | Eliminate and guess if 2 options eliminated |
| < 30% sure | Skip unless you can eliminate 2+ options |

---

## Common Exam Day Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Spending too long on one question** - 90 seconds max, then skip
2. **Not attempting Part B fully** - Free marks, no negative marking
3. **Changing correct answers** - First instinct is usually right
4. **Ignoring negative marking** - Every wrong answer costs 0.33 marks
5. **Not reading options carefully** - "NOT" and "EXCEPT" change everything
6. **Calculator expectation** - ISRO CBT has NO calculator - practice mental math
7. **Starting with hard questions** - Kills confidence and wastes time
8. **Not managing time** - Check clock every 15-20 questions
