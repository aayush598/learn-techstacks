## Detailed Subject-wise Analysis

### 1. Analog Electronics (10.8% weightage)

**Average Questions**: 10-11 per paper
**Average Marks**: 10-11

#### Topic-wise Distribution

| Topic | Typical Questions | Importance |
|-------|-------------------|------------|
| Operational Amplifiers | 3-4 | ★★★★★ |
| Diode Circuits | 2-3 | ★★★★☆ |
| BJT Amplifiers | 2-3 | ★★★★★ |
| MOSFET Circuits | 2-3 | ★★★★☆ |
| Feedback Amplifiers | 1-2 | ★★★☆☆ |
| Oscillators | 1-2 | ★★★☆☆ |
| Power Amplifiers | 1-2 | ★★★☆☆ |

#### Key Topics to Focus

1. **Operational Amplifiers**
   - Inverting and non-inverting configurations
   - Differential amplifiers
   - Instrumentation amplifiers
   - Active filters (Sallen-Key, MFB)
   - Comparators and Schmitt triggers
   - Integrator and differentiator circuits

2. **Diode Circuits**
   - Rectifier circuits (half-wave, full-wave, bridge)
   - Clipper and clamper circuits
   - Zener diode voltage regulators
   - LED circuits
   - Diode resistance models

3. **BJT Amplifiers**
   - Common emitter, collector, base configurations
   - Biasing techniques (fixed bias, voltage divider, collector feedback)
   - Small signal analysis (h-parameter, hybrid-pi)
   - Frequency response (low frequency, high frequency, Miller effect)
   - Cascaded amplifiers

4. **MOSFET Circuits**
   - Enhancement and depletion modes
   - Common source, drain, gate configurations
   - Biasing techniques
   - Small signal analysis
   - CMOS basics

#### Recommended Questions Practice
- Solve 150+ problems from this section
- Focus on Op-Amp circuits (most frequent)
- Practice numerical problems from BJT/MOSFET analysis

| A/D and D/A Converters | 1-2 | ★★★★☆ |

#### Key Topics to Focus

1. **Boolean Algebra & Logic Gates**
   - Boolean theorems and identities
   - Karnaugh maps (up to 5 variables)
   - Boolean function simplification
   - Logic gate implementations
   - Universal gates (NAND, NOR)

2. **Combinational Circuits**
   - Multiplexers and demultiplexers
   - Encoders and decoders
   - Adders and subtractors (half, full, parallel)
   - Magnitude comparators
   - Parity generators/checkers

3. **Sequential Circuits**
   - Flip-flops (SR, JK, D, T, Master-Slave)
   - Counters (synchronous, asynchronous, up/down)
   - Shift registers (SISO, SIPO, PISO, PIPO)
   - State machine design (Mealy, Moore)
   - State diagram and state table

4. **Number Systems & Codes**
   - Binary, octal, hexadecimal conversions
   - BCD, Gray code, Excess-3
   - 1's complement and 2's complement
   - Signed and unsigned numbers

5. **A/D and D/A Converters**
   - Flash ADC
   - Successive approximation ADC
   - Dual slope ADC
   - R-2R ladder DAC
   - Weighted resistor DAC

#### Recommended Questions Practice
- Solve 120+ problems
- Focus on sequential circuits (most frequent)
- Practice state machine design problems

## Strategic Implications

### High-Return Investment Subjects

These subjects give maximum marks for minimum effort:

1. **Digital Electronics** (Easy to score, high weightage)
   - Standard problems repeat
   - Conceptual clarity helps
   - High scoring potential

2. **Measurements** (Easy to score, moderate weightage)
   - Formula-based questions
   - Limited topic coverage
   - Quick to prepare

3. **Power Electronics** (Moderate effort, decent weightage)
   - Conceptual understanding needed
   - Standard problem types
   - Good ROI

### Subjects Requiring Most Effort

1. **Electromagnetic Theory** (Complex, high weightage)
   - Mathematical heavy
   - Conceptual depth needed
   - Long preparation time

2. **Analog Electronics** (Complex, high weightage)
   - Multiple circuit types
   - Numerical intensive
   - Requires extensive practice

3. **Signals and Systems** (Mathematical, high weightage)
   - Transform analysis
   - Mathematical derivations
   - Practice essential
