# ISRO CBT ECE - Complete Reference Book List

## Master Book List with Chapter Mapping

---

## 1. Engineering Mathematics

### Primary: Higher Engineering Mathematics by B.S. Grewal
- Chapter 1: Matrices (determinants, eigenvalues, Cayley-Hamilton)
- Chapter 7: Differential Equations (1st and 2nd order)
- Chapter 12: Partial Differential Equations
- Chapter 21: Probability and Statistics

### Supplementary: Advanced Engineering Mathematics by Erwin Kreyszig
- Linear Algebra chapters
- Complex Analysis (residues, conformal mapping)
- Numerical Methods

---

## 2. Network Theory

### Primary: Engineering Circuit Analysis by Hayt, Kemmerly and Durbin
- Chapters 1-2: Circuit variables, basic laws (KCL/KVL)
- Chapter 3: Methods of analysis (nodal/mesh)
- Chapter 4: Circuit theorems (Thevenin, Norton, superposition)
- Chapter 6: Capacitance and inductance
- Chapters 7-8: First and second order circuits (transients)
- Chapter 10: Sinusoidal steady state analysis

### Alternative: Electric Circuits by Joseph Edminister
- All chapters excellent for practice problems
- Particularly good for two-port networks

### For ISRO Specific: Network Analysis by M.E. Van Valkenburg
- Driving point impedance synthesis
- Signal flow graphs
- Filter networks

---

## 3. Electronic Devices

### Primary: Microelectronic Circuits by Sedra and Smith (8th Edition)
- Chapter 4: Diodes (PN junction, special diodes)
- Chapters 5-6: BJT (physics, biasing, small-signal)
- Chapters 7-8: MOSFET (physics, biasing, small-signal)

### Supplementary: Electronic Devices and Circuit Theory by Boylestad
- Simpler explanations for quick understanding
- Good for BJT biasing circuits, stability factor

### For Semiconductor Physics: Semiconductor Physics and Devices by Donald Neamen
- Energy band theory
- Carrier concentration calculations
- Fermi level problems
- Breakdown mechanisms

---

## 4. Analog Electronics

### Primary: Microelectronic Circuits by Sedra and Smith
- Chapters 1-2: Op-amp fundamentals
- Chapter 10: Feedback
- Chapter 12: Oscillators
- Chapter 13: Active filters

### Alternative: Op-Amps and Linear Integrated Circuits by Ramakant Gayakwad
- Excellent for op-amp circuits specifically
- IC voltage regulators (78xx, LM317)
- Active filter design

### For Power Amplifiers: Integrated Electronics by Millman and Halkias
- Class A/B/C/D/F amplifier analysis
- Efficiency derivations

---

## 5. Digital Electronics

### Primary: Digital Design by M. Morris Mano (5th Edition)
- Chapters 1-2: Number systems, Boolean algebra, K-maps
- Chapters 3-4: Combinational circuits
- Chapters 5-6: Sequential circuits
- Chapters 7-8: Registers, counters, memory

### For VHDL: VHDL Primer by J. Bhasker
- Complete VHDL coverage for ISRO
- Data types, signal vs variable
- Process blocks, delta delays

### For Verilog: Verilog HDL by Samir Palnitkar
- Module definitions
- Blocking vs non-blocking
- Always/initial blocks

---

## 6. Signals and Systems / DSP

### Primary: Signals and Systems by Oppenheim and Willsky
- Chapter 1: Introduction to signals and systems
- Chapter 2: Time-domain analysis (convolution)
- Chapter 3: Fourier Series
- Chapter 4: Continuous-time Fourier Transform
- Chapter 5: Laplace Transform
- Chapter 8: Sampling theorem
- Chapter 9: Z-transform

### For DSP: Digital Signal Processing by Proakis and Manolakis
- FFT butterfly diagrams
- Filter design (Butterworth, Chebyshev)
- Windowing techniques

---

## 7. Communication Systems

### Primary: Communication Systems by Simon Haykin (4th Edition)
- Chapters 3-4: AM and FM modulation
- Chapter 5: Sampling and quantization (PCM)
- Chapter 7: Digital communication (PSK, QAM, FSK)
- Chapter 8: Information theory (entropy, channel capacity)
- Chapter 9: Error control coding

### Alternative: Modern Digital and Analog Communication Systems by B.P. Lathi
- Simpler explanations
- Good for modulation index problems

---

## 8. Electromagnetics

### Primary: Elements of Electromagnetics by Matthew Sadiku (6th Edition)
- Chapters 1-3: Vector analysis, electrostatics, magnetostatics
- Chapter 6: Maxwell's equations
- Chapter 8: Wave propagation
- Chapter 10: Transmission lines

---

## 9. Microwave Engineering

### Primary: Microwave Engineering by David M. Pozar (4th Edition)
- Chapter 1: EM review and transmission line theory
- Chapter 2: Impedance matching
- Chapter 3: Transmission lines and S-parameters
- Chapter 6: Power dividers, directional couplers
- Chapter 8: Amplifier design (noise figure)

### Supplementary: Microwave Devices and Circuits by L.S. Liao
- Microwave devices (Gunn, IMPATT, TWT, klystron)

---

## 10. Antenna Theory

### Primary: Antenna Theory by Constantine Balanis (4th Edition)
- Chapter 2: Fundamental parameters (directivity, gain)
- Chapter 4: Linear antennas (dipole, monopole)
- Chapter 6: Antenna arrays, array factor
- Chapter 9: Reflector antennas (parabolic)
- Chapter 14: Microstrip/patch antennas

---

## 11. Control Systems

### Primary: Modern Control Engineering by Katsuhiko Ogata (5th Edition)
- Chapters 2-3: Transfer functions, block diagrams
- Chapter 5: Routh-Hurwitz stability criterion
- Chapter 6: Root locus method
- Chapters 8-9: Bode plots, Nyquist criterion
- Chapter 10: State-space analysis
- Chapter 12: Compensator design (lead/lag)
- Chapter 13: PID control

### Supplementary: Control Systems Engineering by Norman Nise
- More examples and practice problems

---

## 12. Power Electronics

### Primary: Power Electronics by P.S. Bimbhra
- Chapter 1: Static characteristics of SCR
- Chapter 2: SCR triggering circuits
- Chapter 3: Converters (single-phase, three-phase)
- Chapter 4: Inverters
- Chapter 5: Choppers
- Chapter 6: Commutation techniques

### Supplementary: Power Electronics by Mohan, Undeland, Robbins
- Modern treatment of DC-DC converters
- SMPS topologies

---

## 13. Measurements and Instrumentation

### Primary: A Course in Electrical and Electronic Measurements and Instrumentation by A.K. Sawhney
- DC bridges (Wheatstone, Kelvin)
- AC bridges (Wien, Maxwell, Schering, Hay)
- PMMC instruments
- Electronic instruments (DVM, CRO)
- Transducers (LVDT, strain gauge, thermocouple)

### Supplementary: Electronic Instrumentation by H.S. Kalsi
- LVDT details
- Strain gauge applications

---

## 14. Aptitude and Reasoning (Part B)

- Quantitative Aptitude by R.S. Aggarwal
- A Modern Approach to Verbal and Non-Verbal Reasoning by R.S. Aggarwal
- Non-Verbal Reasoning practice from CAT/GMAT sections

## Book Reading Strategy

1. First read the chapter summary
2. Go through solved examples
3. Study theory only for concepts you do not understand
4. Solve end-chapter problems (at least 10 per chapter)
