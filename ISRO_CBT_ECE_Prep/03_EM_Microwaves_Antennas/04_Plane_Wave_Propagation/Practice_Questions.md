# Plane Wave Propagation - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) Intrinsic impedance of free space:
(A) 377 ohm  (B) 50 ohm  (C) 120 ohm  (D) 75 ohm

**Answer: (A) η0 = 377 ohm (120π)**

---

### Q2. (Moderate) Phase velocity in free space:
(A) c  (B) c/√2  (C) c/2  (D) 0

**Answer: (A) v = c = 3e8 m/s (light speed)**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) Intrinsic impedance of lossless medium:
(A) sqrt(μ/ε)  (B) sqrt(ε/μ)  (C) μ/ε  (D) ε/μ

**Answer: (A) η = sqrt(μ/ε)**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) In a good conductor, skin depth:
(A) delta = 1/sqrt(π f μ σ)  (B) sqrt(f)  (C) large  (D) constant

**Answer: (A) skin depth δ = 1/sqrt(π f μ σ) (decreases with freq)**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) Wave in free space propagating in +z dirn E=E0 cos(wt-kz); H is:
(A) Same direction  (B) Orthogonal (mutually perpendicular)  (C) Opposite  (D) Zero

**Answer: (B) E and H mutually perpendicular (EM wave)**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) E/H ratio in a medium = :
(A) η (intrinsic impedance)  (B) Z0  (C) 1  (D) 377 always

**Answer: (A) E/H = η**

---

### Q7. (Moderate) Skin effect is:
(A) Current concentrated near surface at high freq  (B) Core  (C) Zero  (D) None

**Answer: (A) Skin effect - current confined near conductor surface at high frequency**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) Frequency with wavelength 1 cm:
(A) 30 GHz  (B) 3 GHz  (C) 300 MHz  (D) 30 MHz

**Solution:** f = c/λ = 3e8/0.01 = 3e10 = 30 GHz
**Answer: (A) 30 GHz**

---

### Q9. (Moderate) In dielectric, η < 377 because:
(A) μ/ε < μ0/ε0  (B) Higher ε  (C) Same  (D) Lower speed

**Answer: (B) permittivity ε > ε0 -> η = sqrt(μ/ε) smaller**

---

### Q10. (Moderate) Phase constant k related to wavelength:
(A) 2π/λ  (B) λ  (C) 2πλ  (D) 1/λ

**Answer: (A) k = 2π/λ**
