# Maxwell's Equations - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) Gauss's law for electric field:
(A) ∇·D = ρv  (B) ∇·B = 0  (C) ∇×E = -∂B/∂t  (D) ∇×H = J

**Answer: (A) ∇·D = ρv**

---

### Q2. (Easy) ∇·B = 0 means:
(A) Magnetic monopoles don't exist  (B) E nonzero  (C) Charge  (D) Current

**Answer: (A) No magnetic monopoles (B solenoidal)**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) Faraday's law differential:
(A) ∇×E = -∂B/∂t  (B) ∇×H = J  (C) ∇·D = ρ  (D) ∇×B

**Answer: (A) ∇×E = -∂B/∂t (changing B induces E)**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) Ampère-Maxwell law:
(A) ∇×H = J + ∂D/∂t  (B) ∇×E = 0  (C) ∇·H  (D) none

**Answer: (A) ∇×H = J + ∂D/∂t (includes displacement current)**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) Displacement current density:
(A) J  (B) ∂D/∂t  (C) σE  (D) ρ

**Answer: (B) ∂D/∂t (Maxwell's addition enabling wave propagation)**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) In free space, Maxwell equations reduce with:
(A) ρ=0, J=0  (B) σ large  (C) only H  (D) only E

**Answer: (A) No free charge (ρ=0) and no conduction current (J=0)**

---

### Q7. (Moderate) In a source-free region, wave propagates because of:
(A) Conduction  (B) Coupling of E and B (displacement current)  (C) Charge  (D) DC

**Answer: (B) Mutual induction between changing E and B (EM wave)**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) Integral form: ∮E·dl = -d/dt∬B·dS is:
(A) Gauss  (B) Faraday  (C) Ampère  (D) Gauss magnetic

**Answer: (B) Faraday's law**

---

### Q9. (Moderate) Continuity equation:
(A) ∇·J = -∂ρ/∂t  (B) ∇·J = 0  (C) ∇×J = 0  (D) ∂ρ/∂t = 0

**Answer: (A) ∇·J = -∂ρ/∂t (charge conservation)**

---

### Q10. (Moderate) Boundary condition for electric flux normal:
(A) D1n - D2n = ρs  (B) E1t = 0  (C) H1n  (D) none

**Answer: (A) D1n - D2n = ρs (surface charge)**
