# Antenna Types - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) Half-wave dipole length for 100 MHz:
(A) 1.5 m  (B) 3 m  (C) 0.75 m  (D) 15 m

**Solution:** λ = c/f = 3e8/1e8 = 3m; half-wave = 1.5 m
**Answer: (A) 1.5 m**

---

### Q2. (Moderate) Horn antenna is:
(A) Aperture antenna (waveguide flared)  (B) Wire  (C) Loop  (D) Microstrip

**Answer: (A) Aperture (flare of waveguide) - directive**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) Parabolic reflector antenna provides:
(A) High gain/directionality  (B) Omnidirectional  (C) Broadband tiny  (D) Low gain

**Answer: (A) High gain, high directivity (focus into beam, dish)**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) Yagi-Uda has:
(A) Driven element + reflectors + directors  (B) Single dipole  (C) Only reflector  (D) Only lens

**Answer: (A) Driven element with parasitic reflector + directors (high forward gain)**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) Microstrip patch antenna is:
(A) Planar, low profile, moderate gain  (B) Very high gain  (C) Huge  (D) Omnidirectional always

**Answer: (A) Planar low-profile (PCB) - moderate gain, cheap**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) Loop antenna small loop radiates:
(A) Weakly (small radiation resistance)  (B) Strongly  (C) Donut  (D) No

**Answer: (A) Small loop low radiation resistance - used as receiving/less efficient radiator**

---

### Q7. (Moderate) Helical antenna radiates:
(A) Circular polarization  (B) Only vertical  (C) Only horizontal  (D) No polarization

**Answer: (A) Circular polarization (axial mode)**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) Log-periodic antenna is:
(A) Broadband (frequency independent)  (B) Narrowband  (C) Only receive  (D) Only ground

**Answer: (A) Broadband - frequency independent gain across wide band**

---

### Q9. (Moderate) Phased array beam steering by:
(A) Controlling phase of elements  (B) Only gain  (C) Only power  (D) Frequency

**Answer: (A) Adjusting relative phase of element currents (electronic beam steering)**

---

### Q10. (Moderate) Quarter-wave monopole over ground has:
(A) ~half dipole radiation resistance / pattern  (B) Same as dipole  (C) Zero  (D) Double gain

**Answer: (A) Monopole is half of dipole (over ground plane), rad resistance ~36.5 ohm**
