# Transmission Lines - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) Characteristic impedance of lossless line:
(A) sqrt(L/C)  (B) sqrt(R/G)  (C) L*C  (D) R

**Answer: (A) Z0 = sqrt(L/C)**

---

### Q2. (Moderate) Z0 for 3mm coax (lossless) usually ~:
(A) 50 ohm  (B) 1 ohm  (C) 1000 ohm  (D) 0 ohm

**Answer: (A) 50 ohm (standard, also 75/93 for video/data)**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) Velocity of propagation on lossless line:
(A) 1/sqrt(LC)  (B) 1/sqrt(LC)  (C) sqrt(LC)  (D) 1/LC

**Answer: (A/B) v = 1/sqrt(LC)**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) VSWR for ZL=Zo (matched):
(A) 1  (B) 0  (C) infinity  (D) 2

**Answer: (A) 1 (no reflection, perfect match)**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) Reflection coefficient for open circuit (ZL=inf):
(A) 0  (B) +1  (C) -1  (D) 0.5

**Answer: (B) +1 (full reflection, in phase)**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) Reflection coefficient for short circuit:
(A) +1  (B) -1  (C) 0  (D) 0.5

**Answer: (B) -1 (full reflection, inverted)**

---

### Q7. (Moderate) Quarter-wave line acts as:
(A) Impedance transformer (Zin = Zo^2/ZL)  (B) Match  (C) Short  (D) Open

**Answer: (A) Quarter-wave transformer Zin=Zo^2/ZL**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) VSWR from reflection Gamma=0.5:
(A) 1  (B) 3  (C) 2  (D) 1.5

**Solution:** VSWR=(1+|Γ|)/(1-|Γ|) = (1.5)/(0.5) = 3
**Answer: (B) 3**

---

### Q9. (Moderate) VSWR from Gamma=0.2:
(A) 1.5  (B) 1.2  (C) 2  (D) 0.8

**Solution:** (1.2)/(0.8) = 1.5
**Answer: (A) 1.5**

---

### Q10. (Moderate) Line is matched (ZL=Zo); reflected power:
(A) All reflected  (B) Zero  (C) Half  (D) Max

**Answer: (B) Zero (no reflected wave)**
