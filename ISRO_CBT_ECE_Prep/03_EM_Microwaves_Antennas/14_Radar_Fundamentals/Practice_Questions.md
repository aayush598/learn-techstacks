# Radar Fundamentals - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) Radar stands for:
(A) Radio Detection And Ranging  (B) Radio And Distance  (C) Rapid Direction  (D) None

**Answer: (A) RAdio Detection And Ranging**

---

### Q2. (Moderate) Radar range R from time delay t:
(A) R = c t / 2  (B) R = c t  (C) R = c t/4  (D) R = 2ct

**Answer: (A) R = c*t/2 (round trip: 2R = c t)**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) Max unambiguous range with pulse repetition T:
(A) R = c T/2  (B) R = c T  (C) R = c/2T  (D) R = 2cT

**Answer: (A) Rmax = c*T/2 (unambiguous, PRF = 1/T)**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) Radar range equation: received power Pr:
(A) ∝ 1/R^4  (B) ∝ 1/R^2  (C) ∝ R^4  (D) ∝ R^2

**Answer: (A) Pr ∝ 1/R^4 (round-trip) - crucial for radar**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) Doppler radar measures:
(A) Radial velocity (frequency shift)  (B) Range only  (C) Angle only  (D) Power only

**Answer: (A) Radial Velocity via Doppler frequency shift**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) Pulse radar measures:
(A) Range (delay)  (B) Only Doppler  (C) Only weather  (D) Color

**Answer: (A) Range from echo time delay**

---

### Q7. (Moderate) Higher PRF (shorter T) causes:
(A) Ambiguous range (shorter max range)  (B) Longer range  (C) No effect  (D) False Doppler

**Answer: (A) Shorter unambiguous range (R=cT/2); may cause ambiguity**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) Radar cross section (RCS) σ of target:
(A) Effective scattering area  (B) Weight  (C) Speed  (D) Color

**Answer: (A) Effective area that scatters energy toward receiver**

---

### Q9. (Moderate) CW radar (no pulses) measures:
(A) Doppler/velocity only (no range)  (B) Range only  (C) Both  (D) Neither

**Answer: (A) Continuous wave - measures Doppler (velocity), cannot measure range directly**

---

### Q10. (Moderate) MTI (moving target indication) removes:
(A) Clutter/stationary returns  (B) Moving targets  (C) Noise only  (D) All

**Answer: (A) Removes stationary clutter (uses Doppler to cancel fixed echoes)**
