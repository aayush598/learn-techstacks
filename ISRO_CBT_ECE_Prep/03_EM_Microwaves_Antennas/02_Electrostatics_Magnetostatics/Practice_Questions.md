### Q1. Electric Field Calculation

**Answer: (b)**

### Q2. Gauss's Law Application
A spherical conductor of radius 5cm has charge 2μC. The electric field at 10cm from center is:
(a) 1.8 × 10⁶ V/m
(b) 7.2 × 10⁶ V/m
(c) 3.6 × 10⁶ V/m
(d) 0.9 × 10⁶ V/m

**Answer: (a)**
E = Q/(4πε₀r²) = (2×10⁻⁶)/(4π×8.854×10⁻¹²×0.01) = 1.8 × 10⁶ V/m

### Q3. Capacitance
A parallel plate capacitor has plate area 100cm² and separation 5mm. With dielectric εᵣ = 4, capacitance is:
(a) 70.8 pF
(b) 141.6 pF
(c) 35.4 pF
(d) 283.2 pF

**Answer: (a)**
C = ε₀εᵣA/d = 8.854×10⁻¹²×4×100×10⁻⁴/(5×10⁻³) = 70.8 pF

### Q4. Magnetic Field (Biot-Savart)
The magnetic field at center of circular loop of radius 20cm carrying current 5A is:
(a) 15.7 μT
(b) 31.4 μT
(c) 62.8 μT
(d) 7.85 μT

**Answer: (a)**
B = μ₀I/(2R) = 4π×10⁻⁷×5/(2×0.2) = 15.7 μT

### Q5. Ampere's Law
For infinite wire carrying 10A, the magnetic field at distance 5cm is:
(a) 40 μT
(b) 20 μT
(c) 10 μT
(d) 80 μT

**Answer: (a)**
B = μ₀I/(2πρ) = 4π×10⁻⁷×10/(2π×0.05) = 40 μT

### Q10. Capacitance of Coaxial Cable

### Q11. Hall Effect
A Hall effect sensor has n = 10²⁶ carriers/m³, thickness 1mm, width 5mm, current 10mA, and B = 0.5T. Hall voltage is:
(a) 3.125 mV
(b) 0.3125 mV

**Answer: (a)**

### Q12. Force Between Charges

### Q17. Magnetic Boundary Condition

### Q20. Mutual Inductance
