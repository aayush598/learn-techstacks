# Vector Analysis - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) The divergence of a vector field is:
(A) A vector  (B) A scalar  (C) A matrix  (D) Zero

**Answer: (B) A scalar**

---

### Q2. (Easy) The curl of a vector field is:
(A) A scalar  (B) A vector  (C) Zero always  (D) A scalar product

**Answer: (B) A vector**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) Divergence of F = 3x i + 2y j + z k:
(A) 6  (B) 3  (C) 2  (D) 0

**Solution:** div = d(3x)/dx + d(2y)/dy + d(z)/dz = 3+2+1 = 6
**Answer: (A) 6**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) Curl of a gradient:
(A) Gradient  (B) Always zero  (C) Divergence  (D) Nonzero

**Answer: (B) Always zero (irrotational field)**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) Divergence of a curl:
(A) Zero  (B) Nonzero  (C) Vector  (D) Gradient

**Answer: (A) Always zero (solenoidal)**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) Gradient operator applied to scalar:
(A) Scalar  (B) Vector  (C) Zero  (D) Laplacian

**Answer: (B) Vector (points max rate of change)**

---

### Q7. (Moderate) Laplacian of potential V is:
(A) Gradient of V  (B) Divergence of gradient of V  (C) Curl of V  (D) Scalar V

**Answer: (B) div(grad V) = d2V/dx2 + d2V/dy2 + d2V/dz2**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) Stokes theorem relates:
(A) Surface integral of curl to line integral  (B) Volume to surface  (C) Divergence  (D) None

**Answer: (A) ∮F·dl = ∬(∇×F)·dS (curl to line integral)**

---

### Q9. (Moderate) Divergence theorem (Gauss) relates:
(A) Line to surface  (B) Surface integral of divergence to volume  (C) Curl  (D) Gradient

**Answer: (B) ∲F·dS = ∭(∇·F)dV**

---

### Q10. (Moderate) For divergence-free field:
(A) ∇·F = 0  (B) ∇×F = 0  (C) ∇F = 0  (D) none

**Answer: (A) ∇·F = 0 (solenoidal, no sources/sinks)**
