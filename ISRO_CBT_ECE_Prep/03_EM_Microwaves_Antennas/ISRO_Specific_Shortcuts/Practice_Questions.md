# EM/Microwaves/Antennas - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) Free space intrinsic impedance:
(A) 50 ohm  (B) 75 ohm  (C) 377 ohm  (D) 120 ohm

**Answer: (C) 377 ohm**

---

### Q2. (Easy) Dominant mode in rectangular waveguide:
(A) TE01  (B) TE10  (C) TM10  (D) TE11

**Answer: (B) TE10**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) TE10 cutoff for a = 2 cm waveguide:
(A) 3.75 GHz  (B) 5 GHz  (C) 7.5 GHz  (D) 15 GHz

**Solution:** fc = c/(2a) = 3e8/(2*0.02) = 3e8/0.04 = 7.5e9 = 7.5 GHz
**Answer: (C) 7.5 GHz**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) Impedance to match 50 to 75 ohm via quarter-wave:
(A) 60 ohm  (B) 61.2 ohm  (C) 62.5 ohm  (D) 65 ohm

**Solution:** Zq = sqrt(Z0*ZL) = sqrt(50*75) = sqrt(3750) = 61.2 ohm
**Answer: (B) 61.2 ohm**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) Cable with L=0.2uH/m, C=80pF/m characteristic impedance:
(A) 40 ohm  (B) 50 ohm  (C) 62.5 ohm  (D) 75 ohm

**Solution:** Z0 = sqrt(L/C) = sqrt(0.2e-6/80e-12) = sqrt(2500) = 50 ohm
**Answer: (B) 50 ohm**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) In an isotropic antenna, directivity:
(A) 0  (B) 1  (C) 3  (D) depends on freq

**Answer: (B) 1 (0 dBi)**

---

### Q7. (Moderate) Aerial with G = 3 and lambda = 0.3m effective aperture:
(A) 0.021 m^2  (B) 0.0716 m^2  (C) 0.3 m^2  (D) 1.2 m^2

**Solution:** Ae = lambda^2 G/(4pi) = 0.09*3/12.57 = 0.27/12.57 = 0.0215 m^2
**Answer: (A) 0.021 m^2**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) Half-wave dipole radiation resistance:
(A) 36.5  (B) 73  (C) 50  (D) 300 ohm

**Answer: (B) 73 ohm**

---

### Q9. (Hard) Radar echo from target at R returns after 100 us. Range:
(A) 30 km  (B) 15 km  (C) 7.5 km  (D) 3 km

**Solution:** R = c*T/2 = (3e8 * 100e-6)/2 = 30000/2... = 3e8*1e-4/2 = 15000 m = 15 km
**Answer: (B) 15 km**

---

### Q10. (Moderate) QPSK bandwidth for bit rate 1 Mbps:
(A) 500 kHz  (B) 1 MHz  (C) 2 MHz  (D) 250 kHz

**Solution:** QPSK: BW = Rb = 1 MHz (2 bits/sym, BW = Rb)
**Answer: (B) 1 MHz**
