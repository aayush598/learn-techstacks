# Antenna Fundamentals - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) Directivity of isotropic radiator:
(A) 1 (0 dB)  (B) 2  (C) 0  (D) 1.5

**Answer: (A) 1 (0 dB, radiates equally in all directions)**

---

### Q2. (Moderate) Half-wave dipole directivity:
(A) 1.64 (~2.15 dB)  (B) 1  (C) 3  (D) 100

**Answer: (A) 1.64 (2.15 dB)**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) Gain relates to directivity and efficiency:
(A) G = η * D  (B) G = D/η  (C) G = η+D  (D) G = D

**Answer: (A) G = η * D (efficiency times directivity)**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) Radiation resistance of thin half-wave dipole:
(A) ~73 ohm  (B) 50  (C) 377  (D) 0

**Answer: (A) ~73 ohm (75 ohm approx)**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) Antenna effective aperture Ae:
(A) D λ^2 / 4π  (B) λ/D  (C) D/λ  (D) 4π D λ^2

**Answer: (A) Ae = D λ^2 / 4π**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) Half-power beamwidth (HPBW) of isotropic:
(A) Undefined (no beam)  (B) 360  (C) 90  (D) 180

**Answer: (A) Isotropic has no beam (no directional lobe)**

---

### Q7. (Moderate) Bandwidth of antenna where VSWR <= 2 is freq range where:
(A) Return loss good enough  (B) Always  (C) Never  (D) only DC

**Answer: (A) Frequency range where antenna match (VSWR<=2) acceptable**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) An antenna with length L at freq f; for narrowband operation:
(A) L comparable to wavelength  (B) L << λ  (C) L >> λ  (D) DC

**Answer: (A) Dimension comparable to λ for efficient radiation**

---

### Q9. (Moderate) Antenna efficiency includes:
(A) Radiation + conduction + mismatch losses  (B) Only radiation  (C) Only reflection  (D) None

**Answer: (A) η = Rr/(Rr + Rloss) (radiation vs conduction+loss)**

---

### Q10. (Moderate) Far field (Fraunhofer) distance:
(A) 2D^2/λ  (B) D/λ  (C) λ/2  (D) D/2

**Answer: (A) Far field region starts ~ 2D^2/λ**
