# Impedance Matching - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) Purpose of impedance matching:
(A) Reduce gain  (B) Max power transfer/no reflection  (C) Increase loss  (D) Change freq

**Answer: (B) Maximum power transfer, minimize reflection**

---

### Q2. (Moderate) Stub matching uses:
(A) Series/shunt transmission line sections  (B) Transformers only  (C) Capacitors  (D) Resistive pads

**Answer: (A) Transmission-line (open/short) stubs**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) Quarter-wave transformer matches ZL to Zo when Z1:
(A) sqrt(Zo ZL)  (B) Zo+ZL  (C) Zo/2  (D) ZL/2

**Answer: (A) Z1 = sqrt(Zo * ZL)**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) Single stub matching is possible if distance d such that admittance real part:
(A) = Y0  (B) =0  (C) =2Y0  (D) =1

**Answer: (A) Distance chosen so load admittance real part equals characteristic admittance Y0**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) Matching reduces:
(A) Gain  (B) Reflection coefficient/VSWR  (C) Frequency  (D) Power delivered

**Answer: (B) Reduces reflections (Γ→0, VSWR→1)**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) In quarter-wave transformer, Zin = Zo^2/ZL. If ZL=Zo:
(A) Zin=Zo (matched)  (B) Zin=0  (C) Zin=inf  (D) Zin=2Zo

**Answer: (A) Zin = Zo (ZL=Zo -> transformer has no effect, already matched)**

---

### Q7. (Moderate) Stub is:
(A) A short length of transmission line (open/short term)  (B) A resistor  (C) A diode  (D) A lens

**Answer: (A) Short transmission-line section terminated in open or short**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) Maximum power transfer condition:
(A) ZL = Zo* (conjugate match)  (B) ZL = 2Zo  (C) ZL = Zo/2  (D) ZL=0

**Answer: (A) ZL = conjugate of source impedance (for complex) or ZL=Zo**

---

### Q9. (Moderate) Matching a 50-ohm line to 50-ohm load needs:
(A) Transformer  (B) Nothing (already matched)  (C) Stub  (D) Open

**Answer: (B) Already matched, no matching needed**

---

### Q10. (Moderate) Balanced/unbalanced (balun) transformer is used for:
(A) Coax to balanced load  (B) Only filters  (C) Only gain  (D) Only power

**Answer: (A) Converting balanced line/load to unbalanced (coax)**
