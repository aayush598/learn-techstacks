# Wave Propagation - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) Ground wave propagation is best at:
(A) Low frequencies (long distance along earth)  (B) Very high freq  (C) Optical  (D) Microwaves

**Answer: (A) LF/MF - follows earth surface**

---

### Q2. (Moderate) Sky wave depends on:
(A) Ionosphere reflection  (B) Line of sight  (C) Only ground  (D) Only free space

**Answer: (A) Ionosphere reflection (HF region)**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) VHF/UHF propagation is primarily:
(A) Line-of-sight (space wave)  (B) Ionospheric  (C) Ground  (D) Only sky

**Answer: (A) Line-of-sight/space wave (above ~30 MHz)**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) Sky wave usable mainly at:
(A) High frequency (HF, 3-30 MHz)  (B) VHF  (C) UHF  (D) Optical

**Answer: (A) HF band where ionosphere reflects signals (long distance)**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) Ionization is caused primarily by:
(A) Solar radiation  (B) Rain  (C) Wind  (D) Temperature only

**Answer: (A) Solar (UV/EUV) radiation ionizes upper atmosphere**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) Maximum usable frequency (MUF) relates to:
(A) Critical frequency and skip distance  (B) Only power  (C) Only antenna  (D) Only receiver

**Answer: (A) MUF depends on critical frequency (ionization) and angle of incidence**

---

### Q7. (Moderate) Skip distance is:
(A) Distance between transmitter and first return  (B) Wavelength  (C) Height  (D) Power

**Answer: (A) Distance to first point where refracted wave returns to earth**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) Ducting (tropospheric) propagation:
(A) Trapped in troposphere for long distance  (B) Ionospheric  (C) Ground only  (D) None

**Answer: (A) Tropospheric duct - signals trapped by atmospheric layers (extended VHF/UHF)**

---

### Q9. (Moderate) Fading is:
(A) Variation in received signal strength  (B) Fixed  (C) Faster only  (D) Amplification

**Answer: (A) Time/frequency variation in signal amplitude (multipath etc.)**

---

### Q10. (Moderate) Line-of-sight range approx:
(A) ~sqrt(2 h) miles (h in ft)  (B) Constant  (C) Independent of height  (D) h^2

**Answer: (A) LOS distance ≈ sqrt(2 h) (antenna height dependent)**
