# Subject-Wise Test - Power Electronics

## 20 Questions, 25 Minutes, +1/−0.33

## Questions

**Q1.** An ideal buck converter has `Vin=20 V` and duty ratio `D=0.5`. Its average output voltage is:

A. 5 V  
B. 10 V  
C. 15 V  
D. 20 V

**Q2.** An ideal boost converter has `Vin=12 V` and `D=0.5`. Its output voltage is:

A. 6 V  
B. 12 V  
C. 24 V  
D. 36 V

**Q3.** An ideal inverting buck-boost converter has `Vin=12 V` and `D=1/3`. The output-voltage magnitude is:

A. 3 V  
B. 6 V  
C. 9 V  
D. 12 V

**Q4.** A power stage takes 20 W input and has 50% efficiency. Its output power is:

A. 5 W  
B. 10 W  
C. 20 W  
D. 40 W

**Q5.** Converter ripple factor is defined as:

A. `V_r,rms/V_o`  
B. `V_r,pk/V_in` only  
C. `I_o/I_in`  
D. `f/P`

**Q6.** The average output of an ideal single-phase half-wave rectifier is:

A. `0.318V_m`  
B. `0.450V_m`  
C. `0.637V_m`  
D. `0.900V_m`

**Q7.** The average output of an ideal single-phase full-wave bridge rectifier is:

A. `0.318V_m`  
B. `0.450V_m`  
C. `0.637V_m`  
D. `0.900V_m`

**Q8.** Once an SCR is triggered, it normally remains conducting until:

A. Its anode current falls below holding current  
B. A positive gate pulse is removed  
C. Supply voltage reaches zero only at infinite time  
D. Its gate voltage becomes positive

**Q9.** A gate-turn-off thyristor is designed to be turned off by:

A. A negative gate command  
B. Permanent forward current  
C. Reverse supply only after the next cycle  
D. Increasing forward anode current

**Q10.** A power MOSFET is primarily voltage controlled because its gate is:

A. Insulated by oxide  
B. Directly connected to the source  
C. Mechanically pressed against the drain  
D. A permanent magnetic contact

**Q11.** An IGBT combines:

A. MOSFET gate control with bipolar output conduction capability  
B. SCR gate control with no switching  
C. Mechanical contacts with an optocoupler  
D. Only a diode junction

**Q12.** Reverse-recovery time is important when selecting a diode for:

A. High-frequency switching  
B. Mechanical strength  
C. Optical emission  
D. Static DC only

**Q13.** PWM controls a converter primarily by varying:

A. Switching duty ratio  
B. Input resistance only  
C. Magnetic polarity continuously  
D. Device temperature

**Q14.** Soft-switching converters attempt to reduce switching loss by:

A. Changing voltage or current overlap to zero at switching  
B. Increasing both stress and overlap  
C. Operating without switching  
D. Eliminating all stored energy

**Q15.** The dominant characteristic harmonic in an ideal 6-pulse three-phase rectifier is commonly the:

A. 2nd  
B. 3rd  
C. 5th  
D. 7th only

**Q16.** For a balanced ideal 6-pulse three-phase full-wave bridge, average DC voltage is approximately:

A. `0.45V_LL`  
B. `0.75V_LL`  
C. `1.35V_LL`  
D. `2.34V_LL`

**Q17.** An ideal step-down chopper applies a source `V_s` for duty ratio `D`. Its average output is:

A. `D V_s`  
B. `V_s/D`  
C. `D V_s/2`  
D. `V_s(1−D)`

**Q18.** An ideal step-up chopper average output is approximately:

A. `D V_s`  
B. `V_s/(1−D)`  
C. `(1−D)V_s`  
D. `V_s/D`

**Q19.** A DC-AC inverter produces:

A. Controlled AC from a DC source  
B. Controlled DC from an AC source only  
C. Mechanical motion from electrical power  
D. A steady DC increase without switching

**Q20.** Active power-factor correction is used to:

A. Reduce reactive current and bring input PF near unity  
B. Increase switching loss intentionally  
C. Eliminate all harmonics in every load  
D. Set output voltage to zero
