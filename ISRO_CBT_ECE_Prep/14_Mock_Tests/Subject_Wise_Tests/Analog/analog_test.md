## Questions

**Q1.** An inverting amplifier has `Rf=100 kΩ` and `R1=10 kΩ`. Its ideal voltage gain is:

A. −10  
B. +10  
C. 11  
D. −11

**Q2.** A non-inverting amplifier has `R1=1 kΩ` and `Rf=9 kΩ`. Its gain is:

A. 1  
B. 9  
C. 10  
D. −10

**Q3.** The input resistance of an ideal op-amp is:

B. Infinite

**Q4.** Common-mode rejection ratio measures an op-amp's ability to:

A. 50 kHz  
B. 100 kHz  
C. 200 kHz  
D. 5 MHz

**Q6.** A slew rate of 10 V/μs limits a 2 V-peak sine wave to approximately:

A. 159 kHz

C. 796 kHz

**Q7.** The maximum theoretical efficiency of a complementary Class-B push-pull amplifier is:

A. 25%  
B. 50%

D. 100%

**Q8.** Class-AB biasing is used primarily to reduce:

A. Gain  
B. Crossover distortion

A. 0 V  
B. 4 V  
C. 8 V  
D. 16 V

**Q10.** An RC low-pass filter has `R=10 kΩ` and `C=100 nF`. Its cutoff frequency is:

A. 159 Hz  
B. 1.59 kHz  
C. 15.9 kHz  
D. 159 kHz

**Q11.** A Wien bridge with `R=10 kΩ` and equal capacitors of 10 nF oscillates at:

A. 159 Hz  
B. 1.59 kHz

D. 159 kHz

**Q12.** Negative feedback uses `A=100` and `β=0.2`. The closed-loop gain is:

A. 4.76

A. Reduces gain and increases bandwidth  
B. Increases gain and reduces bandwidth

A. Very large currents directly
