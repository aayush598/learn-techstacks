## Quick Solutions

2. `VSWR=(1+0.5)/(1−0.5)=3`.  
3. `Z0=√(50×200)=100 Ω`.  
4. `fc=c/(2a)=5 GHz`.  
5. Reflected power ratio is `|Γ|²=0.16`.  
8. `Pr=10000/(4π)²≈253 W`.  
10. `Ae=Gλ²/(4π)=0.143 m²`.  
20. `4.12(√h₁+√h₂)` is the standard 4/3-earth approximation with the effective-radius factor absorbed into the coefficient.
