# Subject-Wise Test - Measurements and Transducers

## 20 Questions, 25 Minutes, +1/−0.33

## Questions

**Q1.** An LVDT is primarily used to measure:

A. Temperature  
B. Linear displacement  
C. Radio frequency  
D. Light intensity

**Q2.** The gauge factor of a strain gauge is:

A. `(ΔR/R)/strain`  
B. `strain/(ΔR/R)`  
C. `R×strain`  
D. `ΔR/R` alone

**Q3.** A Wheatstone bridge is balanced when:

A. All four resistors are equal  
B. `R1/R2=R3/R4` for the corresponding ratio convention  
C. Any resistor is open  
D. Supply voltage is zero

**Q4.** A thermocouple generates a voltage from:

A. The Seebeck effect  
B. The Hall effect  
C. The piezoelectric effect only  
D. Capacitive displacement

**Q5.** A metal resistance thermometer normally has:

A. Strongly negative temperature coefficient  
B. Positive temperature coefficient  
C. Infinite resistance at all temperatures  
D. No temperature dependence

**Q6.** An NTC thermistor has resistance that:

A. Increases with temperature  
B. Decreases with temperature  
C. Is constant with temperature  
D. Becomes infinite at absolute zero only

**Q7.** A capacitive displacement transducer changes:

A. Resistance only  
B. Capacitance with plate spacing or area  
C. Magnetic flux only  
D. Coil resistance

**Q8.** An inductive proximity sensor is commonly used to detect:

A. Color  
B. Metallic objects without contact  
C. Ambient humidity only  
D. Optical polarization

**Q9.** A piezoelectric sensor is especially suitable for dynamic measurements because it can detect:

A. Static force indefinitely  
B. Changing force and vibration  
C. Only DC voltage supply  
D. Magnetic resonance

**Q10.** An ultrasonic distance sensor uses echo delay `t` in a medium of speed `v`. The one-way distance is approximately:

A. `vt`  
B. `vt/2`  
C. `v/t`  
D. `2vt`

**Q11.** A Hall-effect current sensor measures magnetic field produced by:

A. Voltage  
B. Current  
C. Light  
D. Temperature directly

**Q12.** A digital tachometer normally measures:

A. Voltage magnitude  
B. Pulse frequency or period  
C. Temperature  
D. Resistance

**Q13.** The vertical sensitivity of a CRO is specified in:

A. Hz/cm  
B. V/div  
C. Ω/div  
D. s/div

**Q14.** The time base of a CRO is specified in:

A. V/div  
B. Time/div  
C. Ω/div  
D. dB/div

**Q15.** A voltmeter has sensitivity 20 kΩ/V and is set to the 10 V range. Its input resistance is:

A. 20 kΩ  
B. 100 kΩ  
C. 200 kΩ  
D. 2 MΩ

**Q16.** An ammeter has 5 mA full-scale current and 50 mV full-scale deflection. The required shunt resistance is:

A. 10 kΩ  
B. 1 kΩ  
C. 100 Ω  
D. 10 Ω

**Q17.** To minimize circuit-voltage error, an ammeter should have:

A. Very low series resistance  
B. Very high series resistance  
C. Zero current capability  
D. Infinite internal resistance

**Q18.** To minimize circuit-current error, a voltmeter should have:

A. Zero input resistance  
B. Very high input resistance  
C. Negative resistance  
D. A shunt in series

**Q19.** A common 3½-digit display range has approximately:

A. 99 counts  
B. 999 counts  
C. 3,999 counts  
D. 9,999 counts

**Q20.** Percentage measurement error is:

A. `(true−measured)/true×100` with consistent sign convention  
B. `true/measured×100` always  
C. The full-scale value only  
D. The number of digits only
