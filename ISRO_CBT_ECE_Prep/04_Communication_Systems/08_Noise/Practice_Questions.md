# Noise Analysis - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) Thermal noise power is:
(A) k T B (independent of R value)  (B) kTB plus R  (C) Dependent on R  (D) Zero

**Answer: (A) P = kTB (depends on T and bandwidth B, not resistance)**

---

### Q2. (Moderate) Noise figure (NF) of ideal noiseless device:
(A) 1 (0 dB)  (B) 0  (C) 0.5  (D) 2

**Answer: (A) NF = 1 (0 dB) ideal - no added noise**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) Noise factor F relates input/output SNR:
(A) F = (SNR_in)/(SNR_out)  (B) F = SNR_out/SNR_in  (C) F = 1/SNR  (D) none

**Answer: (A) F = SNR_in / SNR_out (>=1)**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) Thermal noise voltage across R:
(A) Vn = sqrt(4 k T B R)  (B) sqrt(kTBR)  (C) kTB  (D) kTBR

**Answer: (A) Vn = sqrt(4 k T B R) (rms voltage)**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) White noise has:
(A) Flat (constant) power spectral density across freq  (B) High freq only  (C) Low only  (D) Zero

**Answer: (A) White noise - flat PSD (constant over wide band)**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) Equivalent noise temperature Te relates to F:
(A) Te = (F-1) T0  (B) Te = F T0  (C) Te = T0/F  (D) Te = F

**Answer: (A) Te = (F - 1) * T0 (T0 = 290 K)**

---

### Q7. (Moderate) Noise bandwidth vs 3dB bandwidth (for ideal):
(A) Same/related  (B) Always larger  (C) Smaller  (D) Zero

**Answer: (A) Noise bandwidth ≈ 3dB bandwidth for flat-ish response (related)**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) Increasing receiver gain:
(A) Doesn't improve SNR (noise amplified too)  (B) Big improvement  (C) Reduces noise  (D) No effect ever

**Answer: (A) Gain doesn't improve SNR - amplifies signal AND noise equally**

---

### Q9. (Moderate) Best place to improve system noise is:
(A) Reduce noise of first (front-end) stage  (B) Last stage  (C) Add gain  (D) Reduce BW

**Answer: (A) Improve front-end (LN amplifier) - early stages dominate noise figure**

---

### Q10. (Moderate) SNR improve by reducing:
(A) Temperature and bandwidth  (B) Only gain  (C) Only antenna  (D) None

**Answer: (A) Lower T (cooling) and smaller B reduce noise power -> better SNR**
