# Eye Diagrams and BER - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) Eye diagram is used to evaluate:
(A) Quality of digital signal (ISI, noise, timing)  (B) Analog power  (C) RF gain  (D) Antenna

**Answer: (A) Digital transmission quality - ISI, noise margin, timing jitter**

---

### Q2. (Moderate) Wide-open eye indicates:
(A) Good signal quality (low noise/ISI)  (B) Bad  (C) Distortion  (D) No signal

**Answer: (A) Wide/clear eye = good SNR, low ISI, good timing margin**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) Closed (narrow) eye means:
(A) High noise/ISI (degradation)  (B) Good  (C) Fast  (D) Clear

**Answer: (A) Closed eye = poor quality (high noise/intersymbol interference)**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) Eye height relates to:
(A) Noise margin  (B) Timing margin  (C) Only frequency  (D) Power only

**Answer: (A) Eye (vertical) opening = noise margin**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) Eye width relates to:
(A) Timing margin (jitter tolerance)  (B) Noise  (C) Voltage  (D) Gain

**Answer: (A) Horizontal opening = timing margin / jitter**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) BER for matched-filter binary (P_e):
(A) Q(sqrt(2 Eb/N0)) approx  (B) always 0  (C) =1  (D) SNR only

**Answer: (A) BER = Q(sqrt(2 Eb/N0)) for BPSK (anti-podal)**

---

### Q7. (Moderate) Better BER at same Eb/N0 means:
(A) More efficient modulation  (B) Noisy  (C) Wider  (D) Slower

**Answer: (A) Same Eb/N0 lower BER = better (more energy efficient) scheme**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) To reduce BER, increase:
(A) Eb/N0 (signal energy / noise)  (B) Noise  (C) ISI  (D) Distance

**Answer: (A) Higher Eb/N0 (or coding gain) reduces BER**

---

### Q9. (Moderate) ISI arises from:
(A) Symbol smearing (bandwidth/channel memory)  (B) Only noise  (C) Only power  (D) Only antenna

**Answer: (A) Intersymbol interference - previous symbols interfere due to channel bandwidth**

---

### Q10. (Moderate) Nyquist (ISI-free) pulse spacing for R symbols/s:
(A) T = 1/R, ideal low-pass bandwidth R/2  (B) T arbitrary  (C) T=2R  (D) T=R

**Answer: (A) Zero-ISI at T=1/R with bandwidth >= R/2 (Nyquist criterion)**
