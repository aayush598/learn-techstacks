# SSB and VSB - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) SSB transmission bandwidth:
(A) fm (message freq)  (B) 2fm  (C) 3fm  (D) fm/2

**Answer: (A) SSB bandwidth = fm (single sideband, half of DSB)**

---

### Q2. (Moderate) Main advantage of SSB over DSB:
(A) Half bandwidth, less power  (B) Higher quality  (C) Simpler  (D) Easier demod

**Answer: (A) Bandwidth (W vs 2W) and power saved (no carrier/other sideband)**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) SSB is used mainly in:
(A) Voice/HF radio (bandwidth & power efficient)  (B) TV  (C) FM broadcast  (D) Video

**Answer: (A) Voice communication (HF) - efficient bandwidth/power use**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) VSB is used for:
(A) TV video (partial sideband, simpler filter)  (B) Voice  (C) AM only  (D) Morse

**Answer: (A) TV video transmission (VSB - compromise, allows non-ideal filter)**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) SSB demodulation requires:
(A) Coherent (synchronous) detection  (B) Only envelope  (C) Squarer  (D) No carrier

**Answer: (A) SSB needs coherent/synchronous demodulation (no carrier to envelope detect)**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) SSB-SC power for speech is ___ of DSB-SC:
(A) ~half (no 2nd sideband)  (B) Same  (C) Double  (D) Quarter

**Answer: (A) ~Half (one sideband vs two) - actually depends, approx half for equal**

---

### Q7. (Moderate) Filter method of SSB generation uses:
(A) Sharp bandpass filter after DSB  (B) Only oscillator  (C) Only multiplier  (D) Delay

**Answer: (A) Generate DSB then sharply filter one sideband**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) Phasing method eliminates a sideband using:
(A) 90° phase split (Hilbert)  (B) Filter  (C) Diode  (D) Only carrier

**Answer: (A) Phasing (Hilbert) method - 90° phase shifted paths cancel one sideband**

---

### Q9. (Moderate) SSB carrier ~ speech, sinc-signal; coherent local oscillator must be:
(A) Frequency locked & phase locked  (B) Any freq  (C) Slightly off  (D) None

**Answer: (A) Coherent (both freq & phase locked) for faithful demodulation**

---

### Q10. (Moderate) VSB bandwidth lies between:
(A) W and 2W  (B) W and 0  (C) 2W and 3W  (D) 0 and W/2

**Answer: (A) Between SSB (W) and DSB (2W)**
