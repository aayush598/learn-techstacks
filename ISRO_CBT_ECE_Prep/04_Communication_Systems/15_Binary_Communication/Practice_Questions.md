# Binary Communication - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) BPSK bandwidth for bit rate Rb:
(A) Rb (approx)  (B) 2Rb  (C) Rb/2  (D) 4Rb

**Answer: (A) BPSK main-lobe bandwidth ~ Rb (null-to-null ~2Rb)**

---

### Q2. (Moderate) BPSK uses:
(A) 2 antipodal phases (0, 180)  (B) 2 amplitudes  (C) 4 phases  (D) freq

**Answer: (A) Binary Phase Shift Keying - two antipodal (opposite) phases**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) FSK demodulated by:
(A) Frequency discrimination / envelope  (B) Only phase detector  (C) Only multiplier  (D) filter only

**Answer: (A) Frequency discrimination (or noncoherent envelope)**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) BPSK symbol time T for bit rate R:
(A) T = 1/R  (B) T = R  (C) T = 2R  (D) T = R/2

**Answer: (A) T = 1/R (1 bit per symbol in binary)**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) FSK is less efficient in:
(A) Bandwidth than PSK (wider)  (B) Power  (C) Detection  (D) Nothing

**Answer: (A) FSK requires more bandwidth than PSK for same rate**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) For 16-QAM, bits per symbol:
(A) 4  (B) 2  (C) 8  (D) 16

**Solution:** log2(16) = 4 bits/symbol
**Answer: (A) 4**

---

### Q7. (Moderate) M-PSK bandwidth vs bits per symbol:
(A) Bandwidth reduced (M higher -> narrower)  (B) Wider only  (C) No change  (D) Doubles

**Answer: (A) Higher M -> more bits/symbol -> reduces required bandwidth (at cost of SNR)**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) Coherent detection needs:
(A) Carrier & phase reference  (B) No reference  (C) Only envelope  (D) Only power

**Answer: (A) Coherent - needs carrier phase reference (carrier recovery)**

---

### Q9. (Moderate) Noncoherent (DPSK) avoids:
(A) Carrier phase recovery complexity  (B) All noise  (C) Sync  (D) Timing

**Answer: (A) DPSK - differential, avoids absolute phase carrier recovery (simpler)**

---

### Q10. (Moderate) Bandwidth efficiency (bps/Hz) increases with:
(A) Higher M-ary modulation  (B) Lower M  (C) Noisy  (D) Only FSK

**Answer: (A) Higher M-ary (more bits per symbol per Hz)**
