# Digital Modulation - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) BPSK bandwidth for bit rate 10 kbps:
(A) 5 kHz  (B) 10 kHz  (C) 20 kHz  (D) 40 kHz

**Answer: (C) 20 kHz (BW = 2*Rb)**

---

### Q2. (Easy) QPSK bits per symbol:
(A) 1  (B) 2  (C) 3  (D) 4

**Answer: (B) 2**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) For QPSK with symbol rate Rs, bit rate:
(A) Rs  (B) 2Rs  (C) Rs/2  (D) 4Rs

**Answer: (B) 2Rs**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) Which has better BER performance at same Eb/N0?
(A) BPSK  (B) QPSK  (C) 16-QAM  (D) 64-QAM

**Answer: (A) BPSK (best BER, lowest order, highest Eb per bit)**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) 16-QAM carries how many bits per symbol?
(A) 2  (B) 3  (C) 4  (D) 8

**Answer: (C) 4 (16 = 2^4)**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) Noncoherent FSK error probability is:
(A) Q(sqrt(2Eb/N0))  (B) 0.5*exp(-Eb/(2N0))  (C) Q(sqrt(Eb/N0))  (D) 0.5*exp(-Eb/N0)

**Answer: (B) 0.5*exp(-Eb/(2N0))**

---

### Q7. (Hard) Spectral efficiency of 64-QAM (ideal Nyquist):
(A) 2 bps/Hz  (B) 4 bps/Hz  (C) 6 bps/Hz  (D) 8 bps/Hz

**Answer: (C) 6 bps/Hz (log2(64) = 6)**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) Gray coding in QAM is used to:
(A) Increase bandwidth  (B) Ensure adjacent symbols differ by 1 bit  (C) Reduce power  (D) Add redundancy

**Answer: (B) Adjacent symbols differ by 1 bit (reduces BER)**

---

### Q9. (Hard) Bandwidth of 8-PSK at bit rate Rb:
(A) 2*Rb  (B) Rb  (C) 2*Rb/3  (D) Rb/3

**Answer: (C) 2*Rb/3 (BW = 2*Rb/log2(8) = 2Rb/3)**
