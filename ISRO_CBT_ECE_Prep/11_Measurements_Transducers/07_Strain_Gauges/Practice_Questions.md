# Strain Gauges - Practice Questions

## Q1. (ISRO Pattern)
A strain gauge has a gauge factor of 2.0 and nominal resistance of 350Ω. If the strain is 500 με, the change in resistance is:
- (A) 0.35 Ω
- (B) 0.70 Ω
- (C) 1.75 Ω
- (D) 0.175 Ω
**Answer: (A)** ΔR = R × GF × ε = 350 × 2 × 500×10⁻⁶ = 0.35 Ω

## Q2.
The gauge factor of a strain gauge is defined as:
- (A) ΔR/ε
- (B) (ΔR/R)/ε
- (C) R/(ΔR × ε)
- (D) ε/(ΔR/R)
**Answer: (B)**

## Q3.
In a quarter bridge strain gauge circuit, if V_s = 5V, GF = 2, and ε = 1000 με, the output voltage is:
- (A) 2.5 mV
- (B) 5 mV
- (C) 10 mV
- (D) 1.25 mV
**Answer: (A)** V_out = 5/4 × 2 × 10⁻³ = 2.5 mV

## Q4.
The purpose of a dummy gauge in strain measurement is:
- (A) Increase sensitivity
- (B) Temperature compensation
- (C) Increase gauge factor
- (D) Reduce bridge resistance
**Answer: (B)**

## Q5.
A full bridge strain gauge circuit has how many active gauges?
- (A) 1
- (B) 2
- (C) 3
- (D) 4
**Answer: (D)**

## Q6.
The gauge factor of a metallic strain gauge is approximately:
- (A) 100
- (B) 2
- (C) 50
- (D) 10
**Answer: (B)**

## Q7.
A strain gauge with GF = 2.1, R = 120Ω shows ΔR = 0.252Ω. The strain is:
- (A) 1000 με
- (B) 100 με
- (C) 500 με
- (D) 2000 με
**Answer: (A)** ε = ΔR/(R×GF) = 0.252/(120×2.1) = 1000 με

## Q8.
Temperature compensation in a half bridge is achieved because:
- (A) Two gauges have different temperature coefficients
- (B) Both gauges experience same temperature but different strains
- (C) Temperature effects add up
- (D) Bridge is balanced at all temperatures
**Answer: (B)**

## Q9.
For a half bridge, the output sensitivity is:
- (A) Same as quarter bridge
- (B) Half of quarter bridge
- (C) Double the quarter bridge
- (D) Four times quarter bridge
**Answer: (C)**

## Q10.
The typical nominal resistance of a strain gauge is:
- (A) 50 Ω
- (B) 120 Ω or 350 Ω
- (C) 1000 Ω
- (D) 10 Ω
**Answer: (B)**

## Q11.
Strain is defined as:
- (A) Force per unit area
- (B) Change in length per unit length
- (C) Change in resistance per unit resistance
- (D) Voltage per unit strain
**Answer: (B)**

## Q12.
A semiconductor strain gauge has GF of about:
- (A) 2
- (B) 10
- (C) 100-200
- (D) 0.5
**Answer: (C)**

## Q13.
In a full bridge with V_s = 10V, GF = 2, ε = 500 με, output is:
- (A) 10 mV
- (B) 5 mV
- (C) 2.5 mV
- (D) 20 mV
**Answer: (A)** V_out = 10 × 2 × 500×10⁻⁶ = 10 mV

## Q14.
The gauge factor depends on:
- (A) Only dimensional change
- (B) Only piezoresistive effect
- (C) Both dimensional change and piezoresistive effect
- (D) Neither
**Answer: (C)** GF = 1 + 2ν + (Δρ/ρ)/ε

## Q15.
The specimen's CTE mismatch with gauge causes:
- (A) Increased sensitivity
- (B) Apparent strain
- (C) Zero error only
- (D) Non-linearity
**Answer: (B)**

---
**ISRO Tip:** Gauge factor (GF = (ΔR/R)/ε) and quarter bridge output (V_out = V_s/4 × GF × ε) are the most tested strain gauge formulas.
