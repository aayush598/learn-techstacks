# LVDT - Practice Questions

## Q1. (ISRO Pattern)
An LVDT has a sensitivity of 50 mV/mm. For a displacement of 2mm, the output voltage is:
- (A) 100 mV
- (B) 25 mV
- (C) 200 mV
- (D) 50 mV
**Answer: (A)** V = 50 mV/mm × 2mm = 100 mV

## Q2.
At the null position of LVDT, the output voltage is:
- (A) Maximum
- (B) Minimum but not zero
- (C) Zero
- (D) Half of maximum
**Answer: (C)**

## Q3.
The LVDT output voltage phase reverses when:
- (A) Excitation frequency changes
- (B) Core crosses the null position
- (C) Temperature changes
- (D) Load changes
**Answer: (B)**

## Q4.
LVDT is based on the principle of:
- (A) Self-inductance
- (B) Mutual inductance
- (C) Capacitance variation
- (D) Piezoelectric effect
**Answer: (B)**

## Q5.
An LVDT with sensitivity 80 mV/mm/V has 5V excitation. For 3mm displacement, output:
- (A) 1.2V
- (B) 240 mV
- (C) 400 mV
- (D) 1200 mV
**Answer: (D)** S = 80 × 5 = 400 mV/mm; V = 400 × 3 = 1200 mV = 1.2V

## Q6.
The secondary windings of LVDT are connected in:
- (A) Series-aiding
- (B) Series-opposition
- (C) Parallel
- (D) Independent
**Answer: (B)**

## Q7.
Resolution of LVDT is theoretically:
- (A) Limited to 1 μm
- (B) Limited to 0.1 mm
- (C) Infinite
- (D) Limited by core size
**Answer: (C)**

## Q8.
Which component in LVDT signal conditioning preserves direction information?
- (A) Low-pass filter
- (B) Amplifier
- (C) Phase-sensitive demodulator
- (D) RMS converter
**Answer: (C)**

## Q9.
The typical linearity of a good LVDT is:
- (A) ±5%
- (B) ±1%
- (C) ±0.25%
- (D) ±0.01%
**Answer: (C)**

## Q10.
LVDT output at null position is not exactly zero due to:
- (A) Core weight
- (B) Winding asymmetry and stray capacitances
- (C) Low excitation voltage
- (D) High frequency operation
**Answer: (B)**

## Q11.
Increasing excitation voltage of LVDT:
- (A) Decreases sensitivity
- (B) Increases sensitivity
- (C) Has no effect on sensitivity
- (D) Reduces linearity
**Answer: (B)**

## Q12.
The core material of LVDT is typically:
- (A) Copper
- (B) Aluminum
- (C) Soft iron or nickel-iron
- (D) Ferrite
**Answer: (C)**

## Q13.
LVDT is classified as a:
- (A) Passive transducer
- (B) Active transducer
- (C) Digital transducer
- (D) Temperature transducer
**Answer: (A)** Requires external excitation.

## Q14.
The typical excitation frequency for LVDT is:
- (A) 50 Hz
- (B) 1-10 kHz
- (C) 1 MHz
- (D) DC
**Answer: (B)**

## Q15.
An LVDT has full-scale output of 5V for ±10mm stroke. Its sensitivity is:
- (A) 500 mV/mm
- (B) 250 mV/mm
- (C) 50 mV/mm
- (D) 25 mV/mm
**Answer: (B)** S = 5V / 20mm = 250 mV/mm (considering total stroke of 20mm)

---
**ISRO Tip:** LVDT sensitivity (mV/mm), null position, and phase reversal for direction are the most tested concepts.
