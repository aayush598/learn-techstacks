# CRO and Oscilloscopes - Practice Questions

## Q1. (ISRO Pattern)
The rise time of a CRO with 100 MHz bandwidth is:
- (A) 3.5 ns
- (B) 35 ns
- (C) 0.35 ns
- (D) 350 ns
**Answer: (A)** t_r = 0.35/BW = 0.35/100MHz = 3.5 ns

## Q2.
In Lissajous pattern, 3 horizontal tangencies and 2 vertical tangencies are observed. If fx = 1 kHz, fy is:
- (A) 1.5 kHz
- (B) 667 Hz
- (C) 2 kHz
- (D) 3 kHz
**Answer: (A)** fy/fx = 3/2 = 1.5 -> fy = 1.5 kHz

## Q3.
A Lissajous pattern appears as a circle when the phase difference is:
- (A) 0 degrees
- (B) 45 degrees
- (C) 90 degrees
- (D) 180 degrees
**Answer: (C)**

## Q4.
In dual trace CRO, CHOP mode is preferred for:
- (A) High frequency signals
- (B) Low frequency signals
- (C) DC signals only
- (D) Digital signals
**Answer: (B)** CHOP mode is better for low frequency to avoid flicker.

## Q5.
The time base of a CRO uses which waveform?
- (A) Sine wave
- (B) Square wave
- (C) Sawtooth (ramp)
- (D) Triangular
**Answer: (C)**

## Q6.
A CRO has 10 MHz bandwidth. What is its rise time?
- (A) 35 ns
- (B) 3.5 ns
- (C) 350 ns
- (D) 0.35 ns
**Answer: (A)** t_r = 0.35/10MHz = 35 ns

## Q7.
The input impedance of a typical CRO is:
- (A) 50 ohm
- (B) 1 MOhm in parallel with 15-25 pF
- (C) 10 MOhm
- (D) 100 kohm
**Answer: (B)**

## Q8.
For accurate frequency measurement using Lissajous patterns, we need:
- (A) Known Y frequency only
- (B) Known X frequency only
- (C) Both frequencies known
- (D) Neither frequency known
**Answer: (B)** Apply known reference to X, unknown to Y.

## Q9.
A CRO displays 4 complete cycles of a waveform on 10 horizontal divisions with Time/div = 1 ms. The signal frequency is:
- (A) 400 Hz
- (B) 250 Hz
- (C) 4 kHz
- (D) 2.5 kHz
**Answer: (A)** T = 10 x 1ms / 4 = 2.5ms; f = 1/2.5ms = 400 Hz

## Q10.
The trigger function in CRO is used for:
- (A) Amplifying the signal
- (B) Synchronizing sweep with input signal
- (C) Measuring frequency
- (D) Changing vertical sensitivity
**Answer: (B)**

## Q11.
To display a 20 MHz sine wave accurately, the CRO bandwidth should be at least:
- (A) 20 MHz
- (B) 40 MHz
- (C) 60 MHz
- (D) 10 MHz
**Answer: (C)** BW >= 3 x f_max = 60 MHz

## Q12.
A sampling oscilloscope is used for:
- (A) Low frequency signals
- (B) Single-shot events
- (C) High frequency repetitive signals
- (D) DC measurements
**Answer: (C)**

## Q13.
The Lissajous pattern is a straight line at 45 degrees when phase is:
- (A) 0 degrees
- (B) 90 degrees
- (C) 45 degrees
- (D) 180 degrees
**Answer: (A)** Line at any angle means 0 or 180 degrees.

## Q14.
ALT mode in dual trace CRO is best for:
- (A) Low frequency signals
- (B) High frequency signals
- (C) DC signals
- (D) Noise measurements
**Answer: (B)**

## Q15.
The phosphor coating on CRT screen:
- (A) Produces electrons
- (B) Converts electron energy to visible light
- (C) Deflects the beam
- (D) Accelerates electrons
**Answer: (B)**

---
**ISRO Tip:** Rise time (tr = 0.35/BW) and Lissajous frequency ratio (fy/fx = Nh/Nv) are the most tested CRO formulas.
