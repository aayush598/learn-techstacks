# PMMC Instruments - Practice Questions

## Q1. (ISRO Pattern)
A PMMC instrument has R_m = 50Ω and I_m = 1mA. To convert it to a 10A ammeter, the shunt resistance required is:
- (A) 0.005 Ω
- (B) 0.05 Ω
- (C) 0.5 Ω
- (D) 5 Ω
**Answer: (A)** R_sh = R_m×I_m/(I-I_m) = 50×0.001/(10-0.001) = 0.05/9.999 ≈ 0.005 Ω

## Q2.
A PMMC has I_m = 50μA and R_m = 2kΩ. To make a 10V voltmeter, the series resistance needed is:
- (A) 198 kΩ
- (B) 200 kΩ
- (C) 180 kΩ
- (D) 20 kΩ
**Answer: (A)** R_s = V/I_m - R_m = 10/50μ - 2000 = 200000 - 2000 = 198 kΩ

## Q3.
The scale of a PMMC instrument is:
- (A) Non-linear
- (B) Linear
- (C) Logarithmic
- (D) Exponential
**Answer: (B)** Due to radial magnetic field.

## Q4.
PMMC instruments use which type of damping?
- (A) Air friction
- (B) Fluid friction
- (C) Eddy current
- (D) Electromagnetic
**Answer: (C)**

## Q5.
The deflecting torque in a PMMC is given by:
- (A) T_d = NBIA
- (B) T_d = NBIA²
- (C) T_d = NB²IA
- (D) T_d = N²BIA
**Answer: (A)**

## Q6.
A PMMC has sensitivity of 20 kΩ/V. On the 5V range, its internal resistance is:
- (A) 20 kΩ
- (B) 4 kΩ
- (C) 100 kΩ
- (D) 200 kΩ
**Answer: (C)** R_v = S × V = 20k × 5 = 100 kΩ

## Q7.
The controlling torque in PMMC is provided by:
- (A) Permanent magnet
- (B) Spring
- (C) Aluminum former
- (D) Iron core
**Answer: (B)**

## Q8.
If the current through a PMMC is doubled, the deflection:
- (A) Doubles
- (B) Quadruples
- (C) Remains same
- (D) Halves
**Answer: (A)** θ ∝ I

## Q9.
For a PMMC ammeter, the shunt resistance should be:
- (A) High
- (B) Equal to R_m
- (C) Low
- (D) Infinite
**Answer: (C)**

## Q10.
The purpose of the soft iron core in PMMC is:
- (A) Increase field strength
- (B) Make field radial and uniform
- (C) Provide damping
- (D) Support the coil
**Answer: (B)**

## Q11.
A 100μA, 1kΩ PMMC movement is converted to 1mA ammeter. Shunt resistance:
- (A) 111.1 Ω
- (B) 100 Ω
- (C) 10 Ω
- (D) 1000 Ω
**Answer: (A)** R_sh = 1000×0.1/(1-0.1) = 100/0.9 = 111.1 Ω

## Q12.
The controlling torque of PMMC varies with deflection as:
- (A) θ²
- (B) θ
- (C) 1/θ
- (D) Constant
**Answer: (B)** T_c = kθ

## Q13.
In PMMC, the aluminum former serves the purpose of:
- (A) Increasing magnetic field
- (B) Eddy current damping
- (C) Supporting the coil
- (D) Providing controlling torque
**Answer: (B)**

## Q14.
A PMMC with I_m = 1mA is to measure up to 100V. Multiplier resistance:
- (A) 99 kΩ
- (B) 100 kΩ
- (C) 10 kΩ
- (D) 1 MΩ
**Answer: (A)** R_s = 100/0.001 - R_m ≈ 100k - R_m ≈ 99kΩ (assuming R_m negligible)

## Q15.
PMMC instrument cannot measure AC directly because:
- (A) Springs cannot handle AC
- (B) Average torque for AC is zero
- (C) Eddy current damping fails
- (D) Magnet loses strength
**Answer: (B)** Average value of sinusoidal AC is zero.

---
**ISRO Tip:** Shunt (R_sh = R_m/(n-1)) and multiplier (R_s = R_m(V/V_m - 1)) are the most tested PMMC formulas.
