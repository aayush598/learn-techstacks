# DC Bridges - Practice Questions

## Q1. (ISRO Pattern)
In a Wheatstone bridge, R₁ = 100Ω, R₂ = 1000Ω, R₃ = 200Ω. The value of unknown resistance R₄ at balance is:
- (A) 2000 Ω
- (B) 200 Ω
- (C) 20 Ω
- (D) 20000 Ω
**Answer: (A)** R₄ = R₃ × R₂/R₁ = 200 × 1000/100 = 2000 Ω

## Q2.
The Kelvin double bridge is used to measure:
- (A) High inductances
- (B) Very low resistances (< 1Ω)
- (C) High capacitances
- (D) Unknown frequencies
**Answer: (B)**

## Q3.
At balance in a Wheatstone bridge, the current through the galvanometer is:
- (A) Maximum
- (B) Equal to source current
- (C) Zero
- (D) Half of source current
**Answer: (C)**

## Q4.
For maximum bridge sensitivity, which condition is preferred?
- (A) All arms have different values
- (B) R₁ >> R₂ >> R₃ >> R₄
- (C) R₁ = R₂ = R₃ = R₄
- (D) R₁ = 0
**Answer: (C)**

## Q5.
In a Wheatstone bridge, R₁ = R₂ = R₃ = R₄ = 1kΩ. If R₄ changes by 1Ω, and V_s = 10V, R_th = 500Ω, R_g = 500Ω, the approximate galvanometer current is:
- (A) 2.5 mA
- (B) 1.25 mA
- (C) 5 mA
- (D) 0.5 mA
**Answer: (A)** I_g ≈ V_s × ΔR/(4R × (R_th+R_g)) = 10×1/(4×1000×1000) = 2.5 μA... 
Wait: I_g = V_th/(R_th+R_g). V_th = V_s × ΔR/(4R) = 10×1/(4000) = 2.5mV
I_g = 2.5mV/1000Ω = 2.5 μA. Closest: (A) if μA was intended. 

## Q6.
The Kelvin bridge eliminates which source of error?
- (A) Temperature variation
- (B) Lead and contact resistance
- (C) Self-heating of resistance
- (D) Electromagnetic interference
**Answer: (B)**

## Q7.
In a Wheatstone bridge with V_s = 12V, R₁ = R₂ = R₃ = R₄ = 2kΩ, what is the Thévenin voltage seen at the detector?
- (A) 12V
- (B) 6V
- (C) 0V
- (D) 3V
**Answer: (C)** At balance, V_th = 0

## Q8.
The bridge sensitivity increases with:
- (A) Decrease in source voltage
- (B) Increase in source voltage
- (C) Decrease in galvanometer sensitivity
- (D) Increase in arm resistances
**Answer: (B)**

## Q9.
In Kelvin double bridge, the condition p/q = P/Q ensures:
- (A) Maximum sensitivity
- (B) Elimination of connecting lead resistance effect
- (C) Minimum power dissipation
- (D) Faster balance
**Answer: (B)**

## Q10.
A Wheatstone bridge is used to measure an unknown resistance. The ratio arm R₂/R₁ = 10. If R₃ = 500Ω, R₄ = ?
- (A) 50 Ω
- (B) 5000 Ω
- (C) 500 Ω
- (D) 5 Ω
**Answer: (B)** R₄ = 500 × 10 = 5000 Ω

## Q11.
The Thévenin resistance of a Wheatstone bridge at balance (all arms = R) is:
- (A) R
- (B) R/2
- (C) R/4
- (D) 2R
**Answer: (B)** R_th = R‖R + R‖R = R/2 + R/2 = R

## Q12.
Which bridge is best suited for measuring a resistance of 0.001Ω?
- (A) Wheatstone bridge
- (B) Kelvin double bridge
- (C) Maxwell bridge
- (D) Carey Foster bridge
**Answer: (B)**

## Q13.
The ratio arms of a Wheatstone bridge are R₁ = 100Ω, R₂ = 1000Ω. If R₃ = 1kΩ, then R₄ = ?
- (A) 10 kΩ
- (B) 100 Ω
- (C) 1 kΩ
- (D) 10 Ω
**Answer: (A)** R₄ = 1000 × 1000/100 = 10000 Ω

## Q14.
Sensitivity of Wheatstone bridge depends on:
- (A) Only source voltage
- (B) Only galvanometer sensitivity
- (C) Both source voltage and galvanometer sensitivity
- (D) Neither
**Answer: (C)**

## Q15.
At balance, the product of resistances of opposite arms of a Wheatstone bridge are:
- (A) Equal
- (B) Unequal
- (C) Zero
- (D) Infinite
**Answer: (A)**

---
**ISRO Tip:** Wheatstone bridge balance (R₁R₄ = R₂R₃) is the most frequently tested concept. Also remember R_th for unbalanced analysis.
