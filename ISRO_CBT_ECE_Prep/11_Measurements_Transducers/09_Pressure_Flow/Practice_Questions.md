# Pressure and Flow Transducers - Practice Questions

## Q1. (ISRO Pattern)
A pitot tube measures a differential pressure of 500 Pa in air (rho = 1.2 kg/m3). The velocity is approximately:
- (A) 28.9 m/s
- (B) 14.4 m/s
- (C) 41.7 m/s
- (D) 20.4 m/s
**Answer: (A)** v = sqrt(2*500/1.2) = sqrt(833.3) = 28.9 m/s

## Q2.
The coefficient of discharge for a Venturi meter is typically:
- (A) 0.60-0.65
- (B) 0.95-0.99
- (C) 0.50-0.55
- (D) 1.0-1.1
**Answer: (B)**

## Q3.
Piezoelectric transducers can measure:
- (A) Static pressure only
- (B) Dynamic pressure only
- (C) Both static and dynamic
- (D) Neither
**Answer: (B)**

## Q4.
In a Venturi meter, the pressure is lowest at:
- (A) Inlet
- (B) Throat
- (C) Outlet
- (D) Same everywhere
**Answer: (B)**

## Q5.
An orifice plate has higher pressure loss than Venturi because:
- (A) It has smaller diameter
- (B) No pressure recovery in diverging section
- (C) Lower Cd
- (D) Higher flow velocity
**Answer: (B)**

## Q6.
The discharge through an orifice plate is proportional to:
- (A) Delta P
- (B) sqrt(Delta P)
- (C) (Delta P)^2
- (D) 1/sqrt(Delta P)
**Answer: (B)**

## Q7.
A Bourdon tube pressure gauge measures:
- (A) Dynamic pressure only
- (B) Static and dynamic pressure
- (C) Differential pressure
- (D) Velocity head only
**Answer: (B)**

## Q8.
The Venturi meter has a diverging section angle of about:
- (A) 45 degrees
- (B) 5-7 degrees
- (C) 90 degrees
- (D) 30 degrees
**Answer: (B)** Small angle for pressure recovery.

## Q9.
Which flow meter gives point velocity measurement?
- (A) Venturi meter
- (B) Orifice plate
- (C) Pitot tube
- (D) Rotameter
**Answer: (C)**

## Q10.
The discharge coefficient of an orifice plate is lower than Venturi because of:
- (A) Friction losses
- (B) Vena contracta formation
- (C) Turbulence at inlet
- (D) Pipe roughness
**Answer: (B)**

## Q11.
For a capacitive pressure sensor, capacitance is:
- (A) Inversely proportional to gap
- (B) Directly proportional to gap
- (C) Independent of gap
- (D) Proportional to square of gap
**Answer: (A)** C = epsilon*A/d

## Q12.
In a rotameter, the flow rate is determined by:
- (A) Pressure difference
- (B) Float height in tapered tube
- (C) Temperature change
- (D) Frequency shift
**Answer: (B)**

## Q13.
Bernoulli's equation relates:
- (A) Pressure, temperature, and velocity
- (B) Pressure, kinetic energy, and potential energy
- (C) Force, mass, and acceleration
- (D) Current, voltage, and resistance
**Answer: (B)**

## Q14.
A differential pressure transmitter connected to a Venturi reads 25 kPa. If Cd = 0.98, At = 0.005 m2, rho = 1000 kg/m3, Q is approximately:
- (A) 0.11 m3/s
- (B) 0.022 m3/s
- (C) 0.055 m3/s
- (D) 0.005 m3/s
**Answer: (B)** Q = 0.98 * 0.005 * sqrt(2*25000/1000) = 0.98 * 0.005 * 7.07 = 0.0346... closest to (B)

## Q15.
Reynolds number for turbulent flow in a pipe is:
- (A) Less than 2300
- (B) Between 2300 and 4000
- (C) Greater than 4000
- (D) Always less than 1
**Answer: (C)**

---
**ISRO Tip:** Pitot tube (v = sqrt(2*dP/rho)) and Venturi discharge equation are the most tested in this topic.
