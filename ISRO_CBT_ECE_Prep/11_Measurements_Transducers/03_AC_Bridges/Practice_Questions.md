# AC Bridges - Practice Questions

## Q1. (ISRO Pattern)
In a Maxwell bridge, R₂ = 1kΩ, R₃ = 1kΩ, C₄ = 0.01μF. The unknown inductance Lₓ at balance is:
- (A) 10 mH
- (B) 100 mH
- (C) 1 mH
- (D) 100 μH
**Answer: (A)** Lₓ = R₂R₃C₄ = 1000 × 1000 × 0.01×10⁻⁶ = 10 mH

## Q2.
Which AC bridge is most suitable for measuring the dissipation factor of a capacitor?
- (A) Maxwell bridge
- (B) Wien bridge
- (C) Schering bridge
- (D) Anderson bridge
**Answer: (C)**

## Q3.
The Wien bridge is commonly used for:
- (A) Inductance measurement
- (B) Frequency measurement
- (C) Resistance measurement
- (D) Current measurement
**Answer: (B)**

## Q4.
In a Schering bridge at balance: Cₓ = 100pF, R₃ = 1kΩ, R₄ = 10kΩ, C₂ = ?
- (A) 100 pF
- (B) 1000 pF
- (C) 10 pF
- (D) 10000 pF
**Answer: (B)** C₂ = CₓR₃/R₄ = 100×1000/10000 = 10 pF... Wait: Cₓ = C₂(R₄/R₃) → C₂ = CₓR₃/R₄ = 100×1k/10k = 10pF. Answer: (C)

## Q5.
The Hay bridge is preferred over Maxwell bridge when:
- (A) Q is very low
- (B) Q is very high
- (C) Inductance is very small
- (D) Frequency is very low
**Answer: (B)** Hay is better for high-Q coils (Q > 10).

## Q6.
At what frequency does a Wien bridge with R = 10kΩ and C = 0.01μF oscillate?
- (A) 1591.5 Hz
- (B) 159.15 Hz
- (C) 15.915 kHz
- (D) 1.5915 kHz
**Answer: (A)** f = 1/(2πRC) = 1/(2π×10000×10⁻⁸) = 1591.5 Hz

## Q7.
In a Maxwell bridge, the quality factor of the coil is given by:
- (A) Q = ωR₄C₄
- (B) Q = ω/(R₄C₄)
- (C) Q = 1/(ωR₄C₄)
- (D) Q = R₄C₄/ω
**Answer: (A)**

## Q8.
The general AC bridge balance condition is:
- (A) Z₁ + Z₄ = Z₂ + Z₃
- (B) Z₁Z₂ = Z₃Z₄
- (C) Z₁Z₄ = Z₂Z₃
- (D) Z₁/Z₂ = Z₄/Z₃
**Answer: (C)**

## Q9.
The dissipation factor of a capacitor measured by Schering bridge is:
- (A) D = ωC₄R₄
- (B) D = 1/(ωC₄R₄)
- (C) D = ωR₄/C₄
- (D) D = ω²C₄R₄
**Answer: (A)**

## Q10.
For a Wien bridge oscillator with R = 10kΩ, C = 0.001μF, the oscillation frequency is:
- (A) 15.9 kHz
- (B) 1.59 kHz
- (C) 159 kHz
- (D) 15.9 Hz
**Answer: (A)** f = 1/(2π×10⁴×10⁻⁹) = 15915 Hz ≈ 15.9 kHz

## Q11.
Which bridge requires frequency to be known accurately?
- (A) Maxwell bridge
- (B) Anderson bridge
- (C) Hay bridge
- (D) All of these
**Answer: (C)** Hay bridge equations contain ω explicitly.

## Q12.
In a Maxwell bridge, if R₂ and R₃ are doubled while C₄ is halved, Lₓ becomes:
- (A) Double
- (B) Half
- (C) Same
- (D) Quadruple
**Answer: (C)** Lₓ = R₂R₃C₄ = (2R₂)(2R₃)(C₄/2) = 2R₂R₃C₄... 
Wait: (2R₂)(2R₃)(C₄/2) = 2R₂R₃C₄. So doubled. Answer: (A)

## Q13.
The Wien bridge oscillation condition requires the amplifier gain to be:
- (A) 1
- (B) 2
- (C) 3
- (D) 10
**Answer: (C)** A ≥ 3 for oscillation.

## Q14.
In a Schering bridge, the dissipation factor D = 0.01 at ω = 10⁴ rad/s, R₄ = 10kΩ. Find C₄:
- (A) 100 pF
- (B) 1000 pF
- (C) 10 pF
- (D) 1 nF
**Answer: (A)** C₄ = D/(ωR₄) = 0.01/(10⁴×10⁴) = 100 pF

## Q15.
Anderson bridge uses how many arms?
- (A) 4
- (B) 5
- (C) 6
- (D) 8
**Answer: (C)** Anderson bridge has 6 arms.

---
