# Frequency Response - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) -3dB point corresponds to voltage ratio:
(A) 0.5  (B) 0.707  (C) 1  (D) 0.9

**Answer: (B) 0.707**

---

### Q2. (Moderate) Bandwidth of amp with fL=20Hz, fH=20kHz:
(A) 20 kHz  (B) 20.02 kHz  (C) 19.98 kHz  (D) 400 kHz

**Answer: (C) ~19.98 kHz (fH-fL)**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) Miller capacitance Cm for Cbc=1pF, Av=50:
(A) 1pF  (B) 51pF  (C) 50pF  (D) 500pF

**Solution:** Cm = 1p*(1+50) = 51 pF
**Answer: (B) 51 pF**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) Op-amp GBW=10MHz used at gain 100. Closed-loop BW:
(A) 10 MHz  (B) 1 MHz  (C) 100 kHz  (D) 10 kHz

**Solution:** BW = GBW/Av = 10M/100 = 100 kHz
**Answer: (C) 100 kHz**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) Coupling capacitor causes ___ response:
(A) High-pass  (B) Low-pass  (C) Band-pass  (D) All-pass

**Answer: (A) High-pass (low-freq rolloff)**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) Parasitic capacitance (Miller) causes ___ response:
(A) High-pass  (B) Low-pass (high-freq rolloff)  (C) Notch  (D) DC

**Answer: (B) Low-pass (upper cutoff)**

---

### Q7. (Moderate) Two identical stages each BW=1MHz. Combined BW:
(A) 1 MHz  (B) 0.64 MHz  (C) 2 MHz  (D) 0.5 MHz

**Solution:** BW_total = 1M*sqrt(2^(1/2)-1) = 1M*0.643 = 0.64 MHz
**Answer: (B) 0.64 MHz**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) Cascode is used to improve:
(A) Low-freq  (B) High-freq response (reduce Miller)  (C) Gain flatness  (D) DC

**Answer: (B) High-freq response (reduce Miller cap)**
