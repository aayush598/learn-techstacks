# Active Load and CMOS Amplifiers - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) Active load uses a ___ as load element:
(A) Resistor  (B) Transistor  (C) Capacitor  (D) Inductor

**Answer: (B) Transistor**

---

### Q2. (Moderate) Advantage of active load over resistor load:
(A) Lower gain  (B) High impedance w/o large DC drop  (C) Simpler  (D) Cheaper

**Answer: (B) High output impedance with small DC drop (large swing)**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) CS with active load gain:
(A) -gm(RD)  (B) -gm(ro1||ro2)  (C) -gm/gm2  (D) RL

**Answer: (B) -gm(ro1||ro2)**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) A current mirror sets:
(A) Voltage  (B) Bias current  (C) Capacitance  (D) Resistance

**Answer: (B) Bias current (replicas reference)**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) MOSFET mirror output current ratio is set by:
(A) Vt  (B) W/L ratio  (C) lambda  (D) substrate

**Answer: (B) W/L ratio**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) CMOS inverter biased at VDD/2 gives:
(A) Zero gain  (B) High small-signal gain  (C) Unity gain  (D) Negative gain

**Answer: (B) High small-signal gain (steep transition)**

---

### Q7. (Moderate) Cascode boosts:
(A) Gain bandwidth only  (B) Output resistance  (C) Power  (D) Capacitance

**Answer: (B) Output resistance (gm*ro^2), higher gain & better isolation**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) r_o of MOSFET with ID=1mA, lambda=0.02:
(A) 50 k  (B) 2 k  (C) 500 k  (D) 20 k

**Solution:** r_o = 1/(lambda ID) = 1/(0.02e-3) = 50000 = 50k
**Answer: (A) 50 k**
