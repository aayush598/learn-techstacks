# Diode Circuits - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) Peak voltage Vm=10V. Full-wave bridge Vdc (ideal):
(A) 3.18V  (B) 6.37V  (C) 10V  (D) 20V

**Solution:** Vdc = 2Vm/pi = 20/3.14 = 6.37V
**Answer: (B) 6.37V**

---

### Q2. (Easy) Bridge rectifier PIV with Vm=10V:
(A) 10V  (B) 20V  (C) 5V  (D) 40V

**Answer: (A) 10V (bridge PIV = Vm)**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) Silicon diode forward drop is approx:
(A) 0.3V  (B) 0.7V  (C) 1.2V  (D) 0V

**Answer: (B) 0.7V**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) Half-wave rectifier ripple factor:
(A) 0.48  (B) 0.812  (C) 1.21  (D) 2.0

**Answer: (C) 1.21**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) VT at 300K (kT/q):
(A) 26 mV  (B) 0.7V  (C) 26 V  (D) 1.2V

**Answer: (A) 26 mV**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) Which rectifier has lowest PIV requirement for same output?
(A) Full center-tap  (B) Bridge  (C) Half-wave  (D) Same all

**Solution:** Bridge PIV=Vm, center-tap=2Vm, so bridge lowest among full-wave
**Answer: (B) Bridge**

---

### Q7. (Moderate) A Zener is operated in:
(A) Forward region  (B) Breakdown region  (C) Cutoff  (D) Saturation

**Answer: (B) Breakdown region**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) Clamping circuit is used to:
(A) Change amplitude  (B) Change DC level  (C) Rectify  (D) Filter

**Answer: (B) Change DC level (shift without altering shape)**

---

### Q9. (Moderate) To reduce ripple, use:
(A) Larger capacitor  (B) Smaller capacitor  (C) Larger resistor  (D) A and C

**Answer: (D) Larger capacitor or load resistance (higher RC)**

---

### Q10. (Moderate) Full-wave output frequency is ___ line frequency:
(A) Same as  (B) Twice  (C) Half  (D) Four times

**Answer: (B) Twice (2f for full-wave rectification)**
