# IC Voltage Regulators - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) IC 7805 provides:
(A) -5V  (B) +5V  (C) +12V  (D) -12V

**Answer: (B) +5V (78xx positive fixed, 79xx negative)**

---

### Q2. (Easy) 7812 output:
(A) 5V  (B) 12V  (C) 15V  (D) 24V

**Answer: (B) +12V**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) LM317 output with R2=1k, R1=240 ohm (Iadj negligible):
(A) 5V  (B) 6.46V  (C) 12V  (D) 1.25V

**Solution:** Vout = 1.25(1+1000/240) = 1.25(1+4.17) = 1.25*5.17 = 6.46V
**Answer: (B) ~6.46V**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) Linear regulator with Vin=12V, Vout=5V, I=1A efficiency:
(A) 100%  (B) 41.7%  (C) 5%  (D) 60%

**Solution:** eta = Vout/Vin = 5/12 = 41.7%
**Answer: (B) 41.7%**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) LDO advantage:
(A) High dropout  (B) Low dropout (efficient at low headroom)  (C) High noise  (D) Complex

**Answer: (B) Low dropout - good for battery (Vin only slightly > Vout)**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) Buck converter output with Vin=10V, D=0.5:
(A) 20V  (B) 5V  (C) 10V  (D) 15V

**Solution:** Vout = D*Vin = 0.5*10 = 5V
**Answer: (B) 5V**

---

### Q7. (Moderate) Line regulation measures output change vs:
(A) Load  (B) Input voltage  (C) Temperature  (D) Time

**Answer: (B) Input voltage change (ΔVout/ΔVin)**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) Load regulation measures output change vs:
(A) Input  (B) Load current  (C) Temp  (D) Frequency

**Answer: (B) Load current change**
