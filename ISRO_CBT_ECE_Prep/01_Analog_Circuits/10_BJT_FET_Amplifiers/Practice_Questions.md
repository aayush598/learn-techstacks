# BJT and FET Amplifiers - Practice Questions

---

### Q1. (Easy) CE amplifier voltage gain is:
(A) Positive  (B) Negative (inverting)  (C) Unity  (D) Zero

**Answer: (B) Negative, 180 deg phase**

---

### Q2. (Easy) BJT gm = IC/VT with IC=1mA, VT=26mV:
(A) 0.026 S  (B) 0.0385 S  (C) 26 mS  (D) 38.5 mS

**Solution:** gm = 1e-3/0.026 = 0.0385 S = 38.5 mS
**Answer: (D) 38.5 mS**

---

### Q3. (Moderate) CE Av = -gm*RC, gm=50mS, RC=2k:
(A) -100  (B) -50  (C) -10  (D) -200

**Solution:** -50e-3 * 2000 = -100
**Answer: (A) -100**

---

### Q4. (Moderate) Common collector amplifier has Av:
(A) High  (B) ~1  (C) Negative  (D) Infinite

**Answer: (B) ~1 (emitter follower/buffer)**

---

### Q5. (Moderate) Which amp has highest input impedance?
(A) CE  (B) CB  (C) CC  (D) CG

**Answer: (C) CC (emitter/source follower)**

---

### Q6. (Moderate) MOSFET gm = 2ID/Vov, ID=2mA, Vov=0.2V:
(A) 20 mS  (B) 10 mS  (C) 5 mS  (D) 40 mS

**Solution:** gm = 2*2m/0.2 = 4m/0.2 = 20 mS
**Answer: (A) 20 mS**

---

### Q7. (Moderate) For beta=99, alpha:
(A) 0.99  (B) 0.5  (C) 90  (D) 1.01

**Solution:** alpha = beta/(beta+1) = 99/100 = 0.99
**Answer: (A) 0.99**

---

### Q8. (Moderate) Miller effect capacitor Cm = Cgd(1+Av), if Cgd=1pF, Av=100:
(A) 1pF  (B) 101pF  (C) 100pF  (D) 10100pF

**Solution:** Cm = 1p*(1+100) = 101 pF
**Answer: (B) 101 pF**

---

### Q9. (Moderate) Cascode configuration is used to reduce:
(A) Gain  (B) Miller capacitance  (C) Bandwidth  (D) Power

**Answer: (B) Miller capacitance (improve bandwidth)**

---

### Q10. (Moderate) Q-point (DC bias) stability improved by:
(A) Removing emitter R  (B) Adding emitter resistor  (C) High beta  (D) No bias

**Answer: (B) Adding emitter resistor (self-bias/negative feedback)**
