# NEW MOTOR INSTALLATION — PROCUREMENT

**Folder:** `09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/02-New-Motor-Installation/`

---

## 1. WHY THIS FILE EXISTS

The "contacts" member buys a motor from his cousin at a higher price with no
warranty, and the colony discovers the difference only when the motor fails.
Procurement is open, compared, and recorded — the purchasing power of all
members, used honestly.

## 2. BRAND AND MODEL APPROVED BY THE ASSEMBLY

1. The brand and model are approved by the assembly **after** the sizing file
   (`01-Motor-Sizing.md`) is presented.
2. The choice weighs: matching the sizing calculation, availability of genuine
   spare parts and service in the town, and the maker's reputation.
3. No member's brand loyalty binds the system. If a member insists on a brand
   for personal reasons, the system buys what the evidence supports.

## 3. BUY FROM AN AUTHORISED DEALER — WARRANTY IS THE POINT

1. The motor is purchased from an **authorised dealer** of the maker. A
   non-authorised shop may be cheaper by a few hundred rupees and cost many
   thousands when the warranty is rejected.
2. The authorised status is verified (dealer certificate, maker's website, or
   a call to the maker's service line) and noted in the file.

## 4. COMPARISON OF TWO OR THREE SHOPS

| Criterion | Shop A | Shop B | Shop C |
|-----------|--------|--------|--------|
| Price of the approved model | | | |
| Warranty period and terms | | | |
| After-sales service response | | | |
| Delivery time | | | |
| Authorised dealer? | | | |

The comparison table is filed and published. The cheapest authorised-dealer
price with the best warranty terms normally wins; a price advantage that
sacrifices warranty is not an advantage.

## 5. DOCUMENTS TO KEEP — FROM THE DAY OF PURCHASE

- Invoice (with GST where applicable);
- Warranty card, stamped and dated by the dealer;
- Serial number and model number of the unit actually delivered;
- Delivery note;
- Dealer name and service contact.

All are filed in the project file and entered in the warranty register
(`04-Warranty-Management.md`) and the Asset Register
(`09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/05-Infrastructure-Asset-Register/01-Asset-List.md`).

## 6. NO SINGLE PERSON'S "CONTACTS"

1. Any member may suggest a shop; nobody's friendship decides the purchase.
2. A member who is related to a dealer, or who stands to gain, must disclose
   and withdraw from the decision and from handling the money
   (`06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/`).
3. Procurement is done by two members from the oversight group, and the payment
   is made from the joint account against the invoice with two signatures
   (`06-FINANCE/03-Bookkeeping/02-Expense-Voucher-Rule.md`).

## 7. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

Three shops quote for the approved 1.5 HP model. Shop A is not authorised and
is ₹700 cheaper; Shop B is authorised with a 3-year warranty and same-town
service; Shop C is authorised but slower. The system buys from Shop B. Two years
later, when the motor fails, the authorised warranty is honoured on the spot —
the ₹700 "saving" would have become a ₹9,000 loss.

## 8. PAYMENT

- No advance beyond what the dealer's invoice structure requires; the balance
  is paid on verified delivery of the correct serial-numbered unit.
- Every payment is by bank/cheque/UPI from the joint account, vouchered and
  public.

---

*Procurement is a public comparison on paper. The system buys the best
combination of price, warranty, and service — never a relative's favour.*
