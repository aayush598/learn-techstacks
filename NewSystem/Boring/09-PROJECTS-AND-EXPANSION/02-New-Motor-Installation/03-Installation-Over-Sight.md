# NEW MOTOR INSTALLATION — INSTALLATION OVER-SIGHT

**Folder:** `09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/02-New-Motor-Installation/`

---

## 1. WHY THIS FILE EXISTS

A motor bolted on by one man and switched on the same afternoon can fail in
days — from misalignment, bad wiring, or no earthing. Installation is not a
one-man event; it is supervised, checked against a list, photographed, and
verified before any full payment.

## 2. SUPERVISION

1. Installation is supervised by **two members** (drawn by lot or from the
   project oversight group) together with the system's electrician — or a
   licensed hired electrician if the system has none.
2. No installation proceeds with only the vendor and the vendor's helper
   present.
3. The two members record their names and the date in the installation log;
   the log is public (P6).

## 3. THE INSTALLATION CHECKLIST

Each item is ticked and initialled by both supervisors before the motor starts:

| # | Check |
|---|-------|
| 1 | Motor and pump alignment correct |
| 2 | Cable of the correct size, no joints inside the well |
| 3 | Cable properly clamped to the pipe |
| 4 | Earthing installed and tested |
| 5 | MCB/isolator fitted on the control panel |
| 6 | Control panel connections tight and labelled |
| 7 | Delivery pipe and check valve installed |
| 8 | Dry-run protection / low-level cut-off fitted |
| 9 | All bolts, gaskets, and guards in place |
| 10 | Worksite clean and safe for handover |

An un-ticked box stops the motor from starting. The checklist is signed, dated,
and filed with the project file.

## 4. PHOTO AND VIDEO RECORD

1. Photographs (and a short video, where possible) of the key stages — motor
   lowered, wiring, panel, earthing, start-up — are taken by the supervisors.
2. The photos are posted to the members' group and filed; they are evidence if
   the vendor later claims the installation was never checked.

## 5. PAYMENT ON VERIFIED MILESTONES

1. Payments to the installer follow the contract milestones and are released
   only on written verification by the supervisors
   (`01-New-Boring-Installation/03-Contractor-Selection.md`).
2. The final payment is released only after the motor completes the trial run
   schedule (e.g., 7 days of scheduled running with the run log
   `04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/04-Monitoring-and-Logs/`) without a reportable fault.

## 6. THE TRIAL RUN

| Day | Check |
|-----|-------|
| Day 1 | Motor starts, current drawn within nameplate range |
| Day 2-3 | Normal delivery at the top tap; no abnormal noise/vibration |
| Day 4-7 | Run log clean; panel temperature normal; no trips |

The trial-run sheet is signed by the duty operators and the supervisors and
filed.

## 7. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

The vendor says "just switch it on." The checklist shows no earthing box
ticked. The supervisors stop the work until earthing is installed and tested.
Two weeks later, during a thunderstorm, the motor survives because the
earthing is there — a decision on paper that saved the motor.

## 8. OPERATOR TRAINING

- After handover, the duty roster's next operators are shown the control panel,
  the cut-off, and the stop procedure — every operator already holds basic
  training from `03-ROTATION-DUTY/02-Duty-Training/`. A motor is only as safe
  as the hand that switches it.

---

*Installation is a checklist with two witnesses and a camera — not a promise
from the vendor. Nothing starts until the list is done.*
