# NEW BORING INSTALLATION — NEEDS ASSESSMENT

**Folder:** `09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/01-New-Boring-Installation/`

---

## 1. WHY THIS FILE EXISTS

A borewell is expensive, permanent, and draws a common resource. In the old
system a boring was drilled whenever a well-off member "felt like it," or
because his relative owned a rig. This file makes sure a boring is drilled only
when the records show the colony needs one.

## 2. WHEN A NEW BORING IS JUSTIFIED

| Ground | Meaning | Required evidence |
|--------|---------|-------------------|
| Yield drop | The boring no longer produces the water the system needs | Water-level and yield records over 12 months |
| Motor failure due to low water | The motor keeps running dry or cavitating | Duty logs and fault reports |
| Capacity shortfall from growth | New houses exceed the old boring's capacity | Connection register + consumption data |
| Water quality failure | Contamination that cannot be treated economically | Lab reports |

A single ground is enough if the evidence supports it. A combination strengthens
the case. Without at least one evidenced ground, no assembly may approve a new
boring.

## 3. EVIDENCE, NOT FEELINGS (P5)

The system decides on recorded facts. The following are **evidence**:

- Static and dynamic water-level readings (`04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/04-Monitoring-and-Logs/`);
- Yield/pumping-test logs and motor run logs (`04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/04-Monitoring-and-Logs/`);
- The complaint register, with dates and details;
- Meter/consumption records, if meters exist
  (`06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/03-Consumption-Based-Charges.md`);
- Lab reports on water quality.

The following are **not evidence**: "the water seems less this month," a
member's cousin's opinion, rumours that another colony's boring failed, or a
contractor's sales talk. A member who claims shortage must point to a log entry
or the duty team must verify it on the same day.

## 4. THE SURVEY

1. **Surveyors:** two or more members drawn by lot (not self-appointed), plus
   the current duty/technical member if one exists.
2. **What is measured:** static water level, dynamic level under a continuous
   run, yield over a timed period, a field water sample for testing, condition
   of the casing, and the number of connections now and projected over the next
   three years.
3. **The survey record:** date, time, names of surveyors, instruments used, and
   every reading — written on the same day and published.
4. A survey is not a decision. It produces the facts the assembly needs.

## 5. THE REPORT TO THE ASSEMBLY

1. The survey report, the cost estimate, and a summary of quotations are placed
   on the agenda of the General Assembly.
2. The assembly decides whether the project proceeds — Class C, two-thirds (2/3)
   vote (`09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/00-Project-Overview.md`).
3. The decision and the evidence behind it are recorded in the minutes. If the
   facts later change (water returns, a repair suffices), any member may reopen
   the decision with new evidence.

## 6. TIMELINES

| Step | Deadline |
|------|----------|
| Complaint or proposal logged | Day 0 |
| Duty team verifies within 48 hours | Day 2 |
| Survey completed | Within 21 days of logged proposal |
| Report + estimate on assembly agenda | Next scheduled assembly |
| Assembly decision | Within 45 days of the survey |

## 7. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

Two members complain that pressure is weak. Complaints alone do not drill. The
duty logs for the past eight months show the static level falling from 90 to
110 feet, and the motor ran dry three times last month. The survey confirms the
yield has dropped by a third. The assembly now has documented justification for
a new boring — and a written record that says why.

## 8. PROJECT REJECTION AND REASSESSMENT

- If the assembly rejects the project for lack of evidence, the rejection is
  recorded with the reasons. The proponent may resubmit when new evidence
  arrives — the earlier rejection does not block a later, better-documented
  proposal.
- If the assembly approves on inadequate evidence, any member may raise the
  objection through the Truth Veto
  (`00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/02-Single-Member-Truth-Veto.md`).

---

*Needs assessment is about facts, not favours. A boring approved without
evidence is a risk borne by every member's pocket — so no assembly may approve
one on feeling alone.*
