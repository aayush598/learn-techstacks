# NEW BORING INSTALLATION — FUNDING AND SHARES

**Folder:** `09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/01-New-Boring-Installation/`

---

## 1. WHY THIS FILE EXISTS

The old failure is a famous one: ten houses paid for a boring, then twenty more
joined and used it free, while the ten treated it as their property. This file
decides, on the day the project is approved, how the money is raised, how it is
recorded, and what the payers do — and do not — get in return.

## 2. COST-SPLIT OPTIONS

The assembly chooses one of these two options when the project is approved, and
the choice is recorded with the arithmetic:

| Option | Meaning | Used when |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| **Equal per connection** | Total project cost divided by current member connections | All connections will benefit equally |
| **Share-based** | Split by a published factor: expected usage, household size, or connection size | Benefit or burden clearly differs between houses |

A share-based split must be explained in plain arithmetic on the notice board
before the vote. No split may be invented after the project starts.

## 3. THE SHARE LEDGER

1. Every contribution to the project is recorded in the **Share Ledger**:
   member name, date, amount, and the share units bought.
2. The ledger is public (P6), kept with the books
   (`06-FINANCE/03-Bookkeeping/`), and auditable.
3. Shares are units of a funded project, not ownership of the boring. The
   boring itself belongs to the system and all members (P1).

## 4. FOUNDERS' CAPITAL — REPAYABLE SHARE, NOT CONTROL

1. Capital contributed for the boring is a **debt owed by the system** to the
   contributor.
2. It is repaid on the published refund schedule
   (`01-MEMBERSHIP/03-Exit-and-Transfer/03-Refund-Procedure.md`), from the
   Reserve Fund / asset fund as it stabilises.
3. Capital buys **no permanent control**: no extra vote (P2), no keys, no
   priority water, no right to run the motor. This repeats the founding-member
   rule in `01-MEMBERSHIP/02-Types-of-Members/01-Founding-Members.md`.
4. The system may, by open resolution, treat part of a founder's capital as a
   **donation** and waive repayment — but never for one founder and not another,
   and never as a reward for influence.

## 5. RESERVE FUND IS THE FIRST SOURCE

| Order | Source | Note |
|-------|--------|------|
| 1 | Reserve Fund | The designed first payer for major renewal |
| 2 | Project fund-raise | A one-time, published collection from current members |
| 3 | Buy-in capital from late joiners | Repays the fund over time (`03-Late-Joiner-Buy-in/`) |

The Reserve Fund rules in
`06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/02-Reserve-Fund.md` govern the withdrawal; a
withdrawal above the budgeted line requires an assembly resolution. A project
fund-raise is a Class C decision (two-thirds vote) with the amount, deadline,
and refund terms published before collection.

## 6. NO SPONSORSHIP FOR INFLUENCE

1. No single family "sponsors" a boring in exchange for influence, keys, or
   water priority.
2. A voluntary extra contribution is accepted only as a recorded donation, and
   confers no privilege.
3. A member who attempts to buy control through funding is violating the
   constitution; the funding is still recorded and repaid, but the attempt is
   dealt with under `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/`.

## 7. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

Ten houses fund a new boring at ₹1,20,000 — ₹12,000 each, recorded in the Share
Ledger. The Reserve Fund covers ₹60,000 and the houses cover the rest by a
published one-time call. When the fund stabilises, the ₹60,000 is repaid to the
Reserve Fund and the ten houses' capital is repaid on the refund schedule. When
twenty more houses join later, they pay buy-in (`03-Late-Joiner-Buy-in/`), and
the fund recovers the founders' money. No one "owns" the boring; everyone owns
it.

## 8. TIMELINE OF A FUNDING PLAN

| Step | When |
|------|------|
| Cost estimate and split option published | At least 15 days before the vote |
| Assembly approves project, amount, and split | Class C, 2/3 vote |
| Share Ledger opened and contributors recorded | Same day as each payment |
| Reserve Fund withdrawal resolution passed | Before any payment to the contractor |
| First call on members | Per published schedule, with receipts |
