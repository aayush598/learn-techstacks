# NEW BORING INSTALLATION — PROJECT CLOSE-OUT

**Folder:** `09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/01-New-Boring-Installation/`

---

## 1. WHY THIS FILE EXISTS

Projects in the old system ended in a handshake and later a dispute: "we agreed
on 8 lakh, you say 10." Close-out makes the ending public, documented, and
auditable, so that when the boring is supplying water for years, no one can
rewrite its history.

## 2. FINAL BILL VERSUS CONTRACT

1. The contractor's final bill is checked **line by line** against the contract
   and against approved change orders.
2. Any variation beyond the contract must have **prior written approval** by the
   oversight members and the assembly's spending authority. A bill for unbudgeted
   extras without that approval is not payable from common funds.
3. The comparison (contracted amount, approved changes, amount paid) is written
   into the close-out report and published.

## 3. THE PUNCH LIST

Before final payment, the oversight members inspect the finished work and list
outstanding items:

| Item | Status |
|------|--------|
| Casing top sealed and clean | |
| Surface protection / platform in place | |
| Delivery line and valve installed | |
| Site left clean and safe | |

Each item is completed or formally waived with a reason and a photo. Final
payment is released only when the punch list is cleared.

## 4. WARRANTY AND DOCUMENTS FILED

The following are collected from the contractor and filed in the project file:

- Warranty certificates and their start/end dates;
- Depth log, casing and screen records, pumping test log;
- Laboratory water-quality report;
- Invoices and payment vouchers;
- The signed acceptance certificate
  (`01-New-Boring-Installation/04-Water-Testing-and-Approval.md`).

## 5. ASSET ADDED TO THE REGISTER

The completed boring is entered in the Infrastructure Asset Register
(`09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/05-Infrastructure-Asset-Register/01-Asset-List.md`)
with its asset ID, description, date, cost, warranty, and location — so that
from day one it is common property on the books, not an unlisted relic.

## 6. FINAL ACCOUNTING REPORT TO THE ASSEMBLY

The close-out report contains:

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| Income | Reserve Fund withdrawal, share contributions, donations, buy-in |
| Expense | Contract payments, changes, testing, treatment, admin |
| Balance | Surplus or under-spend, with reasons |
| Assets | The new boring and its valuation basis |

1. **Surplus** returns to the Reserve Fund, or is applied to reduce the
   project calls, by assembly decision.
2. **Under-spend** must be covered by a funded resolution — never recovered
   silently from members or from the contractor outside the contract.

## 7. WATER TURN-ON AND TESTING

1. After acceptance, first supply begins on the published schedule
   (`04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/01-Daily-Schedule/`).
2. Pressure and flow are observed at the farthest and highest taps and logged
   for the first two weeks.
3. Defects found in those weeks are claimed under the contractor's warranty —
   the warranty register is opened on the day of acceptance
   (`02-New-Motor-Installation/04-Warranty-Management.md`).

## 8. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

The boring cost twenty percent over the quote. The overspend came from a
mid-way change — the depth had to be increased — that the oversight team
approved in writing when the rig log proved the water-bearing zone was deeper.
The close-out report shows the change order, the extra payment, and the new
final figure. Nothing is hidden; the contractor's warranty later covers the
casing joint that leaks in week three, and the register proves the boring's
warranty start date.

## 9. WHO SIGNS THE CLOSE-OUT

The close-out report is signed by the two oversight members, the Finance
Committee, and the assembly chair at the meeting where it is presented. It is
kept permanently with the records (`11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/03-Records-Retention/`).

---

*A project is not finished when the pump starts. It is finished when the books
balance, the warranty is filed, and the asset is on the register — all public.*
