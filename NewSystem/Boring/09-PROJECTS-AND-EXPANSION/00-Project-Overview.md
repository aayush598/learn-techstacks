# PROJECTS AND EXPANSION — OVERVIEW

**Folder:** `09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/`

---

## 1. WHY THIS FOLDER EXISTS

Big structural matters — drilling a new boring, installing or replacing a motor,
building a tank, relaying pipes, fitting meters — were the way the old system
kept itself unfair. Whoever had money and influence arranged the work, and that
person then behaved as the owner: they ran the motor, kept the keys, collected
from everyone else, and decided whose leak got fixed first.

This folder removes that. A project is a **system project**: owned by all
members through the common funds and recorded shares. It begins from evidence,
is funded by published rules, is supervised openly, and ends with public records.

## 2. WHAT COUNTS AS A PROJECT

| Work | Classification | Authority |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Routine maintenance, minor repairs, parts under the repair budget | Maintenance | `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/` (Repair Approval Matrix) |
| Any capital work above the project threshold (default ₹5,000) or creating a new asset | **Project** | General Assembly |
| New boring, new motor, tank, meter network, major pipe line | **Project** | General Assembly |

The project threshold is set by the Annual Budget assembly and may be revised
only there. Below the threshold, no rule in this folder is bypassed if the work
creates a new asset — a new asset always requires assembly approval.

## 3. THE STANDARD PROJECT CYCLE

Every project, large or small, passes through the same six stages. No stage is
skipped, whatever the urgency:

| Stage | Rule | Reference |
|-------|------|-----------|
| 1. Needs assessment | Documented evidence of need, survey, report | `01-New-Boring-Installation/01-Needs-Assessment.md` |
| 2. Cost estimate | Itemised estimate from the market | `01-New-Boring-Installation/02-Funding-and-Shares.md` |
| 3. Funding plan | Reserve Fund first, then shares/buy-in | `01-New-Boring-Installation/02-Funding-and-Shares.md` |
| 4. Contractor selection | 3+ written quotes, comparison, written contract | `01-New-Boring-Installation/03-Contractor-Selection.md` |
| 5. Oversight | 2+ members supervise, checklist, records | `02-New-Motor-Installation/03-Installation-Over-Sight.md` |
| 6. Close-out | Final bill vs contract, tests, warranty, asset register, final accounts | `01-New-Boring-Installation/05-Project-Close-Out.md` |

## 4. ASSEMBLY DECISION CLASSES

| Class | What it covers | Decided by | Record |
|-------|----------------|------------|--------|
| A | Routine maintenance within budget | Finance Committee / Repair Matrix | Ledger |
| B | Minor repair above routine, under project threshold | Finance Committee + published notice | Minutes + ledger |
| C | Project above threshold, new asset, new debt | General Assembly, **two-thirds (2/3) vote** | Resolution in minutes |
| D | Change to constitution, permanent new fee | Amendment process | `00-CONSTITUTION/05-Amendment-Process/` |

No member, family, committee, or office-holder authorises a Class C project
alone. Supermajority rules apply as in
`02-GOVERNANCE/03-Decision-Making/03-Supermajority-Rules.md`.

## 5. PROJECTS BELONG TO ALL MEMBERS — NO PERMANENT CONTROL

1. A project is funded by all members, through funds and shares, and its output
   (boring, motor, tank, pipes, meters) is **common property** (P1).
2. No project creates a permanent controlling interest for any person, family,
   or group. Whoever funds, arranges, or supervises gains:
   - no extra vote (P2);
   - no ownership of the asset or the keys;
   - no priority in water slots;
   - no claim to "his" boring or "her" motor.
3. A member who contributes to a project is recorded in the share ledger
   (`01-New-Boring-Installation/02-Funding-and-Shares.md`) and repaid per the
   refund schedule — as a loan, not as a purchase of power.
4. "Sponsoring" a project to gain influence is prohibited. Voluntary extra
   donations are accepted only as recorded donations with no privileges.

## 6. NO PROJECT WITHOUT EVIDENCE (P5)

- A project begins only from documented need: water-level records, duty logs,
  complaint-register entries, meter readings, test reports. It never begins
  from "my son knows a rig operator" or "the water feels less."
- Every project file contains: the assessment, all quotes, the contract,
  invoices, photographs, acceptance tests, and the final accounting. The file
  is public (P6).

## 7. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

Ten houses fund a new boring and a motor. Under the old system they became the
owners and everyone who joined later had to beg for water. Under this
constitution: the boring is common property, the ten houses are recorded as
founders with a repayable capital share, and twenty later houses enter by the
published buy-in (`03-Late-Joiner-Buy-in/`). The founders get repaid and
honoured — never enthroned.
