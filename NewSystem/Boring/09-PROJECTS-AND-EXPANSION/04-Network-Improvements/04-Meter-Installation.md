# NETWORK IMPROVEMENTS — METER INSTALLATION

**Folder:** `09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/04-Network-Improvements/`

---

## 1. WHY THIS FILE EXISTS

Meters change billing from "same for everyone" to "pay for what you use." Done
well, they make sharing fair and expose leaks. Done badly — uncalibrated
meters, single readers, fudged numbers — they become a new machine for cheating.
This file governs the whole meter life: selection, calibration, reading,
billing, and disputes.

## 2. ADOPTION AND SELECTION

1. Meters are adopted **only by a two-thirds (2/3) assembly vote** in the
   Annual Budget, per `06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/03-Consumption-Based-Charges.md`.
   No committee or individual can bring in meters alone.
2. Brand/model is approved by the assembly; 2-3 shops are compared (price,
   accuracy class, seal quality, service) and the comparison is filed.
3. Each meter is a **sealed unit with a unique number**; the number and the
   seal are photographed and entered in the Asset Register
   (`09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/05-Infrastructure-Asset-Register/01-Asset-List.md`).
4. Meters are common property (P1); a meter is never "the member's meter" even
   though it sits at their tap.

## 3. CALIBRATION

| Stage | Rule |
|-------|------|
| On purchase | Calibration/verification certificate taken from the dealer and filed |
| Periodically | Re-verification on the manufacturer's schedule, or every 5 years |
| On suspicion | A meter with a complaint is tested on a flow rig or against a measured bucket; if faulty it is replaced |
| Seals | A broken seal is logged immediately; it is treated as tampering until proven otherwise |

Calibration records are public (P6).

## 4. THE READING PROCEDURE — TWO READERS, PHOTOS

1. **Two members read together**, from different households, per the duty
   roster — the meter-reader plus one witness. A single reader never reads
   alone.
2. **Photos** of each meter reading are taken and posted to the members' group
   the same day.
3. Readings are taken on a **fixed date** published in the roster (e.g., the
   1st of the month).
4. Both readers **sign** the reading sheet; the sheet and photos are attached
   to the monthly billing entry in the ledger.
5. Estimated reads are allowed only when access is genuinely impossible; they
   are marked "EST" and corrected at the next actual read.

## 5. BILLING PER CONSUMPTION TIER

1. The per-kilolitre (kL) rate — and any tier structure — is derived from the
   budget and published with it
   (`06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/03-Consumption-Based-Charges.md`).
2. Each member's bill is computed from the **signed reading sheet**, not from a
   reader's memory.
3. Payment follows the monthly contribution rules: due by the 10th, late fee
   per `06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/04-Penalties-and-Fines.md`.

## 6. THE DISPUTE RULE — EVIDENCE DECIDES (P5)

1. A member who disputes a bill compares their own photo against the signed
   sheet and the bill — all three are public.
2. A joint re-read is arranged with the meter-reader and one other member; a
   photo is taken and posted.
3. If the dispute continues, a written objection is filed; the Finance
   Committee answers in writing within **7 days**, attaching the evidence.
4. The dispute is settled by the **evidence** — photos, seals, the signed
   sheet, bank records — never by a vote, never by who shouts louder.
5. **Meter fault:** a defective meter (seal intact, reading clearly wrong) is
   settled at the average of the last three good months; the meter is repaired
   or replaced and the fault noted in the register.
6. **Tampering:** a broken seal, a bypass, or an altered reading is
   unauthorised use under `02-Water-Usage-Agreement.md`, not a billing dispute.

## 7. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

House X's bill doubles in one month. The member checks the photo and the signed
sheet: both show the same reading. The member then finds a leaking private line
behind their house and a borewell pump in the yard drawing from the same sump —
the increase is theirs. The dispute closes on evidence, the meter is exonerated,
and the private leak is repaired at the member's cost.

## 8. WHO PAYS FOR THE METER

- Meter cost and installation are a **project expense** from the Reserve Fund
  or a project fund — never a private charge on one member, and never a
  surcharge that only the poor end up paying.

---

*The meter is a scale for water, not a weapon. Two readers, a photo, a signed
sheet, and a published rate keep the scale honest.*
