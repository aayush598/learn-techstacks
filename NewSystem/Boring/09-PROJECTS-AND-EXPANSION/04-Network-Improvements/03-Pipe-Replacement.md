# NETWORK IMPROVEMENTS — PIPE REPLACEMENT

**Folder:** `09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/04-Network-Improvements/`

---

## 1. WHY THIS FILE EXISTS

Worn, pitted pipes leak water and money. In the old system, whose leak got
fixed first depended on who was powerful; a broken pipe under the street was
"someone else's problem," and wastage was everyone's loss and nobody's
responsibility. This file makes replacement a planned, fair, recorded project.

## 2. IDENTIFICATION OF WORN AND LEAKING LINES

Sources of truth:

| Source | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| Leak log and fault reports (`05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/02-Breakdown-Repair/`) | Where leaks happen and how often |
| Monthly wastage analysis (pumped vs. delivered, if meters exist) | Which sections lose water |
| Duty inspection rounds | Visible damage, corrosion, exposed joints |
| Water-level log of the tank (`02-Overhead-Tank.md`) | Abnormal drops pointing to a line leak |

No line is replaced on rumour. A line is a replacement candidate only when two
independent sources point at it.

## 3. PRIORITY — BY LEAK SEVERITY, NOT FAVOUR (P5)

The replacement order is set by a published scoring:

| Criterion | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Leak volume / wastage (measured or estimated) | High |
| Risk to roads, foundations, or safety | High |
| Frequency of repairs on the section | Medium |
| Number of houses served by the line | Medium |

The score is computed from records and shown on the notice board. A
well-connected member's cosmetic crack ranks below a leaking main under the
road — friendship does not move a score.

## 4. ASSEMBLY-APPROVED PROJECT

1. Pipe replacement is a **project** under the standard cycle
   (`09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/00-Project-Overview.md`): needs assessment,
   estimate, funding plan, 3+ quotes
   (`05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/04-Vendor-Management/02-Multiple-Quotes-Rule.md`),
   written contract, oversight, close-out.
2. The assembly approves the priority order and the budget by a two-thirds (2/3)
   vote for work above the project threshold.

## 5. FUNDING

1. Common lines are paid from **system funds** — Reserve Fund first
   (`06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/02-Reserve-Fund.md`), then a project
   fund-raise. No member is billed privately for a common line.
2. A pipe inside a member's private plot (beyond the connection point) is the
   member's own cost, per the consumption rules
   (`06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/03-Consumption-Based-Charges.md`).

## 6. RECORDS

For every replaced section, the project file records:

| Field | Entry |
|-------|-------|
| Section replaced | (e.g., main line, temple to gate) |
| Material and size | PVC/HDPE, diameter |
| Length | metres |
| Cost | with vouchers |
| Date of work | |
| Photos | before and after |

The new section is entered in the Asset Register
(`09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/05-Infrastructure-Asset-Register/01-Asset-List.md`).

## 7. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

The line behind the temple leaks an estimated 40% of night flow — proved by the
wastage analysis and the tank level log. A cosmetic crack near the entrance is
favoured by a prominent member's family. The scoring table ranks the temple
line first; it is replaced that quarter, and the entrance crack is scheduled
next. The prominent family's preference changed no score.

## 8. WATER SUPPLY DURING REPLACEMENT

- The duty team publishes the shut-off window at least 48 hours ahead
  (`04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/01-Daily-Schedule/`); a temporary bypass or reduced
  schedule is provided where feasible.
- Emergency repair of a burst follows `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/02-Breakdown-Repair/`.

---

*A line is replaced because the records say it leaks, in the order the score
says is urgent — never because someone asked loudly.*
