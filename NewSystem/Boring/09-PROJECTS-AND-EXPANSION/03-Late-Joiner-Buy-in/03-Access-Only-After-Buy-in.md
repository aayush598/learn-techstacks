# LATE JOINER BUY-IN — ACCESS ONLY AFTER BUY-IN

**Folder:** `09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/03-Late-Joiner-Buy-in/`

---

## 1. WHY THIS FILE EXISTS

"Connect us first, we will pay later" is the oldest trick in the colony. The
payment never comes, and the next twenty families get water free on top of the
ten who paid for the boring. This file sets the gate: **no connection until the
first installment is paid and the agreements are signed.**

## 2. THE GATE RULE

A household is connected to the network only when **both** conditions hold:

1. The **signed agreements** — Membership, Water Usage, and Contribution
   (`11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/01-Binding-Agreement/`) — are on file.
2. The **first buy-in installment** is paid and recorded in the Share Ledger.

Until then the household is a **pending applicant**: no tap, no slot, no
connection work at system expense. The gate is mechanical — the Registry and
Finance records show both conditions, so no officer "waives" it.

## 3. WHY THE GATE EXISTS

1. It prevents the classic injustice of joining **after all the cost is paid**:
   the people who funded the system are not asked to gift its use to
   newcomers (P4).
2. It prevents the opposite injustice too: a member who is connected without
   paying has no reason to settle, and the founders are pushed into bullying
   them — which this system refuses to do.

## 4. HUMANITARIAN GRACE — DOCUMENTED HARDSHIP

1. A household in **genuine, documented hardship** (medical emergency, disaster,
   extreme poverty) may receive water before the full buy-in.
2. Grace is granted only by the **General Assembly by a two-thirds (2/3) vote**,
   in writing, with: the reason, the reduced amount or period, and the
   repayment plan. It is recorded, time-bound (default 60 days), and **never
   secret**.
3. Grace is not permanent. A family under grace that later can pay must settle
   the balance per the plan.
4. A "hardship" claim used to avoid payment by a family that can pay is dealt
   with under the enforcement rules (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/`).

## 5. UNAUTHORISED CONNECTIONS

1. A pending household that takes water — through a direct tap, a pipe into the
   line, or a neighbour's tap used as its own — commits **unauthorised use**, a
   serious offence under the Water Usage Agreement
   (`11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/01-Binding-Agreement/02-Water-Usage-Agreement.md`).
2. The connection is removed and the unauthorised consumption is billed at the
   published rate; the buy-in remains owed.
3. The Enforcement Ladder applies
   (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`).

## 6. TIMELINE FOR A NEW HOUSEHOLD

| Step | Deadline |
|------|----------|
| Application and verification | Per `01-MEMBERSHIP/01-Enrollment/01-New-Member-Admission.md` |
| Buy-in statement issued | 7 days after admission |
| Agreements signed | Before first installment |
| First installment received | Before connection |
| Connection work | Within 14 days of first installment |

## 7. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

A new family asks to be connected "just for the wedding week" without paying.
The gate rule refuses, and the published formula plus a 4-month installment
plan are offered in writing. The wedding uses tanker water at the family's
cost — system water begins when the agreements are signed and the first
installment lands. Meanwhile, a family whose house was flooded receives a
recorded 60-day grace from the assembly, water on day one, and a written plan
to settle the balance.

## 8. NO EXCEPTIONS BY INFLUENCE

- No member, committee, or office-holder may open the gate for a relative,
  friend, or benefactor. A connection made without the two conditions is void
  and the responsible member faces the enforcement rules.

---

*The gate is not cruelty; it is the fairness that keeps the ten who paid from
subsidising the twenty who joined later — and keeps the twenty from ever
becoming servants of the ten.*
