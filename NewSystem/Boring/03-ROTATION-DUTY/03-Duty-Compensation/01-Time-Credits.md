# 01 - TIME-CREDITS

**Folder:** `03-ROTATION-DUTY/03-Duty-Compensation/`  
**Cross-reference:** `00-CONSTITUTION/01-Preamble/03-Definitions.md`, `04-Equal-Opportunity-to-Earn.md`, `03-Credit-Redemption.md`, `06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/01-Common-Fund.md`

## 1. WHAT A TIME-CREDIT IS

A time-credit is the unit of compensated time in this system. **One duty hour =
one credit.** Credits are the default way the system pays for work, because they
work even when the common fund is low on cash. They convert the duty system from
"who is willing to do a favour" to "who is owed a fair balance".

## 2. EARNING CREDITS

Credits are earned for, at minimum:

1. Operation duty (on/off + log + asset check).
2. Training conduct (running a session).
3. Supervision of a new member's first duty (buddy role).
4. Backup substitution for a missed duty.
5. Any other paid role the assembly adds by resolution (repair coordination,
   record keeping, audit support).

Every hour worked earns exactly 1 credit, regardless of whether the member is
"important" or a quiet new joiner. Equal hours, equal credits — this is what
P2 and P8 mean in practice.

## 3. THE CREDIT VALUE

1. The rupee value of 1 credit is set **quarterly by the General Assembly**,
   using the published formula in `06-FINANCE/`.
2. The value is published at least 7 days before the quarter begins.
3. The value is the same for every member. There is no seniority rate and no
   special rate for "old-timers".
4. Credits are liabilities of the common fund: they are real money the system
   owes the member, not symbolic points.

## 4. THE LEDGER

1. Every member has a **credit ledger page** showing: date, duty/role, hours,
   credits earned, credits redeemed, running balance.
2. The ledger is updated within 7 days of each duty.
3. The ledger is a public document; any member may inspect any other member's
   ledger, because P6 (radical transparency) applies to compensation too.
4. At the quarterly assembly, each member's balance is read aloud in summary
   form and confirmed.

## 5. HOW CREDITS REDUCE CONTRIBUTION

1. A member's monthly contribution can be reduced up to the amount of their
   accumulated credit value, per the rules in `03-Credit-Redemption.md`.
2. This makes duty attractive: taking your fair share of duty measurably
   lowers your money contribution, while someone else covering for you pays
   more of the running cost.
3. Credits may also be held and paid out in cash when a member exits the system
   (`03-Credit-Redemption.md`).

## 6. SCENARIO

The assembly sets the credit value at ₹X per credit. Member A does 4 weeks of
duty (28 credits) and reduces her contributions accordingly. Member B, who is
medically exempt, buys out each week; his buy-out money, plus the general
contributions, funds the value behind A's credits. Nobody works free, nobody
pays double, and every hour is accounted for.
