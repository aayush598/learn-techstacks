# 04 - EQUAL OPPORTUNITY TO EARN

**Folder:** `03-ROTATION-DUTY/03-Duty-Compensation/`  
**Cross-reference:** `01-Time-Credits.md`, `02-Monetary-Stipend.md`, `01-Duty-Roster/02-Roster-Generation-by-Lot.md`, `00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/03-Right-to-Equal-Power.md`

---

## 1. THE PRINCIPLE

Every member must have an **equal chance** to earn credits or stipend. The
moment one person or one household can corner the paid duty, the old system has
re-entered through the payroll: the "worker" becomes the new boss, and everyone
else becomes his customers.

## 2. THE RULES THAT KEEP EARNING EQUAL

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Lot-drawn roster | The duty order comes from the public lot draw (`01-Duty-Roster/02-Roster-Generation-by-Lot.md`); nobody can volunteer themselves into a permanent income. |
| One duty per household per week | Two adults in one household do not double-earn in the same week. |
| Earning cap | No member may earn more than **X credits (or stipend equivalent) per quarter** — set by the assembly so that a quarter of duty earns the same as everyone else's fair share. |
| Backup duty capped too | Backup substitutions count toward the same cap, so the "always available" member cannot monopolise paid cover. |
| No trading of duties for pay | A member may not buy out someone else's duty and then be paid for it while also paying to skip their own; each duty is earned and paid once. |

## 3. THE CAP IS FOR FAIRNESS, NOT PUNISHMENT

1. The cap exists so the compensation pool is spread across members rather than
   pooled into one household.
2. A member who wants more earning can earn it in **other sanctioned roles**
   (training, record keeping, audit, repair coordination) which are also
   rotated and also capped where needed.
3. The cap is reviewed quarterly; it is a financial control, not a badge of
   trust.

## 4. THE QUIET MEMBER'S PROTECTION

Members who never push themselves forward must not lose out. The roster is
mechanical: the lot draw puts the quiet member in the order just like everyone
else, and their duty is recorded and paid exactly like everyone else's. Earning
opportunity does not depend on personality, friendship, or who is remembered at
the assembly.

## 5. SCENARIO

One retired member has plenty of free time and offers to "just handle all the
duty" every week. He is thanked but refused: the roster order governs, and his
availability is used only for genuine backup cover. His offer is redirected —
he trains other members instead, for which he earns the same capped credits.
The colony keeps an even distribution of work, income, and power.

## 6. ENFORCEMENT

1. The record keeper flags any member approaching the cap; the credit ledger
   shows the count openly.
2. An attempt to exceed the cap (e.g., by chaining swaps to take extra weeks)
   is refused at the swap register and reported to the assembly.
3. Deliberately arranging extra paid duty in someone's name is a ledger
   violation and is handled under `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/`.
