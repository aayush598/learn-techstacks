# 03 - CREDIT REDEMPTION

**Folder:** `03-ROTATION-DUTY/03-Duty-Compensation/`  
**Cross-reference:** `01-Time-Credits.md`, `02-Monetary-Stipend.md`, `06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/01-Common-Fund.md`, `01-MEMBERSHIP/03-Exit-and-Transfer/`

---

## 1. THE VALUE PROMISE

Credits are real liabilities of the common fund. A credit earned must be
redeemable, or it becomes a hollow promise and duty becomes forced free labour
(P8 violation). This document defines exactly how credits are redeemed.

## 2. WAYS TO REDEEM CREDITS

| Method | How it works |
|--------|--------------|
| Offsetting monthly contribution | Credits reduce the member's monthly contribution at the current quarterly rate. |
| Cash on exit | On leaving the system, the member's remaining credits are paid in cash from the common fund. |
| Transfer within the household | Credits may be transferred between adult members of the **same household** (same connection). |
| Buying a replacement | A member may use credits to buy out a duty period they cannot take, at 1.5x the duty value. |

## 3. REDEMPTION RULES

1. **No expiry.** Credits never lapse. A credit earned in one year is
   redeemable in any later year. The system may not "forget" old balances.
2. Redemption uses the **current quarterly rate** at the time of redemption,
   not the rate of the year the credit was earned.
3. Transfer is limited to **members of the same household**. Credits cannot be
   sold or given to another household; that would let work be cornered and
   traded outside the system.
4. Offsetting is capped so that a member still contributes at least the minimum
   base amount defined by the assembly, keeping the common fund solvent.
5. Cash payment on exit is due within **30 days** of the exit settlement being
   approved.

## 4. RECORDING REDEMPTIONS

1. Every redemption is entered in the member's credit ledger and in the duty
   log register as a financial entry.
2. The common fund ledger records the cash or contribution offset in the same
   entry, so the two books always match.
3. At the quarterly assembly, the total outstanding credit liability is
   reported openly — the system must know its own debt to its members.

## 5. PROTECTION AGAINST ABUSE

1. The credit ledger is public; a member who claims more credits than the
   register shows can be checked in minutes.
2. A record keeper who forges credits is handled under
   `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/` — falsifying the ledger is a serious violation
   because it attacks P5 (truth) at the financial level.
3. The quarterly assembly must verify that the common fund can cover
   outstanding credits; if not, the credit value or the redemption cap is
   reviewed — openly, not silently.

## 6. SCENARIO

A retired member leaves the colony after 8 years. Her ledger shows 340
unredeemed credits. At the exit settlement, the record keeper values them at the
current rate and the common fund pays her within 30 days. Her work over the
years was never free labour — the system honoured the balance she had earned.
