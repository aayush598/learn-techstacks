# 02 - MONETARY STIPEND

**Folder:** `03-ROTATION-DUTY/03-Duty-Compensation/`  
**Cross-reference:** `01-Time-Credits.md`, `06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/01-Common-Fund.md`, `06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/01-Common-Fund.md`

---

## 1. WHEN A STIPEND APPLIES

The assembly may choose to pay duty in **cash (stipend)** instead of, or
alongside, time-credits. The stipend path is used when the common fund is
comfortable in cash and members prefer money over ledger balances.

## 2. SETTING THE STIPEND RATE

1. The stipend rate is set by the General Assembly **quarterly**, at the same
   meeting that sets the credit value.
2. The rate is published in rupees per duty period (or per hour), on the notice
   board, 7 days before it takes effect.
3. The rate is **one rate for all members**. There are no higher rates for
   friends, relatives, or the "knowledgeable".
4. The rate must not be lower than the credit value for the same work, so that
   the cash option is never a way to underpay work.

## 3. WHO MAY BE PAID

1. Any member who performs a compensated role: duty operator, backup
   substitute, trainer, buddy, record keeper, audit helper.
2. Payment is for work actually performed and recorded in the duty log
   register (`01-Duty-Roster/04-Duty-Log-Register.md`). No work, no stipend.
3. Committee members do not receive a stipend merely for being on a committee;
   they are paid only for concrete recorded work, the same as anyone else.

## 4. HOW STIPEND IS PAID

1. Stipend is paid **from the common fund** on a **voucher**.
2. The voucher shows: member name, role, duty dates, hours, rate, total
   amount, the ledger entry reference, and the signature of the member
   receiving payment.
3. A voucher requires **two signatures** for payment: the record keeper and one
   other committee signatory (per the joint-account rules in
   `06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/`).
4. Stipends are paid monthly, within 10 days of month-end.

## 5. STIPEND AND CREDITS CANNOT BOTH BE CLAIMED FOR THE SAME HOUR

1. A duty period is paid **either** in credits **or** in cash, never both.
   The assembly's quarterly decision fixes which.
2. If a member performs a duty in a cash quarter, they may still carry forward
   old credit balances, but the new duty earns cash only.

## 6. RECORD AND AUDIT

1. Every voucher is filed in the duty log register and summarised in the common
   fund ledger.
2. Stipend totals are reported at every assembly in the financial report.
3. Auditors (any member, at any time) can trace every rupee of stipend from
   voucher to bank statement.

## 7. SCENARIO

During the summer months the assembly chooses the stipend option to ensure duty
is never skipped. The rate is published. A member who does 7 duties in the
quarter receives her stipend voucher at month-end, signs it, and the amount is
paid from the common fund. Her neighbour, who also did 7 duties, receives the
same rate — identical work, identical pay, on record.
