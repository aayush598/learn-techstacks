# 02 - ROSTER GENERATION BY LOT

**Folder:** `03-ROTATION-DUTY/01-Duty-Roster/`  
**Cross-reference:** `01-Roster-Design.md`, `12-TEMPLATES/02-Duty-Roster-Sheet.md`, `00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/03-Right-to-Equal-Power.md`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

The roster order is decided by **lot, in public, in front of witnesses**, so
that no person — not even a well-liked one — can "hand-pick" who does duty when.
Favouritism and grudge-list scheduling become impossible.

## 2. THE LOT DRAW

1. The lot draw is held **once every quarter** (or whenever the assembly
   decides), at a fixed time and place announced 7 days in advance.
2. Every eligible member's name is written on an identical slip, folded
   identically, and placed in a single sealed box.
3. Names are drawn **one at a time, by a different member each time** — the
   order of the drawers also rotates so no one controls the process.
4. The drawn order becomes the **roster order** for the next quarter.
5. The draw is witnessed by **at least two members who are not standing for
   the draw** and recorded in writing in the duty log register.

## 3. WHO MAY BE EXCLUDED FROM THE DRAW

1. Members with a documented medical hardship (`01-Roster-Design.md`, Section 4)
   are recorded in a **separate buy-out list** and do not enter the operator
   draw. Their buy-out is compulsory and recorded.
2. Exclusion requires a document (medical certificate or CRB decision) filed in
   the member's file. A verbal claim of hardship is not enough.

## 4. NO HAND-PICKING

1. Nobody may volunteer to "take over the roster" permanently or choose their
   favourite weeks. That recreates a permanent operator.
2. A member may volunteer to cover an **extra** duty in place of someone who
   has bought out, but only after the lot order has been followed.
3. Any proposal to "just let X do it because X is good at it" is rejected by
   rule: the lot order governs, and X's skill is shared through training
   instead of being hoarded.

## 5. RE-DRAW RULES

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| A member becomes medically exempt after the draw | The member moves to the buy-out list; their position is filled by the next name in order. |
| A member leaves the colony | Their name is removed; the order closes up. |
| A new member joins mid-quarter | They enter at the end of the current order and re-enter the next lot draw. |
| A member withdraws from duty with a documented grievance | The CRB decides whether the withdrawal is justified; the position is covered by backups meanwhile. |
| The lot draw itself is challenged as rigged | A fresh draw is held immediately before at least 5 witnesses; the challenged draw is void. |
| The recorded order is lost | The draw is re-run; the earlier record, if later found, is treated as historical only. |

## 6. RECORD OF THE DRAW

The record must capture, for every draw:

1. Date, time, and place of the draw.
2. Names of all members entered.
3. Names of all drawers and witnesses.
4. The drawn order, exactly as pulled.
5. Signatures of all witnesses.
6. Any objections raised and their resolution.

The record is stored in the duty log register and is a public document.

## 7. SCENARIO

At the quarterly draw, a member argues that "Ramesh should go first because his
motor knowledge is better." This request is refused: the order is decided by
lot, not by reputation. Ramesh's knowledge is used to train others
(`02-Duty-Training/`), and he takes duty only where the lot places him — exactly
as everyone else does.
