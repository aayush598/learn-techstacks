# 03 - DUTY SWAP RULES

**Folder:** `03-ROTATION-DUTY/01-Duty-Roster/`  
**Cross-reference:** `01-Roster-Design.md`, `04-Duty-Log-Register.md`, `12-TEMPLATES/02-Duty-Roster-Sheet.md`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

Swapping lets members trade duties to fit their own lives without breaking the
system's guarantee that **every duty period is always covered**. A swap is a
mutual exchange of duty periods between two (or more) members, approved in
advance and recorded.

## 2. SWAP ELIGIBILITY

1. Only trained, roster-eligible members may operate, so a swap partner must be
   trained for the duty they take on.
2. A buy-out is not a swap. Buying out pays a replacement
   (`01-Roster-Design.md`, Section 5); swapping is an exchange of equal duties.

## 3. THE RULES

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Mutual consent | Both members must agree. A swap imposed on one member is void. |
| Written/recorded | The swap is recorded in the swap register before it takes effect. |
| Minimum notice | At least **24 hours** notice, unless the assembly has declared an emergency. |
| Same compensation basis | Swapped duties carry the same credit/stipend value, so neither member gains or loses pay. |
| No coercion | Offering to "forgive a favour" or pressuring a member to swap is prohibited. |
| Max one swap per member per week | Prevents one member from building a permanent schedule by chain-swapping. |

## 4. NO SWAP CHAINS THAT LEAVE GAPS

1. **Swap chains** (A gives to B, B gives to C, C gives to D) are allowed only
   if the **entire chain is recorded at once**, in one register entry, and every
   member in the chain confirms.
2. A chain is rejected if, at any link, a member would end up with **two duties
   in the same 24-hour period** or a duty for which they are not trained.
3. The rule of thumb: **the roster after the swap must have exactly one
   operator for every duty period and zero uncovered gaps.** If the swap leaves
   a gap, it is refused.

## 5. THE SWAP REGISTER

1. Every swap is entered in the swap register with: date, members involved,
   duties exchanged, confirmation signatures, and date the swap takes effect.
2. The swap register is part of the duty log register and is a public document.
3. The swap is reflected in the published roster within 24 hours.

## 6. RECORDED INSTRUCTIONS TO THE OPERATOR

1. When a swap is approved, the swap register entry **is** the handover
   instruction: the incoming member knows which dates and times they own.
2. The outgoing member must brief the incoming member on anything unusual
   (a gauge behaving oddly, a scheduled repair visit, etc.). The briefing is a
   one-line note in the swap register entry.

## 7. SCENARIOS

1. **Valid swap:** Meena (assigned Sunday morning) has a family function; Arun
   (assigned Saturday evening) agrees to take Sunday morning if Meena takes
   Saturday evening. Both are trained, both consent, 48 hours notice given, the
   swap is registered. Approved.
2. **Rejected chain:** A swaps with B, B swaps with C, C swaps with D, but D
   ends up with duty on a day when D has a second duty already. The chain leaves
   a collision; it is refused and a simpler two-way swap is suggested.
3. **Void swap:** A "swaps" by simply not showing up and telling B "you do it".
   No notice, no consent, no record. This is a missed duty by A and is treated
   under `04-Duty-Failure/01-Missed-Duty-Penalty.md`, not as a swap.
