# 01 - ROSTER DESIGN

**Cross-reference:** `02-Roster-Generation-by-Lot.md`, `12-TEMPLATES/02-Duty-Roster-Sheet.md`, `04-Duty-Failure/01-Missed-Duty-Penalty.md`

## 1. THE ROSTER IS THE CONSTITUTION OF TIMING

The roster decides when the motor runs and who operates it. It is a **published
schedule**, never a secret kept by one person. Any member must be able to read
it, check it, and hold the day's operator to it.

## 2. DUTY CYCLE LENGTH

1. The default duty cycle is **weekly** (Sunday to Saturday). The assembly may
   adopt a different cycle by resolution, but no cycle may exceed 14 days.
2. Each week the duty passes to the next eligible member in the roster order.
3. The roster runs continuously. There is no break between cycles; a duty that
   falls on a holiday is still performed.

## 3. ROTATION FAIRNESS RULES

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Sequential rotation | Members take duty in roster order; no one may be skipped except by documented exemption. |
| Fair share of night duty | Night and early-morning duties are distributed evenly; the roster is designed so no member gets night duty more than twice in any 8-week window unless they volunteer. |
| Fair share of weekend duty | Weekend and festival duty rotates on the same principle. |
| No permanent "good" or "bad" slots | Slots are assigned by a rotating offset so the same person is not permanently on the 2 a.m. duty or the 8 a.m. duty. |
| Family grouping | Where two adult members of one household both hold connections, only one duty per household per week is counted. |

## 4. WHO IS ELIGIBLE

1. Every adult member in good standing who has completed compulsory training
   (`02-Duty-Training/01-Compulsory-Operation-Training.md`) is eligible.
2. Members with a **documented medical hardship** are exempt from operating but
   remain on the roster in the buy-out track.
3. New members join the roster **within 30 days** of completing training.
4. A member serving on an elected committee is not exempt; committees add to
   work, they do not subtract from it.

## 5. THE BUY-OUT OPTION

A member who cannot, or will not, operate may **buy out** their duty:

1. The buy-out charge equals **1.5 times** the applicable stipend/credit value
   for the duty period, payable in advance to the common fund
   (`06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/01-Common-Fund.md`).
2. The money is used to compensate the replacement operator.
3. Buy-out is a legitimate, honourable path. It is recorded in the duty log
   register but carries no stigma.
4. Buy-out does **not** excuse training; an able-bodied member who refuses to
   train is dealt with under `04-Duty-Failure/03-Chronic-Avoidance-Measures.md`.

## 6. PUBLICATION

1. The roster is **published 7 days in advance** on the notice board and the
   colony notification channel.
2. Publication date is fixed: the roster for the coming week is posted every
   Friday evening.
3. Once published, it may change only through a swap (`03-Duty-Swap-Rules.md`)
   or an emergency substitution (`04-Duty-Failure/02-Immediate-Replacement-System.md`).
4. The published copy is signed by the duty roster recorder and kept in the duty
   log register.

| 4 | Member D | On/off + log + check | 7 credits |
| 5 | Member A | Night-shifted pattern (offset applied) | 7 credits |

## 8. ROSTER RECORDS

1. Every roster is stored in the duty log register for **at least 3 years**.
2. The roster is a public document. Any member may inspect it at any time.
3. The template in `12-TEMPLATES/02-Duty-Roster-Sheet.md` is the standard
   format; no member may use a private format that is harder to audit.
