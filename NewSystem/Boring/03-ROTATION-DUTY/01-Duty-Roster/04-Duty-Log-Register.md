# 04 - DUTY LOG REGISTER

**Folder:** `03-ROTATION-DUTY/01-Duty-Roster/`  
**Cross-reference:** `04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/04-Monitoring-and-Logs/01-Motor-Run-Log.md`, `12-TEMPLATES/03-Duty-Log-Entry.md`, `00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/01-Principle-of-Truth-over-Majority.md`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

The duty log register is the **memory of the system**. Every duty performed,
every swap, every buy-out, every anomaly is recorded here. It is the first
place checked when someone says "the motor did not run today" or "the timer was
changed without informing us".

## 2. WHAT IS RECORDED FOR EACH DUTY

Each duty period produces one duty log entry. Using `12-TEMPLATES/03-Duty-Log-Entry.md`, the entry records:

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Date and duty period | The calendar date and time window |
| Operator | Name of the member who performed the duty |
| Time motor switched ON | Recorded at the moment of switching |
| Time motor switched OFF | Recorded at the moment of switching |
| Gauge reading at start | Water level / pressure reading |
| Gauge reading at end | Water level / pressure reading |
| Total run minutes | Calculated from the two times |
| Anomalies | Any unusual sound, leak, fault indicator, tampering, power cut |
| Handover note | Anything the next operator must know |
| Signature | Operator's signature; witnesses where available |
| Compensation entry | Credits or stipend earned (linked to `03-Duty-Compensation/`) |

## 3. WHEN AND HOW IT IS WRITTEN

1. The entry is made **at the motor room**, on paper or a photo-copied template,
   at the time of the event — not later from memory.
2. The paper entry is transcribed to the register within 24 hours.
3. A member who cannot write may dictate; another member records and signs as
   witness, and the dictating member signs or thumb-impresses.
4. Cross-outs are never erased. A correction is made by striking the wrong line,
   writing the correct value beside it, and initialling the correction.

## 4. THE LOG AS LEGAL EVIDENCE

1. The register is a **contemporaneous record** (written at the time, by the
   people who did the work). Under P5 (truth over majority), the log is treated
   as fact-proof: a log entry showing "motor off 02:10–06:00" cannot be voted
   away by members who claim the motor ran all night.
2. The register is admissible evidence in any CRB proceeding, in the colony's
   own appeals, and before government authorities
   (`08-GOVERNMENT-INTERACTION/04-Disputes-with-Government/`).
3. For this reason the register is never kept at a single person's house. The
   current volume is kept in the duty box and archived copies are stored as per
   `05-Key-and-Control/02-Duplicate-Keys.md`.

## 5. AUDIT AND INSPECTION

1. Any member may inspect the register at any time, with no appointment and no
   fee.
2. A monthly reconciliation compares the register to the electricity bill and
   the gauge readings; unexplained differences are raised in the assembly.
3. At each quarterly lot draw, the outgoing and incoming record keepers verify
   the register page count and sign a continuity note.

## 6. SCENARIO

At 2 a.m. a member switches the motor on "just to fill his own tank". The next
morning's operator logs a run from 02:10 to 02:35 that matches nobody's
schedule. The log is the proof; the member cannot outvote it because the run is
a recorded fact, not an opinion. The matter proceeds to the CRB under
`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/`.
