# 01 - KEY HOLDER ROTATION

**Folder:** `03-ROTATION-DUTY/05-Key-and-Control/`  
**Cross-reference:** `02-Duplicate-Keys.md`, `01-Duty-Roster/02-Roster-Generation-by-Lot.md`, `04-Duty-Failure/02-Immediate-Replacement-System.md`

## 1. THE HISTORICAL PROBLEM

In the old system, whoever held the keys held the water. The key holder decided
when the motor ran, who was allowed in, and when repairs were "convenient".
This folder ends that: **the keys are an operational tool, never a position of
power.**

## 2. KEYS ROTATE WITH DUTY

1. The current operator of the week holds the **operating key** to the motor
   room for exactly their duty period.
2. At handover, the key passes to the next operator as part of the handover
   procedure — the same moment the roster changes.
3. A key holder's term is therefore **never more than one duty cycle**. No
   family, group, or individual holds the operating key permanently.
4. The emergency backups also hold keys (`02-Duplicate-Keys.md`), so a missed
   handover never locks the motor room.

## 3. THE HANDOVER PROCEDURE

1. **Before taking duty:** the incoming operator collects the key from the duty
   box or from the outgoing operator and signs the key register.
2. **During duty:** the operator is the sole named holder of the operating key;
   they must not lend it to anyone.
3. **At the end of duty:** the operator returns the key to the duty box, or
   hands it to the next operator face-to-face, and signs the key register.
4. The key register records: date, time, key handed from, key handed to,
   both signatures.
5. If the outgoing operator is unreachable, the backup key from the duty box is
   used and the handover is completed at the next meeting.

## 4. THE KEY REGISTER

1. Every key movement — issue, return, duplicate creation, lost key — is
   recorded in the key register.
2. The key register is a public document, stored beside the duty log register.
3. A monthly reconciliation confirms that every key is accounted for: operating
   key, duty box key, backup keys, and the sealed emergency key.

## 5. PROHIBITIONS

1. A key holder may not keep the key "for convenience" after their duty ends.
2. A key holder may not make their own copy without recording it
   (`02-Duplicate-Keys.md`).
3. A key holder may not decide on their own to change the motor schedule; the
   schedule is the roster's business.
4. A member who repeatedly "forgets" to return keys is treated under the duty
   failure rules.

## 6. SCENARIO

Member A finishes her week of duty on Saturday evening. Member B arrives for
the handover, signs the register, and receives the key in front of a witness.
That night, a pipe bursts and the emergency response needs the motor room; the
backup list holder opens the duty box and uses the backup key — nobody has to
wake B or break the door. The key register records the whole sequence by
Monday.
