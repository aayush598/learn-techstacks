# 04 - NO SINGLE POINT OF FAILURE

## 1. THE TEST THE SYSTEM MUST PASS

The system must keep working if **any one person** — even the "most
knowledgeable", the longest-serving, or the friendliest — leaves, falls sick,
refuses, or is away. The test is asked monthly:

> *"If Member X were gone tomorrow, would the motor still run on schedule,
> the logs still be kept, and the money still be accounted?"*

If the answer is "no" for any single member, that member is a single point of
failure and the gap must be closed.

## 2. THE CROSS-TRAINING MATRIX

Every critical skill has **at least 2–3 trained members**. The matrix is
displayed on the notice board and maintained by the record keeper:

| Skill | Minimum trained members | Status check |
|-------|-------------------------|--------------|
| Motor on/off operation | All trained members (≥90% of adults) | Refreshed yearly |
| Reading gauge and logs | ≥5 members | Refreshed yearly |
| Using the digital/GSM control | ≥3 members | Refreshed when option installed |
| Reconciling the ledger and bank | ≥3 members | Quarterly |
| Handling government documents | ≥2 members | Per `08-GOVERNMENT-INTERACTION/01-Government-Motor/01-Document-Retention.md` |
| Minor electrical safety response | ≥2 members | Yearly |

If any cell in the matrix drops below the minimum (a member leaves), the colony
must **train replacements within 60 days** — not hope, train.

## 3. MONTHLY VALIDITY CHECK

1. Every month, the record keeper verifies that:
   - Every backup list has at least 3 names.
   - Every key is accounted for in the key register.
   - The trained-member register is current.
   - The cross-training matrix is complete.
2. The check is recorded in the duty log register; an empty month means an
   unchecked system.
3. Any gap found triggers the training schedule (`02-Duty-Training/02-Training-Schedule.md`)
   or a key re-log within 14 days.

## 4. NO ONE IS INDISPENSABLE — AND NO ONE IS SUPPRESSED

1. A highly skilled member is an **asset to be shared**, not a person to be
   depended on. Their knowledge is deliberately transferred through training
   and documented in the handbooks.
2. Conversely, the system never punishes a member for being capable. Being
   skilled earns them compensation (`03-Duty-Compensation/`); it does not make
   them a slave to the colony, and it does not make them the boss either.
3. A member who is "always available" is actively rotated out so the system
   does not quietly become dependent on their goodwill.

## 5. THE DEPARTURE DRILL

1. When any member announces departure, the record keeper runs a 7-day
   handover checklist: keys returned and re-logged, skills re-mapped, backup
   lists updated, any government documents in their custody transferred
   (`08-GOVERNMENT-INTERACTION/`).
2. The departure is announced to the assembly with a simple statement: *"The
   system has been checked and continues without Member X."*
3. This drill is not aimed at any person; it is a standing routine that makes
   leaving normal and harmless.

## 6. SCENARIO

The colony's best electrician — who once single-handedly fixed the starter —
is diagnosed with a condition requiring bed rest for months. Two weeks earlier,
the monthly check had already flagged that only one other member knew the
starter procedure. A make-up training session was held, and by the time the
electrician is hospitalised, three members can safely inspect the starter.
The motor runs every day of his absence, and his rest is undisturbed.
