# 01 - MISSED DUTY PENALTY

**Folder:** `03-ROTATION-DUTY/04-Duty-Failure/`  
**Cross-reference:** `02-Immediate-Replacement-System.md`, `03-Chronic-Avoidance-Measures.md`, `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`, `01-Duty-Roster/01-Roster-Design.md`

## 1. WHAT A MISSED DUTY IS

A duty is **missed** when the assigned operator does not perform it and no
valid swap, buy-out, or documented exemption covers it. Examples:

- The operator does not appear at the scheduled time.
- The operator appears but refuses to operate and refuses to arrange cover.
- The operator left a swap half-finished and the duty period was uncovered.
- The operator performed a wrong-time run that did not serve the published
  schedule.

Missing a duty is not just a personal matter: **someone else's water is
delayed**, and the work quietly falls on another member. That is why the rules
below exist.

## 2. THE IMMEDIATE RESPONSE

1. The backup list (`02-Immediate-Replacement-System.md`) covers the missed
   duty immediately. Water must keep flowing; nobody waits at an empty tap
   while the colony "decides what to do".
2. The missed duty is recorded in the duty log register the same day, with the
   facts: who was assigned, who covered, times, and outcome.

## 3. THE PENALTY

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Missed-duty charge | **Buy-out charge at 1.5x the current stipend/credit rate** for that duty period |
| Purpose | Reimburses the replacement operator and discourages casual skipping |
| Record | Entered in the member's ledger and the duty log register |
| Payment due | Within 14 days of the assembly notice |
| Repeat within a quarter | Additional measure under `03-Chronic-Avoidance-Measures.md` |

The charge is **in addition to** the fact that the member does not earn credits
for the missed duty (no work, no pay — the replacement gets the pay).

## 4. GENUINE EMERGENCY EXCEPTION

1. A missed duty is excused if the member can show a **documented genuine
   emergency**: hospital admission, accident, death in the family, or similar.
2. The document is filed within 7 days (or as soon as possible after the
   emergency).
3. The record keeper or a CRB member decides whether the emergency qualifies.
   "I forgot" is not an emergency. "My son's school suddenly called" is
   assessed honestly against the test: would the community's water have waited
   for this?
4. An excused emergency is recorded as "excused absence" and carries no charge.

## 5. THE PENALTY IS NOT A PUNISHMENT TRAP

1. The 1.5x buy-out is a **predictable, published** charge — it is not a fine
   invented by whoever is angry.
2. A member who is genuinely struggling is offered the documented-hardship path
   or a permanent buy-out arrangement, so the charge never becomes a debt trap
   for the poor.
3. Water is **never** cut for a first missed duty. Water is protected under
   P7 and may only be touched through the full due-process ladder
   (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`).

## 6. SCENARIO

On a rainy Tuesday, the operator assigned to the 6 a.m. run does not arrive.
The backup member covers the run and logs it. That evening, the operator says
his bike had a puncture. That is not a documented emergency; the missed-duty
charge is applied, and the backup is paid at the full rate. The schedule held,
the water flowed, and the record is complete.
