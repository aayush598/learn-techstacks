# 02 - IMMEDIATE REPLACEMENT SYSTEM

**Folder:** `03-ROTATION-DUTY/04-Duty-Failure/`  
**Cross-reference:** `01-Missed-Duty-Penalty.md`, `02-Duty-Training/02-Training-Schedule.md`, `01-Duty-Roster/02-Roster-Generation-by-Lot.md`

---

## 1. THE NON-NEGOTIABLE: NO UNCOVERED DUTY

The colony's water cannot wait for a missing member. **"No one was available"
is not an acceptable outcome in this system.** Every duty period must have a
backup before it starts, so that a missed duty is handled in minutes, not days.

## 2. THE BACKUP LIST

1. A **backup list** of at least **3 trained members** exists for every duty
   period.
2. The list is drawn from the trained-member register
   (`02-Duty-Training/02-Training-Schedule.md`, Section 4) and rotates so the
   same three people are not always on call.
3. The list is published with the roster, so every member knows who the backups
   are before the week begins.
4. If a member on the backup list is themselves unavailable (documented), the
   next trained member on the list moves up.

## 3. BACKUP ROTATION

1. Backups are assigned in order: Backup 1 covers first, Backup 2 if Backup 1
   is unavailable, Backup 3 last.
2. After covering, a backup is moved to the end of the backup order for the
   next period, so coverage is spread fairly.
3. Backup cover is paid work: the covering member earns the full credit/stipend
   for the duty (`03-Duty-Compensation/`).

## 4. THE PROTOCOL WHEN DUTY IS MISSED

| Step | Action | Deadline |
|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | The record keeper (or any member who notices) contacts Backup 1 | Within 30 minutes of the missed start |
| 2 | Backup 1 covers the run and completes the log | At the scheduled time |
| 3 | If Backup 1 is unreachable, Backup 2 is called; then Backup 3 | Within 30 minutes each |
| 4 | The missed duty is recorded with the reason | Same day |
| 5 | The missed-duty penalty process begins | Same day |

## 5. WHY THE MINIMUM IS THREE

1. **Three** backups means one can be sick, one can be away, and the duty is
   still covered by the third.
2. A single backup is a single point of failure; the whole point of this system
   is that no one person's unavailability stops the water.
3. The three-backup minimum is checked monthly: the record keeper verifies the
   list against the trained-member register and the medical-exemption list, and
   the check is recorded.

## 6. THE ROSTER SHOWS THE BACKUP ALWAYS

The published weekly roster includes a "Backup 1 / 2 / 3" column. If a member
reads the roster and sees a blank backup column, they must raise it — a blank
means the system has a gap, and gaps are everyone's business to close.

## 7. SCENARIO

At 5:40 a.m. the assigned operator's phone is unreachable. The record keeper
calls Backup 1, who lives two lanes away, and the motor is switched on by
5:58 a.m. — 2 minutes before the scheduled start. Later, the record keeper
finds Backup 1 had shifted to the tail of the backup order, and Backup 2's name
moves up. The register shows the whole sequence. The colony lost zero water
minutes.
