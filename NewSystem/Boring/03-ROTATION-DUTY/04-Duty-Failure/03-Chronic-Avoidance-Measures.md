# 03 - CHRONIC AVOIDANCE MEASURES

## 1. WHAT CHRONIC AVOIDANCE IS

Chronic avoidance is a **pattern**, not a single slip:

- Three or more missed duties within two quarters, or
- A repeated refusal to train, or
- Repeatedly arranging swaps that "accidentally" fall through at the last
  minute, or
- A member who is simply never there when it is their turn.

A single missed duty is handled by the penalty (`01-Missed-Duty-Penalty.md`).
Chronic avoidance is different because it **silently shifts work onto others
forever**, and silence about it is unfairness made permanent.

## 2. THE CORE RULE: NEVER SILENT TOLERANCE

1. Every missed duty is recorded, counted, and named in the duty log register.
2. The colony does **not** quietly reassign the chronic avoider's duties while
   smiling. That is how the burden slides onto the cooperative few and how the
   old hierarchy secretly rebuilds itself.
3. Every step below is documented, timed, and reviewable.

## 3. THE ENFORCEMENT LADDER FOR CHRONIC AVOIDANCE

| Step | Action | Timeline |
|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Written notice listing the missed duties, with the register references | Within 7 days of the third miss |
| 2 | A meeting with the member to hear their side (they may be struggling silently) | Within 14 days |
| 3 | Forced buy-out for all future duties: the member pays 1.5x stipend value in advance, and a replacement is assigned | From the next roster |
| 4 | Escalation to the CRB for a hearing | Within 21 days of step 2 |
| 5 | If the pattern persists, the CRB may suspend **non-water privileges** (event water booking, committee participation) — never water itself | After the hearing |
| 6 | Only the full due-process ladder may touch water access, and only as the final rung for refusal of every contribution path | Per `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md` |

## 4. WHAT NEVER HAPPENS

1. The colony never "cancels" a member's duty secretly and pretends all is well.
2. No member is shamed publicly for struggling; the meeting in step 2 is
   private, and hardship can be declared there.
3. Water is never cut for avoidance without the complete ladder. P7 protects
   even the member who annoys everyone.
4. The avoider's family is never punished for the avoider's actions; sanctions
   are personal.

## 5. THE LEGITIMATE WAY OUT

1. A member who genuinely cannot serve is offered the **documented hardship
   path** at step 2: a medical certificate or CRB decision converts them to the
   buy-out track permanently.
2. A member who can serve but has a real conflict with the system can raise a
   grievance (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/`) — the system listens instead of
   assuming bad faith.
3. The buy-out is always available as an honourable option. Chronic avoidance
   only hardens when a member refuses **all** options: serving, buying out, or
   explaining.

## 6. SCENARIO

A member has missed five duties in four months. Each time the backups covered,
and each time the register recorded it. At the third miss, the notice is issued;
at the meeting, the member says they were working nights and were exhausted.
The colony converts them to the buy-out track with a documented arrangement —
they pay, someone else serves, the water keeps flowing, and their dignity is
intact. The pattern is stopped without either silent tolerance or a feud.
