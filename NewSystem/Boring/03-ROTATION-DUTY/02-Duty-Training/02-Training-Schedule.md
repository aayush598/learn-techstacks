# 02 - TRAINING SCHEDULE

**Folder:** `03-ROTATION-DUTY/02-Duty-Training/`  
**Cross-reference:** `01-Compulsory-Operation-Training.md`, `01-MEMBERSHIP/04-Member-Registry/`, `12-TEMPLATES/`

---

## 1. CYCLE OF TRAINING

Training never stops. New members must be brought up to speed, and old members
must refresh their skills before their knowledge fades.

| Session type | Frequency | Who attends |
|--------------|-----------|-------------|
| Full hands-on session | At least **2 per year** (minimum), one before the summer peak | All members, especially any not yet trained |
| Refresher | At least **1 per year** for all trained members | All trained members |
| New-member session | Within **30 days** of joining the colony | New member and household adults |
| On-demand session | On request of any member, within 14 days | The requesting member and any helpers |

## 2. THE 30-DAY RULE FOR NEW MEMBERS

1. A new household receives water from the date of enrolment
   (`01-MEMBERSHIP/01-Enrollment/`).
2. At least one adult of the new household must complete operation training
   **within 30 days** of that date.
3. Until trained, the household's duty obligation is covered by buy-out; the
   household is not silently excused.
4. The membership registry records the date of each member's first training.

## 3. SESSION FORMAT

1. **Classroom part (20 minutes):** the Operation Handbook
   (`03-Operation-Handbook.md`) is read and explained line by line.
2. **Hands-on part (30 minutes):** every attendee switches the motor on and off
   at least once, under supervision, and fills a practice duty log entry.
3. **Fault drill (10 minutes):** attendees identify fault indicators and
   practise the "stop and report" rule.
4. Each attendee signs the session attendance register.

## 4. RECORD OF TRAINED MEMBERS

1. A **training register** records: date of session, trainer, attendees,
   topic covered, and each attendee's signature.
2. The register is kept with the duty log register and is a public document.
3. A summary list of "trained members" is displayed on the notice board and
   used to build the backup list (`04-Duty-Failure/02-Immediate-Replacement-System.md`).
4. A member whose training was more than 3 years ago is shown as "needs
   refresher" on the list.

## 5. WHO TRAINS

1. Any trained member may conduct a session; no single "official trainer" may
   be the only trainer, because that person becomes a single point of failure.
2. Sessions are scheduled so that **at least two trainers** can run them, so a
   trainer's illness never cancels a session.
3. A trainer may claim time-credits for conducting sessions, at the published
   rate (`03-Duty-Compensation/01-Time-Credits.md`).

## 6. MISSED SESSIONS

1. A member who misses a scheduled session is given a make-up session within 14
   days.
2. Missing two sessions without a documented reason moves the member to the
   buy-out track and to `04-Duty-Failure/03-Chronic-Avoidance-Measures.md`.
3. No member is ever denied water for missing a session; the consequence is
   on the duty/compensation track only.

## 7. SCENARIO

The colony's most experienced member — the only person who ever repaired the
panel — announces he is migrating. The assembly does not panic: two refresher
sessions are held within a month, every member re-practises the operation, and
the colony now has a trained base that never depended on him alone. His
knowledge has been transferred to the register and the handbook, and the system
runs on.
