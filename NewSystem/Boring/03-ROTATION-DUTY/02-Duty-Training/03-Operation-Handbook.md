## 1. PURPOSE AND STATUS

This handbook is the **plain-language, step-by-step** guide to operating the
motor. A trained member should be able to follow it even after years without
practice. A printed copy is kept inside the motor room and one in the duty box,
so the operator never depends on memory.

## 2. BEFORE SWITCHING ON — THE FOUR CHECKS

1. **Check the water need:** confirm today's schedule from the published roster
   and the duty log entry.
2. **Check the tank/bore:** verify the overhead tank has room and the boring's
   water level reading is within normal range on the gauge.
3. **Check the panel:** the breaker is in the ON position, no fault light is
   glowing, no burning smell.
4. **Check the room:** motor room is dry, no standing water near the panel, no
   wire exposed, no sign of tampering.

If any check fails: **do not switch on.** Note the fault in the log and report
through the maintenance channel (`05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/`).

## 3. START PROCEDURE

1. Open the motor room door with the current duty key
   (`05-Key-and-Control/01-Key-Holder-Rotation.md`).
2. Stand clear of the panel. Do not touch any wiring.
3. Turn the selector/switch to the ON position.
4. Observe for 30 seconds: the motor should start smoothly and the delivery
   pipe should begin to fill.
5. If the motor hums but does not start, or trips the breaker:
   **switch OFF immediately** and record the fault. Do not retry more than twice.

## 4. DURING THE RUN

1. Note the gauge reading at start and record it in the duty log entry.
2. Check the delivery line for leaks at the pipe joints.
3. Stay within earshot of the panel; the operator is responsible for the run.

## 5. OFF PROCEDURE

1. Switch the motor OFF at the panel.
2. Record the gauge reading and the total run time.
3. Note the water level after the run.
4. Verify the fault indicator is not lit.
5. Lock the motor room door and confirm the lock clicks.

## 6. FAULT INDICATORS — STOP AND REPORT

| Indicator | Likely meaning | Operator action |
|-----------|----------------|-----------------|
| Breaker trips | Overload / short circuit | Switch off, report, do not reset repeatedly |
| Burning smell | Motor overheating / winding damage | Switch off, report, do not operate |
| No water but motor runs | Dry run / air lock / bore failure | Switch off within 60 seconds, report |
| Unusual grinding noise | Bearing / impeller problem | Switch off, report |
| Gauge reading abnormal | Bore or tank problem | Note, report, do not adjust settings |
| Leaking starter | Electrical fault | Switch off, report, keep children away |

The operator's job is to **observe, record, and report** — never to "fix" the
panel. Repairs belong to the maintenance process, which has its own paid,
rotating roles.

## 7. THE RULES THAT CANNOT BE BROKEN

1. Never bypass the breaker or starter.
2. Never run the motor dry for more than a minute.
3. Never open the panel with the power on.
4. Never hand the keys to a non-operator.
5. Never "adjust" the schedule without recording it.
6. When in doubt: stop, lock the room, record, report.

## 8. SCENARIO

A young member doing her first solo duty switches on, hears a hum, and the
breaker trips. She follows the handbook: switches off, records "breaker tripped
twice, no start, no smell", locks the room, and reports on the maintenance
channel within 15 minutes. The system works exactly as designed — she protected
the motor and produced the record the repair team needs.
