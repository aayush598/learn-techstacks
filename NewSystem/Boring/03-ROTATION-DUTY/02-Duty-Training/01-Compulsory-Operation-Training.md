# 01 - COMPULSORY OPERATION TRAINING

**Folder:** `03-ROTATION-DUTY/02-Duty-Training/`  
**Cross-reference:** `00-CONSTITUTION/03-Fundamental-Duties/02-Duty-to-Learn-and-Participate.md`, `02-Training-Schedule.md`, `03-Operation-Handbook.md`

---

## 1. WHY TRAINING IS COMPULSORY

A system with no single point of failure is only as strong as the number of
people who can operate it. If only three members know how to switch the motor,
those three are the new rulers — whether they want to be or not. **Every adult
member must therefore complete operation training before they take their first
duty.** Training is not a certificate of merit; it is the equal distribution of
power.

## 2. WHAT TRAINING COVERS

Training covers exactly what duty requires (`00-Duty-System-Overview.md`, Section 2):

1. Safe switching on and off of the motor.
2. Reading the gauge and the control panel indicators.
3. Filling in the duty log entry and the motor run log.
4. Recognising and reporting faults (burning smell, unusual noise, leaking
   starter, low discharge).
5. Knowing what NOT to touch (breaker settings, starter adjustments, wiring).
6. Using the notification channel to announce the day's run.

## 3. WHO MUST TRAIN

1. Every adult member of every connected household must complete training
   within the timeframe set by `02-Training-Schedule.md`.
2. Where a household has multiple adult members, each adult trains. At least
   one adult per household **must** be trained; if more than one trains, all of
   them become eligible for duty and the household's roster load is shared.
3. Tenants and family members who will operate duty must also train.

## 4. EXEMPTION — DOCUMENTED MEDICAL HARDSHIP ONLY

1. A member is exempt from operating duty only with a **written medical
   certificate** or a CRB decision, filed in the member's file
   (`01-MEMBERSHIP/04-Member-Registry/`).
2. Exemption is for the duration stated in the document, after which the member
   returns to training and duty.
3. An exempt member does **not** stop contributing. They pay the buy-out per
   `01-Duty-Roster/01-Roster-Design.md`, Section 5, so their share of work is
   still covered and still compensated to whoever does it.
4. Exemption is never a punishment and never a privilege. It is a documented
   fact about the member's body.

## 5. THE BUY-OUT ALTERNATIVE FOR THE EXEMPT AND THE REFUSING

| Situation | Path |
|-----------|------|
| Medically exempt | Buy-out at 1.5x stipend value, compulsory, recorded. |
| Able but refuses to train | Offered make-up training twice. If still refusing, placed on buy-out and addressed under `04-Duty-Failure/03-Chronic-Avoidance-Measures.md`. Never loses water without due process. |
| Able, trained, but refuses a specific duty | Covered by missed-duty and swap rules; this is a duty failure, not a training issue. |

## 6. TRAINING GIVES POWER, NOT DEPENDENCE

Training converts a member from a **recipient of charity** ("will someone
switch the motor for me?") into an **operator with full capability**. After
training, no member ever has to beg another person for water. If the schedule
fails, they can act within the rules. This is the practical meaning of P10
(dignity) in the duty system.

## 7. SCENARIO

A new family joins the colony. The husband works long hours and the wife is
expected to manage the household alone. She is offered training at the next
Saturday session. She completes it, takes her first duty with a buddy
(`02-Training-Schedule.md`), and from then on operates independently. Nobody
controls her family's water; her skills are now common knowledge.
