# 01 - AVAILABLE SCHEMES

**Folder:** `08-GOVERNMENT-INTERACTION/02-Grants-and-Subsidies/`  
**Cross-reference:** `00-Government-Scope.md`, `02-Application-Process.md`, `03-Subsidy-Fund-Handling.md`

---

## 1. WHAT THIS DOCUMENT IS AND IS NOT

This document describes the **categories and process** of government schemes
that a colony water system may access. It deliberately does **not** state
specific amounts, deadlines, or scheme names as fixed facts — schemes change,
rename, and re-announce every budget cycle. **The current facts must always be
verified against the official notice** before any application, and the verified
copy becomes the governing document.

## 2. THE GENERAL CATEGORIES

| Category | What it typically covers | Where it comes from |
|----------|--------------------------|---------------------|
| State/UT water board schemes | Boring, motor, pipeline, tank, and their repairs; rural/urban drinking water supply | State water supply board, rural/urban development departments |
| MLA/MP (elected representative) funds | One-time grants for community assets like a borewell, pump set, or tank | The elected representative's local area development fund |
| Urban water/sanitation schemes | Augmentation, metering, infrastructure in municipalities | Municipal corporation / urban development authority |
| Rural drinking water schemes | Borewells, hand pumps, solar pumping, piped water to habitations | Rural water supply department / panchayat |
| Borewell/motor subsidy schemes | Partial reimbursement or subsidised purchase of motor, submersible, panel | Agriculture, minor irrigation, or water department (scheme-dependent) |
| Solar pump schemes | Subsidised solar pumping for irrigation/drinking water | Ministry/state renewable energy agency, agriculture department |
| Jal Jeevan Mission and similar national missions | Household tap connections, source sustainability | Central-state mission implementing agencies |

## 3. THE PROCESS, GENERICALLY

Whatever the scheme, the same disciplined process applies:

1. **Verify** the scheme on the official source (department website, gazette,
   district office). Do not trust forwarded WhatsApp messages.
2. **Check eligibility** for a community water asset in a colony context.
3. **Assemble documents** from the government file
   (`01-Government-Motor/01-Document-Retention.md`).
4. **Pass an assembly resolution** authorising the application
   (`02-Application-Process.md`).
5. **Apply** through the official channel with the two-signatory rule.
6. **Track** the application in the grants register until the outcome.
7. **Handle** approved funds per `03-Subsidy-Fund-Handling.md`.

## 4. THE ASSEMBLY DECIDES — NOT ONE PERSON

1. **Only the General Assembly** may decide that the colony will apply for a
   scheme. An individual member may propose it, but no member may apply "for
   the colony" on their own name without a resolution.
2. The reason is simple: a scheme in one person's name is a scheme in that
   person's control — the old system, funded by the state.
3. If a member has a personal scheme (e.g., their own land), that is their
   private matter and is not the colony's business.

## 5. DUE DILIGENCE BEFORE APPLYING

1. Read the scheme's **eligibility and conditions**; note any that conflict
   with the constitution (e.g., a scheme that requires a "single owner" name —
   the assembly must decide how to reconcile this openly, not quietly).
2. Note recurring obligations (reporting, maintenance clauses, lock-in
   periods) — a "free" motor that binds the colony for ten years is not free.
3. The record keeper files the scheme notice, the application, and the
   eligibility analysis in the government file.

## 6. SCENARIO

A member forwards a message about a "solar pump subsidy scheme" and says he can
"get it done fast". The assembly does not apply on a WhatsApp forward. The
record keeper verifies the scheme on the department website, confirms the
eligibility for the colony's case, and brings a one-page summary to the
assembly. The assembly passes a resolution to apply. The fast-talker's offer
was not needed — the process, not a connection, is what wins.
