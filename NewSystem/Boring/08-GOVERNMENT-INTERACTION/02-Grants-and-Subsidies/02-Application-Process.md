# 02 - APPLICATION PROCESS

**Folder:** `08-GOVERNMENT-INTERACTION/02-Grants-and-Subsidies/`  
**Cross-reference:** `01-Available-Schemes.md`, `03-Subsidy-Fund-Handling.md`, `02-GOVERNANCE/03-Decision-Making/`

---

## 1. A GRANT IS PUBLIC MONEY

Government money is public money, and a subsidy won in the colony's name is
held for the whole colony. The application process exists to guarantee that:
(1) only the assembly can authorise an application, (2) no person benefits
personally from the process, and (3) every step is documented.

## 2. STEP 1 — ASSEMBLY RESOLUTION

1. The proposed scheme and application are presented to the General Assembly
   with a one-page summary: what it is, what the colony must pay, what the
   colony must do later (reporting, maintenance).
2. The assembly passes a **resolution** authorising the application, by the
   majority rules of `02-GOVERNANCE/03-Decision-Making/`.
3. The resolution names the scheme, the amount applied for (if known), and the
   two signatories.
4. Without a resolution, no application is filed in the colony's name.

## 3. STEP 2 — THE TWO-SIGNATORY RULE

1. Every application and every document submitted to a scheme is signed by
   **two members from different households** — never one person's signature.
2. The two signatories are named in the resolution and rotated each application
   so the same pair does not become a "signing office".
3. The signatories are agents of the assembly, not owners of the grant.

## 4. STEP 3 — DOCUMENTS AND RECORDS

1. The record keeper assembles the supporting documents from the government
   file and the member registry (proof of connections, resolution copy, etc.).
2. **Two complete copies** of the application set are kept: one for the file,
   one digital backup.
3. The application is entered in the **grants register** with: scheme, date,
   reference number from the department, amount, and the names of the
   signatories.

## 5. STEP 4 — SUBMISSION AND TRACKING

1. Submission uses the official channel: the department portal (save the
   application ID), registered post, or counter submission with a stamped
   received copy.
2. Follow-up happens on the same timeline as complaints
   (`01-Government-Motor/02-Complaint-Channel.md`, Section 5): reminders at 15
   and 30 days, escalation at 60.
3. Any reply from the department is entered in the grants register the same
   day.

## 6. NO PERSONAL BENEFIT

1. A member connected with the scheme process may not take the grant, the
   subsidy amount, or the equipment in their personal name.
2. Any "processing fee" charged by an intermediary is presumed to be a bribe
   and is refused; the colony applies directly.
3. A member who claims the subsidy for their own household while the colony
   paid for the application is reported to the assembly and dealt with under
   `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/`.

## 7. SCENARIO

A "consultant" offers to file the subsidy application for a fee and a "cut of
the sanction". The assembly refuses: the application is filed directly, with
two signatories, on the official portal, and the application ID is entered in
the grants register. The consultant's fee disappears, the subsidy arrives
untouched, and every member can trace the process from resolution to sanction.
