# 00 - GOVERNMENT SCOPE

**Folder:** `08-GOVERNMENT-INTERACTION/`  
**Cross-reference:** `00-CONSTITUTION/00-MASTER-CONSTITUTION.md`, `11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/01-Binding-Agreement/`

---

## 1. THE SYSTEM LIVES INSIDE GOVERNMENT RULES

The colony water system is a community arrangement, but it is **not above the
law**. The boring, the motor, the electricity connection, and the land may be
owned or regulated by the state, the municipality, the panchayat, or the state
water board. Every rule in this constitution applies **within** the framework
of those government rules, never against them.

## 2. WHEN THIS FOLDER APPLIES

This folder governs the colony's dealings with government authorities. It
applies in every one of these cases:

| Situation | What applies |
|-----------|--------------|
| The boring/motor is **fully government-owned** | `01-Government-Motor/` — the colony operates a government asset; document retention, complaints, RTI, and visit rules all apply. |
| The boring is **partly government-funded** (subsidy, MLA/MP funds, scheme money) | `02-Grants-and-Subsidies/` — the funds are public money and come with conditions. |
| The boring is **fully private** but the colony interacts with government (electricity connection, permission, complaints) | The general rules in this folder still govern how the colony speaks and appears before authorities. |
| The boring is private but receives **government support** of any kind | `03-Private-Boring-with-Government-Support/01-Hybrid-Arrangements.md` merges the two rulebooks. |

## 3. WHY GOVERNMENT INTERACTION MUST BE COLLECTIVE

In the old system, one person "knew the government people". That person could:

- Hide official letters and the real reason water was cut.
- Claim they "handled it" while doing nothing.
- Take the subsidy or grant in their own name.
- Negotiate privately with officials for personal benefit.

This folder ends all of that. Government interaction follows the same
constitutional logic as the keys and the roster: **no single person owns the
relationship.**

## 4. GOVERNMENT DOCUMENTS ARE PART OF THE SYSTEM'S RECORDS

1. Every government paper — sanction letter, allocation order, bill, circular,
   scheme notice — is a system record once it concerns the colony's water.
2. Such papers are filed under `01-Government-Motor/01-Document-Retention.md`
   and are public to every member.
3. A document hidden from members is a document treated as stolen.

## 5. THE THREE ABSOLUTES

1. **Truth:** the colony gives authorities true facts and never instructs a
   member to lie for the colony.
2. **Transparency:** every government communication is logged and readable by
   all members.
3. **No single point of failure:** any member may send an authorised complaint
   (`01-Government-Motor/02-Complaint-Channel.md`), and no member may claim to
   be "the only one who can talk to the government".

## 6. SCOPE AND ORDER OF READING

1. `01-Government-Motor/` — when the motor belongs to the government.
2. `02-Grants-and-Subsidies/` — public money, correctly applied and handled.
3. `03-Private-Boring-with-Government-Support/` — mixed ownership.
4. `04-Disputes-with-Government/` — when the government itself misbehaves.
