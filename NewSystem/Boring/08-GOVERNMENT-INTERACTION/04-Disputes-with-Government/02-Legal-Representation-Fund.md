# 02 - LEGAL REPRESENTATION FUND

**Folder:** `08-GOVERNMENT-INTERACTION/04-Disputes-with-Government/`  
**Cross-reference:** `01-Documented-Appeals.md`, `06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/`, `00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/02-Right-to-Information-and-Audit.md`

## 1. WHY THE COLONY NEEDS A LEGAL OPTION

Most disputes with the government are won with documents and patience. But some
are not: a wrong that is lawful-looking on paper, an authority that ignores
every appeal, or a matter where the department's own rules are being bent
against the colony. For those, the colony needs the ability to engage a lawyer
and, if the matter warrants it, to approach a court — **without each time
begging members for money or trusting one member to "manage the case".**

## 2. THE FUND

1. A small **legal representation fund** is maintained as a marked sub-fund of
   the common fund (`06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/`).
2. The fund is built by a small monthly earmark set by the assembly and is
   never merged with the general balance in the accounts.
3. The target size is set quarterly; once reached, the earmark pauses until the
   fund is spent.
4. The fund is not for personal lawsuits of members; it exists only for matters
   that concern the colony's water system.

## 3. HOW LEGAL ACTION IS AUTHORISED

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | The matter must have exhausted (or be documented as having passed) the appeal chain (`01-Documented-Appeals.md`). |
| 2 | The record keeper prepares a one-page case summary: the facts, the documents, the appeals filed, the legal question. |
| 3 | The **General Assembly** debates and passes a resolution authorising legal action and the spending limit. |
| 4 | The resolution names the lawyer or a transparent selection process, and two signatories for the engagement. |
| 5 | Every expense and every document is entered in the case file and reported to the assembly. |

No member may hire a lawyer "for the colony" without this resolution. The
resolution is what makes the action collective instead of personal.

## 4. CHOOSING REPRESENTATION TRANSPARENTLY

1. The lawyer is chosen by an open process: quotations from at least two
   advocates, verified qualifications, no relatives of members unless the
   assembly explicitly consents and the member declares the relation.
2. The engagement letter states the fees, the scope, and the reporting duty
   (the lawyer reports to the assembly's named members, never to one person
   alone).
3. Legal advice given to the colony is filed in the case file; it is not a
   secret counsel kept by one member.

## 5. USE OF THE FUND

1. Fees, court fees, drafting and notary costs, and travel of the two
   signatories to hearings are payable from the fund.
2. The fund is **not** for paying officials, bribes, or "expediting" anyone.
   Such a payment is a serious violation and grounds for the strictest
   measures under `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/`.
3. If the fund is exhausted, the assembly decides whether to replenish or
   stop; the case file must show the decision and the reason.

## 6. SCENARIO

The department refuses to restore a water allocation for a second year, and
every appeal is answered with silence. The colony holds an assembly: the case
summary shows a complete, dated appeal trail. The assembly passes a resolution
authorising a writ petition and sets a spending limit from the legal fund. Two
quotations are compared, an advocate is engaged by the named signatories, and
the case progresses with full reporting. The colony's pushback is collective,
funded, and documented — not one person's gamble.
