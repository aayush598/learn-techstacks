# 01 - DOCUMENTED APPEALS

**Folder:** `08-GOVERNMENT-INTERACTION/04-Disputes-with-Government/`  
**Cross-reference:** `01-Government-Motor/02-Complaint-Channel.md`, `01-Government-Motor/03-RTI-and-Petitions.md`, `02-Legal-Representation-Fund.md`, `00-Government-Scope.md`

## 1. WHEN THE GOVERNMENT IS THE PROBLEM

The government can act wrongly: it refuses a repair that is clearly owed, it
cuts the water allocation without notice, it denies a subsidy it approved, or
it simply does nothing for months. The colony must have a way to push back
that does not depend on one angry member shouting at an office window. That
way is the **documented appeal**.

## 2. THE CORE PRINCIPLE

**Every step is written, dated, and filed.** An oral complaint is not an
appeal. A written trail is the only thing that survives the transfer of an
officer, the closure of an office, or the passage of a year.

## 3. THE APPEAL CHAIN

| Step | Action | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Written complaint (portal / registered post) | Use the complaint channel (`01-Government-Motor/02-Complaint-Channel.md`) |
| 2 | First reminder to the same officer | 15 days after filing |
| 3 | Copy the officer's superior | 30 days, or on the first refusal |
| 4 | Formal representation / appeal to the appellate authority | 60 days, citing the complaint reference and replies |
| 5 | Petition to the elected representative (MLA/MP/corporator) | With the full written record attached |
| 6 | RTI to force the record | Where the issue is a missing or hidden document (`01-Government-Motor/03-RTI-and-Petitions.md`) |
| 7 | Legal action through the representation fund | Only by assembly resolution (`02-Legal-Representation-Fund.md`) |

## 4. THE APPEAL FILE

1. Every step above creates a folder in the government file: complaint, all
   reminders, replies, the refusal order (if any), the appeal, and the final
   decision.
2. A one-page **timeline summary** sits on top of the folder: date, step,
   document ID, outcome.
3. The appeal file is a public document. Any member can read it and see exactly
   where the matter stands.

## 5. ESCALATION RULES

1. Escalation is **automatic by date**, not by emotion. If the complaint is 15
   days old with no reply, the reminder goes out — no meeting required.
2. A member may propose to skip a step (e.g., go straight to the MLA) only
   with an assembly resolution; the record must show why the chain was
   shortened.
3. The colony never threatens, abuses, or bribes officials. The appeal is in
   documents, not in threats. P10 applies to officials too.

## 6. SCENARIO

The department refuses to repair the colony's sanctioned motor, saying it is
"not in this year's plan". The colony files a complaint (portal ID recorded).
At 15 days, a reminder. At 30 days, a copy to the Executive Engineer. At 60
days, a formal representation with the 2019 sanction letter and the refusal
photocopied. At 75 days, the repair is ordered. The whole trail is in the
appeal file — if it had failed, the file would be the evidence for the next
step, not a memory of a fight.
