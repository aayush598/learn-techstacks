# 02 - COMPLAINT CHANNEL

**Folder:** `08-GOVERNMENT-INTERACTION/01-Government-Motor/`  
**Cross-reference:** `03-RTI-and-Petitions.md`, `04-Disputes-with-Government/01-Documented-Appeals.md`, `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/02-Resolution-Process/`

## 1. WHY A FORMAL CHANNEL EXISTS

Government services fail: the motor breaks and the department delays, the
allocation is cut, the electricity connection faults. When the motor is
government-owned, the colony's only real power is a **written, dated, traceable
complaint trail**. A verbal complaint to "the officer" is worth nothing in a
month's time — no date, no name, no proof.

## 2. WHO MAY COMPLAIN

1. **Any member** may file a complaint on behalf of the colony. Nobody owns the
   channel; a member who is told "only the committee can complain" may point to
   this rule.
2. A member filing a complaint must inform the CRB/record keeper within 48
   hours so the complaint is entered in the register (radical transparency).
3. The assembly may designate a **complaint co-ordinator** for the year, but
   that is a convenience, not a monopoly — the co-ordinator cannot refuse a
   member's complaint.

## 3. THE COMPLAINT PROCEDURE

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Write the complaint: facts, dates, documents referenced, what remedy is sought. |
| 2 | Get it forwarded: the record keeper adds the colony's document references. |
| 3 | Send it through a **traceable channel**: registered post with acknowledgement, the official online portal (with the portal ID saved), or hand delivery with a stamped copy retained. |
| 4 | Record it in the complaint register: date, channel, reference number, copies filed. |
| 5 | Follow up in writing at fixed intervals (below). |

## 4. THE COMPLAINT REGISTER

Every complaint is entered in the **complaint register** with:

1. Complaint ID, date, and subject.
2. The authority and office addressed.
3. The channel used (portal ID / post receipt / hand-delivery stamp).
4. The member who filed it.
5. The follow-up log: each written reminder, its date, and any reply.
6. The outcome.

The register is a public document and is legal evidence under
`04-Disputes-with-Government/01-Documented-Appeals.md`.

## 5. FOLLOW-UP TIMELINE

| Time after filing | Action |
|-------------------|--------|
| 15 days | First written reminder (portal or letter) |
| 30 days | Second reminder, copied to the officer's superior |
| 60 days | Escalation under `04-Disputes-with-Government/01-Documented-Appeals.md` |
| Any reply | Copied into the register the same day it is received |

No complaint is ever "dropped because it got awkward". If a member stops
following up, the register shows it and the record keeper takes over.

## 6. SCENARIO

The department-promised repair is three weeks late. A young member files the
complaint on the official portal, saves the portal ID, and enters it in the
register. When the officer claims "no complaint was received", the colony shows
the portal ID, the follow-up letters, and the register — all dated. The repair
is scheduled within a week. The channel worked because it was written,
traceable, and open to every member.
