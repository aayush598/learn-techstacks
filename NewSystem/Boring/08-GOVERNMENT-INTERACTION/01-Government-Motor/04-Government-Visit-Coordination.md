# 04 - GOVERNMENT VISIT COORDINATION

**Folder:** `08-GOVERNMENT-INTERACTION/01-Government-Motor/`  
**Cross-reference:** `01-Document-Retention.md`, `00-Government-Scope.md`, `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/`

---

## 1. WHY VISITS MUST BE COLLECTIVE

Officials come to inspect: the boring, the motor, the accounts, the scheme
conditions. If one member receives them alone, that member can be charmed,
pressured, or bribed privately — and whatever was agreed stays in that one
person's head. **No member ever meets an official alone on the colony's
business.**

## 2. THE MINIMUM RULES

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Two or more members receive the official | At least 2 members, from different households; one is a note-taker. |
| Prior notice to the colony | Any member who learns of a planned visit announces it at least 24 hours ahead, or immediately if short-notice. |
| A written record | Minutes of the visit are drafted within 48 hours and read out at the next assembly. |
| No one negotiates alone | Offers made or accepted by a single member are not binding on the colony. |
| No cash at visits | Payments, if any, are made by the common fund process only, never by hand to an official on the spot. |

## 3. BEFORE THE VISIT

1. The receiving members review the government file (`01-Document-Retention.md`)
   so they know the history: sanction, allocation, pending complaints.
2. The note-taker prepares a blank visit minutes form (template in
   `12-TEMPLATES/`).
3. The members agree on what the colony will ask and what it will not accept.

## 4. DURING THE VISIT

1. The official is shown the actual system: motor room, panel, gauge, and the
   duty log register if the visit concerns operation.
2. The note-taker records: who came, designation, what was inspected, what was
   said by both sides, what was promised, and any documents shown.
3. Requests from the official (documents, signatures, favours) are recorded and
   answered **by the colony**, not by one member's nod.
4. If the official asks to speak to "the head of the colony", the members reply:
   "There is no head. We are all members; you are with two of us now, and every
   word is written down."

## 5. AFTER THE VISIT

1. The minutes are finalised within 48 hours and signed by both receiving
   members.
2. The minutes are read at the next assembly and filed in the government file.
3. Any promise by the official is entered in the complaint/visit register with
   a follow-up date; silence about a promise is how promises die.
4. If the visit exposed a problem (deteriorating motor, account question), the
   maintenance or finance process picks it up within 7 days.

## 6. SCENARIO

An assistant engineer arrives unannounced and asks to inspect the motor room,
suggesting he can "arrange a subsidy" if the colony is cooperative — and asks
for a private word with the "senior member". The two receiving members show him
the motor, take notes on his subsidy claim, politely refuse the private word,
and record the visit. At the assembly, the minutes are read. No deal was made
in a corridor; the claim is now a public document to be verified — or
forgotten, which is itself a recorded fact.
