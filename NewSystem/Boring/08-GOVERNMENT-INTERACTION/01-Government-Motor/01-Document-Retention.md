## 1. WHY GOVERNMENT PAPERS MATTER

When the motor belongs to the government, the colony's entire relationship with
the state rests on documents: who sanctioned the boring, what allocation was
approved, what repairs the department owes, what the electricity connection
covers. **A paper that is lost is a right that is lost.** Keeping every
government paper is therefore a constitutional duty, not a clerical chore.

## 2. THE GOVERNMENT FILE

A dedicated **government file** holds every paper that concerns the colony:

1. Sanction and allocation letters (boring, motor, pipeline, tank).
2. The connection number and bill records for electricity.
3. Repair and maintenance claims submitted to the department.
4. Department circulars and scheme notices relevant to the colony.
5. Correspondence with officers (letters in, copies of letters out).
6. Receipts, vouchers, and fee challans connected to government dealings.

## 3. WHAT IS COPIED AND BACKED UP

1. Every document in the file is **photographed or scanned** and stored as a
   digital copy on the colony's shared archive.
2. The digital copy is backed up in **two places** (e.g., a shared drive and a
   second device) so a lost phone or burnt house does not destroy the record.
3. The original paper and the digital copy are reconciled quarterly.

## 4. WHO KEEPS THE ORIGINALS — ROTATING KEEPER

1. The originals are held by a **rotating keeper**, appointed for one year by
   the assembly, with a **backup keeper** from a different household.
2. The keeper's name, address, and the file inventory are recorded in the
   document register.
3. The keeper is not the file's owner — they are its custodian. Any member may
   inspect the file at a scheduled time, and no member may be refused a look.
4. On handover, the outgoing and incoming keepers count every document against
   the inventory and sign the transfer in the register.

## 5. THE DOCUMENT REGISTER

| Field | Example |
|-------|---------|
| Document ID | GOV-2026-014 |
| Date of document | 12 Mar 2026 |
| Source | Water Board, Assistant Engineer's office |
| Subject | Allocation of 2 HP motor, sanction letter |
| Original kept by | Rotating keeper (household 9) |
| Digital copy | Archive folder + backup device |
| Notes | Letter also sent to MR with complaint |

The register is public and is itself backed up.

## 6. RULES OF HANDLING

1. Originals **never leave the colony** for a single person's errand; copies
   are taken for submissions, and the original stays in the file unless the
   authority legally requires the original, which is then noted and tracked.
2. A member who loses, damages, or quietly removes a government document is
   reported to the assembly; the incident is logged, and a re-issue is sought
   immediately.
3. When a document is missing, the loss is announced within 7 days so the
   colony can reconstruct the paper before it is needed.

## 7. SCENARIO

The water board refuses to repair the motor, claiming "there is no record of
your allocation". The record keeper pulls the file: the 2019 sanction letter,
the 2021 motor handover memo, and the repair claim receipts are all present,
both in paper and in the digital archive. Copies are filed with the complaint
the same day. The department's claim collapses because the colony kept its
papers.
