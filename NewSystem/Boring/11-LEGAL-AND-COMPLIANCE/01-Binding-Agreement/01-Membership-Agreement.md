# BINDING AGREEMENT — MEMBERSHIP AGREEMENT

**Folder:** `11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/01-Binding-Agreement/`

---

## 1. WHY THIS FILE EXISTS

In the old system, membership was a handshake and a rumour: nobody had signed
anything, so every obligation — "you never told me," "we never agreed" — could
be denied. The Membership Agreement turns rights and duties into a signed,
kept, permanent record. It is the document that gives this whole resource its
legal grip (`00-Legal-Framework.md`).

## 2. WHO SIGNS AND HOW MANY COPIES

1. Every member — the registered connection holder — signs on admission and
   signs again to acknowledge amendments.
2. **Three copies:** one with the member, one in the records system
   (`03-Records-Retention/`), one in the member's registry file
   (`01-MEMBERSHIP/04-Member-Registry/`).
3. The member signs in the presence of two witnesses (members) with the
   Registry Keeper; date and connection ID are recorded.
4. A member who cannot read or hear has the agreement read and explained in a
   language they understand, and signs or thumb-prints with a witness note
   (P10 — dignity is not skipped at the signing table).

## 3. WHAT THE AGREEMENT CONTAINS

| Clause | Content |
|--------|---------|
| Acceptance of the constitution | All 10 Supreme Principles and the Master Constitution |
| Contribution duty | Monthly, buy-in, and project shares per the published formulas |
| Duties | Duty roster, honest logs, care of the network (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/`) |
| Water rights | Equal share, fair slots, protection from targeting |
| Records | Agreement that system records are public (P6) and the member's own records are retained on exit |
| Dispute path | Internal resolution, then arbitration, then court (`04-Dispute-Adjudication/`) |
| Exit | Refund and transfer rules per `01-MEMBERSHIP/03-Exit-and-Transfer/` |
| Compliance | Commitment to operate within Indian law (`00-Legal-Framework.md`) |

## 4. THE SIGNING PROCESS

| Step | Detail |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Member is given a copy to read at least 48 hours before signing |
| 2 | Two witnesses present; the Registry Keeper records date, time, connection ID |
| 3 | Member signs or thumb-prints each page (or the cover and last page) |
| 4 | A signed copy is handed to the member on the spot |
| 5 | Registry file and records copy are filed the same day |

No connection is issued without a signed agreement
(`09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/03-Late-Joiner-Buy-in/03-Access-Only-After-Buy-in.md`).

## 5. UPDATES AND AMENDMENT ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

1. When the constitution is amended (`00-CONSTITUTION/05-Amendment-Process/`),
   members acknowledge the change in writing within **60 days**.
2. Acknowledgment is recorded against the member's file; a member who refuses
   to acknowledge is asked to comply or to exit through the exit rules — but an
   amendment never strips a basic right, and no member is forced to sign
   anything that violates the basic structure (`00-CONSTITUTION/00-MASTER-CONSTITUTION.md`).

## 6. WHAT THE AGREEMENT DOES NOT DO

- It does not make a member surrender the right to go to court.
- It does not authorise the system to punish a member outside the published
  due-process ladder.
- It does not convert contributions into ownership of the boring.

## 7. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

A member later claims, "I never agreed to pay for the new motor." The signed
Membership Agreement shows the funding rule was accepted, and the minutes show
the project resolution. The claim fails on record — not because the member was
silenced, but because the paper existed.

member: bound by duties, protected by rights, and able to prove both.*
