# BINDING AGREEMENT — WATER USAGE AGREEMENT

**Folder:** `11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/01-Binding-Agreement/`

---

## 1. WHY THIS FILE EXISTS

Rules of fair use exist throughout this resource, but without a signed
agreement every offender's defence is "I didn't know." The Water Usage
Agreement makes the rules explicit and signed. It is a condition of connection
and is signed together with the Membership Agreement.

## 2. WHAT THE AGREEMENT CONTAINS

| Rule | Content |
|------|---------|
| Fair use | Take your share in your slot; no waste; no hoarding beyond the slot |
| No tampering | No altering valves, slots, meters, seals, or logs |
| No selling water | Water is common property (P1); selling system water is a serious offence |
| No draining the system | No private tank fills for resale or unlimited storage beyond the slot |
| Duty cooperation | Access for meter reading and repairs within reasonable notice |
| Reporting | Duty to report leaks, tampering, and faults promptly |

## 3. PENALTIES — PUBLISHED, NOT INVENTED

| Offence | First occurrence | Repeated / serious |
|---------|------------------|--------------------|
| Waste / overdraw | Written warning + wastage charge | Fine per `06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/04-Penalties-and-Fines.md` |
| Minor tampering | Fine + repair at member's cost | Escalation |
| Selling water | Fine + disconnection procedure | Up to disconnection after due process |
| Persistent violation of any rule | Escalation | Per the Enforcement Ladder (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/`) |

No penalty is applied on the spot by an individual; every penalty follows the
published fine schedule and is recorded.

## 4. THE DISCONNECTION PROTOCOL — DUE PROCESS, NOT FURY

1. **Written notice** to the member, stating the violation and the evidence;
2. **7-day reply** window for the member;
3. On request, review by the Conflict Review Board (CRB)
   (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/02-Resolution-Process/`);
4. **Assembly decision by a two-thirds (2/3) vote** for disconnection;
5. **Restoration** on remedy plus payment of dues, within 48 hours.

Exceptions:
- A **safety emergency** (contamination, live wire, open main) allows a
  temporary cut with immediate public disclosure and a review within 48 hours.
- A member is never disconnected for a financial dispute alone without the
  full ladder — the right to water is protected
  (`00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/01-Right-to-Fair-Water.md`).

## 5. SIGNED WITH MEMBERSHIP

1. The Water Usage Agreement is signed with the Membership Agreement at
   admission; it binds the registered connection holder and the household.
2. It is re-signed only on amendment; routine schedule changes do not need new
   signatures, only published notice.

## 6. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

A member fills twenty drums every night to water a kitchen garden and a small
poultry shed. The tank level log shows the draw. Under the signed agreement
this is unauthorised use; the warning and the fine from the published schedule
follow, the log closes, and the drums stop — without a single argument about
who knew what.

## 7. NO SELLING WATER — THE HARD RULE

- System water may not be sold, bartered, or given as a private favour to
  non-members without assembly approval.
- A member caught selling water faces the full disconnection procedure; the
  proceeds, if recovered, go to the Common Fund.

---

*The agreement does not police the family's daily habits; it polices the lines
that keep water common — no tampering, no selling, no hoarding, and due process
before any cut.*
