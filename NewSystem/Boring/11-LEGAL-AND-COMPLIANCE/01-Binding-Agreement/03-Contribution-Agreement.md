# BINDING AGREEMENT — CONTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

**Folder:** `11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/01-Binding-Agreement/`

---

## 1. WHY THIS FILE EXISTS

"Pay whatever I say" was the old rule of the money-keeper. The Contribution
Agreement binds payments to **published formulas only**: a member pays what the
formula produces, nothing more, nothing less. It ends discretionary charges —
the "emergency collection" for a wedding, the "repair money" that becomes
pocket money.

## 2. WHAT THE AGREEMENT CONTAINS

| Clause | Content |
|--------|---------|
| Monthly contribution | By the published formula (`06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/01-Monthly-Contribution.md`) |
| Buy-in / project shares | By the published formulas (`09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/03-Late-Joiner-Buy-in/`) |
| Payment method | Bank transfer, UPI, or cheque; cash only against a numbered receipt — never to an individual |
| Late fees | By the published schedule (`06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/04-Penalties-and-Fines.md`) |
| Hardship | The member's right to apply for tiers or installments (P7) |
| Refund | By the published exit and refund procedure (`01-MEMBERSHIP/03-Exit-and-Transfer/03-Refund-Procedure.md`) |

## 3. NO DISCRETIONARY CHARGES

1. No member, officer, or committee may impose, waive, or discount any charge
   outside the published formulas.
2. A charge not produced by a formula is **void** — the member may refuse it
   without penalty.
3. A member who waives or discounts a formula charge for someone else, or
   invents one, commits a financial offence under the enforcement rules
   (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/`).
4. Only the General Assembly may create, change, or remove a charge, by the
   voting rule the formula requires (usually two-thirds)
   (`02-GOVERNANCE/03-Decision-Making/03-Supermajority-Rules.md`).

## 4. RECEIPTS AND RECORDS

1. Every payment by the member produces a numbered receipt or bank record
   (`06-FINANCE/03-Bookkeeping/03-Receipt-Requirement.md`).
2. The ledger is public (P6); a member may check their own balance and every
   member's dues at any time.

## 5. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

An office-holder demands ₹500 from every house "for the wedding of a committee
member's son." The Contribution Agreement has no formula that produces ₹500 for
a wedding. The charge is void; a member who refuses is not fined; and the
office-holder faces the enforcement rules for attempting a discretionary levy.

## 6. HARDSHIP — THE OTHER SIDE OF THE COIN

1. A member in genuine hardship applies for the hardship tier or an installment
   plan through the published path
   (`06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/02-Contribution-Tiers.md`).
2. The agreement expressly states that the system protects the weak (P7): dues
   are collected by formulas and deadlines, but a documented hardship is
   handled by published accommodation, never by distress or public shaming.

## 7. SIGNING AND DURATION

- Signed with the Membership and Water Usage Agreements at admission; one copy
  each for the member, the records, and the registry file.
- It binds while the member holds a connection and covers obligations that
  survive exit (unpaid dues, refund accounting).

*The formula is the only tax collector the colony ever has. The agreement
guarantees that no person — however powerful, however angry — can stand in its
place.*
