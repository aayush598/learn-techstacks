# LEGAL AND COMPLIANCE — LEGAL FRAMEWORK

**Folder:** `11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/`

---

## 1. WHAT THIS FOLDER IS

This whole resource is a **governance agreement** — a constitution-style
rulebook for how the colony runs its water system. It is not a law, a statute,
or a substitute for a lawyer or a court. Its practical force comes from one
simple thing: the members **sign agreements** that bind them to it.

## 2. WHERE THE SYSTEM'S LEGAL FORCE COMES FROM

1. The **Membership Agreement**, **Water Usage Agreement**, and **Contribution
   Agreement** (`01-Binding-Agreement/`) are written, signed, and kept. These
   create binding obligations between the member and the system and between
   members themselves, under the ordinary law of contract.
2. By signing, each member accepts the constitution, the published formulas,
   the duty roster, the records rules, and the dispute path — internal
   resolution, then arbitration, then court.
3. A member who refuses to sign is not a member and is not connected; a member
   who signs is bound by what they signed.

## 3. OPERATING WITHIN INDIAN LAW

1. Nothing in this resource asks anyone to break the law — the opposite: every
   rule is written to operate within Indian law.
2. Areas of Indian law that touch the system include:
   - **Contract law** — the signed agreements (Indian Contract Act);
   - **Water and groundwater** — state groundwater authority rules and permits
     for drilling/abstraction where they apply;
   - **Local authorities** — panchayat or municipal permissions, water-supply
     bylaws, and connections;
   - **Public safety and labour** — safe electrical installation, and fair
     treatment of hired workers;
   - **Taxation** — GST on purchases, and any registration that applies.
3. Where a law sets a higher or lower standard than this resource, **the law
   prevails**. The system then documents what it did to comply, so there is no
   conflict and no doubt.

## 4. LEGAL ADVICE

1. Every member may seek independent legal advice at any stage, at their own
   cost, and is never punished for doing so (`04-Dispute-Adjudication/03-Court-Last-Approach.md`).
2. The system keeps a list of accessible legal-aid contacts (P7) so that a
   member facing a genuine legal matter is not alone.
3. When a real dispute reaches that level, the system should consult a lawyer
   before acting, and the records made under `03-Records-Retention/` are the
   evidence the lawyer will use.

## 6. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

A pipe bursts and floods a member's house. The member threatens a court case.
The framework answers: the claim first follows the internal path
(`02-Liability-and-Indemnity/02-Property-Damage.md`), the records are produced
(`03-Records-Retention/`), arbitration is available if the claim is not settled
(`04-Dispute-Adjudication/02-Arbitration-Clause.md`). The member is free to go
to court — but every step is ready, documented, and fair, so the case is won or
lost on facts, not on who shouted.

## 7. RELATION TO THE CONSTITUTION

- This folder is subordinate to the Master Constitution
  (`00-CONSTITUTION/00-MASTER-CONSTITUTION.md`) and the Norm Hierarchy
  (`00-CONSTITUTION/06-Supremacy-and-Conflict/01-Norm-Hierarchy.md`).
- The constitution governs the system's internal affairs; the signed agreements
  give that internal law its external, enforceable grip.

---

*The system does not tell members they cannot go to law. It makes its own house
so orderly — signed agreements, clean records, fair procedures — that going to
law is a last resort and rarely a surprise.*
