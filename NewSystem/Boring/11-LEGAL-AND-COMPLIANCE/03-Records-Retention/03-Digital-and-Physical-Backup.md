# RECORDS RETENTION — DIGITAL AND PHYSICAL BACKUP

**Folder:** `11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/03-Records-Retention/`

---

## 1. WHY THIS FILE EXISTS

A single file drawer burns; a single phone with all the photos is lost; a
single laptop holds the only ledger and the only member list. The rule of this
file is one sentence: **no single copy.** Every important record exists in at
least two forms and in at least three places.

## 2. DUAL FORM — PHYSICAL AND DIGITAL

1. Every permanent and important record exists in **physical form** (signed
   agreements, register books, vouchers) and in **digital form** (scans and
   photos stored in the members' group and a shared drive).
2. A document that exists only physically can be lost to fire; a document that
   exists only digitally can be lost to a dead phone. Both forms, always.

## 3. BACKUP LOCATIONS — AT LEAST THREE

| Copy | Where |
|------|-------|
| 1 | Physical register at the meeting place |
| 2 | Second physical copy at a different house |
| 3 | Digital copies in the members' group / shared drive |
| 4 | Second digital copy on a rotating member's device |

**No two copies in the same building.** A fire, a flood, or a quarrel in one
house must never destroy the system's memory.

## 4. THE QUARTERLY BACKUP CHECK

Every quarter, the Records Keeper and one witness (drawn by lot) run the check:

| Check | Result |
|-------|--------|
| Physical files present and legible | |
| Digital files open and readable | |
| Latest records (minutes, ledger, photos) copied | |
| One copy moved to a new rotating location | |
| Check recorded with date and names | |

The check itself is logged — an unperformed backup check is a risk, not a
formality (P3).

## 5. WHAT MUST BE BACKED UP

Constitution and amendments, assembly minutes, member registry, signed
agreements, ledgers, vouchers, share ledger, asset register, contracts,
warranties, project files, duty and run logs, meter reading sheets and photos,
insurance policies and claim files.

## 6. DISASTER RECOVERY

| Disaster | Recovery |
|----------|----------|
| Meeting room fire destroys the register | Second physical copy + digital copies restore the ledger and registry within one week |
| Members' group deleted | The second digital copy and the physical files restore it |
| One member's device dies | The rotating second digital copy is used; the member's copy is refreshed |

The Records Keeper assigns a recovery owner after every disaster event and
logs the restoration.

## 7. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

A fire in the meeting room destroys the physical register and every voucher in
it. Because a second physical copy sits at another house and the digital scans
are in the shared drive, the ledger, the member list, and the buy-in records
are restored within a week. No one says "our history is gone" — because the
rules forbade a single copy.

---

*The system's memory must survive fire, flood, phone-loss, and betrayal. Three
copies, two forms, quarterly checks: that is how the collective remembers.*
