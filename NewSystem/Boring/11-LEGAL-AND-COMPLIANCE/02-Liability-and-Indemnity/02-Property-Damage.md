# LIABILITY AND INDEMNITY — PROPERTY DAMAGE

**Folder:** `11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/02-Liability-and-Indemnity/`

---

## 1. WHY THIS FILE EXISTS

A common pipe bursts and floods a member's house. In the old system the answer
was a shrug — "not my problem," "talk to the plumber," "maybe it was your
pipe." P7 says no victim is left alone. This file gives every member whose
property is damaged by the system a written claim path to compensation.

## 2. WHAT IS COVERED

| Damage | Example |
|--------|---------|
| Member's property, caused by system work or failure | Burst pipe flooding a house, water seeping through a wall, a trench collapsing a boundary wall |
| Third-party property caused by the system | A pedestrian's goods, a neighbour's compound damaged by a burst |
| Consequential loss tied to the incident | Food stocks lost to flooding, where reasonable and provable |

Damage caused **by the victim's own unauthorised action** — a member digging
into the main without approval, or a private pipe they modified bursting — is
not covered; it is the member's own cost, per the network rules
(`09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/04-Network-Improvements/03-Pipe-Replacement.md`).

## 3. THE CLAIM PROCESS

| Step | Deadline |
|------|----------|
| Victim reports in writing with photos | Within 7 days of the incident |
| Two members drawn by lot verify and record the damage (list, photos, two repair quotes) | Within 7 days of the report |
| Assessment presented to the assembly or liability sub-committee | Next meeting |
| Compensation decided and paid from the Common Fund and/or insurance | Within 30 days of the report |
| Appeal to the CRB, if the victim disagrees | Per `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/02-Resolution-Process/` |

## 4. HOW COMPENSATION IS DECIDED

1. The verified repair cost is based on **two written quotes**, not the
   victim's estimate alone and not the system's guess.
2. Depreciation applies to replaced goods (a 5-year-old cupboard is not
   compensated at new price) unless the assembly decides otherwise for
   hardship.
3. The decision and the figures are recorded and public (P6).

## 5. FUNDING AND INSURANCE

1. The Common Fund pays the verified compensation; if insurance covers the
   loss (`03-Insurance.md`), the claim is filed and the payout reimburses the
   fund.
2. **No victim is left alone:** if the insurer is slow and the victim is in
   hardship, the system advances the compensation from the fund and recovers it
   from the claim proceeds (P7).

## 6. RECORDS

- The claim file contains: the report, the verification sheet, photos, both
  repair quotes, the payment voucher, and the insurer correspondence.
- It is kept under the retention rules
  (`11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/03-Records-Retention/02-Retention-Periods.md`) and
  is available to every member.

## 7. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

A pipe joint fails and floods house B's godown, ruining stored grain. House B
reports the same day with photos. Two members verify and take two repair
quotes. The assembly approves compensation; the Common Fund pays it within 25
days; the insurer is claimed and repays the fund. House B is compensated by
record, not by favour.

## 8. RELATION TO OPERATOR LIABILITY

- If the failure traces to a duty operator, the operator-protection rule
  (`01-Operator-Protection.md`) decides whether the system or the operator
  bears the loss. The victim's claim is unaffected: the system pays the victim
  first, then settles internal responsibility.

---

*The system that caused the flood pays the flood. No victim is left to carry a
loss the collective made — that is what it means for water to be common
property with common responsibility.*
