# LIABILITY AND INDEMNITY — INSURANCE

**Folder:** `11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/02-Liability-and-Indemnity/`

---

## 1. WHY THIS FILE EXISTS

One big loss — a drowned motor, a flooded house, an electrocution — can break
the Common Fund and force a sudden collection. Insurance spreads that risk over
time at a small, predictable cost. This file makes insurance a routine budget
line, not a decision taken in the panic of a crisis.

## 2. WHAT THE SYSTEM INSURES

| Cover | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **Pump/motor set and panel** (with control gear) | Damage, fire, and theft of the main pumping asset |
| **Third-party liability** | Damage the system's assets cause to members' or neighbours' property |
| **Group personal-accident (optional)** | Duty operators who work on live electrical equipment — recommended |

The exact policy, sum insured, and insurer are decided by the assembly on the
comparison of 2-3 quotes, and the policy documents are filed in the records
(`03-Records-Retention/`).

## 3. PREMIUMS

1. Premiums are a **Common Fund budget line**, approved in the Annual Budget
   (`06-FINANCE/03-Bookkeeping/05-Annual-Budget.md`).
2. Renewals are in the records calendar so a policy is never silently lapsed.
3. A deliberate decision **not to insure** must be an assembly choice with
   reasons recorded — never an accidental gap.

## 4. THE CLAIM PROCEDURE — DOCUMENTED, NOT DISCOVERED

| Step | Detail |
|------|--------|
| 1. Incident | Log it, photograph it, the same day (`04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/04-Monitoring-and-Logs/`) |
| 2. Notify insurer | In writing within the policy window (usually 24-72 hours) |
| 3. Claim file | Two members + the operator prepare: policy number, incident report, photos, repair quotes |
| 4. Pursue | The claim is followed to a decision; status reported at the next assembly |
| 5. Credit | The payout is credited to the Common Fund / Reserve Fund, replacing what was spent |

A claim delayed is a claim denied — the procedure exists so the window is never
missed.

## 5. WHAT HAPPENS WHEN A CLAIM IS REJECTED

1. The rejection letter is kept in the claim file.
2. The CRB reviews whether the claim was correctly prepared; if so, the loss
   falls on the funds per the loss rules (`02-Property-Damage.md`,
   `01-Operator-Protection.md`).
3. The experience feeds the next policy choice: a product that refuses valid
   claims is marked and replaced at renewal.

## 6. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

The motor burns on a bad voltage day. The operator logged it, photos were
taken, and the insurer was notified the next morning. The claim file — policy,
log, photos, repair quote — produces a payout that tops up the Reserve Fund
within six weeks. The members paid a small premium; the fund never felt the
₹18,000 blow.

## 7. RELATION TO OTHER RULES

- Operator protection and insurance together cover the same incidents
  (`01-Operator-Protection.md`).
- The Asset Register's replacement-cost valuation sets the sum insured
  (`09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/05-Infrastructure-Asset-Register/02-Asset-Valuation.md`).

collect a lot from its own. The policy is a document; the claim file is its
