# DISPUTE ADJUDICATION — COURT LAST APPROACH

**Folder:** `11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/04-Dispute-Adjudication/`

---

## 1. WHY THIS FILE EXISTS

Courts are the slowest, costliest, and most divisive path available — but they
exist, and they are the final guardian when everything else fails. This file
orders the approach so the system uses courts as a **last resort**, never as a
first reaction — and guarantees that **no member is ever punished for using
them.**

## 2. COURTS ARE THE LAST RESORT — THE ORDER

1. **Internal resolution first** (`01-Internal-Exhaustion.md`) — the 45-day
   guarantee.
2. **Arbitration next** (`02-Arbitration-Clause.md`) — the binding middle rung.
3. **Court only after** internal steps and arbitration, except for crimes and
   genuine emergencies.

The system itself does not file a court case except:
- by an **assembly resolution** (two-thirds vote), and
- after internal and arbitration paths have been used or are inapplicable,
- or for a crime or emergency requiring immediate legal action.

## 3. NO PUNISHMENT FOR GOING TO COURT

1. A member who goes to court is **not disloyal**. The right to approach a
   court is every citizen's right under Indian law; this resource recognises it
   fully (`00-Legal-Framework.md`).
2. The system **never retaliates** against such a member: no water cuts, no
   fines, no extra charges, no secret discrimination, no campaign of exclusion.
   Retaliation is a violation of P7 and P10 and is handled by the enforcement
   rules (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/`).
3. The member remains entitled to their fair water share while the matter is
   pending, subject only to the normal enforcement rules for genuine,
   proven violations — never as punishment for litigating.

## 4. THE SYSTEM'S DEFENCE — SUPPORTED AND PUBLISHED

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| Legal costs of the system's side | Advanced from the Common Fund by a **two-thirds (2/3) assembly vote**, published |
| Lawyer | Appointed on 2-3 quotes, not by a member's relative |
| Member in a case against the system | May apply for hardship support per P7; the assembly decides in open session |

A defence funded by the collective is a defence of the collective — but it is
authorised in public, never by one office-holder's "arrangement."

## 5. EVIDENCE-READY RECORDS

Because of the retention rules (`03-Records-Retention/`), the system is always
ready:

1. On notice of any legal proceeding, the Records Keeper **freezes** the
   relevant files — no edits, no additions, no deletions.
2. A complete copy is prepared for the lawyer, with a numbered index.
3. The records — minutes, agreements, ledgers, logs, photos — are the case;
   the system wins or loses on the paper it kept, not on the influence it
   can buy.

## 6. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

A member whose grandfather funded the original boring sues claiming the boring
is his family's property. The court is his right — the system does not threaten,
cut his water, or demand he withdraw. Instead it answers with the founding
records, the signed Membership Agreement, and the ledger showing capital
repayments. The claim fails on documents, not on power — and the member keeps
his water throughout.

## 7. RELATION TO GOVERNMENT DISPUTES

- Disputes with the government or its agencies follow the same spirit:
  records first, dialogue first, escalation only when necessary
  (`08-GOVERNMENT-INTERACTION/04-Disputes-with-Government/`).

---

*The court is the floor of the ladder, not its ceiling over the colony. The
system keeps its house in order so that going to court is rare, prepared, and
never vengeful.*
