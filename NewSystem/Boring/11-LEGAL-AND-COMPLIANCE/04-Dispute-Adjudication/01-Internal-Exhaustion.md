# DISPUTE ADJUDICATION — INTERNAL EXHAUSTION

**Folder:** `11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/04-Dispute-Adjudication/`

---

## 1. WHY THIS FILE EXISTS

In the old system, the first reaction to a dispute was a police station or a
court — and the system collapsed into litigation while the strong won by
dragging the weak. This file makes **internal resolution the first step**: the
colony solves its own disputes through its own process before anyone walks
outside — with two honourable exceptions: crimes and genuine emergencies.

## 2. THE RULE — INTERNAL FIRST

1. A member with a system dispute must use the internal mechanisms before
   external proceedings.
2. The internal ladder:
   - informal discussion (one member speaks to the other, or to the duty team);
   - a **written grievance** filed with the CRB
     (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/02-Resolution-Process/`);
   - CRB review and hearing;
   - final internal answer, or reference to the assembly/jury per the
     resolution rules.
3. **External immediately, without internal steps, allowed only for:**
   - **crimes** — theft, assault, threats, trespass that is criminal;
   - **emergencies** — danger to life, contamination, serious damage.

## 3. THE 45-DAY GUARANTEE

The system must give a **final internal answer within 45 days** of a written
grievance. The clock is enforced by deadlines on every step:

| Step | Deadline from grievance |
|------|--------------------------|
| Acknowledge the grievance | 3 days |
| Informal discussion attempt | 7 days |
| Written review by CRB | 14 days |
| CRB hearing | 21 days |
| **Final internal answer** | **45 days** |

## 4. IF THE SYSTEM MISSES THE DEADLINE

1. The member may proceed externally after 45 days, with a written
   acknowledgment from the system that internal steps were exhausted (or that
   the system failed to answer).
2. The missing of the deadline is itself recorded and reviewed — a silent
   system has no defence for silence.

## 5. THE MEMBER IS NOT TO BLAME FOR ASKING

- Filing a grievance is a right, not an offence (P5, P10).
- No member may be penalised, mocked, or isolated for raising a dispute
  (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/03-Protection-Mechanisms/`).
- The member's duty is to follow the ladder; the system's duty is to answer in
  time.

## 6. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

A member's wall is flooded when a pipe bursts. He files a written grievance.
The system verifies and proposes compensation in 20 days; he objects; the CRB
hears both sides at day 30 and issues the final answer at day 38 — within 45.
He does not need a court, and his wall is fixed by the network project that
replaces the pipe.

## 7. RELATION TO ARBITRATION AND COURT

- If the internal answer is still unacceptable to either party, the next step
  is arbitration (`02-Arbitration-Clause.md`), and only then court
  (`03-Court-Last-Approach.md`).
- Internal exhaustion is a condition before arbitration, except in the crime
  and emergency exceptions above.

---

*The colony answers its own first. The 45 days are not a delay — they are the
system's promise that a member who asks will be heard and answered.*
