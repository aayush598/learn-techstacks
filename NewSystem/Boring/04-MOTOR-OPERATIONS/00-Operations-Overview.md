# 04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS — OVERVIEW

**Folder:** `04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/`

---

## 1. THE PROBLEM THIS SYSTEM KILLS

In the old system, one person or one group held the switch and decided everything:
when the motor ran, for how long, for whom, and who got told. That person could:

- Change the timings arbitrarily, so the schedule was whatever they felt like.
- Tell only favoured members about a change, so everyone else missed water.
- Run extra water for their own functions and guests without charge.
- Give themselves the best slots and everyone else the leftovers.
- Use the switch as a weapon: water for supporters, no water for critics.

Every one of those abuses was possible because **one person had discretion over
the switch**. This folder removes that discretion completely.

## 2. THE TWO PILLARS OF THE NEW SYSTEM

| Pillar | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **The schedule is the only authority** | The motor runs exactly as the published schedule says. No person can add, remove, or shift a run by individual choice. |
| **Everything is logged** | Every run, every gauge reading, every notice, every event booking is written down. Facts can never be hidden because they are always on paper or in the video record. |

## 3. HOW A DAY OF OPERATIONS RUNS END-TO-END

1. **Schedule is public.** The published schedule (`01-Daily-Schedule/03-Schedule-Publication.md`)
   states every run's start time, end time, and the group served. It is on the
   notice board, in the members group, and as a printed handout.
2. **Duty operator executes.** The duty operator for the day, drawn by lot under
   `03-ROTATION-DUTY/01-Duty-Roster/01-Roster-Generation-by-Lot.md`, switches the
   motor on and off **only at the schedule's times**.
3. **Run is logged.** The operator records the run in the Motor Run Log
   (`04-Monitoring-and-Logs/01-Motor-Run-Log.md`): time on, time off, gauge,
   anomalies.
4. **Readings are taken.** Pressure and quality are recorded on the periodic
   log (`04-Monitoring-and-Logs/02-Pressure-and-Quality-Log.md`).
5. **Anomalies are reported.** Anything off-schedule, off-gauge, or off-quality
   is reported through `05-Irregularities/04-Reporting-Violations.md`.
6. **Members verify.** Any member may inspect the logs at any time under the
   Right to Information and Audit
   (`00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/02-Right-to-Information-and-Audit.md`).

## 4. WHO IS ALLOWED TO TOUCH THE SWITCH

1. The **duty operator** assigned for that duty period — and only to execute the
   published schedule.
2. The **backup operator** named in the roster, only when the primary is
   genuinely unavailable (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/04-Duty-Failure/02-Immediate-Replacement-System.md`).
3. The **repair team**, only with a logged fault and a logged work order
   (`05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/02-Breakdown-Repair/01-Fault-Reporting.md`).

**No one else — however important, senior, or well-connected — may touch the
switch.** This includes committee members and the water committee. Their role is
to approve rules, not to operate them.

## 5. NO INDIVIDUAL DISCRETION — THE RULE

1. A duty operator who runs the motor **off-schedule** is committing an offence
   (`05-Irregularities/01-Unauthorized-Turning-On.md`), even if the reason was
   "a friend needed water" or "I thought it was helpful".
2. A committee member who **grants or denies** a run, a slot, or event water
   personally has overstepped. Approval happens by rule and by resolution, never
   by favour (`03-Event-Water/04-No-Personal-Preference-Rules.md`).
3. The only legitimate reasons to run outside the schedule are those published
   in advance: a booked-and-paid event slot, a logged emergency repair, or a
   missed-slot compensation run — each with its own paper trail.

## 6. WHY THE LOG MAKES EVERYTHING SAFE

| Event | What the log proves |
|-------|---------------------|
| "The motor never ran for our group" | The Motor Run Log shows the exact start and stop of every run, and who operated. |
| "Someone took water at night" | The log (and, where installed, video evidence) shows whether a run happened outside the schedule. |
| "Our area got low pressure" | The Pressure and Quality Log records readings per slot, so the complaint is checked against facts, not mood. |
| "They changed the timing secretly" | Schedule publication is photographed and timestamped, so every change is provably visible to all. |

Facts decided by evidence — never by vote, never by the loudest voice. See
`00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/01-Principle-of-Truth-over-Majority.md`.
