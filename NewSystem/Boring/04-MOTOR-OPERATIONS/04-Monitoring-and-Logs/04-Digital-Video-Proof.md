# DIGITAL VIDEO PROOF

**Folder:** `04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/04-Monitoring-and-Logs/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

The log is written by people, and people can make mistakes or tell lies. Where
a dispute turns on **who ran the motor, when, and for how long**, a dated
photo or video of the panel and a visible clock is accepted as **objective
evidence**. This file defines how such proof is captured without conflict and
kept so it can settle disputes later.

## 2. WHAT IS ACCEPTED AS EVIDENCE

| Evidence | What it must show | Accepted as |
|----------|-------------------|-------------|
| Video, panel + clock | The control panel (switch/indicator state) and a clock with date/time, recorded in one continuous shot | Proof of motor state at that moment |
| Photo, panel + clock | Same, as a still | Proof of motor state at that moment |
| Video/photo, gauge + clock | The gauge reading and a clock | Proof of pressure at that moment |
| Witness statement | Written, signed, dated — supporting or disputing the above | Corroborating material |

The **clock** is essential: a photo of a running motor without a readable clock
proves nothing about time. Where the colony has installed a network camera or
energy meter with a timestamp, its output is accepted on the same footing.

## 3. HOW TO CAPTURE WITHOUT CONFLICT

1. Any member who believes an unauthorized run is happening may take a photo or
   video **of the panel and clock** from the motor room doorway or window.
   Standing outside and recording the panel area is not an intrusion.
2. The member must not **physically touch the panel, switches, or the person
   operating** — capturing evidence is not the same as intervening.
3. If a person is operating, the recorder does not confront them; the evidence
   and a report go to the proper channel
   (`05-Irregularities/04-Reporting-Violations.md`).
4. A member who prevents another from photographing the panel is obstructing
   the evidence and commits an offence (the panel area is common property).

## 4. WHAT THE DUTY OPERATOR SHOULD CAPTURE

As a routine, at the **start and stop** of every run, the operator records:

| Shot | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Panel + clock at motor start | Proves the run began at the scheduled time |
| Panel + clock at motor stop | Proves the run ended on time |
| Gauge + clock | Proves the recorded gauge value |

Where the colony provides a phone or a fixed camera for this, the operator uses
it. Where it is the member's own phone, the file is uploaded to the common
archive at the end of the duty period.

## 5. STORAGE — THE EVIDENCE FILE

1. All evidence files are stored in the **Evidence File**: a folder on the
   common archive, mirrored in print where needed, with a contents index.
2. Each file is named with **date, time, and subject** (for example,
   `2026-06-14_0512_panel-morningG1.jpg`).
3. The Evidence File is open to members under the Right to Information
   (`00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/02-Right-to-Information-and-Audit.md`)
   and is retained per `11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/03-Records-Retention.md`.
4. Files are **not edited, cropped, or filtered** before storage; a doctored
   file is a falsification offence.

## 6. HOW THE EVIDENCE IS USED

1. In any dispute (unauthorized run, missed slot, overrun, damage), the
   evidence file is produced first; the CRB or the assembly decides facts on it
   plus the log and statements
   (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/02-Resolution-Process/`).
2. Photo/video evidence **overrides conflicting oral memory**, but does not
   automatically prove motive or intent — those are determined by the rules and
   the surrounding record.
3. A member who fabricates, stages, or alters such evidence faces the same
   falsification classification as a forged log entry (Level 4).

## 7. SCENARIO: "SOMEONE RAN THE MOTOR AT 2 A.M."

A member hears the motor at 2 a.m., walks to the motor room, and records 20
seconds of the panel showing the switch ON with the clock reading 02:03. The
file is uploaded with the timestamp. Next morning, the member reports the
irregularity. The panel-ON evidence plus the missing log entry for 2 a.m. is
enough to open the unauthorized-run investigation
(`05-Irregularities/01-Unauthorized-Turning-On.md`). **No shouting match was
needed; the clock and the switch had already testified.**

---

*The panel and the clock do not forget, do not take sides, and do not fear
anyone. The colony keeps them on record so that truth can be proven quietly —
and misconduct can never hide in the dark.*
