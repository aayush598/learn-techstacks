# REPORTING VIOLATIONS

**Folder:** `04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/05-Irregularities/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

A rule that cannot be reported is a rule that cannot be enforced. This file
defines **how any member reports an irregularity**, what protection the
reporter has, and how the system responds. The goal: reporting is easy, safe,
and effective — so misconduct is never tolerated in silence.

## 2. WHAT SHOULD BE REPORTED

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Schedule violations | Unauthorized run, overrun, early stop, skipped slot |
| Log violations | Missing entry, false entry, erased entry, withheld log |
| Event-water violations | Unbooked event run, waived fee, queue-jumping, favour |
| Wastage | Overflow, open bypass, leak, tanker misuse |
| Abuse of power | A committee member granting/denying water personally, threatening an operator |
| Target conduct | Information denial, harassment of a member over water |

## 3. HOW TO REPORT — FOUR ROUTES

| Route | How | Use when |
|-------|-----|----------|
| **Fault/irregularity form** | The written form `12-TEMPLATES/05-Fault-Report-Form.md`, dropped in the reports box or given to the issuing officer | A specific technical or schedule irregularity |
| **Written grievance** | Formal grievance under `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/02-Resolution-Process/02-Step-2-Written-Grievance.md` | A complaint against a person or a decision |
| **Anonymous channel** | The protected whistleblower channel (`02-GOVERNANCE/04-Transparency/04-Whistleblower-Channel.md`) | The reporter fears retaliation |
| **Verbal at assembly** | Raising it in the assembly | Public, low-stakes matters |

Every report gets a **reference number** and an entry in the Reports Register,
so nothing is lost or forgotten.

## 4. ANONYMITY OPTION

1. A reporter may remain anonymous. The report is still investigated; the
   evidence is still checked.
2. An anonymous report is not "weaker" — but it carries no witness statement,
   so it relies more on logs and records. The collector notes "anonymous" and
   proceeds.
3. The reporter's identity, where known, is **never disclosed without their
   consent** except where a court or the CRB lawfully requires it.

## 5. NO RETALIATION — THE ABSOLUTE RULE

1. **Retaliating against a reporter doubles the penalty of the original
   offence** (P7 — `00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/04-Right-to-Protection-from-Targeting.md`).
2. Retaliation includes: cutting or delaying the reporter's water, mocking or
   boycotting them, threatening them, or excluding them from notices.
3. A reporter whose water is touched in any way during a live investigation
   invokes the immediate-restoration procedure
   (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/03-Protection-Mechanisms/05-Individual-Economic-Protection.md`).
4. No one may be penalised for a **good-faith report**, even if the report turns
   out to be wrong. Deliberate false reporting is a separate offence (Section 7).

## 6. HOW THE CRB HANDLES REPORTS

| Step | Action | Time |
|------|--------|------|
| 1 | Report registered with a reference number | Same day |
| 2 | Water committee reviews: is this a fact-check (logs/evidence) or a conduct matter? | Within 48 hours |
| 3 | Fact-check: evidence gathered (log, gauge, video, statements) and the finding published | Within 7 days |
| 4 | Conduct matter: referred to the **Conflict Review Board (CRB)** under `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/` | Within 48 hours |
| 5 | CRB hears the parties, applies the rules, and issues its decision on the Enforcement Ladder | Within 14 days |
| 6 | Decision published; enforcement follows | Promptly |

The **reporter is informed** at each step of their report's progress — a report
that goes silent is itself a failure the reporter may escalate.

## 7. FALSE REPORTING

1. A report made **knowingly false** (fabricating evidence, naming an innocent
   member) is an offence — Level 2, escalated to Level 3 if it caused harm or
   waste of the system's time.
2. A report that is **mistaken but good-faith** is never penalised.
3. The distinction is decided by evidence, not by how annoyed the accused is.

## 8. SCENARIO: "I CANNOT REPORT, HE WILL KILL MY WATER"

A member saw a committee member's relative fill a tanker off-schedule at night
and fears losing their own water if they speak. The member drops the anonymous
form in the reports box. The water committee collects the video evidence from
the panel camera, finds the missing log entry, and charges the unauthorized run
at penalty rate. The reporter's identity is never sought; the reporter's water
continues exactly on schedule. **The fear was real; the system made it
irrelevant.**

---

*Reporting is a duty as much as a right (`00-CONSTITUTION/03-Fundamental-Duties/03-Duty-of-Transparency.md`).
The system protects the reporter absolutely, investigates every report on
evidence, and treats silence as the enemy. Water's abuses ended the day reporting
became safe.*
