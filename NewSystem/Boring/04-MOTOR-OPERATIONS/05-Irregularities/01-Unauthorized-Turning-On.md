# UNAUTHORIZED TURNING-ON OF THE MOTOR

**Folder:** `04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/05-Irregularities/`

---

## 1. THE OFFENCE

**Running the motor outside the published schedule, without an approved and
logged reason, is an offence.** It is not a mistake, not a courtesy, and not a
minor matter. It is the exact abuse the new system was built to end: a person
using the common motor for personal or private ends.

## 2. WHAT COUNTS AS UNAUTHORIZED TURNING-ON

| Act | Status |
|-----|--------|
| Running the motor at a time not on the published schedule | Offence |
| Extending a scheduled run beyond its published end time | Offence |
| Running the motor for a "friend" without a booking | Offence |
| Running the motor for one's own event without the process | Offence |
| Switching the motor on "to test" without a logged work order | Offence |
| Running the motor during a scheduled outage/stoppage resolution | Offence |

**Approved and logged reasons are only:**

1. A booked-and-paid event slot (`03-Event-Water/`).
2. A logged repair or test run with a work order
   (`05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/02-Breakdown-Repair/01-Fault-Reporting.md`).
3. A missed-slot compensation run (`05-Irregularities/02-Missed-Slot-Protocol.md`).
4. A declared emergency per the published emergency protocol
   (`10-RISK-AND-EMERGENCY/`).

## 3. SEVERITY — LEVEL 3+

1. Unauthorized turning-on is classified **at least Level 3** on the
   Enforcement Ladder (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`).
2. Aggravating factors raise the level:
   - Done **during summer grouping** or rationing (steals scarce water from
     others) — Level 4.
   - Done **repeatedly** after a warning — escalated each time.
   - Done by a **duty operator** (abuse of the switch they hold) — Level 4.
   - Done to **deny others** (running the motor so another group's slot fails)
     — targeted deprivation, Level 4+.
3. "I did it for a friend" is **not a defence**; it is the description of the
   crime. A friend's need goes through the event or hardship process, never
   through the switch.

## 4. WATER CONSUMED — CHARGED AT PENALTY RATE

1. The water consumed during the unauthorized run is measured from the log
   (time × recorded flow, or the storage/meter change) and charged to the
   **person who turned on the motor** at the **penalty rate** — the published
   event rate multiplied by a published penalty factor (default: 3x).
2. If the beneficiary is identifiable (the operator's own tank, a specific
   house), the beneficiary is jointly liable; the operator is always liable.
3. The charge is invoiced with the evidence (log + video/photo where available)
   and is due within 15 days. Non-payment follows the dues-collection rules of
   `06-FINANCE/`.

## 5. RESTORATION

1. **Restoration** means undoing the harm: the affected group's missed or
   reduced water is compensated under `02-Missed-Slot-Protocol.md` at the
   colony's cost, which is then recovered from the offender together with the
   penalty.
2. Restoration is never optional and never "forgiven" by a friendly committee.
   A vote cannot cancel restoration — it is a proven fact of harm
   (`00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/`).

## 6. THE DUTY OPERATOR'S POSITION

1. A duty operator who **refuses** to turn on the motor off-schedule is
   protected: compliance with the schedule is always correct, and no one may
   punish the operator for refusing an unlawful request.
2. A duty operator who is **pressured or threatened** to run off-schedule must
   report it through `05-Irregularities/04-Reporting-Violations.md` the same day.
3. A duty operator who **obeys** the pressure and turns on the motor commits the
   same offence as the person who asked — with the aggravated factor that they
   held the switch.

## 7. INVESTIGATION AND EVIDENCE

1. The investigation uses the Motor Run Log, the gauge log, the publication
   schedule, and any video evidence (`04-Monitoring-and-Logs/04-Digital-Video-Proof.md`).
2. Facts are decided by this evidence, never by vote or by social standing.
3. The operator whose duty period covered the unauthorized run is questioned
   first; the log must account for every minute the motor was on.

## 8. SCENARIO: THE NIGHT RUN "FOR A FRIEND"

At 02:10 a duty member on a different shift (not the day's operator) enters the
motor room with a neighbour, switches on the motor, fills the neighbour's
tanker, and leaves at 03:00. A resident records the panel with the clock. The
next morning: the log has no 02:10 entry; the video and the resident's report
identify the pair; the schedule shows no booking. The run is logged as an
irregularity, the consumed volume is charged at 3x to the operator, the
beneficiary is jointly liable, the night's missing storage is restored to the
affected group, and the Ladder proceeds at Level 4. **The "favour" cost its
giver real money, real standing, and the colony's restored trust — and it ended
the belief that the switch could ever be private again.**

---

*The switch belongs to the schedule. Whoever turns it on outside the schedule —
for themselves, for a friend, or for spite — has stolen water, stolen time, and
attacked the system. The rules answer with penalty, restoration, and the
Enforcement Ladder.*
