# SCHEDULE PUBLICATION

**Folder:** `04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/01-Daily-Schedule/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

The schedule has no value if it is a secret. The publication rule exists so that
**every member receives the exact same information, at the exact same time,
through the exact same channels** — with no advance copy for friends and no
deliberate omission of critics.

## 2. THE 7-DAY ADVANCE RULE

1. Every schedule (Standard, Summer Grouping, Seasonal, and any amendment) is
   published **at least 7 full days** before it takes effect.
2. The 7 days are counted from the moment the last of the three mandatory
   channels (below) has carried the schedule.
3. A schedule that takes effect early, or is published late, is **not in
   force**. The previous published schedule continues until the new one has
   completed its 7-day publication.

## 3. THE THREE MANDATORY CHANNELS — ALL THREE, ALWAYS

| Channel | What is posted | When |
|---------|----------------|------|
| **Community notice board** | Printed copy of the schedule, signed and dated by the issuing officer, fixed in a tamper-evident frame | Same calendar day |
| **Members group / digital channel** | Exact same text, posted once to the official members group | Same calendar day |
| **Printed handout** | Physical copy delivered to every household's registered address or in person at a house-to-house circulation | Same calendar day, by 20:00 |

The rule is **all three, on the same day**. Using only the notice board, or only
the group, is not publication.

## 4. PUBLICATION PROOF

1. Whoever publishes **photographs the notice board copy** with a visible
   timestamp (newspaper held up, phone clock, or any accepted time proof) and
   the handout stack, and files the photos in the Publication Evidence file.
2. The handout distribution is recorded on a **circulation sheet** listing each
   household, who delivered, and who received (or the doorstep photo).
3. These proofs are part of the records under the Right to Information
   (`00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/02-Right-to-Information-and-Audit.md`).
4. If publication proof is missing, the schedule is **deemed unpublished** and
   cannot be enforced against a member who relied on the old schedule.

## 5. NO ADVANCE-ONLY-FOR-FRIENDS

1. No member may receive a schedule **before** the three channels are released.
2. A member who obtains an early copy through an inside contact has not been
   served a valid notice; the formal publication still governs.
3. The officer responsible for early or partial disclosure commits an offence
   under the Enforcement Ladder (targeted information denial is a Level 3+
   offence — see `00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/04-Right-to-Protection-from-Targeting.md`).

## 6. CONTENT OF THE PUBLISHED SCHEDULE

Every published schedule must show:

| Element | Example |
|---------|---------|
| Effective date(s) | 12 June to 11 July |
| Every slot's start and end | 05:30–06:30 |
| Group served per slot | Group A (Streets 1–3) |
| Any buffer allowed | +10 min, logged |
| Reason for the schedule | Seasonal grouping, rotation week, etc. |
| What changed from the previous schedule | "Evening Group B moves from 18:40 to 19:10" |
| How to object | "Raise objection at the assembly or via grievance, with the 7-day period open" |

## 7. SCENARIO: THE "QUIET CHANGE"

An officer once "reminded" a few houses of a new timing while the board stayed
blank. Under this rule:

1. The change was never published (board blank, no group post, no handout), so
   it is **void**.
2. The houses who were "reminded" cannot be punished for missing a run that was
   never formally announced.
3. The officer's conduct is reported under
   `05-Irregularities/04-Reporting-Violations.md` and assessed as an attempt at
   targeted information denial.

## 8. AMENDMENTS TO THE SCHEDULE

1. Schedule amendments follow the same 7-day publication rule, in addition to
   the assembly resolution required by
   `01-Daily-Schedule/01-Standard-Schedule.md`.
2. An amendment that affects one group's timings is published to **all** members
   — never only to the affected group.

---

*Publication is simultaneous, complete, provable, and identical for every
member. Whoever publishes the schedule first to a friend has not published it to
anyone; they have breached the rule.*
