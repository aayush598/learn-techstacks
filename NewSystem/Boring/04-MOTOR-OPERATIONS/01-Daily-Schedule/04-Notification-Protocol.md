# NOTIFICATION PROTOCOL

**Folder:** `04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/01-Daily-Schedule/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

Notification is how the system keeps every member equally informed. The rules
here govern **routine schedule notices** and the **emergency no-water notice**.
The guiding rule: information reaches **all members at once**, never a chosen
few.

## 2. STANDARD CHANNELS (IN ORDER OF USE)

| Rank | Channel | Used for |
|------|---------|----------|
| 1 | Community notice board | Every official notice, signed and dated |
| 2 | Official members group (digital) | Same text, posted once by the issuing officer |
| 3 | Doorstep handout / circulation | For schedule publications and any notice requiring a signature |
| 4 | Announcement at the assembly | Supplementary; never a substitute for 1–3 |

A notice is **valid** only if it reaches at least one published channel
properly. The assembly may designate one digital channel as the official one;
all members are registered to it at admission
(`01-MEMBERSHIP/04-Member-Registry/01-Registry-Format.md`).

## 3. THE EMERGENCY "NO-WATER-TODAY" PROTOCOL

When a run must be cancelled on short notice (motor failure, power cut, pipe
burst, contamination — see `10-RISK-AND-EMERGENCY/`), the following must happen:

| Step | Action | Time limit |
|------|--------|------------|
| 1 | Duty operator or repair lead confirms the fact and notifies the water committee convenor | Immediately on confirming |
| 2 | Emergency notice posted to the members group and the notice board | Within 30 minutes |
| 3 | Notice also sent to any member without digital access (via a printed slip at the door or a phone call recorded in the emergency log) | Within 2 hours |
| 4 | Missed water is acknowledged and compensated per `05-Irregularities/02-Missed-Slot-Protocol.md` | Same day |
| 5 | Cause, duration, and expected restoration are logged | Same day |

The emergency notice text must state: what is cancelled, when it is expected to
resume, and how the missed water will be made up.

## 4. ACKNOWLEDGMENT EXPECTATION

1. Members are expected to **read** official notices on the published channels
   — the notice board and the official group.
2. For schedules, the handout circulation sheet captures an acknowledgment
   signature per household. This is **evidence of delivery**, not a condition
   for water rights.
3. A member who fails to read a properly published notice bears the cost of
   their own forgetfulness in **organising their storage** — but never loses
   water rights (Section 5).

## 5. FAILURE TO READ NEVER LOSES WATER RIGHTS

1. The right to a fair water share
   (`00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/01-Right-to-Fair-Water.md`) is
   **independent of whether the member read the notice**.
2. A member who missed a run because of **improper or partial publication** (the
   board was blank, the post never happened, or the change was not published to
   all) has a **missed slot** and receives compensation under
   `05-Irregularities/02-Missed-Slot-Protocol.md`.
3. "They should have read the notice" can **never** be used to justify silently
   taking away a member's water.
4. Repeated missed runs by the same member are investigated as possible
   targeted information denial (`00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/04-Right-to-Protection-from-Targeting.md`).

## 6. WHO MAY SEND OFFICIAL NOTICES

1. The **issuing officer** designated by the assembly (rotates by lot).
2. The **duty operator** for operation-related notices (run log entries,
   emergencies during their duty).
3. The **water committee** for rule-based notices (fees, seasonal orders).
4. **No one else.** A member who broadcasts a false or unofficial "notice"
   (for example, claiming the schedule changed when it did not) is reported
   under `05-Irregularities/04-Reporting-Violations.md`.

## 7. NOTICE RECORDS

1. Every notice is kept in a **Notices File**: the text, the date, the channel,
   the publication photo, and the circulation sheet.
2. The Notices File is open to all members under the Right to Information
   (`00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/02-Right-to-Information-and-Audit.md`).
3. A notice without a file entry is treated as never issued.

## 8. SCENARIO: POWER CUT AT 06:00

The morning run is due at 06:30 but the area has a power cut. The operator:

1. Confirms the power cut and that the borewell cannot run.
2. Posts the emergency notice within 30 minutes.
3. Logs the cancellation in the Motor Run Log.
4. The missed slot is queued for compensation per `02-Missed-Slot-Protocol.md`.

The member who lives at the far end, who saw the group post at 06:45, is
treated identically to the member who was already awake. **Same information,
same treatment.**

---

*Notification is a duty of the system to every member — not a favour to any
member. A notice sent to some is a notice sent to no one.*
