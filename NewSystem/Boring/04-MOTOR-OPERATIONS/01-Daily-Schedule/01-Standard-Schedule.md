# STANDARD SCHEDULE — NORMAL SEASON

**Folder:** `04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/01-Daily-Schedule/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

The Standard Schedule defines the **normal-season daily runs** of the motor.
It is the default that applies whenever no scarcity grouping
(`02-Seasonal-Rotation/01-Summer-Grouping.md`) and no special seasonal order is
in force. The purpose is absolute predictability: every member knows, weeks in
advance, exactly when water will flow.

## 2. PRINCIPLES GOVERNING THE SCHEDULE

| Principle | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| Fixed, not vague | Every run has a **start time and an end time**. "Morning" is not a time. |
| Same for all | The schedule is one document that applies to every member identically. |
| Published in advance | The schedule is published at least 7 days before it takes effect. |
| Changeable only by process | No person can change it. Only the General Assembly can, by resolution with 7 days' notice. |
| Serve all groups | Every area/group appears in the schedule; none may be silently omitted. |

## 3. THE DEFAULT NORMAL-SEASON SCHEDULE

Two fixed daily windows cover the normal season, split so that each area/group
receives water within each window:

| Run | Start | End | Served |
|-----|-------|-----|--------|
| Morning Run A | 05:30 | 06:30 | Group A (e.g., streets 1–3, houses 1–20) |
| Morning Run B | 06:40 | 07:40 | Group B (e.g., streets 4–6, houses 21–40) |
| Evening Run A | 17:30 | 18:30 | Group A |
| Evening Run B | 18:40 | 19:40 | Group B |

Defaults noted:

1. The exact **group boundaries and times are set by the assembly** at the
   founding, not by any individual, and printed in the schedule register.
2. Times are chosen with reference to the actual borewell yield and the number
   of connections, per `01-Daily-Schedule/02-Slot-Definition.md`.
3. The **normal season** is the default. In summer, the schedule is replaced by
   the grouping schedule (`02-Seasonal-Rotation/01-Summer-Grouping.md`); in
   winter/rain, runs may be reduced (`02-Seasonal-Rotation/04-Winter-and-Rainy-Season.md`).

## 4. CHANGING THE STANDARD SCHEDULE

1. Any member may **propose** a schedule change in writing to the assembly.
2. The proposal is **published with reasons** at least 7 days before the vote,
   so every member can see exactly what changes and why.
3. The assembly passes a **resolution** with reasons recorded. The change takes
   effect only **after the 7-day notice period** has passed and the new schedule
   has been published per `01-Daily-Schedule/03-Schedule-Publication.md`.
4. **No person, committee, or officer may change the schedule by themselves.**
   A change made without the resolution is void and the old schedule continues.

## 5. EMERGENCY DEVIATIONS (LIMITED AND LOGGED)

The schedule may be deviated from only in these published situations, and every
deviation must be logged:

| Situation | Who may act | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| Motor breakdown or power cut in the middle of a run | Duty operator + backup | Re-run the missed portion under `05-Irregularities/02-Missed-Slot-Protocol.md`. |
| Repair or testing of the motor | Repair team with logged work order | Timed test runs logged in the Motor Run Log. |
| Booked-and-paid event water | Event committee per the booking register | `03-Event-Water/01-Event-Request-Process.md`. |
| Prolonged drought rationing | Assembly resolution | `10-RISK-AND-EMERGENCY/01-Drought-and-Scarcity/01-Rationing-Plan.md`. |

**There is no "I will run it a little longer" clause.** A run that exceeds its
published end time is an unauthorized run and is treated under
`05-Irregularities/01-Unauthorized-Turning-On.md`.

## 6. HARDSHIP AND MEDICAL PROTOCOL

1. Genuine medical need (for example, a member on dialysis, a chronic patient)
   is handled by a **public, temporary, verifiable** hardship provision — not by
   any person's kindness.
2. The hardship arrangement is approved by the assembly, published, and
   re-reviewed quarterly. It never silently removes another member's water.
3. Details are defined in `04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/03-Event-Water/` by analogy for
   extra-water needs, and the right itself is protected by
   `00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/01-Right-to-Fair-Water.md`.

## 7. DISPLAY OF THE SCHEDULE

The current Standard Schedule must be:

1. Printed and fixed on the community notice board.
2. Sent to the members group and every registered member simultaneously
   (`01-Daily-Schedule/03-Schedule-Publication.md`).
3. Kept with the duty roster so the operator has it at hand at the motor room.

---

*The Standard Schedule is the promise the system makes to every member. It is
published, predictable, and changeable only by the assembly's published process —
never by the mood, convenience, or interest of any individual.*
