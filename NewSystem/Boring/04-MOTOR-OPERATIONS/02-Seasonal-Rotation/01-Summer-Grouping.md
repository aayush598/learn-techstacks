# SUMMER GROUPING

**Folder:** `04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/02-Seasonal-Rotation/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

In summer the borewell yield drops and every household cannot be served in the
normal two daily windows. **Summer grouping** is the published process by which
the assembly divides members into groups, gives each group its own slot, and
keeps the sharing fair and rotating. It replaces improvisation with a plan.

## 2. WHEN GROUPING IS DECLARED

1. The water committee measures the discharge yield on a daily basis in the
   Pressure and Quality Log (`04-Monitoring-and-Logs/02-Pressure-and-Quality-Log.md`).
2. If the measured yield falls below the **grouping threshold** (default: 70% of
   the normal-season discharge for 5 consecutive days), the water committee
   recommends grouping to the assembly.
3. The **assembly declares grouping season by resolution**. Until that
   resolution, the Standard Schedule continues unchanged — no individual may
   declare grouping.
4. Grouping ends by the same route: measured recovery to normal levels for 7
   consecutive days, then assembly resolution ending the grouping season.

## 3. HOW HOUSEHOLDS ARE DIVIDED INTO GROUPS

The assembly chooses one of two methods; both are published:

| Method | How it works | Used when |
|--------|--------------|-----------|
| **By area** | Households divided by street/blocks, giving contiguous groups with shorter pipe runs and equal tap conditions | Normal case, where areas are roughly equal in size |
| **By lot** | Households drawn by lot into groups, ensuring every group has a mix of locations, heights, and tap levels | Where areas are unequal in size or tap levels differ greatly |

Rules for every grouping:

1. Every connection appears in **exactly one group**; none is left out.
2. Groups are **as equal as possible in connection count** (difference of at
   most one connection between any two groups).
3. Tap-level and pipe-distance compensation continues to apply within groups
   (`09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/04-Network-Improvements/01-Tap-Height-Compensation.md`).
4. The grouping table (household → group) is published with the grouping
   schedule, and any household shown wrongly may object at the assembly before
   the schedule takes effect.

## 4. EACH GROUP GETS ITS OWN SLOT

1. With grouping, the motor runs **fewer, longer slots**, one per group per
   window, so each group's stored volume is protected even at low yield.
2. Example grouping schedule (illustrative — times fixed by assembly by
   measurement):

| Slot | Start | End | Group |
|------|-------|-----|-------|
| Morning G1 | 05:00 | 06:00 | Group 1 |
| Morning G2 | 06:15 | 07:15 | Group 2 |
| Morning G3 | 07:30 | 08:30 | Group 3 |
| Evening G1 | 16:30 | 17:30 | Group 1 |
| Evening G2 | 17:45 | 18:45 | Group 2 |
| Evening G3 | 19:00 | 20:00 | Group 3 |

3. Slot durations are set by the **fair-share formula** in
   `01-Daily-Schedule/02-Slot-Definition.md`, never by favour.

## 5. GROUPINGS ARE REVISED BY LOT — NO PERMANENT DISADVANTAGE

1. The **membership of groups** is re-drawn (by lot) at the start of each
   grouping cycle, so no area or set of families is permanently stuck in the
   worst group.
2. The **order in which groups are served** rotates weekly per
   `02-Group-Order-Rotation.md`, so first-slot advantage is shared.
3. A group that repeatedly suffers low pressure or low volume across a cycle is
   compensated under `03-Slot-Fairness-Compensation.md` using the logs, not by
   appeals.

## 6. SCENARIO: SUMMER OF 2024

The borewell yield dropped to 60% for a week. The water committee's daily
readings crossed the grouping threshold. The assembly resolved to declare
grouping, the colony was split into three groups by lot, and the rotation order
changed every Monday. A member who objected to being in Group 3 was shown the
published rotation calendar: the following week Group 3 became the first group,
and the gauge log confirmed every group's stored volume at the end of each week.
The objection was recorded, the facts answered, and no favour was sought.

## 7. RELATION TO RATIONING

If even grouping cannot meet minimum needs, the assembly moves to the published
**rationing plan** (`10-RISK-AND-EMERGENCY/01-Drought-and-Scarcity/01-Rationing-Plan.md`).
Grouping is the first response; rationing is the last resort. Both are
decided by process and measurement — never by who is closest to the pump.

---

*Summer is not a reason for one member to decide who drinks. It is a signal for
the assembly to apply the pre-written grouping process, measured and published
like everything else.*
