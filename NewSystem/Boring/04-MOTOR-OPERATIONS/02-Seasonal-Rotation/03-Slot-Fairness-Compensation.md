# SLOT FAIRNESS COMPENSATION

**Folder:** `04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/02-Seasonal-Rotation/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

Rotation shares the advantage of good slots over time. But where physical
inequality (pressure, yield, tap height) remains measurable, the system must
**compensate in fact, not in words**. This file defines how fairness is
measured and how the disadvantaged group is made whole.

## 2. THE MEASUREMENT PRINCIPLE — P5

1. Fairness is decided by **gauges, meters, and logs** — never by feelings,
   complaints, or how persuasive the speaker is
   (`00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/01-Principle-of-Truth-over-Majority.md`).
2. The primary evidence is the **Pressure and Quality Log**
   (`04-Monitoring-and-Logs/02-Pressure-and-Quality-Log.md`) and the **Motor Run
   Log** (`04-Monitoring-and-Logs/01-Motor-Run-Log.md`).
3. A claim of unfairness without logged evidence is recorded and investigated,
   but it does not, by itself, trigger compensation.

## 3. MEASURABLE INEQUALITIES THAT QUALIFY

| Inequality | Measured by | Typical cause |
|------------|-------------|---------------|
| Low delivered pressure | Gauge/flow reading at a group's sample tap during its slot | Group served late in the window; long pipe run |
| Low stored volume | Storage check per connection at slot end | Low yield; short slot |
| High tap / roof tank cannot fill | Fill time at representative connections | Tap level, pipe distance (`09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/04-Network-Improvements/01-Tap-Height-Compensation.md`) |
| Repeated yield drop during a group's slot | Borewell discharge gauge during the slot | Yield falling through the day |

## 4. THE THREE COMPENSATION MECHANISMS

The assembly adopts one or more, published with the schedule. Compensation is
applied for the **measured shortfall**, not as a gift.

| Mechanism | How it works |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Order rotation** | The weekly rotation (`02-Group-Order-Rotation.md`) ensures no group is permanently at the worst position. Applied automatically. |
| **Longer duration** | A group that measurably receives less volume gets extra pumping minutes next cycle, equal to the measured shortfall converted to minutes by the recorded flow rate. |
| **Credits / adjustment** | Where extra minutes are not possible (borewell exhausted), the group receives a documented credit (time-credit or monetary adjustment) published in the accounts. |

## 5. HOW COMPENSATION IS TRIGGERED

| Step | Action | By whom | Time |
|------|--------|---------|------|
| 1 | Readings are recorded for every slot | Duty operator | Each run |
| 2 | Weekly summary compared against the fair-share target | Water committee | Every Sunday |
| 3 | Shortfall above the **materiality threshold** (default: 10% of the group's weekly target volume) is identified | Water committee | Weekly |
| 4 | Compensation decision published with the data and the mechanism | Water committee (rule-based) | Within 7 days |
| 5 | Member may object to the data at the assembly | Any member | Next assembly |

The **materiality threshold** and the **conversion formula** (litres → minutes →
credits) are set by assembly resolution and published, so the arithmetic is
checkable by any member.

## 6. WHAT IS NOT COMPENSABLE

1. A shortfall caused by **misuse or wastage** (overflow, open bypass, unauthorised
   run) is not compensable; it is a violation
   (`05-Irregularities/03-Water-Wastage-Detection.md`).
2. A shortfall caused by the member's own pump or pipework (their own blocked
   line, their own burst pipe) is a member-side matter, not a slot-fairness
   matter — unless the network is the documented cause.
3. "I feel we got less" is not a claim; "our group stored 30% below target for
   3 weeks, here is the log" is a claim.

## 7. SCENARIO: THE LAST GROUP SHORTFALL

In summer, Group C is always served last in the evening. Over two weeks the
storage check shows Group C at 22% below target each evening while Groups A and
B are at target. The water committee:

1. Confirms the numbers from the logs.
2. Publishes the shortfall and the cause (yield drop at day's end, recorded on
   the discharge gauge).
3. Adds the equivalent minutes to Group C's morning slot for the following week
   (with the rotation giving Group C the early slot anyway).
4. Records the compensation in the accounts and the log.

The next week Group C is at target. No member had to argue, beg, or threaten —
the logs did the arguing.

## 8. RECORD OF COMPENSATION

1. Every compensation granted is recorded: group, measured shortfall, mechanism,
   amount, and dates.
2. The record is public under the Right to Information
   (`00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/02-Right-to-Information-and-Audit.md`).
3. Compensation records are reviewed at the end of each season to decide whether
   a **network improvement** (pipeline, booster, tap-height work) is more
   economical than continued compensation
   (`09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/04-Network-Improvements/`).

---

*Fairness is not a sentiment; it is an arithmetic statement backed by logs. The
system compensates what the gauges prove, not what the voice demands.*
