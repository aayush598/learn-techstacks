# EVENT WATER — FEE

**Folder:** `04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/03-Event-Water/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

Event water consumes power, wears the motor, and draws from a common resource.
The fee exists so that an event's cost is paid by the event's beneficiaries —
never silently by the whole colony. The fee is **published, uniform, and paid
into the Common Fund**.

## 2. THE FEE IS SET BY THE ASSEMBLY — PUBLISHED, NOT PERSONAL

1. The assembly sets the event water fee **by resolution**, along with the
   measurement unit and the effective date.
2. The fee is **one published number for every member**. There is no "rate for
   relatives" and no "waiver for friends".
3. The fee is reviewed at least once a year (or when power/diesel costs change
   materially) and republished.

## 3. FEE STRUCTURE — TWO PUBLISHED UNITS

| Unit | How it is charged | Use when |
|------|-------------------|----------|
| **Per 1000 litres** | A fixed fee per thousand litres of event water drawn, measured by the meter/tank volume or the logged run at recorded flow rate | Storage-style events (filling a tank, staging water) |
| **Per hour of extra run** | A fixed fee per hour (or part) of an extra motor run, covering power + wear + reserve | Service-style events (running the tap for guests/cooking) |

The assembly chooses the unit, or both, and publishes the **rupee amounts**.
Both are documented in the Event and Extra Charges file
(`06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/05-Event-and-Extra-Charges.md`), which is the
authoritative tariff.

## 4. WHAT THE FEE COVERS

| Component | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| Power | Electricity/diesel for the extra run, at the actual tariff |
| Motor wear | Depreciation contribution for the extra running hours |
| Reserve | Contribution to the Reserve Fund for eventual replacement (`06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/02-Reserve-Fund.md`) |
| Administration | Booking, logging, and publication overhead |

The fee is deliberately **more than the raw power cost**, because the event also
uses the colony's asset and its time. Undercharging would make the whole colony
subsidise one family's event.

## 5. PAYMENT RULES

1. The booking is confirmed only when the fee is **paid or a binding payment
   schedule is agreed** in the register.
2. Payment is made **into the Common Fund** with a receipt, and the receipt
   number is written on the booking entry. Payment to any individual is not
   payment.
3. If the event is cancelled **at least 48 hours before** the booked slot, the
   fee is refunded in full or converted to a credit. Cancellation inside 48
   hours forfeits 50% (the booked slot may have blocked others).
4. If the event draws **more than booked**, the extra is charged at the same
   published rate and must be paid within 7 days.

## 6. SCENARIO: THE "FREE WATER" WEDDING

A prominent family books event water and, on the day, claims the colony "must
give free water for a marriage". The operator's answer is the published tariff:
the same fee every family pays. The family pays, the run is logged, the receipt
is filed. If a committee member had quietly waived the fee, that member would be
reported under `04-No-Personal-Preference-Rules.md` and the Enforcement Ladder.
**No insider discount exists.**

## 7. RECORDS AND AUDIT

1. Every event fee received is recorded in the accounts with the receipt number,
   the booking register entry, and the run log entry — three records for one
   event.
2. All three are public under the Right to Information
   (`00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/02-Right-to-Information-and-Audit.md`).
3. The annual audit (`06-FINANCE/04-Audit/`) reconciles event fees collected
   against event runs logged. A run without a fee, or a fee without a run, is
   flagged as an irregularity.

---

*Event water is not charity and not a perk. It is a priced service of the common
asset, priced by the assembly, paid into the Common Fund, and provable in three
public records.*
