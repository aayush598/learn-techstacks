# EVENT WATER — NO PERSONAL PREFERENCE RULES

**Folder:** `04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/03-Event-Water/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

The old system's worst abuse was that event water flowed to friends, family,
and supporters, and was denied to everyone else. These rules make **personal
preference impossible** by removing the person from the decision entirely: only
the form, the fee, and the queue decide.

## 2. NOBODY CAN GRANT EVENT WATER PERSONALLY

1. No member — including the water committee convenor, any office-bearer, or
   the most senior family in the colony — may approve, deny, speed up, or
   block an event water request by personal decision.
2. Approval happens **only** through the published process
   (`01-Event-Request-Process.md`): a written form, verification against the
   rules, entry in the register, and a published confirmation.
3. A request approved outside this process is **void**; the extra run must not
   take place, and if it does, it is an unauthorized run
   (`05-Irregularities/01-Unauthorized-Turning-On.md`).

## 3. THE SAME FORM, FEE, AND QUEUE FOR EVERYONE

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| Form | The same `12-TEMPLATES/11-Event-Water-Request.md` form, blank for every member. No VIP version. |
| Fee | The same published tariff (`02-Event-Fee.md`). No discounts, waivers, or "adjustments" for insiders. |
| Queue | The same Booking Register in arrival order (`03-Queue-and-Booking.md`). No express lane for the influential. |
| Priority | The same published priority ladder. No "he is important, give him first". |
| Logging | Every event run in the Motor Run Log. No unlogged "quiet" runs. |

## 4. NO FREE EVENT WATER FOR INSIDERS

1. **Free event water does not exist.** Every event run is paid into the Common
   Fund with a receipt, without exception.
2. A committee member who waives, discounts, or defers a fee without the
   published waiver rule has committed an offence:
   - Misuse of the Common Fund (financial violation), and
   - Breach of equal treatment (P1, P10).
3. The penalty is assessed on the Enforcement Ladder
   (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`); the
   unpaid amount is recovered from the person who arranged the waiver.

## 5. THE "IMPORTANT MEMBER" CLAUSE

1. No member is "important" for the purpose of event water. Every member's
   standing is equal (P2 — see
   `01-MEMBERSHIP/05-Equal-Standing/`).
2. If an influential member attempts to obtain event water through pressure or
   expectation rather than the form, the attempt is recorded and reported
   (`05-Irregularities/04-Reporting-Violations.md`).
3. The reverse is also barred: a member may not be **denied** event water
   because the committee "does not like them". Denial requires a written,
   rule-based reason, appealable like any decision.

## 6. WHO WATCHES THE WATCHERS

1. The water committee's event-water decisions are **reconciled monthly**
   against the register, the receipts, and the run log by a rotating member
   (never the same person who approved them).
2. Any discrepancy is reported to the assembly and the audit
   (`06-FINANCE/04-Audit/`).
3. Members may inspect the register and the log at any time; a member who finds
   an unapproved or unpaid event run reports it through
   `05-Irregularities/04-Reporting-Violations.md` with anonymity available.

## 7. SCENARIO: THE VIP REQUEST

The colony's largest landowner asks the committee convenor for "a tanker's
worth" of free water for his grandson's wedding next week. The convenor's only
correct reply is the rulebook: form, fee, queue, and a free slot if one exists.
The landowner files the form, pays the published fee, gets a slot, and the run
is logged like anyone else's. His wealth changed nothing — because the system
left no room for it to.

## 8. CONSEQUENCES

| Act | Consequence |
|-----|-------------|
| Granting event water without the process | Void booking; run treated as unauthorized (Level 3+); waiver recovered from the grantor |
| Waiving/discounting the fee | Recovery of the amount + Ladder penalty |
| Denying event water to a member without written rule-based reasons | Offence under the Right to Fair Water; booking processed immediately |
| Using personal influence to jump the queue | Booking void; member reported; repeat = Ladder escalation |

---

*Event water is a service of the common asset, delivered by form, fee, and
queue. Whoever they are, a member who skips the process has not found a faster
path — they have found an offence.*
