# EVENT WATER — REQUEST PROCESS

**Folder:** `04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/03-Event-Water/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

Weddings, functions, and religious gatherings need extra water. In the old
system, extra water went to whoever was close to the operator. Under these
rules, event water is **booked, paid, and logged** — available to every member
equally, through one published process, with no personal discretion.

## 2. WHAT COUNTS AS EVENT WATER

| Kind | Examples |
|------|----------|
| Scheduled event | Wedding, reception, housewarming, naming ceremony, community feast |
| Commercial/one-time | Water for construction work, vehicle/equipment washing, temporary tanks |
| Community event | Colony-level function, festival arrangement, cricket match |
| NOT event water | Routine household storage, garden watering, personal tanker filling — these stay within the normal schedule |

## 3. THE REQUEST PROCESS

| Step | Action | Time limit |
|------|--------|------------|
| 1 | Member submits the written **Event Water Request** form (`12-TEMPLATES/11-Event-Water-Request.md`): date, purpose, litres or hours needed, proposed slot, booking fee | Minimum **7 days** before the event |
| 2 | The form is entered in the public **Booking Register** in arrival order | Same day |
| 3 | The water committee verifies the request against the published rules (fee paid or scheduled, slot free, no clash) | Within 2 days |
| 4 | The committee **applies the rule and confirms or returns** the booking with written reasons | Within 2 days |
| 5 | Approved booking is published to all members | With confirmation |
| 6 | On the day, the duty operator runs the extra slot per the booking and logs it | As booked |

## 4. WHO DECIDES — THE RULE, NOT A PERSON

1. The water committee **does not grant favours**; it applies published rules.
   A request that meets the rules is approved. A request that does not is
   returned with the rule cited.
2. The **assembly sets the rules** (fees, priority, maximum volumes) — see
   `02-Event-Fee.md` and `03-Queue-and-Booking.md`.
3. No committee member, office-bearer, or influential member may approve or
   reject a request personally
   (`04-No-Personal-Preference-Rules.md`).
4. If the committee is unsure whether a rule covers the request, it refers the
   matter to the assembly — it never improvises.

## 5. TIMELINES — WHY 7 DAYS

1. Seven days give the colony time to publish the booking, let any member
   object, and plan the schedule without disrupting normal runs.
2. A request with **less than 7 days' notice is not rejected** automatically:
   it enters the queue and is served if a slot is free, at the same fee and the
   same rules. It never jumps the queue.
3. A genuinely urgent need (for example, water for a medical arrangement) uses
   the hardship/emergency route in `10-RISK-AND-EMERGENCY/`, not the event route.

## 6. THE EXTRA RUN ITSELF

1. The event slot has a defined start and end, and a defined volume, exactly
   like a normal slot (`01-Daily-Schedule/02-Slot-Definition.md`).
2. The duty operator logs the event run in the Motor Run Log
   (`04-Monitoring-and-Logs/01-Motor-Run-Log.md`) with reason "event booking".
3. Event water may be drawn **only during the booked slot**. Water drawn before
   or after is an unauthorized run (`05-Irregularities/01-Unauthorized-Turning-On.md`).
4. If the event needs more than booked, the family books an **additional slot**
   through the same process — the operator cannot "extend a little".

## 7. SCENARIO: THE WEDDING REQUEST

Two families request water for weddings on the same weekend. Family A files on
the 1st for the 15th; Family B files on the 3rd for the 14th. The committee
checks the rules: both pay the published fee, both slots are free. Both are
approved and published. Family B's event comes first, but Family A booked
first — the log and the register show both were served per the rules. Neither
family needed a friend in the committee.

---

*Event water is a right exercised through a form, a fee, and a log — available
to every member on identical terms. The only "influence" that works is filling
the form correctly and paying the published fee.*
