# EVENT WATER — QUEUE AND BOOKING

**Folder:** `04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/03-Event-Water/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

When two events want the same slot, or several want event water in one week,
the **queue and priority rules** decide — mechanically, publicly, and without
anyone's mood. The booking register is the single source of truth for event
water.

## 2. FIRST-COME, FIRST-SERVED — THE BASE RULE

1. Event requests are served in the order their forms arrive in the Booking
   Register (`01-Event-Request-Process.md`).
2. Arrival order is recorded by **date and time of submission**, not by
   reputation, friendship, or urgency claims.
3. The base rule yields only to the published priorities in Section 3.

## 3. PRIORITY RULES — ALL PUBLISHED, NONE BY MOOD

When slots clash, priority is applied in this fixed order:

| Rank | Priority | Example |
|------|----------|---------|
| 1 | **Medical need** | Water for dialysis, a hospital discharge arrangement, a documented patient need (verified, temporary) |
| 2 | **Scheduled events** (in booking order) | A wedding booked 30 days ahead outranks a feast booked 10 days ahead within this rank |
| 3 | **Others** (in booking order) | Construction water, vehicle washing, community convenience |

Rules that apply on top of the priority ladder:

1. **Priority is a published category, never a named person.** A member cannot
   claim medical priority without the published verification (a medical note on
   file).
2. Within each rank, **earlier booking wins**.
3. A member who books repeatedly and blocks others by holding slots without
   paying may be restricted to one pending booking at a time (assembly rule).
4. No priority category lets a request **jump the schedule silently**: even a
   medical booking is published to all members.

## 4. THE PUBLIC BOOKING REGISTER

The register contains, for every event:

| Column | Example |
|--------|---------|
| Serial number | EV-2026-014 |
| Date requested | 05 June |
| Family / member ID | M-031 |
| Event date and purpose | 25 June, wedding |
| Volume or hours booked | 4000 litres / 2.5 hours |
| Proposed slot | 25 June, 09:00–11:30 |
| Fee paid / receipt no. | Paid, R-1182 |
| Status | Approved, Published, Completed |

1. The register is kept physically (the Booking Register book) and mirrored
   digitally where the assembly decides.
2. **Every member may inspect the register** at any time under the Right to
   Information (`00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/02-Right-to-Information-and-Audit.md`).
3. Entries are made on the day of submission, in order; backdating is a
   falsification offence (`04-Monitoring-and-Logs/01-Motor-Run-Log.md`).

## 5. RESOLVING A CLASH — THE MECHANICAL PATH

1. The committee lists both requests with their priorities and booking times.
2. The rule is applied: medical first, then scheduled by booking order, then
   others by booking order.
3. The losing request is offered the **next free slot** at the same terms.
4. The decision and its reasoning are written into the register and published.
5. A member who believes the rule was misapplied files a grievance
   (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/02-Resolution-Process/02-Step-2-Written-Grievance.md`)
   — the register and the log are the evidence, not anyone's recollection.

## 6. NO RESERVED SLOTS FOR ANYONE

1. There are **no standing reservations** — no family, no committee, no
   officer has a pre-booked permanent event slot.
2. Every event, including the assembly's own functions, enters the queue like
   any other.
3. A request that uses influence instead of the register is void and reported
   (`04-No-Personal-Preference-Rules.md`).

## 7. SCENARIO: TWO WEDDINGS, ONE SLOT

Two weddings book the same Saturday slot. Family A submitted 3 June; Family B
submitted 4 June. Neither has medical priority. The rule is applied: Family A
gets the slot; Family B is offered Sunday morning at the same fee. Both
decisions are written into the register and published. Family B's relative, who
sits on the committee, is told the same thing: the queue decides. **The register
settled it; no one had to choose sides.**

---

*The queue is a public record, not a negotiation. Priority is a published list,
not a personal favour. What is written in the register is what happened — for
everyone.*
