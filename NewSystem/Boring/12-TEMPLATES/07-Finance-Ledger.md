## Rules Reminder

- No cash moves without a voucher or receipt number.
- Each entry is entered by one member and verified by a different member.
- Balance is carried forward and checked against cash in hand each month.

---

**Notes:**
- Two eyes on every entry prevents single-point failure and errors (P2, P3).
- Vouchers and receipts are filed in sequence and are open to any member (P6).
- Month-end verification must match bank/cash balance and be presented to the assembly (P5).
