# MISUSE CONSEQUENCES

**Folder:** `06-FINANCE/04-Audit/`

---

## 1. WHAT MISUSE IS

Misuse of money is any act that moves system funds outside the rules:

| Act | Example |
|-----|---------|
| Receiving money without a receipt | Cash collected "for repairs", unrecorded |
| Spending without a voucher | Paying a mechanic with no paper |
| Falsifying records | Back-dated vouchers, two sets of books, altered pages |
| Cash handling | Keeping system money in a pocket or drawer |
| Abuse of crisis calls | A fake or inflated emergency collection |
| Favouritism | Fines for enemies, waivers for friends, selective charges |
| Personal gain | Using the funds or a "repair" for one's own household |
| Denial of access | Hiding the books, passbook, or vouchers from a member |

## 2. THE STANDARD OF PROOF

1. Misuse must be proven **by evidence**: records, bank statements, vouchers,
   receipts, witness accounts, photos. Rumour and "everyone knows" prove
   nothing.
2. Findings proceed through the Conflict Resolution process
   (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/02-Resolution-Process/`), where the facts decide
   (P5; `00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/`).
3. A reasonable explanation is heard before any conclusion. No one is
   condemned on an accusation alone.

## 3. THE CONSEQUENCES

When misuse is proven, the consequences apply in sequence:

| Step | Consequence |
|------|-------------|
| 1 | **Restitution** — the full diverted amount (plus any interest earned) is returned within a deadline set by the assembly, not exceeding 60 days, or by an installment plan for large sums |
| 2 | **Removal from all roles** — from the Finance Committee, signatory duty, receipt-book control, and every system office, immediately and for the whole office term |
| 3 | **Level 4 offence** — recorded in the register as a serious offence under the Enforcement Ladder |
| 4 | **Ladder escalation** — water restriction, exclusion, and due process per `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md` if restitution fails |
| 5 | **Legal option** — the assembly may, by 3/4 vote, pursue recovery through legal channels |

Restitution does not replace steps 2-5; it is the first step, not the last.

## 4. FAMILY AND COLLECTIVE RESPONSIBILITY

1. If two signatories from one household collude, it is one misuse, but both
   are removed and both are liable.
2. A committee that knowingly hides a member's misuse shares responsibility
   and faces the same consequences.
3. Misuse by a non-member (hired worker, vendor) ends the engagement; recovery
   is pursued and the records are corrected.

## 5. FALSE ACCUSATIONS

1. An accusation that is **honest but wrong** — made on genuine belief with
   real evidence and later disproved — has **no consequence** for the
   accuser. The system depends on members speaking up and will not punish
   honest error.
2. An accusation made in **bad faith** — invented or malicious, intended to
   damage an innocent member — faces consequences: a written apology, removal
   of the false record, and a Level 2 offence for the accuser
   (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/01-Conflict-Types/`).
3. The distinction is drawn from evidence of **intent**, not from who won the
   argument. An honest reporter of a real-looking discrepancy is thanked even
   when the discrepancy turns out to be a recording error.

## 6. RELATION TO OTHER PROCESSES

1. Misuse findings feed the dues register and the Enforcement Ladder; they do
   not replace it.
2. An ongoing misuse case does not stop the water supply of other members;
   the system's accounts are protected by removing the person, not by
   punishing the colony.
3. The books are audited again after any proven misuse to confirm the damage
   is fully mapped (`04-Audit/01-Regular-Audit.md`).

## 7. THE POINT OF THESE CONSEQUENCES

The aim is not revenge; it is that **money diverted is returned, power over
money is removed, and the next person tempted knows the price**. A colony
that restores its funds and cleans its roles protects every member (P7, P10).
