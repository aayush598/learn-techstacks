# REGULAR AUDIT

**Folder:** `06-FINANCE/04-Audit/`

---

## 1. THE ANNUAL INDEPENDENT AUDIT

At least once a financial year, the books are checked by an **independent
auditor** — a person with no stake in the colony's internal politics. The
audit is not a formality: its findings are read at the Annual General Meeting
(AGM), and its recommendations are acted on within a fixed time.

## 2. WHO MAY AUDIT

The auditor is chosen by lot or by invitation from another colony, as
described in `02-Auditor-Rotation.md`. The auditor must be:

- **Independent** — not a member, not a relative of a member of the Finance
  Committee, and not connected to any signatory's household;
- **Capable** — literate in accounts and willing to go row by row;
- **Impartial** — paid a modest, budgeted honorarium, not a fee that depends
  on the outcome.

## 3. WHAT THE AUDIT CHECKS

The audit is a **four-way cross-check** plus the minutes:

| Check | What is compared |
|-------|------------------|
| Ledger vs Bank | Every ledger row against the bank statement; every deposit and withdrawal explained |
| Ledger vs Vouchers | Every expense row has a voucher with attachments; no voucher has no row |
| Ledger vs Receipts | Every income row has a receipt or bank record; every receipt number is accounted for |
| Ledger vs Minutes | Every approved spend appears in the minutes; every minute-approved spend appears in the ledger |
| Books vs Rules | Fine schedule applied, budget ceilings respected, no cash held, fund transfers authorized |

The auditor also:

- counts cash (there should be **none** in any hand);
- verifies the three sub-account balances;
- spot-checks at least 20% of transactions by voucher;
- checks that no single household controlled both entry and verification.

## 4. THE AUDIT REPORT

1. The auditor produces a written report with:
   - a clean/qualified statement per area;
   - a numbered list of **findings** (errors, gaps, anomalies) and
     **recommendations**;
   - the amounts involved for each finding.
2. The report is **read aloud at the AGM** and a copy is posted with the
   statement file.
3. The report is kept in the records retention system
   (`11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/03-Records-Retention.md`).

## 5. ACTION ON FINDINGS — 30 DAYS

1. The Finance Committee responds to **every** finding in writing within
   **30 days** of the report: correction made, or explanation with evidence,
   or escalation to the assembly.
2. Corrections to the ledger are made by the strike-through method
   (`03-Bookkeeping/01-Ledger-Format.md`) — never by rewriting history.
3. Findings that point to possible misuse are handed to the Conflict
   Resolution process and `05-Misuse-Consequences.md` immediately; they are
   not buried in the response letter.
4. Unresolved findings carry over into the next audit and are reported as
   "carried over".

## 6. WHAT THE AUDIT IS NOT

- It is not an "all-clear" certificate from a friend.
- It is not a chance to "adjust" the books to look clean.
- It does not replace the right of any member to audit at any time
  (`04-Right-of-Any-Member-to-Audit.md`) or the surprise audit
  (`03-Surprise-Audit.md`).

## 7. AUDIT OF THE AUDITOR

The auditor's own conduct — fees taken, findings suppressed, books returned —
is checked at the next audit. A suppressor of findings is reported to the
assembly and the records are opened.

---

*The yearly audit is the full-body check-up. A clean report is only true if
the check was real; the rules above make sure it is.*
