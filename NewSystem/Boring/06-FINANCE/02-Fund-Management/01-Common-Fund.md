# COMMON FUND

**Folder:** `06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/`

---

## 1. WHAT THE COMMON FUND IS

The Common Fund is the **day-to-day operating wallet** of the system. It pays
power, stipends, administrative expenses, and the monthly allocation to the
Repair Fund. It is the only fund from which routine spending happens without
a special assembly vote, and even then only:

- within the approved Annual Budget (`03-Bookkeeping/05-Annual-Budget.md`),
- against an approved voucher (`03-Bookkeeping/02-Expense-Voucher-Rule.md`),
- by dual signatories (`04-Bank-Account-and-Signatories.md`).

## 2. WHERE THE MONEY COMES FROM

| Source | Rule |

| Repayment of internal loans, if any | As decided |

## 3. MINIMUM BALANCE RULE

1. The Common Fund must always hold a **minimum balance** equal to
   **two months of the total Common Fund budget** (power + stipends + admin +
   repair allocation). The exact figure is fixed in the Annual Budget.
2. No expense may be approved that would take the balance **below the
   minimum**, except:
   - to pay power (power is never deferred), or
   - by a **3/4 assembly vote** for a declared reason, or
   - in a genuine emergency with ratification at the next assembly.
3. When the balance falls below the minimum, the Finance Committee must
   report it at the next meeting and the assembly decides the restoration
   path (budget adjustment or a crisis call under
   `05-Fund-Raise-Crisis-Calls.md`).

## 4. WHO CAN WITHDRAW — AND WHO CANNOT

1. Withdrawal is made **only** from the joint account, by **any two of the
   three signatories** acting together
   (`04-Bank-Account-and-Signatories.md`).
2. **No single member, including a signatory, can withdraw, transfer, or
   borrow from the fund.**
3. Signatories come from **three different households** and rotate yearly;
   no family can control the account.
4. Every withdrawal is entered in the ledger with its voucher number **before
   or on the same day** the payment is made.

## 5. WHAT THE COMMON FUND PAYS

| Expense | Basis |
|---------|-------|
| Electricity | Power bill, on time, never deferred |
| Stipends / compensation | `03-ROTATION-DUTY/03-Duty-Compensation/01-Time-Credits.md` |
| Repair Fund allocation | Monthly budgeted transfer (`03-Repair-Fund.md`) |
| Admin (stationery, bank charges, board, notice copies) | Budget line |
| Audit honorarium (if any) | Budget line |

Repairs are paid from the **Repair Fund**, not directly from the Common Fund.
A repair is paid from Common Fund only if the Repair Fund is exhausted **and**
the assembly has authorized the interim transfer.

## 6. MONTHLY STATEMENT

1. The Finance Committee issues a **Common Fund statement every month**:
   opening balance, all income, all expenses (each with its entry and voucher
   number), ending balance, and the minimum-balance position.
2. The statement is posted to the members' group and filed in the books
   (`03-Bookkeeping/04-Bimonthly-Statement.md` is the formal published
   statement; the monthly one is the internal working copy).
3. Any member may verify the statement against the ledger and the bank
   records at any time (`04-Audit/04-Right-of-Any-Member-to-Audit.md`).

## 7. WHAT MUST NEVER HAPPEN

1. Money kept at a member's house "for safety".
2. "Temporary loans" from the fund to a member or officer.
3. A second, unofficial cash kitty outside the bank account.
4. Paying a member for work without a voucher, "because he will take cash".

Each of these is suspected misappropriation (`04-Audit/05-Misuse-Consequences.md`)
and is a Level 4 offence when proven.

---

*The Common Fund is the visible heart of the system: no secret deposits, no
hidden withdrawals, no cash. Every member can watch it beat.*
