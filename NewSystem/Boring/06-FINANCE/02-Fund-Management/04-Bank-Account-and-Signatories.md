# BANK ACCOUNT AND SIGNATORIES

**Folder:** `06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/`

---

## 1. THE SINGLE ACCOUNT RULE

All system money lives in **one joint bank account** in the name of the
colony water system (with its own name and registered details), operating
with sub-accounts (Common, Reserve, Repair) tracked in the ledger. There are
**no other accounts** and **no cash kits**. In the old system money was kept
in a drawer, in a tin, or in someone's savings account — this rule ends that
permanently.

## 2. THE THREE SIGNATORIES

1. The account has exactly **three signatories**, one from **three different
   households**.
2. **Any two of the three** must sign together for any withdrawal, transfer,
   or payment. Two signatures are mandatory; the bank is instructed
   accordingly ("Any 2 of 3").
3. No signatory may be a spouse or close relative of another signatory, and
   no two signatories may come from the same household or business.
4. No signatory may also be the current auditor
   (`04-Audit/02-Auditor-Rotation.md`).
5. The three signatories are chosen by the General Assembly from a published
   list of volunteers. No one self-appoints.

> **Scenario:** A brother and sister are put up as two signatories. Rejected:
> they are from the same household's influence circle, and a "dual signature"
> that belongs to one family is a single point of failure (P3).

## 3. YEARLY ROTATION

1. Signatories serve **one year**, from one Annual Budget assembly to the
   next.
2. At each yearly rotation, the assembly elects signatories afresh. A
   signatory may serve again only after **one full year away** (except when
   no eligible volunteer exists, in which case the assembly records the
   exception and the auditor's notice).
3. Rotation is staggered so the account never depends on one person, and
   handover is documented in the ledger and minutes.

## 4. TRANSACTION RULES

1. **All** payments are by cheque, bank transfer, or UPI from the joint
   account. Cheques are written only with a voucher number on the counterfoil.
2. A withdrawal requires: the voucher (for expenses) or the approved transfer
   note (for fund movement), and two signatures, **in that order** — voucher
   first, money second (`03-Bookkeeping/02-Expense-Voucher-Rule.md`).
3. **Deposits:** all income goes into the joint account. Cash deposited by a
   single person is acceptable **only** with an immediate numbered receipt
   from the receipt book (`03-Bookkeeping/03-Receipt-Requirement.md`) and the
   deposit slip attached; otherwise it is treated as suspected
   misappropriation.
4. No "petty cash drawer" exists. If a small purchase (e.g. stationery) is
   unavoidable in cash, the buyer pays their own money, banks the balance
   first, and is reimbursed from the account against the voucher within 7
   days. Cash reimbursement happens by transfer, never from a drawer.

## 5. EVIDENCE AT EVERY MEETING

1. The **passbook or online statement** is shown at **every General Assembly
   meeting** and every Finance Committee meeting, along with the ledger.
2. The current balance is read aloud and recorded in the minutes.
3. Any member may photograph the passbook or download the statement at the
   meeting or at any working hour
   (`00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/02-Right-to-Information-and-Audit.md`).

## 6. BANK STATEMENTS ARE FILED

1. Every month, the bank statement is **printed or saved** and filed with the
   books in date order.
2. The Finance Committee reconciles the bank statement against the ledger
   each month; any difference is investigated the same week and resolved.
3. Statements, passbook pages, and ledger pages are matched by the auditors
   (`04-Audit/01-Regular-Audit.md`).

## 7. THINGS THAT ARE FORBIDDEN

1. Two signatories from the same family.
2. A single signatory withdrawing alone.
3. A signatory holding the passbook or debit card at home "for safety".
4. An account in one signatory's personal name.
5. Withdrawing money before a voucher exists.
6. A second account opened by any individual or committee.

Each is a serious finance offence (Level 4 when proven) and grounds for
immediate removal from the signatory role.

## 8. CHANGE OF SIGNATORIES

1. If a signatory leaves, dies, moves, or is removed, the assembly appoints a
   replacement at the earliest meeting; until then the remaining two may
   transact only the minimum needed to keep power and supply running, and
   must report the situation immediately.
2. Bank nomination documents are updated within 30 days.

---

*The account is not anyone's money; it is the system's single nerve centre,
guarded by three independent hands, witnessed by the whole colony.*
