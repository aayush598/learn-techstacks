# RESERVE FUND

**Folder:** `06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/`

---

## 1. WHAT THE RESERVE FUND IS

The Reserve Fund is the **long-term safety cushion** of the system. It exists
so that the motor can be replaced, a new boring can be drilled, or a major
crisis can be met **without begging every household for money at the worst
moment**. In the old system every breakdown triggered a panic collection at an
unverifiable amount. The Reserve Fund makes those panic collections the
exception, not the routine.

## 2. WHAT IT IS FOR

The Reserve Fund covers only **large, planned, or catastrophic** costs:

1. Replacement of the motor or major pumpset;
2. A new boring / re-boring (`09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/01-New-Boring-Installation/02-Funding-and-Shares.md`);
3. Major network replacement;
4. Any genuine emergency that the assembly or the ratified procedure declares
   to be a Reserve case.

Routine repairs and preventive maintenance are **not** Reserve Fund items;
they belong to the Repair Fund (`03-Repair-Fund.md`).

## 3. HOW IT IS BUILT

1. A **fixed monthly top-up**, set in the Annual Budget as a line item (for
   example, 10-15% of the monthly contribution, or a fixed sum per month).
   The top-up is part of the monthly contribution formula
   (`01-Contributions/01-Monthly-Contribution.md`), so it is not an optional
   donation.
2. **Buy-in capital** from new and late-joining members is credited here per
   `09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/03-Late-Joiner-Buy-in/` and
   `09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/01-New-Boring-Installation/02-Funding-and-Shares.md`.
3. Interest earned on the account, if any, is credited to the Reserve Fund.
4. A share of any year-end surplus may be added by the assembly
   (`05-Distribution-and-Refunds/01-Surplus-Distribution.md`).

## 4. THE TARGET

1. The assembly sets a **target** for the Reserve Fund in the Annual Budget:
   the estimated replacement cost of the motor (or the largest single item the
   fund is expected to cover) plus one year's operating buffer.
2. The estimate is refreshed **every year** using current market quotes
   (motor replacement cost, boring cost).
3. When the target is reached, the monthly top-up may be reduced or
   redirected by a 2/3 assembly vote; it may never be redirected by the
   Finance Committee alone.

## 5. WHEN THE MONEY CAN BE USED

| Case | Authority |
|------|-----------|
| Planned project (motor replacement, new boring) | **3/4 of all member connections** voting in the General Assembly, after a published cost sheet |
| Genuine emergency (imminent total failure, no water) | Immediate action by dual signatories **limited to the minimum needed**, with the invoice; **ratification by 3/4 vote at the next assembly**; if ratification fails, the withdrawn amount is restored from the Common Fund and the signatories answer for the decision |
| Routine repair | **Never** from Reserve; use Repair Fund |
| Any other purpose | Assembly 3/4 vote |

No individual and no committee, including the Finance Committee, can touch
the Reserve Fund for their own judgment call.

## 6. WHAT "GENUINE EMERGENCY" MEANS

A genuine emergency exists only when **all** of the following are true:

1. The water supply is stopped or will stop within days;
2. The Repair Fund is insufficient;
3. Waiting for an assembly meeting would cause or prolong a complete
   shutdown;
4. The minimum possible amount is needed to restore supply.

Emergency use is recorded immediately, posted publicly, and brought for
ratification at the next assembly. Using the "emergency" label to skip the
3/4 vote for a non-emergency is a Level 4 offence.

## 7. WHY THIS PROTECTS MEMBERS

1. Members are **protected from sudden distress collections**: a motor death
   is paid from money already saved, not from fresh demands on a household
   already stretched thin.
2. Members are **protected from inflated emergency quotes**: the Reserve Fund
   is spent through the same multiple-quote and approval process as any repair
   (`05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/04-Vendor-Management/`).
3. Members **see the cushion grow**: the Reserve balance appears in every
   bimonthly statement (`03-Bookkeeping/04-Bimonthly-Statement.md`).

---

*The Reserve Fund is the answer to the old question "where will the money come
from when the motor dies?" — from the fund, already saved, publicly, on the
assembly's authority.*
