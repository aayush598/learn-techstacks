# REFUND ON EXIT

**Folder:** `06-FINANCE/05-Distribution-and-Refunds/`

---

## 1. THE PRINCIPLE

A member who leaves the system is entitled to **their own money back** — the
amount they paid, minus what they actually consumed, minus any dues and
damages. In the old system a departing member was told "your money is gone
into the system" and nothing was returned. Here the exit settlement is a
**calculation with a formula and a deadline**, signed off by two people, so
leaving is fair and leaving is clean.

## 2. WHAT THE MEMBER HAS PAID

A departing member's total paid-in amount includes:

| Item | Included? |
|------|-----------|
| Monthly contributions | Yes |
| Buy-in capital | Per its own rule — `09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/03-Late-Joiner-Buy-in/` and `09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/01-New-Boring-Installation/02-Funding-and-Shares.md` |
| Event fees, fines, penalties | No — these were charges for services and harm, not savings |
| Crisis-call payments | Yes, as paid |
| Time-credits | Settled separately — `04-Credit-Settlement.md` |

## 3. THE SETTLEMENT FORMULA

```
Refund =
  ( Total contributions paid )
  - ( Consumed share )
  - ( Dues and unpaid charges )
  - ( Damages attributable to the member )
  + ( Any credit due to the member, e.g. surplus shares or over-payments )
```

Where:

- **Consumed share** is the member's fair share of operating cost over their
  period of membership (pro-rata of the year's actual per-connection cost, or
  the actual billed consumption if meters exist
  (`01-Contributions/03-Consumption-Based-Charges.md`)).
- **Dues** are from the ledger and the dues list
  (`03-Bookkeeping/04-Bimonthly-Statement.md`).
- **Damages** are only proven, documented amounts (for example, meter or
  pipe damage with an invoice) — never an invented "exit fee".
- **No exit fee exists.** A departure is settled by formula, not by a
  discretionary charge.

## 4. THE CALCULATION IS PUBLIC

1. The Finance Committee prepares a **settlement statement** for the member
   in writing: each line, each amount, each source entry number.
2. The member may question any line; questions are answered with the ledger
   evidence.
3. The settlement follows the same evidence rule as billing disputes (P5,
   `01-Contributions/03-Consumption-Based-Charges.md`): facts decide, not
   the majority and not the departing member's temper.

## 5. THE TIMELINE

| Step | Deadline |
|------|----------|
| Written exit notice received | Day 0 |
| Settlement statement issued to the member | Within 21 days |
| Member's questions answered / agreement reached | Within 35 days |
| Refund paid by transfer from the joint account | Within 60 days of the notice |

1. The 60-day outer limit protects the system from paying before the annual
   reconciliation, while the 21-day statement protects the member from
   endless delay.
2. If the settlement is negative (dues exceed the refund), the member clears
   the balance within 30 days or the dues go to the Enforcement Ladder.

## 6. DUAL SIGN-OFF

1. The settlement is **signed by two members** before any payment: one from
   the Finance Committee and one independent member (or the signatories
   acting under `02-Fund-Management/04-Bank-Account-and-Signatories.md`).
2. Two signatures prevent one officer from "adjusting" a refund to punish or
   favour a leaver.
3. The departing member signs the settlement as acknowledgement; refusal to
   sign does not stop the process, but the settlement is held open for 30
   days for objections.

## 7. BUY-IN CAPITAL — ITS OWN RULE

1. Buy-in capital is not the same as monthly contributions. It is governed
   by its own rules: whether it is refundable, transferable, or converted to
   a share of assets depends on `09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/03-Late-Joiner-Buy-in/`
   and `09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/01-New-Boring-Installation/02-Funding-and-Shares.md`.
2. Where the buy-in rule is silent, the refund follows this file's formula;
   where the buy-in rule is specific, the specific rule wins.

## 8. PAYMENT MODE AND RECORD

1. The refund is paid by bank transfer, never in cash.
2. The payment is entered in the ledger with the exit entry number and the
   member's account is closed (or marked "exit pending clearance").
3. The settlement record is filed and retained
   (`11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/03-Records-Retention.md`).

---

*Exit is a member's right, not a favour. The formula, the public statement,
the two signatures, and the 60-day deadline make the door as honest as the
house.*
