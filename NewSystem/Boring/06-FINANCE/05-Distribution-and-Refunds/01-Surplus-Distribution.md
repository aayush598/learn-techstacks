# SURPLUS DISTRIBUTION

**Folder:** `06-FINANCE/05-Distribution-and-Refunds/`

---

## 1. WHAT A SURPLUS IS

A surplus arises when, at year end, the actual income exceeds the actual
spending after the Reserve Fund target has been met. A surplus is **good
news** — and, in the old system, the most dangerous news, because a surplus
was the moment the money-keeper "forgot" to return anything. Here the surplus
is visible, decided in public, and never kept secretly.

## 2. HOW A SURPLUS IS FOUND

1. At year end, after the audit (`04-Audit/01-Regular-Audit.md`), the
   Finance Committee presents **actuals vs budget**
   (`03-Bookkeeping/05-Annual-Budget.md`).
2. The surplus is computed in writing:

```
Surplus = ( total actual income )
          - ( total actual expense )
          - ( any shortfall in Reserve Fund top-ups for the year )
```

3. The figure is published with the year-end statement
   (`03-Bookkeeping/04-Bimonthly-Statement.md`).

## 3. THE FIRST CLAIM ON THE SURPLUS: THE RESERVE FUND

Before any distribution, the Reserve Fund top-ups for the year are
completed. The Reserve Fund is the colony's cushion
(`02-Fund-Management/02-Reserve-Fund.md`); the surplus does not skip the
cushion.

## 4. THE OPTIONS

After the Reserve top-ups, the assembly chooses, by vote, among these options
(or a published combination):

| Option | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Rebate** | Cash refund to members, proportional to what each actually contributed (`02-Dividend-by-Contribution.md`) |
| **Credit** | Amount credited against each member's future monthly contributions |
| **Extra Reserve** | Keep part or all in the Reserve Fund, above target, for future projects |
| **Repair top-up** | Move part to the Repair Fund for a declared upcoming need |

The decision is made at the **Annual General Assembly**, by a vote of member
connections, recorded in the minutes.

## 5. NEVER SECRETLY KEPT

1. No officer, committee, or signatory may retain, reinvest, or spend a
   surplus on their own. Any such act is suspected misappropriation
   (`04-Audit/05-Misuse-Consequences.md`).
2. A surplus that is not distributed in cash must be visible: as credit on
   members' ledger accounts, or as an increased Reserve/Repair balance shown
   in the statement. It never vanishes into a "balance fund" that nobody can
   name.
3. The members' group is shown the surplus line in the same statement that
   announces the decision.

## 6. SMALL SURPLUSES

If the surplus is smaller than one month's total contribution, the assembly
may, by simple vote, roll it into the next year's budget as an income line,
shown in the statement. Even a small surplus is a numbered line, never a
silent leftover.

> **Scenario:** Year-end actuals show ₹18,000 above plan. The assembly votes
> to give a rebate: members who contributed fully get a proportional credit
> against next month's bill. The credit appears as a line on every member's
> ledger. Nobody kept anything.

## 7. SURPLUS AND DIVIDEND PRINCIPLE

Distribution, when in cash or credit, follows the dividend rule in
`02-Dividend-by-Contribution.md`: those who contributed more receive
proportionally more. This is not a bonus for the rich; it is the fair return
of each member's own money (P4, P10).

---

*A surplus proves the system is healthy. It is returned to the members who
created it, in the open, or it is clearly parked in a named fund — never
silently absorbed.*
