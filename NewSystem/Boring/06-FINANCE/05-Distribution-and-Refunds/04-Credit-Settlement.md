# CREDIT SETTLEMENT

**Folder:** `06-FINANCE/05-Distribution-and-Refunds/`

---

## 1. WHAT TIME-CREDITS ARE

Time-credits are the system's record of **work done for the community**:
duty shifts, watch duty, skilled repairs, meter reading, training, and other
service, valued at the published rate
(`03-ROTATION-DUTY/03-Duty-Compensation/01-Time-Credits.md`). They exist so
that contribution can be paid in labour as well as money (P4, P8) — and they
must settle honestly when a member leaves, exactly like money.

## 2. THE CREDIT LEDGER

1. Every member has a credit account maintained in the ledger
   (`03-Bookkeeping/01-Ledger-Format.md`) or its companion duty ledger:
   credits earned (date, duty, hours, rate) and credits used (offset against
   dues, cash paid out).
2. The balance is public to the member and shown in the duty statements.
3. Credits are earned against **recorded, verified duty** — the duty log
   signed by the verifier (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/01-Duty-Roster/`). Hours that
   were not logged were not served.

## 3. WHAT CREDITS CAN BE USED FOR

| Use | Rule |
|-----|------|
| Offset against own dues / monthly contributions | At the current published rate |
| Offset against fines | Only for duty-related misses, per `01-Contributions/04-Penalties-and-Fines.md` |
| Cash payout | Only on exit, at the current rate (below) |
| Contribution counting | Counted as contribution for distribution purposes (`02-Dividend-by-Contribution.md`) |

Credits are **personal** — they cannot be transferred to another member's
account or bought and sold.

## 4. SETTLEMENT ON EXIT — THE OPTIONS

On exit (`03-Refund-on-Exit.md`), the member's credit balance is settled in
one of two ways, at the member's choice unless the assembly rule says
otherwise:

| Option | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Payout at the current rate** | The credit balance is converted to money at the **current published rate** and added to the refund from the joint account |
| **Offset against dues** | The credit balance cancels the member's unpaid dues, fines, and damages first; the remainder (if any) is paid out |

1. The rate used is the rate in force on the day of settlement — never a
   "face value" invented for the occasion, and never the historic rate if the
   published rate has changed.
2. If the member chooses offset, the offset is recorded: dues cancelled,
   credits debited, balance zeroed or reduced.
3. If the member chooses payout, the amount is paid by transfer within the
   same 60-day window as the refund.

## 5. THE RECORD KEPT

1. Every settlement — credits debited, cash paid, dues offset — is entered in
   the ledger with the exit entry number and signed by the same two members
   who sign the refund (`03-Refund-on-Exit.md`, Section 6).
2. The settled credit account is closed and filed.
3. A credit record may be audited like any book
   (`04-Audit/04-Right-of-Any-Member-to-Audit.md`).

## 6. PROTECTIONS

1. A member's credits are **their property**, earned by their work; no
   committee may cancel, reduce, or "donate" them without the member's
   written consent.
2. If the current published rate has fallen below the rate at which the
   credits were earned, the member is still paid at the **current** rate —
   the rate is a rule, not a penalty. (Symmetrically, a member who owes dues
   offsets at the current rate.)
3. A member who served work that was never recorded may claim it within 30
   days of the duty date, with two witness confirmations; after that the
   log is final.

## 7. CREDITS AND HARDSHIP

A hardship-tier member (`01-Contributions/02-Contribution-Tiers.md`) who
pays part of their contribution through alternate duty hours earns the same
credits at the same rate as any member. Their credits are settled by the
same rules — the system never discounts the poor member's labour.

---

*Time is money in this system only in one direction: honestly measured,
publicly recorded, and fairly settled. The credit account guarantees that a
day of work for the colony is never a day lost.*
