# EXPENSE VOUCHER RULE

**Folder:** `06-FINANCE/03-Bookkeeping/`

---

## 1. THE RULE IN ONE LINE

> **No voucher, no payment.**

In the old system the pattern was: pay cash to a mechanic, bring back a
memory, and the "expense" was whatever the money-keeper said. This rule makes
the paper come **before** the money.

## 2. WHAT A VOUCHER CONTAINS

Every expense voucher carries at least:

| Field | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| Date | Of the approval and of the payment |
| What | The item or service purchased |
| Why | The fault, the maintenance schedule item, or the budget line it serves |
| Who approved | Name + role (per the Repair Approval Matrix or budget authority) |
| Amount | In words and figures |
| Payment mode | Bank transfer / cheque / UPI, with reference |
| Attachments | Quote, invoice, bill, photos, delivery note, bank slip |
| Voucher No. | Running number from the voucher book |

A voucher with a missing "what", "why", or "who approved" is incomplete and
**cannot be paid**.

## 3. THE ORDER OF EVENTS

```
Approval (matrix)  -->  Voucher written  -->  Voucher entered in ledger
-->  Two signatories pay from the account  -->  Attachments filed with voucher
```

1. First the repair or purchase is approved under the applicable rule
   (`05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/02-Breakdown-Repair/03-Repair-Approval-Matrix.md`
   for repairs; the Annual Budget for operating items).
2. Then the voucher is written with the approved amount.
3. Then the ledger row is made (`01-Ledger-Format.md`) with the voucher
   number.
4. **Only then** do two signatories release payment from the joint account
   (`02-Fund-Management/04-Bank-Account-and-Signatories.md`).
5. The invoice/bill and evidence are clipped to the voucher.

## 4. WHAT NO VOUCHER MEANS

1. Payment without a voucher is **void as a system expense**. The payer
   (whoever paid) has no claim against the system for it.
2. Payment claimed "orally" or "we will make the voucher later" is treated as
   suspected misappropriation and investigated
   (`04-Audit/05-Misuse-Consequences.md`).
3. A voucher that is back-written after the payment to make it look clean is
   falsification of records — Level 4.

## 5. PAYMENT MODES

1. Payments are made **only** from the joint account by transfer, cheque, or
   UPI — never in cash from a drawer.
2. If a vendor insists on cash for a tiny purchase, a member may pay from
   their own pocket and claim reimbursement against a voucher within 7 days;
   the system repays by transfer.
3. "Pay cash to the mechanic, he does not take transfer" is not an excuse to
   break the rule; the payment can wait until a bank-transfer arrangement is
   made, except in a genuine emergency where the amount is small and is
   receipted and banked immediately.

## 6. FILING

1. Vouchers are filed in **date order**, each in a numbered pocket, in the
   common register box.
2. The voucher number on the ledger row must lead directly to the paper.
3. Vouchers are retained per the records retention rule
   (`11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/03-Records-Retention.md`).

## 7. WHO CAN APPROVE A VOUCHER

| Category | Approver |
|----------|----------|
| Budgeted operating item (power, stipend, admin) | Finance Committee within the budget line |
| Small repair | Duty/PM team per the matrix |
| Medium repair | Finance Committee + one independent member |
| Large repair / project | Assembly on a published cost sheet |

No approver may approve a voucher for a payment to themselves, their spouse,
or their household; such a voucher needs the assembly or two independent
members.

---

*The voucher is the paper spine of every rupee spent. Money follows paper;
paper never follows money.*
