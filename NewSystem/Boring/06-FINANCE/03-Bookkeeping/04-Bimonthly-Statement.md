# BIMONTHLY STATEMENT

**Folder:** `06-FINANCE/03-Bookkeeping/`

---

## 1. THE PUBLICATION DUTY

Every **two months**, the Finance Committee publishes a **statement of
accounts to all members**. This is not optional and not "for the committee
only". In the old system accounts were a mystery revealed once a year, or
never. Here they are published on a fixed rhythm that no member has to ask
for.

## 2. WHAT THE STATEMENT CONTAINS

| Part | Content |
|------|---------|
| Income | Every source in the period: contributions, event fees, fines, crisis calls, grants, interest — each with its entry numbers |
| Expenses | Every payment: power, stipends, repair spends, admin — each with its voucher numbers |
| Balances | Common Fund, Reserve Fund, Repair Fund opening and closing balances |
| Dues list | Each member not paid in full, with the amount and period (amount and arrear only; no commentary) |
| Receipts issued | Count of receipts issued vs cash banked |
| Over-collection / refunds | Any crisis-call refunds pending or paid |
| Notes | Corrections made, anomalies found, items flagged for audit |

The statement also shows the **minimum-balance position** of the Common Fund
and the **target position** of the Reserve Fund
(`02-Fund-Management/01-Common-Fund.md`, `02-Fund-Management/02-Reserve-Fund.md`).

## 3. FORMAT AND DELIVERY

1. The statement follows the template at `12-TEMPLATES/07-Finance-Ledger.md`
   (statement pages) and is dated, numbered (S-01, S-02, ...), and signed by
   two Finance Committee members.
2. It is published **in all three places**: the notice board, the members'
   group (as photo or file), and the common register box.
3. It is published **by the 20th** of every second month (for periods ending,
   e.g., 30 June, 31 August, 31 October, 31 December, 28 February, 30 April).
4. A printed copy is filed with the books.

## 4. QUESTIONS MUST BE ANSWERED

1. Any member may raise questions about the statement in writing or in the
   members' group.
2. The Finance Committee answers **in writing, with entry and voucher
   references**, within **7 days**. A question that cannot be answered with
   paper is a defect in the books, not in the questioner.
3. Questions and answers are kept with the statement file; repeated
   unanswered questions are escalated to the auditor
   (`04-Audit/03-Surprise-Audit.md`).

## 5. THE DUES LIST

1. The dues list names every member with arrears and the exact amount.
   Dues information is a fact, not a judgement, and is published as a fact.
2. A member whose arrears are fully settled is removed from the list in the
   next statement.
3. Arrears are not "forgiven" by any committee; a dues waiver is an assembly
   decision only, and only for hardship under
   `01-Contributions/02-Contribution-Tiers.md`.
4. Continued arrears move to the Enforcement Ladder
   (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`).

## 6. WHEN THE STATEMENT IS DISCUSSED

1. The statement is an agenda item at every **General Assembly meeting that
   falls in the month of publication**.
2. The Finance Committee reads the summary aloud; members may go row by row.
3. The meeting minutes record the statement number, the balances, and every
   question raised and answered.

## 7. IF THE STATEMENT IS LATE

A late statement is itself a flag:

1. The Finance Committee explains the delay in writing at the next meeting.
2. A delay beyond 30 days triggers a **surprise audit** automatically
   (`04-Audit/03-Surprise-Audit.md`).
3. Two late statements in a row is a governance failure, reported to the
   assembly and the auditor.

---

*The bimonthly statement is the pulse of the system that members read without
asking. If the pulse stops, the patient is examined — not told to wait.*
