# CONTRIBUTION TIERS

**Folder:** `06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/`

---

## 1. THE DEFAULT: ONE BASE FOR ALL

The default rule is a **single base contribution for every member connection**,
set by the formula in `01-Monthly-Contribution.md`. Equal connections, equal
charges. This is the fairness baseline and it stands unless the assembly
expressly adopts a tier system by the rules below.

## 2. WHEN TIERS ARE ALLOWED

Tiers may exist **only** in two situations:

1. The assembly has adopted **consumption-based metering** for all or a subset
   of connections (`03-Consumption-Based-Charges.md`), or
2. The assembly has adopted a **hardship tier** to protect members in genuine
   financial distress (Section 5 below).

No other tier — "big-house tier", "shop tier", "favourite tier", "relative
tier" — may be created. A tier invented to charge one member more and another
member less is void and a Level 4 offence.

## 3. FAIRNESS CONDITIONS FOR ANY TIER SYSTEM

A tier structure is fair **only if all of the following hold**:

| Condition | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Every member can choose** | No tier is forced on any member; joining a higher or lower tier is the member's own choice, not a committee's decision |
| **Tiers are published** | The full tier table (rates, what each tier includes) is on the notice board and in the group before it takes effect |
| **Tiers are formula-based** | Each tier rate is derived from a published calculation, not a mood |
| **The base tier is always available** | A member who chooses the base tier is never penalised or shamed |
| **Tier choice is reviewable** | A member may move up or down once per quarter by written notice |

## 4. CONSUMPTION TIERS

Where meters exist (`03-Consumption-Based-Charges.md`):

- The published per-kilolitre (kL) rate is identical for all members; **usage
  tiers apply to consumption, not to the person**. A heavier user pays more
  because they use more, and everyone can see why.
- The meter reading procedure and dispute rule are in
  `03-Consumption-Based-Charges.md`.

> **Scenario:** The Finance Committee announces a new "gold tier" that costs
> more, and quietly puts two member-families into it without asking them.
> This is void: every member must choose their own tier, and the tier table
> must be published first.

## 5. THE HARDSHIP TIER

To honour P7 (protection of the weak):

1. **Who qualifies:** a member who can document genuine hardship — loss of
   employment, medical crisis, crop failure, or similar — and who applies in
   writing to the Finance Committee.
2. **Reduced fee:** the hardship tier pays a **reduced monthly contribution**
   (floor: 50% of the base rate, decided by the assembly in the budget).
3. **Alternate contribution:** the reduced fee is balanced by an alternate
   contribution that does not involve money — for example, additional duty
   shifts, watch-and-chat duty, or skilled volunteer hours — at a published
   rate per hour (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/03-Duty-Compensation/01-Time-Credits.md`).
4. **Documented:** the hardship decision, the reason, and the alternate
   contribution are written into the ledger as a note and into the minutes.
   The reason is recorded in one line; it is not a public confession.
5. **Reviewed every 6 months:** hardship status expires automatically after
   six months. Renewal requires a fresh application. A member found to have
   hidden their recovery is liable for the unpaid difference plus the fine
   schedule.
6. **No humiliation:** the hardship member's water right, vote, and dignity
   are unchanged. A member who mocks or publicises another's hardship commits
   a dignity offence under P10.

## 6. WHO DECIDES AND WHEN

- Tiers (if any) are part of the **Annual Budget**, adopted by the same 2/3
  vote as the budget.
- Tier changes follow the same yearly, published, 2/3-vote process as the
  monthly contribution (`01-Monthly-Contribution.md`).
- No committee can add, remove, or alter a tier between budgets.

## 7. NOTHING HERE CHANGES WATER SHARE

A tier affects **what a member pays**. It never affects how much water a
member is entitled to; water share is equal and fixed by the Constitution
(P1). Paying more buys no extra water and paying less loses no water.
