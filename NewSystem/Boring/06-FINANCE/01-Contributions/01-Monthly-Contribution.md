# MONTHLY CONTRIBUTION — THE FORMULA

**Folder:** `06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/`

---

## 1. THE PROBLEM THIS SOLVES

In the old system the monthly charge was whatever the money-keeper said it
was. He could double it overnight, reduce it for friends, and raise it as a
"repair" whenever he wanted pocket money. **This rule removes that power
completely: no person can set, change, waive, or increase the monthly
contribution. Only the published formula can.**

## 2. THE FORMULA

The monthly contribution per connection is:

```
Monthly Contribution =
  ( Power cost estimate for the year
    + Preventive maintenance provision
    + Stipend / compensation provision
    + Administrative provision
    + Reserve Fund top-up
    - Expected other income (fees, fines, subsidies) )
  / ( Number of member connections )
  / 12
```

All figures come from the approved Annual Budget
(`03-Bookkeeping/05-Annual-Budget.md`). The calculation is written out in
plain arithmetic and published with the budget. Any member can reproduce it
with a calculator and a copy of the budget.

## 3. PUBLICATION AND EFFECT

1. The contribution is published with the Annual Budget **at least 30 days
   before the start of the financial year** and again on the notice board.
2. It takes effect automatically on the first day of the new financial year.
3. It is **identical for every member connection** in the same tier
   (`02-Contribution-Tiers.md`).
4. A member who joins mid-year pays the pro-rata amount from the month of
   joining.

## 4. WHEN AND HOW IT IS CHANGED

1. The contribution is reviewed **once a year**, at the Annual Budget assembly.
2. A change requires a **two-thirds (2/3) vote of all member connections
   present and voting** at the General Assembly, and the change must be based
   on a revised, published calculation. A vote to change without a revised
   calculation is void.
3. No change takes effect during the financial year except through the
   crisis-call procedure (`02-Fund-Management/05-Fund-Raise-Crisis-Calls.md`).
4. **No officer, committee, family, or individual may change, waive, or reduce
   the contribution for any member**, including themselves. A reduction for one
   member without an assembly decision is a Level 4 offence
   (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`).

> **Scenario:** A member stands at a meeting and says, "I am raising the
> monthly charge from tomorrow." That statement is void. The charge changes
> only by a 2/3 assembly vote on a revised formula, and only from the next
> financial year.

## 5. COLLECTION

1. **Default method:** bank transfer, UPI, or cheque to the joint account.
   The member keeps the transfer record.
2. **Fallback:** payment in cash is accepted only at the collection counter of
   the Finance Committee, against a **numbered receipt from the receipt book**
   immediately (`03-Bookkeeping/03-Receipt-Requirement.md`). Cash so received
   must be banked the same working day. No member ever collects cash door to
   door.
3. Payment is due by the **10th of the month** for that month. After that the
   Late Fee schedule applies (`04-Penalties-and-Fines.md`).
4. The payment is credited to the **Common Fund** (`02-Fund-Management/01-Common-Fund.md`).

## 6. WHAT THE MONTHLY CONTRIBUTION IS NOT

- It is **not** a repair levy. Repairs are paid from the Repair Fund and the
  Reserve Fund, not by ad-hoc collections.
- It is **not** a fine. Fines are separate and go to the Common Fund.
- It is **not** negotiable by any office-holder.
- It is **not** collected in cash by volunteers who then "remember" the amount.

## 7. NON-PAYMENT

- Non-payment triggers the Late Fee schedule, then a dues notice
  (`03-Bookkeeping/04-Bimonthly-Statement.md`), then the Enforcement Ladder.
- A member in genuine hardship may apply for the hardship tier
  (`02-Contribution-Tiers.md`) or an installment plan under the crisis-call
  rule. The system protects the weak; it never abandons a member who asks
  through the published path.

---

*Applies to all member connections. Superseded only by a 2/3 assembly vote on
a revised, published formula. See Norm Hierarchy in
