# CONSUMPTION-BASED CHARGES

**Folder:** `06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/`

---

## 1. WHEN THIS RULE APPLIES

This rule applies **only if and only when** the General Assembly has adopted
water meters for connections, by a 2/3 vote in the Annual Budget. Without
assembly adoption, the flat monthly contribution (`01-Monthly-Contribution.md`)
remains the only charge. No meter, no committee decision, and no individual
can create consumption-based charges on their own.

## 2. THE PRINCIPLE

Where meters exist, charges follow **actual usage, openly measured**:

- A published **per-kilolitre (kL) rate**, identical for every member.
- Monthly charge = monthly usage in kL x per-kL rate, plus the fixed base
  contribution for system standing costs.
- The per-kL rate is derived from the budget (total expected consumption
  divided into the variable cost share) and is published with the budget.

## 3. METER INSTALLATION AND INVENTORY

1. Meters are common property (P1). They are listed in the Infrastructure
   Asset Register (`09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/05-Infrastructure-Asset-Register/`).
2. Meter cost and installation are a project expense funded from the Reserve
   Fund or a project fund, never as a private charge on one member.
3. Each meter has a sealed unit with a unique number; the sealing is noted in
   the register and photographed.

## 4. THE READING PROCEDURE

Every reading follows this exact ritual, so no one can fudge numbers:

1. **Two members read together**, from different households, per the duty
   roster — usually the meter-reader plus one witness. A single reader is not
   allowed.
2. **Photos are taken** of each meter reading (or a shared photo with the
   reading visible) and posted to the members' group on the same day.
3. Readings happen on a **fixed date** published in the roster (for example,
   the 1st of the month).
4. The two readers **both sign** the reading sheet; the sheet is attached to
   the monthly billing entry in the ledger.
5. Readings and photos are filed with the books
   (`03-Bookkeeping/`), where any member may inspect them.

## 5. BILLING

- The Finance Committee computes each member's bill from the signed reading
  sheet and the published rate, and publishes the bill list with the monthly
  statement.
- Payment follows the monthly contribution rules
  (`01-Monthly-Contribution.md`): bank transfer or numbered receipt, due by
  the 10th, late fee per `04-Penalties-and-Fines.md`.
- Estimated reads are allowed only when access is genuinely impossible; the
  estimate is marked "EST" in the ledger and corrected at the next actual
  read.

## 6. DISPUTES — RESOLVED BY EVIDENCE (P5)

A member who disputes their bill follows the evidence path:

1. **Compare evidence:** the member checks their own photo against the signed
   reading sheet and the bill. Both are public.
2. **Request a re-read:** a joint re-read with the meter-reader and one other
   member; a photo is taken and posted.
3. **Formal review:** if the dispute continues, the member files a written
   objection. The Finance Committee must respond in writing within 7 days,
   attaching the evidence.
4. **Truth rules:** the dispute is settled by the **evidence** (photos,
   seals, reading sheet, bank records) — not by a vote, not by who shouts
   louder, and not by the meter-reader's rank (P5, Truth over Majority).
5. **Meter fault:** if the meter is found defective (seal intact but reading
   clearly wrong), the member pays the average of the last three good months;
   the meter is repaired or replaced and the fault is noted in the register.
6. **Tampering:** a broken seal, a bypassed meter, or an altered reading is
   unauthorized water use (`04-Penalties-and-Fines.md`) and is not a billing
   dispute.

## 7. WHO PAYS FOR LEAKS AND WASTE

- Leaks in the **common network** are repaired from the Repair Fund; the
   wasted water cost is a system cost, not a member's bill.
- A leak **inside a member's private line** (beyond the connection point) is
   repaired at the member's cost after a written estimate; if the member
   ignores it, the Common Fund may not subsidise an open leak, and the duty
   team may disconnect the private line until repaired.

---

*No provision here may reduce the equal right to water. Charges reflect
usage; water share remains equal. See
`00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/01-Right-to-Fair-Water.md`.*
