# PENALTIES AND FINES

**Folder:** `06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/`

---

## 1. THE PRINCIPLE

Fines exist only to protect the common system from **measured harm** — late
money, missed duty, wasted or stolen water. A fine is **never** a tool of
punishment by a person against a person. In the old system fines were imposed
on one house to punish an argument and skipped for friends. That is now
impossible: a fine is imposed **by a published rule, on stated facts**, and
the amount is the same for every member.

## 2. WHAT A FINE REQUIRES

Every fine must have all four elements, or it is void:

| Element | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| **Rule** | The offence and its amount exist in this file or the budget — not invented on the spot |
| **Fact** | The offence is proven by evidence (ledger, roster, photo, reading sheet), not by rumour |
| **Same rate** | The identical amount applies to every member, without exception |
| **Publication** | The fine appears in the ledger and the next bimonthly statement |

## 3. THE FINE SCHEDULE

Amounts are set in the Annual Budget and published with it. Illustrative
schedule (the assembly may set different numbers, never different principles):

| Offence | Evidence | Fine (illustrative) |
|---------|----------|---------------------|
| Contribution paid after the 10th of the month | Ledger / bank record | 5% of the monthly amount, capped at 10% |
| Payment after 30 days | Ledger | 10% of the amount, plus dues notice |
| Duty shift missed without arranging a substitute | Duty log | 2 duty-shift credit equivalents as a **buy-out**, or one extra duty shift |
| Duty buy-out in cash | Duty log | Amount = published duty-compensation rate for the missed shift (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/03-Duty-Compensation/01-Time-Credits.md`) |
| Unauthorized water use (bypass, tap outside schedule, open-valve theft) | Photo / witness / log | Fixed penalty, plus restitution of the estimated water value |
| Tampering with meter, seal, or pipe | Photo / inspection | Fixed heavy penalty, plus repair cost, plus enforcement escalation |
| Failing to let a second member witness a meter read | Reading sheet | Fixed small penalty on the reader |

## 4. THE DUTY MISS BUY-OUT

1. Missing a duty shift is not automatically a cash fine. The member may:
   - **swap** with another member (arranged and recorded), or
   - **pay a buy-out** at the published rate, which goes into the Common Fund,
     or
   - **serve an extra shift** next cycle.
2. A member who neither swaps, nor buys out, nor serves the extra shift
   accumulates duty failure penalties per `03-ROTATION-DUTY/04-Duty-Failure/`.
3. Buy-out money is income of the Common Fund; it is not a personal reward to
   the person who covered the shift.

## 5. WHERE FINES GO

**Every fine, penalty, and buy-out rupee goes to the Common Fund**
(`02-Fund-Management/01-Common-Fund.md`) and is shown as income in the ledger
and the bimonthly statement. Fines are never paid to an individual, never
held back "as a token", and never used to top up a friend's shortfall.

## 6. IMPOSED BY RULE, NOT BY PERSON

1. The Finance Committee **records** fines; it does not **invent** them. If
   the fact fits a rule, the fine applies automatically.
2. A fine that has no matching rule in this file or the budget is **void**.
3. A member who believes a fine was applied selectively may object in writing;
   the objection goes to the Conflict Resolution process
   (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/02-Resolution-Process/`), where the facts decide.

> **Scenario:** A committee member says, "Last week this house missed duty, so
> we are fining them ₹1,000." If ₹1,000 is not the published amount for a duty
> miss, the fine is void regardless of who ordered it.

## 7. FINE AMOUNTS ARE PUBLISHED

- The full fine schedule is part of the Annual Budget
  (`03-Bookkeeping/05-Annual-Budget.md`) and is posted on the notice board.
- Every imposed fine appears by amount, rule, and entry number in the
  bimonthly statement — no anonymous fines.
- The schedule can change only with the budget, by the same 2/3 vote.

## 8. NO DOUBLE PUNISHMENT

1. One proven offence gets **one fine**, under one rule, once. If two rules
   could apply, the lighter applies.
2. Paying the fine does not erase restitution (repairing or paying for damage,
   returning stolen water value) and does not stop the Enforcement Ladder for
   serious offences — but a member is never fined twice for the same act.
3. A fine already paid is not "revoked" by a later vote; fines can be
   cancelled only if the underlying fact is shown to be false, in which case
   the amount is refunded and the error is corrected in the ledger.

## 9. HARDSHIP AND FINES

A member in the hardship tier (`02-Contribution-Tiers.md`) is not exempt from
fines for duty or water offences. If paying a fine would create genuine
distress, the member may request an installment plan through the crisis-call
rule (`02-Fund-Management/05-Fund-Raise-Crisis-Calls.md`); the fine is never
waived by an individual.

---

*Fines protect the system, never a person's ego. A fine that humiliates,
targets, or exempts is void and its imposer faces the Enforcement Ladder.*
