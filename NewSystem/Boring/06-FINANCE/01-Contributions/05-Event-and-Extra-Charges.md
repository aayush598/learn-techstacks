# EVENT AND EXTRA CHARGES

**Folder:** `06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/`

---

## 1. THE PRINCIPLE

Water is common property (P1). A private event that needs extra water or an
extra motor run consumes water and power that belong to all. Therefore it
pays a **published fee to the Common Fund** — not to the duty person, not to
the committee, not into a pocket.

In the old system a wedding "fee" was collected in cash and nobody knew where
it went. This rule ensures every event rupee is billed, receipted, and shown
in the public ledger.

## 2. WHAT IS AN EVENT OR EXTRA USE

1. **Event water:** water for a wedding, function, festival, ceremony, or any
   gathering hosted by a member or a third party on or off the colony.
2. **Extra run:** an additional motor run outside the published daily
   schedule (`04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/01-Daily-Schedule/`) requested for an event.
3. **Outside parties:** supply of water to a non-member household, a tanker,
   a construction site, or any person outside the colony, if approved.
4. **Bulk/construction use:** filling a tanker or large storage beyond the
   household's normal allowance.

## 3. THE FEES

| Use | Fee basis | Set by |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| Event water (member) | Fixed event fee per event + per-kL over the standard day's draw | Event Fee rule |
| Extra motor run | Fixed extra-run fee, or pro-rata of running cost | `04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/03-Event-Water/02-Event-Fee.md` |
| Outside-party water | Market-rate fee (never below the member cost) | Finance Committee, published |
| Tanker / bulk | Per-kL at the outside-party rate | Finance Committee, published |

All fees are published in the Annual Budget (`03-Bookkeeping/05-Annual-Budget.md`)
and on the notice board. The Finance Committee cannot change a published fee;
only the assembly can.

## 4. THE PROCESS — NO FREE WATER, NO HIDDEN WATER

1. **Advance request:** a member or organizer requests event water in writing
   (or on the standard form) at least **48 hours** ahead, stating the date,
   purpose, and expected quantity.
2. **Fee quote:** the Finance Committee gives a written quote from the
   published schedule.
3. **Payment in advance:** the fee is paid to the Common Fund by bank transfer
   or against a numbered receipt before the event run
   (`03-Bookkeeping/03-Receipt-Requirement.md`).
4. **Duty instruction:** the receipt/voucher number is attached to the duty
   instruction, so the duty person runs the motor only for a **paid and
   recorded** event. No receipt, no extra run.
5. **Log it:** the extra run is entered in the motor log
   (`04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/04-Monitoring-and-Logs/`) with the event entry
   number.
6. **Over-use:** if the event draws more water than quoted, the difference is
   billed at the same rate and collected after the event; if the user refuses,
   the matter goes to dues and the Enforcement Ladder.

## 5. GOES TO THE COMMON FUND

1. Every event fee, extra-run fee, and outside-party payment is recorded as
   income of the **Common Fund** in the ledger.
2. It is shown in the next bimonthly statement (`03-Bookkeeping/04-Bimonthly-Statement.md`).
3. It is **never** paid directly to the duty person, the pump operator, or
   the organizer. Payment to any individual — "for his hard work" — is treated
   as suspected misappropriation (`04-Audit/05-Misuse-Consequences.md`).

> **Scenario:** A member organizes a wedding, hands ₹500 cash to the duty
> person "for the extra run", and the run happens off the books. Both acts are
> void: the duty person must not run the motor without a receipted entry, and
> the member's payment is not recorded until it is receipted and banked.

## 6. EVENTS BY NON-MEMBERS

1. Water to anyone outside the colony is allowed **only** with assembly
   approval for the arrangement and a published rate; the Finance Committee
   cannot sell water to outsiders on its own.
2. Proceeds go to the Common Fund and appear in the statements.

## 7. RELIEF FROM EVENTS

1. A member in the hardship tier (`02-Contribution-Tiers.md`) pays **half**
   the event fee for their own family's essential ceremony, on application.
2. Fees for religious or charitable events are decided by the assembly in the
   budget; the Finance Committee has no power to waive a fee.
