# FINANCE — OVERVIEW

**Folder:** `06-FINANCE/`

---

## 1. WHY THIS SYSTEM EXISTS

Money is the most common instrument of exploitation in a shared water system.
In the old system one person or group could:

- raise the monthly charge at will, whenever they pleased;
- collect contributions in cash and hide how much was actually collected;
- quote any amount for a repair, since nobody else knew the real cost;
- use "repair" as an excuse to collect extra money with no evidence;
- favour friends and punish opponents through selective charges and fines.

This folder replaces discretion, cash, and secrecy with formula, evidence, and
publicity. The entire finance system rests on one golden rule:

> **NO cash transactions. Every rupee recorded. Every record public.**

## 2. THE MONEY FLOW

### 2.1 Income

| Source | Detail | Goes to |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Monthly contribution | Fixed per-connection amount by formula | Common Fund |
| Event / extra charges | Marriage water, event tankers, extra runs | Common Fund |
| Penalties and fines | Late fees, duty buy-outs, unauthorized use | Common Fund |
| Subsidies and grants | Government or agency support | Common Fund (or per grant terms) |
| Buy-in capital | New member / late joiner buy-in | Reserve or project fund per its rule |
| Surplus dividends | Never; surplus is distributed or retained by assembly | Per `05-Distribution-and-Refunds/` |

### 2.2 Expense

| Expense | Fund that pays | Rule |
|---------|---------------|------|
| Power (electricity) | Common Fund | Per budget line |
| Preventive maintenance | Repair Fund | `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/01-Preventive-Maintenance/` |
| Breakdown repair | Repair Fund | `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/02-Breakdown-Repair/03-Repair-Approval-Matrix.md` |
| Stipends and compensation | Common Fund | `03-ROTATION-DUTY/03-Duty-Compensation/01-Time-Credits.md` |
| Projects and new boring | Reserve Fund / project fund | `09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/01-New-Boring-Installation/02-Funding-and-Shares.md` |
| Administrative expenses | Common Fund | Per budget line |

## 3. THE THREE FUNDS

| Fund | Purpose | Built by | Decides spending |
|------|---------|----------|------------------|
| **Common Fund** | Day-to-day operation: power, stipends, admin | Monthly contributions, fees, fines | Finance Committee within budget |
| **Reserve Fund** | Motor replacement, new boring, major crisis | Fixed monthly top-up, buy-in capital | Assembly (3/4 vote), except genuine emergency |
| **Repair Fund** | Preventive maintenance and breakdown repairs | Budgeted allocation from Common Fund | Per Repair Approval Matrix |

Each fund is a **sub-account of the single joint bank account** (`02-Fund-Management/04-Bank-Account-and-Signatories.md`).
Money never moves between funds by word of mouth; every movement is a recorded
transfer with a voucher.

## 4. THE GOLDEN RULE, UNPACKED

### 4.1 NO cash transactions

1. All contributions are paid by bank transfer, UPI, cheque, or deposit, and
   every one is tied to a numbered receipt or bank record.
2. All payments are made by cheque, bank transfer, or UPI from the joint
   account, only with two valid signatures and a voucher.
3. No member, including the Finance Committee, ever holds system money in
   their own hand or pocket. Cash that is received must be banked the same
   working day and recorded immediately.

### 4.2 Every rupee recorded

- Every income and every expense appears in the ledger
  (`03-Bookkeeping/01-Ledger-Format.md`).
- Every income has a numbered receipt or bank record
  (`03-Bookkeeping/03-Receipt-Requirement.md`).
- Every expense has an approved voucher (`03-Bookkeeping/02-Expense-Voucher-Rule.md`).

### 4.3 Every record public

- Any member may inspect, copy, and photograph every financial record at any
  time, without permission (`04-Audit/04-Right-of-Any-Member-to-Audit.md`).
- A statement of accounts is published every two months
  (`03-Bookkeeping/04-Bimonthly-Statement.md`).
- Regular, surprise, and member audits keep the books honest
  (`04-Audit/`).

## 5. PRINCIPLES SERVED

| Principle | How finance serves it |
|-----------|------------------------|
| P4 Contribution fair; work compensated | Formula-based contributions, published stipend rates |
| P6 Radical transparency | Every rupee open to every member |
| P7 Protection of the weak | Hardship tiers, installments, no distress collections |
| P8 Work rewarded | Time-credits and stipends paid and settled |
| P2 Equal power | No person or family can control the account or the books |
| P3 No single point of failure | Three signatories, rotating auditors, dual entry |
| P1 Water is common property | No fund can be tapped for private gain |
