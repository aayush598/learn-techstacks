# GLOSSARY OF TERMS

**Folder:** `14-GLOSSARY/`

---

## 1. HOW TO USE THIS GLOSSARY

This glossary collects the key terms used across the constitution and its
folders, with a one-line meaning and the authoritative definition location.
Where a term has a formal definition, that definition wins
(`00-CONSTITUTION/01-Preamble/03-Definitions.md`).

## 2. PEOPLE AND ROLES

| Term | Meaning | Authority |
|------|---------|-----------|
| **Member** | The registered connection holder of a household current on all obligations | `01-MEMBERSHIP/00-Membership-Overview.md` |
| **Household** | A dwelling unit receiving water through one connection | `00-CONSTITUTION/01-Preamble/03-Definitions.md` |
| **Connection** | One registered tap/delivery point with a unique connection ID | same |
| **Founding member** | A member whose contribution was part of the original installation | `01-MEMBERSHIP/02-Types-of-Members/01-Founding-Members.md` |
| **Regular member** | A member whose buy-in is fully paid and obligations are current | `01-MEMBERSHIP/02-Types-of-Members/02-Regular-Members.md` |
| **Late joiner** | A member admitted after installation whose buy-in is not fully paid | `01-MEMBERSHIP/02-Types-of-Members/03-Late-Joiners.md` |
| **Tenant member** | A renter with usage rights and duties; vote only by written owner delegation | `01-MEMBERSHIP/02-Types-of-Members/04-Tenant-Members.md` |
| **Member on leave** | A member temporarily absent under the published leave rules | `01-MEMBERSHIP/02-Types-of-Members/05-Members-on-Leave.md` |
| **Skilled member** | A member with a recorded skill (electrical, plumbing, welding, accounting, legal) | Skill Register, `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/03-Skilled-Member-Program/` |
| **Duty operator** | The member assigned to switch on/off and log the motor for a duty period | `03-ROTATION-DUTY/01-Duty-Roster/` |
| **Registry Keeper** | The rotating custodian of the Member Registry | `01-MEMBERSHIP/04-Member-Registry/02-Registry-Keeper-Rotation.md` |
| **Registry (Member Registry)** | The authoritative list of members, dues, and buy-in status | `01-MEMBERSHIP/04-Member-Registry/01-Registry-Format.md` |

## 3. MONEY AND CONTRIBUTIONS

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Monthly contribution** | Fixed monthly charge per connection, set by formula |
| **Buy-in** | One-time entry payment of a late joiner = fair backdated share of assets |
| **Backdated contribution** | The method computing the buy-in as if the joiner had paid from the start |
| **Common Fund** | Main operational fund (monthly contributions) |
| **Reserve Fund** | Emergency / asset-replacement fund |
| **Repair Fund** | Sub-fund for maintenance and repairs |
| **Time-credit** | Unit of compensated time for duty (1 hour = 1 credit) |
| **Credit rate** | Rupee value of one time-credit, published quarterly |
| **Stipend** | Cash paid from the common fund for paid roles |
| **Consumed share** | Depreciation of assets attributable to a member's period of use |
| **Refund** | Paid capital minus consumed share minus dues minus damages |
| **Dues** | Unpaid contributions, installments, or charges on a connection |
| **Event water** | Extra water for a function, booked and paid at the published tariff |

## 4. GOVERNANCE AND PROCESS

| Term | Meaning | Authority |
|------|---------|-----------|
| **General Assembly** | Meeting of all members; the supreme decision body | `02-GOVERNANCE/01-General-Assembly/` |
| **Quorum** | Minimum attendance for a valid assembly | `02-GOVERNANCE/01-General-Assembly/02-Quorum-Rules.md` |
| **Resolution** | A formal decision passed by the assembly | `02-GOVERNANCE/03-Decision-Making/` |
| **Vote** | One per registered connection, equal for all | `01-MEMBERSHIP/05-Equal-Standing/01-One-Member-One-Vote.md` |
| **Truth Veto** | A member's formal objection freezing a decision pending evidence review | `00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/02-Single-Member-Truth-Veto.md` |
| **CRB (Conflict Review Board)** | Temporary body that adjudicates grievances on evidence | `02-GOVERNANCE/02-Committees/05-Conflict-Review-Board.md` |
| **Grievance** | A written complaint by a member under the resolution process | `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/02-Resolution-Process/02-Step-2-Written-Grievance.md` |
| **Enforcement Ladder** | The graduated set of consequences for violations | `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md` |

| **Amendment** | A change to the constitution through Part V process | `00-CONSTITUTION/05-Amendment-Process/` |

## 5. WATER AND ASSETS

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Boring / Borewell** | The drilled water source |
| **Motor (pump)** | The pump lifting water from the boring |
| **Control panel** | Electrical box with switch, starter, and safety devices |
| **Overhead tank (OHT)** | Storage tank, if installed |
| **The Keys** | Physical access keys to the motor room under the rotation rule |
| **Slot** | One fixed time window in the schedule for a defined group/area |
| **Group** | A subset of members sharing a slot (used in rotation/summer grouping) |
| **Rotation order** | The sequence in which groups receive slots, rotated so no group keeps the best slot |
| **Tap level** | A household's tap elevation affecting pressure and volume |
| **Flow rate** | Volume of water per minute at the tap |
| **Discharge yield** | Total water the boring can supply in a run |
| **Rationing** | Published, equal reduction of slots during declared scarcity | `10-RISK-AND-EMERGENCY/01-Drought-and-Scarcity/` |
| **Hardship protocol** | Public, time-bound exception for genuine medical need |
