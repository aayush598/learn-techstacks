# FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Folder:** `14-GLOSSARY/`

Answers are drawn from the constitution and its folders. Where a short answer
points to a rule, that rule is the authority.

---

## 1. JOINING AND PAYMENTS

**Q1. I bought a house here. What do I pay to get water?**

A. You pay three things: (1) any arrears/outstanding buy-in on the dwelling
cleared by the seller before transfer, (2) the dwelling's buy-in (your fair
backdated share, payable in installments up to 12 months), and (3) the monthly
contribution from the day you register. You must be admitted by the assembly
and sign the entry agreement first. See `01-MEMBERSHIP/03-Exit-and-Transfer/04-Selling-House-to-New-Owner.md`.

**Q2. Why should a late joiner pay anything? The boring is already there.**

A. The boring, motor, and pipes were funded by members who paid before you.
Without a buy-in you would use assets you never helped buy — a free ride,
which the constitution forbids (P4). The buy-in makes you an equal co-owner.
See `01-MEMBERSHIP/01-Enrollment/02-Buy-in-Contribution.md`.

**Q3. I cannot pay the whole buy-in at once. Can I join?**

A. Yes. You sign a written installment schedule (maximum 12 months) and your
connection is opened immediately. Your water and your vote are never reduced
for paying in installments. See `01-MEMBERSHIP/02-Types-of-Members/03-Late-Joiners.md`.

**Q4. I rent a house. Am I a member? Can I vote?**

A. You are a tenant member: full water rights and duty obligations, but no
vote of your own. You may vote only if the owner delegates the dwelling's vote
to you in writing. The owner stays liable for dues. See `01-MEMBERSHIP/02-Types-of-Members/04-Tenant-Members.md`.

**Q5. Can the assembly raise my monthly contribution just because they want to?**

A. Only by changing the published formula through a proper resolution with a
published reason — and the change applies to every connection equally. An
individual surcharge aimed at you is void and is retaliation if it follows a
dispute. See `01-MEMBERSHIP/05-Equal-Standing/02-Equal-Burden-Equal-Benefit.md`.

**Q6. A group wants to raise MY charges while keeping theirs unchanged. What do I do?**

A. Raise a formal objection. Factual and rule questions are not decided by
votes (P5). You can file a grievance, and if a resolution still tries to pass,
file a Truth Veto on the ground that it violates the equal-burden rule. See
`00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/02-Single-Member-Truth-Veto.md`.

## 2. POWER, KEYS AND CONTROL

**Q7. The old member who ran the motor won't hand over the keys. What happens?**

A. The keys belong to the rotating system, not to him. His refusal is a key
violation (Level 3), handled by the enforcement procedure, including a
lock-change and, if needed, lawful authority. His old contribution gives him
honour and a repayment — not the keys. See `01-MEMBERSHIP/02-Types-of-Members/01-Founding-Members.md` and `03-ROTATION-DUTY/05-Key-and-Control/`.

**Q8. One family holds keys, money, and the register. Is that allowed?**

A. No. The three separations forbid it: keys, cash, and records must be in
independent rotating hands. This is the anti-domination guardrail. See
`01-MEMBERSHIP/05-Equal-Standing/03-Anti-Domination-Guardrails.md`.

**Q9. Can I refuse duty because I am busy?**

A. Refusing your turn is a duty failure with published consequences (cover
charge, then ladder levels). But if you genuinely cannot serve, apply for
leave or a swap with another member — swap arrangements are the normal,
honest way. Leave is granted by rule, not by favour. See
`01-MEMBERSHIP/02-Types-of-Members/05-Members-on-Leave.md`.

**Q10. A skilled member is "asked" to repair the motor free. Is that fine?**

A. No. All work is compensated (P8). A skilled member earns time-credits or a
stipend at the published rate, whether it is a 20-minute wiring job or a
two-day repair. Forced free labour is exactly what this constitution ends.
See `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/03-Skilled-Member-Program/`.

## 3. WATER, SCHEDULES AND SUMMER

**Q11. My neighbour takes more water and nobody stops him. What can I do?**

A. Water is common property (P1). Hoarding or unauthorized drawing is an
offence. Report the fact with evidence (photo, time, meter/log) — the duty
log and the Enforcement Ladder handle it. Facts are never voted away. See
`04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/05-Irregularities/`.

**Q12. In summer there is less water. Who decides who gets cut?**

A. The published drought/scarcity rationing table decides — equal, transparent
minutes, applied to all, with only documented hardship exceptions. Nobody
decides individually, and nobody's "friendship" protects them. See
`10-RISK-AND-EMERGENCY/01-Drought-and-Scarcity/`.

**Q13. My street has low pressure. Can I have an extra slot?**

A. Physical factors are handled by the published compensation rules (tap
height, distance), not by asking a committee for favours. Every member with
the same physical situation gets the same treatment. See
`09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/04-Network-Improvements/01-Tap-Height-Compensation.md`.

**Q14. I need a function's extra water (wedding). How does that work?**

A. Book event water under the published tariff, pay the published rate, and
the event is logged. Event water never takes the slot of another member. See
`04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/03-Event-Water/`.

## 4. MONEY, BOOKS AND AUDIT

**Q15. Who can see the accounts?**

A. Every member, at any announced time. Books, receipts, logs, minutes, and
the registry are open (P6). No secret books exist. See
`06-FINANCE/03-Bookkeeping/` and `06-FINANCE/04-Audit/`.

**Q16. Who can change my buy-in amount after I signed?**

A. No one. Once your installment schedule is signed, the amount is fixed.
Changing it would breach your entry agreement. See `01-MEMBERSHIP/01-Enrollment/04-Entry-Agreement.md`.

**Q17. I am leaving the colony. How much do I get back?**

A. Refund = paid capital − consumed share − dues − damages, computed by the
published formula and paid within 30 days of the final calculation. See
`01-MEMBERSHIP/03-Exit-and-Transfer/03-Refund-Procedure.md`.

**Q18. Who keeps the money? What if the treasurer runs away?**

A. Money is with rotating finance handlers, accounts are reconciled monthly,
the registry records every rupee, and an independent audit happens each cycle.
Theft is a Level 5 offence with lawful recovery. The system is designed so no
one can run away with much and every member can check the books tomorrow. See
`06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/` and `06-FINANCE/04-Audit/`.

## 5. DISPUTES, ENFORCEMENT AND PROTECTION

**Q19. A majority passed a rule that hurts me. Do I have to obey?**

A. Not if the rule violates a right, a fact, or the constitution. A repressive
or rights-violating resolution is void regardless of votes (P5). File a Truth
Veto or grievance. See `00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/` and
`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/04-Right-vs-Majority-Procedures/`.

**Q20. What is a Truth Veto and when can I use it?**

A. It is a formal objection that a decision is factually false, rule-violating,
or rights-violating. It freezes the decision until evidence review. You cannot
use it merely because you dislike an option. See
`00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/02-Single-Member-Truth-Veto.md`.

**Q21. The committee is ignoring my complaint. What next?**

A. Take the steps in order: informal talk, written grievance, CRB review, then
Enforcement Ladder, then arbitration. Delays are themselves grievable. The
CRB must answer on evidence and in writing. See
`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/02-Resolution-Process/`.

**Q22. What happens if I am caught tampering with the motor or tapping a line?**

A. It is an offence on the Enforcement Ladder, with due process, evidence, and
graded consequences from warning to serious penalties. Water exclusion follows
only the full ladder — never an angry majority's vote. See
`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`.

**Q23. A group is threatening to boycott my family if I keep complaining. Can they?**

A. No. Social boycott, humiliation, and targeting are explicitly forbidden
(P7, P10). This is a serious offence and the protection mechanisms apply,
including the individual economic protection procedure. See
`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/03-Protection-Mechanisms/`.

**Q24. The motor broke and "repair will happen next week." Can I do anything?**

A. Yes. Breakdown repair has a published timeline; crossing it triggers
penalties and a mandatory alternative arrangement (water vendor/rationed
supply). A repair cannot be delayed to punish anyone. See
`05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/02-Breakdown-Repair/04-Repair-Timeline-and-Penalties.md`.

## 6. MEMBERSHIP AND EXIT

**Q25. Can my family be refused membership because we are new / from another state / renters?**

A. No. Admission grounds are fixed and non-discriminatory. Any refusal based
on caste, religion, region, gender, occupation, or renter status is void. See
`01-MEMBERSHIP/01-Enrollment/01-New-Member-Admission.md`.

**Q26. Do I lose anything for being a founding member if I never paid buy-in?**

A. Founding members' original capital is their buy-in. They keep one vote like
everyone, get honour, and may receive capital repayment on the schedule. They
gain no extra control. See `01-MEMBERSHIP/02-Types-of-Members/01-Founding-Members.md`.

**Q27. I am going away for a year for work. Do I keep membership?**

A. Yes — apply for leave (up to 12 months, renewable). Your contribution
continues, your duty turns are covered by the reserve, and your vote travels
by postal ballot. See `01-MEMBERSHIP/02-Types-of-Members/05-Members-on-Leave.md`.

**Q28. If I sell my house, can I hand over "my water share" to the buyer?**

A. No. The share transfers through the system, only after the buyer is
admitted, clears the dwelling's buy-in, and signs the entry agreement. A
private handover of keys or rights is not recognized. See
`01-MEMBERSHIP/03-Exit-and-Transfer/04-Selling-House-to-New-Owner.md`.

**Q29. My data is in the registry. Can anyone see my phone number?**

A. The registry is open for inspection of membership, buy-in, and dues. Your
contact number and health/hardship flags are protected and are not published
or shared. See `01-MEMBERSHIP/04-Member-Registry/03-Data-Privacy-Rules.md`.

**Q30. The system feels stuck in a conflict between rules. Which rule wins?**

A. The Norm Hierarchy decides: Basic Structure > Fundamental Rights >
Constitution > resolutions > committee decisions > interim rules > custom. If
still ambiguous, the interpretation most favourable to the affected member
wins. See `00-CONSTITUTION/06-Supremacy-and-Conflict/01-Norm-Hierarchy.md`.
