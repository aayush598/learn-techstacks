# DECISION-MAKING 2 — ORDINARY VOTING

**Folder:** `02-GOVERNANCE/03-Decision-Making/`

---

## 1. ONE MEMBER, ONE VOTE

- Every registered member has **exactly one vote** per connection
  (`01-MEMBERSHIP/05-Equal-Standing/01-One-Member-One-Vote.md`).
- No role, wealth, age, seniority, or skill grants extra votes (P2).
- No proxy voting by non-members; a member's vote is their own.

## 2. WHO MAY VOTE

| Person | May vote? |
|--------|-----------|
| Registered member (current on obligations) | Yes |
| Registered member in arrears | Yes — dues are handled by the contribution rules, never by silent loss of vote |
| Member on leave | Yes, by written ballot if absent (`02-Quorum-Rules.md`) |
| Non-member resident | No |
| Second adult of the same household | No — one vote per connection |

A vote can never be confiscated as punishment; that would violate P2 and the
basic structure.

## 3. VOTING METHODS

| Method | When used |
|--------|-----------|
| **Show of hands** | Default for ordinary Class A items |
| **Secret ballot** | Automatically when ANY member requests it; mandatory for decisions about a specific person's conduct, dues, or entitlements |
| **Written/absentee ballot** | For members who cannot attend, provided the ballot is signed by the actual member, witnessed, and submitted before the count |

- A request for a secret ballot cannot be voted down. One member's request
  suffices — the purpose is to protect that member from coercion (P7, P10).
- Ballot papers are sealed at the end and kept with the minutes for 3 years.

## 4. COUNTING RULES

1. Two counters, from **different households**, are drawn by lot at the
   meeting to count.
2. The counters count for/against/absent and record the numbers openly.
3. A member who votes "abstain" is recorded as abstaining; abstentions are
   reported but do not count for or against a Class A motion.
4. For **Class B and C**, silence and absence count as **against**
   (`03-Supermajority-Rules.md`).
5. A tie on a Class A motion fails (no change). The status quo wins in doubt.

## 5. RESULT PUBLICATION

- The facilitator declares the result **exactly as counted**.
- The result is recorded in the minutes with the vote split
  (`01-General-Assembly/05-Meeting-Records.md`).
- Results of all votes are published within **48 hours** with the counts —
  part of P6. For sensitive secret ballots, counts are published without
  individual names, but the numbers and method are shown.

## 6. RECOUNT AND CHALLENGE

- Any member may ask for an immediate open recount.
- A member may challenge a vote only on grounds of: wrong class, missing
  quorum, coercion, or miscount — **not** because they dislike the outcome.
- A challenge goes first to the facilitator for a recount, then to the CRB if
  it alleges a procedural or coercion defect.

## 7. ABSENTEE VOTING PRACTICE

- Ballot forms are made available on the notice board and members' group
  before the meeting.
- A filled ballot is submitted to the notice box with a witness signature, or
  handed to the lot-drawn minute-taker before the count.
- The system deliberately makes absence harmless: a member who cannot come is
  never "out-voted by default" on a Class A item, and on Class B/C items their
  absence counts as a visible "against", not as silence.

## 8. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

A proposal to raise the monthly contribution by ₹50 was put to a show of
hands. A widow who feared the dominant families watching her hand was asked by
the facilitator if she wanted a secret ballot; she said yes. The ballot was
counted by two lot-drawn counters. The result — 9 for, 7 against — was
published the same evening. No one ever knew how she voted, and no one could
punish her for it.
