# DECISION-MAKING 4 — TRUTH REVIEW OVERRIDE

**Folder:** `02-GOVERNANCE/03-Decision-Making/`

---

## 1. THE TRUTH GATE

Every decision of the assembly must survive the **Truth Gate** before it can
take effect. The gate checks three things:

| Check | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Factually true | Not based on false information |
| Rule-consistent | Does not contradict an existing unamended rule |
| Rights-respecting | Does not weaken a Fundamental Right or protection |

A decision that fails the gate is **void**, no matter how many votes it
received. This is the governance-side application of
`00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/01-Principle-of-Truth-over-Majority.md`.

## 2. FACTS ARE NOT VOTABLE

- "Was the pump on at 5:10?" — evidence decides (logs, photos, witnesses).
- "How much did the repair cost?" — receipts decide.
- A vote on a question of fact is stopped by the facilitator, or by any
  member filing a Truth Veto.
- See `00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/04-No-Voting-on-Proven-Facts.md`.

## 3. THE SINGLE-MEMBER TRUTH VETO

- **Any single member** may file a Truth Veto against a proposed decision on
  the grounds that it is factually false, rule-violating, or right-violating.
- **Effect: the decision is frozen immediately** — no implementation, no
  partial implementation, no "just for now".
- The full procedure is in
  `00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/02-Single-Member-Truth-Veto.md`; the CRB
  runs the review (`02-Committees/05-Conflict-Review-Board.md`).
- A veto is **not a preference objection**: "I prefer another option" is not a
  veto; "this decision violates the buy-in rule" is.

## 4. THE REVIEW — EVIDENCE, NOT VOTES

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| Reviewer | CRB (or a 3-person lot-drawn panel if the CRB is conflicted) |
| Method | Evidence and cited rules — a popularity poll is never a review |
| Timeline | Max 7 days for facts; 14 if external documents are needed |
| Outcome | Written, reasoned, signed |

## 5. OUTCOMES OF THE REVIEW

| Outcome | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Upheld** | The decision is void. If partially implemented, restoration/rebalance per `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/04-Right-vs-Majority-Procedures/05-Restoration-and-Rebalance.md` |
| **Rejected** | The decision may proceed, but the vetoing member's dissent stays recorded with no penalty |
| **Referred back** | The defect is fixed (e.g., the rule is correctly applied) and the corrected decision proceeds without a new vote if purely drafting |

## 6. TRUTH OVER THE ASSEMBLY'S OWN MAJORITY

- The Truth Gate applies to **every class** — even a Class C supermajority
  cannot pass a decision the gate rejects.
- A decision that violates the basic structure (Class D) is void even if
  unanimous and even if nobody vetoes it.
- After adoption, a wrong decision can still be challenged through
  `00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/03-Objection-to-Wrong-Resolution.md`.

## 7. THE DUTY OF THE FACILITATOR

- Before every vote, the facilitator states the gate for the item: "This is a
  matter of choice between options X and Y. Facts and rights are not part of
  this vote."
- If a member files a veto, the facilitator **stops the vote and freezes the
  item**, records the veto in the minutes, and refers it to the CRB with the
  CRB reference noted.
- A facilitator who proceeds with a vote despite a valid veto has committed a
  procedural violation, and the vote is void.

## 8. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

The assembly voted 14–2 to cut water to a household for "wasting water last
month". A single member filed a Truth Veto: the rulebook permits water cuts
only through the Enforcement Ladder with due process. The decision froze. The
CRB reviewed, found the house had not been through the ladder, and the
"decision" was declared void. Fourteen votes could not do what the rules
forbade — the gate held, and a lone member with a cited rule had stopped the
crowd.

hundred hands raised for an injustice cannot bring it down.*
