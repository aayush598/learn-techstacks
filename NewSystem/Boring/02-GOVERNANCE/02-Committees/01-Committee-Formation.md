# COMMITTEES 1 — COMMITTEE FORMATION

**Folder:** `02-GOVERNANCE/02-Committees/`

---

## 1. WHY COMMITTEES EXIST

Committees exist to **execute** the assembly's decisions in between meetings.
They exist to make the system work daily — logs, books, repairs — without
requiring the whole assembly for every small act. They are **never** bodies of
power; they are bodies of work.

## 2. THE GOLDEN RULE: EXECUTION-ONLY

| Power | Committee has it? |
|-------|-------------------|
| Execute assembly decisions | Yes |
| Make rules or amend rules | No |
| Change the schedule or contributions | No |
| Spend beyond an approved budget line | No |
| Decide penalties or rights of members | No |
| Speak on behalf of the colony to outsiders | Only as instructed |
| Keep its acts secret | No — all acts recorded and open (P6) |

A committee act that exceeds these limits is **void** and the committee must
refer the matter to the assembly.

## 3. FORMATION BY LOT AND ROTATION

- Committees are formed by **lot** at the Annual General Meeting, not by
  election of "popular" people (`05-Rotation-and-Fairness/02-Lot-Draw-System.md`).
- Membership of every committee is **voluntary but rotateable**: the lot picks
  the order of call; any member may serve, decline (the next on the list
  serves), or request a swap with another member.
- No member is compelled to serve, but the duty to serve once in a while is
  part of the duty of participation
  (`00-CONSTITUTION/03-Fundamental-Duties/02-Duty-to-Learn-and-Participate.md`),
  and service earns time-credits (P8).
- Committee seats are drawn fresh at the AGM and at staggered mid-year
  intervals, so no committee is ever self-perpetuating.

## 4. SIZE

- Each standing committee has **3 to 5 members**.
- Size is set by the assembly when it creates the committee; an odd number is
  preferred so internal votes (if needed) are clean.
- Two members of the same household may **not** serve on the same committee at
  the same time (`06-Term-Limits-and-Rotation.md`).

## 5. CREATING AND DISSOLVING COMMITTEES

| Action | Who decides | Class |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| Create a standing committee | General Assembly | Class A |
| Change its size or mandate | General Assembly | Class A |
| Dissolve a committee | General Assembly | Class A |
| Act outside mandate | Never valid; refer to assembly | — |

- The assembly may create **ad-hoc committees** for one task (e.g., "Festival
  Water Sub-committee"). An ad-hoc committee dissolves automatically when its
  task is completed and reported.

## 6. ACCOUNTABILITY AND REMOVAL

- A committee reports to the **monthly assembly** on every act and every rupee
  spent.
- A committee member may be removed only by the assembly, for: failure to
  perform, breach of transparency, or misconduct as found by the CRB
  (evidence-based, not by popularity).
- A committee member may resign at any time; the next member on the lot list
  fills the seat for the remainder of the term.

## 7. ALL COMMITTEE ACTS RECORDED

- Every committee keeps a **Committee Log** with entries: date, act,
  decision, member who acted, and any money involved.
- The log is part of the Open Records (`04-Transparency/01-Open-Records.md`).
- A committee that meets in private without a written record has **not met**
  for decision purposes; only its recorded acts count.

## 8. MEETING PRACTICE

- Committees may meet as often as the work needs, at a public time and place
  announced on the notice board.
- Committee meetings are **open**; any member may observe.
- A committee decides by majority of its own members where the assembly has
  given it discretion — but for any matter affecting money, two independent
  members must concur (dual custody, `06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/`).

## 9. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

The assembly decided at its AGM that vendor quotes above ₹2,000 must be
compared three-way. The Repair Committee did not "decide" this — it executes
it. When a member asked why a particular vendor was chosen, the committee
pointed to its log: three quotes, two signed comparisons, date and names. The
answer was in the record, not in anyone's head.

---

*Committees are hands, not heads. The head of this system is the assembly, and
the assembly alone.*
