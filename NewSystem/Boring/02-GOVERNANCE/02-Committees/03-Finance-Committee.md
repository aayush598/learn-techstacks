# COMMITTEES 3 — FINANCE COMMITTEE

**Folder:** `02-GOVERNANCE/02-Committees/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

The Finance Committee manages the colony's money **under the assembly's
authority**. Its job is bookkeeping, banking, and budget preparation. It is
deliberately structured so that **no single person controls the money**
(P3, P6).

## 2. COMPOSITION AND SEPARATION OF CONTROL

- 3–5 members drawn by lot (`01-Committee-Formation.md`).
- Term: 12 months (`06-Term-Limits-and-Rotation.md`).
- The committee's roles are **split across different members**:

| Role | Held by | Never combined with |
|------|---------|---------------------|
| Signatory A (bank) | Member A | Signatory B or cash custody |
| Signatory B (bank) | Member B | Signatory A or cash custody |
| Cash box custodian | Member C | Either signatory role |
| Ledger keeper | Member D | Cash custody |
| Reconciliation pair | Two members | The same member who received the money |

- **Dual signatories:** any bank withdrawal or payment above a nominal sum
  (e.g., ₹500) requires **two signatures**, and both signatories must belong
  to different households.
- **No cash handling by a single person:** cash collections are counted by two
  members together and deposited within 48 hours.

## 3. DUTIES

| Duty | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Bookkeeping | Maintain the ledger and the cash book in the standard formats (`06-FINANCE/03-Bookkeeping/`) |
| Bank liaison | Manage the colony bank account under dual signature rules |
| Contributions | Record monthly contributions, issue receipts, track arrears per `06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/` |
| Budget preparation | Draft the Annual Budget for the assembly to adopt (`06-FINANCE/03-Bookkeeping/05-Annual-Budget.md`) |
| Payment execution | Pay approved bills, always with two signatories and matching the approved budget line |
| Monthly report | Present income, expenses, and balances at every monthly assembly |

## 4. WHAT THE FINANCE COMMITTEE CANNOT DO

| Forbidden act | Why |
|---------------|-----|
| Set or change contribution rates | Only the assembly (Class B) |
| Authorise a new expense | Only the assembly within budget, or a line already approved |
| Keep cash at a member's home beyond 48 hours | Cash concentration is a single point of failure and risk |
| Hold the account alone (single signatory) | Dual signature is mandatory (P3) |
| Hide a transaction "until the audit" | Radical transparency — every entry is open daily (P6) |
| Lend the colony's money to anyone | Not in the mandate; assembly decision required |

## 5. CASH COLLECTION AND SAFEKEEPING

- Contributions are collected at a fixed public time and place, by two members
  (one counts, one records), with a receipt given immediately.
- The counted cash is deposited in the bank within **48 hours**, or locked in
  the double-locked cash box (two different key holders) if the bank is
  closed.
- No member's home is the "treasury". The money is either in the bank, in the
  double-locked box, or in transit with two members.
- This is detailed in `06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/`.

## 6. REPORTING AND TRANSPARENCY

- A one-page **monthly finance statement** is published: opening balance,
  income, expense by line, closing balance, arrears list (names + amounts),
  and bank passbook photo.
- Every member may verify any entry against the ledger and receipts at any
  time (`04-Transparency/02-Right-to-Inspect.md`).

## 7. HANDOVER

- At term end, the committee hands over with a **closing statement signed by
  all members** and a physical count of cash, matched to the ledger.
- The outgoing ledger is kept as a permanent record; the incoming committee
  starts a fresh page, never a fresh truth.
- A mid-term departure triggers the same handover within 7 days.

## 8. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

In the old system, one man collected ₹200 per household, noted payments in his
own book, and "forgot" who had paid. Under this rule, two members collect
together, each household gets a numbered receipt, the pair deposits the cash,
and the monthly statement lists every household's status. When a member
questioned an entry, the committee produced the receipt book and the bank
slip within an hour — not a word, but a document.

---

*Money is the most common tool of domination. It is therefore the most
carefully fragmented control point in this system.*
