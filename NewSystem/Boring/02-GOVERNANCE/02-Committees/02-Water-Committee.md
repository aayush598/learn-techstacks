# COMMITTEES 2 — WATER COMMITTEE

**Folder:** `02-GOVERNANCE/02-Committees/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

The Water Committee executes the assembly's water decisions. It is the
operational arm for the daily water routine. It has **no power to change
water policy** — schedule, timing, and distribution rules are decided only by
the assembly (`03-Decision-Making/`).

## 2. COMPOSITION

- 3–5 members drawn by lot at the AGM
  (`01-Committee-Formation.md`, `05-Rotation-and-Fairness/02-Lot-Draw-System.md`).
- Term: 12 months (subject to `06-Term-Limits-and-Rotation.md`).
- No two members from the same household.

## 3. WHAT THE WATER COMMITTEE DOES

| Duty | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Publish the schedule | Print and post the assembly-approved schedule on the notice board and members' group; update for seasonal changes only as the assembly decides |
| Monitor logs | Check that the motor run log and gauge readings are filled correctly by duty operators (`04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/04-Monitoring-and-Logs/`) |
| Coordinate the duty roster | Maintain and circulate the roster drawn by lot (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/01-Duty-Roster/02-Roster-Generation-by-Lot.md`); arrange swaps when a duty member is genuinely unavailable |
| Handle reported irregularities | Record complaints about missed slots, leaks, or overdrawing, and pass them to the CRB if they involve conduct |
| Report to the assembly | Present a water report at every monthly assembly |

## 4. WHAT THE WATER COMMITTEE CANNOT DO

| Forbidden act | Why |
|---------------|-----|
| Change any slot timing on its own | Only the assembly can change the schedule (Class B) |
| Grant extra water to a member | Water shares are common property (P1); only the assembly may allocate |
| Cut anyone's water | Water denial is only ever through the Enforcement Ladder with due process |
| Operate the motor itself permanently | Duty rotates among all members (P3) |
| Keep the motor key | Keys are held under the key rotation (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/05-Key-and-Control/`) |

## 5. RELATIONSHIP WITH THE DUTY ROSTER

- The Water Committee **coordinates** the roster but does not choose who
  serves. Names come from the lot draw.
- If a duty member misses a slot, the committee follows the Duty Failure
  procedure (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/04-Duty-Failure/`), records it, and arranges
  cover from the backup list.
- The committee may not quietly let one willing person cover every missed
  slot — that recreates a single point of failure (P3).

## 6. WATER EVENTS AND SPECIAL REQUESTS

- Event water (weddings, functions) is decided by the assembly's event rules
  (`04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/03-Event-Water/`).
- The committee executes the event booking: collect the published fee (via the
  Finance Committee, never in cash alone), schedule the extra run, and log it.

## 7. RECORDS

- The committee keeps a Water Log: schedule versions, roster copies, swap
  notes, irregularity reports, and event bookings.
- All entries are open to members (`04-Transparency/01-Open-Records.md`).

## 8. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

A member asked the Water Committee to move the morning slot from 6:30 to 7:00
"just for our family this month". The committee explained it has no such
power; the request must go on the agenda as a proposal. At the next assembly,
the change was proposed and failed for want of a two-thirds majority. The
schedule stayed as the whole membership had set it — exactly as intended.

---

*The Water Committee is the servant of the published schedule, never its
master.*
