# COMMITTEES 5 — CONFLICT REVIEW BOARD (CRB)

**Folder:** `02-GOVERNANCE/02-Committees/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

The Conflict Review Board (CRB) is the evidence body of the system. It exists
because a crowd's vote cannot decide the truth. The CRB reviews Truth Vetoes,
grievances, and disputes **by evidence, not by popularity** (P5). It is a
"committee" in the loose sense, but with one decisive difference: the CRB is
**independent of the assembly's discretion**. It decides facts and applies
rights; it never decides policy.

## 2. COMPOSITION

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| Members | **3**, drawn by lot (`05-Rotation-and-Fairness/02-Lot-Draw-System.md`) |
| Term | **3–6 months** (set by lot draw at creation) |
| Families | No member may serve if a party to any pending case is from their household |
| Renomination | Subject to `06-Term-Limits-and-Rotation.md`; no consecutive terms without a gap |

## 3. THE INDEPENDENCE PRINCIPLE

- The CRB is **not removed by the assembly** mid-term except for proven
  misconduct found by a second, ad-hoc lot-drawn panel.
- The assembly cannot instruct the CRB how to decide a case. It can only
  request review, never dictate outcomes.
- This independence is what makes P5 real: the majority cannot influence the
  fact-finder.

## 4. WHAT THE CRB HANDLES

| Matter | Basis of decision |
|--------|-------------------|
| Truth Veto reviews (`03-Decision-Making/04-Truth-Review-Override.md`) | Evidence + cited rule |
| Grievances (non-emergency) | Evidence + written procedure |
| Allegations of targeting, coercion, discrimination (P7) | Evidence |
| Disputes over facts (logs, receipts, timestamps) | Preponderance of credible evidence |
| Review of committee conduct | Records and logs |
| Certification for Class C amendments (`05-Amendment-Process/03-Voting-Rules.md`) | Checklist against rights and basic structure |

## 5. EVIDENCE-BASED, NEVER VOTE-BASED

- The CRB decides by **weighing evidence**: documents, logs, photographs,
  timestamps, receipts, and witnesses — not by counting who is believed.
- Its findings must be **written and reasoned**, with the evidence cited.
- A CRB decision is not a "majority vote of three people for convenience"; it
  is a reasoned determination. All three members sign; a dissent is recorded
  with its reasons.
- The CRB may call for records from any committee or member
  (`04-Transparency/02-Right-to-Inspect.md`).

## 6. RECUSAL AND CONFLICT

- A CRB member **must recuse** when the case involves: their household, a
  blood relation, a landlord/tenant relation, or a pending personal dispute.
- If recusal leaves fewer than 3 members, replacements are drawn by lot from
  the standing pool.
- A party may challenge a CRB member's impartiality in writing; the challenge
  is decided by the other two CRB members, or by a fresh lot-drawn panel if
  both are challenged.

## 7. TIMELINES

| Stage | Deadline |
|-------|----------|
| Accept a grievance/veto | Within 48 hours |
| Notice to all parties | Within 3 days |
| Evidence submission closed | Within 10 days (may extend to 14 for external documents) |
| Written finding published | Within 3 days of closing evidence |
| Total for a facts-only case | Max 7 days (`00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/02-Single-Member-Truth-Veto.md`) |

## 8. POWERS AND LIMITS

| The CRB may | The CRB may not |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Suspend a decision pending review (Truth Veto) | Impose punishments itself (that is the assembly's enforcement role, per the ladder) |
| Declare a decision void for facts/rights violations | Make policy or change the schedule |
| Recommend consequences to the assembly | Order anyone's water cut |
| Certify or refuse Class C amendments at the Truth Gate | Create new rights or rules |

## 9. RECORDS

- Every CRB finding is written, signed, dated, and filed in the Open Records
  (`04-Transparency/01-Open-Records.md`).
- Identities of whistleblowers are protected per
  `04-Transparency/04-Whistleblower-Channel.md`.

## 10. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

A member vetoed the repair committee's choice of vendor, claiming the chosen
vendor is the committee chair's cousin. The CRB examined the comparison sheet,
checked the vendor's registration, and interviewed both parties. It found the
choice had followed the three-quote rule, but noted a genuine appearance of
bias and recommended the assembly re-run the selection with the chair recused.
The finding was published in two days. The matter was decided by documents,
not by whose relatives filled the hall.

---

*The CRB is the quiet guardian of P5: it exists so that a lone member with a
receipt can still beat a room full of people with opinions.*
