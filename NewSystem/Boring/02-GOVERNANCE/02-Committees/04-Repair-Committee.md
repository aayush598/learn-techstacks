# COMMITTEES 4 — REPAIR COMMITTEE

**Folder:** `02-GOVERNANCE/02-Committees/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

The Repair Committee coordinates faults, repairs, and maintenance. Its role is
to make sure a breakdown is **handled fast, transparently, and without one
person owning the repair trade** — the old system's quiet power was the man who
"knew the electrician".

## 2. COMPOSITION

- 3–5 members drawn by lot (`01-Committee-Formation.md`).
- Term: 12 months (`06-Term-Limits-and-Rotation.md`).
- Members with relevant skill may be identified for coordination, but skill is
  a contribution, not a power (`05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/03-Skilled-Member-Program/`).

## 3. DUTIES

| Duty | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Fault intake | Receive and log every reported fault with date, time, and reporter |
| First response | For breakdowns, follow `10-RISK-AND-EMERGENCY/02-Motor-Breakdown.md`; for routine faults, apply the maintenance schedule (`05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/01-Preventive-Maintenance/`) |
| Quotes | Obtain **at least three written quotes** for any repair above the assembly-set threshold; compare on price, warranty, and reference |
| Timeline | Publish the repair plan and expected completion date; report progress at the monthly assembly |
| Skilled-member coordination | Coordinate colony members who volunteer skill; pay them fair stipend/credits per `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/03-Skilled-Member-Program/` |
| Spares and inventory | Maintain the parts register (`05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/05-Parts-and-Inventory/`) |
| Vendor register | Keep the list of tested vendors **on the notice board**, so the "plumber's number" is common property (P1, P6) |

## 4. THRESHOLDS AND AUTHORITY

| Amount/act | Who decides |
|------------|-------------|
| Emergency first-aid (safety, stop loss) | Repair Committee may act immediately, then report within 48 hours |
| Repair within approved budget line | Repair Committee executes per the budget adopted by the assembly |
| Repair beyond budget line | Assembly approval required (Class A), or emergency assembly on 48h notice |
| Choosing a vendor above the threshold | Three written quotes, comparison recorded, lowest/optimal selected by the committee's recorded vote |
| Changing the maintenance schedule | Assembly (Class A or B per `01-Decision-Classes.md`) |

The assembly may delegate a **small emergency band** (e.g., up to ₹3,000) to
the committee for immediate action, with a strict rule: act, then publish the
receipts within 48 hours. The band is set by the assembly and is never
self-increased.

## 5. WHAT THE REPAIR COMMITTEE CANNOT DO

| Forbidden act | Why |
|---------------|-----|
| Authorise a repair beyond its delegated band without the assembly | Spending power belongs to the assembly |
| Pay vendors directly in cash without two signatures | Dual custody (see Finance Committee) |
| Hide a vendor contact "for convenience" | Vendor knowledge must be common property (P3, P6) |
| Decide a repair is "not worth it" and skip it | Repair priorities are set by the assembly and the maintenance plan |
| Favour one vendor repeatedly without cause | Favouritism is a form of capture; repeated choice must be minuted with reasons |

## 6. REPAIR TIMELINES

| Fault type | Target response |
|------------|-----------------|
| Complete breakdown (no water) | First response within 6 hours; emergency assembly on 48h notice if needed |
| Major fault (partial supply) | Assess within 24 hours; plan at next assembly if beyond budget |
| Minor fault (tap/leak/junction) | Within 7 days, per the maintenance calendar |
| Preventive maintenance | Per the annual schedule (`05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/01-Preventive-Maintenance/`) |

Every delay is recorded with a reason. Silence is not an answer.

## 7. RECORDS

- The Repair Log records every fault, quote, decision, payment reference, and
  completion — open to all members.
- Vendor comparisons are kept for 3 years (`11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/03-Records-Retention.md`).

## 8. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

The motor failed. Within hours the Repair Committee logged the fault, called
the emergency assembly per the schedule, and collected three quotes: ₹4,800,
₹5,400, and ₹6,100, with different warranty periods. The comparison sheet was
pinned on the notice board. The assembly chose the ₹5,400 quote (36-month
warranty). The committee did not "decide" — it presented facts; the assembly
decided.

---

*Repairs are a fact problem, not a power problem. The committee's only
authority is the speed and honesty of its coordination.*
