# GENERAL ASSEMBLY 1 — ASSEMBLY SCHEDULE

**Folder:** `02-GOVERNANCE/01-General-Assembly/`

---

## 1. THE REGULAR MONTHLY ASSEMBLY

- A regular General Assembly is held **once every calendar month**, on a fixed,
  published day (for example, the first Sunday of the month at 10:00 a.m.).
- The monthly date and time are set at the Annual General Meeting and may be
  changed only by an assembly decision (Class B).
- A month without an assembly is a **failure of the system**, not a holiday.
  If the scheduled date is a major festival or a declared emergency, the
  assembly is moved by notice to the nearest Sunday and is still held within
  the same calendar month.
- The purpose of the monthly assembly is routine governance: schedule,
  finance, duty, repairs, new business, and grievances.

## 2. THE ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING (AGM)

- The AGM is held **once a year**, within the first 45 days of the financial
  year, at a date announced at least 30 days in advance.
- The AGM must:
  1. Read the **Annual Report** (`04-Transparency/03-Annual-Report.md`).
  2. Adopt the **Annual Budget** for the coming year
     (`06-FINANCE/03-Bookkeeping/05-Annual-Budget.md`).
  3. Confirm the coming year's rotation calendar and lot draws.
  4. Review the enforcement and grievance summary for the past year.
  5. Decide any items that require a full-year context.
- The AGM agenda is published **30 days in advance** so members can propose
  agenda items.

## 3. THE EMERGENCY (SPECIAL) ASSEMBLY

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| Who may call | **Any single member** |
| Notice | At least **48 hours** before the meeting, in writing and on the notice board and members' group |
| Reason | A genuine emergency: motor breakdown, threat to water supply, fraud suspected, safety hazard |
| Quorum | Same quorum as a regular assembly (`02-Quorum-Rules.md`) |
| Decisions | Only the emergency items on the notice; no unrelated surprises |

- Calling an emergency assembly is a right, not a favour. A member does not
  need another member's permission.
- If a member calls repeated emergency assemblies with no genuine emergency,
  the CRB may warn them and, on a second repetition, require the request to be
  co-signed by a second member — but the right itself is never removed
  (P2, P5).

## 4. SPECIAL (THEMED) ASSEMBLIES

- The assembly may, by a Class A decision, call a special assembly for a
  single subject — for example, a town-hall to plan a major repair or a
  dispute hearing.
- A special assembly may take decisions only on the subject stated in its
  notice.

## 5. NOTICE RULES FOR ALL MEETINGS

| Meeting type | Minimum notice | Where published |
|--------------|----------------|-----------------|
| Monthly assembly | 7 days | Notice board + members' group |
| AGM | 30 days | Notice board + members' group + written |
| Emergency assembly | 48 hours | Notice board + members' group + phone/WhatsApp |
| Special assembly | 7 days | Notice board + members' group |

- Every notice must state: date, time, place, and **the agenda**
  (`03-Agenda-Fixing.md`).
- A meeting convened with less notice than required is **void** unless every
  affected decision is re-done at a properly convened meeting.

## 6. VENUE AND FORMAT

- The venue is a public space of the colony (community hall, terrace, or a
  member's courtyard made open to all) so that no member is excluded by
  location.
- If one venue is inconvenient, the assembly may rotate venues monthly by lot.
- Meetings are open to **all members and resident observers**; only members
  vote.

## 7. CALENDAR OF THE YEAR

| Month | Meetings |
|-------|----------|
| First month of financial year | AGM (annual report + budget + rotation calendar) |
| Months 2–12 | Monthly assembly each month |
| Any month | Emergency/special assembly on 48h notice if needed |

## 8. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

The borewell motor burnt out on a Wednesday. The Repair Committee posted the
report. Two members immediately called an emergency assembly for Friday
evening (48 hours' notice was given on Wednesday afternoon). At that meeting
the assembly approved a repair budget line and the mode of procurement. No one
needed to wait for "the leader" to decide; the meeting itself decided.

---

*Regularity is a protection. A system that meets on schedule cannot be captured
by a few people meeting in private.*
