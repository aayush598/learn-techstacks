# GENERAL ASSEMBLY 2 — QUORUM RULES

**Folder:** `02-GOVERNANCE/01-General-Assembly/`

---

## 1. WHAT QUORUM IS

Quorum is the minimum number of members who must be present for the assembly
to take decisions. It exists so that a small group can never meet quietly and
decide things for everyone else.

- **Standard quorum: 60% of all registered members** present (or voting by
  valid written ballot submitted before the count).
- The count of members is taken from the Member Registry
  (`01-MEMBERSHIP/04-Member-Registry/`), not from memory or "who usually comes".

## 2. WHAT COUNTS AS PRESENT

| Status | Counts toward quorum? |
|--------|----------------------|
| Physically present and signing the attendance sheet | Yes |
| Present by written proxy/ballot, witnessed and submitted before the count | Yes, as "voting member", with the ballot on record |
| Absent | No |
| Present but silent | Yes (they are there and can speak or vote if they choose) |

A member counted as present by ballot must have signed the ballot; a ballot
without the member's signature and witness does not count.

## 3. WHAT HAPPENS IF QUORUM IS MISSED

1. The facilitator announces that quorum is missing and **records the count**.
2. The meeting is **adjourned** for **30 minutes** and reconvened once. If
   quorum is still not met, the meeting is adjourned to a **second call**.
3. **Second call rule:** a fresh meeting is called within **7 days**, with
   notice stating "second call — quorum will be 40% of members".
4. At the second call, the quorum is **40% of all members**. This is the
   floor; it may never go lower.
5. If even the second call fails, the matter is not dropped. The remaining
   members record the failed attempts, publish them, and the items are taken
   up at the next scheduled monthly assembly. A hard decision may also be
   referred to written ballot to every member.

## 4. QUORUM THAT CAN NEVER BE WAIVED

The following categories of decisions require the **standard 60% quorum** and
it can **never** be waived, not even at a second call:

1. Any decision affecting a member's **rights** (water share, protection,
   inspection, equal vote).
2. Any **Class B or Class C** decision (`03-Decision-Making/01-Decision-Classes.md`).
3. Any decision involving **expenditure above the ordinary budget line**.
4. Any decision about **admission or exclusion** of a member.
5. Any **amendment** to the constitution or these rules.

A decision taken in one of these categories below quorum is **void**.

## 5. THE ABSENCE RULE (HEARING THE PERSON CONCERNED)

- If a decision concerns a **specific member** — their conduct, their dues,
  their exemption, their grievance, or a penalty — that member must be
  **notified in writing** of the item at least **7 days** in advance and given
  a **full hearing**.
- The absent member may be heard through the minutes read at the meeting, but
  the decision is **not valid** unless either (a) the member attended, or
  (b) the member's written response is on record, or (c) the member was given
  notice and chose not to respond, in which case the decision may proceed but
  the member may appeal it within 15 days.
- No decision about a person's conduct, penalty, or entitlements may be taken
  "in their absence" without proof of notice to them.
- This rule is part of P7 (protection of the weak) and P10 (dignity).

## 6. RECORDING QUORUM

- The attendance sheet and the quorum count are part of the minutes
  (`05-Meeting-Records.md`).
- The minutes record: total members, present, ballots, absent, quorum
  threshold applicable, and whether quorum was met.
- If the meeting is adjourned for want of quorum, the attempted meeting is
  still recorded with the count, so that non-attendance is visible to all
  (P6).

## 7. WHAT ABSENT MEMBERS SHOULD KNOW

- A member who stays away cannot claim a decision is invalid if the decision
  was taken with valid quorum and notice. Participation is a **duty**
  (`00-CONSTITUTION/03-Fundamental-Duties/02-Duty-to-Learn-and-Participate.md`).
- A member who stays away from a **Class B or C** vote is counted as
  **against**, per `03-Decision-Making/03-Supermajority-Rules.md`. Silence
  does not pass high-class decisions.
- A member who cannot attend may vote by written ballot (Section 2) — the
  system is designed so that absence never means loss of voice.

## 8. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

Only 8 of 20 members came to a monthly meeting. The facilitator recorded the
count, waited 30 minutes, and then adjourned. A second call was posted for the
following Sunday with "quorum 40%" in bold. Ten members came; the meeting
decided the routine items. A separate item — a proposal to change the evening
schedule (Class B) — was deferred because it needed the full 60% threshold,
and the minutes said so. Nobody could later claim the schedule was changed
behind their back.

---

*Quorum is not bureaucracy. It is the shield that stops a handful of people
from deciding water, money, and punishment for everyone else.*
