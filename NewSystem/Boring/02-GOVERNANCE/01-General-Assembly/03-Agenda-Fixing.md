# GENERAL ASSEMBLY 3 — AGENDA FIXING

**Folder:** `02-GOVERNANCE/01-General-Assembly/`

---

## 1. WHO MAY ADD AGENDA ITEMS

- **Any single member** may add an item to the agenda of any assembly. No
  seconding, no committee, and no permission is required.
- Items may also come from: committees (as reports or proposals), the CRB
  (as notices of review), and the notice box (grievances and proposals).
- The right of every member to place an item is part of P2 and P5. A member
  cannot be told "your matter is not important enough".

## 2. THE AGENDA REGISTER

- Every item receives a number (AG-2026-001, AG-2026-002, ...) and an entry:
  proposer, subject, one-line summary, date, and status.
- The Agenda Register is public and part of the Open Records
  (`04-Transparency/01-Open-Records.md`).

## 3. PUBLICATION IN ADVANCE

| Meeting | Agenda published at least | Where |
|---------|---------------------------|-------|
| Monthly assembly | 5 days before | Notice board + members' group |
| AGM | 30 days before | Notice board + members' group + written |
| Emergency assembly | 48 hours before (with the notice) | Notice board + members' group |
| Special assembly | 7 days before | Notice board + members' group |

- The published agenda must list every item with: the proposer, the subject,
  and the **class of decision** proposed
  (`03-Decision-Making/01-Decision-Classes.md`) so members know what is at stake.
- An item that was not published cannot be voted on, except as allowed in
  Section 5.

## 4. NO SURPRISE ITEMS AFFECTING INDIVIDUALS

- An item that affects a **specific member** (conduct, dues, exemption,
  penalty, or entitlements) must be published **by name** in the agenda with
  the member's written notice, in line with the Absence Rule
  (`02-Quorum-Rules.md` Section 5).
- It is forbidden to raise a person's conduct under a vague heading like
  "general discussion" and then move a penalty. Any such "decision" is void.
- This protects P7 (no targeting) and P10 (dignity).

## 5. LATE ITEMS — ONLY GENUINE EMERGENCIES

| Condition | Late item allowed? |
|-----------|--------------------|
| A genuine emergency that arose after publication (breakdown, hazard, fraud, danger) | Yes, with two-thirds approval to add it, and it is added as "emergency item" in minutes |
| A matter that merely "came to mind" at the meeting | No |
| A matter affecting an individual that was not published | No (void) |

- A late item must be justified at the meeting before it can be discussed.
- Even an allowed late item is still subject to the Truth Review Override and
  rights protections; an emergency never authorises a rights violation.

## 6. STANDARD AGENDA ORDER

1. Quorum and attendance
2. Reading and correction of previous minutes
3. Water schedule and operations report
4. Finance report
5. Duty roster report
6. Repairs and maintenance report
7. Committee reports
8. Grievances and CRB notices
9. New items (published)
10. Late items (emergency only)
11. Any closed ballot items
12. Announcements and dates of next meeting

## 7. HANDLING A REJECTED OR SKIPPED ITEM

- If an item cannot be reached due to time, it is carried to the **next
  assembly automatically** and has first priority on the agenda.
- An item can be postponed only by a Class A vote, and never more than once
  without the proposer's consent — a member's matter cannot be buried by
  postponement.

## 8. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

Ramesh wanted the morning slot shifted because his wife's hospital visits
conflicted with the current timing. He submitted the item on the notice-board
form. It appeared on the published agenda as "Morning schedule adjustment —
proposed by Ramesh — Class B." No one could claim it was a secret. The
facilitator noted it affects Ramesh's household specifically, so the meeting
ensured he was heard first. The change, if passed, would need two-thirds of
all members — not just whoever happened to be in the hall that day.

*The agenda is the constitution of the meeting. Whoever controls the agenda
controls the meeting; therefore, no one may control the agenda.*
