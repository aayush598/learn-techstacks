# GOVERNANCE — MODEL AND OVERVIEW

**Folder:** `02-GOVERNANCE/`

---

## 1. WHAT GOVERNANCE MEANS IN THIS SYSTEM

Governance is the set of rules by which the colony takes collective decisions
about water, money, duties, and repairs. This system was created because one
person or group once controlled everything — the on/off switch, the timing, the
repairs, and the money — and exploited the rest. Governance here therefore has
one overriding goal:

> **No single person or group can ever control the system.**

That goal is achieved by three mechanisms, all described in this folder:

1. **Supremacy of the General Assembly** — the only body that may take
   decisions is the assembly of all members.
2. **Execution-only committees** — committees and office-bearers only carry
   out assembly decisions; they never make policy.
3. **Fragmentation of power** — the keys, the money, the records, and the
   tools are held by different, rotating people, so no one can combine control
   of everything.

## 2. THE SUPREME BODY — THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY

- The **General Assembly** is all registered members meeting together. It is
  the highest authority of the system (`01-General-Assembly/`).
- Only the General Assembly may: change the water schedule, change
  contributions, approve spending, form or dissolve committees, admit members,
  adopt amendments, and decide rights-affecting matters.
- Assembly decisions are valid only if made with **quorum** and **recorded in
  minutes** (`01-General-Assembly/02-Quorum-Rules.md`,
  `01-General-Assembly/05-Meeting-Records.md`).
- The assembly does not decide by acclamation or by the loudest voice. It
  decides by the decision classes defined in
  `03-Decision-Making/01-Decision-Classes.md`, and every decision must survive
  the Truth Review Override (`03-Decision-Making/04-Truth-Review-Override.md`).

## 3. COMMITTEES ARE EXECUTION-ONLY

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| Role | Carry out decisions already taken by the assembly |
| Policy power | **None.** No committee makes rules, sets charges, or changes schedules |
| Spending power | Only within the budget line the assembly approved (`06-FINANCE/03-Bookkeeping/05-Annual-Budget.md`) |
| Record | Every act is recorded and open to all members (`04-Transparency/01-Open-Records.md`) |
| Authority | Only what the assembly delegates, for a fixed term |

A committee that exceeds its mandate has acted **without power**, and its act
is void and correctable.

## 4. NO PRESIDENT, NO RULER

- There is **no president, head, manager, or "in-charge"** with decision
  authority anywhere in this system.
- Meetings are chaired by a **rotating facilitator** who is a servant of
  procedure — they cannot decide, override, or direct
  (`05-Rotation-and-Fairness/01-Rotating-Leadership.md`).
- Every role has a fixed term and rotates by lot
  (`05-Rotation-and-Fairness/02-Lot-Draw-System.md`).
- Any attempt to create a permanent role or transfer control to one person is
  **void** under `00-CONSTITUTION/05-Amendment-Process/05-Prohibition-on-Repressive-Amendments.md`.

## 5. FRAGMENTATION OF POWER

Physical and financial control is deliberately split so that no one person or
household can simultaneously:

1. Turn the motor on/off at will,
2. Hold the ledger and bank access alone,
3. Keep the repair vendor's contact private,
4. Control the minutes and records.

| Control point | Held by | Never by the same household as |
|---------------|---------|-------------------------------|
| Motor keys / operator duty | Rotating duty roster | Cash or ledger |
| Cash box and bank signatory | Rotating Finance Committee | Operator duty |
| Ledger and receipts | Finance Committee (dual custody) | Signatories' cash access |
| Minutes and record register | Rotating minute-taker | The facilitator of the same meeting |
| Repair vendor contacts | Repair Committee | Finance custody |

This separation is enforced in
`02-Committees/06-Term-Limits-and-Rotation.md`.

## 7. THE POWER-CONCENTRATION TEST

Before any governance act, any member may apply this test:

> *"Does this act make it easier for one person or a small group to control
> water, money, records, or people?"*

If yes, the act is void regardless of votes, per P2, P3, and P6.

## 8. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

In the old system, one man held the motor key, the ledger, the plumber's number,
and the collection box. He decided who got water and who paid late. Under this
model, the key rotates weekly, the ledger is held under dual custody by the
Finance Committee, the plumber's number is on the notice board, and the cash box
is opened only by two members together. Four different rotating people now hold
the four powers he once held alone. No one of them can repeat what he did.

---

*Governance is not a gift from leaders to the people. It is the people
themselves governing, through rules that make it impossible for any one of them
to become a ruler.*
