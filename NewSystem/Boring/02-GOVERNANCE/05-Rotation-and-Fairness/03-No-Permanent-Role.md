# ROTATION AND FAIRNESS 3 — NO PERMANENT ROLE

**Folder:** `02-GOVERNANCE/05-Rotation-and-Fairness/`

---

## 1. THE RULE

**There is no permanent role in this system.** No "president for life", no
"in-charge forever", no "secretary who has always done it". Every role ends,
rotates, and is drawn anew. This is the concrete expression of P2 and P3.

## 2. WHAT "PERMANENT" MEANS HERE

| Situation | Permanent? |
|-----------|-----------|
| A member holding a role beyond its term | Prohibited; their acts are void |
| A member informally "always available" so the lot always falls to them | Prohibited in effect; the roster balances by recorded swaps |
| A member holding the same title year after year with a one-meeting "gap" | Prohibited; the gap must be real and recorded |
| A family line holding a role across generations | Prohibited; eligibility is per household, and separation rules apply |
| An unwritten "he is the water man" arrangement | Prohibited; only the lot decides duties |

## 3. HOW A PERMANENT ROLE COULD BE ATTEMPTED — AND WHY IT IS VOID

A coalition might try to "elect" a president, or amend the rules to create a
term without a gap, or simply keep calling the same person to every role.
All such attempts are **void**:

1. **Vote-based attempts** — any resolution creating a permanent or
   effectively-permanent role is void under the prohibition on repressive
   amendments (`00-CONSTITUTION/05-Amendment-Process/05-Prohibition-on-Repressive-Amendments.md`),
   which bans "control transfer" and "slippery-slope" steps like "a president
   just for admin".
2. **Custom-based attempts** — "we have always let X do it" is the lowest norm
   in the hierarchy and yields to any written rule
   (`00-CONSTITUTION/06-Supremacy-and-Conflict/01-Norm-Hierarchy.md`).
3. **Practiced attempts** — a member who simply keeps doing the role receives
   a written notice; if it continues, it is obstruction and a CRB matter.
4. **Constitutional amendments to legalize it** — touching equal power is a
   Class C change and fails the Truth Gate; touching the basic structure is
   forbidden outright.

## 4. THE SPIRIT TEST

When any member suspects a role is becoming permanent, apply the test from
`00-CONSTITUTION/05-Amendment-Process/05-Prohibition-on-Repressive-Amendments.md`
Section 4:

> *"Would this arrangement, in the real world of this colony, make it easier
> for a dominant person or group to control water, money, or people?"*

If yes, it is prohibited regardless of wording or votes.

## 5. DETECTION AND ENFORCEMENT

| Signal | Response |
|--------|----------|
| Same name in every draw | The draw sheet is checked; repeat draws are shuffled by full re-draw |
| A role unfilled because "only X can do it" | Training + handover (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/02-Duty-Training/`); no role is skill-single-owner |
| A member refusing to hand over | Handover notice; then obstruction procedure (`02-Committees/06-Term-Limits-and-Rotation.md` Section 6) |
| A role invented with no term | The assembly fixes a term by Class A decision; until then the role holds no authority |
| A title appearing in notices | Struck and re-worded by the minutes' correction process |

- Any member may file a Truth Veto against a decision that would create a
  permanent role (`03-Decision-Making/04-Truth-Review-Override.md`).

## 6. THE BACKUP CHAIN (P3)

Because no role is permanent, **every role has backups**:

| Role | Backup |
|------|--------|
| Facilitator | Next name on the lot list |
| Minute-taker | Next name on the lot list |
| Duty operator | Backup operator named in the roster |
| Committee member | Next name on the lot list |
| Key holder | Second key, different household, sealed and logged |

A system with no permanent role is a system that cannot be captured by
withholding one person.

## 7. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

At an AGM, a member proposed "keeping our respected senior as permanent
committee convenor because he has 20 years of experience." The CRB noted the
proposal creates an effectively permanent role — a prohibited control
transfer. The member was heard with full respect (P10), but the proposal was
declared void and the lot draw proceeded. The senior joined the draw like
everyone else, and — as the lot happened — was not chosen. He served later
when his name came up in another cycle, as a member among members.

---

*No permanent role is the one rule that makes all the other rules possible.
The day a role becomes permanent, the constitution becomes a museum.*
