# TRANSPARENCY 4 — WHISTLEBLOWER CHANNEL

**Folder:** `02-GOVERNANCE/04-Transparency/`

---

## 1. WHY THE CHANNEL EXISTS

Some wrongs are seen by the person who is **afraid to speak** — a tenant who
noticed a theft, a junior worker at the motor room, a member who watched a
strongman taking a bribe. The whistleblower channel lets the truth reach the
system **without the teller being destroyed** (P7, P10). Secrecy for the
reporter is the price of truth.

## 2. THE CHANNELS

| Channel | How |
|---------|-----|
| Anonymous letter box | A sealed, slot-locked box at the notice board; letters need no name |
| Anonymous address | A PO-box-style address kept by the CRB, or an anonymous email/phone number managed by the CRB |
| Named report | A member may report in their own name at any time |
| Direct to CRB | In person, in writing, or through any CRB member |

- A report may be made by **any person** — member, tenant, worker, or
  outsider — about anything touching the system: money, water, duty, repair,
  coercion, or records.

## 3. WHAT A REPORT MUST CONTAIN (BEST EFFORT)

1. What happened (facts, dates, places).
2. Who was involved (as far as known).
3. Any evidence (receipts, photos, logs, witnesses).
4. Why the reporter believes it is wrong.

- A report is accepted even if incomplete. The CRB investigates gaps, not the
  reporter's writing skills.

## 4. HOW ANONYMITY IS PROTECTED

| Protection | Rule |
|------------|------|
| Sealed handling | The letter box is opened by **two CRB members together**, in each other's presence |
| Identity lockdown | No record links a report to its author; the CRB register stores the letter with a report number, not a name |
| No source-hunting | Asking "who do you think wrote this?" is itself an offence; investigating the reporter is prohibited |
| Testimony protection | If the reporter comes forward, their identity is not disclosed without written consent |
| Limited access | The whistleblower register is the only record with limited access (`01-Open-Records.md` Section 4) |

## 5. NO RETALIATION

- Retaliation against a reporter — threats, shunning, boycott, loss of water
  slot, harassment of family — is **targeting** (P7) and is treated as a
  serious offence under the Enforcement Ladder
  (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/`).
- A person who reports in good faith can never be punished even if the report
  turns out to be wrong.

## 6. FALSE REPORTING

- Good-faith mistakes are protected. **Malicious false reports** — reports
  made to damage someone, knowingly false — are a different matter:
  | Report | Outcome |
  |--------|---------|
  | Good faith, accurate | Protected; issue investigated |
  | Good faith, mistaken | Protected; no penalty; correction recorded |
  | Malicious, knowingly false | CRB finding; the reporter is warned, then treated under the Enforcement Ladder for defamation/targeting |
- The line between "mistaken" and "malicious" is drawn by the CRB on
  evidence, and the protection of good faith is presumed.

## 7. FORWARDING TO THE CRB

- Every report goes to the **CRB** (`02-Committees/05-Conflict-Review-Board.md`),
  which owns the channel.
- Handling timeline:

| Step | Deadline |
|------|----------|
| Log report (number + date) | Within 24h of opening |
| Screen: within scope, credible | Within 3 days |
| Investigate (evidence-based) | Within 14 days (may extend once, with written reason) |
| Written finding | Within 3 days of investigation close |
| Action | Referred to assembly/enforcement ladder if it finds wrongdoing |

- The reporter is never required to testify. If they choose to, the finding
  may cite their evidence without their name.

## 8. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

A tenant delivering iron at the colony noticed a contractor paid for "two
machines" deliver only one. She dropped an anonymous note in the box. The CRB
opened it in pairs, verified delivery records against the payment, and found
₹14,000 unaccounted. The finding named the contractor and the signatures on
the voucher — but not the note's author. The money was recovered. The tenant
continued living in the colony, unknown and unthreatened.

---

*The whistleblower channel is the system's ear for the truths that would
otherwise never be spoken. Protecting the speaker is not a kindness; it is how
the system hears at all.*
