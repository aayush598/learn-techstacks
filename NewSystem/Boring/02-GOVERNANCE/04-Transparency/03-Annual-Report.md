# TRANSPARENCY 3 — ANNUAL REPORT

**Folder:** `02-GOVERNANCE/04-Transparency/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

Once a year the system must **account to itself**. The Annual Report is the
yearly summary that makes the whole colony — not just those who attend
meetings — able to see what happened, what was spent, and what changed.

## 2. WHEN AND WHERE

- Prepared for the **Annual General Meeting** (`01-General-Assembly/01-Assembly-Schedule.md`).
- The draft is published **15 days before** the AGM so members can read it
  before attending.
- Read aloud at the AGM, then **posted publicly** (notice board + digital)
  and filed in the Open Records.

## 3. WHAT THE REPORT MUST COVER

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **Water** | Hours supplied, schedule changes, seasonal changes, complaints and how handled |
| **Finance** | Opening and closing balances, income (contribution, buy-in, fees), expenses by line, arrears summary, audit outcome (`06-FINANCE/04-Audit/`) |
| **Duty** | Roster compliance, swaps, duty failures, time-credits earned and paid |
| **Repairs** | All faults, repairs, vendor choices, costs, downtime |
| **Governance** | Meetings held, quorum record, decisions by class, amendments adopted |
| **Conflicts** | Grievances filed, Truth Vetoes, CRB findings, enforcement actions (anonymised where needed) |
| **Changes** | Every rule change of the year, with its class and vote |
| **Next year** | Budget proposal, rotation calendar, planned works |

The required structure follows `12-TEMPLATES/16-Annual-Report-Template.md`.

## 4. WHO PREPARES IT

- The Finance Committee compiles the financial sections; the Water, Repair,
  and other committees supply their sections.
- **One member reviews the whole draft** and signs it — the "compiler", drawn
  by lot, not a permanent reporter.
- No section may be left blank "because nothing happened"; nothing happening
  is itself a statement.

## 5. THE AGM READING AND CHALLENGE

- The report is read section by section. Members may ask questions on any
  line.
- Corrections found at the AGM are incorporated before the report is
  finalised.
- The final report is **adopted by a Class A vote** of those present.
- A member who believes a figure is wrong may file a Truth Veto; the CRB
  verifies the figure against the records
  (`03-Decision-Making/04-Truth-Review-Override.md`).

## 6. PUBLIC POSTING

- The adopted report is posted on the notice board and members' group, and a
  printed copy is kept in the Open Records.
- The report remains available for the whole year — it does not disappear
  after the AGM.
- Arrears and penalty data appear only where the system's published rules
  allow; identities of whistleblowers never appear.

## 7. NON-COMPLIANCE

- Failure to produce the Annual Report is a serious transparency failure. The
  AGM is not "skipped" because the report is missing — the AGM is held and the
  failure is minuted with reasons, and the CRB reviews who failed and why.

## 8. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

At the AGM, the report showed electricity costs had risen 12% while supply
hours stayed the same. One member asked why. The answer in the report pointed
to the seasonal increase in pumping depth. Another member noted the repair
section listed a ₹4,800 motor repair with three quotes attached. Members left
the hall knowing where every rupee of the year had gone — the opposite of the
old system where the yearly totals lived only in one man's memory.

---

*The Annual Report is the colony's mirror. Held once a year, it lets everyone
see what the system actually did — not what anyone claims it did.*
