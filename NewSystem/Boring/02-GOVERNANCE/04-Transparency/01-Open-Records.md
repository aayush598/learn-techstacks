# TRANSPARENCY 1 — OPEN RECORDS

**Folder:** `02-GOVERNANCE/04-Transparency/`

---

## 1. THE PRINCIPLE: EVERYTHING IS OPEN

Radical transparency (P6) means the colony keeps **no secret books**. Every
decision, rupee, log, and roster entry is open to every member. Secrecy is not
a privilege a committee can grant itself; it is an offence.

## 2. WHAT IS "OPEN" PRACTICALLY

| Record | Format | Where kept | Updated |
|--------|--------|------------|---------|
| Assembly minutes | Physical + digital | Notice board + digital archive | Within 48h of each meeting |
| Member registry | Physical + digital | Registry file | On every change |
| Ledger and cash book | Physical + digital | Finance file | Every transaction |
| Bank passbook | Physical copy + photo | Finance file | Monthly |
| Motor run log | Physical | Motor room | Each duty cycle |
| Duty roster | Physical + digital | Notice board | On every draw |
| Repair log and vendor comparisons | Physical + digital | Repair file | Each fault |
| Amendment register | Physical + digital | Constitutional file | Each proposal |
| CRB findings | Physical + digital | Conflict file | Each finding |
| Whistleblower register | Secure, access-limited | CRB custody | Each report |

## 3. PHYSICAL AND DIGITAL TOGETHER

- Every key record exists in **two forms**: a physical register (book) and a
  digital copy (members' group / shared drive).
- The physical register is the authoritative original; the digital copy is a
  mirror for easy reading. Both must match.
- If they differ, the discrepancy is itself published and corrected; the
  physical register prevails unless the CRB finds tampering.

## 4. WHO MAY READ

- **Every member** may read any record, at any time, without asking
  permission (`02-Right-to-Inspect.md`).
- Non-members may read public notices and the annual report but not the
  member registry or individual arrears lists.

## 5. WHAT MUST BE PUBLISHED REGULARLY

| Publication | Frequency |
|-------------|-----------|
| Monthly finance statement | Monthly |
| Water schedule (current version) | On change |
| Duty roster | Per draw |
| Meeting minutes | Within 48h of each meeting |
| Repair status | Per fault |
| Annual report | Yearly at the AGM (`03-Annual-Report.md`) |

## 6. DENIAL OF ACCESS IS AN OFFENCE

- Refusing a member a record, hiding a page, or keeping a parallel unofficial
  book is a **transparency offence** — not an inconvenience.
- Handling: report to the CRB; a first finding is a warning plus mandatory
  disclosure; a repeat is escalated in the Enforcement Ladder
  (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/`).
- A member denied access has the automatic right to raise the denial at the
  next assembly, and the minutes record it.

## 7. RECORD RETENTION

- Records are retained per `11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/03-Records-Retention.md`
  (minutes, ledgers, and CRB findings kept for at least 5 years; registers
  kept permanently).
- Destruction of records before the retention period is an offence.

## 8. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

A member noticed the monthly statement showed an electricity bill higher than
the tariff rate. Because the passbook and receipts are open records, she
simply opened the file, compared the bill with the tariff slab, and raised a
question at the next assembly with documents in hand. The overcharge was
recovered from the supplier. No meeting, no permission, no "who are you to
ask" — the record was hers to read by right.

---

*Openness is not a favour done to members. It is the blood that keeps the
system alive; stop it, and the system dies of secrecy.*
