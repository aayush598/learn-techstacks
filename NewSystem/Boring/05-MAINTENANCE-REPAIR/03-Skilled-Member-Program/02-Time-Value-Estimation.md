# 02 - TIME VALUE ESTIMATION

**Folder:** `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/03-Skilled-Member-Program/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

Skill has a price. The moment the colony says "your time is worth nothing", it
has recreated the old system where skilled members were bled dry as "duty".
This rule fixes a **published value for a skilled member's hour** before any
work begins, so there is never a negotiation at the workshop door.

## 2. THE TWO VALUATION MODES

A skilled member's time is valued in one of two ways — **chosen by the member**
before the work starts (`03-Credit-or-Payment-Choice.md`):

| Mode | Value | Paid from | Notes |
|------|-------|-----------|-------|
| **Time-credits** | 1 hour = 1 time-credit | Credit ledger (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/03-Duty-Compensation/01-Time-Credits.md`) | Same unit as duty compensation; value published quarterly |
| **Cash (hourly rate)** | Published hourly rate for the skill | Repair Fund | Rate reflects the local market for that skill |

## 3. HOW THE HOURLY RATE IS SET

1. The Repair Committee surveys the **local market rate** for each skill once
   a year: what an independent electrician or plumber charges per hour in the
   area, from two or more sources.
2. The published hourly rate is set at a fair fraction of the local market —
   **between 70% and 100% of the local rate** — because a member is also a
   co-owner of the system, not a stranger charging top market.
3. The rate table is presented to and approved by the **assembly**, then
   published on the notice board.

| Skill | Indicative local rate | Colony published rate |
|-------|----------------------|-----------------------|
| Electrical / motor work | Set from local survey | Set from local survey |
| Plumbing / pipe work | Set from local survey | Set from local survey |
| Welding | Set from local survey | Set from local survey |
| General labor assistance | Set from local survey | Set from local survey |

Indicative rates are a starting point; actual numbers are always the surveyed
local rates, reviewed yearly, never fixed by one person.

## 4. RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

1. **Agreed before work.** Before any paid assignment starts, the member and
   the Repair Committee record: skill, expected hours, mode (credits or cash),
   and rate. No work starts on "we will settle later".
2. **Travel and waiting count.** Time spent travelling to a parts shop for the
   repair, or waiting for the vendor, is billed at the same hourly rate unless
   the member agrees otherwise in writing.
3. **One rate, public.** The same member is paid the published rate for the
   same skill every time. No "special friend rate" and no "emergency rate"
   invented on the spot.
4. **Minimum bill.** A short visit is compensated as a minimum of 1 hour, so a
   member is not rewarded with a token for showing up.
5. **Overtime.** Work beyond 8 hours in a day is compensated at 1.5x the rate,
   or agreed in advance.

## 5. HOW CREDITS ARE VALUED

1. Credits follow the quarterly published **credit rate** — the rupee value of
   one time-credit — set by the assembly
   (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/03-Duty-Compensation/01-Time-Credits.md`).
2. A member who chooses credits is guaranteed the credit is recorded, tracked,
   and redeemable on the published terms. Credits are never "too complicated
   to pay out".
3. The colony settles credit balances at least once a year against the credit
   rate, so credits do not become an excuse to defer payment forever.

## 6. SCENARIO — "I KNOW ELECTRIC WORK"

A member says "I know electric work" during a fault. Under this rule, that
sentence starts a recorded conversation: *which skill, how many hours, credits
or cash, at the published rate, agreed before touching the panel.* The member
then works as a compensated skilled member, and the colony gains a service it
can price, verify, and pay. Without the rule, that same sentence would have
become a free-labor trap — and, later, a "you owe me" grievance.

## 7. RATE REVIEW AND DISPUTES

1. Rates are reviewed yearly with the local market survey and approved by the
   assembly. A rate that becomes unfair (too high or too low) is corrected at
   the review, not in the middle of a job.
2. If a member disputes hours or rate, the fault record and the written
   engagement note are the evidence; disputes follow
   `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/`.
