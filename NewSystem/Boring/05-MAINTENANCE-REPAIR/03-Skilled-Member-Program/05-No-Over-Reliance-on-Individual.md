# 05 - NO OVER-RELIANCE ON ANY INDIVIDUAL

**Folder:** `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/03-Skilled-Member-Program/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE (P3)

In the old system the colony was managed so that **one person was
indispensable**: the only one who knew the plumber, the only one who could
read the panel, the only one whose "understanding" settled every dispute. Such
a person could not be questioned, because the colony could not function
without them. That is precisely the hostage structure this resource forbids.

> **No individual, however brilliant, may become the single point on which the
> colony's water depends.**

## 2. THE TWO-COMPETENT-PERSONS RULE

For every critical skill, the colony must have **at least two capable
resources** — either two skilled members, or one skilled member and a
contracted vendor:

| Skill | Resource 1 | Resource 2 | Checked |
|-------|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Electrical / motor repair | Skilled member A | Approved electrician (vendor) | Annually |
| Plumbing / pipe work | Skilled member B | Approved plumber (vendor) | Annually |
| Operation (switch on/off) | Duty roster (all trained) | Backup list | Quarterly |
| Diagnosis | Approved electrician | Pump-shop technician | Annually |
| Accounts / records | Finance Committee member | Auditor | Annually |

The Repair Committee maintains this **skill matrix** and reviews it once a
year at the skill refresh (`01-Skill-Registry.md`). A skill with only one
capable person is a **reported gap** and must be closed within 6 months —
through training a member, registering a backup vendor, or both.

## 3. TRAINING AND KNOWLEDGE SHARING

1. **Knowledge is shared, not hoarded.** Any member who holds a working
   understanding of the motor, the panel, or the plumbing is expected to teach
   it in the training sessions held at least twice a year
   (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/02-Duty-Training/`).
2. **Operation training is compulsory** for all able members
   (`00-CONSTITUTION/03-Fundamental-Duties/02-Duty-to-Learn-and-Participate.md`).
3. The colony may sponsor a second person's training for a skill where only one
   member is capable (P9). Sponsored training is a PM-budget line
   (`01-Preventive-Maintenance/04-PM-Budget.md`).
4. When a skilled member performs a notable repair, they are asked (and paid)
   to explain the work at the next meeting or record a short note, so the
   knowledge outlives the repair.

## 4. THE INDISPENSABLE-PERSON TRAP

| Symptom | Response |
|---------|----------|
| "Only Ramesh can do it" | The gap is reported; training or a backup vendor is arranged within 6 months |
| A skilled member refuses to teach | Teaching the shared training is a duty; refusal is addressed, not accommodated |
| A vendor's price only ever heard through one member | The member becomes "interested"; quotes go through the committee, not through them |
| Keys/records/logs live in one house | Dual-key and public-record rules apply (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/05-Key-and-Control/`) |
| "Ask X, X knows everything" in every discussion | The assembly consciously rotates the point of contact |

## 5. THE GOOD SAMARITAN PROBLEM

A member who genuinely fixes everything cheaply is valuable — and dangerous.
Even a benevolent single point of failure breaks the day they move away, fall
sick, or die. Therefore:

1. Their generosity is honored — they are paid or credited like anyone else,
   and thanked publicly.
2. But the colony **must** still train a second person and keep a backup
   vendor, even while the hero is available. "X is here, we don't need a
   backup" is a forbidden sentence.
3. The hero's departure is planned for, not feared. The colony is structured so
   that when they leave, nothing changes except their compensation stops.

## 6. VENDOR SINGLE-DEPENDENCY

1. No single vendor may be the only provider of a critical service. The
   Approved Vendor List (`04-Vendor-Management/01-Approved-Vendor-List.md`)
   keeps at least two vendors per critical trade.
2. If one vendor is removed, suspended, or unavailable, the second vendor
   covers; if only one vendor exists in the area, the committee records the
   fact and actively looks for a second (including out-of-colony shops) each
   year.

## 7. YEARLY INDEPENDENCE REVIEW

Once a year the assembly reviews:

1. The skill matrix — every critical skill has 2+ resources?
2. The training log — how many members trained and retrained?
3. The vendor list — at least 2 vendors per trade?
4. Any member's departure in the last year — did water continue without
   disruption?

The review is recorded. A colony that cannot answer "who else?" to any skill
has work to do before the next crisis.
