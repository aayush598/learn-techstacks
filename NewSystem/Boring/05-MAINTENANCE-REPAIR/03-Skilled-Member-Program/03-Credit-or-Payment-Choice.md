# 03 - CREDIT OR PAYMENT CHOICE

**Folder:** `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/03-Skilled-Member-Program/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

A skilled member has earned compensation, but compensation comes in two forms:
time-credits or cash. The choice must be the member's, made **before** the
work, and recorded. "We thought you wanted credits" is a dispute that this
rule eliminates before it can begin.

## 2. THE CHOICE

1. Before a paid assignment begins, the skilled member chooses **one** of:

   | Choice | Meaning |
   |--------|---------|
   | **Credits only** | 1 hour = 1 time-credit, per `02-Time-Value-Estimation.md` |
   | **Cash only** | Paid the published hourly rate from the Repair Fund |
   | **Both (split)** | A recorded split, e.g., "2 hours credits, 3 hours cash" |

2. The choice is recorded in the engagement note under the fault number, with
   the member's signature.
3. The choice is fixed for that assignment. It cannot be changed mid-job to
   take advantage of a later situation, and the colony cannot change it to
   reduce what it pays.

## 3. WHY CHOICE MATTERS

1. **Dignity (P10).** The member decides the form of their own reward; the
   colony does not decide on their behalf.
2. **Fairness (P8).** A member who needs cash for a school fee is not forced
   into credits; a member who prefers credits for the yearly settlement is not
   forced into cash.
3. **Clarity (P6).** A recorded choice means the ledger, the credit register,
   and the voucher all agree. There is no "double counting" and no ambiguity.

## 4. RECORDING RULES

1. The choice is written into the engagement note **before work starts**, in
   the format:

   | Field | Value |
   |-------|-------|
   | Member | |
   | Assignment / fault number | |
   | Skill and estimated hours | |
   | Mode chosen | Credits / Cash / Both |
   | If both: split | |
   | Rate basis | Published rate (date) |
   | Signed by member | |
   | Signed by Repair Committee | |
   | Date | |

2. The same record is used for the voucher at payment time
   (`06-FINANCE/03-Bookkeeping/02-Expense-Voucher-Rule.md`).
3. If the work takes more hours than estimated, the extra hours are paid on
   the same mode at the same rate; if the member wants to change mode for the
   extra hours, that is a new recorded choice before the extra hours are done.

## 5. CONVERSION AND SETTLEMENT

1. Credits are redeemed at the published quarterly credit rate
   (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/03-Duty-Compensation/01-Time-Credits.md`).
2. The colony may, at the yearly settlement, offer to convert a member's
   accumulated credits to cash at the credit rate. The member may accept or
   keep the credits. Conversion is never forced.
3. Cash payments are made only by bank transfer or UPI with a voucher — never
   in hand.

## 6. SCENARIO — THE AMBIGUOUS "GIFT"

A member fixes a leaking main line on a Sunday, and later tells the assembly
"that was my gift to the colony". Under this rule the engagement note shows the
truth: was the work agreed and compensated, or was it a voluntary gift
recorded under `04-No-Forced-Free-Labor.md`? The record, not the memory,
decides. Nobody's goodwill is exploited, and nobody's claim is inflated.
