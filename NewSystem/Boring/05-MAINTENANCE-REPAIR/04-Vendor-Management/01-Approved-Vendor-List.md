# 01 - APPROVED VENDOR LIST

**Folder:** `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/04-Vendor-Management/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

In the old system, "the plumber" was a private contact of one person — his
price, his availability, and his word were controlled by that person. The
Approved Vendor List makes vendors a **public, vetted, replaceable resource**
of the colony. The colony deals with the list, not with one person's "my
plumber".

## 2. THE LIST

The list is maintained by the Repair Committee and published. It covers every
trade the colony needs:

| Trade | Purpose | Minimum count on list |
|-------|---------|-----------------------|
| **Electrician** | Motor/panel checks, fault diagnosis, wiring | 2 |
| **Pump shop / motor technician** | Winding, pump service, motor replacement | 2 |
| **Plumber** | Pipe joints, leaks, valves, line work | 2 |
| **Welder** | Pipe/mounting/bracket repairs | 1-2 |
| **Water tanker supplier** | Emergency supply (`10-RISK-AND-EMERGENCY/01-Drought-and-Scarcity/03-Bulk-Purchase-Contingency.md`) | 1-2 |
| **Lab (water testing)** | Quality testing (`10-RISK-AND-EMERGENCY/04-Health-and-Safety/01-Water-Quality-Testing.md`) | 1 |

## 3. HOW A VENDOR GETS ONTO THE LIST

| Criterion | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Two references** | Two members (or two known customers) who have used the vendor's service and vouch for quality |
| **Price reasonableness** | Quoted rates comparable to the local market survey |
| **Licence / registration** | GST/registration or trade licence where applicable; copies kept on file |
| **Availability** | Works in the area, reachable by phone, responds within the timeline rules |
| **Assembly approval** | The Repair Committee proposes; the assembly approves by simple majority at a meeting with quorum |

The committee records each vendor's approval date and the evidence (references,
rate card, licence copy) in the vendor file, which is public.

## 4. HOW A VENDOR GETS REMOVED

| Reason | Step |
|--------|------|
| Repeated late completion | Warning (rated down) then removal per `03-Vendor-Performance-Rating.md` |
| Inflated or fraudulent billing | Removal after a recorded finding; report to authorities if criminal |
| Poor quality / repeated callbacks | Warning then removal |
| Commission or kickback involvement | Removal immediately; see `04-No-Personal-Vendor-Kickbacks.md` |
| Unreachable / relocated | Removed at the yearly review |

Removal is a Repair Committee decision recorded and published; the affected
vendor is informed in writing and may appeal to the assembly.

## 5. REVIEW

1. The list is **reviewed once a year** by the committee and the assembly:
   ratings reviewed, prices compared with the market survey, contact numbers
   confirmed.
2. Any member may propose adding a vendor at any time; the proposal goes
   through the same vetting.
3. The yearly review is recorded and published. A stale list (outdated
   numbers, dead vendors) is a process failure, not a triviality.

## 6. USING THE LIST

1. All quote requests go to vendors **on the list**
   (`02-Multiple-Quotes-Rule.md`). A vendor not on the list may be used only
   for a one-time emergency, with the reason recorded and the vendor considered
   for the list afterwards.
2. The committee is the single point of contact, but the **contact details and
   addresses of all vendors are public** — any member can reach them. No one
   holds the vendors hostage (P3).
3. Vendors are told their ratings are public and their continued listing
   depends on performance.

## 7. SCENARIO — "ONLY HE KNOWS THE PLUMBER"

A member says "only my plumber can fix this, everyone else is useless." Under
this rule the claim is tested: the list has two plumbers, both vetted; the
fault goes to the standard quote process; if "my plumber" is really better,
they are simply added to the list and then treated like every other vendor —
quoted, compared, rated, and paid only after verification. The private plumber
becomes a public resource or a non-factor. Either way, the member loses the
leverage.
