# 03 - VENDOR PERFORMANCE RATING

**Folder:** `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/04-Vendor-Management/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

A vendor list without feedback is just a phone directory. The performance
rating turns every completed job into a **score** that decides whether a vendor
stays, is warned, or is removed — so that quality, not friendship, keeps a
vendor on the list.

## 2. WHAT IS RATED

After every completed job, the Repair Committee rates the vendor on four
scales, each 1-5 (5 = best):

| Criterion | What it measures |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Timeliness** | Completed within the quoted and approved timeline |
| **Quality** | Work matches the diagnosis; no callbacks; parts genuine |
| **Price honesty** | Final bill matches the approved quote; no surprise line items |
| **Communication** | Reported progress, responded to calls, honoured the fault number |

## 3. THE RATING LOG

1. Ratings are recorded in the vendor rating log, one row per job, under the
   fault number:

   | Fault | Vendor | Date | Timeliness | Quality | Price honesty | Communication | Overall | Verifier |
   |-------|--------|------|-----------|---------|---------------|---------------|---------|----------|
   | F-2026-014 | | | | | | | | |

2. The verifier (`02-Breakdown-Repair/05-After-Repair-Verification.md`) and
   the Repair Committee both contribute; the final entry is signed.
3. The log is public — any member may read it. **Vendors are told their
   ratings are public** and that continued listing depends on them.

## 4. HOW RATINGS ARE USED

| Average over last 3 jobs | Action |
|---------------------------|--------|
| **4.5+** | Preferred vendor; may be asked for quotes first |
| **3.5 – 4.4** | Normal status |
| **2.5 – 3.4** | Written warning; next job is probation |
| **Below 2.5** | Removal from the Approved Vendor List; recorded reason; appeal to assembly possible |

1. Warnings are written and given to the vendor in person or by message, with
   the specific failing jobs named.
2. Removal requires two committee members' signatures and a published reason.
3. A removed vendor may appeal to the assembly, which may re-list them with a
   probation period.

## 5. THE VENDOR'S VIEW

1. The colony publishes the rating policy to vendors when they join the list:
   "Your price, speed, and honesty are scored after every job, and the scores
   are public."
2. Vendors may respond in writing to a poor rating; the response is attached
   to the rating entry.
3. A vendor who consistently scores well is told so — good vendors are worth
   keeping, and the colony says so.

## 6. SCENARIO — THE ALWAYS-LATE VENDOR

A plumber is cheap but late. Job 1: two days late, scored 2. Job 2: one day
late with a callback for a leaking joint, scored 2.5. The committee issues the
written warning after job 2. Job 3 is probation; he is late again. He is
removed from the list, and the second plumber on the list becomes the primary
contact — the colony's water no longer depends on a vendor who treats the
schedule as optional.
