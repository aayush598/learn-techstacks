# 01 - CRITICAL SPARE PARTS

**Folder:** `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/05-Parts-and-Inventory/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

In the old system, a repair "waited" for parts — and the waiting was a weapon.
When the only one who knew where to buy a capacitor went on holiday, the
colony was dry. A stocked spare-parts inventory makes the most common faults
repairable **the same day**, without waiting, without panic buying, and without
inflated emergency prices (P9).

## 2. THE CRITICAL STOCK LIST

The colony keeps the following stock in the motor room, in a locked inventory
cabinet:

| Part | Purpose | Typical life | Stock level |
|------|---------|--------------|-------------|
| **Motor capacitor(s)** | Starting/running for single-phase motors | 2-4 years | 2 |
| **Starter / contactor (matching rating)** | Switchgear replacement | 5-8 years | 1 |
| **Overload relay** | Protection | 5-8 years | 1 |
| **Power cable (10-20 m, matching gauge)** | Cable fault repair | 10+ years | 1 coil |
| **Cable lugs and glands** | Termination | — | 1 packet |
| **Union couplings / GI fittings** | Pipe repair | — | 2-4 |
| **Gate valves (matching size)** | Line isolation | 5-10 years | 1-2 |
| **Foot valve / strainer** | Suction protection | 2-5 years | 1 |
| **Fuses and fuse wire** | Panel protection | — | 1 box |
| **Grease, oil, rust remover** | Lubrication | — | 1 each |
| **Spare pipe pieces (2-3 m, matching size)** | Burst section replacement | — | 2 |
| **Insulation tape, pvc tape, binding wire** | Joint repairs | — | 2 rolls |
| **Bolt/nut/washer set, gasket sheet** | Mounting, flanges | — | 1 set |
| **Plumbing sealant / thread tape** | Joint sealing | — | 2 rolls |
| **Motor cover / weather protection** | Monsoon readiness | 2-3 years | 1 |

Items marked with a dash for life are consumables, replenished as used. Exact
quantities and sizes are set by the Repair Committee for the actual motor, pipe
size, and panel model, recorded in the inventory ledger.

## 3. HOW STOCK IS CHOSEN

1. Parts must match the **actual motor rating, panel model, and pipe size** —
   recorded in the asset file. Buying a random capacitor "that looks similar"
   is forbidden.
2. Branded/graded parts (ISI-marked cable, matching-wattage capacitor) are
   preferred; the purchase record notes brand and spec.
3. The stock list is reviewed at each quarterly PM round
   (`01-Preventive-Maintenance/01-PM-Schedule.md`) and adjusted to what the
   system actually uses.

## 4. FUNDING

1. Stock is purchased from the **Repair Fund** per the approved PM budget
   (`01-Preventive-Maintenance/04-PM-Budget.md`).
2. The stock value is an asset recorded in the books and counted at inventory
   time. It is common property (P1), never a private stash.
3. Replenishment follows the refill process (`03-Stock-Refill-Process.md`).

## 5. SCENARIO — THE SUMMER CAPACITOR

On a June evening the motor hums and will not start: the capacitor is dead.
In the old system, the colony would wait one or two days while someone found a
shop, and pay whatever the shop asked in the seller's market. Under this rule,
a member checks the inventory ledger, takes the spare matching capacitor from
the cabinet (logged out), the electrician fits it, and water flows the same
evening. The failure cost the colony a capacitor's price — not a week of
blackmail.

## 6. WHO MAY USE STOCK

1. Stock is released only against a fault number or a PM round, and only by
   the inventory custodian (a committee member, rotating monthly).
2. Using stock for a private household repair is theft of common property and
   a Level 3-4 offence.
3. The stock is never sold to outsiders, even at a profit; it is the colony's
   reserve.
