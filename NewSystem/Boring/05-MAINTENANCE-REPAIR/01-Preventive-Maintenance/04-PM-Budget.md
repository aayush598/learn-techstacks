# 04 - PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE BUDGET

**Folder:** `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/01-Preventive-Maintenance/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

Preventive maintenance must be **pre-funded and pre-approved**, so that a
check is never cancelled because "there is no money right now". The PM budget
is a fixed annual line item. Its size comes from the schedule, not from a
mood.

## 2. WHAT THE BUDGET COVERS

| Budget line | Examples | Who prices it |
|-------------|----------|---------------|
| **Electrician PM fee** | 4 quarterly rounds + summer monthly light checks | Approved electrician's published rate |
| **Skilled-member compensation** | Time-credits or stipend for plumbing/civil checks | `03-Skilled-Member-Program/02-Time-Value-Estimation.md` |
| **Consumables** | Grease, oil, tape, sandpaper, contact cleaner, cable lugs, fuses, gland packing | Repair Committee from local market |
| **Spare parts replenishment** | Stocking/refilling the parts inventory per `05-Parts-and-Inventory/03-Stock-Refill-Process.md` | Approved pump shop quotes |
| **Testing and consumables** | Megger test, tank cleaning material, disinfectant | Approved supplier |

## 3. ANNUAL BUDGET FORMAT

The Repair Committee prepares the budget each year in this format and presents
it to the assembly:

| Line | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Q4 | Annual |
|------|----|----|----|----|--------|
| Electrician PM fee | ₹ | ₹ | ₹ | ₹ | ₹ |
| Skilled-member compensation | ₹ | ₹ | ₹ | ₹ | ₹ |
| Consumables | ₹ | ₹ | ₹ | ₹ | ₹ |
| Spare stock refill | ₹ | ₹ | ₹ | ₹ | ₹ |
| Testing | ₹ | ₹ | ₹ | ₹ | ₹ |
| **Total** | | | | | **₹** |

Indicative planning figure for a standard colony (20-40 households): the annual
PM budget is typically **2-5% of the annual electricity + contribution base**.
Actual figures are set by the assembly using real local rates.

## 4. FUNDING SOURCE

1. The PM budget is funded from the **Repair Fund**, which is itself fed by a
   fixed allocation from the Common Fund
   (`06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/03-Repair-Fund.md`).
2. If the Repair Fund balance cannot cover the approved PM budget, the shortfall
   is drawn from the Reserve Fund only by assembly resolution.
3. PM spending does not require case-by-case approval beyond the approved
   budget; the Repair Committee spends within the approved line items and
   reports actuals quarterly.

## 5. APPROVAL

1. The annual PM budget is presented to the **General Assembly** before the
   start of the financial year.
2. Approval requires a simple majority resolution at a meeting with quorum.
3. The budget, once approved, is published. The published approved budget is
   the ceiling for the year's PM line items.
4. Any **re-budgeting** (moving money between lines) above ₹2,000 requires a
   recorded Repair Committee decision; above ₹5,000, assembly approval.

## 6. ACCOUNTING AND CONTROL

1. Every PM payment is made by bank transfer or UPI with a voucher, never in
   cash (`06-FINANCE/03-Bookkeeping/02-Expense-Voucher-Rule.md`).
2. Actual spending is reconciled against the approved lines every quarter and
   published in the bimonthly statement.
3. If the PM budget is under-used because rounds were skipped, that is a
   process failure, not a saving. The assembly reviews the log
   (`03-Maintenance-Log.md`) and the reason for any under-spend.
