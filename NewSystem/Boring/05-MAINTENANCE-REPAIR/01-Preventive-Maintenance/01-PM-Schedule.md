# 01 - PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE

**Folder:** `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/01-Preventive-Maintenance/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

Preventive maintenance exists so that "the motor is not working" can never
again be a usable excuse. A motor that is checked quarterly, cleaned, and
protected against dry-running rarely fails without warning. When a fault does
appear, it appears as a **recorded observation** — not as a sudden mystery.

The standing rule:

> **No quarter passes without a preventive maintenance (PM) round. A missed PM
> is itself a reportable event, logged and explained to the assembly.**

## 2. QUARTERLY CHECKLIST (CORE CHECKS)

The following checks are performed **once every quarter**, before summer and
before monsoon at a minimum. Timing rules are in Section 3.

| # | Check | What it catches | Performed by |
|---|-------|-----------------|--------------|
| 1 | **Winding test** (megger/insulation resistance) | Burned or weak winding, shorted turns | Approved electrician |
| 2 | **Insulation resistance test** (body-to-earth) | Leakage, imminent shock/failure risk | Approved electrician |
| 3 | **Starter / contactor check** (points, coils, springs) | Sticking contactor, burnt points, single phasing | Approved electrician |
| 4 | **Cable and gland inspection** (power cable, joints) | Frayed cable, water ingress, loose lugs | Electrician + skilled member |
| 5 | **Pipe joints and line check** | Leaks, loose couplings, burst risk | Skilled member (plumbing) |
| 6 | **Overhead / sump tank cleaning** | Sludge, algae, mosquito breeding | Duty rotation members |
| 7 | **Strainer / foot valve check** (if accessible) | Choked suction, sand ingress, valve leakage | Skilled member |
| 8 | **Bore drawdown / discharge test** (pump performance) | Declining yield, pump wear | Electrician + skilled member |

Every check is marked **PASS / FAIL / OBSERVATION** on the standard checklist
(`02-Checklist.md`) and signed by the persons who performed it.

## 3. SCHEDULING RULES

1. The Repair Committee fixes the PM date **at the start of each quarter** and
   publishes it at least 7 days in advance.
2. PM is scheduled **outside peak water times**:
   - never during a scheduled morning/evening supply slot;
   - prefer late-morning to afternoon hours when household demand is lowest;
   - on tank-fill days the tank is filled to capacity the evening before.
3. If a PM must interrupt supply, the members are told at least 24 hours in
   advance, with the expected duration (`10-RISK-AND-EMERGENCY/02-Motor-Breakdown/04-Communication-to-Members.md`).
4. The electrician's visit and the skilled member's availability are booked in
   the maintenance log before the date, so PM is never cancelled for "no one
   was free".

## 4. WHO PERFORMS PM

1. **Electrical checks (1-4):** an approved electrician from the Approved
   Vendor List (`04-Vendor-Management/01-Approved-Vendor-List.md`), accompanied
   by a registered skilled member. Two people are always present (P3).
2. **Civil / plumbing checks (5-8):** skilled member(s) registered in the
   Skill Registry (`03-Skilled-Member-Program/01-Skill-Registry.md`).
3. The electrician and the skilled member are **paid the published rate**
   (`03-Skilled-Member-Program/02-Time-Value-Estimation.md`). PM is not free
   labor (P8).
4. A third person — a duty member or any member — attends as a **witness** and
   signs the checklist. The witness may be the Repair Committee member of the
   quarter.

## 5. QUARTERLY TIMING TABLE

| Period | Minimum PM actions |
|--------|--------------------|
| **Before summer (Feb-Mar)** | Full quarterly round, winding test, tank cleaning, line check, stock review |
| **Summer peak (May-Jun)** | Monthly light check: motor temperature, current draw, water level guard status |
| **Before monsoon (Jun-Jul)** | Full quarterly round, cable/panel water protection, earthing re-check |
| **Post-monsoon (Sep-Oct)** | Winding test (monsoon moisture check), strainer check, drawdown test |
| **Every quarter** | All 8 core checks in Section 2 |

## 6. FAILURE TO HOLD PM

1. If a scheduled PM is not held within the quarter, the Repair Committee
   records the reason in the maintenance log and reports to the assembly at the
   next meeting.
2. A PM skipped without a documented reason is treated as a dereliction and
   follows the Enforcement Ladder
   (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`).
3. If any check fails, the fault is immediately entered into the fault stream:
   it becomes a numbered fault under `02-Breakdown-Repair/01-Fault-Reporting.md`,
   even if the motor is still running.
