# 02 - PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE CHECKLIST

**Folder:** `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/01-Preventive-Maintenance/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE AND USE

This is the working checklist for every preventive maintenance (PM) round. It
is printed or copied into the maintenance log, completed on site, and signed.
A checklist that is not signed is treated as **not performed**.

Rules of use:

1. One checklist line per item; mark **PASS** (P), **FAIL** (F), or
   **OBSERVATION** (O) with a note.
2. Every **FAIL** or notable **OBSERVATION** becomes a numbered fault in the
   fault stream (`02-Breakdown-Repair/01-Fault-Reporting.md`) the same day.
3. Two performing persons **and** one witness sign at the bottom.
4. A copy is pinned on the notice board within 48 hours. The original goes in
   the maintenance log.

## 6. AFTER THE ROUND

1. The Repair Committee records the completed checklist in the maintenance log
   (`03-Maintenance-Log.md`).
2. All FAIL items are tracked to closure with fault numbers; closure is
   verified by a different member than the one who performed the check
   (`02-Breakdown-Repair/05-After-Repair-Verification.md`).
3. The checklist becomes public evidence. If a dispute later arises — "we
   checked the winding in April" — the signed checklist is the proof.

## 7. NON-COMPLIANCE

Signing a checklist for work that was not done, or hiding a FAIL, is a serious
offence under the Enforcement Ladder
(`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`). It endangers
every household, because a false PASS means a breakdown is hidden on purpose.
