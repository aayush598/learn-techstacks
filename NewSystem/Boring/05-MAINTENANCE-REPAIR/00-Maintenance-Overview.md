# MAINTENANCE AND REPAIR — OVERVIEW

**Folder:** `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/`

---

## 1. WHY THIS SYSTEM EXISTS

In the old system, the person who controlled repairs controlled the colony.
The "repair" excuse was the most powerful weapon in their hand:

- The motor would "not start" for weeks, and no one could challenge the excuse.
- Repairs were deliberately delayed so that a favour would be done first.
- Repair amounts were quoted at whatever the operator pleased, because nobody
  else knew the plumber or the price of a capacitor.
- Skilled members were asked to work free "for the colony" until they were
  exhausted — and then replaced by the operator's favourite.
- The colony was held hostage because only one person knew the plumber, only
  one person had the key, and only one person "understood" the motor.

This folder destroys that power. Its goal is simple and measurable:

> **Breakdowns are rare. When one happens, it is reported, priced, approved,
> fixed, and verified within published timelines — by no single person.**

## 2. THE REPAIR PIPELINE

Every repair, without exception, flows through this seven-step pipeline:

| Step | What happens | By whom | Where it is defined |
|------|--------------|---------|---------------------|
| 1. Report | Any member files a fault report; the fault gets a number | Reporting member + Repair Committee | `02-Breakdown-Repair/01-Fault-Reporting.md` |
| 2. Diagnose | Written diagnosis by 2+ competent persons | Electrician + skilled member (if available) | `02-Breakdown-Repair/02-Diagnosis-and-Quoting.md` |
| 3. Quote | Minimum 2 independent written quotes for paid work above ₹1,000 | Repair Committee | `02-Breakdown-Repair/02-Diagnosis-and-Quoting.md` |
| 4. Approve | Approval per the Repair Approval Matrix | Repair Committee / assembly per size | `02-Breakdown-Repair/03-Repair-Approval-Matrix.md` |
| 5. Fix | Repair done within the published deadline | Vendor / skilled member | `02-Breakdown-Repair/04-Repair-Timeline-and-Penalties.md` |
| 6. Verify | Independent check and test run by a non-repairer | A different member | `02-Breakdown-Repair/05-After-Repair-Verification.md` |
| 7. Pay | Payment only after verification, by voucher | Finance Committee | `06-FINANCE/03-Bookkeeping/02-Expense-Voucher-Rule.md` |

Step 6 and Step 7 are never merged. **Payment before proof is forbidden**
(P6). This single rule closes the door on inflated and phantom repairs.

## 3. WHO COORDINATES — THE REPAIR COMMITTEE

1. A **Repair Committee** of 3 members is drawn by lot each year from the
   member roll, so control never settles in one person or family (P2, P3).
2. The committee coordinates reporting, quotes, approvals, and verification.
   It does not perform repairs for money itself and does not pay itself from
   the repair fund without a separate recorded decision.
3. The committee is the sole point of contact for vendors for the year, but
   every decision it takes is recorded and public. It can never act silently.
4. Committee members serve no more than one consecutive year, and at least one
   member must be newly drawn each year so knowledge keeps spreading (P9).

## 4. PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE COMES FIRST

Breakdowns are not bad luck. Most motor failures — burned windings in summer,
dry running, starter failure, cable faults — are the predictable result of
neglect. The colony therefore:

1. Runs the quarterly preventive maintenance schedule
   (`01-Preventive-Maintenance/01-PM-Schedule.md`) without waiting for a fault.
2. Maintains a spare-parts inventory so common repairs never wait on shopping
   (`05-Parts-and-Inventory/`).
3. Trains and registers skilled members so the colony does not depend on one
   vendor or one person (`03-Skilled-Member-Program/`).

## 5. MONEY

1. Preventive maintenance and repairs are paid from the **Repair Fund**
   (`06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/03-Repair-Fund.md`).
2. The annual PM budget is prepared by the Repair Committee and approved by
   the General Assembly (`01-Preventive-Maintenance/04-PM-Budget.md`).
3. No cash changes hands for any repair. Every payment is by bank transfer or
   UPI with a voucher (`06-FINANCE/03-Bookkeeping/02-Expense-Voucher-Rule.md`).
4. Inflated prices are prevented by the multiple-quotes rule
   (`04-Vendor-Management/02-Multiple-Quotes-Rule.md`).

## 6. PRINCIPLES SERVED

| Principle | How this folder serves it |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| P1 Water is common property | The motor and its maintenance belong to no one; no one profits from its failure |
| P2 Equal power | Rotating Repair Committee; no single person controls repairs |
| P3 No single point of failure | Dual diagnosis, backup vendors, trained members, spare stock |
| P4 Contribution fair; work compensated | Skilled members are paid the published rate, never coerced |
| P5 Truth over majority | Written diagnosis, written quotes, verified outcomes beat opinions |
| P6 Radical transparency | Every fault, quote, payment, and log is public |
| P7 Protection of the weak | No member is left without water because a "repair" is being delayed |
| P8 Work rewarded | Every repair hour is credited or paid; no forced free labor |
| P9 Self-sufficiency | The colony can diagnose, fix, and verify without any one expert |
| P10 Dignity | Skilled members are compensated and respected, not guilted into work |
