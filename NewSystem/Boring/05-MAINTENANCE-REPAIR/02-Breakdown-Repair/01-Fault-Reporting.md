# 01 - FAULT REPORTING

**Folder:** `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/02-Breakdown-Repair/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

In the old system, "the motor is not working" was a statement made by one
person, heard by no one, and acted upon when it suited them. This rule makes
a fault a **public, numbered, dated event** that nobody can ignore or hide.

Every member has the right and the duty to report a fault. Reporting is not
"complaining about the operator" — it is the correct functioning of the system.

## 2. WHO MAY REPORT

1. **Any member** may report a fault — no permission, no prior approval.
2. A duty operator who notices an anomaly during a run
   (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/00-Duty-System-Overview.md`) must report it.
3. A PM round that finds a failing item automatically creates a fault
   (`01-Preventive-Maintenance/02-Checklist.md`).
4. A member who sees water wastage from a leak, noise from the motor room, or
   a burnt smell from the panel should report it immediately.

## 3. HOW TO REPORT — THREE CHANNELS

| Channel | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Fault Report Form** | Fill the standard form `12-TEMPLATES/05-Fault-Report-Form.md` |
| **Phone / WhatsApp** | Call or message the Repair Committee duty number; the committee converts it into a written form within 4 hours |
| **Register** | A paper fault register is kept on the notice board; anyone may write in it |

All three channels feed the **single fault register** maintained by the Repair
Committee. The paper register is the master; digital entries are copied into
it.

## 4. THE FAULT NUMBER

1. Every reported fault receives a **fault number**, issued in order:
   e.g. `F-2026-014`.
2. The number is given at the time of registration, and the reporter is told
   the number immediately.
3. Every subsequent step — diagnosis, quotes, approval, repair, verification —
   is filed under that number. The fault number is the tracking key from start
   to close.
4. If a fault stays open beyond its deadline, it is the number — not a person —
   that is escalated.

## 5. PHOTOGRAPHIC EVIDENCE

1. The reporting member photographs the visible evidence (leak, smoke, panel
   indicator, wet cable) on their phone and attaches it to the fault record.
2. If a photo cannot be taken, the reporter's written description is
   sufficient; the Repair Committee adds its own photo during diagnosis.
3. Photos become part of the fault record and help the diagnosis and the
   vendor quotes, so no one can claim ignorance of the problem.

## 6. IMMEDIATE NOTICE TO ALL MEMBERS

If the fault affects or may affect water supply:

1. The Repair Committee notifies **all members within 2 hours** — group
   message, notice board, and phone calls to elderly/special-need households
   (`10-RISK-AND-EMERGENCY/02-Motor-Breakdown/04-Communication-to-Members.md`).
2. The notice states: what is known, whether supply is interrupted, the fault
   number, and the next update time.
3. Members are updated at least every 12 hours until supply is restored.
4. No fault affecting supply may be kept silent "until we know more".

## 7. WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

1. The fault enters the diagnosis stage within **24 hours**
   (`02-Diagnosis-and-Quoting.md`).
2. A fault that is not acted on within its deadlines triggers the penalties in
   `04-Repair-Timeline-and-Penalties.md`.
3. When the fault is closed (verified fixed), the fault number is archived with
   its full record in the maintenance log.

## 8. PROTECTION OF THE REPORTER

1. Reporting a fault is never itself an offence. There is no penalty, fee, or
   deduction for reporting.
2. Retaliation against a reporter — verbal, financial, or through water access —
   is a serious offence under the Enforcement Ladder
   (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`) and
   violates P7 and P5.
3. A false report made knowingly and maliciously is an offence, but honesty
   errors are never punished.
