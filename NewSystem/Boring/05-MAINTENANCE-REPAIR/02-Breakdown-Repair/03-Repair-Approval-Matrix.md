# 03 - REPAIR APPROVAL MATRIX

**Folder:** `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/02-Breakdown-Repair/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

Approval exists to stop three abuses: a repair approved by one man for his own
benefit, a repair inflated to "justify" a high spend, and an emergency used to
skip all rules. The matrix ties the **size of the approval authority** to the
**size of the spend** — and requires that no decision ever rests on a single
signature (P2, P3).

## 2. THE APPROVAL MATRIX

| Repair value | Approving body | Requirement | Notes |
|--------------|----------------|-------------|-------|
| **₹0 – ₹2,000** | Repair Committee (2 of 3 members) | Both approving members sign the fault record | Only for work inside the approved PM budget lines or a reported fault; quote record kept |
| **₹2,001 – ₹10,000** | Repair Committee **+ 1 member witness** | Written diagnosis + 2 quotes + 2 committee signatures + 1 witness signature | Witness is any member who is not an interested party |
| **Above ₹10,000** | General Assembly resolution | Written diagnosis + 2 quotes + recorded resolution at a meeting with quorum | Includes motor rewinding, new pump, panel replacement |
| **Emergency (safety only)** | On-duty response team | Immediate action limited to safety + informing; full post-approval within 7 days | See Section 5 |

Thresholds are adjusted only by assembly resolution and only when published
for at least 30 days before the change takes effect.

## 3. RULES THAT APPLY AT EVERY LEVEL

1. **No single signature.** Every approval requires at least two signatories
   from different households. One person can never approve a spend alone.
2. **No interested member signs.** A member whose household, relative, or
   vendor benefits from the repair cannot approve it. They may provide
   information but not a signature
   (`04-Vendor-Management/04-No-Personal-Vendor-Kickbacks.md`).
3. **Approval follows the fault number.** There is no approval without a
   numbered fault, a written diagnosis, and (above ₹1,000) two written quotes.
4. **Approval is recorded and public.** The approval entry names the members
   who approved, the amount, and the fault number. It is published with the
   quotes.
5. **No approval, no spending.** The Finance Committee pays only on a fault
   record bearing the required signatures and a voucher
   (`06-FINANCE/03-Bookkeeping/02-Expense-Voucher-Rule.md`).

## 4. SPLITTING IS FORBIDDEN

1. A repair may not be split into smaller invoices to evade a higher approval
   level. If a single job is reasonably expected to exceed ₹2,000, it is
   approved at the ₹2,001+ level even if billed in parts.
2. Any member who discovers split billing must report it; it is a finance
   offence (P6).

## 5. EMERGENCY OVERRIDE — SAFETY ONLY

1. If a fault is an immediate danger — live cable exposed, panel smoking,
   flooding into the motor room, risk of electrocution — the on-duty response
   team may act immediately to make the site safe.
2. The emergency override permits only: isolating power, making the site safe,
   informing all members, and calling the emergency contacts. It **does not**
   authorize spending above ₹2,000 or signing a large repair contract.
3. Every emergency action is documented and **post-approved by the assembly
   within 7 days**. If the assembly rejects the action, the cost is recovered
   from those who acted without authority, not from the common fund.
4. "Emergency" is never an excuse to skip quotes for a routine motor repair,
   however hot the summer is.

## 7. DISPUTED APPROVALS

1. Any member who believes an approval was given wrongly may raise a written
   objection under the Truth Veto
   (`00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/02-Single-Member-Truth-Veto.md`).
2. The spend is frozen pending review if the objection is filed before
   payment. Payment never proceeds under a live objection.
