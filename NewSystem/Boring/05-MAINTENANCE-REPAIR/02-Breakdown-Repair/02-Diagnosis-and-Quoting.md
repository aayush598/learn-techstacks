# 02 - DIAGNOSIS AND QUOTING

**Folder:** `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/02-Breakdown-Repair/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

In the old system the plumber's word was law and the price was whatever the
middleman said. This rule replaces opinion with **written diagnosis** and
**competing written quotes**, both public. Nobody can say "I fixed it" without
saying what was wrong and what it cost.

## 2. DIAGNOSIS — BY 2+ PERSONS

1. Within **24 hours** of the fault report, the Repair Committee arranges a
   diagnosis.
2. Diagnosis is performed by **two competent persons minimum**:
   - an approved electrician or pump-shop technician, and
   - a registered skilled member (if available) from
     `03-Skilled-Member-Program/01-Skill-Registry.md`.
   Two pairs of eyes and two signatures prevent one person from naming a wrong
   or inflated fault (P3).
3. The diagnosis is **written**, in the form of a short diagnosis sheet under
   the fault number, stating:
   - observed symptom and readings (e.g., "no current on one phase",
     "winding IR 0.8 MΩ", "impeller choked with sand");
   - suspected root cause;
   - recommended repair (what exactly, with quantities of parts);
   - indicative cost range.
4. A diagnosis that cannot be made on site is written as "further testing
   needed", with the test named and the follow-up time, not as a vague
   complaint.
5. The diagnosis is published on the notice board / group within 48 hours.

## 3. WRITTEN QUOTES — THE MULTIPLE-QUOTES RULE

1. For any paid repair **above ₹1,000**, the Repair Committee obtains a
   **minimum of 2 independent written quotes**.
2. Quotes are requested from vendors on the Approved Vendor List
   (`04-Vendor-Management/01-Approved-Vendor-List.md`). If only one approved
   vendor can do the job, the committee records that fact and obtains one
   independent reference price from a second vendor for parts anyway.
3. Quotes must be **written** — on the vendor's letterhead or the standard
   quote template `12-TEMPLATES/06-Repair-Quote-Comparison.md`. A verbal
   "he said ₹8,000" is not a quote.
4. Quotes are compared in a **public comparison table** under the fault number:
   parts cost, labor cost, warranty, delivery time.
5. The comparison is published before approval, so members see exactly why a
   vendor was chosen (P6). Members may add their own quotes within the quoting
   window.

## 4. WHAT THE QUOTES MUST CONTAIN

| Field | Required? |
|-------|-----------|
| Vendor name and contact | Yes |
| Parts with brand/spec and unit price | Yes |
| Labor / service charge | Yes |
| Total in words and figures | Yes |
| Warranty offered | Yes |
| Time to complete | Yes |
| Date of quote | Yes |

## 5. THE QUOTING WINDOW

1. Quote collection is done **within 48 hours** of the written diagnosis.
2. If the work is urgent (water cut affecting households), the committee may
   fast-track to the shortest available window, but never below the rules of
   `04-Vendor-Management/02-Multiple-Quotes-Rule.md`.
3. During summer, when "motor winding burned" quotes are most prone to
   inflation, the two-quote rule is mandatory even below ₹1,000 if any member
   asks for it.

## 6. SCENARIO — THE INFLATED PLUMBER

A plumber tells the committee "the whole pump is gone, ₹25,000". The committee
notes the diagnosis was "impeller choked, strainer torn" from the site check.
The two quotes received: ₹12,500 (rebuild) and ₹9,800 (clean + replace strainer
+ refit). The comparison is published, the cheaper complete remedy is approved,
and the first plumber is rated down (`04-Vendor-Management/03-Vendor-Performance-Rating.md`).
The inflated quote dies because it could not survive public comparison.

## 7. DISPUTED DIAGNOSIS

1. If two members dispute the diagnosis, the assembly may order a **third-party
   diagnosis** from an independent pump shop, paid from the Repair Fund.
2. The third-party diagnosis is treated as evidence, not as final truth; any
   member may object under P5.
