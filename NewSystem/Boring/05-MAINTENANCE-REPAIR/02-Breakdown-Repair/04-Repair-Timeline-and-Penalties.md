# 04 - REPAIR TIMELINE AND PENALTIES

**Folder:** `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/02-Breakdown-Repair/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

The old system's core abuse was **delay**: a repair "in progress" for weeks,
with no date, no deadline, and no consequence. This rule gives every fault a
clock. Deadlines are published; lateness is visible; silence is itself an
offence.

## 2. THE DEADLINES

| Stage | Deadline | Clock starts when |
|-------|----------|-------------------|
| Fault registered | Immediately (within 4 hours of report) | Fault reported |
| Members informed (if supply affected) | Within 2 hours | Fault registered |
| Diagnosis done | Within 24 hours | Fault registered |
| Quotes obtained (if paid repair) | Within 48 hours of diagnosis | Diagnosis written |
| Approval given | Within 24 hours of quotes | Quotes ready |
| **Critical repair done** | **Within 72 hours of approval** | Approval given |
| Non-critical repair done | Within 7 days of approval | Approval given |
| Verification done | Within 24 hours of repair claimed done | Vendor reports completion |
| Payment made | Within 7 days of verification | Verification passed |

For a water-cutting fault in summer, the committee may shorten these clocks by
public notice, never lengthen them without assembly approval.

## 3. WHAT COUNTS AS "CRITICAL"

| Classification | Example | Deadline |
|----------------|---------|----------|
| **Critical** | Motor down (no supply), main line burst, contamination, live-wire hazard | Diagnose 24h, repair 72h |
| **Non-critical** | Leak on a branch line, low pressure on one slot, panel light fault | Diagnose 24h, repair 7 days |
| **Scheduled** | Work already in the approved PM budget | Per PM schedule |

The Repair Committee classifies a fault at registration. The classification is
written on the fault record and can be appealed by any member at the next
assembly.

## 4. DEADLINE TRACKING

1. Every fault record carries its deadlines in writing — not in someone's
   memory.
2. The fault register shows each open fault as **IN TIME / LATE**. The late
   list is published weekly on the notice board.
3. When a deadline is missed, the fault status automatically becomes LATE; no
   committee resolution is needed to make lateness real.

## 5. PENALTIES FOR DELAY

Delay is penalized to make it unprofitable. Lateness is examined in three
steps:

| Step | What happens |
|------|--------------|
| **1. Recorded finding** | The committee writes in the fault record why the deadline was missed and who was responsible (vendor, committee, member). The finding is published. |
| **2. Escalation to assembly** | If a critical fault is not repaired within 72 hours, the fault is reported to the assembly at the next meeting (or by emergency notice if sooner). The assembly examines the cause. |
| **3. Damage compensation** | If members suffered loss because of unjustified delay — e.g., water bought from tankers during a delay that had no documented reason — the cost may be recovered from the responsible party as per the Enforcement Ladder (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`). |

For a **vendor**: repeated lateness moves them down the Vendor Performance
Rating (`04-Vendor-Management/03-Vendor-Performance-Rating.md`) and can end in
removal from the Approved Vendor List.

For a **committee member**: an unjustified delay is a dereliction of duty and
follows the enforcement ladder, including forfeiture of any compensation for
that quarter.

## 6. THE COMMITTEE CANNOT SIT SILENTLY

1. A fault is **never** considered closed by silence. Only a verified repair
   closes a fault (`05-After-Repair-Verification.md`).
2. If the committee takes no action on a fault for more than 24 hours, any
   member may raise it directly at the assembly or with the Conflict Review
   Board.
3. Hiding a fault from members — "nothing is wrong, don't worry" — while a
   fault record exists is a transparency offence (P6) and is penalized at
   Level 2-3 of the enforcement ladder.

## 7. VALID REASONS FOR EXTENSION

A deadline may be extended only with a **documented, published reason**:

| Valid reason | Example |
|--------------|---------|
| Part not locally available | Motor bearing of a rare size, ordered and expected in 4 days |
| Vendor emergency | Approved electrician hospitalized; alternate vendor line confirmed |
| Safety hold | Waiting for a rain to pass before working on the open panel |
| Members' own choice | Assembly agrees the repair can wait until after a festival |

The extension, its reason, and the new deadline are recorded on the fault
record the same day. An excuse without a record is treated as delay.

## 8. SCENARIO — THE WEEK-LONG "REPAIR"

In the old system a member could say "motor will be ready next week" for six
weeks. Under this rule: the fault `F-2026-007` is classified critical on day 0.
By day 3 it is LATE and published. On day 4 the assembly examines it. The
vendor's excuse is unrecorded and contradicted by the quote's promised 48-hour
delivery. The finding is recorded against the vendor, the colony's rented pump
cost is recovered from the vendor's withheld payment, and the vendor is rated
down one full level.
