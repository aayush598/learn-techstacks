# 03 - BULK PURCHASE CONTINGENCY (WATER TANKERS)

**Folder:** `10-RISK-AND-EMERGENCY/01-Drought-and-Scarcity/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

When the bore alone cannot meet need — during rationing, or while the motor is
down — the colony can buy water. In the old system, buying water was an
opportunity for one person to pick the tanker, pocket a commission, and hand
out water as favours. This rule makes tanker water a **budgeted, approved,
fairly distributed common purchase** (P1, P6).

## 2. PRE-APPROVED SUPPLIERS

1. The colony maintains **1-2 approved tanker suppliers** on the Approved
   Vendor List (`05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/04-Vendor-Management/01-Approved-Vendor-List.md`),
   with their rates per 1,000 litres recorded.
2. Supplier rates are reviewed yearly with the vendor list. The recorded
   "normal rate" is the reference price; anything above it during a crisis is
   challenged in writing.
3. Supplier contact is public on the emergency contacts list
   (`05-Communication-Emergency/02-Emergency-Contacts.md`).

## 3. WHO MAY ORDER A TANKER

| Situation | Who orders | Limit |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Shortfall during rationing, or motor down for ≤ 2 days | Repair Committee (2 members) | Up to ₹5,000 per order, against the approved emergency allocation |
| Larger or repeated supply (motor down > 2 days, extended drought) | Assembly resolution | Amount set in the resolution |
| Emergency (motor down, heatwave, members at risk) | On-duty response team | Up to ₹2,000, strictly bounded, ratified within 7 days (`05-Communication-Emergency/03-Decision-During-Communications-Breakdown.md`) |

No single member ever orders a tanker for the colony.

## 4. ORDERING AND PAYMENT

1. The order specifies: litres, supplier, rate, delivery time, and the fault or
   crisis reference number.
2. **Two committee members** receive the tanker; they check the quantity
   (tanker capacity, dip stick / meter), note the receipt, and sign.
3. Payment is by voucher from the Repair Fund or the approved crisis line —
   never cash to the driver (`06-FINANCE/03-Bookkeeping/02-Expense-Voucher-Rule.md`).
4. The order and receipt are published with the crisis updates.

## 5. FAIR DISTRIBUTION

1. Tanker water enters the system through the **public distribution points**
   under the same rationing rules as bore water (`01-Rationing-Plan.md`).
2. No household may be given a private tanker delivery while others are on
   rations, except by assembly resolution (e.g., an event exception — and
   event water is suspended during rationing anyway).
3. Distribution follows the priority order (`02-Priority-Allocation.md`).
4. A member found diverting tanker water for private resale or private storage
   commits a Level 3-4 offence: water is common property (P1).

## 6. SCENARIO — THE COMMISSION TANKER

A member suggests "my cousin has a tanker, quick and cheap". Under this rule
the offer is not refused — it is processed: the cousin is measured against the
approved supplier rates, the relationship is disclosed
(`05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/04-Vendor-Management/04-No-Personal-Vendor-Kickbacks.md`),
the member takes no part in the order, two committee members receive the
delivery, and payment goes by voucher at the recorded rate. If the cousin is
genuinely cheaper, he becomes an approved supplier; if the "cheap" price hides
a commission, the ledger catches it.

## 7. RECORDING

Every tanker purchase is entered in the crisis log with: date, supplier,
litres, rate, total, reference number, receivers' signatures. The crisis log is
public and is reviewed at the post-crisis assembly
(`00-Risk-Framework.md`).
