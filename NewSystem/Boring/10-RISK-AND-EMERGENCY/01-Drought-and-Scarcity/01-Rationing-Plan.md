# 01 - RATIONING PLAN

**Folder:** `10-RISK-AND-EMERGENCY/01-Drought-and-Scarcity/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

Scarcity is when fairness is tested hardest. The rationing plan is written and
approved **in normal times**, so that when yield falls, the colony does not
discover "who gets what" by argument. The plan is mechanical: it triggers on a
measured number, it is published, and no single person can change it
mid-crisis (P1, P2).

## 2. THE TRIGGER

Rationing is **declared by the assembly** when the measured facts show
scarcity:

| Trigger | Measured how |
|---------|--------------|
| **Yield below the scarcity threshold** | The drawdown test in the PM schedule shows discharge below the threshold set by the assembly (e.g., 70% of the recorded baseline) |
| **Sustained dry run** | Tank level falls below the reserve mark for 2 consecutive supply windows |
| **Crisis forecast** | A published municipal/water-board notice of regional shortage, or a formal assessment by the Repair Committee |

The threshold numbers are set by the assembly **every year before summer** and
published. Once set, they are not adjusted in mid-crisis by any single person.

## 3. DECLARATION

1. The Repair Committee proposes the declaration with the measured evidence
   (yield numbers, tank levels).
2. The assembly declares rationing by simple majority at a meeting with
   quorum. In a true emergency (motor down plus heatwave), the on-duty
   response team may declare an **interim 72-hour rationing** and the assembly
   ratifies or adjusts it within 72 hours.
3. The declaration is **published** with: the trigger numbers, the start date,
   the per-household limits, the rotation, and the expected duration.
4. The declaration is dated and given a reference number. It is a public
   document, kept with the maintenance log.

## 4. WHAT RATIONING MEANS

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Per-household limit** | A fixed litres-per-day or fixed minutes-per-slot limit, set by the assembly, same for every household |
| **Groups by lot** | Households are grouped by lot (not by friendship) for slots; the groups rotate so no group permanently gets the good hours |
| **Rotation** | Slot order rotates weekly (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/` rotation logic) |
| **Duration limit** | The plan states a maximum duration (e.g., 60 days) after which it must be re-declared with fresh evidence |
| **Event water** | Event water (`04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/03-Event-Water/`) is suspended during rationing unless the assembly grants an exception |

## 5. ENFORCEMENT

1. Exceeding one's limit (overdrawing, storing beyond need, taking water
   outside one's slot) is a recorded offence under the Enforcement Ladder
   (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`).
2. Hoarding — filling extra containers when the household's own storage is
   already full — is specifically named and penalized, because hoarding is how
   scarcity turns into a black market inside the colony.
3. Reported violations are verified by two members before any penalty is
   applied (P5). No punishment on a single word.

## 6. THE PLAN CANNOT BE CHANGED MID-CRISIS BY ONE PERSON

1. The rationing parameters can be changed **only by assembly resolution**
   with the same formality as the declaration.
2. The Repair Committee and the on-duty team **administer** the plan; they do
   not amend it. A committee member who silently adjusts a household's share
   is committing a Level 3-4 offence.
3. If a member believes a parameter is unjust, the remedy is a Truth Veto or a
   grievance to the Conflict Review Board — not unilateral action
   (`00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/02-Single-Member-Truth-Veto.md`).

## 7. SCENARIO — THE MID-SUMMER SHORTFALL

In April the drawdown test shows yield at 55% of baseline. The published
threshold is 70%. The committee proposes rationing; the assembly declares it:
40 litres per household per day, groups by lot, weekly rotation, 60-day
limit. A member who "knows the operator" asks for an extra hour quietly. The
request is refused — the plan, not the operator, sets the share. The same
member is later seen filling extra barrels; the violation is witnessed,
verified, recorded, and penalized. Rationing holds because it was decided
before it was needed.
