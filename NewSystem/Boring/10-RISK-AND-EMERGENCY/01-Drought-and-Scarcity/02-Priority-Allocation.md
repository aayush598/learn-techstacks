# 02 - PRIORITY ALLOCATION

**Folder:** `10-RISK-AND-EMERGENCY/01-Drought-and-Scarcity/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

When water is short, the question "who gets what is left" is where a colony
either protects its weakest or reveals its cruelty. Priority allocation makes
the answer **published, objective, and verifiable** — decided by documented
need, never by friendship, wealth, or the favour-graph (P7).

## 2. THE PUBLISHED PRIORITY ORDER

The order below is fixed by this rule. It is published on the notice board
when rationing is declared, and it cannot be re-ordered by any individual:

| Priority | Category | Basis |
|----------|----------|-------|
| **1** | **Medical need** | Documented need: doctor's prescription/note requiring assured water (dialysis, medication hydration, chronic illness) |
| **2** | **Elderly / disabled** | Household with a resident aged 75+ or a person with a registered disability |
| **3** | **Young children** | Household with children under 5 years |
| **4** | **General** | All other households |

## 3. HOW PRIORITY IS APPLIED

1. Priority affects the **order and security of supply**, not the volume:
   - a Priority-1 household is never left dry during a supply window;
   - if supply must be cut, the cut list is drawn from the bottom of the order
     upward, so general households are affected before medical households.
2. Priority households are served **first in the rotation order** when slots
   are scarce, and their slot time is recorded so it cannot be silently
   reduced.
3. The base per-household volume limit (`01-Rationing-Plan.md`) is the same
   for all; priority is about reliability and order, not extra litres. Any
   extra allocation for documented medical need requires an assembly
   resolution with the evidence attached.

## 4. OBJECTIVE CRITERIA — NOT FRIENDSHIP

1. A household is in a priority class only if it can **produce the documented
   basis**: a doctor's note, a disability certificate, a birth record, an age
   record.
2. Claims are verified by the **hardship panel** (Section 5). A claim without
   documentation is treated as general until documentation is provided.
3. No member may claim priority on behalf of another household without that
   household's consent.
4. The priority list is published (with the documented basis stated in
   general terms, e.g., "medical note on file") so the assignment is visibly
   fair. Personal details stay with the panel.

## 5. THE HARDSHIP PANEL

1. When rationing is declared, a **hardship panel of 3 members** is drawn by
   lot from the member roll — with the requirement that no two panelists are
   from the same household and no panelist is in a priority class themselves.
2. The panel verifies all priority claims within 72 hours of the declaration.
3. The panel re-verifies claims **once a month** during rationing (a
   prescription may expire; a child may turn 5; a household's situation may
   change).
4. A panelist with a personal interest in a claim recuses; the panel acts by
   majority and records its decisions.
5. The panel cannot change the priority order itself — it only applies it to
   facts.

## 6. PROTECTION FROM TARGETING

1. A priority household may never be punished with a worse slot or less water
   "for being a nuisance".
2. Retaliation against a priority household is a Level 3-4 offence under the
   Enforcement Ladder (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`).
3. A household that no longer qualifies is moved down at the monthly
   re-verification, with written notice and the reason, so there are no
   surprises and no grudges.

## 7. SCENARIO — THE WELL-CONNECTED HEAVY USER

A member with connections asks for "a bit more, I have guests". Under this
rule the request is tested against the order: the member is Priority 4
(general). The household is served after Priority 1-3 households are secured.
The member's friend cannot grant extra water because nobody has the power to
grant it. Meanwhile a small household with a dialysis patient presents a
doctor's note, the panel verifies it in 2 days, and the household's slot is
recorded as never-dry. The colony's scarce water went where the published
order says it goes.
