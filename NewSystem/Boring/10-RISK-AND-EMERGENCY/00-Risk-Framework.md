# RISK AND EMERGENCY — FRAMEWORK

**Folder:** `10-RISK-AND-EMERGENCY/`

---

## 1. WHY THIS FOLDER EXISTS

Crises are when the old system did its worst damage. When water ran short, the
people in control decided who suffered: friends got water, rivals got "no
supply today". When the motor failed, the same people decided when — or
whether — it got fixed. Panic was their opportunity; confusion was their
shield.

This folder removes the opportunity. Its principle:

> **Every foreseeable crisis has a written plan, agreed in advance, and
> triggered mechanically. No one profits from panic, because there is no panic
> to exploit.**

## 3. HOW A RISK BECOMES AN EMERGENCY

| Level | Definition | Example | Response source |
|-------|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| **Watch** | Warning signs observed, supply normal | Yield down 10%, power cuts announced | PM schedule, monthly review |
| **Alert** | Supply reduced or interrupted, risk confirmed | Motor down, contamination suspected | Specific plan activated (this folder) |
| **Emergency** | Sustained failure beyond the colony's normal response | Motor down + no tanker + heatwave | All plans + assembly emergency session |

The Repair Committee raises the level publicly in writing. Downgrading the
level is an assembly decision, never a private one.

## 4. THE STANDING RULES OF EVERY CRISIS

1. **Plans before panic.** No ad-hoc invention mid-crisis. The written plan
   for the situation is read, followed, and only then improved.
2. **Public information.** Every affected member is informed immediately and
   updated on a fixed schedule (`02-Motor-Breakdown/04-Communication-to-Members.md`).
   Silence is not a plan.
3. **The weak first.** Allocation and help follow the published priority order
   (`01-Drought-and-Scarcity/02-Priority-Allocation.md`), not friendship.
4. **Money is still controlled.** Even in a crisis, spending follows the
   approval limits; the emergency override covers safety and informing only
   (`05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/02-Breakdown-Repair/03-Repair-Approval-Matrix.md`,
   `05-Communication-Emergency/03-Decision-During-Communications-Breakdown.md`).
5. **Everything is recorded.** Every crisis action is logged, vouchered, and
   ratified. What is not recorded did not happen.

These are maintained **in normal times** so the emergency is only the
activation of a prepared plan.

## 6. REVIEW AND DRILLS

1. The risk register is reviewed **annually** at the assembly; likelihoods and
   plans are updated with what the year taught.
2. At least **one drill per year**: the colony simulates a motor breakdown and
   practices notification, response-team activation, and the rental-pump call.
   The drill is recorded; failures found in the drill are fixed.
3. After any real emergency, a **post-crisis review** is held within 30 days:
   what worked, what failed, what the plan should say next time.

## 7. PRINCIPLES SERVED

| Principle | How this folder serves it |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| P1 Water is common property | Crisis allocation is public and equal, never at someone's discretion |
| P2 Equal power | No single person declares, ends, or exploits an emergency |
| P3 No single point of failure | Backup supply, backup vendors, backup communication |
| P5 Truth over majority | Plans are based on measured facts (yield, test results), not claims |
| P6 Radical transparency | Every crisis action and cost is public and ratified |
| P7 Protection of the weak | Priority allocation protects the vulnerable first |
| P9 Self-sufficiency | The colony has its own response; it does not beg as a first move |
