# COLONY WATER SUPPLY SYSTEM — COMPLETE BLIND-TRUST GOVERNANCE RESOURCE

**Root:** `NewSystem/Boring/`  
**Type:** Constitution-style governing system for a colony borewell (boring) water supply
**Philosophy:** One boring motor serves every household. **No single person or group controls
the water, the money, the schedule, or the repairs. Everyone contributes. The right always
wins — even against a majority.**

---

1. Read `00-CONSTITUTION/00-MASTER-CONSTITUTION.md` — the supreme law of this system.

## 4. THE NON-NEGOTIABLE BASIC STRUCTURE

These six clauses can **never** be amended, by any vote, ever:
1. Water is common property — no one owns or controls it.
2. One member, one vote — equal power.
3. A member's proven right prevails over any majority.
4. Every water receiver must contribute.
5. Every member's right to inspect all records is absolute.
6. No member loses water except through the full due-process Disconnection Protocol.
