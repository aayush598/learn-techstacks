## 1. THE CENTRAL COMMITMENT

The old system failed because conflicts were decided by **power**: whoever
controlled the motor could target individuals, punish them, deny them water,
spread false stories, and no one could stop them.

This system replaces power with **process**. Every conflict in this colony is
decided by **evidence and rules**, never by numbers, anger, money, or muscle.
This folder is the operating manual for that promise.

## 2. CONFLICTS ARE NORMAL — NOT A CRISIS

Conflicts between members over water, money, duty, and repair are **expected**
in any group of humans. A conflict is not a scandal and not a sign of failure.
The system is judged by one question only: *was the conflict resolved without
anyone being targeted, silenced, or coerced?*

- A conflict that is spoken aloud early is cheap to solve.
- A conflict that is hidden or crushed silently becomes sabotage later.
- Therefore no member is punished merely for raising a dispute in good faith.

## 3. THE FOUR GUARANTEES OF THIS MODEL

| Guarantee | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **No targeting** | No individual or small group can be singled out for disadvantage by any process. |
| **No domination** | No coalition can steer a dispute to protect its own and punish others. |
| **Right beats numbers** | A large group that is WRONG loses even against one right member. P5. |
| **Restoration after wrong** | Every wrong resolution is reversed, compensated, and rebalanced. |

## 4. THE 5-STEP ESCALATION LADDER

Conflicts are resolved at the lowest workable step first. Skipping steps is not
a shortcut; it is a procedural violation. The two fastest paths are **Step 1
(dialogue)** and **Step 2 (written grievance)** — most conflicts should end in
one of these two.

| Step | Name | Who handles it | Time target | Cost |
|------|------|----------------|-------------|------|
| 1 | Open Dialogue | The two parties + a neutral facilitator | Same day to 3 days | Nearly zero |
| 2 | Written Grievance | Conflict Review Board (CRB) intake | 1 day to register | Low |
| 3 | CRB Hearing | Lot-drawn CRB panel | Decision within 7 days of hearing | Moderate |
| 4 | Lot-Drawn Jury | Fresh 5-person jury drawn by lot | Rehearing, final internal decision | High |
| 5 | Final Appeal | Full General Assembly, then external forum | Per `11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/04-Dispute-Adjudication/` | Highest |

Each step is described in full in `02-Resolution-Process/`.

## 5. WHAT IS DECIDED AT EACH STEP

- **Step 1–2:** Settlement, facts, and remedies both sides agree to.
- **Step 3:** Binding reasoned decision on facts, rule application, and remedy.
- **Step 4:** A fresh, independent rehearing of the same record plus new
  evidence; the final internal decision.
- **Step 5:** Interpretation of the rules themselves (not re-trying facts),
  then external arbitration or court if still unresolved.

## 6. EVIDENCE, NOT HEADS

Every step above answers questions in this order:

1. **What are the facts?** Established by logs, receipts, photos, timestamps,
   witnesses, and meters — never by a show of hands
   (`04-Right-vs-Majority-Procedures/01-Fact-Establishment.md`).
2. **What does the rule say?** The written rule is applied as written.
3. **Is a fundamental right engaged?** If yes, the right stands. No vote can
   override it.

Only genuine choices between valid options — schedule, budget, vendor —
are ever put to a vote.

## 7. THE ONE-MEMBER GUARANTEE

The signature rule of this system: **a single member with a provable fact or a
cited rule wins against any majority.** The mechanism is the Truth Veto
(`00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/02-Single-Member-Truth-Veto.md`), applied
practically in `04-Right-vs-Majority-Procedures/02-Truth-Veto.md`.

*Example:* nine members vote to cut one member's water because "she is always
arguing". She produces the log showing her dues are paid and the rule
prohibiting water denial as punishment. The vote is void. The nine members'
mistake costs nothing; the member's right is restored immediately.

## 8. STANDING RULES FOR EVERY DISPUTE

1. **Status quo until heard.** No step may worsen the situation while a dispute
   is pending.
2. **Presence rule.** No member's water, money, or duty is decided without the
   member being notified and heard.
3. **Written reasons.** Every adverse decision is written, with reasons, and
   appealable.
4. **No retaliation.** Filing a grievance or testifying is protected. Retaliation
   doubles the original penalty (`03-Protection-Mechanisms/04-No-Retaliation-Rule.md`).
5. **Whistleblowers protected.** Reporting wrongdoing through any channel is
   always lawful (`03-Protection-Mechanisms/03-Whistleblower-Protection.md`).
