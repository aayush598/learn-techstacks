# NO-RETALIATION RULE

**Folder:** `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/03-Protection-Mechanisms/`  
**Cross-reference:** `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/02-Resolution-Process/02-Step-2-Written-Grievance.md`, `00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/05-Right-to-Voice-and-Objection.md`

---

## 1. WHAT THIS RULE DOES

The no-retaliation rule protects **everyone who participates honestly in the
conflict system**: the member who files a grievance, the member who objects at a
meeting, the member who testifies as a witness, the member who votes against a
wrong resolution, the member who serves on a panel.

Without this rule, a dispute would not be resolved by evidence — it would be
resolved by whoever is brave enough to speak and then survives the backlash.
The old system silenced exactly this way.

## 2. WHO IS PROTECTED

| Role | Example |
|------|---------|
| Complainant | A member who files a written grievance |
| Objector | A member who raises a Truth Veto or opposes a wrong resolution |
| Witness | A member who testifies, even against the majority's story |
| Panel member | A CRB or jury member who decides against a powerful party |
| Whistleblower | A member reporting wrongdoing through any channel |
| Voter | A member who votes against a coalition in the assembly |

## 3. WHAT CONSTITUTES RETALIATION

| Act | Example |
|-----|---------|
| Water retaliation | Slot moved, run shortened, repair delayed for the complaining house |
| Financial retaliation | Charges raised, refund delayed, fine imposed only on the objector |
| Duty retaliation | Duty assigned unfairly, or the objector excluded from earning credits |
| Social retaliation | Boycott, refusing to speak, spreading rumours about the objector |
| Procedural retaliation | Blocking the objector's legitimate requests without written reasons |
| Intimidation | Threats, warnings to "back off", pressure on family members |

## 4. THE DOUBLE-PENALTY RULE

**Retaliation doubles the penalty of the original wrong.**

- If the original offence would attract Level 2 (fine + extra duty), retaliation
  attracts Level 4.
- If the original wrong was targeting (Level 3 minimum), retaliation attracts
  Level 5 territory and immediate legal referral where a crime is involved.

The doubling applies to the **retaliator**, not to the victim. The victim
receives restoration under `04-Right-vs-Majority-Procedures/05-Restoration-and-Rebalance.md`.

## 5. RETALIATION IS ITSELF A FRESH OFFENCE

Retaliation is not "part of the original case". It is a **new, independent
offence**:

1. It is investigated separately, with its own case number.
2. It is recorded separately in the grievance register.
3. It may be prosecuted even if the original grievance was withdrawn — the
   victim cannot be pressured into withdrawing protection by withdrawing the
   case.
4. Retaliation against a whistleblower specifically triggers
   `03-Whistleblower-Protection.md`.

## 6. THE RETALIATION PRESUMPTION

Where a member files a grievance or testifies, and **immediately after** suffers
an adverse action (slot moved, charges raised, repair refused), the adverse
action is **presumed retaliation** unless the actor proves a legitimate,
pre-existing reason with evidence (a written rule, a prior decision, a log).

*Example:* A member complains that the operator's log is faked. Three days
later her repair request is "not possible". The operator must show a written
rule or a prior record explaining the refusal. Silence means the refusal is
retaliation.

## 7. TIMELINE AND PROCESS

| Step | Time |
|------|------|
| Allegation of retaliation | Treated as urgent — status quo order within 24 hours |
| Investigation | Within 7 days |
| Finding | Written, reasoned |
| Remedy | Restore the victim's position + double penalty on the retaliator |
| Appeal | Normal steps 4–5 of the ladder |

## 8. PRESSURE TO WITHDRAW

Withdrawing a grievance is the victim's choice. Pressure to withdraw — threats,
offers, family pressure, group appeals — is itself retaliation. A withdrawal
obtained under pressure is void, and the case continues. The CRB records any
withdrawal in a short, private check: "Is this withdrawal your own decision?"

## 9. THE PRINCIPLE

No member should ever have to weigh the truth against their water, their money,
or their standing in the colony. This rule makes that weighing unnecessary.
