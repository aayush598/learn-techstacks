# INDIVIDUAL ECONOMIC PROTECTION

**Folder:** `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/03-Protection-Mechanisms/`  
**Cross-reference:** `06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/`, `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/04-Disconnection-Protocol.md`

---

## 1. WHAT THIS MECHANISM DOES

No member should be **financially ruined** by the water system, and no member
should ever lose water merely because of a money dispute. The system collects
dues and applies fines — but it never destroys a household to do it. This
mechanism is P7 (Protection of the weak) applied to money.

## 2. THE FOUR PILLARS

| Pillar | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Fair dues only** | A member owes exactly what the published contribution rules say — nothing more. No individual tariffs, no "special levies" invented at a meeting. |
| **No confiscation of water for money disputes** | Basic water is never cut for a pure money dispute until the full Enforcement Ladder — including Level 5's requirements — has been completed. |
| **Hardship protection** | A member in genuine hardship gets instalments, deferral, or credit, never harassment. |
| **Protection from unfair fines** | A fine must be proportional, recorded, and appealable. No fine may be used as leverage. |

## 3. FAIR DUES — THE RULE AGAINST SINGLING OUT

| Protected | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| No individual tariff | A group of 10 members demands higher charges from one house "because he argues a lot". This is targeting, not finance. The demand is void and the instigators face the ladder. |
| No retroactive surprise | A member cannot be charged for a rule adopted after the expense. |
| No interest on disputed amounts | While a member disputes a charge in good faith, the disputed amount is held without interest or penalty. |
| No charges for exercising rights | A member who requests an audit, a log copy, or a hearing is never billed for it. |

## 4. DISPUTED DUES — THE PROCESS

1. The member disputes the charge in writing (Step 2 grievance).
2. The disputed amount is **held**, not collected, while the dispute is pending.
3. The CRB examines the register and supporting receipt under the evidence rule
   (`01-Conflict-Types/04-Financial-Conflicts.md`).
4. If the charge is valid, the member is given a payment plan if needed.
5. If the charge is wrong, the register is corrected and the member's record is
   cleared.

## 5. HARDSHIP — NOBODY IS SQUEEZED INTO RUIN

| Situation | Protection |
|-----------|------------|
| Sudden illness, job loss, crop failure | Instalment plan of up to 12 months on application, kept confidential |
| Old-age or disability | Deferral or contribution-in-kind (skill, time) under `06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/` |
| Widow / single earner | The hardship review is fast-tracked; no public shaming |
| Temporary hardship | Credit for up to 3 months before any recovery step begins |

Hardship applications are decided by the treasurer and CRB on **documented
facts**, never by popularity. The record is confidential; the colony publishes
aggregates only.

## 6. FINES — BOUNDED AND APPEALABLE

| Rule | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| Proportional | Fine cannot exceed the published ceiling for the offence in `05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`. |
| Recorded | Every fine is entered in the register with the offence, the rule, and the case number. |
| Appealable | Any fine is appealable through the normal steps. |
| Not a revenue tool | Fines are never used to balance the budget; dues fund the system, fines only deter. |
| No compound punishment | A single offence is fined once — no repeated, escalating fines for the same act. |

## 7. WHAT ECONOMIC PUNISHMENT CANNOT DO

- **Cannot cut basic water** for a money dispute unless Level 5's full protocol
  has run (`05-Enforcement/04-Disconnection-Protocol.md`).
- **Cannot confiscate** the member's share, refunds, or earned credits beyond
  the amount actually owed.
- **Cannot expose** a member's hardship to the colony.
- **Cannot starve** a household into paying — the member always keeps access to
  minimum water and dignity.

## 8. THE PRINCIPLE

The system wants **paid, not punished** members. A member who owes is brought
back to contributing through plans and process — never crushed so they leave
the colony, and never shown off as a warning to others. P4 (fair contribution)
and P7 (protection of the weak) hold together.
