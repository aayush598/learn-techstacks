# WHISTLEBLOWER PROTECTION

**Folder:** `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/03-Protection-Mechanisms/`  
**Cross-reference:** `02-GOVERNANCE/04-Transparency/04-Whistleblower-Channel.md`, `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/05-Legal-Recourse.md`

---

## 1. WHAT THIS MECHANISM DOES

A whistleblower is a member who reports **wrongdoing** in the system — money
missing, water stolen, a log falsified, a member being targeted, a vendor
bribe. Without protection, no one will report; and without reporting, the old
system's secrets survive forever. This mechanism makes reporting **safe and
honourable**.

## 2. THE REPORTING CHANNELS

| Channel | How | Identity |
|---------|-----|----------|
| **Open** | Grievance form, or speaking at a meeting | Known |
| **Anonymous** | Drop box, anonymous form, phone message, through a trusted third member | Unknown |
| **Confidential** | Reported to the CRB intake officer, who keeps it sealed | Known only to the officer and panel |

A member may choose any channel. Choosing the anonymous channel is never
treated as suspicion of bad faith — fear of retaliation is a rational, protected
reason.

## 3. IDENTITY SECRECY

- The whistleblower's identity is kept secret from everyone except the minimum
  persons needed to act (intake officer and panel).
- Records that would reveal the identity are stored sealed and opened only with
  the whistleblower's consent or by an order of the Lot-Drawn Jury.
- The panel may take protective steps: hear the whistleblower separately, by
  phone, at a different time from other witnesses, or in writing only.
- **Leaking** a whistleblower's identity is an offence at Level 4.

## 4. NO RETALIATION — AND NO BOYCOTT

A whistleblower is protected from every form of retaliation:

| Form of retaliation | Prohibited |
|---------------------|------------|
| Water, slot, or charge changes | Prohibited |
| Duty over-assignment | Prohibited |
| Social boycott, refusing to speak or help | Prohibited — this was a classic weapon of the old system |
| Rumours, name-calling, mockery | Prohibited (P10) |
| Blocking repairs or services | Prohibited |

Any retaliation against a whistleblower **doubles the penalty of the original
wrong** and is itself a fresh offence (`04-No-Retaliation-Rule.md`).

## 5. CONSEQUENCES FOR EXPOSING OR PUNISHING A WHISTLEBLOWER

| Act | Consequence |
|-----|-------------|
| Exposing a whistleblower's identity | Level 4 |
| Retaliating against a whistleblower | Double penalty of the original wrong, Level 3 minimum |
| Organising a boycott of a whistleblower | Level 4 + Cease and Desist |
| Blocking the whistleblower's water or services | Immediate restoration + Level 4 |
| Physical threats against a whistleblower | Level 4/5 + legal referral without delay |

## 6. GOOD-FAITH REPORTS VS MALICIOUS FALSE REPORTS

The system must protect honest reporters without becoming a weapon for liars.
The distinction:

| Good-faith report | Malicious false report |
|-------------------|------------------------|
| Reporter believed the facts, even if partly wrong | Reporter knew the facts were false |
| Reported what was seen, with any evidence available | Invented or fabricated the accusation |
| Aim: to stop wrongdoing | Aim: to damage, isolate, or extort the accused |
| Outcome: protected, no penalty for honest error | Outcome: treated as defamation and targeting; Level 3+, legal recourse available to the victim |

A good-faith report that turns out to be mistaken costs the reporter nothing
and is recorded as honest error. A **pattern** of careless false reporting is
noted and, if it amounts to harassment, is treated as an offence itself.

## 7. PROTECTION FROM THE OLD SYSTEM'S WEAPONS

The old system punished truth-tellers with rumours, denial of water, and social
isolation. This mechanism reverses each of those weapons:

- **Rumours against the reporter** → defamation case, Level 3+, Cease and
  Desist.
- **Water denial as punishment for reporting** → immediate restoration within
  24 hours and the targeting ladder.
- **Social boycott** → prohibited conduct; the boycotters face individual
  liability under `02-Protection-from-Coalitions.md`.

## 8. THE ANONYMOUS CHANNEL IN PRACTICE

1. The report arrives without a name, or with a code name.
2. The intake officer logs it with a case number and a secret token the
   reporter keeps for follow-up.
3. The panel investigates as it would any report.
4. An anonymous report that alone accuses someone carries less weight than a
   report with evidence — anonymity protects the reporter, it does not exempt
   the claim from proof.

## 9. THE PRINCIPLE

Reporting a wrong is never a wrong. This colony's safety depends on members
who speak, and this mechanism is the guarantee that speaking costs them
nothing.
