# PROTECTION FROM COALITIONS

**Folder:** `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/03-Protection-Mechanisms/`  
**Cross-reference:** `00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/04-Right-to-Protection-from-Targeting.md`, `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/01-Conflict-Types/02-Group-vs-Individual.md`

---

## 1. WHAT THIS MECHANISM DOES

A coalition is a group of members acting **together** to harm a member — to
pressure them, isolate them, outvote them, or punish them. A coalition is more
dangerous than one wrongdoer, because:

- Numbers make the group feel legitimate.
- Each member hides behind "everyone was doing it".
- The victim faces many accusers and no single responsible person.

This mechanism ensures a coalition has **no hiding place** and **no strength in
numbers**.

## 2. INDIVIDUAL LIABILITY — NO HIDING IN THE CROWD

If a group is proven to be acting together to target a member:

1. **Each participant is separately liable** for the full offence.
2. Liability is **not divided or diluted** by the number of participants.
3. **"Everyone did it" is not a defence** — it is an aggravating fact: it
   converts the act into organised targeting.
4. Each participant receives the individual penalty, and the **organiser(s)**
   receive the top penalty applicable (Level 4+).

*Example:* Six members, acting in a coordinated group, tell House 9's widow that
she must pay double the common charge or her water will be "unavoidably"
cut. When proven, all six face the ladder individually, and the two who planned
it face Level 4. The widow's charge is corrected and her water protected.

## 3. PROVING COLLUSION

Collusion is proven by evidence, not by suspicion:

| Evidence of coordination | Example |
|--------------------------|---------|
| Written messages | A group chat where the plan is discussed |
| Timing | Several complaints against one member filed the same day in identical words |
| Pattern | Repeated identical claims, one after another, that collapse under the same evidence |
| Witness accounts | Members who describe being asked to "join in" or to sign a common letter |
| Same-error evidence | The same false fact repeated by several people, traceable to one source |

## 4. PROCEDURAL PROTECTIONS FOR THE VICTIM

| Protection | Rule |
|------------|------|
| **Never face the whole group at once** | The victim gives their account once, and may be heard separately from the group. Group members testify individually, one at a time. |
| **No public cross-fire** | The victim is not interrogated by a row of accusers. Questions come through the panel chair. |
| **Complaint consolidation** | Group complaints become one case, so the victim is not worn down by twelve separate hearings. |
| **Separate rooms if needed** | The CRB may hold hearings in separate sessions to keep the victim safe. |
| **Legal support** | Where the coalition's acts crossed into crime (threats, assault, tampering), the victim gets the system's documented support (`05-Enforcement/05-Legal-Recourse.md`). |

## 5. THE "POPULARITY VOTE" IS BANNED IN COALITION CASES

No coalition case is decided by asking the assembly or any body to vote on
"who they believe". The decision rests on evidence, under
`04-Right-vs-Majority-Procedures/01-Fact-Establishment.md`. A popularity poll
is not evidence and is itself a targeting technique.

## 6. THE COALITION THAT IS RIGHT

Nothing in this mechanism protects a wrong individual. If a group genuinely
complains against a member and the evidence supports the group, the member is
held accountable normally. The protections here regulate **how** the group
proceeds — evidence, individual hearings, no intimidation — not the outcome.

## 7. CONSEQUENCES FOR COALITION CONDUCT

| Conduct | Consequence |
|---------|-------------|
| Coordinated targeting proven | Level 3 minimum per participant; Level 4 for organisers |
| Intimidating witnesses or the victim | Level 4 + Cease and Desist |
| Lying in coordination to destroy a member | Defamation + Level 4 + legal referral at the victim's option |
| Coalition formed to defend a wrongdoer and silence the victim | Treated as obstruction and retaliation; the underlying wrong is re-examined |

## 8. THE PRINCIPLE

Numbers must never convert a wrong into a right. This colony remembers what
happens when a crowd decides — and has built these rules so that one honest
member never has to be braver than the whole colony at once.
