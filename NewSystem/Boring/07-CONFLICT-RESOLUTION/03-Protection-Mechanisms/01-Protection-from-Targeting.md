## 1. WHAT THIS MECHANISM DOES

This is the operational manual for Fundamental Right 4. Where the constitution
declares the right, this file says exactly **how the colony prevents, stops,
and remedies targeting** — the single worst failure mode of the old system.

Targeting means any act by an individual, a dominant person, or a group that
imposes a **disadvantage on a member** — water denial, extra charges, extra
duty, isolation, false accusations — as pressure or punishment.

## 2. OPERATIONAL RULES AGAINST TARGETING

| Rule | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **No collective punishment** | A wrong by one member is answered against that member alone. Never against their household, family, relatives, or friends. |
| **Written reasons** | Any decision that reduces a member's water, funds, credits, or rights must be in writing, with reasons, and appealable. "We decided at the meeting" is not a reason. |
| **Presence rule** | No adverse decision about a member is valid unless that member was notified and heard first. A decision made in their absence is void. |
| **Immediate restoration** | If targeting has cut a member's water, water is restored within **24 hours** pending investigation. Investigation can never suspend water. |
| **No retaliation** | Any adverse act linked to a complaint, objection, or testimony is retaliation and doubles the original penalty. |
| **Status quo** | Once targeting is alleged, the CRB issues a status-quo order within 24 hours: nothing may worsen the member's situation. |

## 3. THE TARGETING ALARM

Any member who believes they are being targeted may **press the alarm** — in
person, by phone, or through the anonymous channel. The alarm has three effects,
automatically:

1. **Status quo order** within 24 hours (`02-Resolution-Process/02-Step-2-Written-Grievance.md`).
2. **Water restoration** within 24 hours if water was the tool of targeting.
3. **Fast-track hearing** within 7 days, ahead of the ordinary queue.

## 4. THE RECOGNITION LIST

Targeting often arrives dressed as a routine decision. The list below is
non-exhaustive and each item is presumed targeting until shown otherwise:

| Disguise | Reality |
|----------|---------|
| "House 7 is now on the 2 a.m. slot." | Slot moved only for one member, without roster change. |
| "We need a special levy for your house." | Individual tariff, no rule permits it. |
| "Your refund is delayed, wait your turn." | Only this member's refund is delayed. |
| "Everyone agrees you are the problem." | A complaint with no evidence, only numbers. |
| "We are not assigning you duty anymore." | Exclusion presented as relief. |
| "Your name was not in the notice." | Information withheld so a member misses meetings or slots. |

## 5. CONSEQUENCES FOR PROVEN TARGETING

Targeting is treated at **Level 3 or above** on the Enforcement Ladder
(`05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`):

- Level 3 minimum for a single act of targeting (e.g., moving one member's slot).
- Level 4 for coordinated targeting by a coalition, or targeting that caused
  water denial.
- Referral to legal recourse if the targeting involved theft, assault, or
  tampering.

Every participant in coordinated targeting is separately liable. "Everyone was
doing it" is not a defence.

## 6. THE VICTIM IS NEVER THE PROBLEM

After targeting is proven, the victim is never asked to "move on quietly", "not
make a fuss", or "forgive and forget" as a condition of restoration. Restoration
is automatic (`04-Right-vs-Majority-Procedures/05-Restoration-and-Rebalance.md`).
Forgiveness is private and optional; the system's correction is not.

## 7. IF THE TARGETING CONTINUES

Escalating pressure after a finding is a fresh offence:

1. CRB issues a **Cease and Desist** order immediately
   (`05-Enforcement/02-Cease-and-Desist.md`).
2. Violation of the order accelerates penalties one level per violation.
3. Repeated violation triggers the Lot-Drawn Jury review and, if needed,
   removal from roles and legal referral.

No member of this colony should ever fear that standing alone against a crowd
will cost them water. That fear is what this mechanism exists to eliminate.
