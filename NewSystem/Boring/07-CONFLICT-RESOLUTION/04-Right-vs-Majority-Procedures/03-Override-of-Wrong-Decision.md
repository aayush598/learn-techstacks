## 1. WHAT THIS FILE DOES

Sometimes a wrong decision is not stopped in time — it is passed, published, or
even acted upon before someone proves it wrong. This file defines how an
already-passed wrong decision is **nullified and reversed**, and what survives
of it.

## 2. THE GENERAL RULE: VOID AB INITIO

A decision that was wrong on **facts, rules, or fundamental rights** is **void
ab initio** — null from the start, as if it never existed. It cannot be
"ratified later", "made legal by time", or "accepted as a done thing".

Wrong means any of:

1. Based on a false fact (the fact was disproven by evidence).
2. Violating a written rule (the rule was misapplied or ignored).
3. Violating a fundamental right (P1–P10 engaged — e.g., targeting, water
   denial as punishment).
4. Made in violation of process (presence rule violated, member not heard).

## 3. WHO DECLARES A DECISION VOID

| Forum | Power |
|-------|-------|
| Any member present | May raise the invalidity at any meeting; the chair must record it |
| CRB | Declares voidness after review (facts/rules/rights) |
| Lot-Drawn Jury | Final internal authority on voidness of a CRB or jury decision |
| General Assembly | On rule-interpretation grounds only (Step 5) |
| External forum | Per `11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/04-Dispute-Adjudication/` |

## 4. PARTIAL VOIDNESS

A decision is severed where possible:

- **Whole decision void** — the wrong applies to the entire act.
- **One clause void** — the illegal clause is struck; the remaining lawful
  clauses stand if they can operate without the void part.
- **One member's part void** — a penalty imposed on the wrong member is struck;
  penalties on members validly found guilty stand.

*Example:* A resolution fines five members for "wasting water", but only two of
them are actually proven to have wasted it. The fine on the other three is void
ab initio; the two proven members' fines stand.

## 5. NO STATUTE OF LIMITATIONS FOR RIGHTS VIOLATIONS

A rights violation can be raised **at any time**:

- A member who was wrongly cut off water ten years ago may still have the
  decision declared void and the records corrected.
- The remedy for past harm follows the restoration rules — practical restoration
  (records, refunds within the member's actual loss) is given; "forgiveness"
  is never required as a condition.
- This does not open every old dispute: **financial** claims follow the ordinary
  records and evidence rules, and a member cannot revive a case purely to
  harass.

## 6. THE CONSEQUENCES OF VOIDNESS

1. **Nothing may be built on the void decision.** Later decisions that relied on
   it are re-examined.
2. **Actions taken under it are reversed** per
   `05-Restoration-and-Rebalance.md` — water restored, money refunded, records
   corrected, reputation repaired.
3. **Those who acted on the void decision in bad faith** face the ladder
   (`04-Cost-of-Defying-Truth.md`); those who acted in honest reliance are not
   penalised.
4. **The decision is struck from the record** with a dated annotation:
   "VOID AB INITIO — case reference [number]".

## 7. THE PROCESS TO OVERRIDE

1. A member raises the invalidity in writing to the CRB (or at a meeting).
2. The CRB reviews the decision against facts, rules, and rights.
3. The CRB issues a written override order: what is void, what stands, what is
   reversed.
4. Restoration begins within 24 hours of the override order.
5. The override itself is recorded in the register and notice board, so no one
   accidentally relies on the dead decision again.

## 8. THE PRACTICAL CASE

*Example:* Six months ago the assembly "voted" to fine House 12 ₹5,000 for
"wasting water during a shortage". House 12 paid under protest. Now, during an
audit, the log shows House 12's usage was normal — the "vote" was based on a
rumour. House 12 raises the matter. The CRB declares the fine void ab initio,
orders the ₹5,000 refunded within 7 days, and corrects House 12's record. The
rumour's source is investigated as defamation.

## 9. THE PRINCIPLE

A wrong decision that becomes history is still a wrong. In this colony, truth
is not limited by how long it takes to surface — P5.
