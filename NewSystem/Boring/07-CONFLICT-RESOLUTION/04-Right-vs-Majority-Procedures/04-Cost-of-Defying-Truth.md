# COST OF DEFYING TRUTH

**Folder:** `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/04-Right-vs-Majority-Procedures/`  
**Cross-reference:** `00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/01-Principle-of-Truth-over-Majority.md`, `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`

## 1. WHAT THIS FILE DOES

A Truth Veto is frozen, and a review begins. Then a group **defies the truth**:
they ignore the freeze, proceed with the wrong decision, vote it through again,
or act on it before the review finishes. This file defines the consequences of
deliberately proceeding against truth.

The purpose is not revenge on the majority. It is to make **truth cheaper to
obey than to defy**, so that no one ever finds defiance worthwhile.

## 2. WHAT "DEFYING TRUTH" MEANS

| Act | Example |
|-----|---------|
| Ignoring a freeze | Proceeding to cut a member's slot while a Truth Veto is pending |
| Re-voting the same wrong | Re-passing the same decision the day after a veto, hoping for a bigger majority |
| Acting before review | Paying out, cutting water, or changing charges before the review completes |
| Suppressing evidence | Hiding or altering the log, receipt, or photo that would prove the truth |
| Intimidating the truth-teller | Pressuring the member who filed the veto to withdraw |
| Proceeding on a declared-void decision | Continuing to enforce a decision the CRB has voided |

## 3. THE CONSEQUENCES

| Consequence | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **The decision is void** | The defiant act is void ab initio, even if it was voted again or performed in public. `03-Override-of-Wrong-Decision.md`. |
| **Individual liability** | Each person who knew the freeze/veto existed and proceeded anyway is individually liable. "The assembly told me to" is not a defence for the treasurer who paid, the operator who cut, or the chair who put it to vote again. |
| **Compensation to the victim** | The victim's full actual loss is compensated: water make-up runs, refunds, repair costs, and documented losses. |
| **Level 4 for deliberate defiance** | A member found to have deliberately defied a known veto or void finding faces Level 4: restitution, removal from all roles, exclusion from voting on finance for a fixed period. |
| **Organisers face the top** | Those who organised the defiance (called the second vote, spread the false story, pressured others) face the maximum the ladder allows at their level. |

## 4. HOW LIABILITY IS ASSIGNED

1. The CRB first determines **who knew**. Knowledge is proved by presence at the
   meeting, receipt of the notice, or membership of the organising group.
2. Those who knew and proceeded are liable; those who were genuinely unaware
   are not penalised (but the void result still stands for everyone).
3. Those who **objected in the room** are recorded as objecting and are
   protected — the lone dissenter is never swept into the group's liability.
4. The victim's compensation is paid from the fund first, then recovered from
   the liable members, so the victim is never made to wait on the offenders.

## 5. THE FIVE CHECKS BEFORE ANY DEFIANCE CONSEQUENCE

A member is only penalised for defiance after all five are shown:

1. A veto or void finding existed (documented).
2. The defiant member knew of it (proved).
3. They had a practical chance to stop or refuse (proved).
4. They proceeded anyway (proved).
5. The defiance caused or risked harm (proved).

## 6. THE RECURRENCE RULE

If the same group defies truth a second time — same pattern, same members — the
penalties escalate directly to the top of the applicable level, and the matter
is referred to `05-Legal-Recourse.md` for obstruction and, where relevant,
criminal mischief.

## 7. THE COST IS ON THE OFFENDERS, NOT THE COLONY

The colony fund advances compensation to the victim immediately. Recovery from
the liable members follows. The colony therefore never makes the victim pay for
the majority's mistake, and never lets the offenders escape behind the crowd.

## 8. THE PRACTICAL CASE

*Example:* A Truth Veto freezes a proposal to cut House 2's water. That night,
the operator — pressed by the group — closes House 2's valve "just to show
them". The next morning the CRB hears House 2's report, confirms the veto, and
voids the act. House 2's water is restored that day and compensation credits
are granted. The operator is found to have known of the freeze: Level 4, removal
from the operator roster for a fixed period, restitution recorded. The members
who pressed the operator are each liable as organisers.

## 9. THE PRINCIPLE

Truth is not fragile — it can be ignored, but it cannot be outvoted. The cost
of defying it is designed to be high enough that even a hostile crowd pauses
before it acts. P5.
