# FACT ESTABLISHMENT

**Cross-reference:** `00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/04-No-Voting-on-Proven-Facts.md`, `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/01-Conflict-Types/05-Operational-Conflicts.md`

## 1. WHAT THIS FILE DOES

Before any dispute is decided, the facts must be established. This file defines
**how** the colony finds the truth — and why the truth can never be decided by
a vote, a crowd, or a stronger voice.

## 2. THE CARDINAL RULE

**Facts are decided by evidence. Never by vote. Never by who speaks loudest.
Never by who is more liked.**

"Was the motor running at 6:30?" is answered by the log, the gauge, and the
witnesses — not by a ballot. A vote cannot change what happened.

## 3. WHAT COUNTS AS EVIDENCE

| Evidence type | What it proves | Strength |
|---------------|----------------|----------|
| Motor run log / duty log | Whether the motor ran, when, for how long | High — contemporaneous |
| Receipts, vouchers, bank statements | Whether money moved, when, how much | High |
| Photographs with timestamps | Physical state at a time (pipes, meters, panels) | High |
| Meter / gauge readings | Water drawn, line pressure | High |
| Time-stamped messages (WhatsApp, SMS) | Communications, promises, threats | High |
| Witness accounts | What people saw and heard | Medium — stronger when corroborated |
| Official documents | Registrations, panchayat notices, vendor bills | High |
| General belief ("everyone knows") | Nothing, unless traced to a source | Negligible alone |

## 4. BURDEN OF PROOF

- The party **asserting** a fact carries the burden of supporting it.
- A complainant is not required to prove their case twice; once with credible
  evidence is enough.
- A member is **never** required to prove their innocence merely because a
  group accuses them.
- Silence or unpopularity is never evidence of wrongdoing.

## 5. THE STANDARD — PREPONDERANCE OF CREDIBLE EVIDENCE

A fact is treated as established when the **weight of credible evidence**
supports it more than the opposite. This is deliberately a lower bar than
"beyond reasonable doubt" (which belongs to criminal law) and higher than
suspicion. Concretely:

- Log plus receipt beats one person's memory.
- A dated photograph beats an undated assertion.
- Two independent witnesses beat "someone told me".
- A pattern of consistent records beats a single angry account.

## 6. HOW EVIDENCE IS GATHERED

1. **Preservation first.** The panel orders that logs, meters, pipes, and
   records be preserved the moment a dispute is filed — nothing gets "cleaned
   up" before inspection.
2. **Collection.** The panel (or intake officer) copies the relevant log
   pages, photographs, receipts, and messages.
3. **Each side's evidence is admitted.** Both sides submit everything they
   hold; hiding evidence is treated as obstruction.
4. **Cross-check.** Where possible, evidence is cross-checked against an
   independent source: the gauge vs. the log, the bank statement vs. the
   register, the vendor's bill vs. the panchayat rate.

## 7. THE WEIGHT OF THE LOG

Logs and registers are the system's institutional memory. Because they are
checked and countersigned (`01-Conflict-Types/05-Operational-Conflicts.md`),
they carry the weight of official record. A log entry stands unless a member
provides **contrary evidence** — a witness, a photo, a correction record. The
absence of a log entry is itself a recorded fact.

## 8. WITNESSES

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| Witnesses are heard separately | So no witness is coached or intimidated by hearing others. |
| The witness states what they saw, not what they conclude | "I saw him at the panel at 6:40" is evidence; "He must have turned it on" is opinion. |
| Family members of either side are heard but weighed critically | Natural bias is recorded and discounted, not ignored. |
| A witness who changes their story without explanation | The change is recorded; the panel weighs the earliest version. |

## 9. WHEN FACTS ARE CLEAR BUT RULES ARE DISPUTED

If the facts are agreed but the **rule** is in question, the matter is one of
interpretation and may follow `05-Step-5-Final-Appeal.md` to the assembly. The
facts remain where they were established.

## 10. FACTS VS. CHOICES — THE ONE VOTE THAT IS ALLOWED

Only **genuine choices** between valid options are voted on: which vendor, which
schedule hour, which budget line. The moment a "choice" contradicts a proven
fact or a written rule, it stops being a choice and becomes a proposed
violation — and it is stopped, not voted on.
