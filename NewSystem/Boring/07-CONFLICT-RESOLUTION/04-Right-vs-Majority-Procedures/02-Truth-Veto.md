## 1. WHAT THIS FILE DOES

The constitution defines the Truth Veto in principle
(`00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/02-Single-Member-Truth-Veto.md`). This
file is the **operational manual**: who can use it, how it works in a live
dispute, what gets frozen, and what happens when it is abused.

## 2. RECAP — THE VETO IN ONE PARAGRAPH

Any **single member** may formally object that a proposed decision or action is
(1) factually false, (2) rule-violating, or (3) right-violating. On filing, the
decision or action is **frozen** — numbers do not matter during the freeze.
A review on evidence decides whether the veto stands.

## 3. THE THREE MOVES: FREEZE, REVIEW, DECIDE

| Move | What happens | Who | Time |
|------|--------------|-----|------|
| **Freeze** | The proposed decision/action is suspended. No implementation, no partial implementation, no "just for now". | Any single member files | Immediate |
| **Review** | Evidence is examined: Is the fact true? Is the rule correctly cited? Is a right engaged? | CRB or 3-person lot-drawn panel | Max 7 days (14 if outside documents needed) |
| **Decide** | Veto upheld → decision is void. Veto rejected → decision may proceed, objector's dissent recorded without penalty. | The review panel | Written, reasoned |

## 4. WHO MAY USE IT

| Member | Can veto |
|--------|----------|
| Any current member | Yes, on facts, rules, or rights |
| A member with a pending retaliation order against them | No, for decisions about their own case — but they may file a normal grievance |
| A non-member / outsider | No |

The veto is deliberately cheap to raise and expensive to crush: the burden is
only to **show evidence or cite the rule**. The lone member is not required to
prove a masterpiece — a receipt, a log page, a cited clause is enough to start
the review.

## 5. HOW TO FILE IT PRACTICALLY

1. At any meeting: say "I raise a Truth Veto" and state the ground (fact/rule/
   right) plus the supporting evidence or citation. The minute-taker records it
   on the spot, dated.
2. Outside a meeting: file in writing to the CRB within the same day the wrong
   action is proposed.
3. The veto must be specific: which decision, what is wrong with it, what
   evidence or rule supports it. A general "I don't agree" is not a veto.

## 6. WHAT THE FREEZE COVERS

- The specific decision objected to — not the whole world.
- Everything needed to implement it: signatures, payments, orders, changes.
- **Status quo means exactly that.** If the water slot was Sunday 6 a.m., it
  stays Sunday 6 a.m. until review. No "temporary" change while we discuss.

## 7. UPHOLD — THE RESULT

If the review upholds the veto:

1. The decision is **void ab initio** — as if it never happened
   (`03-Override-of-Wrong-Decision.md`).
2. Anything already implemented is reversed and restored
   (`05-Restoration-and-Rebalance.md`).
3. The objecting member is recorded as correct — no penalty, no "troublemaker"
   label.

## 8. REJECT — THE RESULT

If the review rejects the veto:

1. The decision may proceed, but only after the full review is complete.
2. The objector's dissent stays recorded — honest objection is never punished.
3. A member whose vetoes are repeatedly rejected receives a warning, then Level
   1 obstruction consequences, per the constitution's abuse rules.

## 9. ABUSE AND MISUSE — THE GUARDRAILS

| Abuse | Handling |
|-------|----------|
| Repeated baseless vetoes | Warning, then Level 1 obstruction; veto restricted to written form with prior warning |
| Veto used to delay repairs harming many members | CRB fast-tracks review; if baseless, veto set aside, objector warned |
| Veto used to shield own wrongdoing | Treated as obstruction; the underlying wrongdoing proceeds |
| Veto used as revenge against a member | The abuser faces targeting consequences; the victim is protected |

The veto protects **truth**, not stubbornness, and not personal preference.
Preference disputes go to normal voting.

## 10. THE PRACTICAL CASE

*Example:* At a heated meeting, seven members propose cutting House 5's water
for "three days, to teach a lesson". House 5's owner raises a Truth Veto: the
rule in `05-Enforcement/04-Disconnection-Protocol.md` requires a CRB finding,
a 3/4 assembly vote, 15 days' notice, and never allows "teaching a lesson".
The vote is frozen on the spot. The CRB reviews within 7 days, upholds the
veto, and the resolution is declared void. House 5's water was never cut —
because one member had a rule and used it.
