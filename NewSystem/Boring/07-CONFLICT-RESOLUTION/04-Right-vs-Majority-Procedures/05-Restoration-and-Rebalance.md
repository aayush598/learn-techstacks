# RESTORATION AND REBALANCE

**Folder:** `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/04-Right-vs-Majority-Procedures/`  
**Cross-reference:** `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/04-Right-vs-Majority-Procedures/03-Override-of-Wrong-Decision.md`, `00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/04-Right-to-Protection-from-Targeting.md`

## 1. WHAT THIS FILE DOES

Every wrong ends with **restoration**. A wrong is not finished when it is
proven; it is finished when the victim's water, money, records, and dignity are
put back — and when the system's balance is restored so no grudge leaks into
future decisions.

## 2. THE FOUR THINGS TO RESTORE

| Thing | What it means | How |
|-------|---------------|-----|
| **Water** | The victim's water rights, slot, pressure, and priority | Restored within 24 hours of the finding; make-up runs for every missed slot; damage repaired |
| **Money** | Refunds, cleared fines, returned deposits, compensation | Paid within 7 days of the finding; interest where the system held the money |
| **Records** | The official record must reflect the truth | Register corrected, void annotations added, case record updated with the finding |
| **Dignity** | Reputation and standing in the colony | Public correction equal in reach to the original wrong; rumours answered by the record |

## 3. THE CORRECTION FORMULA — APOLOGY PLUS CORRECTION

A proper restoration has two parts, and **both are mandatory**:

1. **Correction of the harm** — water, money, records restored. This is
   mechanical and never discretionary.
2. **Public correction** — where the wrong was visible (rumours, meeting
   decisions, notices), the correction is equally visible: the same notice
   board, the same meeting agenda, a statement in the minutes that the victim
   was right and the finding has been reversed.

An apology is encouraged but is **voluntary and genuine** or it is nothing.
A forced apology does not replace the correction; a correction is never
conditional on an apology.

## 4. UNDO WHAT WAS DONE

| Wrong action | Reversal |
|--------------|----------|
| Water slot moved | Slot restored; make-up for lost runs |
| Water cut | Restored within 24 hours; extended if the cut caused documented loss |
| Fine imposed wrongly | Refunded with interest, recorded as void |
| Charges raised wrongly | Original charge restored; over-collection refunded |
| Repair refused or delayed | Repair scheduled immediately; vendor penalty where delay was deliberate |
| Membership record soiled | Record corrected and the case annotation added |

## 5. COMPENSATE THE LOSSES

Where a wrong caused a real loss — missed wedding water, lost business water,
a paid private repair that should have been common, fees for a plumber the
system's delay forced — the victim claims documented loss and the CRB orders
compensation from the offenders' liability or the fund
(`04-Cost-of-Defying-Truth.md`). Compensation is bounded by the member's
actual, documented loss — it is not a lottery prize and not a punishment to the
offender (punishment is separate, in the Enforcement Ladder).

## 6. REBALANCE DUTY AND CREDITS

If the wrong involved duty or credits:

- A member who was wrongly over-assigned duty receives credit adjustments.
- A member who was wrongly excluded from earning credits is put back on the
  roster and credited for what they lost.
- The roster is re-drawn (or corrected) so the wrong does not freeze the 
  member's position for a cycle.

## 7. NO PERMANENT GRUDGE IN RULE APPLICATION

The colony's hardest discipline: a past wrong must not colour future decisions
about the same member.

| Rule | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Clean-slate application** | Every rule is applied to the member's present conduct, never to their past dispute. |
| **No "settling old scores" via process** | Using a later case to punish a member for winning an earlier one is retaliation — Level 3+. |
| **No permanent labels** | "Troublemaker", "liar", "complainant" are not statuses. The register records findings, not reputations. |
| **Panel neutrality** | A panel member who cannot decide neutrally after a past case must recuse. |

## 8. THE RESTORATION ORDER

Every finding that includes restoration must issue a **written restoration
order** stating:

1. The wrong (case number, decision voided).
2. Each restoration step with a deadline (water: 24h; money: 7 days; records:
   7 days; repair: schedule in 3 days).
3. Who is responsible for executing each step.
4. The right of the victim to escalate if a step is missed.

The CRB tracks restoration like any order — a missed restoration deadline is
itself a matter for the Enforcement Ladder.

## 9. THE PRACTICAL CASE

*Example:* False rumours about Member K (that she steals water) were spread at
a meeting and on a notice. Her repair was delayed "until this is sorted". The
CRB finds the rumours false and the delay retaliatory. The restoration order:
(1) her repair is done within 3 days; (2) a notice board correction states the
rumour was false and she was right; (3) the minutes record the finding; (4) the
member who spread the rumour pays the fine and issues nothing forced — the
correction speaks instead; (5) her credit ledger is corrected for the lost
earnings. Life returns to normal, and the next dispute about her is decided on
its evidence, not on this history.

## 10. THE PRINCIPLE

Restoration is not charity to the victim — it is the system repairing itself.
A colony that restores after every wrong becomes a place where members can
afford to be wronged and to be right.
