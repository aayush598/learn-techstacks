# 04 - FINANCIAL CONFLICTS

**Folder:** `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/01-Conflict-Types/`  
**Cross-reference:** `06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/`, `06-FINANCE/03-Bookkeeping/`, `00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/04-No-Voting-on-Proven-Facts.md`

---

## 1. WHAT THEY ARE

Disputes over money: unpaid dues, disputed charges, refunds, fines, repair
bills, the cost of a burst pipe, the value of a time-credit, an expense the
treasurer says was approved but no one remembers voting on.

## 2. THE EVIDENCE RULE

Money disputes are decided by **documents**, never by memory, emotion, or
popularity. The paper trail is the only truth:

| Evidence type | Proves |
|---------------|--------|
| Receipts (counterfoil / UPI / bank) | That a member paid, and when |
| Bank statements | That money moved in and out of the system account |
| Vouchers and bills | That an expense was real and was paid |
| Approved minutes | That an expense was authorised by the assembly |
| Contribution register | The running balance of each member's account |

*Example:* Two members fight over a burst pipe repair of ₹8,000 that was paid
out of the common fund. One says it was a private line and should be repaid to
the fund; the other says it is a common line. The CRB does not ask "who is more
trusted". It asks: Is the line in the asset register as common? Who authorised
the repair in the minutes? Where was the work done relative to the meter? The
documents decide.

## 3. NO VOTING ON FINANCIAL FACTS

"Did House 4 pay its March dues?" is a question of fact. It is answered by the
contribution register and bank statement — **not by a show of hands**. A vote
cannot make a paid bill unpaid, and a vote cannot make an unpaid bill paid.

## 4. THE LADDER OF FINANCIAL DISPUTES

1. **Account check.** The member raises the issue; the treasurer produces the
   register entry and supporting receipt within 3 days
   (`06-FINANCE/03-Bookkeeping/`).
2. **Written correction.** If the entry is wrong, it is corrected with a
   dated, signed note and both sides keep a copy.
3. **If the member disagrees with the entry** → Step 1 dialogue, then Step 2
   written grievance to the CRB.
4. **CRB financial hearing** under `02-Resolution-Process/03-Step-3-Conflict-Review-Board.md`,
   applying the evidence rule above.
5. **Recovery.** If a member genuinely owes, the recovery follows the
   enforcement ladder — it never begins with cutting water
   (`03-Protection-Mechanisms/05-Individual-Economic-Protection.md`).

## 5. THE THREE PROTECTED MEMBERS

| Situation | Protection |
|-----------|------------|
| Member disputing a charge in good faith | The disputed amount is held, not collected, until decided. No interest or fine accrues while the dispute is pending. |
| Member under hardship | Instalments and deferral under `06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/`; the system never squeezes a member into ruin. |
| Member owed a refund | Refund is paid within the timeline of `06-FINANCE/05-Distribution-and-Refunds/`; delay is a financial grievance itself. |

## 6. MISAPPROPRIATION

The most serious financial conflict: money that should be in the system account
is missing, or was spent without authorisation.

| Sign | Response |
|------|----------|
| Bank balance does not match the register | Immediate account freeze and full audit within 7 days |
| Spending without minutes | Recorded as unauthorised; the spender must justify or restore |
| Suspicious bulk withdrawal / missing vouchers | Referred to the audit process and, if needed, legal recourse under `05-Enforcement/05-Legal-Recourse.md` |
| Evidence destroyed | Presumption against the custodian; the matter escalates |

Misappropriation is **not** a private matter and is **not** settled by asking
the accused to "just return it quietly". It is a system offence with a written
record, because P6 (radical transparency) requires the whole colony to see how
money is handled.

## 7. WHAT FINANCIAL PUNISHMENT CAN AND CANNOT DO

A financial wrong can lead to fines and recovery of dues through the
enforcement ladder. It can **never** justify cutting a member's basic water for
a pure money dispute unless the full ladder of
`05-Enforcement/04-Disconnection-Protocol.md` has been completed. The protocol
is about proven non-payment after due process — never about "teaching someone a
lesson".
