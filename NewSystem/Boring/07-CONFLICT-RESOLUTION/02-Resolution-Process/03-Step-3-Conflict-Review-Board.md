# STEP 3 — CONFLICT REVIEW BOARD HEARING

**Folder:** `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/02-Resolution-Process/`  
**Cross-reference:** `02-GOVERNANCE/02-Committees/05-Conflict-Review-Board.md`, `13-HANDBOOKS/05-Conflict-Reviewer-Handbook.md`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

The Conflict Review Board (CRB) hearing is the **first adjudicative step**: a
neutral panel hears both sides, examines evidence, and issues a **binding,
reasoned written decision**. Unlike dialogue and grievance intake, this step
decides the dispute.

## 2. THE CRB PANEL

- The CRB is composed of members drawn by lot, not appointed, so no one can
  stack the panel (`02-GOVERNANCE/02-Committees/05-Conflict-Review-Board.md`).
- A panel for a hearing is typically 3 members, with reserves.
- **Recusal:** a member related to, friends with, or in a dispute with either
  party steps aside and is replaced by a reserve. Recusal is mandatory, not
  optional; a panel member who refuses to recuse when a conflict exists is
  removed and reported.

## 3. NOTICE AND PREPARATION

1. Both sides receive the case file: the grievance, the response, and any
   evidence already on record.
2. Each side may bring evidence and witnesses. Both sides are told in writing
   what the panel will ask about.
3. A member who cannot attend may submit written testimony; a member in
   hardship or with a disability is accommodated (evening hearing, home visit,
   phone, or a trusted person to read).

## 4. THE HEARING CONDUCT

| Stage | Rule |
|-------|------|
| Opening | The panel states the issue, the burden of proof, and the ground rules. |
| Complainant presents | Facts, evidence, witnesses — uninterrupted. |
| Respondent presents | Same right, same time. No side goes second forever. |
| Questions | Panel asks both sides; each side may put questions to the other through the panel chair. |
| No cross-fire | Shouting, interruptions, and personal attacks end the hearing. The panel chair warns once, then removes the offender from the room and hears the rest in their absence. |
| Witnesses | Heard separately, one at a time, so no witness is intimidated. |
| Closing | Each side states in two minutes what they want decided and why. |

## 5. EVIDENCE AND BURDEN

- Facts are decided by **preponderance of credible evidence**: whichever side's
  account is more supported by logs, receipts, photos, timestamps, witnesses,
  and meters wins (`04-Right-vs-Majority-Procedures/01-Fact-Establishment.md`).
- The party asserting a fact carries the burden of supporting it.
- There is **no voting on facts** — the panel decides as a body of fact-finders
  and must reconcile its reasoning.
- Hearsay alone ("someone told me", "everyone knows") is noted but carries
  little weight. Corroborated hearsay from a credible chain is weighed.

## 6. THE DECISION — WITHIN 7 DAYS

The panel issues a written decision within **7 days** of the hearing. It must
contain:

1. The issue stated.
2. The facts as found, and the evidence each finding rests on.
3. The rules applied, cited precisely.
4. The remedy ordered (restore water, refund, correction, apology, fine, duty,
   cease-and-desist, referral to the Enforcement Ladder).
5. The right of appeal (Step 4) and its 10-day deadline.

The decision is filed in the case record, published in the grievance register,
and given to both parties.

## 7. BINDING UNLESS APPEALED

- The decision is **binding on both parties** from the day it is issued.
- It may be appealed to the **Lot-Drawn Jury** within **10 days**
  (`04-Step-4-Lot-Drawn-Jury.md`).
- If not appealed in time, it becomes final and is enforced through
  `05-Enforcement/`.
- Pending an appeal, a decision that orders *ongoing* harm to stop is enforced
  immediately (Cease and Desist); a decision that imposes a penalty is stayed
  until the appeal ends.

## 8. PRESSURE ON THE PANEL

A panel that is pressured, threatened, or lobbied must record the pressure in
the decision file. Attempting to influence a panel member is an offence at
Level 3+. Panels sit in confidence, deliberate alone, and publish reasons —
pressure rarely survives a published, reasoned record
(`13-HANDBOOKS/05-Conflict-Reviewer-Handbook.md`, section 8).

## 9. IF THE CRB IS CONFLICTED

When the dispute is about the CRB itself, or a panel member, or involves most
committee members, the hearing is conducted by a **lot-drawn 3-member ad hoc
panel** drawn under `02-GOVERNANCE/05-Rotation-and-Fairness/02-Lot-Draw-System.md`.
The ordinary steps below do not change.
