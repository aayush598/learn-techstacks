# STEP 5 — FINAL APPEAL

**Folder:** `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/02-Resolution-Process/`  
**Cross-reference:** `11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/04-Dispute-Adjudication/`, `02-GOVERNANCE/01-General-Assembly/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

Step 5 is the **last internal appeal**. A party may appeal a Lot-Drawn Jury
decision to the **full General Assembly** — but only on one ground: **that the
rules were misinterpreted or misapplied**. Facts, which have been established at
two hearings, are not re-tried at this level.

After Step 5, the dispute is finished inside the system. If a party still
disagrees, the only remaining forum is external: arbitration or court, under
`11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/04-Dispute-Adjudication/`.

## 2. WHAT CAN BE APPEALED (AND WHAT CANNOT)

| Can be appealed | Cannot be appealed |
|-----------------|--------------------|
| "The jury applied the wrong tariff rule." | "The jury believed the other side's witness." |
| "The penalty exceeds what the Enforcement Ladder allows." | "I want a different penalty." |
| "The decision contradicts the Master Constitution." | "I want a re-run of the whole case." |
| "A rule used here has never been adopted." | "Most people agree with me." |

The appeal is refused summarily if it raises only facts or preferences. The
refusal is in writing, with reasons.

## 3. THE PROCEDURE

1. The party files the appeal in writing within **10 days** of the jury
   decision, stating the exact rule alleged to be misapplied and the correct
   interpretation.
2. The assembly is convened under the ordinary meeting rules
   (`02-GOVERNANCE/01-General-Assembly/`), with notice to both parties.
3. The parties address the assembly, limited to the rule question.
4. The assembly decides the **interpretation** of the rule — it does not
   rehear the dispute.
5. The decision on the rule interpretation is recorded, and the case is
   remanded to the enforcement process to apply the clarified rule.

## 4. THE ASSEMBLY CANNOT REVERSE A FACT

Even a unanimous assembly cannot decide that a fact is otherwise than the
evidence showed. Facts are established by evidence, and the two hearings (CRB
and jury) are the final finders of fact. If the assembly dislikes a factual
finding, it must say so as a rule problem or not at all.

## 5. THE ASSEMBLY CANNOT VIOLATE THE BASIC STRUCTURE

An assembly appeal that would weaken a fundamental right, legalise targeting,
or permit water denial as punishment is **void ab initio** regardless of how
many members vote for it. The Master Constitution's Section 4 applies here
more than anywhere.

## 6. EXTERNAL RECOURSE — THE LAST STOP

When internal steps are exhausted, a party may take the matter **outside**:

| Forum | When | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|
| **Internal arbitration** | Agreed by the parties under the membership agreement | Fast, cheap, binding |
| **Mediation / Lok Adalat** | Voluntary | Encouraged before court |
| **Consumer forum / civil court / police** | Serious wrongs, crimes, rights violations | The system never blocks a member from seeking justice |

The system supports a member exercising external rights: it releases records
they are entitled to, does not retaliate, and never punishes "taking the matter
outside". See `05-Enforcement/05-Legal-Recourse.md` and
`11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/04-Dispute-Adjudication/`.

## 7. FINALITY AND DIGNITY

At every level, including this one, the losing party is given written reasons
and a fair record. Nobody is "ruined" by losing an internal appeal: fines are
bounded, water rights are protected, and the losing party keeps their voice in
the colony. The purpose of finality is to let the colony move on — not to
humiliate the loser.

## 8. SUMMARY OF THE FULL LADDER

| Step | Forum | Decides | Time |
|------|-------|---------|------|
| 1 | Dialogue | Settlement | 3 days |
| 2 | Written grievance | Case registered | 1 working day |
| 3 | CRB hearing | Facts, rules, remedy | 7 days after hearing |
| 4 | Lot-Drawn Jury | Fresh rehearing, final facts | 14 days |
| 5 | General Assembly | Rule interpretation only | Next assembly |
| — | External | Anything not settled internally | Per law |
