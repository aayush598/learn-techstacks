# DISCONNECTION PROTOCOL

**Cross-reference:** `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`, `00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/01-Right-to-Fair-Water.md`

## 1. WHAT THIS FILE DOES

Disconnection — stopping a member's water supply — is the **last resort** of the
whole system. Because water is common property and essential to life (P1), the
system treats disconnection as a heavy, rare, reversible act with a strict
protocol. This file defines the only three grounds, the full process, and the
limits.

## 2. THE ONLY THREE GROUNDS

Disconnection may be ordered **only** for:

| Ground | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Proven non-payment** | Dues genuinely owed after the full collection ladder was run (notices, instalments offered, hardship considered) — a pure refusal to contribute (P4) |
| **Proven deliberate sabotage** | Deliberate damage to the common system — motor, panel, line, meters |
| **Proven criminal tampering** | Theft of water, bypassing the meter, interference with equipment amounting to criminal mischief |

**Never** for: personal dislike, "teaching a lesson", punishing an argument,
deterring complaints, or any act the ladder classifies below Level 5.

## 3. THE FULL PROTOCOL — SEVEN STEPS, NONE SKIPPABLE

| Step | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| 1. **Investigation and finding** | CRB finds the ground proven, on evidence, with the member heard (presence rule). |
| 2. **Reasons in writing** | The finding states the ground, the evidence, the rule. |
| 3. **Assembly vote** | **3/4 majority** of the full membership (not of those present) approves the disconnection. |
| 4. **Written notice** | 15 days' written notice to the member, stating the ground, the start date, the appeal path, and the cure. |
| 5. **Due process window** | The member may appeal to the Lot-Drawn Jury during the 15 days; the disconnection is stayed pending appeal. |
| 6. **Execution** | Only a named executor (never a crowd, never the aggrieved party) implements it, logged and witnessed. |
| 7. **Restoration on cure** | The moment the member cures the ground (pays, repairs, removes tampering), water is restored. |

## 4. HARD LIMITS

| Limit | Rule |
|-------|------|
| Not a punishment for behaviour | Behaviour below Level 5 grounds is handled at lower levels |
| Never indefinite | Each order has a fixed term (≤ 30 days initially); extension requires a fresh finding |
| Not for non-members' disputes | Disconnection is about the member's own conduct |
| No collective disconnection | Never the household's other homes, never a group |
| No public shaming at execution | The act is logged, witnessed, and private; the record is public, the drama is not |
| Never for a pure money dispute without the full ladder | The member must have been offered instalments and heard first |

## 5. THE CURE AND RE-ADMISSION

- The member is told, in the notice, exactly what action cures the ground:
  payment plan honoured, sabotage repaired, tampering removed and proved.
- On cure, water is restored **within 24 hours**. No re-application fee, no
  "come begging" posture.
- A member who is disconnected for non-payment may also choose a hardship
  review at any time; a proven hardship converts the case to an instalment
  plan.

## 6. THE PRACTICAL CASE

*Example:* A member has not paid dues for 10 months. The treasurer ran the
normal collection steps: notices, two reminders, an instalment offer. The
member refuses flatly. The CRB finds non-payment proven. The assembly votes —
**19 of 24 members** must approve; only 15 do, so the motion fails. The case
returns to reminders and the ladder continues. Six months later, with 19 votes,
the 3/4 threshold is met; a 15-day notice gives the member a final chance; the
member pays in the 12th day. No disconnection occurs. That is the protocol
working: heavy, slow, and always reversible.

## 7. WHEN THE PROTOCOL IS MISUSED

An attempted disconnection outside this protocol — a crowd demanding a cut, an
operator closing a valve "informally" — is itself an offence:

1. The water is restored immediately (Cease and Desist).
2. The offenders face Level 4 (informal water cuts are the old system's exact
   abuse).
3. The victim's case is then decided on the evidence — the attempted cut is
   never treated as "a warning that worked".

## 8. THE PRINCIPLE

Disconnection exists to protect the **system** (the collective's water and
money) from genuine, proven, curable abuse. It exists to protect the system
from freeloaders and saboteurs — never to let the system become the abuser. The
member's right to fair water is only ever suspended, never destroyed, and never
without a path back.
