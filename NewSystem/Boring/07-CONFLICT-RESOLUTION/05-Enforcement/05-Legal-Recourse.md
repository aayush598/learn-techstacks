# LEGAL RECOURSE

**Folder:** `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/`  
**Cross-reference:** `11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/04-Dispute-Adjudication/`, `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/02-Resolution-Process/05-Step-5-Final-Appeal.md`

---

## 1. WHAT THIS FILE DOES

The system resolves internal conflicts. But some acts are not merely internal:
**theft, assault, criminal tampering, threats, destruction of property** are
crimes, and members have rights under the law that no colony decision can
replace. This file defines how the system refers to legal recourse — and
guarantees it never blocks a member from justice.

## 2. WHEN THE LAW ENTERS

| Situation | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| Internal steps exhausted (Steps 1–5) and a party still disputes a decision | Voluntary arbitration, then civil forum per `11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/04-Dispute-Adjudication/` |
| A crime is alleged (theft, assault, tampering, threats, defamation) | Documented referral to police / legal aid immediately — **no internal ladder needs to finish first** |
| A member asks to go to court | The member's right. The system facilitates, never blocks. |
| A member is the victim of a crime by another member | The system gives full support and records, and the victim may proceed with or without internal action |

## 3. THE DOCUMENTED REFERRAL

Any referral follows a fixed path so the victim is never left alone:

1. **Intake:** the victim's report is taken down with date, time, evidence.
2. **Referral note:** a written note states the alleged act, the evidence
   already gathered (logs, photos, receipts), and the contact for the police
   station, legal aid, or the consumer forum.
3. **Records released:** every record the victim is entitled to (logs, meters,
   register, minutes) is handed over within 7 days — P6 works in the victim's
   favour.
4. **Statement of non-blocking:** a signed note confirms the system will not
   penalise the victim for seeking law, will not expel them, and will not
   pressure them to withdraw.

## 4. THE LEGAL FUND

The colony maintains a small **legal-assistance fund** (financed from a fixed
line in the budget):

| Use | Amount rule |
|-----|-------------|
| Victim's first consultation | Covered |
| Documentation and lawyer fees for the victim | Covered within the fund's ceiling, on documented need |
| The system's own defence against frivolous abuse | Limited, requires CRB + assembly approval |
| The offender's defence | Not covered — the fund helps victims, not offenders |

The fund is audited with everything else (P6). It exists so that the member
with less money is not the member who cannot seek justice.

## 5. THE SYSTEM NEVER BLOCKS A MEMBER

| Old-system behaviour | This system |
|----------------------|-------------|
| "Do not go to police, settle inside" | The member decides. The system records their choice and helps either way. |
| Expelling a member who sued | Expulsion or retaliation for seeking justice is itself a Level 4/5 offence. |
| Destroying records the police request | All records are retained (`11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/03-Records-Retention/`) and released. |
| Punishing the victim for "airing dirty laundry" | Protection from retaliation extends to legal action (`03-Protection-Mechanisms/04-No-Retaliation-Rule.md`). |

## 6. PARALLEL PATHS — INTERNAL AND LEGAL

A criminal act may run internally (ladder) and legally (police/court) at the
same time:

- The internal process uses the same evidence; it does not retry the crime, it
  applies the colony's own consequences (restitution, removal from roles).
- The legal process is the state's. The colony's finding is its own and does
  not bind the court, and vice versa.
- A member is never asked to choose between the two.

## 7. THE PRACTICAL CASE

*Example:* A member is caught tampering with a neighbour's meter to reduce his
own bill. The CRB records the evidence (photos, meter seals, the register) and
finds tampering at Level 4. The victim wants to file a police complaint. The
referral note is prepared the same day with all records; the legal fund covers
the first consultation; nothing in the colony's finding stops the police case.
The tamperer faces both: the colony's consequences and the law's.

## 8. ARBITRATION BEFORE COURT

Where both parties agree, internal matters that reach Step 5 are first offered
**arbitration** under the membership agreement: a neutral arbitrator chosen by
lot, fast, binding, cheaper than court. Arbitration is voluntary; no one is
forced into it, and a party may always go to court instead.

## 9. THE PRINCIPLE

The colony's power ends where the law begins. A member who is wronged here
should find that the system hands them the file and walks with them to the
door of justice — because that is what a self-respecting collective does.
