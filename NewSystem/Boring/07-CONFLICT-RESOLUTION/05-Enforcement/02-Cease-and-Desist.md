# CEASE AND DESIST

**Cross-reference:** `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/03-Protection-Mechanisms/01-Protection-from-Targeting.md`, `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`

## 1. WHAT THIS ORDER DOES

A **Cease and Desist (C&D)** is an immediate order to stop an ongoing harm. It
is issued fast, because some harms — water being cut, a member being harassed, a
meter being tampered with — damage the victim while a full hearing is still
days away. The C&D stops the harm now; the hearing decides the rights later.

## 2. WHEN A C&D IS ISSUED

| Situation | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| Targeting in progress | A member's water cut "as punishment"; slot moved to hurt them |
| Retaliation in progress | Harassment after a grievance was filed |
| Tampering | A meter or connection being interfered with |
| Harassment / intimidation | Threats, boycotts, rumours escalating against a member |
| Breach of a prior order | Continuing an act a panel already told a member to stop |

## 3. WHO MAY ISSUE IT, AND HOW FAST

| Authority | Speed |
|-----------|-------|
| Any CRB member (single) | Within **24 hours** of the complaint — the fastest possible route |
| CRB panel | Within 24 hours |
| Any member present at a meeting | May demand the chair issue an immediate stop order; the chair must act or record refusal with reasons |

A complaint may be filed by the victim, by a witness, or anonymously. A C&D
does not require a full hearing — it requires a **credible allegation of ongoing
harm**. The hearing follows.

## 4. WHAT THE ORDER CONTAINS

1. The specific act to stop (naming the person and the conduct).
2. The restoration to happen at once (e.g., "restore House 5's water within
   24 hours", "release the meter for inspection").
3. The deadline for compliance.
4. A warning that violation accelerates penalties.
5. Notice to the enforcement persons (operator, treasurer, repair coordinator)
   who must help implement it.

## 5. THE EFFECT ON THE UNDERLYING CASE

- The C&D is **provisional**, not a verdict. It freezes the situation but does
  not decide who is right.
- The full case continues through the normal steps; the C&D protects the status
  quo in the meantime.
- If the case later shows the C&D was unnecessary, no penalty falls on anyone —
  but the stopped act is not resumed automatically; normal rules apply.

## 6. VIOLATION OF A C&D

| Violation | Consequence |
|-----------|-------------|
| First violation | Penalty accelerated **one level** above what the original offence would draw |
| Second violation | Accelerated two levels; the violator may be removed from roles immediately |
| Continued violation | Level 4/5 territory; immediate legal referral where a crime is involved (harassment, criminal tampering) |

A violation of a C&D is **not** a minor procedural matter. It shows a member
will not obey the system, and it is treated as a direct assault on the
protection mechanism itself.

## 7. THE PRACTICAL CASE

*Example:* A member's water is cut "as punishment" after a loud argument with
the operator. The next morning she files a complaint. A CRB member issues a C&D
within hours: the operator must restore her water within 24 hours and must not
touch her connection again until the case is decided. The operator complies. The
hearing later finds the cut was targeting; the operator faces Level 3, plus the
C&D is on record. Her water was down for a day, not a week — because the order
arrived fast.

## 8. THE C&D LOG

All C&D orders are logged with: date issued, by whom, the act ordered stopped,
compliance deadline, compliance status. The log is public (P6). An order that
was complied with is noted "complied"; one violated is referred to the ladder.

## 9. THE PRINCIPLE

Justice that arrives after the water is gone is half justice. The C&D is the
system's way of saying: the harm stops now, and the questions can be answered
afterwards.
