# SUSPENSION OF PRIVILEGES

**Folder:** `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/`  
**Cross-reference:** `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`, `04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/03-Event-Water/`

---

## 1. WHAT THIS FILE DOES

Level 3 of the Enforcement Ladder suspends **privileges** — extras a member
enjoys beyond their basic rights. This file defines precisely what may be
suspended, what may **never** be suspended, for how long, and how the
suspension is reviewed.

## 2. THE CORE DISTINCTION: PRIVILEGE VS RIGHT

| Privilege (may be suspended) | Right (never suspended) |
|------------------------------|-------------------------|
| Event-water booking (weddings, functions) | Basic water supply to the household |
| Serving on committees / CRB / panels | The member's voice at meetings |
| Holding keys, operating duties, earning credits | The right to inspect all records and audit (P6) |
| Priority in extras (extra storage, guest connections) | The right to be notified, heard, and given reasons |
| — | Dignity, equal treatment, appeal rights (P10, P5) |

A member may lose the **extras**, never the **essentials**. Suspending the
essentials would make the ladder a weapon again.

## 3. WHAT CAN BE SUSPENDED — LEVEL 3 LIST

| Privilege | Duration ceiling | Notes |
|-----------|------------------|-------|
| Event-water booking | Up to 3 months | Personal-use event water; a family's basic daily water is untouched |
| Committee / panel membership | Up to 3 months | Roles are rotated anyway; suspension is a recorded consequence, not exile |
| Key-holding / operating duty | Up to 3 months | The member is retrained before resuming |
| Earning credits from extra duties | Up to 3 months | Basic contribution duties remain |
| Extra-water options (priority extras) | Up to 3 months | As defined in the schedule rules |

## 4. WHAT IS NEVER SUSPENDED

| Never suspended | Because |
|-----------------|---------|
| Basic water | Water is common property (P1) and the household's minimum is protected |
| Voice / vote at meetings | P2, equal power; silencing a member is what the old system did |
| Audit and information rights | P6 — a suspended member must still be able to verify the system |
| Dignity and fair treatment | P10 — no public shaming, no permanent labels |
| Right to appeal | The ladder itself must remain open |

## 5. HOW A SUSPENSION WORKS

1. **Finding:** a CRB decision identifies the offence and assigns Level 3.
2. **Written order:** names the privilege, the period, and the reason.
3. **Notice:** the member is told what they lose and what they keep (the
   essentials).
4. **Beginning and end:** the period is fixed (e.g., "2 months from 1 Aug").
5. **Record:** the suspension is entered in the register with a clear end date.

## 6. REVIEW AND REVIEW EARLY

- **Automatic review at midpoint.** The CRB reviews the suspension halfway
  through: if the member's conduct and restoration are on track, the remainder
  may be lifted.
- **Early lifting:** a member who has corrected the harm, paid any fine, and
  shown changed conduct may apply for early lifting; the CRB decides within 7
  days.
- **No automatic extension.** A suspension ends on its date unless a **fresh**
  offence produces a fresh finding. Hanging a longer suspension over a member
  "in case they misbehave again" is prohibited.

## 7. THE PRACTICAL CASE

*Example:* A member is proven to have spread false rumours against another
member to isolate them (Level 3). The CRB orders: event-water booking
suspended 2 months, no committee role for 2 months, but her basic water, her
vote, and her right to audit remain untouched. She appeals — the jury confirms.
At midpoint, with the correction notice posted and no repeat conduct, the
remainder is lifted. She never lost a drop of water and never lost her voice —
she lost exactly the extras the ladder said she would lose.

## 8. THE PRINCIPLE

Suspension of privileges is a **measured** consequence. It communicates that
conduct has consequences without making the member's life unlivable. The day
the ladder starts threatening a member's water or silence, it has become the
thing it was built to replace.
