# ENFORCEMENT LADDER

**Folder:** `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/`  
**Cross-reference:** `00-CONSTITUTION/00-MASTER-CONSTITUTION.md`, `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/02-Resolution-Process/`, `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/04-Disconnection-Protocol.md`

---

## 1. WHAT THIS FILE DOES

The Enforcement Ladder is the system's **only** set of punishments. No member
may be punished outside this ladder, and no step may be skipped or exceeded. It
exists so that consequences are **graduated, predictable, and appealable** — and
so that water can never be used as an arbitrary weapon.

## 2. THE RULES GOVERNING ALL ENFORCEMENT

1. **Due process first.** Every penalty follows a finding by the CRB (or jury
   on appeal) under `02-Resolution-Process/`.
2. **Recorded reasons.** Every penalty is written with the offence, the rule
   violated, the evidence, and the level chosen.
3. **Appeal rights.** Every penalty is appealable through the ladder's steps.
4. **No skipping.** A first offence does not jump to Level 4; a proven repeat
   escalates.
5. **Water protection always retained.** The accused member's basic water is
   never cut as punishment outside the Level 5 protocol
   (`04-Disconnection-Protocol.md`), and even then only for the specific
   grounds listed there.
6. **Proportional and individual.** Punishment is against the person who
   acted, never their household, family, or group.

## 3. THE LADDER

| Level | Name | Consequences | Used for (examples) |
|-------|------|--------------|---------------------|
| **0** | Advice / Notice | Verbal or written note; a reminder of the rule, no penalty | First minor slip: forgot to log once, small delay |
| **1** | Written Warning | Formal written warning, filed in the member's record | Repeated minor slips, first obstruction |
| **2** | Fine + Extra Duty / Education | Published fine (within ceiling) plus extra duty shift or a training session | Missed duty repeatedly, minor negligence, first financial slip |
| **3** | Suspension of Privileges | Loss of event-water booking, committee roles, key-holding, earning credits for a fixed period | Targeting, retaliation, minor tampering, abuse at meetings |
| **4** | Major Consequences | Restitution, removal from all roles, exclusion from voting on finance for a fixed period | Deliberate defiance of truth, coordinated targeting, misappropriation |
| **5** | Disconnection | Suspension of water connection under the Disconnection Protocol only | Proven non-payment after full ladder, proven sabotage, proven criminal tampering |
| — | **Legal recourse** | Referral to police/courts alongside or after internal steps | Theft, assault, criminal tampering, threats |

## 4. LEVEL DETAILS AND LIMITS

| Level | Ceiling | Who decides | Review |
|-------|---------|-------------|--------|
| 0 | None (no record harm) | Operator / committee | Any member may object |
| 1 | Stays on record 6 months | CRB single member | Written appeal |
| 2 | Fine ≤ published cap (e.g., ₹500) + one duty shift | CRB panel | Appeal to jury |
| 3 | Privileges suspended ≤ 3 months | CRB panel | Appeal to jury |
| 4 | Roles removed ≤ 6 months; finance vote excluded ≤ 6 months | CRB + assembly confirmation | Appeal to jury |
| 5 | Disconnection ≤ 30 days initially | CRB finding + 3/4 assembly vote + 15-day notice | Full protocol applies |

## 5. ESCALATION AND DE-ESCALATION

- **Escalation:** A proven repeat of the same offence moves one level up. A
  deliberate, coordinated, or defiant offence may move two levels.
- **De-escalation:** An offender who has completed the consequence, corrected
  the harm, and completed restoration may apply to have their record
  "stepped down" after the record period — the ladder is for correction, not
  for life sentences on a member's reputation.

## 6. WHAT IS NEVER A PENALTY

| Never allowed | Why |
|---------------|-----|
| Cutting basic water as punishment | Only Level 5 protocol, and only for its listed grounds — never "to teach a lesson" |
| Public humiliation or shaming | P10 |
| Seizing a member's contribution or refund beyond the actual debt | Theft by the system is still theft |
| Punishing a household for one member's act | Collective punishment is targeting |
| Punishing a member for filing, testifying, or appealing | Retaliation — doubles the penalty |
| Permanent exclusion from water | The protocol is temporary and restorable on cure |

## 7. THE DUE-PROCESS CHECKLIST BEFORE ANY PENALTY

1. Was the member notified of the specific allegation in writing?
2. Was the member heard (or did they refuse to appear after notice)?
3. Is the finding written, with evidence cited?
4. Is the penalty at the correct level, within ceiling, with reasons?
5. Was the appeal right stated?

If any box is unticked, the penalty is **void** and must be re-done properly.

## 8. THE PRACTICAL CASE

*Example:* A member skipped duty three Sundays in a row. Level 0 reminder → then
Level 1 warning → then, on the third miss, Level 2 (fine + one extra duty
shift). He disputes it, appealing to the jury. The jury confirms on the logs.
He completes the shift and the fine. His record is noted and will step down
after six months. No one ever threatened his water — because skipping duty is
not a Level 5 matter.

## 9. THE PRINCIPLE

The ladder is the colony's substitute for anger. When a member is wronged, the
response is not a personal act of revenge — it is a recorded, proportionate,
appealable consequence. That is how a colony punishes without becoming the
thing it replaced.
