# DATA PRIVACY RULES

**Folder:** `01-MEMBERSHIP/04-Member-Registry/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

Radical transparency (P6) means every member can see the system's books and
logs. It does **not** mean every member's private facts are broadcast to the
whole colony. These rules fix the boundary: **system data is open; personal
data is protected.** Both rules bind everyone, including committees.

## 2. PRINCIPLES

| Principle | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Purpose limitation** | Member data (name, contact, health flags, financial status) is collected and used only for the functioning of the water system |
| **Minimum necessary** | Only the columns in `01-Registry-Format.md` are collected. No political, religious, caste, or family-history data is kept. |
| **Accuracy** | Members may correct their own data at any time |
| **No publishing** | No member's private data is posted on notice boards, WhatsApp groups, or assembly screens |
| **Consent for sharing** | Data goes to outside bodies (banks, government) only with the member's consent or a lawful demand |

## 3. WHAT IS OPEN (TRANSPARENT BY RULE)

| Data | Open to |
|------|---------|
| Connection ID, phase, buy-in status, dues | All members, on inspection |
| Schedule, logs, minutes, receipts, audit reports | All members |
| Duty roster and leave status | All members |
| Any data the member consents to share | All members |

## 4. WHAT IS PROTECTED (PRIVATE BY RULE)

| Data | Rule |
|------|------|
| Contact number | Inspectable only with the member's consent, or by the keeper to contact the member |
| Health / hardship flags | Known only to the members who administer the hardship protocol; not public |
| Family composition | Not recorded beyond "household" and "member name" |
| Financial hardship | Known only to Finance handlers for relief decisions |
| Dispute history of a member | Referenced in minutes only as a case; personal details limited to what is necessary |

## 5. CORRECTING ONE'S OWN DATA

1. A member may request correction of their own entry in writing.
2. The Registry Keeper corrects within **7 days** and logs the correction in
   the change log.
3. A member who disagrees with a correction may appeal to the CRB; the data
   stands as-is until the appeal decides.

## 6. WHAT IS NEVER ALLOWED

| Action | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Posting a member's phone number or dues on the public notice board | Prohibited |
| Sharing the registry (or photos of rows) outside the system | Prohibited |
| Using member data to campaign, threaten, or shame | Prohibited — Level 2+ offence |
| Keeping a parallel "secret register" with extra personal data | Prohibited — destroyed on discovery, keeper removed |
| Asking about caste/religion/politics in any form | Prohibited — not collected, not asked |

## 7. EXAMPLES FROM COLONY LIFE

- **Correct:** At the assembly, the Finance head states "Row 22 has ₹300 dues,
  due date passed." No name shouted, no number read out; members who want
  detail inspect the registry.
- **Wrong:** Someone circulates a list on WhatsApp of "who has not paid, with
  phone numbers." That is a privacy violation and a dignity violation (P10),
  and is an offence under the Enforcement Ladder.
- **Correct:** A member asks the keeper to correct the spelling of her name;
  the keeper corrects it and logs the change the same week.

## 8. RETENTION AND DESTRUCTION

- Records are retained per `11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/03-Records-Retention/`.
- After a connection is exited and any legal period passes, personal contact
  data is scrubbed from retained records, leaving only the financial and
  connection history.

## 9. ENFORCEMENT

- Privacy violations are graded on the Enforcement Ladder
  (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`):
  - Accident / minor lapse: warning + correction
  - Deliberate exposure to shame a member: Level 3
  - Use of data to threaten or extort: Level 4 (serious offence)
- A member whose data was misused may also claim restoration/rebalance per
  `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/04-Right-vs-Majority-Procedures/05-Restoration-and-Rebalance.md`.
