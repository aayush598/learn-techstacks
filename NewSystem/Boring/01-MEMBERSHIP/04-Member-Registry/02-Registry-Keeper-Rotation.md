# REGISTRY KEEPER ROTATION

**Folder:** `01-MEMBERSHIP/04-Member-Registry/`

---

## 1. WHY ROTATION

In the old system, the person who kept the list of who owed what also held the
keys and the money. One family could quietly inflate someone's dues, delete
someone's payments, or add a "relative" as a member. Rotation of the Registry
Keeper is the P3 (no single point of failure) and P6 (transparency) answer to
that.

## 2. THE ROLE

| Responsibility | Detail |
|----------------|--------|
| Maintain the registry table | `01-Registry-Format.md` |
| Record admissions, payments, exits | With dates and receipt numbers |
| Issue connection IDs | In sequence, no skips |
| Keep the change log | Every change: what, when, who, why |
| Present registry at assemblies | At every monthly assembly |
| Hand over cleanly | Full handover at term end |

## 3. HOW THE KEEPER IS CHOSEN

1. The Registry Keeper is drawn **by lot** from eligible members at the annual
   assembly (or filled by rotation order if the roster already exists).
2. **Term:** 6 months, renewable at most once (maximum 1 year continuous).
3. **Eligibility:** any member who can read and write and has not been the
   keeper in the previous 12 months. Every household takes the turn in
   rotation; a literate member of the household serves on its behalf.
4. **Not eligible:** a member who is a party to an unresolved grievance about
   registry data.

## 4. THE BACKUP KEEPER

- A **backup keeper** is drawn at the same time, from the next household in
  rotation.
- The backup keeper attends the handover and a 30-minute training session, and
  takes over immediately if the keeper is absent, ill, or removed.
- No moment exists at which the registry is "with no one." If both keeper and
  backup are unavailable, the assembly designates the next household in
  rotation the same evening.

## 5. NO SINGLE FAMILY MONOPOLISES

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Consecutive terms | No household serves two terms in a row |
| Family cap | Two members of the same family may not hold registry, finance, and key roles in the same period (`05-Equal-Standing/03-Anti-Domination-Guardrails.md`) |
| Related households | Cousins/relations may not both be keeper and backup in the same term |
| Overlap | The outgoing keeper cannot be the incoming keeper |

## 6. HANDOVER PROCEDURE

| Step | Detail |
|------|--------|
| 1. Count pages/rows | Keeper and backup count and initial every page together |
| 2. Reconcile | Dues columns reconciled with Finance books in front of both |
| 3. Sign | Both sign the handover sheet stating the exact state on the date |
| 4. Store | Registry placed in system custody (dual-custody box per `03-ROTATION-DUTY/05-Key-and-Control/`) |
| 5. Announce | The assembly is told the registry changed hands on that date |

## 7. REMOVAL

A keeper is removed early only for:

- Falsifying or deleting entries (Level 2+ offence),
- Refusing handover at term end,
- Three unexcused absences from handover/duty,
- A CRB order finding the keeper unreliable.

Removal is decided by the CRB on evidence, not by a hostile majority vote.
Removed keepers are recorded in the change log and remain eligible for future
rotation after 12 months, unless disqualified by an enforcement order.

## 8. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

For years, "Bhaiya" kept the register and nobody else touched it. Under this
rule, in June the lot falls to Shabana, a teacher, and her backup is Patel.
Shabana learns the table at a 30-minute session with the outgoing keeper,
counts the 32 rows, and signs the handover. In December she hands back to the
next household. When Bhaiya later asks to "see his old book," he is shown the
current registry like everyone else — nothing is his.

## 9. COMPENSATION

- Registry keeping is duty, compensated in time-credits/stipends at the
  published rate (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/03-Duty-Compensation/`). Refusing to serve
  without grounds is a duty failure; serving is paid, not volunteered.

---

*The registry changes hands like the keys do: on schedule, by lot, with
backups, and in front of everyone. Whoever holds it is a servant of the
records, never their owner.*
