# RECOGNITION AND REGISTRATION

**Folder:** `01-MEMBERSHIP/01-Enrollment/`

---

## 1. WHAT RECOGNITION MEANS

"Recognition" is the formal, recorded act by which the system acknowledges a
household as a full member. It happens **after** admission by the assembly and
is completed by registration. Recognition is what converts a resident into a
member with rights, duties, and a vote.

## 2. THE REGISTRATION STEPS

| Step | Detail | Responsible |
|------|--------|-------------|
| 1. Admission minute | Assembly records the admission decision with date | Assembly / minute-taker |
| 2. Connection ID issued | A unique ID per connection, e.g., `CWSS-021` | Registry Keeper |
| 3. Registry entry | Household, member name, contact, phase, buy-in status, dues entered | Registry Keeper |
| 4. Entry agreement signed | See `01-Enrollment/04-Entry-Agreement.md` | Member + two witnesses |
| 5. Connection opened / slot assigned | The household joins the standard schedule | Duty roster / operations |
| 6. Acknowledgement letter | A written letter to the member confirming status, ID, and rights | Registry Keeper |

## 3. CONNECTION ID

- Every connection gets **exactly one** connection ID. It is never reused after
  exit, so records stay clean.
- The ID is printed in the registry, on the acknowledgement letter, and used in
  logs, dues, and voting.
- Format: `CWSS-` followed by the next free sequence number, assigned by the
  Registry Keeper in order. No skipping, no "lucky numbers."

## 4. WHAT RECOGNITION GRANTS — AND DOES NOT

| Grants | Does not grant |
|--------|----------------|
| Water slot on the standard schedule | Any special or larger slot |
| One equal vote (P2) | Extra votes for money, seniority, or skill |
| Duty participation and time-credits | Exemption from duty |
| Full audit and information rights (P6) | Access to another member's private data |
| Protection from targeting (P7) | Immunity from the Enforcement Ladder |

## 5. REGISTRATION TIMELINE

| Event | Deadline |
|-------|----------|
| Assembly admission | Next assembly after verification |
| Registry entry + connection ID | Within 7 days of admission |
| Entry agreement signed | Within 14 days of admission |
| Acknowledgement letter | Within 7 days of registry entry |

If registration is delayed beyond these deadlines, the member may file a
grievance (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/02-Resolution-Process/02-Step-2-Written-Grievance.md`).
A delay is not the new member's fault and cannot reduce their rights.

## 6. CORRECTION OF RECORDS

- Any member who finds an error in their own registry entry (name spelling,
  contact, phase) may request correction in writing. The Registry Keeper
  corrects it within **7 days** and notes the correction.
- Correction of another member's entry is allowed only with that member's
  written consent or a CRB order.

## 7. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

The Patel family is admitted on 5 January. The next Sunday the Registry Keeper
issues ID `CWSS-022`, opens the registry row, and the family signs the entry
agreement in front of two members. Their acknowledgement letter states their
slot, their vote, and their duty turn. From that day, if anyone says "the
Patels have no rights because they came last year," the letter and the registry
are the answer — and any hostile member who acts on that belief is targeting a
member (P7).

## 8. RECOGNITION IS NOT A FAVOUR

Recognition is a **right** of an admitted, verified household — not a favour
that the Registry Keeper or any group can withhold or sell. A Registry Keeper
who refuses to register an admitted household is obstructing a member's right
and faces the Enforcement Ladder.

---

*Recognition closes the enrollment process. From this moment the member is
bound by the entry agreement and protected by every Fundamental Right.*
