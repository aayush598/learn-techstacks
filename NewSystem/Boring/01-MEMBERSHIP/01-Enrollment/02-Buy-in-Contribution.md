# BUY-IN CONTRIBUTION (LATE JOINER'S FAIR SHARE)

**Folder:** `01-MEMBERSHIP/01-Enrollment/`

---

## 1. WHY A BUY-IN EXISTS

The boring, motor, pipeline, overhead tank, and control panel were funded by
members who contributed before or during installation. A household that joins
**after** installation would otherwise receive water for free from assets it
never helped buy — while the early members carry the whole capital cost. That
would violate P4 (no free riders).

The **buy-in** is the one-time payment that makes the late joiner a fair,
equal co-owner of the system's assets instead of a free rider.

## 2. THE PRINCIPLE: BACKDATED FAIR SHARE

A late joiner pays their fair backdated share of the system's capital assets,
as if they had been a member from the start. The detailed method, worked
example, and depreciation logic are defined in:

`09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/03-Late-Joiner-Buy-in/01-Backdated-Contribution.md`

## 3. WHAT THE BUY-IN COVERS

| Component | Covered by buy-in? |
|-----------|--------------------|
| Drilling of the boring | Yes |
| Motor and pump set | Yes |
| Pipeline and distribution | Yes |
| Overhead tank / sump | Yes |
| Control panel and safety devices | Yes |
| Monthly running costs (electricity, diesel) | No — paid via monthly contribution |
| Past repairs | Covered via the depreciation-adjusted share, per the method file |

## 4. WHEN THE BUY-IN MUST BE PAID

1. A new household **receives no water connection** until a buy-in amount is
   agreed and the payment schedule is signed
   (`02-Types-of-Members/03-Late-Joiners.md`).
2. "Receives no water" includes filling a drum, "temporary" hose use, and
   borrowing from a neighbour's tap for the new house. All such use counts as
   using the system.
3. The Registry Keeper records buy-in status in the registry
   (`04-Member-Registry/01-Registry-Format.md`).

## 5. INSTALLMENT OPTION

- A late joiner who cannot pay the full buy-in at once may pay in **equal
  monthly installments** over a maximum of **12 months**, on the installment
  rules in `09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/03-Late-Joiner-Buy-in/`.
- A written installment schedule is signed before connection is opened. It
  states: total buy-in, number of installments, per-installment amount, and
  due dates.
- Until the buy-in is **fully paid**, the member is a "late joiner" with the
  voting limits in `02-Types-of-Members/03-Late-Joiners.md`.

## 6. THE BUY-IN IS NOT A FEE

| Misconception | Truth |
|---------------|-------|
| "The buy-in is a fine for joining late." | No. It is a fair share of assets the joiner will co-own. |
| "The buy-in goes into someone's pocket." | It goes to the Common/Asset Fund and is fully accounted, open to audit (P6). |
| "Buy-in buys me water." | No. Water is common property (P1); the buy-in buys a fair share of the physical assets. |
| "Once I pay, I can stop paying monthly." | No. Monthly contribution is a separate, continuing duty. |

## 7. NON-DISCRIMINATION

- The buy-in amount and installment terms are the **same for every late joiner**
  in the same phase. No one may be charged more or less because of friendship,
  hostility, caste, or any other ground (`05-Equal-Standing/02-Equal-Burden-Equal-Benefit.md`).
- Any individualised discount or surcharge requires an assembly resolution with
  a written, published reason — and even then it must not reduce a member's
  share of water or vote.

## 8. RECORDS

- Every buy-in receipt is numbered, dated, and filed. The paying member gets a
  receipt copy immediately.
- Buy-in balances are reported at every assembly and are auditable at any time
  (`06-FINANCE/04-Audit/`).

## 9. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

After the boring was drilled, the Sharma family buys the house at the colony
edge. The buy-in, computed per the backdated-share method, is ₹24,000. The
family cannot pay it at once. They sign a 12-month installment schedule of
₹2,000/month, the connection is opened the same day, and the registry marks
them "Late joiner — buy-in ₹0/24,000". They receive full water and full vote,
but under the Late-Joiner rule they may not outvote older members on capital
matters while the balance is outstanding.

## 10. ARREARS AND CONSEQUENCES

- Missed installments follow the normal dues procedure
  (`06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/`), not water cuts without due process.
- A late joiner who refuses to sign any schedule and uses water anyway is
  treated as an unregistered user (Level 3 offence) until registered.

---

*The buy-in is one of the few legitimate differences between members, and it is
purely financial. It can never touch water share, voting weight, or dignity.*
