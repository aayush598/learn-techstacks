# NEW MEMBER ADMISSION

**Folder:** `01-MEMBERSHIP/01-Enrollment/`

---

## 1. WHO MAY APPLY

Any household physically connected, or applying for connection, to the colony
water network may apply for membership. The household must be a real dwelling
in the colony — not a shed, shop on a non-residential plot, or vacant site.

## 2. HOW TO APPLY

1. The household completes the standard **Membership Application** form:
   `12-TEMPLATES/01-Membership-Application.md`.
2. The form must be signed and dated by the adult who will be the registered
   connection holder (the "member"). Only one member per connection.
3. The form is submitted to the current Registry Keeper
   (`04-Member-Registry/02-Registry-Keeper-Rotation.md`), who stamps it with
   the date and time of receipt.
4. Receipt of the application must be acknowledged in writing within **48
   hours**, with a copy given to the applicant.

## 3. HOUSEHOLD VERIFICATION

Two members, **drawn by lot** (not self-appointed), visit the household within
**7 days** of application to verify:

| Check | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| The dwelling exists and is occupied | Prevents empty-plot or fake registration |
| The address matches the application | Prevents double registration |
| The applicant resides there | One member per household |
| Whether a physical tap connection already exists | Determines buy-in and connection work |

The verification report is filed and open to all members under P6. Verification
is about facts, not about likes or dislikes of the family.

## 4. GROUNDS THAT MUST NOT BE USED — ZERO DISCRIMINATION

Admission may **never** be refused or delayed on grounds of:

- Caste, religion, language, or region
- Gender or marital status
- Occupation (including daily-wage, vendor, scrap-collector work)
- Political affiliation or past disputes with any member
- Appearance, disability, or health condition
- Tenure (renter status is handled separately in
  `02-Types-of-Members/04-Tenant-Members.md`)

Any admission decision that uses a forbidden ground is **void** and a Level 3
offence under `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`.

## 5. ADMISSION BY THE ASSEMBLY

1. The application, verification report, and buy-in calculation are placed on
   the agenda of the next General Assembly.
2. Admission is decided by a **simple majority** of members present and voting,
   with the assembly quorum rules applied
   (`02-GOVERNANCE/01-General-Assembly/02-Quorum-Rules.md`).
3. Reasons for refusal must be written, factual, and based only on allowed
   grounds (Section 3 failures or unpaid obligations of a prior holder of the
   same connection). A bare "the members do not like this family" is not a
   reason.
4. Refusal can be challenged under `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/02-Resolution-Process/`
   and through the Truth Veto (`00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/02-Single-Member-Truth-Veto.md`).
5. If the assembly does not decide within **30 days** of verification, the
   application is deemed pending and the applicant may raise it as a grievance;
   the decision may not be silently delayed.

## 6. TIMELINES

| Step | Deadline |
|------|----------|
| Acknowledge application | 48 hours |
| Verify household | Within 7 days of application |
| Place on assembly agenda | Next scheduled assembly |
| Assembly decision | Within 30 days of verification |
| Registry entry + connection ID | Within 7 days of admission |

## 7. EQUAL TREATMENT OF NEW AND OLD

1. A newly admitted member receives the same nominal water share as every other
   connection from the first slot they take (`00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/01-Right-to-Fair-Water.md`).
2. The new member's vote is equal to the founding members' votes from day one
   (P2). No "probation period" can reduce a vote.
3. The new member enters the duty roster on the same terms as everyone else
   (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/01-Duty-Roster/`).
4. The only thing that differs for a newcomer is the **buy-in**, because they
   join after the installation was funded. That difference is purely financial,
   defined in `01-Enrollment/02-Buy-in-Contribution.md`, and never affects water
   or voting.

## 8. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

A daily-wage family rents and later buys a small house in the colony. Their
neighbours object that "these people will waste water." That is not an allowed
ground. The family is verified, admitted, and placed on the roster like any
other household. Their monthly contribution is the same as every other
connection. Anyone who cuts their slot can be reported as targeting
(`00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/04-Right-to-Protection-from-Targeting.md`).

## 9. REFUSAL AND REAPPLICATION

- A refused applicant may reapply after 90 days if the ground of refusal has
  been remedied.
- A second refusal on the same facts can be reviewed by the Conflict Review
  Board (CRB) as a possible discrimination case.

---

*This procedure binds every assembly. An assembly cannot invent new grounds for
admission without a constitutional amendment under
`00-CONSTITUTION/05-Amendment-Process/`.*
