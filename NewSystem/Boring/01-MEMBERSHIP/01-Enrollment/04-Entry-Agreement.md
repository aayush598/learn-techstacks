# ENTRY AGREEMENT

**Folder:** `01-MEMBERSHIP/01-Enrollment/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

The Entry Agreement is the single written document by which a new household
formally accepts the constitution and all its consequences. It protects both
the system (no one can later say "I never agreed to that") and the member (no
one can impose obligations outside the written rules).

## 2. WHAT THE AGREEMENT CONTAINS

The agreement is the standard template in
`11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/01-Binding-Agreement/` and must state, at minimum:

| Clause | Content |
|--------|---------|
| **Identity** | Connection ID, member name, household address, contact |
| **Acceptance of constitution** | The member accepts the Master Constitution and all subordinate rules, as amended from time to time |
| **Contribution clause** | Commitment to the monthly contribution formula and to any agreed buy-in/installment schedule |
| **Duty clause** | Commitment to perform rotating duties and to learn the required skills |
| **Water clause** | Acceptance that water is common property (P1); no right to hoard, sell, or divert |
| **Arbitration / dispute clause** | Commitment to the internal resolution process and arbitration before any external court action on system matters |
| **Data clause** | Consent for the member's system data to be kept in the registry and used only for system purposes |
| **Exit clause** | Acknowledgment of the refund formula and exit procedure |

## 3. SIGNING PROCEDURE

1. The agreement is read aloud or explained in the member's language before
   signing. No member signs a document they do not understand.
2. It is signed by:
   - the member, and
   - two adult witnesses (preferably not from the same household as the
     Registry Keeper, to keep the record independent).
3. One original copy stays with the member; one is filed in the system records;
   one (if kept) is the digital copy under `06-FINANCE/03-Bookkeeping/`.
4. An illiterate or low-literacy member signs by thumb impression after the
   document is read out in front of the witnesses; the witnesses attest that it
   was read.

## 4. THE ARBITRATION CLAUSE

- All disputes arising from membership, water, contributions, duties, or assets
  go first through the internal ladder:
  `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/02-Resolution-Process/`, then the CRB, then the
  Enforcement Ladder, and finally binding arbitration as defined in
  `11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/04-Dispute-Adjudication/`.
- This clause exists so that a wealthy or connected member cannot bypass the
  internal process and drag a weaker member straight to court; and equally so
  that a member with a genuine right cannot be trapped inside a system that
  refuses to hear them.
- The clause cannot waive Fundamental Rights, which are non-negotiable.

## 5. WHAT THE AGREEMENT DOES NOT DO

| It does not | Because |
|-------------|---------|
| Give anyone control over water | P1 — water is common property |
| Give anyone extra votes | P2 — one member one vote |
| Bind a member to unpaid forced labour | P8 — all work is compensated |
| Let the majority waive a member's rights | P5 — truth/rights over majority |
| Apply retroactively against the member | Rules bind from signature date, unless a published rule explicitly states otherwise |

## 6. DURATION AND REVISION

- The agreement is for the lifetime of the member's connection unless the
  member exits, the house is sold, or the constitution is amended
  (amendments bind all members equally through the published process).
- When the constitution is amended, the notice board and assembly publish the
  change; the amendment binds members without needing a new signature.
- A member who disagrees with an amendment may use the amendment challenge
  process, not unilateral refusal.

## 7. REFUSAL TO SIGN

- No household is registered, and no connection is opened, until the Entry
  Agreement is signed. Water access without signing is not permitted.
- Refusal to sign does not prevent the household from living in the colony; it
  only means they are not part of the water system.

## 8. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

A retired teacher joins the system. The Registry Keeper does not pressure her
to sign quickly; the agreement is read to her in full and she takes a copy to
her son for review. She returns, signs, and is registered. Two years later a
group proposes to double her contribution "because she is old." She shows the
agreement and the contribution formula: the clause commits her to the published
formula, not to arbitrary levies. The proposal is void under P5.

## 9. STORAGE AND ACCESS

- Signed agreements are stored with the registry and are inspectable by any
  member (P6) but not copyable for outside use.
- Originals may not leave the custody of the system except by CRB order or
  lawful demand.

---

*The Entry Agreement is the member's proof of belonging and the system's proof
of consent. Both sides are bound by it equally.*
