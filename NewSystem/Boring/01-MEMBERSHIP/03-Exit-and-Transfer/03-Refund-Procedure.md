# REFUND PROCEDURE

**Folder:** `01-MEMBERSHIP/03-Exit-and-Transfer/`

---

## 1. THE REFUND RIGHT

A member who exits (or whose dwelling's connection passes to a new owner after
sale) is entitled to a refund of the un-consumed, un-owed part of their paid
capital share. The refund is computed **by formula**, never by mood, favour, or
assembly popularity. P6 applies: the calculation is shown fully to the exiting
member and is open to all.

## 2. THE REFUND FORMULA

```
Refund = (Paid capital contributions + paid buy-in)
         − (Consumed share over time)
         − (Outstanding dues)
         − (Assessed damages)
```

| Component | Meaning | Source |
|-----------|---------|--------|
| Paid capital contributions | Buy-in and any capital levies the member actually paid | Registry + receipt book |
| Consumed share | Depreciation of assets attributable to the member's period of use, per the published depreciation table | `06-FINANCE/05-Distribution-and-Refunds/` |
| Outstanding dues | Arrears of monthly contribution, installment balances, event-water charges | Dues register |
| Assessed damages | Tampering, damage, or loss adjudicated against the connection | CRB order |

All numbers come from the books, not from opinions. A dispute about a number is
a factual dispute, resolved by evidence (`00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/`).

## 3. ILLUSTRATION (DEPRECIATION RATE OF 10% PER YEAR)

| Line | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Buy-in paid by member | ₹24,000 |
| Member's period of use | 4 years → consumed share (₹24,000 × 4 × 10%) | ₹9,600 |
| Arrears of contribution | ₹600 |
| Damage order (tampering) | ₹500 |
| **Refund** | ₹24,000 − 9,600 − 600 − 500 = **₹13,300** |

The same depreciation table is used for every member — no one gets a better
rate for being "helpful."

## 4. TIMELINE

| Step | Deadline |
|------|----------|
| Exit notice (or sale transfer trigger) | Day 0 |
| Refund calculation prepared | Within 14 days |
| Calculation shown to member; member may object with evidence | 7 days to object |
| Objection review (CRB if unresolved) | Within 14 days |
| Payment released | Within 30 days of the final calculation |

Delay beyond these deadlines is a grievance the member may raise; the system
also owes interest at the bank rate on late payment.

## 5. JOINT SIGN-OFF

1. The calculation sheet is signed by:
   - the exiting member, and
   - the Treasurer (or Registry Keeper) and one witness from the Finance
     Committee.
2. The member signs that they have seen and accepted the numbers — or writes
   their objection on the sheet, which is then reviewed.
3. No refund is paid without this sign-off, and no sign-off is taken under
   pressure. A member under pressure may sign under duress and later challenge
   it; the challenge is heard by the CRB.

## 6. PAYMENT METHODS

| Method | Rule |
|--------|------|
| Cash | Paid only at a recorded meeting with two witnesses |
| UPI / bank transfer | Preferred; the transfer receipt is filed |
| Adjustment against outstanding dues | Applied first, automatically |

Payment is made from the Asset Fund per `06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/`. The
treasurer's record is cross-checked against the refund sheet at the next audit.

## 7. DISPUTES

- If the member disputes any component, the dispute goes to the CRB as a
  factual question (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/02-Resolution-Process/`).
- A Truth Veto (`00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/02-Single-Member-Truth-Veto.md`)
  can suspend a refund **only** on a factual/rule ground, not to stall money.
- The refund may never be reduced as "punishment for leaving" or for filing a
  grievance — that is retaliation (P7).

## 8. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

The Reddy family exits after 6 years, having paid ₹24,000 buy-in. The consumed
share at 10%/year is ₹14,400; arrears are nil; no damages. Refund = ₹9,600. The
sheet is signed by Mr. Reddy, the Treasurer, and the Finance witness, and the
amount is transferred to his account within the 30-day limit. When he later
rejoins a different dwelling, the registry credits this record toward his new
buy-in.

## 9. REFUND WHEN NO PAYMENT WAS MADE

- A member who never paid a buy-in (e.g., a tenant who only ever paid monthly
  contribution) has **no capital to refund**. Monthly contributions are
  consumption payments, not capital, and are not refundable.

## 10. RECORDS

- Every refund sheet, receipt, and objection is filed with the exit record and
  retained per `11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/03-Records-Retention/`.
- The registry marks the connection's refund status so the audit trail is
  complete.

---

*The refund formula is the mirror image of the buy-in formula. Money that was
paid fairly into the system is returned fairly out of it — with receipts on
both sides.*
