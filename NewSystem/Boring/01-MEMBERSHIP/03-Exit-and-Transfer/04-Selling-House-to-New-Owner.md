# SELLING HOUSE TO NEW OWNER

**Folder:** `01-MEMBERSHIP/03-Exit-and-Transfer/`

---

## 1. THE RULE IN ONE LINE

When a house is sold, the **water connection transfers to the new owner only
after the new owner's own admission, buy-in settlement, and registration** —
never automatically with the sale deed.

## 2. WHY

- The connection is a right and duty of the **system**, granted to a registered
  member — not a fixture that moves with a sale deed.
- Without this rule, a buyer could move in, draw water, and refuse all
  contributions or duties, and the old owner would be long gone — the exact
  "free rider" problem (P4).
- It also protects the buyer: they register on published terms and know exactly
  what the connection costs.

## 3. STEPS FOR A SALE

| Step | Who | Detail |
|------|-----|--------|
| 1. Intimate the sale | Seller | Written notice to the Registry Keeper with the buyer's name and contact, within 7 days of the sale agreement |
| 2. Clear seller's dues | Seller | All arrears, outstanding buy-in installments, and charges on the connection are settled **before** transfer |
| 3. Buyer applies | Buyer | Membership application (`12-TEMPLATES/01-Membership-Application.md`) per `01-Enrollment/01-New-Member-Admission.md` |
| 4. Verify household | Two lot-drawn members | Standard verification within 7 days |
| 5. Compute buy-in | Finance | Dwellings existing-buy-in balance, depreciated, minus any credit from the seller's paid capital; full method in `09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/03-Late-Joiner-Buy-in/01-Backdated-Contribution.md` |
| 6. Admission | Assembly | Normal admission vote at the next assembly |
| 7. Settle buy-in | Buyer | Pay in full, or sign the installment schedule (`01-Enrollment/02-Buy-in-Contribution.md`) |
| 8. Entry agreement | Buyer | Signed per `01-Enrollment/04-Entry-Agreement.md` |
| 9. Registry transfer | Registry Keeper | Connection ID moves to the new member; old ID closed; phase and buy-in status updated |
| 10. Keys / records | Roster | Nothing moves privately; keys are handled by the normal rotation handover |

## 4. THE SELLER'S OBLIGATIONS

| Obligation | Rule |
|------------|------|
| Clear arrears | Before transfer is registered |
| Hand over no "water rights" | The seller cannot privately hand the buyer keys, schedule control, or a "right" to water — the system grants all of these |
| Honest statement | The seller must disclose the connection's status (dues, pending grievances) to the buyer; concealment makes the seller liable |
| Pending grievance | Transfer held until resolved (`02-Share-Transfer.md` Section 5) |

## 5. WHAT THE BUYER PAYS

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Outstanding balance of the dwelling's buy-in | Yes — this is the "new owner's buy-in" |
| Top-up to current buy-in if the dwelling's share has depreciated below the standard | Per the backdated method |
| Fresh monthly contributions | From the date of registration |
| Nothing extra for "permission" | No registration fee set by individuals; only published charges |

## 6. TIMELINE

| Event | Deadline |
|-------|----------|
| Sale intimation | Within 7 days of sale agreement |
| Buyer verification | Within 7 days of application |
| Admission | Next assembly |
| Registration + water + vote | Within 7 days of admission and buy-in settlement |

The buyer may not draw water before registration. A seller who "gives the buyer
the key and the schedule" is enabling an unregistered user — a Level 2 offence
for both, unless the buyer completes registration within the published grace
period.

## 7. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

The Khans buy a house from the D'Souzas. The D'Souzas had ₹300 arrears and an
unfinished buy-in balance of ₹4,000 on the dwelling. They clear both before
transfer. The Khans apply, are verified and admitted, and settle the dwelling's
buy-in — which, after depreciation credit, comes to ₹4,000 — with a signed
installment plan. On registration day the connection ID moves to them. They
start taking their slot and their duty turns the same week. Nobody could have
drawn water in the gap, because the roster recognised no one at that address.
