# SHARE TRANSFER

**Folder:** `01-MEMBERSHIP/03-Exit-and-Transfer/`

---

## 1. WHAT IS A SHARE

A member's "share" is their recorded, depreciated entitlement in the system's
capital assets (boring, motor, pipeline, tank) — the money-equivalent of the
buy-in and any capital contributions, net of consumption and dues. It is a
**financial** right only. It is not a right to control water, keys, or
decisions (P1, P2).

## 2. WHEN CAN A SHARE BE TRANSFERRED

| Situation | Allowed? |
|-----------|----------|
| Selling the house to a new owner | Yes — the share and the connection move to the new owner **after** their own admission and buy-in settlement (`04-Selling-House-to-New-Owner.md`) |
| Gifting the house to a relative | Yes — same procedure as a sale |
| Transferring the share to an unrelated person who does not buy the house | No — the share is tied to the dwelling and the member |
| Selling the share to a third party while keeping the house | No — water rights are not a tradeable commodity |
| Transferring the share while a grievance is pending | No — see Section 5 |

## 3. THE TRANSFER SEQUENCE

1. The seller/transferor gives written notice of the intended transfer with the
   buyer's details.
2. The seller clears all dues on the connection (arrears, outstanding buy-in,
   charges).
3. The buyer applies for membership under
   `01-Enrollment/01-New-Member-Admission.md` and is verified.
4. The buy-in owed on the dwelling is computed per
   `09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/03-Late-Joiner-Buy-in/01-Backdated-Contribution.md`,
   credited with any paid capital attributable to the dwelling, and settled by
   the buyer (or carried on an installment schedule).
5. The registry row is transferred to the new member, and the old member's ID
   is closed.
6. Only after registration is complete does the buyer's household take water
   and the vote. The seller does not hand over keys or transfer water control
   on any other basis.

## 4. ORDER OF MONEY

| Item | Who pays |
|------|----------|
| Seller's arrears and outstanding buy-in | Seller, before transfer |
| Buyer's buy-in (top-up if the depreciated share is less than current buy-in) | Buyer |
| Refund to seller | Only the share attributable to the seller after dues, per `03-Refund-Procedure.md`, if the transfer is an exit rather than a sale-to-new-owner |

In a normal house sale the buyer pays the seller the house price and the buyer
separately settles the dwelling's water buy-in with the system. The system is
never a middleman that lets one party off unpaid dues.

## 5. NO TRANSFER WHILE A GRIEVANCE IS PENDING

1. If a grievance against the connection (unpaid dues, tampering, damages, an
   enforcement case) is pending, **no transfer is registered** until the
   grievance is resolved.
2. Purpose: a member cannot escape liability by selling the house and letting
   the buyer inherit the dispute; and the buyer cannot buy a house whose water
   future is uncertain.
3. A pending grievance does **not** stop the buyer from purchasing the house
   itself — only the water-connection transfer is held. The buyer's
   membership application is kept open and processed after the grievance is
   resolved.

## 6. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

The Iyer family sells its house to the Fernandes family. Mr. Iyer has ₹600
arrears; he clears them before transfer. A tampering complaint against the Iyer
connection is pending, so the registry holds the transfer until the CRB closes
the case with a ₹500 damage order, which Mr. Iyer pays. The Fernandes family is
then admitted, pays the dwelling's buy-in balance, signs the entry agreement,
and the registry moves the connection ID to them.

## 7. FORBIDDEN TRANSFER PRACTICES

| Practice | Status |
|----------|--------|
| Selling "the water share" separately from the house | Forbidden — share is tied to the dwelling |
| Transferring keys/control privately between buyer and seller | Forbidden — control belongs to the rotating system, not individuals |
| Registering a transfer without the seller clearing dues | Forbidden |
| Using a transfer to launder a member in bad standing | Forbidden — grievances follow the connection |

---

*The share is a bookkeeping right, not a sword. Transfers are clean only when
every rupee is accounted and every pending dispute is closed.*
