# VOLUNTARY EXIT

**Folder:** `01-MEMBERSHIP/03-Exit-and-Transfer/`

---

## 1. THE RIGHT TO EXIT

Every member has the unconditional right to leave the system. No assembly, no
coalition, and no other member may prevent a voluntary exit. Exit is the
member's own choice and is honoured without humiliation (P10).

## 2. PROCEDURE

| Step | Detail | Deadline |
|------|--------|----------|
| 1. Written notice | A signed exit notice to the Registry Keeper stating the intended exit date | Any time |
| 2. Acknowledge | Registry Keeper stamps the notice with date and gives a copy | Within 48 hours |
| 3. Dues clearance | All monthly contributions, buy-in balances, and charges calculated and settled | Before final exit |
| 4. Refund calculation | Computed per the refund formula | Within 14 days of notice |
| 5. Transition period | Water continues during the 30-day transition; duties continue normally | 30 days |
| 6. Registry closure | Connection marked exited; ID retired (never reused) | On final exit |

## 3. THE 30-DAY TRANSITION

- After notice, the member continues to receive water and perform duties for
  **30 days** so the exit is orderly and no household is left dry overnight.
- During transition the member remains a full member with a full vote.
- The transition can be shortened only at the member's written request.

## 4. DUTIES DURING TRANSITION

- The exiting member completes their current duty turn and hands over keys,
  tools, and records at the scheduled handover
  (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/05-Key-and-Control/`).
- Refusing to hand over keys is a key-security violation and is handled by the
  enforcement ladder, even during exit.

## 5. DUES CLEARANCE

Before the refund is released, the following must be settled:

| Item | Status |
|------|--------|
| Monthly contribution arrears | Payable in full |
| Outstanding buy-in installments | Payable in full (the buy-in is owed on the dwelling) |
| Event-water or repair assessments | Payable if invoiced |
| Damage due to the member's tampering | Assessed per `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/` and payable |
| Duty cover charges | Payable per `03-ROTATION-DUTY/03-Duty-Compensation/` |

## 6. WHAT HAPPENS TO THE CONNECTION

- The connection physically remains at the dwelling. The system does not rip
  out the pipe; the dwelling stays connected.
- The exiting member's connection ID is closed. If the same household (person)
  moves out entirely and the house stands empty, the connection is dormant.
- A new occupant applies for membership under
  `01-Enrollment/01-New-Member-Admission.md` — the buy-in for a dwelling with
  an existing connection follows `03-Exit-and-Transfer/04-Selling-House-to-New-Owner.md`.

## 7. NO RETALIATION FOR EXITING

- No member may punish an exiting member — no water cuts, no public shaming,
  no social boycott. Such conduct is targeting (P7) and a serious offence.
- The system does not "forgive" or "waive" legitimate dues to persuade a
  member to stay. Fair dues are collected; nothing more.

## 8. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

Anand, who has clashed with the committee, gives written notice on 1 June,
intending to exit on 30 June. He clears his ₹300 contribution arrears and his
remaining buy-in installment of ₹2,000. The refund of his ₹24,000 buy-in is
computed and approved for release after the transition. On 30 June he hands
over the keys and the registry marks his ID closed. Three weeks later a member
mockingly says "good riddance" at an assembly — the minute-taker records it and
it is treated as a warning under P10.

## 9. REJOINING

- A former member may reapply through the normal admission process. Past exit
  is not a ground for refusal.
- If the former member rejoins the same dwelling, their paid buy-in is
  credited according to the records, minus the consumed share already refunded.

---

*Exit is a right, not a scandal. The system's fairness shows in how gracefully
it lets a member go, and in how completely its records still speak for them.*
