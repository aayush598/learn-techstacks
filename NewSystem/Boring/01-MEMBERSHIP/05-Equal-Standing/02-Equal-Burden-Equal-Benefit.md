# EQUAL BURDEN, EQUAL BENEFIT

**Folder:** `01-MEMBERSHIP/05-Equal-Standing/`

---

## 1. THE RULE

Every member carries the **same burden** and receives the **same benefit**,
under the same published rules. Nobody pays less because they are powerful and
nobody gets more because they are friendly with the committee. This is P4 and
P1 in one rule.

## 2. THE SAME CONTRIBUTION FORMULA

| Burden | Rule |
|--------|------|
| Monthly contribution | One formula for all connections, set in `06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/01-Monthly-Contribution.md`; no individual rates |
| Buy-in | One backdated method for all late joiners in the same phase (`01-Enrollment/02-Buy-in-Contribution.md`) |
| Special levies (repairs, events) | Same per-connection share for all, by published resolution |
| Event water | Paid at the same published tariff (`04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/03-Event-Water/`) |

## 3. THE SAME DUTY OBLIGATIONS

| Duty | Rule |
|------|------|
| Duty roster | Every household takes its turn in rotation (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/01-Duty-Roster/`) |
| Skill learning | Every member learns the backup skills for their role (`00-CONSTITUTION/03-Fundamental-Duties/02-Duty-to-Learn-and-Participate.md`) |
| Leave and cover | The same leave rules apply to all (`02-Types-of-Members/05-Members-on-Leave.md`) |
| Compensation | The same published credit rate and stipend scale for the same work (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/03-Duty-Compensation/`) |

No "senior" member is excused duty, and no poor member is forced to work
double. Duty is rotated and paid, for everyone.

## 4. THE SAME SLOT RIGHTS

| Benefit | Rule |
|---------|------|
| Water share | Same nominal share per connection (`00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/01-Right-to-Fair-Water.md`) |
| Slot quality | Advantage rotates; nobody keeps the best slot forever (`04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/02-Seasonal-Rotation/`) |
| Schedule information | Published to all at the same time |
| Hardship access | Only through the published, verifiable hardship protocol — never private favour |

## 5. WHEN CAN A DEVIATION HAPPEN?

A deviation from equal burden/benefit can happen **only** through a published
rule that meets all of:

1. Is written and adopted by the assembly (or exists as a constitutional rule);
2. States its reason openly;
3. Applies to a defined category, not to named individuals by whim;
4. Does not touch water share, vote, or dignity;
5. Survives Truth Review (`00-CONSTITUTION/05-Amendment-Process/04-Truth-Review-Before-Adoption.md`).

Examples of valid deviations:

- **Hardship relief** for a member with medical bills — capped, time-bound,
  published, open to all in the same situation.
- **Age/physical limitation** on heavy duty — replaced by equivalent light
  duty, never by exemption from contribution.
- **Distant-plot pressure compensation** under `09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/04-Network-Improvements/`.

## 6. PROHIBITED DEVIATIONS (VOID)

| Practice | Status |
|----------|--------|
| "Old members pay ₹100, new members pay ₹300" | Void unless by published resolution for all in a defined phase |
| "My family has always run the motor; we don't pay" | Void — duty and contribution bind all |
| "The committee members don't pay for event water" | Void |
| "One member gets a double slot because he contributed extra" | Void — water is common property |
| "You pay more because you objected last year" | Void — retaliation, Level 3 offence |

## 7. HOW EQUALITY IS CHECKED

1. **Formula check:** at each assembly, the contribution list is shown against
   the published formula; any connection paying a different amount must be
   explained on the record.
2. **Duty check:** the roster is published; skipped turns and their cover
   charges are shown openly.
3. **Audit:** `06-FINANCE/04-Audit/` verifies equal application each cycle.
4. **Grievance:** any member can demand proof that a deviation followed a
   published rule; a factual demand cannot be voted away (P5).

## 8. EXAMPLES FROM COLONY LIFE

- **Wrong (old system):** The motor owner's brother was excused from monthly
  payments "because he helps sometimes." Under this rule, the brother pays the
  formula like everyone, and his help is compensated in time-credits — which
  is the honest way to reward help without free riding.
- **Correct:** A retired member with a back problem is shifted from lifting
  duty to log-keeping duty, still taking his roster turns. Burden and benefit
  stay equal in value.
- **Correct:** During summer rationing, every connection loses the same
  minutes by the published table; the member with a dialysis patient gets a
  documented hardship exception that any member can read and match.

---

*Equality here is equality of rules, not a ban on difference. Genuine need is
served by published, capped exceptions — never by private privilege.*
