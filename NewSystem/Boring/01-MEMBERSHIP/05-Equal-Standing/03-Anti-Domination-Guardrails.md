# ANTI-DOMINATION GUARDRAILS

**Folder:** `01-MEMBERSHIP/05-Equal-Standing/`

---

## 1. WHY GUARDRAILS

The old system failed because one person or one family gathered every lever —
keys, money, records, repairs, and membership — into a single hand. These
guardrails exist so that **no hand, and no family of hands, can ever grip all
the levers again**. P3 and P7 are their foundation.

## 2. THE THREE SEPARATIONS

| Separation | Rule |
|------------|------|
| **Keys / control** | Motor-room keys, schedule, and operations are held and rotated independently of finance (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/05-Key-and-Control/`) |
| **Money** | Cash and books are held by rotating finance handlers, never by the same person who holds the keys (`06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/`) |
| **Records** | The registry and minutes are kept by a separate rotating keeper, independent of both keys and money (`04-Member-Registry/02-Registry-Keeper-Rotation.md`) |

No single member or family may simultaneously hold, in any role: the keys, the
cash, and the registry.

## 3. NO FAMILY OR GROUP MAY HOLD ALL ROLES

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Role cap | No more than one of the three core roles (keys/duty coordinator, finance, registry) per household in the same period |
| Related persons | Two related households may not fill two core roles in the same period (cousins, in-laws, siblings count as related) |
| Neighbourhood blocks | A coalition may not arrange that all roles go to one street or one community; the lot system makes this hard and the assembly can void a plainly captured election |
| Term overlap | A role holder may not hand their role to a relative |

## 4. NO COALITION MAY DICTATE

1. A coalition that controls a simple majority cannot use it to:
   - change a member's dues individually,
   - cut one member's water,
   - silence a dissenting voice,
   - exempt its own members from duty,
   - or pass anything that violates a Fundamental Right.
2. Any such resolution is void on the face (P5) even before review.
3. A coalition that attempts to dominate a **permanent** minority (same street,
   same community, the "new families") faces the targeting rules of
   `00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/04-Right-to-Protection-from-Targeting.md`
   and `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/03-Protection-Mechanisms/`.

## 5. WATCH SIGNS OF DOMINATION

| Sign | What it means |
|------|---------------|
| The same 2-3 names in every role for years | Capture — rotation rules are being bypassed |
| Decisions taken in "informal meetings" that are really only one group | Illegitimate — only assembly process decides |
| A family holds the keys "because it always has" | Key violation (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/05-Key-and-Control/`) |
| Books that only one person can "explain" | Transparency failure — books must be readable by any literate member |
| New families "advised" not to attend meetings | Targeting — P7/P10 violation |
| Skilled members repeatedly "requested" to work free | Forced free labour — P8 violation |

## 7. WHAT A DOMINATED MEMBER SHOULD DO

1. **Document** the fact (who holds what, since when).
2. **Raise** it at the assembly as a formal concern; it is always agenda-worthy.
3. **File** a grievance if the assembly is captured
   (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/02-Resolution-Process/02-Step-2-Written-Grievance.md`).
4. **Veto** a specific wrong decision if it is factually/legally wrong
   (`00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/02-Single-Member-Truth-Veto.md`).
5. If the domination crosses into exclusion, threats, or seizure of common
   assets, the lawful-authority procedures in `11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/` apply.

## 8. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

For a decade the "Rajan group" ran the motor, collected money, and kept the
books. Under this constitution the first annual rotation draws: Meera gets the
keys term, Patel the cash, Shabana the registry — none of them related, none
from the Rajan family. When Rajan objects that "nobody knows how it works,"
the assembly points to the trained backups (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/02-Duty-Training/`).
The group's only remaining power is one vote each, exactly like everyone else.

## 9. COMPENSATION FOR ROLE-HOLDERS

- Rotating role-holders are compensated at published rates
  (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/03-Duty-Compensation/`). Rotation is not a punishment and
  service is not free labour — but neither does a stipend turn a role into a
  property that one family keeps.

---

*The guardrails do not distrust any one person; they distrust the design that
let one person become the system. When no one owns the levers, no one can be
oppressed by them.*
