# ONE MEMBER, ONE VOTE

**Folder:** `01-MEMBERSHIP/05-Equal-Standing/`

---

## 1. THE RULE

Exactly **one vote per registered connection**, held by the registered member
of that connection. No member may hold more than one vote, no household may
cast two votes, and no person may vote without being the registered member (or
a written delegate) of a registered connection. This is P2 made concrete.

## 2. THE UNIT OF VOTING

| Unit | Vote |
|------|------|
| Registered connection (regular or late joiner) | 1 vote |
| Founding member | 1 vote (same as all) |
| Tenant member | 0 votes of their own; may hold the dwelling's vote only by written owner delegation (`02-Types-of-Members/04-Tenant-Members.md`) |
| Two houses owned by one person | Each registered connection = 1 vote each, provided each household is separately registered; the person votes once per connection |
| Person with no connection | 0 votes (even if they paid money historically — `02-Types-of-Members/01-Founding-Members.md`) |
| Member on leave | 1 vote, exercisable by postal ballot or written household delegation |

## 3. HOW VOTING WORKS

1. Voting happens at the General Assembly, on the agenda published in advance
   (`02-GOVERNANCE/01-General-Assembly/`).
2. Only registered members (or written delegates) whose names match the registry
   may vote. The Registry Keeper reads the roll before the vote.
3. Methods: raised hands (recorded by the minute-taker), secret ballot for
   person-related matters (to prevent fear), or postal ballot for absent
   members. The method is fixed by the agenda.
4. Results are recorded in the minutes with the count: e.g., "19 in favour,
   3 against, 2 abstentions." Names in the count are not required and not
   recorded for secret ballots.
5. Quorum rules apply before a vote is valid
   (`02-GOVERNANCE/01-General-Assembly/02-Quorum-Rules.md`).

## 4. NO PROXY STACKING

| Practice | Status |
|----------|--------|
| One member collecting 5 neighbours' votes by signing for them | Prohibited — each member or written delegate votes in person |
| A landlord voting for all their rented houses without written delegation | Prohibited |
| A delegate carrying 10 written delegations and voting them all | Prohibited — one person may hold at most **one** delegation per connection and may not personally vote more than two votes in total (own + one delegation) |
| Same member voting twice under two connection IDs | Prohibited — Level 2 offence; both votes void if proven |
| Using a deceased member's name | Prohibited — the connection is transferred per `03-Exit-and-Transfer/` |

The limit exists so a single influential person cannot quietly become a
voting machine for half the colony — the old system's disease.

## 5. DELEGATION RULES

- Delegation is always **in writing**, signed and dated, naming the delegate
  and the period.
- Delegation can be revoked in writing at any time before the vote.
- Delegation is valid for the stated period only; a stale delegation is void.

## 6. COLLUSION PROHIBITED

1. Members may discuss and persuade freely — that is democracy.
2. Collusion means arranging votes to gain **unfair control**: buying votes,
  promising favours or water perks in exchange for votes, threatening voters,
  or forming a permanent bloc that dictates outcomes by numbers alone.
3. Buy-in to votes and favour-trading are Level 3 offences
   (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`).
4. A resolution passed through proven collusion can be voided by the CRB, and
   the matter re-voted under secret ballot.

## 7. VOTES AND TRUTH

- A vote decides **choices and preferences** — not facts and not rights.
- Facts (who paid, what the log says, what the formula yields) are decided by
  evidence, never by votes (`00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/04-No-Voting-on-Proven-Facts.md`).
- A single member's Truth Veto freezes a decision until evidence review
  (`00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/02-Single-Member-Truth-Veto.md`).

## 8. SPECIAL VOTING RULES

| Situation | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| Capital matters with unpaid late joiners | Late joiners' votes are counted but cannot alone overturn the position of fully-paid members (`02-Types-of-Members/03-Late-Joiners.md`) |
| Person-related decisions | Secret ballot to protect voters from pressure |
| Amendments to the constitution | Supermajority + Truth Review per `00-CONSTITUTION/05-Amendment-Process/` |
| Repressive amendments | Void regardless of votes (`00-CONSTITUTION/05-Amendment-Process/05-Prohibition-on-Repressive-Amendments.md`) |

## 9. EXAMPLES FROM COLONY LIFE

- **Correct:** At the June assembly, 24 members raise hands for the repair
  fund proposal, 3 against. The minute records the count. No one's name is
  used to pressure them later.
- **Wrong:** A prominent member collects 10 blank signatures "to save time"
  and votes them at the meeting. Each such vote is void; the matter is
  re-voted at the next assembly, and the collector faces a Level 2 case.
- **Correct:** The two widows of the colony vote by secret ballot on whether
  to hire a specific electrician, so no electrician's supporter can see how
  they voted.

---

*One connection, one member, one vote. Money cannot buy a second vote, age
cannot carry extra weight, and friendship cannot multiply a ballot.*
