# TENANT MEMBERS

**Folder:** `01-MEMBERSHIP/02-Types-of-Members/`

---

## 1. WHO IS A TENANT MEMBER

A **tenant member** is a person who rents a dwelling in the colony and receives
water through the registered connection of that dwelling. The **owner** of the
dwelling is the member with the vote; the tenant is a tenant member with water
rights and duties.

## 2. WATER RIGHTS OF A TENANT MEMBER

A tenant member has the same **usage rights** as any household:

| Right | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Water share | Same nominal share as every connection — tenants are not "guests" |
| Schedule information | Same published schedule, same channels |
| Fair treatment | No one may reduce a tenant's water to pressure the owner |
| Grievance | A tenant may file a grievance about water delivery in their own name |

The water right attaches to the **dwelling**, not to the person's name in the
registry. A house occupied by a tenant receives water like any house.

## 3. DUTIES OF A TENANT MEMBER

| Duty | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Monthly contribution | Paid by the owner under the rental agreement, OR by the tenant if the owner so directs in writing; the owner remains ultimately liable (Section 5) |
| Duty roster | A tenant household takes its duty turns like any household |
| Skill learning | Tenants learn the backup skills like all members |
| Conduct | No hoarding, no tampering, no misuse |

A tenant who performs duty earns time-credits/stipends exactly like any member
(`03-ROTATION-DUTY/03-Duty-Compensation/`).

## 4. VOTING — ONLY BY WRITTEN DELEGATION

1. A tenant has **no vote of their own**. The dwelling has one vote, held by
   the registered owner-member (P2: one member one vote).
2. The owner may **delegate** the dwelling's vote to the tenant **in writing**,
   signed and dated, for a stated period (e.g., the lease term or one year).
   The delegation is filed with the Registry Keeper and shown to any member on
   request.
3. A delegated vote is exercised by the tenant in the assembly. The owner must
   not delegate to more than one person at a time, and the delegation can be
   revoked by the owner in writing at any time.
4. A tenant may **never** vote when the owner has not delegated in writing.
   Voting without delegation is a Level 2 offence.

## 5. OWNER REMAINS LIABLE

| Obligation | Who is liable |
|------------|---------------|
| Monthly contribution | Owner always; tenant only if the owner delegated payment in writing |
| Buy-in on the dwelling | Owner (paid at purchase/registration) |
| Dues and arrears | Owner — arrears follow the connection, not the tenant |
| Damage caused by tenant | Owner first; tenant may be pursued by the owner under rental law |
| Duty | Tenant household when in occupation; if the house is vacant, the owner arranges coverage |

The owner cannot use a tenant as a shield against the system's rules, and the
tenant cannot be made to carry the owner's liabilities alone.

## 6. TENANT VACANCY AND CHANGE

- When a tenant leaves, the owner informs the Registry Keeper within **7 days**
  so the registry notes the change of occupant. The connection ID stays with
  the dwelling.
- A new tenant is registered as a tenant member on a simple occupancy note; no
  new buy-in is charged (the buy-in belongs to the dwelling, not the person).
- The owner remains the member of record throughout.

## 7. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

Mr. Rao rents a house whose owner lives in another city. Mr. Rao gets full
water, takes his duty turn, and is paid in time-credits. He has no vote until
the owner sends a signed one-year delegation. The monthly contribution is paid
by the owner from the rent; when Mr. Rao's household leaves mid-year, the owner
remains liable for the contribution. The next tenant, Ms. Khan, is registered
with the same connection ID and pays no buy-in.

## 8. TENANT PROTECTION

- No assembly resolution and no owner instruction can reduce a tenant's water
  share below the standard while the tenant occupies the dwelling.
- A tenant who is targeted because of a dispute between the owner and other
  members has the same protection from targeting as any member (P7) and may
  approach the Conflict Review Board directly.

---

*Tenants are full users, not second-class users. The only separation is the
vote, which follows the dwelling's registered owner — and that separation is
narrow and written, never discretionary.*
