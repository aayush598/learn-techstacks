# FOUNDING MEMBERS

**Folder:** `01-MEMBERSHIP/02-Types-of-Members/`

---

## 1. WHO IS A FOUNDING MEMBER

A **founding member** is a member whose contribution (money or in-kind) was part
of the original boring/motor installation. Their names are recorded in the
assembly's founding record and in the Member Registry under "phase" = Founding.

## 2. WHY THE OLD SYSTEM FAILED WITH FOUNDERS

In the broken old system, the people who paid for the boring treated it as
"their" borewell: they ran the motor when they pleased, refused keys, collected
money from everyone else, and forced skilled people to work free. The founding
role is therefore deliberately **stripped of all special power** in this
constitution.

## 3. THE RIGHTS OF A FOUNDING MEMBER

| Right | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Refund of capital over time** | A pro-rata return of the original capital contribution, repaid from the Asset Fund on the schedule in `06-FINANCE/05-Distribution-and-Refunds/` — as a fair repayment of a loan, not as a ticket to power |
| **Honour** | Their names appear on the founding record and are acknowledged at assemblies |
| **One vote** | Exactly the same single vote as every other member (P2) |
| **All standard rights** | Water, audit, protection, voice — identical to all members |

## 4. WHAT A FOUNDING MEMBER DOES NOT GET

| No power over | Because |
|---------------|---------|
| The motor switch or schedule | P3 — keys and schedules are rotated, never owned |
| The money | P6 — funds are managed by rotating treasurers with open books |
| Other members' water | P1 — water is common property |
| Repairs and vendors | P5 — facts and published procedures decide, not influence |
| Admissions | Open, non-discriminatory admission is a right |
| Any extra vote | P2 — equal power for all |

## 5. THE "NO SPECIAL CONTROL" RULE

1. A founding member who attempts to behave as the owner of the boring — by
   holding the keys permanently, deciding the schedule alone, collecting money
   for himself, or ordering skilled members to work free — is in violation of
   the constitution, whatever their original contribution was.
2. Such conduct is dealt with under the Enforcement Ladder
   (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`), not with
   gratitude. Gratitude is shown by honour; power is not a reward.
3. The capital contribution creates a **debt owed by the system**, repaid per
   the refund schedule — it does **not** create a share of control.

## 6. CAPITAL REPAYMENT

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| Basis | Pro-rata to the member's recorded original contribution |
| Source | Asset Fund / Fund Management rules (`06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/`) |
| Schedule | Published at founding; repayments begin once the Asset Fund is stable |
| Priority | Equal treatment of all founding members; no one's repayment jumps the queue |
| Status | Reported at every assembly; auditable |

The system may also choose, by resolution, to treat the original contribution
as a **donation** and skip repayment. That choice must be made openly and
cannot favour one founder over another.

## 7. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

Ramu contributed ₹15,000 when the boring was drilled and ran everything for ten
years. Under this constitution he is honoured on the founding record, receives
his ₹15,000 back on the published repayment schedule, and has exactly one vote
like everyone else. When his duty turn comes, he operates the motor under the
same roster and logs the same records as any other member. If he refuses to
hand over his keys at the end of his duty period, that is a key-security
violation (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/05-Key-and-Control/`), not something his old
contribution excuses.

## 8. FOUNDING MEMBERS WHO ARE NOT MEMBERS

- A person who contributed money but does not live in the colony (e.g., an
  investor-relative) is **not** a member. Their repayment is handled purely as
  a financial transaction per Section 6; they have no vote, no water rights,
  and no access to the motor room.

## 9. TRANSITION OF OLD FOUNDERS

- A founding member who was the old "controller" must sign the entry agreement
  and hand over all keys, books, and money within the transition period set by
  the founding assembly. Refusal is a Level 3 offence and is handled by the
  enforcement procedure, including, if necessary, the involvement of lawful
  authorities per `11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/`.

---

*The founding member's reward is a repaid loan and recorded honour — never a
throne. The boring belongs to everyone, or it belongs to no one.*
