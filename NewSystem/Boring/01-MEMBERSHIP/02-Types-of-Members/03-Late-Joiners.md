# LATE JOINERS

**Folder:** `01-MEMBERSHIP/02-Types-of-Members/`

---

## 1. WHO IS A LATE JOINER

A **late joiner** is a member who joined the system after the original
installation and whose buy-in is not yet fully paid. The moment the buy-in
reaches zero, they become a regular member
(`02-Regular-Members.md`). "Late" refers only to the payment of the capital
share — never to the member's standing, dignity, or water.

## 2. WATER ACCESS RULES

1. A late joiner **receives no water connection** until the buy-in amount is
   agreed and either paid or placed on a signed installment schedule
   (`01-Enrollment/02-Buy-in-Contribution.md`).
2. Once a schedule is signed, the connection is opened **immediately**. Water
   is never withheld as pressure to speed up payment — the installment
   schedule is the contract, and the Enforcement Ladder handles default, not
   water punishment without due process.
3. After connection, the late joiner's water share is exactly equal to every
   other member's. No "reduced water until paid" rule exists. P1 applies.

## 3. INSTALLMENT OPTION

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| Maximum period | 12 months |
| Frequency | Monthly, equal installments |
| Agreement | Written schedule signed before connection |
| Missing a payment | Follows the dues procedure in `06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/`; not an instant water cut |
| Early payment | Allowed at any time; reduces interest/penalties if any were agreed |

## 4. VOTING RULES FOR LATE JOINERS

1. A late joiner has **one vote**, equal in weight to every other member, from
   the day they are admitted. P2 applies fully.
2. **Limitation on capital matters:** while any buy-in balance is outstanding,
   a late joiner may not be the deciding factor in resolutions concerning
   capital expenditure, buy-in amounts, or asset disposal that would affect the
   members who already funded those assets.
3. This limitation is implemented as: on such resolutions, late joiners with
   outstanding balances vote, but their votes do not count toward the required
   majority if doing so would overturn the position supported by members with
   no outstanding balance. The exact counting rule is defined in
   `02-GOVERNANCE/03-Decision-Making/`.
4. This limitation is **strictly financial**. On every other matter — water,
   schedule, duties, conduct, enforcement, contributions for running costs —
   the late joiner's vote counts fully.
5. The limitation disappears permanently the day the buy-in reaches zero.

## 5. RIGHTS THAT ARE NEVER REDUCED FOR A LATE JOINER

| Right | Never reduced because |
|-------|----------------------|
| Water share | P1 — water is common property, not a commodity priced by timing |
| Vote on non-capital matters | P2 — equal power |
| Protection from targeting | P7 — applies to all |
| Audit and information | P6 — applies to all |
| Dignity | P10 — applies to all |
| Time-credits for duty | P8 — work is rewarded |

## 6. OBLIGATIONS THAT ARE THE SAME

| Obligation | Same as any member |
|------------|--------------------|
| Monthly contribution | Yes |
| Duty roster | Yes |
| Skill learning | Yes |
| Conduct | Yes |

## 7. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

The Verma family joins a year after the motor was installed. Buy-in: ₹24,000.
They sign a 12-month schedule of ₹2,000/month. They get full water from day one
and full votes on the schedule, duties, and repairs. But at an assembly where a
majority of fully-paid members oppose a new motor purchase, the Vermas' votes
cannot alone push the purchase through while their balance is outstanding.
After their 12th installment, no distinction remains.

## 8. ARREARS AND CONSEQUENCES

- Persistent default on the buy-in schedule follows the dues procedure,
  including the graduated consequences in
  `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`.
- A late joiner who refuses to sign any schedule but uses water is an
  unregistered user (Level 3 offence) until properly registered.
- The assembly cannot increase a late joiner's buy-in after their schedule is
  signed, nor forgive a schedule in a way that discriminates between members.
