# MEMBERS ON LEAVE

**Folder:** `01-MEMBERSHIP/02-Types-of-Members/`

---

## 1. WHO IS A MEMBER ON LEAVE

A **member on leave** is a member who is temporarily absent from the colony for
a genuine, documented reason — job posting in another city, prolonged illness,
hospitalisation, family emergency, or long travel. Leave is a status, granted
by published procedure, and never a privilege given to favourites.

## 2. GRANTING LEAVE

| Reason | Evidence | Maximum duration |
|--------|----------|------------------|
| Job posting / transfer | Offer letter or employer note | Up to 12 months, renewable |
| Illness / hospitalisation | Medical certificate | Up to 6 months, renewable |
| Family emergency | Written statement | Up to 6 months |
| Extended travel | Ticket/itinerary or statement | Up to 6 months |

1. The member informs the Registry Keeper in writing before departure, or
   within 7 days if sudden (accident, hospital).
2. Leave of up to **3 months** is approved administratively by the Registry
   Keeper and recorded. Leave beyond 3 months is noted at the next assembly.
3. Absence without any notice is not "leave" — the rules on duty failure and
   dues apply.

## 3. DUTY OBLIGATIONS DURING LEAVE

| Situation | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| Duty turn falls during approved leave | The turn is covered by the roster reserve (backup operator). No penalty to the member on leave. |
| House stays occupied (family remains) | The occupying household takes the duty turns as usual. |
| House is locked/vacant | The roster reserve covers it; the member may owe a cover charge per the duty compensation rules (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/03-Duty-Compensation/`). |

The member on leave is never charged for "refusing" duty if leave was properly
granted — this prevents the old-system habit of shaming people who had to go
work elsewhere.

## 4. CONTRIBUTIONS DURING LEAVE

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Monthly contribution | Still due. The connection still exists and the house may be used. Only the assembly (with published reason) may waive or reduce it, and then for all members equally in the same category. |
| Water while absent | The house's slot remains on the schedule; if the house is vacant, the water is not "lost" to a favourite — it is managed per the standard schedule (no hoarding, no gifting). |

## 5. VOTING WHILE ON LEAVE

1. A member on leave keeps their single vote (P2).
2. Voting may be exercised by **postal/absentee ballot** through the procedure
   in `02-GOVERNANCE/03-Decision-Making/`, or by written delegation to a
   designated adult of the household — one vote only, never split.
3. Leave must never be used to silence a member. Absence is not loss of rights.

## 6. RETURNING FROM LEAVE

| Step | Rule |
|------|------|
| Intimation | Member informs the Registry Keeper on return (or within 7 days) |
| Record | Leave status is closed in the registry |
| Duties | Member re-enters the roster at the next cycle; no "punishment" for the leave period |
| Arrears | Any dues that accrued during leave are payable per the normal dues procedure; a member in genuine hardship may request the hardship protocol |

## 7. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

Joseph is a mason who works six months a year in another district. He applies
for leave for the working season with his employer's letter. His duty turns are
covered by the reserve operator and his monthly contribution continues. At the
assembly voting on the repair fund, his postal ballot counts. When he returns,
he takes his next roster turn like everyone else. Nobody can brand him a
"non-contributor" — his contribution was on record all along.

## 8. ABUSE OF LEAVE

| Sign | Handling |
|------|----------|
| Repeated "leave" to avoid duty | Checked against departure evidence; unapproved absence is a duty failure |
| Leave to avoid paying dues | Dues still accrue; no waiver unless assembly-granted for all |
| Faking evidence (forged certificate) | Level 2 offence; leave cancelled with retroactive effect |

## 9. RELATION TO OTHER RULES

- Duty coverage: `03-ROTATION-DUTY/01-Duty-Roster/` (reserve system)
- Compensation for covered turns: `03-ROTATION-DUTY/03-Duty-Compensation/`
- Hardship relief: `06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/`

---

*Leave is a fact of life in Indian households — jobs, marriages, health. The
system handles it by rule, not by favour, so that a member who must go away
loses nothing but a few months of their own presence.*
