# REGULAR MEMBERS

**Folder:** `01-MEMBERSHIP/02-Types-of-Members/`

---

## 1. WHO IS A REGULAR MEMBER

A **regular member** is a member who:

1. Has been admitted by the assembly,
2. Has **fully paid** the buy-in (or joined at original installation as a
   founding member whose capital is treated as the buy-in), and
3. Is current on monthly contributions, duties, and conduct obligations.

"Regular" is a status, not a title of honour. It simply means the member holds
the full, undiluted rights of membership.

## 2. RIGHTS OF A REGULAR MEMBER

| Right | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Full water share | Standard slot rights, no discrimination |
| Full vote | One vote, equal to every member |
| Full duty participation | Time-credits and stipends at published rates |
| Full audit rights | Inspect all records at any time |
| Full protection | P7 protection from targeting and P10 dignity |

## 3. DUTIES OF A REGULAR MEMBER

| Duty | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Monthly contribution | On time, at the published formula |
| Rotating duty | Motor operation, logging, checks per roster |
| Skill learning | Learn at least the backup skills for their role |
| Transparency | Report what they observe, hold no secret books |
| Duty to the collective | Protect the common resource; no hoarding or tampering |

See `00-CONSTITUTION/03-Fundamental-Duties/` for the full set.

## 4. HOW A REGULAR MEMBER IS DIFFERENT FROM A LATE JOINER

| Aspect | Regular member | Late joiner |
|--------|----------------|-------------|
| Buy-in | Fully paid | Partially paid or on installments |
| Vote | Full vote | Full vote, but cannot outvote older members on capital matters while owing (see `03-Late-Joiners.md`) |
| Water | Full slot | Full slot (water is never reduced for owing buy-in) |
| Status transition | — | Becomes regular the moment the buy-in is fully paid |

## 5. HOW A REGULAR MEMBER IS DIFFERENT FROM A FOUNDING MEMBER

- None, in rights and duties. Both have one vote and full water.
- The only differences are financial: the founding member has a capital
  repayment entitlement (`01-Founding-Members.md` Section 6) and is recorded in
  the founding record.

## 6. LOSING REGULAR STATUS

A member stops being "regular" only by:

1. Falling into dues arrears beyond the grace period
   (`06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/`) — they remain a member but are in arrears,
   with the consequences defined there.
2. Becoming a member on leave (`05-Members-on-Leave.md`).
3. Exiting (`03-Exit-and-Transfer/01-Voluntary-Exit.md`).
4. An enforcement action under the ladder
   (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`).

Regular status is **not** lost by disagreement, by filing a grievance, by being
a minority voice, or by criticising a committee. Punishing dissent by changing
status is itself an offence (P5, P10).

## 7. RECORD

- Regular status is recorded in the Member Registry under "buy-in status"
  (`04-Member-Registry/01-Registry-Format.md`).
- The status changes automatically to "regular" when the buy-in balance
  reaches zero and the Registry Keeper updates the row with the final receipt.

## 8. EXAMPLE FROM COLONY LIFE

Meera joined six years after the boring was drilled, paid her ₹24,000 buy-in in
monthly installments, and on the final receipt was marked regular. She now
serves as a duty operator, earns time-credits, and votes exactly as the
founding members do. When she raised a complaint about the schedule, nobody
could label her a "second-class member" — regular status is equal status.

---

*Regular membership is the normal state of a full member. It is earned by
fair payment, kept by fair conduct, and never sold or gifted by any group.*
