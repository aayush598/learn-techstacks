# MEMBERSHIP — OVERVIEW

**Folder:** `01-MEMBERSHIP/`

---

## 1. WHO IS A MEMBER

A **member** is the registered connection holder of a household that receives
water from the system and is current on all obligations (contribution, duty,
conduct). A person is a member only after:

1. The household is verified and admitted by the General Assembly
   (`01-Enrollment/01-New-Member-Admission.md`).
2. The buy-in is settled or the installment schedule is signed
   (`01-Enrollment/02-Buy-in-Contribution.md`).
3. The member's entry agreement is signed
   (`01-Enrollment/04-Entry-Agreement.md`).
4. The member is entered in the Member Registry with a connection ID
   (`04-Member-Registry/01-Registry-Format.md`).

## 2. WHAT MEMBERSHIP GRANTS

| Grant | Detail | Source |
|-------|--------|--------|
| **Water right** | A fair, predictable share of water per the published schedule | `00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/01-Right-to-Fair-Water.md` |

| **Protection** | Protection from targeting, humiliation, and exclusion by any coalition | `00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/04-Right-to-Protection-from-Targeting.md` |

## 5. HOW MEMBERSHIP IS LOST

Membership is lost only by:

1. **Voluntary exit** — written notice plus clearance of dues
   (`03-Exit-and-Transfer/01-Voluntary-Exit.md`).
2. **Sale of the house** — connection passes to the new owner only after their
   own admission and buy-in (`03-Exit-and-Transfer/04-Selling-House-to-New-Owner.md`).
3. **Enforcement** — exclusion of water, never of person, only through the full
   Enforcement Ladder with due process
   (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`).

Membership is **never** lost by: caste, religion, language, gender, occupation,
politics, poverty, old disputes, or the will of a majority. See Section 6.

## 6. MEMBERSHIP IS OPEN AND NON-DISCRIMINATORY

1. Any household physically connected to the colony water network may apply.
2. Admission grounds are only those in
   `01-Enrollment/01-New-Member-Admission.md`. No other ground may be added.
3. A new member enjoys the same water share, the same vote, the same duties,
   and the same protections as the oldest founding member. P1 and P2 apply from
   day one.
4. Any admission decision based on caste, religion, gender, occupation, or
   personal animosity is **void** and a serious offence under the Enforcement
   Ladder.
