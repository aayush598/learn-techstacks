# TRUTH SUPREMACY 3 — OBJECTION TO A WRONG RESOLUTION

**Folder:** `00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/`

---

## 1. PURPOSE

This document governs what happens when a resolution **already passed** by the
assembly (or a committee action) is later shown to be wrong. It protects against
the case where a wrong rule or modification has been voted in and someone
discovers the error afterward.

## 2. WHEN A PASSED RESOLUTION CAN BE CHALLENGED

A member may object to a passed resolution if it:

1. Was passed without quorum, notice, or a required vote class.
2. Violates a Fundamental Right or the basic structure.
3. Contradicts an existing rule that was in force at the time.
4. Was based on proven false information that the passers did not know.
5. Was passed by excluding or not hearing an affected member (the "absence
   rule").

## 3. PROCEDURE

| Step | Action | Time |
|------|--------|------|
| 1 | Member files written objection citing ground | Any time, no expiry for right/basic-structure violations; 30 days for procedural defects |
| 2 | CRB issues suspension of implementation if harm is possible | Within 24 hours |
| 3 | CRB review (evidence-based) | ≤ 7 days |
| 4 | If upheld: resolution is declared **void ab initio** (never legally existed) | Immediately |
| 5 | Restore the situation (reversal, refund, re-run) | As per restoration rules |

## 4. VOID vs SUSPENDED

- **Void ab initio:** the resolution is treated as never passed. Everything done
  under it is reversed where possible.
- **Suspended:** implementation stops until the review completes.
- A resolution can also be **partially void** if only one clause is wrong; the
  rest stands.

## 5. NO STATUTE OF LIMITATIONS ON RIGHT VIOLATIONS

- A resolution that violates the basic structure can be challenged **at any
  time**, even years later, because rights are never forfeited by time.

## 6. RESPONSIBILITY OF THE OBJECTOR

- The objector must act in good faith and provide evidence or citation.
- If the objection is upheld, the objector is entitled to a small
  **truth-teller recognition** (credit/acknowledgment) to encourage honest
  challenge.
- If the objection is rejected, no penalty to the objector (see
  `05-Right-to-Voice-and-Objection.md`).
