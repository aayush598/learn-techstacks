# TRUTH SUPREMACY 2 — SINGLE-MEMBER TRUTH VETO

**Folder:** `00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/`

---

## 1. WHAT IT IS

A **Truth Veto** is a formal objection by **any single member** that a proposed
decision or action is:

1. **Factually false** (contradicts evidence), or
2. **Rule-violating** (contradicts an existing rule), or
3. **Right-violating** (infringes a Fundamental Right).

Once filed, the proposed decision is **suspended** until a review determines the
matter. Numbers do not matter during the suspension.

## 2. HOW TO FILE IT

- In person or in writing at any assembly, or to the Conflict Review Board (CRB).
- Must state: (a) the decision objected to, (b) the reason (fact/rule/right),
  (c) the supporting evidence or citation.
- Must be recorded in the minutes and dated. A verbal veto at a meeting is
  recorded by the minute-taker on the spot.

## 3. EFFECT

| Status | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Immediately on filing | The decision/action is **frozen** (status quo). No implementation, no partial implementation, no "just for now". |
| During review | The CRB investigates evidence. Max 7 days for facts; 14 days if documents from outside are needed. |
| If veto upheld | Decision is **void**. If already partially implemented, restoration/rebalancing happens per `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/04-Right-vs-Majority-Procedures/05-Restoration-and-Rebalance.md`. |
| If veto rejected | Decision may proceed, but only after the full review — and the vetoing member's dissent stays recorded without penalty. |

## 4. THE REVIEW — EVIDENCE, NOT VOTES

The review is conducted by the CRB (or a 3-person lot-drawn panel if CRB is
conflicted). It answers:

- Is the fact provable? What do logs, receipts, photos, timestamps, witnesses say?
- Is the rule correctly cited? Is the citation accurate and applicable?
- Is the right genuinely engaged?

The panel's decision must be reasoned and written. A popularity poll is never a
review.

## 5. WHO CANNOT FILE IT

- A member cannot veto a decision merely because they **dislike** it, prefer
  another option, or want more water. Preference differences go to normal
  voting.
- A member with an **ongoing retaliation order** against them cannot veto
  decisions about their own pending case (to prevent abuse), but may still file
  a grievance through the normal process.

## 6. ABUSE AND MISUSE

| Sign | Handling |
|------|----------|
| Repeated vetoes without basis | Warning; then Level 1 obstruction consequence; veto privileges for that member restricted to written form with prior warning. |
| Veto used to delay repair/water for many members | CRB may fast-track review and, if baseless, the veto is set aside and the objector warned. |
| Veto used to protect own wrongdoing | Treated as obstruction; the underlying wrongdoing proceeds. |

## 7. THE RULE OF ONE

Remember: **one member, one veto, on the strength of evidence.** This is the
constitutional guarantee that the honest single voice can never be drowned out
by a crowd — the exact problem the user described when designing this system.
