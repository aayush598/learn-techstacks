# TRUTH SUPREMACY 4 — NO VOTING ON PROVEN FACTS

**Folder:** `00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/`

---

## 1. THE RULE

**A proven fact is not open to vote.** Once evidence establishes what happened,
the only remaining question is what the rules say should happen next — and that
is also not a vote; the rules are applied.

## 2. WHAT COUNTS AS A PROVEN FACT

| Type | Evidence | Example |
|------|----------|---------|
| Time of an event | Log entry + photo timestamp + independent witness | "Motor was off at 6:32" |
| A payment | Bank statement / receipt / UPI reference | "₹2,300 repair was paid" |
| A quantity | Meter reading / gauge / litres filled | "House X received 900L" |
| A condition | Photo/video + inspection | "Pipe leaked at junction J" |
| A communication | Message/screenshot/notice copy | "Notice was posted on 05 June" |

## 3. WHAT HAPPENS IF SOMEONE TRIES TO VOTE ON A FACT

1. The chair (or any member) stops the vote: "This is a matter of fact, not of
   preference. We will review evidence."
2. The evidence review proceeds per `02-Single-Member-Truth-Veto.md`.
3. Any "resolution" passed by voting on a fact is **void** under
   `03-Objection-to-Wrong-Resolution.md`.

## 4. FACTS vs JUDGEMENTS (THE BOUNDARY)

| This is a fact (no vote) | This is a judgement (vote allowed) |
|--------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| The pump failed at 5pm | Whether to repair now or rent a pump |
| The bill is ₹4,100 | Whether to pay from repair fund or special collection |
| House B got 40 min extra | Whether to deduct the extra from B's next slot or let it pass |
| X posted the notice | Whether the notice period was sufficient |

## 5. EVIDENCE DISPUTES

- If two members present contradictory evidence, the CRB evaluates authenticity
  (timestamps, witnesses, logs, chain of custody) and decides on the **preponderance
  of credible evidence** — not on who has more friends.
- Disputed entries in a register: the entry stands unless there is independent
  corroboration against it. A member who wants to correct a log must provide the
  same standard of evidence.

## 6. WHY THIS MATTERS

Every abuse described at this system's creation — "motor wasn't starting",
"we never said 7am", "we didn't get the money", "they got extra water" — is an
attempt to turn a **fact question** into a **power question**. This rule makes
that impossible.
