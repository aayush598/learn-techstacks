# TRUTH SUPREMACY 1 — THE PRINCIPLE OF TRUTH OVER MAJORITY

**Folder:** `00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/`

---

## 1. THE PRINCIPLE

**A fact, a rule, or a right cannot be defeated by counting heads.**

In this system:
- **Facts** are decided by **evidence**, never by vote.
- **Rules** are applied **as written**, never overruled by a show of hands.
- **Rights** are **absolute**, never diluted by a majority's convenience.
- **Fairness for one member** is decided with that member present and with
  reasons, never by a crowd voting in their absence.

This is the single most important design decision in this constitution. It
exists because the problem this system was built to solve is precisely a
situation where a person or group with numbers/control dominated everyone else.

## 2. THREE CONCRETE RULES

| Rule | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **No voting on facts** | "Was the motor turned off at 6:30?" is answered by the log and witnesses, not by ballot. |
| **No voting against a rule** | "Can we skip the buy-in for X?" — only an amendment can change a rule, and even amendments cannot violate the basic structure. |
| **No voting over a right** | "Can we cut X's water for 3 days as punishment?" — no. Punishments follow the Enforcement Ladder, which never includes arbitrary water denial. |

## 3. WHY THE MAJORITY CAN BE WRONG

Majorities are wrong when they are:
- **Misled** — given false or partial facts.
- **Emotional** — acting in anger at a meeting.
- **Coalition-driven** — a few people agreeing to dominate the rest.
- **Self-interested** — voting to benefit themselves at another's cost.
- **Conformist** — unwilling to be the lone dissenter in front of others.

This constitution assumes none of these will happen. It protects against all of
them.

## 4. THE POWER OF THE LONE CORRECT MEMBER

- One member with a **provable fact** or a **cited rule** can stop any wrong
  decision, no matter how many support it.
- The mechanism is the **Truth Veto** (`02-Single-Member-Truth-Veto.md`).
- The burden on that member is only: *show the evidence or cite the rule.* That
  is deliberately small, because in the real-world scenarios this system was
  built for, the lone truth-teller was usually a normal person with a receipt,
  a log, or a calendar.

## 5. LIMITS (SO THIS POWER IS NOT ABUSED)

- The Truth Veto applies to **facts, existing rules, and fundamental rights** —
  not to personal preferences.
- A veto triggers a **review by evidence**, not an automatic victory.
- A member who repeatedly blocks without basis receives a warning and then a
  Level 1 consequence (obstruction), but is never silenced.

## 6. THE TEST FOR ANY DISPUTED DECISION

Before any vote, the chair must confirm:
1. Is this a question of **fact**? → Evidence review, no vote.
2. Is this a question of **applying an existing rule**? → Apply the rule, no
   vote.
3. Is this a question of **a fundamental right**? → The right stands, no vote.
4. Is this a genuine **choice between valid options** (schedule, budget,
   vendor, project)? → Then, and only then, vote (with minority rights intact).
