# FUNDAMENTAL RIGHT 4 — RIGHT TO PROTECTION FROM TARGETING

**Folder:** `00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/`

---

## 1. THE RIGHT

Every member, especially an individual or small group, has the right to be
**free from targeting, coercion, punishment, or disadvantage** imposed by a
larger group, a coalition, or a dominant individual.

## 2. WHAT CONSTITUTES TARGETING (NON-EXHAUSTIVE)

| Act | Example |
|-----|---------|
| Water denial | Cutting a household's slot, reducing its run, or delaying its repair "because they misbehaved". |
| Overburdening | Assigning a member duty every day while others get none. |
| False charges | Spreading fabricated complaints against a member to isolate them. |
| Social boycott | Refusing to speak, attend, or help a member as a collective punishment. |
| Blocking requests | Rejecting a member's legitimate event-water or repair request without written reasons. |
| Financial targeting | Increasing only one member's charges, or delaying only their refunds. |
| Sabotage | Damaging a member's connection, meter, or pipe. |
| Information denial | Excluding a member from notices so they miss slots or meetings. |

## 3. PROTECTIVE GUARANTEES

1. **No collective punishment.** A wrong by one member is addressed against that
   member only — never against their household, family, or group.
2. **Written-reasons rule.** Any decision that reduces a member's water, funds,
   or rights must be in writing, with reasons, and appealable.
3. **Presence rule.** Any decision about a specific member's water/duty/funds is
   void if that member was not notified and heard.
4. **Anonymous channel.** A member who fears retaliation may report through the
   protected channel (`02-GOVERNANCE/04-Transparency/04-Whistleblower-Channel.md`).
5. **No retaliation.** Retaliating against a complainant doubles the original
   offence's penalty.
6. **Immediate restoration.** Where targeting has cut water, water is restored
   within 24 hours pending investigation; investigation cannot suspend water.

## 4. PROCEDURE WHEN TARGETING IS ALLEGED

1. Member files grievance (or uses anonymous channel).
2. Conflict Review Board (CRB) issues a **status-quo order** within 24 hours:
   nothing that worsens the situation may be done.
3. Full hearing within 7 days under `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/02-Resolution-Process/`.
4. If targeting is proven, the targets are restored and the offenders face the
   Enforcement Ladder.

## 5. SPECIAL RULE FOR COALITIONS

- If a group of members is proven to be acting together to target a member,
  each participant faces the penalty individually — numbers do not dilute
  liability. "Everyone was doing it" is not a defence.

## 6. NON-NEGOTIABLE

This right is part of the basic structure.
