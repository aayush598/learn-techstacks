# FUNDAMENTAL RIGHT 1 — RIGHT TO FAIR WATER

**Folder:** `00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/`

---

## 1. THE RIGHT

Every member has the absolute right to receive a **fair, predictable share of
water** according to published rules, without discrimination, and without any
other member being able to cut, reduce, delay, or block it.

## 2. WHAT "FAIR" MEANS

1. **Same nominal share** — every connection has the same scheduled slot rights,
   adjusted only by documented physical factors (tap level, pipe distance) via
   the compensation rules in `09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/04-Network-Improvements/01-Tap-Height-Compensation.md`.
2. **Same information** — every member receives the same schedule, at the same
   time, through the same channels. No advance-only-for-friends.
3. **Same rules** — the same rules for everyone: no special timing, special
   volume, or special exemptions for anyone except through the published
   hardship/medical protocol.
4. **Rotation of advantage** — if slots are physically unequal (first slot gets
   more pressure), slot order rotates so advantage is shared over time
   (`04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/02-Seasonal-Rotation/03-Slot-Fairness-Compensation.md`).

## 3. PROTECTED ELEMENTS

| Element | Guarantee |
|---------|-----------|
| Predictability | Schedule published at least 7 days in advance; changes only by published procedure with notice. |
| Quantity | No member may reduce another's water except by authorized rationing during scarcity (`10-RISK-AND-EMERGENCY/01-Drought-and-Scarcity/`). |
| Timing | No member may move another's slot. Only the assembly (or the published rotation algorithm) may alter timing. |
| Continuity | Breakdowns must be communicated to all and repaired under the repair timeline (`05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/02-Breakdown-Repair/04-Repair-Timeline-and-Penalties.md`). |

## 4. SPECIAL ACCESS FOR GENUINE NEED

- Medical need (e.g., a member on dialysis) and hardship cases are handled by
  the published hardship protocol — **public, verifiable, temporary, and open
  to all equally** — never by one person's favor.
- The hardship protocol is defined in `04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/01-Daily-Schedule/01-Standard-Schedule.md`.

## 5. ENFORCEMENT

- Violation of this right is a **serious offence** (Enforcement Ladder
  Level 3+) — see `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`.
- Any member whose water is unfairly cut can invoke the immediate-restoration
  procedure in `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/03-Protection-Mechanisms/05-Individual-Economic-Protection.md`.

## 6. NON-NEGOTIABLE

This right is part of the basic structure (Master Constitution Section 4). It
can never be removed or diluted by any amendment.
