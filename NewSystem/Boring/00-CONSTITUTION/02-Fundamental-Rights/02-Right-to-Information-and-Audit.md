# FUNDAMENTAL RIGHT 2 — RIGHT TO INFORMATION AND AUDIT

**Folder:** `00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/`

---

## 1. THE RIGHT

Every member has the absolute right to **see, copy, and verify every record**
of the system: money, duty, motor logs, schedules, minutes, vendor quotes,
contracts, government documents, and this constitution itself.

## 2. WHAT MUST BE AVAILABLE

| Record | Where | Update frequency |
|--------|-------|------------------|
| Cash book / ledger | `06-FINANCE/03-Bookkeeping/01-Ledger-Format.md` | Every transaction |
| Motor run log | `04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/04-Monitoring-and-Logs/01-Motor-Run-Log.md` | Every run |
| Duty roster + log | `03-ROTATION-DUTY/01-Duty-Roster/` | Every duty |
| Meeting minutes | `02-GOVERNANCE/01-General-Assembly/05-Meeting-Records.md` | Every meeting |
| Vendor quotes | `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/04-Vendor-Management/02-Multiple-Quotes-Rule.md` | Every repair |
| Bank statements | Joint account — any member may request a copy | Monthly |
| Contracts | Asset/project files | As created |

## 3. ACCESS RULES

1. Records are physically kept in the **common register box** (unlocked, in a
   common area) and digitally backed up.
2. Any member may inspect during any working hour. No appointment required.
3. Any member may take photos/copies.
4. Digital copies are shared in the members-only group weekly.
5. **Denial of access** by any holder of records is an offence (Level 2),
   regardless of the reason. The records are not the holder's private property.

## 4. RIGHT TO VERIFY THE TRUTH

- A member who suspects a record is wrong may:
  1. Request the underlying evidence (receipt, photo, timestamp).
  2. Demand a **fact review** by the Conflict Review Board.
  3. If facts are proven wrong, the record is corrected and the error is
     recorded — a repeated, deliberate falsification is an offence (Level 4).

## 5. ANNUAL INDEPENDENT AUDIT

- At least once a year, an external audit (by a member from another colony or a
  professional at a modest fee) verifies the books. Results are read at the
  assembly and posted publicly.

## 6. NON-NEGOTIABLE

The right to information is part of the basic structure. It can never be
restricted by amendment, emergency, or "committee secrecy".
