# FUNDAMENTAL RIGHT 3 — RIGHT TO EQUAL POWER

**Folder:** `00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/`

---

## 1. THE RIGHT

Every member has the right to **exactly equal decision power** — one vote —
and the right that **no member or group holds permanent authority** over the
system.

## 2. GUARANTEES

1. **One member, one vote.** No role, wealth, age, seniority, or skill grants
   extra votes.
2. **No permanent roles.** All roles rotate and carry fixed term limits
   (`02-GOVERNANCE/02-Committees/06-Term-Limits-and-Rotation.md`).
3. **No veto by the wealthy.** Money buys shares, refunds, and benefits — never
   votes or control.
4. **No "founder privilege."** Founding members are honoured by refund of their
   capital over time (`09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/`), but they hold the same
   single vote as everyone else.
5. **Every member eligible for every duty.** Duty is drawn by lot, not chosen by
   "important people." Everyone who draws a duty does it or arranges a swap.
6. **No bloc rule.** A family with multiple connections gets one vote per
   connection, but cannot use multiple memberships to multiply voting power
   beyond their actual connections, and collusion to dominate voting is
   prohibited (`02-GOVERNANCE/05-Rotation-and-Fairness/03-No-Permanent-Role.md`).

## 3. WHAT EQUAL POWER FORBIDS

- A "president", "head", "in-charge", or "manager" with decision authority.
  Roles are facilitators/coordinators only; they execute assembly decisions.
- Anyone holding the keys, the ledger, the mobile number of the plumber, and the
  bank passbook **all at once** (separation of control).
- Any meeting where decisions are made without quorum or without recording
  votes.
- Any change in schedule, charges, or rules by one person "for convenience".

## 4. ENFORCEMENT

- A member who accumulates control is corrected by automatic reallocation of
  roles and access.
- Any decision made through unequal power is void and must be re-done.

## 5. NON-NEGOTIABLE

This right is part of the basic structure.
