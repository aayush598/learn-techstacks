# FUNDAMENTAL RIGHT 5 — RIGHT TO VOICE AND OBJECTION

**Folder:** `00-CONSTITUTION/02-Fundamental-Rights/`

---

## 1. THE RIGHT

Every member has the right to:
1. **Speak** at any assembly or process on any matter.
2. **Propose** resolutions, agenda items, and amendments.
3. **Object** to any decision, including the Truth Veto (`00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/02-Single-Member-Truth-Veto.md`).
4. **Appeal** any decision that affects them.
5. **Be heard without fear** — no penalty, ridicule, or retaliation for speaking.

## 2. BOUNDARIES OF THE RIGHT

- The right to speak does not include the right to disrupt, shout, or repeat
  the same point beyond a reasonable time (meeting conduct rules in
  `02-GOVERNANCE/01-General-Assembly/04-Meeting-Conduct.md`).
- The right to object requires a genuine basis: a fact, a rule, or a right.
  Objecting merely to obstruct is an offence (Level 1) after a warning.

## 3. THE OBJECTION LADDER

| Step | Action | Outcome |
|------|--------|---------|
| 1 | Speak at the meeting | Point is heard and discussed |
| 2 | Formal written objection | Must be entered in minutes; no silent burial |
| 3 | Truth Veto | Decision blocked until fact/fairness review |
| 4 | Grievance / CRB | Formal adjudication |
| 5 | Appeal to lot-drawn jury | Final internal hearing |
| 6 | Arbitration / legal | External finality (`11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/04-Dispute-Adjudication/`) |

## 4. NO PENALTY FOR OBJECTING

- Filing an objection or grievance is never itself an offence.
- If an objection is found baseless, the only consequence is the normal closure
  of the process — **no fine, no extra duty, no water penalty**.
- Penalizing a person for complaining is retaliation (see
  `04-Right-to-Protection-from-Targeting.md`) and doubles penalties.

## 5. MINORITY VOICE AT ASSEMBLY

- A member (or minority) opposing a motion has the right to have their dissent
  recorded in the minutes with their name and reasons.
- If the minority's objection is based on a provable fact, the motion cannot
  proceed until the fact is checked (Truth Veto).

## 6. NON-NEGOTIABLE

This right is part of the basic structure.
