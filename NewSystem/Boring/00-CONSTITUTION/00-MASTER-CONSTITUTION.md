# MASTER CONSTITUTION OF THE COLONY WATER SUPPLY SYSTEM (BORING)

**Document ID:** CWSS-CONST-001  
**Status:** SUPREME LAW OF THE SYSTEM  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** Date of adoption by the founding General Assembly  
**Amendability:** Only through the process defined in `05-Amendment-Process/`

---

## 1. WHY THIS CONSTITUTION EXISTS

This constitution governs the ownership, operation, maintenance, finance, and
governance of the colony borewell (boring) water supply system. It exists to
guarantee that:

1. **Water reaches every household fairly and equally.**
2. **No single person or group can control, exploit, or rule over the rest.**
3. **Every person who benefits contributes, and every person who contributes
   benefits proportionally.**
4. **Truth and justice win even when one lone member is right and everyone
   else is wrong.**
5. **The system survives any single person's departure, refusal, or sabotage.**
6. **Every present and future situation has a pre-written answer, so no one
   can misuse confusion or silence.**

This document is the single, blind-trust source of truth. All other documents
in this resource derive their authority from this constitution. Nothing in any
subordinate document may contradict it.

## 2. THE SUPREME PRINCIPLES

| # | Principle | Meaning |
|---|-----------|---------|
| P1 | **Water is common property** | Water from the boring belongs to the collective, never to an individual, family, or group. |
| P2 | **Equal power for all** | Every member has exactly one vote. No permanent leader, no ruler, no boss. |
| P3 | **No single point of failure** | Every duty, key, role, and skill must have backups, rotation, and cross-training. |
| P4 | **Contribution is mandatory and fair** | Anyone who benefits must contribute time, money, or skill. Work done is compensated; no one works free forever. |
| P5 | **Truth over majority** | Facts and fairness cannot be voted away. A right objection wins even against 99 members. |
| P6 | **Radical transparency** | Every rupee, every decision, every log is open to every member. No secret books. |
| P7 | **Protection of the weak** | No coalition may target, isolate, or punish an individual or small group. |
| P8 | **Work is rewarded, not exploited** | Time, skill, and effort are paid in credits and/or money at a fair, published rate. |
| P9 | **Self-sufficiency** | This constitution answers every foreseeable situation. Gaps are filled by interim rules that themselves respect P1-P8. |
| P10 | **Dignity of every member** | No member may be ridiculed, excluded, or silenced. Every voice gets a full hearing. |

## 4. THE NON-NEGOTIABLE (BASIC STRUCTURE) OF THIS CONSTITUTION

The following may **never** be amended, in whole or in part, by any process:

1. **Water is common property and no individual or group may own or control it.**
2. **Every member has equal voting power (one member, one vote).**
3. **A member's proven right prevails over any majority or coalition.**
4. **Every member who receives water must contribute to the system.**
5. **Every member's right to inspect all records is absolute.**
6. **No member may be excluded from water as punishment without the full
   due-process ladder defined in `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/`.**

Any amendment attempting to touch these is **void ab initio** (null from the
start), regardless of how many votes it receives.

## 5. HOW TO USE THIS RESOURCE

1. Read this Master Constitution first.
2. For any situation, find the matching folder in the index at the root
   `README.md`, then read all files in the relevant subfolder.
3. If two documents seem to conflict, apply **Part VI** (`06-Supremacy-and-Conflict`)
   which defines the priority order.
4. If the situation is genuinely new, apply `00-CONSTITUTION/06-Supremacy-and-Conflict/03-Interim-Rules-During-Gaps.md`
   and begin a formal amendment proposal under Part V.
5. When in doubt, ask: *"Does my proposed action respect P1-P10?"* If not, it is
   forbidden regardless of procedural technicalities.
