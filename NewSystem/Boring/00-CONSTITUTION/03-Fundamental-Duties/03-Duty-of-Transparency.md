# FUNDAMENTAL DUTY 3 — DUTY OF TRANSPARENCY

**Folder:** `00-CONSTITUTION/03-Fundamental-Duties/`

---

## 1. THE DUTY

Every member, **especially any member handling money, records, keys, or
decisions**, must act and record with **complete transparency** at all times.

## 2. SPECIFIC DUTIES

| Situation | What transparency requires |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| Handling cash/money | Never handle cash; direct all payments to the bank account or issue a numbered receipt immediately. |
| Keeping records | Complete every log entry at the time of the event, signed, never "from memory later". |
| Attending vendors | Never negotiate with a vendor privately; all quotes are obtained by at least two members together and compared in writing. |
| Receiving favours | A member must never accept gifts/kickbacks from vendors; disclosure is mandatory. |
| Making decisions | Decisions happen at meetings with minutes; "I decided" is not allowed. |
| Signing for the system | Only with a prior assembly resolution and dual signature. |
| Communicating | Notices go to all members, not a chosen few. |

## 3. THE RECORDING RULE

- **If it is not written, it did not happen.** Money, runs, duties, repairs,
  decisions, and notices are all recorded at the time they occur.
- Records are written in the standard templates (`12-TEMPLATES/`) so nothing is
  missed.

## 4. CONFLICT OF INTEREST RULES

1. A member with a financial interest in a vendor (own shop, relative's shop)
   must disclose it before any vendor discussion involving that vendor.
2. The interested member may provide the service but is excluded from approving
   their own payment; payment approval needs another signatory.
3. Undisclosed interest is an offence (Level 3).

## 5. CONSEQUENCES OF OPAQUENESS

- Any unrecorded transaction is treated as **suspected misappropriation** and
  triggers an audit and CRB review.
- Falsifying or destroying records is a Level 4 offence with mandatory refund +
  removal from all roles.

## 6. WHY THIS DUTY EXISTS

- Secrecy is the root of every abuse described when this system was created:
  hidden charges, inflated repairs, hidden timing changes, favouritism.
- Transparency converts trust from "belief in a person" to "verification of
  records" — which works even when people are untrustworthy.
