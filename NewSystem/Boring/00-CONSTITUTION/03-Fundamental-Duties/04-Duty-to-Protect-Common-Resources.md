# FUNDAMENTAL DUTY 4 — DUTY TO PROTECT COMMON RESOURCES

**Folder:** `00-CONSTITUTION/03-Fundamental-Duties/`

---

## 1. THE DUTY

Every member must **protect and preserve** the common resources of the system:
water, the boring, the motor, the pipes, the tanks, the records, and the funds.

## 2. WHAT THIS INCLUDES

| Resource | Duties |
|----------|--------|
| **Water** | Report leaks, overflowing tanks, and wastage; don't let taps run needlessly; don't water-waste during shortage. |
| **Motor & panel** | Follow the correct start/stop procedure; report unusual sound/smoke/gauge readings immediately. |
| **Pipes & taps** | Report bursts/leaks with location and photo; don't tamper with others' connections or meters. |
| **Records** | Handle registers carefully; never remove, alter, or destroy any record. |
| **Funds** | Spend only per approved budget; demand receipts; report suspected misuse. |
| **Pump house** | Keep locked as per key rules; report intruders or damage. |

## 3. REPORTING, NOT REPAIRING ALONE

- A member who finds a problem should **report immediately** through the
  fault-report channel (`05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/02-Breakdown-Repair/01-Fault-Reporting.md`).
- Repairing a common asset on one's own without authorization is allowed only
  for **urgent safety** reasons and must be reported the same day for
  reimbursement review — never assumed to be "not my job, not my water".

## 4. NO TAMPERING

Tampering with meters, pipes, valves, or the panel to gain more water or to
harm another is a **serious offence** (Level 4):
- Mandatory restitution,
- CRB hearing,
- Referral to legal action for criminal tampering where applicable.

## 5. NEIGHBOURHOOD WATCH ON ASSETS

- The duty roster includes a **daily asset check** (quick look at pump house,
  pipes, tank) performed by that day's duty operator and logged.

## 6. WHY THIS DUTY EXISTS

- A small leak ignored today becomes a ruined motor tomorrow, paid by everyone.
- Protecting common resources is the cheapest repair of all.
