# FUNDAMENTAL DUTY 2 — DUTY TO LEARN AND PARTICIPATE

**Folder:** `00-CONSTITUTION/03-Fundamental-Duties/`

---

## 1. THE DUTY

Every member must **learn the essential operations of the system** and
**participate** in its running. A system run by everyone cannot function if
members refuse to learn or attend.

## 2. WHAT EVERY MEMBER MUST LEARN (MANDATORY MINIMUM)

1. How to switch the motor **on** and **off** safely.
2. How to read and record the **run log** and **gauge**.
3. How to check the **breaker/indicator** and report faults.
4. How to read the **ledger** and verify a transaction.
5. How to use the **notification channel** (WhatsApp group / notice board).
6. How to **draft a grievance or amendment proposal** (templates provided).

Training is free, happens at least twice a year, and is completed before a
member's first duty (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/02-Duty-Training/`).

## 3. WHAT PARTICIPATION MEANS

| Activity | Expected level |
|----------|----------------|
| General Assembly | Attend at least 50% of meetings/year; sent through proxy only in genuine absence |
| Duty rotation | Accept drawn duties or arrange swap/buy-out |
| Voting | Vote on every resolution (no abstention-by-absence without notice) |
| Feedback | Read notices; raise genuine issues in the right process |

## 4. CONSEQUENCE OF NON-LEARNING / NON-PARTICIPATION

- Missing mandatory training: a notice + make-up session is offered. Persistent
  refusal may limit a member to paid buy-out for duties (never loss of water).
- Persistent non-participation is addressed through the enforcement ladder, but
  **never** by cutting water without due process.

## 5. WHY THIS DUTY EXISTS

- The entire system is designed to have **no single person of knowledge**. That
  only works if everyone learns.
- A member who can't operate the motor can never take duty, which silently
  concentrates work back onto a few people — exactly what we must prevent.

## 6. SUPPORT FOR LEARNING

- The system provides: printed one-page operator card, video demonstration,
  hands-on practice session, and a "buddy" for the first duty
  (`13-HANDBOOKS/02-Operator-Handbook.md`).
- No member is ever ridiculed for learning slowly; that is targeting.
