# FUNDAMENTAL DUTY 1 — DUTY TO CONTRIBUTE

**Folder:** `00-CONSTITUTION/03-Fundamental-Duties/`

---

## 1. THE DUTY

Every member must **contribute fairly** to the system in money, time, or skill,
because everyone receives water. Contribution is the price of membership and is
never optional.

## 2. THE THREE FORMS OF CONTRIBUTION

| Form | What it covers | Default |
|------|----------------|---------|
| **Money** | Monthly contribution + buy-in + event/repair assessments | Mandatory for all |
| **Time** | Duty rotation (motor operation, logging, verification) | Mandatory duty hours per member |
| **Skill** | Repair, accounting, legal, electrical help when registered and called | Voluntary, compensated; cannot be forced free |

## 3. THE FAIRNESS RULE

- Everyone pays the same published monthly contribution per connection, unless
  the assembly has adopted a **consumption-based tier** for measured
  connections (`06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/02-Contribution-Tiers.md`).
- Everyone performs (or buys out) their duty hours. The buy-out option ensures a
  member who genuinely cannot operate the motor still contributes equally in
  money.
- No member can be exempted from contribution except through the published
  hardship protocol — and even then, the member contributes in another form
  (e.g., documentation, cleaning, watch duty).

## 4. PENALTY FOR NON-CONTRIBUTION

- Late contribution: interest + reminder ladder (`06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/04-Penalties-and-Fines.md`).
- Chronic non-payment: dues deducted from any future refund; water can be
  suspended only after the full enforcement ladder including a hearing.
- Non-performance of duty: buy-out charge or swap; chronic avoidance leads to
  Level 2–3 consequences.

## 5. WHY THIS DUTY EXISTS

- If one member stops paying, others silently pay more. That is unfair and must
  not happen.
- Free riders create resentment; resentment creates conflict; conflict creates
  targeting. Contribution prevents all of that.

## 6. LIMITS ON THE DUTY

- Contribution must always be **published, formula-based, and equal**, never
  discretionary or punitive.
- The assembly cannot increase contribution arbitrarily — increases follow the
  budget procedure (`06-FINANCE/03-Bookkeeping/05-Annual-Budget.md`).
