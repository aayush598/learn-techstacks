# 02 - CORE PRINCIPLES (DETAILED)

**Folder:** `00-CONSTITUTION/01-Preamble/`  
**These are the unbreakable foundations. Every rule in every folder derives from them.**

---

## PRINCIPLE 1 — WATER IS COMMON PROPERTY

- Water pumped from the borewell is the collective resource of all contributing
  members. It cannot be "owned" by the person who drilled it, who bought the
  motor, or who holds the keys.
- The person who contributed money for drilling, motor, or boring has a **right
  to proportional benefit and repayment over time** — but never a right to
  control distribution.
- Water allocation is decided only by published rules, never by an individual.

## PRINCIPLE 2 — EQUAL POWER

- One member = one vote. A member means one registered connection holder
  (household head or their nominee).
- No role in this system — committee member, treasurer, duty operator, key
  holder — carries extra voting power.
- All roles are **temporary and rotating**. No one holds any role for more
  than the term limits defined in `02-GOVERNANCE/02-Committees/06-Term-Limits-and-Rotation.md`.

## PRINCIPLE 3 — NO SINGLE POINT OF FAILURE

- Every key has a duplicate in a locked double-containment box.
- Every duty has a trained backup.
- Every skill has at least two members able to perform it (cross-training).
- Every account requires two signatories from different households.
- The system must survive: the departure of the "most important" person, the
  refusal of any single member, and even the coordinated walk-out of a group.

## PRINCIPLE 4 — CONTRIBUTION IS MANDATORY AND FAIR

- No free riders. Every member pays the published contribution and performs or
  pays for their share of duty.
- Contributions are fixed by a published formula (`06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/01-Monthly-Contribution.md`),
  not by anyone's mood.
- Those who genuinely cannot pay (extreme hardship) go through a **hardship
  plan** that preserves dignity and still obligates non-monetary contribution.

## PRINCIPLE 5 — TRUTH OVER MAJORITY

- A matter of **fact** (meter reading, receipt, timestamp, log entry) is decided
  by evidence, not by vote.
- A matter of **fairness** that affects one member cannot be decided in that
  member's absence.
- Any member may raise a **Truth Veto** against a proposed decision if it is
  provably factually wrong or violates a Fundamental Right. The veto blocks the
  decision until an evidence review (not a popularity contest) is completed.
- Details: `00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/`.

## PRINCIPLE 6 — RADICAL TRANSPARENCY

- All records — financial, duty, motor logs, meeting minutes, vendor quotes —
  are open to all members at all times.
- Records are kept in both physical (register) and digital (backed-up) form.
- Any member may copy any record. Denial of access is itself an offence.
- No cash transactions are permitted for any system money; all money moves
  through the bank account or is recorded with a numbered receipt.

## PRINCIPLE 7 — PROTECTION OF THE WEAK

- A coalition of many may not: target an individual's water, delay their repair,
  overload their duty, spread false charges, or block their legitimate requests.
- Any decision that affects a specific member or a minority requires that
  member/minority to be present and heard, and requires written reasons.
- Whistleblowers and truth-tellers are protected absolutely.

## PRINCIPLE 8 — WORK IS REWARDED

- Duty operators, treasurers, repair volunteers, skilled technicians, and any
  contributor earn **time-credits** and/or monetary stipend at published rates
  (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/03-Duty-Compensation/` and
  `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/03-Skilled-Member-Program/`).
- No one is obligated to work free. A member who chooses to donate time may do
  so, but it is never presumed.
- Credits are redeemable against contributions or paid out on exit.

## PRINCIPLE 9 — SELF-SUFFICIENCY

- This resource pre-writes answers for every foreseeable situation, including
  situations that feel "unlikely". That is its purpose.
- Genuinely new situations are handled by `06-Supremacy-and-Conflict/03-Interim-Rules-During-Gaps.md`
  and then formalized by amendment.

## PRINCIPLE 10 — DIGNITY

- No member may be shouted at, insulted, humiliated, or socially boycotted for
  raising a question, objection, or grievance.
- Disagreement is normal; contempt is prohibited.
- Any member who behaves contemptuously is corrected under
  `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/`.

---

## PRINCIPLE CONFLICT RESOLUTION

If a proposed action satisfies all 10 principles, it is allowed.
If it satisfies 9 and violates 1, it is **not allowed** — find a way that
satisfies all 10. There is always a constitution-compliant way.
