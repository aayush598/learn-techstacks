# 03 - DEFINITIONS

**Folder:** `00-CONSTITUTION/01-Preamble/`

Every rule in this resource uses these definitions. If a term is used elsewhere
with a different meaning, this document wins.

---

## A. PEOPLE

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Member** | The registered connection holder of a household that receives water from the system and is current on all obligations (contribution, duty, conduct). |
| **Connection** | One registered water tap/delivery point serving one household, with a unique connection ID. |
| **Household** | A dwelling unit that receives water through one connection. |
| **Founding member** | A member whose contribution (money or in-kind) was part of the original boring/motor installation. |
| **Late joiner** | A member who joins after the original installation and pays the buy-in as per `09-PROJECTS-AND-EXPANSION/03-Late-Joiner-Buy-in/`. |
| **Tenant member** | A person renting a dwelling; has usage rights and obligations but voting rights only if delegated in writing by the owner-member. |
| **Minority / Individual member** | One member, or a small group of members, facing a decision, action, or charge supported by a larger number of members. |
| **Skilled member** | A member who holds a skill (electrical, plumbing, motor repair, welding, accounting, legal, etc.) recorded in the Skill Register. |
| **Duty operator** | The member assigned to operate (switch on/off and log) the motor for a duty period. |
| **Conflict Review Board (CRB)** | The temporary body that adjudicates grievances, formed per `02-GOVERNANCE/02-Committees/05-Conflict-Review-Board.md`. |
| **Interested member** | Any member who is a party to, or directly affected by, a decision. |

## B. ASSETS

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Boring / Borewell** | The drilled water source. |
| **Motor (pump)** | The pump that lifts water from the boring. |
| **Motor room / Pump house** | The enclosed space housing the motor, panel, and switches. |
| **Control panel** | The electrical box containing the switch, starter, and safety devices. |
| **Overhead tank (OHT)** | A storage tank, if installed. |
| **The Keys** | Physical access keys to the motor room, stored under the dual-key rule. |
| **Common Fund** | The main operational fund (monthly contributions). |
| **Reserve Fund** | The emergency/asset-replacement fund (`06-FINANCE/02-Fund-Management/02-Reserve-Fund.md`). |
| **Repair Fund** | Sub-fund for maintenance and repairs. |

## C. MONEY AND TIME

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Monthly contribution** | The fixed monthly charge per connection, set by formula in `06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/01-Monthly-Contribution.md`. |
| **Time-credit** | The unit of compensated time earned for performing duties (1 hour = 1 credit). |
| **Credit rate** | The rupee value assigned to one time-credit, published quarterly. |
| **Stipend** | Cash paid (from the common fund) to members performing paid roles. |
| **Buy-in** | The one-time entry payment of a late joiner, equal to their fair backdated share. |
| **Event water** | Extra water supplied for a wedding/function, booked and paid per `04-MOTOR-OPERATIONS/03-Event-Water/`. |

## D. PROCESS AND EVENTS

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **General Assembly** | The meeting of all members; the supreme decision body. |
| **Quorum** | The minimum attendance for a valid assembly (defined in `02-GOVERNANCE/01-General-Assembly/02-Quorum-Rules.md`). |
| **Resolution** | A formal decision passed by the assembly. |
| **Ordinance / interim rule** | A temporary rule issued during a gap or emergency, valid ≤ 30 days, then requiring assembly ratification. |
| **Truth Veto** | A member's formal objection that blocks a decision pending evidence review (`00-CONSTITUTION/04-Truth-Supremacy/02-Single-Member-Truth-Veto.md`). |
| **Grievance** | A written complaint by a member under `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/02-Resolution-Process/02-Step-2-Written-Grievance.md`. |
| **Enforcement ladder** | The graduated set of consequences defined in `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/05-Enforcement/01-Enforcement-Ladder.md`. |
| **Amendment** | A change to this constitution, valid only through Part V process. |

## E. WATER TERMS

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Slot** | One fixed time window in the daily/weekly schedule during which the motor runs for a defined group/area. |
| **Group** | A subset of members sharing a slot (used in rotation and summer grouping). |
| **Flow rate** | Volume of water per minute delivered at the tap. |
| **Discharge yield** | Total water the boring can supply in a run. |
| **Rotation order** | The sequence in which groups receive their slots, rotated weekly so no group permanently gets the "best" slot. |
| **Tap level** | The elevation/height of a household's tap relative to the main line; affects delivered pressure and volume. |

---

## CONFLICTING DEFINITIONS

If any other file uses a term differently, the definition above prevails and
the other file is deemed to be in error. Report such errors through the
amendment process (`00-CONSTITUTION/05-Amendment-Process/`).
