# 01 - VISION AND PURPOSE

**Folder:** `00-CONSTITUTION/01-Preamble/`  
**Cross-reference:** `00-MASTER-CONSTITUTION.md`

---

## 1. THE VISION

A colony in which **every household receives safe, fair, predictable water**,
delivered through a system that is **owned equally by all**, **run by all**,
**financed by all**, and **controlled by no one**.

We envision a water system where:

- No person can switch the motor on or off for their own convenience at the
  cost of others.
- No person or group can decide who gets water, how much, or when.
- No person can hide money, inflate repair costs, or delay repairs for their
  own gain.
- No skilled member is forced to work free, and no one is indispensable.
- An individual with the truth always wins, even against a crowd.
- Every member can operate, repair-budget, audit, and administer the system,
  because the system belongs to everyone.

---

## 2. THE MISSION

To transform the boring water supply from a **person-controlled utility** into a
**constitution-governed utility** that any person can run, inspect, and trust,
blindly, forever.

---

## 3. THE THREE PILLARS OF THE SYSTEM

1. **Equity** — equal rights, equal voice, equal burden, equal benefit.
2. **Transparency** — every action visible, every rupee traceable, every
   decision recorded.
3. **Resilience** — no single person, key, skill, or fund is ever the only
   option. If anyone leaves, refuses, or sabotages, the system continues.

---

## 4. WHY A CONSTITUTION INSTEAD OF A MANAGER

| Manager-led system (the current broken model) | Constitution-led system (this model) |
|-----------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| One person/group controls on/off | Rotating duty roster, trained everyone, dual keys |
| One person decides timing | Published schedule fixed by assembly, changed only by amendment |
| One person handles money | Joint bank account, dual signatories, open ledger, audit by any member |
| Repairs depend on one person's mood | Repair committee, timelines, penalties, multiple vendors |
| Skilled person forced to work free | Skill register + paid time-credits |
| Groups can target individuals | Anti-targeting protections + truth-supremacy rules |
| Conflicts decided by power | Conflict Review Board + lot-drawn jury + truth veto |

---

## 5. LONG-TERM GOALS (10–25 YEARS)

1. Every household has a registered water connection with equal nominal rights.
2. Every adult member is trained to operate the motor, read the gauge, log runs,
   and understand the accounts.
3. The common fund holds a full reserve capable of replacing the motor and
   drilling a new boring without a distress collection.
4. The system runs itself through rotation such that the departure of any
   single member causes zero disruption.
5. Disputes are rare because rules are pre-written; when they occur, they are
   resolved internally in days, not months.

---

## 6. MEASUREMENT OF SUCCESS

The system is successful when **any random member, on any random day**, can:
- Tell you tomorrow's motor schedule (from the published calendar).
- Open the ledger and trace every rupee in and out.
- Explain who does today's duty and what compensation they earn.
- State the rule for any common situation without consulting any "leader".

If any of these fail, the system is failing and must be corrected under
`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION` and `02-GOVERNANCE/04-Transparency/`.
