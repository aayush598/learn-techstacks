# 04 - APPLICABILITY AND SCOPE

**Folder:** `00-CONSTITUTION/01-Preamble/`

---

## 1. TO WHOM THIS APPLIES

This constitution and all derived rules apply to:

1. Every **member** (connection holder) of the colony water system.
2. Every **household member** living at a connected dwelling.
3. Every **tenant** at a connected dwelling.
4. Every **vendor, contractor, technician, and worker** engaged by the system.
5. Any **government authority** interacting with the system, to the extent the
   system asserts its rights and duties toward that authority.
6. Any person who uses water from this system, even temporarily.

## 2. TO WHAT THIS APPLIES

This applies to:

- The **boring** (borewell) and its water.
- The **motor, panel, pipes, taps, valves, tanks, and meters** belonging to the
  system.
- The **funds** collected from members.
- The **duties, schedules, and decisions** regarding water.
- The **records, registers, and documents** of the system.
- **Disputes** among members concerning the above.

It does **not** apply to purely private matters inside a household that do not
affect water supply, funds, or other members.

## 3. TERRITORIAL SCOPE

- Applies within the colony boundary and the pump house, pipe network, and
  storage serving the colony's connected households.
- If water is sold or given to a neighboring area, separate rules under
  `06-FINANCE/01-Contributions/05-Event-and-Extra-Charges.md` apply; the same
  constitution governs that relationship to the extent applicable.

## 4. TEMPORAL SCOPE

- Comes into force on the adoption date (see Master Constitution Section 6).
- Survives the departure of any member.
- Continues to apply even if a majority attempts to dissolve it, because the
  right to water and the rights of minorities are basic-structure clauses.

## 6. JURISDICTION OVER MEMBERS WHO LEAVE

- A member who exits loses voting and water rights but **retains** the right to
  claim refunds per `01-MEMBERSHIP/03-Exit-and-Transfer/03-Refund-Procedure.md`.
- A member who exits cannot escape liability for obligations (dues, damage)
  accrued while a member.
- A member who exits while a grievance is pending remains bound by the
  resolution of that grievance.

## 7. PREEMPTION OF LOCAL HABIT, CASTE, GENDER, OR SOCIAL RANK

- No practice based on caste, religion, gender, family status, or friendship
  may alter any rule here. Water is distributed by written rules only.
- If an existing local practice contradicts this constitution, the constitution
  prevails, and the contradictory practice is discontinued immediately.

## 8. NON-WAIVER CLAUSE

- A member's failure to enforce a rule on one occasion does not waive the rule.
- Rules are enforced consistently on every occasion to prevent selective
  application (which is itself a form of targeting).
