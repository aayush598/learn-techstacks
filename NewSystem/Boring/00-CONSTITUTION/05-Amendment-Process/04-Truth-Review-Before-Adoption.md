# AMENDMENT PROCESS 4 — TRUTH REVIEW BEFORE ADOPTION

**Folder:** `00-CONSTITUTION/05-Amendment-Process/`

---

## 1. THE TRUTH GATE

An amendment that passes its vote does **not** become law immediately. It passes
through the **Truth Review** — a final check that the change is consistent with
the constitution's facts, rules, and rights. This is the final barrier against
"wrong rules voted in by a large group".

## 2. WHAT THE REVIEW CHECKS

| Check | Question |
|-------|----------|
| Factual soundness | Is the amendment based on any false assumption or false data? |
| Rule consistency | Does it contradict any existing unamended rule? |
| Rights consistency | Does it weaken a Fundamental Right or the protection mechanisms? |
| Basic structure | Does it touch any forbidden clause? |
| Practicality | Is it implementable and unambiguous? |
| Retroactivity | Does it punish past conduct (prohibited unless explicitly stated and justified)? |

## 3. THE REVIEW BODY

- The CRB performs the review with a written checklist.
- If the amendment changes **rights or protection mechanisms** (Class C), the
  review is done by the CRB **plus** two lot-drawn members from households not
  involved in the proposal.
- No member who proposed or strongly benefits from the amendment reviews it.

## 4. OUTCOMES

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Certified** | Amendment is adopted and registered. |
| **Certified with notes** | Adopted; notes clarify application. |
| **Referred back** | Adopted in principle but returned to the proposer for redrafting of specific defects (e.g., ambiguity). Re-vote not needed if defects are purely drafting. |
| **Rejected** | The amendment cannot take effect because it fails a check. The rejection is reasoned and recorded. |

## 5. CHALLENGING A REVIEW

- A rejected amendment can be resubmitted after fixing the defect.
- A certified amendment can still be challenged **after** adoption if a member
  later shows it was certified on false information
  (`04-Truth-Supremacy/03-Objection-to-Wrong-Resolution.md`).

## 6. WHY A GATE AFTER VOTING

- A crowd can be carried away by emotion or manipulation *during* the vote.
- The Truth Review lets a calm, evidence-based check happen **before** the
  change touches anyone's water or money.
- This is the constitutional answer to: *"a large group voted in a wrong rule —
  can the right individual still stop it?"* → Yes, at the Truth Gate, and also
  afterward by the objection process.
