# AMENDMENT PROCESS 2 — PUBLICATION AND DISCUSSION

**Folder:** `00-CONSTITUTION/05-Amendment-Process/`

---

## 1. THE RULE OF NOTICE

No amendment is decided on the day it is first announced. Every proposal must be
**published for discussion** for a minimum **7 days** before voting.

## 2. PUBLICATION CHANNELS (ALL MUST BE USED)

1. **Notice board** — printed copy, posted and photographed (photo stored as proof).
2. **Members' group** (WhatsApp/e-mail) — full text, to every member.
3. **Physical register** — a copy available for reading in the common area.
4. **Assembly announcement** — read aloud at the assembly where discussion starts.

Publication proof (photo + date) is attached to the amendment file.

## 3. DISCUSSION REQUIREMENTS

- During the 7-day window, any member may:
  - Ask questions (answered in writing by the proposer or CRB).
  - Suggest improvements (the proposer may revise the text; revisions restart
    the 7-day window if they change meaning).
  - Raise a **Truth Veto** if the proposal conflicts with a fact, rule, or right.
- A **meeting is held** (or a structured group discussion) where both for and
  against views are heard. No shouting down is permitted.

## 4. THE COUNTER-PROPOSAL RIGHT

- Any member may submit a **counter-proposal** (a different solution to the same
  problem) within the discussion window. If submitted, both the original and the
  counter-proposal are put to the vote together, with a clear "no change" option.

## 5. THE ABSENCE RULE

- If an amendment affects the water, money, or duties of a specific member or
  minority, that member/minority must be individually notified and given a chance
  to respond **before** voting. Voting without this is void.

## 6. EMERGENCY EXPEDITED PROCESS

- For genuine emergencies (e.g., drought rationing), the assembly may, by a
  2/3 vote with all members notified, shorten the window to **48 hours**.
- The 48-hour rule still requires: full text published, an opportunity to
  object, and no basic-structure violation. The CRB certifies the emergency.
