# AMENDMENT PROCESS 3 — VOTING RULES

**Folder:** `00-CONSTITUTION/05-Amendment-Process/`

---

## 1. VOTING CLASSES

Amendments are divided into classes with different thresholds:

| Class | Example | Threshold |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| **Class A — Ordinary** | Clarifying language, procedure details, form changes | Majority (50% + 1) of those present AND ≥ 40% of all members |
| **Class B — Substantive** | Schedule changes, contribution formula changes, duty system changes, event fees | **2/3 (66.7%)** of all members (not just those present) |
| **Class C — Rights-adjacent** | Any change touching a Fundamental Right or the protection mechanisms | **3/4 (75%)** of all members, PLUS no Truth Veto upheld, PLUS CRB certification |
| **Class D — Basic structure** | Water-as-common-property, one-member-one-vote, truth-supremacy, right to audit, contribution duty | **FORBIDDEN.** Void regardless of votes |

## 2. THE CLASSIFICATION PROCESS

- The proposer suggests a class; the CRB confirms it. If in doubt, the higher
  class applies (safer).
- Classification is appealable to the assembly before the vote.

## 3. VOTING METHODS

- **Show of hands** at the assembly (recorded per member in minutes).
- **Secret ballot** when any member requests it (reduces coercion/coalition
  pressure) — e.g., for votes about individuals or sensitive matters.
- **Written/absentee ballots** collected through the notice box for members who
  cannot attend, provided the ballot is the actual member's, witnessed and
  submitted before the count.

## 4. THE COUNT RULES

1. Every registered member = one vote per connection (as defined in
   `01-MEMBERSHIP/05-Equal-Standing/01-One-Member-One-Vote.md`).
2. A member who does not vote is counted as **not voting** for Class A (only
   those present), but as **against** for Class B and C (because higher classes
   need the entire membership's consent, silence cannot pass them).
3. The count is done openly, by two counters from different households, and
   entered in minutes.
4. Results are published within 24 hours with the vote split.

## 5. WHAT CANNOT BE VOTED ON

- **Facts** (evidence decides).
- **Rights** (they stand).
- **Applying an existing rule** (the rule is applied).
- **Basic structure** (forbidden).

## 6. RECORD

Every vote's record includes: amendment number, class, notice dates, discussion
summary, votes for/against/absent, objections raised and their disposition, and
the signed minutes page. All retained forever.
