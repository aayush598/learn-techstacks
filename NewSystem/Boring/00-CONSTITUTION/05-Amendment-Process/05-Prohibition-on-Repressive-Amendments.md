# AMENDMENT PROCESS 5 — PROHIBITION ON REPRESSIVE AMENDMENTS

**Folder:** `00-CONSTITUTION/05-Amendment-Process/`

---

## 1. THE CONCERN

The system was created to stop a controlling group from exploiting others. It
would be self-defeating if a coalition could simply *vote in* amendments that
legalize their control. This document makes that impossible.

## 2. THE PROHIBITED CATEGORIES

The following amendments are **prohibited** — void regardless of votes:

| Category | Example |
|----------|---------|
| **Control transfer** | Giving any person/group the power to decide on/off, timing, or distribution alone. |
| **Vote multiplication** | Granting extra votes to any member, family, or group. |
| **Truth suppression** | Limiting the right to object, veto, audit, or inspect. |
| **Targeting authorization** | Allowing collective punishment, social boycott, or discretionary water cuts. |
| **Free-labour laws** | Making skilled work mandatory without compensation. |
| **Exemption-from-contribution** | Allowing a group to stop paying while still receiving water. |
| **Retroactive punishment** | Making past conduct newly punishable to attack a person. |
| **Silence-by-age** | Rules that expire objections or grievances against rights. |
| **Secrecy** | Making records confidential or committee-only. |
| **Discrimination** | Different treatment by caste, religion, gender, family, or friendship. |

## 3. THE VOIDNESS MECHANISM

- Such an amendment can be proposed, discussed, and even voted for by everyone —
  it still has **no legal effect**.
- The CRB refuses certification at the Truth Gate.
- Any member can invoke the objection process at any time, even years later,
  and the void amendment is struck from the register.
- A member cannot be punished for refusing to follow a void amendment, and is
  encouraged to refuse.

## 4. THE SPIRIT TEST

When in doubt about any proposed amendment, apply the **spirit test**:

> *"Would this amendment, in the real world of this colony, make it easier for
> a dominant person or group to control water, money, or people?"*

If yes, it is prohibited, regardless of its wording.

## 5. THE SLIPPERY SLOPE GUARD

- Amendments are adopted one at a time with reasoning; "bundles" that mix a good
  change with a harmful one are split.
- Any amendment that a member demonstrates is a **first step** toward a
  prohibited category (e.g., "one year president just for admin") is rejected
  because permanent roles are prohibited.
