## 1. WELCOME

Welcome to the colony water supply system. This handbook is your one-page path
to understanding what you get, what you owe, and how everything works. The
system is a **collective**: water is common property, every member has equal
power, and no one can be pushed around by anyone else.

## 2. WHAT YOU GET

| You are entitled to | Details |
|---------------------|---------|
| **Fair water** | Your household's scheduled share, like every other household |
| **Equal voice** | One vote at every assembly — yours counts exactly as much as anyone's |
| **Full information** | Every log, register, and rupee is open to you (P6) |
| **Protection** | No one can cut your water, raise your charges, or punish you except through the written rules |
| **Work and reward** | Take duty shifts and earn time-credits or stipend (P8) |
| **Justice** | Any dispute goes to the Conflict Review Board — evidence decides, not numbers |

## 3. WHAT YOU OWE

| Duty | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Contribution** | Monthly dues per the published tariff; everyone pays the same rule |
| **Buy-in** | One-time buy-in contribution at admission (`01-MEMBERSHIP/01-Enrollment/02-Buy-in-Contribution.md`) |
| **Duty** | Every able member takes rotating duty shifts; training is free |
| **Transparency** | Your records, meter, and usage are open like everyone else's |
| **Care** | Protect the motor, pipes, and meters — they are common property |

## 4. HOW TO OPERATE (IF YOU ARE ON DUTY)

1. Complete the free operation training — held twice a year
   (`03-ROTATION-DUTY/02-Duty-Training/`).
2. Follow the roster: switch the motor on/off at the scheduled times.
3. **Log everything:** time on, time off, gauge reading, anything unusual
   (`12-TEMPLATES/04-Motor-Run-Log.md`).
4. Check the panel for fault indicators; report anything odd to the repair
   coordinator.
5. If you cannot make a shift, arrange a swap or call the backup list — do not
   just not show up.

## 5. HOW TO ATTEND MEETINGS

- Meetings are announced on the notice board and message group at least 7 days
  ahead.
- Every member may speak, propose, and vote. There are no observers-only
  meetings.
- Minutes are published. If you were absent, read them; you are still bound by
  decisions but can always raise a Truth Veto if a decision violates a rule or
  a right.

## 6. HOW TO RAISE AN ISSUE — THE FAST PATH

| Step | What you do |
|------|-------------|
| 1 | **Talk.** Ask for a short dialogue with the person or committee involved. Most issues end here. |
| 2 | **Write.** If talking fails, file a grievance (form in `12-TEMPLATES/09-Grievance-Form.md`) with the CRB. |
| 3 | **Hear.** The CRB hears both sides and decides on evidence within 7 days of the hearing. |
| 4 | **Appeal.** A lot-drawn jury re-hears the case. Then the assembly (rules only), then legal recourse. |

Fear of retaliation? Use the anonymous or confidential channel — reporting is
always safe and protected.

## 7. KEY RULES ON ONE PAGE

1. **Water is never cut as punishment** — only the Level 5 protocol, for the
   three listed grounds.
2. **No one can charge you extra** — the tariff is the same for every member.
3. **No voting on facts** — logs and receipts decide what happened.
4. **Truth beats numbers** — one right member beats a wrong majority.
5. **No collective punishment** — you are responsible for you, not your
   relatives.
6. **No retaliation** — punishing someone for complaining doubles the penalty.
7. **Everything is open** — check any register, any time.
8. **Dignity for all** — no shaming, no boycotts, no labels.

## 8. THE KEY CONTACTS

| Role | Purpose | How chosen |
|------|---------|------------|
| **Operator (current duty)** | Runs the motor per roster | Lot-drawn roster |
| **Treasurer** | Collects dues, maintains the register | Rotating / elected with rotation |
| **Repair coordinator** | Receives faults, manages repairs | Rotating role |
| **Conflict Review Board (CRB)** | Receives grievances, hears disputes | Lot-drawn members |
| **General Assembly** | Decides budget, schedule, projects | All members |

Every role rotates and has backups (P3) — the system never depends on one
person. If a contact seems unreachable, use the next one; nothing is the
exclusive property of one member.

## 9. YOUR FIRST 30 DAYS CHECKLIST

1. Registered in the member registry and signed the entry agreement.
2. Buy-in contribution paid and receipted.
3. First month dues paid and receipted.
4. Operation training booked (or medical exemption filed).
5. Read this handbook and the Master Constitution's Section 4 (the
   non-negotiable basic structure).
6. Know how to raise a grievance — and know that you can.

## 10. THE PROMISE TO YOU

No member of this colony can be targeted, silenced, or left without water by
anyone — not by an individual, not by a crowd. If it ever feels otherwise, the
rules in `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/` are your protection, and they work even
when you are the only one using them.
