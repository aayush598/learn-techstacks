# TREASURER HANDBOOK

**Folder:** `13-HANDBOOKS/`  
**Cross-reference:** `06-FINANCE/`, `07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/01-Conflict-Types/04-Financial-Conflicts.md`

---

## 1. YOUR ROLE

You are the **treasurer**, a rotating role (P3). You are not the owner of the
colony's money — you are its **steward**. The money belongs to the collective;
your job is to collect it, record it, bank it, and report it. Every rupee must
be traceable. P6 (radical transparency) is your job description.

## 2. COLLECTING — THE DAILY ROUTINE

1. Collect monthly dues per the published tariff — the **same rule for every
   member**. There is no "special charge" for any member without a written
   assembly decision.
2. Give a **receipt for every payment** — pre-numbered counterfoil or UPI
   confirmation, recorded the same day.
3. Record every payment in the contribution register: date, member, amount,
   receipt number, mode (cash/UPI/bank).
4. If a member disputes a charge, **do not collect it**. Mark it "disputed —
   pending CRB" and hold it. That is not defiance; it is the law
   (`03-Protection-Mechanisms/05-Individual-Economic-Protection.md`).

## 3. NO CASH IN YOUR POCKET

- Bank the cash within **2 working days**. Never accumulate a large cash pile.
- The register and the bank must tell the same story. Reconcile at least weekly.
- Do not pay personal expenses from the account, not even "temporarily", not
   even "for the colony, will fix later". If the money is not in the account,
   the record is a lie.

## 4. PAYING OUT — VOUCHERS ALWAYS

| Payment | Required |
|---------|----------|
| Any expense (repair, parts, vendor, stipend) | Written voucher: what, why, amount, date, who approved |
| Repair over the approval ceiling | Prior CRB/assembly approval per `05-MAINTENANCE-REPAIR/` |
| Stipends / time-credit payouts | List signed by the member, based on the duty log |
| Refunds | Written refund order from the CRB or treasurer's correction note |

Every payment needs the **voucher + the bill + the approval reference** in the
minutes. No voucher, no payment. If someone is angry with you for demanding a
voucher, that is exactly why the rule exists.

## 5. THE REGISTER AND STATEMENTS

1. Keep one **master register** of every member's account (dues, payments,
   credits, fines, refunds).
2. Publish a **monthly statement** to the whole colony: income, expenses,
   balance, pending dues (aggregated by member — hardship cases are kept
   confidential).
3. Keep every receipt and voucher filed by month. Nothing is thrown away —
   records are retained per `11-LEGAL-AND-COMPLIANCE/03-Records-Retention/`.
4. The register is open to any member's inspection at any time. Say yes.

## 6. AUDIT PREPARATION

| Before an audit | Checklist |
|-----------------|-----------|
| Register complete to today | Every entry, dated and receipted |
| Vouchers filed and numbered | Matching the register entries |
| Bank statements collected | Covering the audit period |
| Reconciliation done | Register balance = bank balance + cash on hand (should be nil) |
| Hardship records sealed | Separate, confidential |

An audit is **not** an accusation against you — it is the system checking its
own health (P6). Cooperate fully and cheerfully.

## 7. WHAT YOU MUST NEVER DO

| Never | Because |
|-------|---------|
| Make a personal financial decision for the colony | No authority; money moves only on published rules or assembly/CRB decisions |
| Collect or pay in cash without recording | Cash is invisible; the record is the truth |
| Hide a member's payment | It is their receipt and their credit |
| Delay a refund to "teach a lesson" | Delaying a refund is retaliation |
| Use the fund to punish or reward | The fund is for the system, not for favourites |
| Decide hardship alone | Hardship is documented and reviewed with the CRB, confidentially |
| Sign blank vouchers | Blank vouchers are theft waiting to happen |

## 8. WHEN YOU SUSPECT MISAPPROPRIATION

1. Do not confront alone and do not cover it up.
2. Record what you found, freeze further movement if possible.
3. Report to the CRB through the whistleblower channel if needed — you are
   protected (`07-CONFLICT-RESOLUTION/03-Protection-Mechanisms/03-Whistleblower-Protection.md`).
4. The CRB orders a full audit within 7 days; the offender is handled by the
   Enforcement Ladder, not by you.

## 9. THE PRACTICAL SITUATION

*Example:* A member claims he paid March dues in cash. Your register has no
entry and no receipt. Do not fight him in public. Say: "Let's check the
register and the receipts together." If there is no record, the honest answer
is that the system has no record of payment, and he is offered the instalment
option. If **you** actually took the cash and did not record it, you confess
immediately — that is misappropriation and the ladder handles it, but an early
honest confession is weighed in your favour.

## 10. YOUR PROTECTION

You cannot be punished for following these rules, for refusing an
unvouchered payment, or for reporting wrongdoing. If anyone pressures you to
record a false entry or release money without a voucher, refuse and report to
the CRB. You are a whistleblower and protected.
