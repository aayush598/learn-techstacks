## Must-know vs good-to-know (for the last 3 days)

### MUST know (high marks, guaranteed questions)
1. Diode models + clip/clamp/rectifier equations and PIV.
2. Any BJT/MOS amplifier: DC bias → small-signal → gain / R_in / R_out.
3. Miller effect and the frequency cutoffs.
4. Op-amp circuits (inv, non-inv, summer, integrator, differentiator).
5. Differential amplifier + CMRR.
6. Current mirror basics + output resistance.
7. Schmitt trigger thresholds + hysteresis.
8. Oscillator conditions (Barkhausen) + which formula for which oscillator.

### GOOD to know (1-mark / safety net)
- Wilson/Widlar/cascode mirror distinctions.
- Sallen-Key Q derivation & Butterworth design loop.
- Practical (real) op-amp parameters & SR.
- Transistor switch timings (storage time).
- Feedback topologies table.
- Bode plot pole counting.
