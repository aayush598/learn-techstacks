## Rules for authors

1. Minimum **30 questions** per file, numbered Q1..Q30+.
2. First ~30% Easy/Moderate, middle ~40% Moderate, last ~30% **GATE-level**
   (traps, multiple-step, mixed concepts).
3. Mix of answer styles: numeric, "which configuration", "what happens if",
   "compare A vs B", waveform sketch descriptions.
4. Every numeric answer must be **verified against the formula sheet** — redo the
   arithmetic manually. GATE grade: wrong numbers destroy trust.
5. Keep the same formatting: `### Q{n}. (Difficulty) text`, `**Answer:**`,
   `**Method:**`, horizontal rule ( `---` ) every few questions, traps at end.
6. No markdown tables unless genuinely clearer; formulas may use plain
   `V = I R`, `gm = I_C/V_T`, `A_v = -gm(R_C||R_L)`.
7. Refer to chapter numbers like `` `02-Diodes-Basics-and-Models.md` ``.
8. Self-check at end: re-verify at least 5 equations by hand.
