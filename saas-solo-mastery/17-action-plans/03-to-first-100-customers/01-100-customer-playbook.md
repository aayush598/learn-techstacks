# Complete Playbook for Acquiring Your First 100 Customers

## Executive Summary

Getting your first 100 customers is the hardest milestone in SaaS. This playbook provides a complete, actionable framework for finding, converting, and retaining your first 100 paying customers. It covers 15+ acquisition channels with specific tactics, templates, and tracking for each.

---

## Section 1: The First 100 Customers Mindset

### 1.1 Why the First 100 Matter

```
Customer 1-10: Problem validation (does anyone want this?)
Customer 10-30: Product validation (does the product work?)
Customer 30-50: Channel validation (can we reach them?)
Customer 50-100: Model validation (does the business work?)

Each stage: Different approach, different metrics.
```

### 1.2 The First 100 Customer Promise

```
For your first 100 customers, you should:

1. Know every single one by name
2. Offer concierge-level service
3. Build features they request
4. Ask for feedback constantly
5. Celebrate every single signup
6. Respond to every email personally
7. Give them your personal phone number
8. Ask every single one for referrals
9. Make them feel special (they are!)
10. Learn everything you can from them
```

### 1.3 Timeline to 100 Customers

```
Aggressive timeline (ideal):
  Week 1-4: 1-10 customers ($0-$1K MRR)
  Week 5-8: 10-30 customers ($1K-$3K MRR)
  Week 9-12: 30-50 customers ($3K-$5K MRR)
  Month 4-6: 50-100 customers ($5K-$10K MRR)

Moderate timeline (typical):
  Month 1: 1-5 customers
  Month 2: 5-15 customers
  Month 3: 15-30 customers
  Month 4: 30-50 customers
  Month 5: 50-75 customers
  Month 6: 75-100 customers

Slow timeline (acceptable for complex B2B):
  Month 1-3: 1-10 customers
  Month 4-6: 10-30 customers
  Month 7-9: 30-60 customers
  Month 10-12: 60-100 customers
```

## Section 3: 15 Acquisition Channels for First 100 Customers

### 3.1 Channel 1: Personal Network

**Best for:** First 1-10 customers
**Effort:** Low
**Conversion rate:** High (10-30%)

```
Strategy:
  - Tell everyone you know what you're building
  - Ask for their business (not just feedback)
  - Offer a "founder's discount" for early supporters
  - Follow up personally with everyone

Tactics:
  - Direct messages to 50+ personal contacts
  - Post on personal social media
  - Tell friends/family to tell their networks
  - Email your personal email list (if you have one)

Template:
  "Hey [Name], I built [Product] to help [target]
  [outcome]. Would love for you to try it — or if
  you know someone who might need it, can you
  introduce me?

  Here's a link with 30 days free: [link with ref code]"
```

### 3.2 Channel 2: IndieHackers Community

**Best for:** First 5-20 customers (if your target is makers)
**Effort:** Medium
**Conversion rate:** 5-15%

```
Strategy:
  - Become an active community member before promoting
  - Share your journey (build in public)
  - Ask for feedback genuinely
  - Offer special deal for IH community

Tactics:
  - Write "How I got my first X customers" posts
  - Participate in daily discussions
  - Share progress updates
  - Comment on other founders' posts
  - List your product in IH products directory

Content that works on IH:
  - "I built [product] in [timeframe] and got [result]"
  - "Here's how I got my first [X] customers"
  - "What would you pay for [solution]?"
```

### 3.3 Channel 3: Reddit

**Best for:** Niche B2B/B2C products
**Effort:** Medium
**Conversion rate:** 2-8%

```
Strategy:
  - Find relevant subreddits for your target
  - Become a helpful community member first
  - Share valuable content (not just your product)
  - Use subreddit rules (don't spam)

Finding subreddits:
  - Reddit.com/r/[your-niche]
  - Use subreddit discovery tools
  - Search for your target audience's questions
  - Look for "what tool do you use" threads

Tactics:
  - Answer questions related to your product (link naturally)
  - Post case studies with results
  - Offer free help to community members
  - Run a "feedback for free access" thread

Important: Reddit is anti-marketing. Be genuine.
If you just spam links, you'll be banned.
```

### 3.4 Channel 4: Niche Communities (Slack, Discord, Facebook Groups)

**Best for:** B2B products with defined audience
**Effort:** Medium
**Conversion rate:** 3-10%

```
Strategy:
  - Find 5-10 communities where your target hangs out
  - Join and observe for 1 week before engaging
  - Add value consistently (answer questions, help)
  - Then share your product when relevant

Finding communities:
  - Facebook Groups: Search for [your niche] + "group"
  - Slack: Slofile.com, standuply.com
  - Discord: Disboard.org, relevant to your niche
  - LinkedIn Groups: Search for your industry

Tactics:
  - Create valuable content and share
  - Host AMA (Ask Me Anything) in the community
  - Offer community-exclusive discount
  - Partner with community leaders
```

### 3.5 Channel 5: LinkedIn Outreach

**Best for:** B2B, professional services, enterprise
**Effort:** High
**Conversion rate:** 5-15% (conversation rate, not sale rate)

```
Strategy:
  - Optimize your LinkedIn profile (clear, professional)
  - Connect with your ICP (ideal customer profile)
  - Engage with their content
  - Send personalized connection requests
  - Convert connections to conversations

Connection request template:
  "Hi [Name], I've been following your work in
  [industry/company]. I built a tool that helps
  [target] [outcome]. Would love to connect!"

After connection:
  "Thanks for connecting! Quick question: does
  [problem] resonate with your team at [Company]?"

Don't: Pitch immediately
Do: Build relationship first, then offer value
```

### 3.6 Channel 6: Twitter/X

**Best for:** B2B, developer tools, creator economy
**Effort:** Medium
**Conversion rate:** 1-5%

```
Strategy:
  - Build a following in your niche (consistent posting)
  - Share valuable content (tips, insights, data)
  - Engage with influencers in your space
  - Use relevant hashtags
  - DM warm leads

Content strategy:
  - Daily: 3-5 tweets about your niche
  - Weekly: 1 thread about your product/learning
  - Weekly: Engage with 20+ people in your niche
  - Share behind-the-scenes of building

Twitter thread example:
  Thread: "I bootstrapped [product] to [X] users.
  Here's exactly how I did it:"
  1/10 [Hook]
  2/10 [The problem]
  3/10 [Why existing solutions suck]
  ...
  10/10 [Link to product]
```

### 3.7 Channel 7: Cold Email

**Best for:** B2B, high-ticket products
**Effort:** High
**Conversion rate:** 1-5% (reply rate), 0.5-2% (meeting rate)

```
Strategy:
  - Build targeted list of 100-500 prospects
  - Research each prospect (company, role, pain)
  - Write personalized emails (not templates)
  - Follow up 3-5 times with value

Cold email structure:
  Subject: [Specific personalization]
  Hi [Name],
  [Specific compliment/observation about their work]
  [1 sentence on how you can help]
  [Social proof if available]
  [Specific, low-friction ask]
  [Your name]

Tools:
  - Apollo.io (lead gen + sequencing)
  - Hunter.io (find emails)
  - Mailshake (sequencing)
  - Mixmax (tracking + templates)
```

### 3.8 Channel 8: Content Marketing (Blog)

**Best for:** Long-term, compounding growth
**Effort:** High (delayed gratification)
**Conversion rate:** 0.5-3%

```
Strategy:
  - Write SEO-optimized blog posts targeting your ICP
  - Answer questions your customers are searching for
  - Include product mentions naturally (not forced)
  - Build email list from blog readers

Content topics:
  1. "How to [solve problem]" (tutorial)
  2. "[X] ways to [outcome]" (listicle)
  3. "[Problem] vs [Problem]: which is better?" (comparison)
  4. "The ultimate guide to [topic]" (pillar content)
  5. "[Product] vs [Competitor]" (comparison)

SEO basics:
  - Keyword research: Ahrefs/Semrush free tools
  - Target: Long-tail keywords (under 50 searches/month)
  - On-page: H1, H2, keyword in first paragraph
  - Internal linking to related posts
  - Meta description with keyword + CTA
```

### 3.9 Channel 9: Partnerships & Affiliates

**Best for:** Established products (best after 50 customers)
**Effort:** Medium
**Conversion rate:** Variable

```
Strategy:
  - Partner with complementary (non-competing) products
  - Offer affiliate commission (20-30% recurring)
  - Cross-promote to each other's audiences
  - Co-create content (webinars, guides, tools)

Partnership types:
  - Integration partners (Zapier, Make, Slack)
  - Reseller partners
  - Referral partners
  - Content partners (co-blog, co-webinar)

Partner outreach template:
  "Hi [Name], I'm a big fan of [Partner Product].
  Our customers use [Partner] for [use case], and we
  think our products would complement each other well.
  Would you be open to exploring a partnership?"
```

### 3.10 Channel 10: Product Hunt

**Best for:** B2B, developer tools, productivity
**Effort:** High (1-2 weeks prep)
**Conversion rate:** 3-10%

```
Strategy:
  - Launch on Product Hunt (see PH checklist)
  - Target #1 Product of the Day
  - Follow up with PH leads immediately

Timing:
  - Launch after you have 10+ customers (don't launch too early)
  - Use PH as an accelerator, not a starter
  - PH audience is tech-savvy but not always your ICP

Follow-up:
  - PH signups convert at 5-15% to paid
  - Follow up within 24 hours
  - Offer PH-exclusive deal
  - Add PH users to your email list
```

### 3.11 Channel 11: App Marketplaces

**Best for:** Products with integrations
**Effort:** Medium
**Conversion rate:** 3-8%

```
Strategy:
  - List on Slack App Directory
  - List on Zapier/Make
  - List on Shopify App Store (if ecommerce)
  - List on Atlassian Marketplace (if PM tool)

Benefits:
  - Built-in distribution (people searching for solutions)
  - Trust signal (being listed = legitimacy)
  - Partner ecosystem exposure

Requirements vary by marketplace:
  - Slack: Requires OAuth + review process
  - Zapier: Requires API + Zapier review
  - Shopify: Requires app submission + review
```

### 3.12 Channel 12: Guest Blogging / Podcasts

**Best for:** Authority building, long-term
**Effort:** High
**Conversion rate:** 1-5%

```
Strategy:
  - Find blogs/podcasts your target audience follows
  - Pitch yourself as a guest
  - Provide valuable content (not product pitch)
  - Mention product naturally where relevant

Guest post pitch:
  "Hi [Name], I'm a big fan of [Publication]. I have
  an idea for a post your readers would love:
  '[Topic]' — it covers [specific value].

  I've written for [other publications/traction].
  Would this be a good fit for [Publication]?"

Podcast pitch:
  "Hi [Name], love the podcast. I'd be a great guest
  to discuss [topic]. I bring [unique perspective]
  and have [traction/hook]."
```

### 3.13 Channel 13: Paid Ads (Google, LinkedIn, Facebook)

**Best for:** Proven products with strong unit economics
**Effort:** Medium (but expensive)
**Conversion rate:** 1-5%

```
Strategy:
  - Start small ($500-1,000/month max)
  - Track CAC religiously
  - Only scale when unit economics are proven
  - Test 3-5 ad variations

When to use paid ads:
  - You have 30+ customers
  - Organic channels are maxed
  - Unit economics are positive (CAC < 1/3 LTV)
  - You have cash to invest

Platforms:
  - Google Ads: Best for high-intent searches
  - LinkedIn Ads: Best for B2B professional targeting
  - Twitter Ads: Best for developer/creator audience
  - Facebook Ads: Best for B2C / broad audience
```

### 3.14 Channel 14: Niche Directories & Review Sites

**Best for:** Low-effort, ongoing lead gen
**Effort:** Low
**Conversion rate:** 1-3%

```
Strategy:
  - List your product on relevant directories
  - Encourage customers to leave reviews
  - Update listings regularly

Directories:
  - G2 (B2B software reviews)
  - Capterra (B2B software reviews)
  - GetApp (B2B software)
  - AlternativeTo (find alternatives)
  - SaaSWorthy (SaaS reviews)
  - Niche-specific directories (e.g., for developer tools)

Optimization:
  - Complete profile (screenshots, description, pricing)
  - Collect reviews from customers
  - Respond to all reviews (positive and negative)
  - Update features and pricing regularly
```

### 3.15 Channel 15: Referral Program

**Best for:** All stages (start early)
**Effort:** Medium setup
**Conversion rate:** 5-20% (of referred leads)

```
Strategy:
  - Create a simple referral program
  - Offer incentive: 1 month free for both parties
  - Make it easy to share (one-click)
  - Ask for referrals at the right time

When to ask for referrals:
  - After customer achieves success
  - After positive support interaction
  - When they give you positive feedback
  - In your onboarding sequence (day 14)

Referral email template:
  "Hi [Name],

  Glad to hear [Product] is helping you [outcome].

  If you know anyone else who might benefit, we'd
  love to help them too. For every person who signs
  up through your referral link, you both get
  1 month free.

  Your referral link: [link]

  Thanks for being a great customer!

  [Your Name]"
```

## Section 5: Customer Outreach & Sales Process

### 5.1 The First 100 Sales Process

```
Step 1: Identify (30 min/day)
  - Find 10-20 potential customers in your target channel
  - Research each one (company, role, needs)
  - Score them: "How likely to buy?" (1-5)

Step 2: Connect (30 min/day)
  - Send personalized outreach
  - Follow up with people who've shown interest
  - Join conversations they're in

Step 3: Engage (30 min/day)
  - Respond to replies and comments
  - Provide value (not just pitches)
  - Schedule conversations

Step 4: Convert (as needed)
  - Demo/consultation calls
  - Handle objections
  - Close the deal

Step 5: Delight (ongoing)
  - Onboarding help
  - Personal check-ins
  - Ask for referrals
```

### 5.2 Outreach Cadence Per Day

```
Outreach target: 20-50 touches per day

Warm outreach:
  - 10 personalized DMs
  - 10 comment replies on posts
  - 5 email responses

Cold outreach:
  - 10 cold emails
  - 10 LinkedIn connection requests
  - 5 cold DMs on Twitter

Community:
  - 5 valuable comments in communities
  - 1 original content post

Total: 50+ touches per day
```

### 5.3 Demo Call Structure

```
FIRST 60 SECONDS (Build rapport):
  - Thank them for their time
  - Confirm their role and company
  - Ask about their current situation

NEXT 5 MINUTES (Understand their needs):
  - What's your current process for [task]?
  - What's working well?
  - What's not working?
  - What have you tried?

NEXT 10 MINUTES (Show product):
  - Show how your product solves THEIR specific problem
  - Connect features to their pain points
  - Show examples of similar customers

NEXT 5 MINUTES (Handle objections):
  - Address concerns directly
  - Share relevant case studies
  - Offer guarantees or trials

LAST 5 MINUTES (Close):
  - Summarize value for them
  - Ask for the commitment
  - Set next steps

CLOSE:
  "Based on what you've shared, it sounds like
  [Product] would help you [outcome]. Would you
  like to get started?

  You can sign up at [link]. I'll personally
  help you get set up."
```

## Section 7: Retention & Expansion for First 100

### 7.1 Retention Strategies for Early Customers

```
1. PERSONAL ONBOARDING
   - Offer 1:1 onboarding call for every new customer
   - Personally help them get set up
   - Ensure they reach "aha moment" within first session

2. REGULAR CHECK-INS
   - Day 1: "How's it going?"
   - Day 7: "Any questions?"
   - Day 14: "Seeing results?"
   - Day 30: "Want to upgrade/renew?"

3. FEATURE REQUESTS
   - Track every feature request
   - Build the most-requested features
   - Tell customers when you ship their request
   - Make them feel heard

4. PRE-CHURN INTERVENTION
   - Monitor usage daily for your first 100
   - If someone stops using, reach out personally
   - Offer extended trial or discount
   - Ask what went wrong

5. COMMUNITY
   - Create a private community for first 100 customers
   - Share updates, ask for feedback
   - Let them feel like insiders
   - Foster peer-to-peer connections
```

### 7.2 Expansion Strategies

```
1. UPSELL AT THE RIGHT TIME
   - When they hit usage limits
   - When they ask for a feature in a higher tier
   - When their team grows
   - When they give positive feedback

2. ASK FOR REFERRALS AT THE RIGHT TIME
   - When they achieve a success milestone
   - When they give you positive feedback
   - After they've been a customer for 30+ days
   - Make it easy (one-click referral link)

3. ANNUAL CONTRACTS
   - Offer 15-25% discount for annual commitment
   - Lock in revenue, reduce churn
   - Improve your metrics for future fundraising
```
