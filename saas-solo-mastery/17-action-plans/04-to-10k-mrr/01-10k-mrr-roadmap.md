# Roadmap from $0 to $10K MRR: Complete Growth Guide

## Executive Summary

$10K MRR ($120K ARR) is the first major milestone for any SaaS. It proves you have a real business with product-market fit. This roadmap provides a complete, stage-by-stage guide to reaching $10K MRR, with specific tactics for each phase.

---

## Section 1: The $10K MRR Framework

### 1.1 What $10K MRR Represents

```
$10K MRR = $120K ARR
At 80% margin = $96K gross profit
At 50% net margin = $60K founder income
Or $120K if you keep it lean

$10K MRR = Proof of concept
  - Multiple customers willing to pay
  - Product that delivers value
  - Repeatable acquisition channel
  - Survival-level business
```

### 1.2 The Four Phases to $10K MRR

```
Phase 1: $0 -> $1K MRR (Validation)
  Focus: First paying customers
  Customers: 5-20 at $50-200 ARPU
  Timeline: 1-3 months
  Key: Convince people to pay

Phase 2: $1K -> $3K MRR (Efficiency)
  Focus: Repeatable acquisition
  Customers: 15-60 at $50-200 ARPU
  Timeline: 2-4 months
  Key: Find scalable channel

Phase 3: $3K -> $5K MRR (Growth)
  Focus: Double down on best channel
  Customers: 40-100 at $50-200 ARPU
  Timeline: 2-4 months
  Key: Systematic growth

Phase 4: $5K -> $10K MRR (Scale)
  Focus: Add channels, hire help
  Customers: 80-200 at $50-200 ARPU
  Timeline: 3-6 months
  Key: Systems + team
```

### 1.3 Total Timeline to $10K MRR

```
Aggressive: 6-9 months
Moderate: 9-12 months
Slow: 12-18 months
Unsustainable: 18+ months (need to change approach)
```

## Section 2: Phase 1 — $0 to $1K MRR

### 2.1 What You Need

```
Goal: Prove someone will pay for your product.
This is the HARDEST phase. Don't rush it.

Requirements:
  - Working MVP (ugly is fine)
  - Payment processing set up
  - Landing page with CTA
  - Personal network or community

Strategy:
  Do things that don't scale.
  Manual outreach, personal demos, concierge onboarding.
  Every customer is a personal win.
```

### 2.2 Getting to $1K MRR

```
OPTION A: Low ARPU ($20-30/month)
  Customers needed: 33-50
  Strategy: Volume play, self-serve
  Best for: B2C, micro-SaaS, PLG products

OPTION B: Medium ARPU ($50-100/month)
  Customers needed: 10-20
  Strategy: Targeted outreach, content
  Best for: B2B tools for small businesses

OPTION C: High ARPU ($200-500/month)
  Customers needed: 2-5
  Strategy: Founder-led sales, consulting vibe
  Best for: Enterprise-lite, agency tools

Pick the option that fits your product.
```

### 2.3 Phase 1 Playbook

```
Week 1-2: Build MVP + get first signups
  - Ship the ugliest thing that works
  - Get 5-10 people to try it
  - No payment yet (just testing)

Week 3-4: Convert to paid
  - Ask every trial user to pay
  - "You've been using it, would you like to
    continue with our paid plan?"
  - Offer personalized help for anyone who signs up

Week 5-6: Iterate based on feedback
  - Fix the issues that block conversion
  - Build the features customers request
  - Improve onboarding for new users

Week 7-8: Push to $1K
  - Double outreach efforts
  - Ask for referrals
  - Publish first content
  - Celebrate when you hit $1K!

STUCK? If after 8 weeks you have 0 paying customers:
  - Your problem isn't painful enough
  - Your solution doesn't work well enough
  - Your pricing is wrong
  - Your target customer is wrong
  - Fix the root cause
```

## Section 3: Phase 2 — $1K to $3K MRR

### 3.1 What Changes

```
Now you know people will pay. Phase 2 is about
finding repeatable acquisition.

Shift from:
  "Will anyone pay?" -> "How do I get more customers?"

Requirements:
  - 5-15 paying customers
  - Clear value proposition
  - Basic content/channel strategy
```

### 3.2 Getting to $3K MRR

```
With $50 ARPU: 20 more customers (total ~40)
With $100 ARPU: 10 more customers (total ~20)
With $200 ARPU: 5 more customers (total ~10)

Average: Need 10-20 more customers per month
That's 2.5-5 per week — achievable with focus.
```

### 3.3 Phase 2 Playbook

```
CHANNEL TESTING:

Test 3-5 channels and measure results:

Channel     | Time Invested | Signups | Paid | Revenue | CAC
Content SEO | 20 hours      | 50      | 5    | $500    | 4 hrs/customer
Cold email  | 15 hours      | 30      | 8    | $800    | 2 hrs/customer
Reddit      | 10 hours      | 40      | 3    | $300    | 3 hrs/customer
LinkedIn    | 15 hours      | 20      | 4    | $400    | 4 hrs/customer

Pick your top channel and double down.

CONTENT PLAYBOOK (if content is your channel):
  - Write 8-12 SEO-optimized articles
  - Target long-tail keywords with buyer intent
  - Include product mentions naturally
  - Promote on social media and communities

OUTREACH PLAYBOOK (if outreach is your channel):
  - 30-50 personalized outreaches per day
  - Follow up 3-5 times with value
  - Track response and conversion rates
  - Refine messaging based on what works

COMMUNITY PLAYBOOK (if community is your channel):
  - Active in 3+ communities daily
  - Provide genuine help (not just promotion)
  - Build relationships with key members
  - Share your journey transparently
```

## Section 4: Phase 3 — $3K to $5K MRR

### 4.1 What Changes

```
Phase 3 is about moving from "founder doing everything"
to "systematic growth engine."

Your goal: Convert your best channel into a system
that consistently delivers customers.

Requirements:
  - 15-40 paying customers
  - One channel showing consistent results
  - Basic understanding of your unit economics
```

### 4.2 Getting to $5K MRR

```
With $50 ARPU: 40 more customers (total ~80)
With $100 ARPU: 20 more customers (total ~40)
With $200 ARPU: 10 more customers (total ~20)

Need: 10-20 new customers per month
Converting to weekly: 2.5-5 per week
This requires: Systematic marketing every week.
```

### 4.3 Phase 3 Playbook

```
SYSTEMIZE YOUR TOP CHANNEL:

Document your exact process:
  1. How to identify prospects (criteria)
  2. How to reach out (template, timing)
  3. How to nurture (sequence)
  4. How to close (objections, pricing)
  5. How to onboard (steps, timeline)

BUILD CONTENT ENGINE:
  - Publish 2-3 articles per week
  - Repurpose into social posts
  - Build email list from blog traffic
  - Guest post on 2-3 industry sites

START REFERRAL PROGRAM:
  - In-app referral prompts
  - Ask every happy customer for referrals
  - Offer compelling incentives
  - Track referral source

IMPROVE ONBOARDING:
  - Reduce time to first value
  - Add milestone-based emails
  - Create help documentation
  - Automate welcome sequence

CUSTOMER SUCCESS:
  - Monitor usage for your first 50 customers
  - Proactive outreach for at-risk accounts
  - Regular check-ins with top customers
  - Collect case studies and testimonials
```

## Section 5: Phase 4 — $5K to $10K MRR

### 5.1 What Changes

```
This is the scaling phase. You need systems and
potentially help. Going from $5K to $10K is often
as hard as going from $0 to $5K.

Requirements:
  - 40-80 paying customers
  - Proven, repeatable acquisition channel
  - Positive unit economics
  - Basic systems in place
```

### 5.2 Getting to $10K MRR

```
With $50 ARPU: 100 more customers (total ~180)
With $100 ARPU: 50 more customers (total ~90)
With $200 ARPU: 25 more customers (total ~45)

Need: 15-30 new customers per month
Converting to weekly: 4-7 per week
This requires: Multiple channels working well.
```

### 5.3 Phase 4 Playbook

```
ADD CHANNELS (don't rely on one):
  Channel 1: [Your proven channel] — scale budget/time
  Channel 2: [New channel] — 30% of marketing time
  Channel 3: [New channel] — 20% of marketing time

CONTENT AT SCALE:
  - Publish 4+ articles per week
  - Build topical authority clusters
  - Start a newsletter (weekly)
  - Guest post on 5+ industry sites
  - Podcast appearances (2-4 per month)

PAID ACQUISITION (if economics work):
  - Test Google Ads ($500/month)
  - Test LinkedIn Ads ($500/month)
  - Test retargeting ads
  - ONLY if CAC < 1/3 LTV

HIRE HELP:
  Consider your first hire:
  - VA for admin/support: $500-1,000/month (part-time)
  - Content writer: $1,000-2,000/month (part-time)
  - Outreach VA: $500-1,000/month (part-time)
  - Actually evaluate ROI before hiring

PRODUCT-LED GROWTH:
  - Viral loops (invite, share)
  - Better free tier that drives upgrades
  - In-app upgrade prompts
  - Self-serve everything (reduce founder time)

PRICING OPTIMIZATION:
  - Consider raising prices (10-20%)
  - Grandfather existing customers
  - Test new pricing page
  - Add annual billing (15-25% discount)
```

## Section 7: Common Obstacles & Solutions

### 7.1 Obstacles at Each Stage

```
$0-$1K: "No one will pay"
  Solution: Talk to more people, fix product, lower price

$1K-$3K: "Can't find channel that works"
  Solution: Test 5+ channels, commit to one, give it time

$3K-$5K: "Growth is stalling"
  Solution: Double down on best channel, improve product

$5K-$10K: "Can't keep up with demand"
  Solution: Build systems, hire help, automate
```

### 7.2 When to Raise Prices

```
Signs you should raise prices:
  - Customers don't negotiate (your price is too low)
  - Churn is low even with price increases
  - You're leaving money on the table (high value, low price)
  - Competitors charge 2-3x more
  - Customers say "it's a steal"

How to raise prices:
  - 20% increase for new customers (immediately)
  - Grandfather existing customers (keep current price)
  - Announce 30 days in advance
  - Frame as "adding more value" not "we need more money"
```
