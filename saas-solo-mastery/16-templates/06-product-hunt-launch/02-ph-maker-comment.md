# Product Hunt Maker Comment Templates

## Executive Summary

Your first comment on Product Hunt is your biggest opportunity to connect with the community. A great first comment can double your upvotes, drive signups, and build relationships. This guide provides tested templates for every type of Product Hunt launch, plus strategies for engaging in the comments.

---

## Section 1: The Anatomy of a Great Maker Comment

### 1.1 What Makes a Great First Comment

```
Elements of a top-performing first comment:

1. PERSONAL STORY (Hook)
   - Why you built this (personal frustration)
   - The insight that led to the product
   - Makes you human and relatable

2. VALUE PROPOSITION (The What)
   - What your product does
   - Who it's for
   - How it's different

3. KEY FEATURES (The How)
   - 3-5 features with emojis
   - Benefit-focused, not spec-focused
   - Quick to scan

4. COMMUNITY OFFER (The Why Now)
   - PH-exclusive discount or offer
   - Creates urgency and incentive
   - Rewards the community for supporting you

5. ENGAGEMENT HOOK (The Call to Action)
   - Ask a question
   - Request feedback
   - Start a conversation
```

### 1.2 Tone & Style Guidelines

```
Do:
  - Be authentic (write like a human, not a marketing bot)
  - Be grateful (thank the hunter and the community)
  - Be specific (names, numbers, stories)
  - Be responsive (reply to every comment)
  - Be humble (if something isn't perfect, acknowledge it)

Don't:
  - Don't use generic marketing language
  - Don't beg for upvotes
  - Don't ignore questions or criticism
  - Don't make the whole comment about yourself
  - Don't forget to include your PH-exclusive offer
```

## Section 3: Comment Engagement Strategies

### 3.1 How to Engage with Every Comment

```
Rule #1: Reply to EVERY comment within 1 hour (first 12 hours)
Rule #2: Be genuinely helpful, not salesly
Rule #3: Ask follow-up questions to continue the conversation
Rule #4: Thank people by name
Rule #5: Address criticism directly and constructively

Response types:

POSITIVE COMMENT:
  "Thank you so much, [Name]! Really glad you like it.
  [Specific feature you mentioned] was one of the hardest
  things to build. What's your favorite use case?
  Also happy to give you [extended offer/free upgrade]."

NEUTRAL/SKEPTICAL COMMENT:
  "Great question, [Name]! Let me clarify.
  [Address their specific concern directly].
  Would love to hear more about what you're looking for —
  happy to hop on a quick call if that helps."

NEGATIVE/CRITICAL COMMENT:
  "Thank you for the honest feedback, [Name]. You make
  a great point about [specific issue].
  We're actually working on [improvement] — it should
  be out in [timeframe]. Would you be open to me
  reaching out when it's ready?

  In the meantime, [alternative/solution]."

FEEDBACK/SUGGESTION COMMENT:
  "This is really smart! We hadn't thought of [idea].
  I'm going to bring this to our team.
  Do you mind if I follow up with you to get more
  details on what you're looking for?"
```

### 3.2 Handling Common Questions

```
Q: "How is this different from [competitor]?"
  A: "Great question! Unlike [competitor], we focus on
  [differentiation]. [Specific example of difference].
  We think of it as [analogy].
  Would love for you to try it and see the difference!"

Q: "What's the pricing?"
  A: "We have [X] tiers starting at $[Y]/month. There's
  also a free tier with [features]. Full pricing here:
  [link]. The PH offer gives [X]% off for [timeframe]!"

Q: "Is my data secure?"
  A: "Absolutely! We take security seriously.
  [Encryption details, compliance certs, data practices].
  Happy to share our security whitepaper if you'd like."

Q: "Do you support [integration]?"
  A: "Not yet, but it's on our roadmap! We currently
  support [integrations]. [Integration X] is coming
  in [timeframe]. Want me to notify you when it's live?"

Q: "Can I use it for [specific use case]?"
  A: "Great question! [Product] can be used for [use case],
  though it's primarily designed for [primary use case].
  Some customers have used it for [similar use case]
  with great results. Would you like to try it?"
```

### 3.3 Advanced Engagement Tactics

```
1. TAG USERS IN YOUR REPLIES
   When someone asks a good question, answer them
   publicly — others have the same question.

2. USE GIFs AND SCREENSHOTS IN REPLIES
   A quick GIF showing a feature is worth 1000 words.
   Use Loom or CleanShot to create quick demos.

3. CREATE "MICRO CONTENT" FROM COMMENTS
   Someone asks "can it do X?" -> Make a quick demo
   showing exactly that. Everyone benefits.

4. REFERENCE YOUR HUNTER
   When relevant, tag your hunter and thank them.
   "Great question! [Hunter] and I were actually
   discussing this..."

5. EXTEND OFFERS IN COMMENTS
   When someone leaves a thoughtful comment, offer
   them an extended trial or upgrade.
   "Love this question! I've extended your trial
   by 30 days — check your email."

6. SHARE LAUNCH PROGRESS
   "Wow, blown away by the support! [X] upvotes and
   [X] signups in [timeframe]. Thank you all!"
   (This creates social proof and momentum)

7. RESPOND QUICKLY TO NEGATIVE FEEDBACK
   The faster you respond to criticism, the more
   the community respects you. Don't get defensive.
```

## Section 4: Post-Launch Comment Follow-Up

### 4.1 Comment Follow-Up Sequence

```
Within 1 hour of each comment:
  Reply with genuine engagement

Within 24 hours:
  Reply to any remaining comments (catching up)

Day 2:
  Follow up with commenters who asked specific questions
  Send "thanks for your feedback" to anyone who helped

Day 7:
  Share launch results in an update comment
  "Thank you all for [X] upvotes and [X] signups!"

Day 30:
  Update comment with what you've built since launch
  "Since our PH launch, we've shipped [features]..."
```

### 4.2 Building Relationships from PH Comments

```
Convert PH commenters into:
  - Customers: Follow up with personalized offer
  - Advisors: Ask for ongoing feedback
  - Beta testers: Invite to test new features
  - Referral sources: Ask them to share with network
  - Friends: Connect on LinkedIn/Twitter

Outreach to engaged commenters:
  "Hey [Name], really appreciated your thoughtful
  comment on our PH launch. I'd love to stay in
  touch — what's the best way to connect?"
```

```

### 6.2 Common PH Abbreviations

```
PH = Product Hunt
TAM = Total Addressable Market
PLG = Product-Led Growth
ICP = Ideal Customer Profile
MVP = Minimum Viable Product
PMF = Product-Market Fit
DAU/MAU = Daily/Monthly Active Users
LTV = Lifetime Value
CAC = Customer Acquisition Cost
NRR = Net Revenue Retention
```

### 6.3 Comment Length Guidelines

```
First comment: 150-300 words (comprehensive but scannable)
Reply to comments: 30-100 words (concise and specific)
Follow-up: 50-150 words (adds value without repeating)
Thank you: 20-50 words (brief and genuine)
```

---

*Your Product Hunt comment is the most important 300 words you'll write about your product. It's your pitch, your story, and your invitation to the community. Make every word count, be genuine, and engage like a human being — not a marketing department.*
