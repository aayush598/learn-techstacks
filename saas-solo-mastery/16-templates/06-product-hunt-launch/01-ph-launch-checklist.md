# Complete Product Hunt Launch Checklist

## Executive Summary

A successful Product Hunt launch can generate thousands of users, hundreds of paying customers, and significant press attention. But the difference between a mediocre launch and a #1 Product of the Day is preparation. This checklist covers everything you need to do before, during, and after your Product Hunt launch.

---

## Section 1: Pre-Launch Preparation (T-30 Days)

### 1.1 Product Readiness (T-30)

```
PRODUCT READINESS CHECKLIST

[ ] Core features are working and tested
[ ] Onboarding flow is smooth (< 2 minutes to value)
[ ] Signup flow works (no bugs)
[ ] Pricing page is clear and accurate
[ ] Free trial or freemium tier is available
[ ] Mobile responsive (60%+ of PH traffic is mobile)
[ ] Load time < 2 seconds (PH community is impatient)
[ ] Error handling is graceful (no 500 errors)
[ ] Analytics are tracking (signups, activations, referrals)
[ ] Support is ready for potential influx
```

### 1.2 Product Hunt Account Setup (T-21)

```
PRODUCT HUNT ACCOUNT SETUP

[ ] Create/update Product Hunt maker account
[ ] Complete profile (bio, photo, links, social)
[ ] Verify social accounts (Twitter, GitHub, LinkedIn)
[ ] Contribute to PH community before launch
  - [ ] Upvote 20+ products (spread over 2 weeks)
  - [ ] Leave thoughtful comments on 10+ products
  - [ ] Follow 50+ PH members in your space
  - [ ] Engage with other makers' launches
```

### 1.3 Launch Assets Preparation (T-21)

```
LAUNCH ASSETS

[ ] Logo: 240x240px PNG, transparent background
[ ] Hero image/GIF: 1164x814px showing product in action
[ ] Product screenshots: 5-10 high-quality images
[ ] Demo video: 30-60 seconds (optional but powerful)
[ ] First comment draft (maker's welcome message)
[ ] Tagline: < 60 characters, benefit-focused
[ ] Description: < 260 characters, value prop + hook
[ ] Launch post draft (can be longer)

Tagline templates:
  "[Product]: [Benefit] for [audience]"
  "The easiest way to [outcome]"
  "[Verb] your [noun] with [product]"

Description templates:
  "[Product] helps [target] [outcome]. Unlike [alternative],
  we [differentiator]. We started building this because
  [personal story/reason].

  Key features:
  - [Feature 1]: [Benefit]
  - [Feature 2]: [Benefit]
  - [Feature 3]: [Benefit]

  [Product] is [free trial details].
  We'd love your feedback!"
```

### 1.4 Community Building (T-21)

```
COMMUNITY PREPARATION

[ ] Build email list of supporters (100+ min, 500+ ideal)
[ ] Create a "Launch Day" newsletter draft
[ ] Identify 20-50 people who will upvote on day 1
  - [ ] Friends and family
  - [ ] Existing customers
  - [ ] Fellow makers/indie hackers
  - [ ] PH power users in your space
[ ] Prepare personalized outreach messages
[ ] Create a "Hunter" strategy (see Section 1.5)
[ ] Join PH-related Slack/Discord communities
[ ] Identify 5-10 people who will leave comments
```

### 1.5 The Hunter Strategy

A "Hunter" is the person who submits your product to PH. Having a well-known hunter can significantly boost visibility.

```
OPTION A: Find a Hunter (Recommended)
  - Identify PH power users in your space
  - Reach out 2-3 weeks before launch
  - Offer them early access + demo
  - Make it easy for them to say yes

OPTION B: Self-Hunt
  - You submit your own product
  - Eligible for "Maker" badge
  - Less boost than a well-known hunter
  - Fine for smaller launches

OPTION C: Product Hunt Team
  - PH team occasionally hunts products they love
  - No way to request this

Outreach template to potential hunter:
  Subject: Would you hunt [Product] on Product Hunt?

  Hi [Name],

  I'm a big fan of your [hunts/products/content] on Product Hunt.

  I'm launching [Product] on [date]. It helps [target] [outcome].

  Key stats: [traction metric], [user count], [growth rate]

  I'd love for you to hunt it if you think it's a good fit.
  Happy to give you early access + a demo before the launch.

  Best,
  [Your Name]
```

### 1.6 Launch Day Logistics (T-7)

```
LAUNCH DAY LOGISTICS

[ ] Choose launch day (Tuesday-Thursday is best)
  - Avoid: Weekends, holidays, major tech events, the week of
    WWDC, Apple events, etc.
  - Best days: Tuesday > Wednesday > Thursday
  - Best time: 12:01 AM PT (midnight Pacific)
[ ] Confirm launch date with hunter (if using one)
[ ] Prepare "cadence" for day-of activities
[ ] Set up monitoring for signups and traffic
[ ] Brief support team/VA on expected influx
[ ] Prepare auto-reply for common questions
[ ] Schedule social media posts
[ ] Prepare email to list (ready to send at launch)
```

### 1.7 Early Access for PH Community (T-7)

```
EARLY ACCESS STRATEGY

[ ] Create special PH landing page or offer
[ ] Offer PH-exclusive discount: [X]% off first [X] months
[ ] Prepare PH-specific onboarding flow
[ ] Create "PH Launch" tracking in analytics

PH exclusive offer ideas:
  - "Makers get [X] months free"
  - "Lifetime deal for first [X] PH users"
  - "50% off first year for PH community"
  - "Free [premium feature] for PH users"
```

```
[ ] Personal outreach to PH supporters
```

## Section 3: Launch Day (The Big Day)

### 3.1 Midnight Launch

```
12:00 AM PT:
[ ] Product goes live on PH (scheduled or manual)
[ ] Publish first comment (maker's welcome)
[ ] Share on social media (Twitter, LinkedIn, etc.)
[ ] Send email to your list
[ ] Share in relevant communities

12:00 AM - 6:00 AM PT (Early adopter hours):
[ ] Monitor early upvotes and comments
[ ] Respond to every comment promptly (< 30 min)
[ ] Engage with early supporters
[ ] Track signup analytics
[ ] Update social media with progress
```

### 3.2 Morning Push

```
6:00 AM - 9:00 AM PT:
[ ] Second wave of social media sharing
[ ] Personal outreach to supporters (check if they've upvoted)
[ ] Respond to all comments (priority: new comments)
[ ] Share in Slack/Discord communities

9:00 AM - 12:00 PM PT (Peak PH hours):
[ ] Most important engagement period
[ ] Respond to comments within 15 minutes
[ ] Share updates on Twitter (tracking upvotes)
[ ] Encourage supporters to comment (not just upvote)
[ ] Monitor for the "Featured" badge (massive traffic boost)
[ ] Consider a "second push" social post
```

### 3.3 Afternoon Sustaining

```
12:00 PM - 3:00 PM PT:
[ ] Continue engaging with comments
[ ] Share progress update on social media
[ ] Thank supporters publicly
[ ] Monitor server load and signup flow

3:00 PM - 6:00 PM PT:
[ ] Final push: share "last chance to upvote"
[ ] Engage with any new comments
[ ] Start collecting feedback from signups
[ ] Prepare day 1 summary post
```

### 3.4 Evening Wrap-Up

```
6:00 PM - 9:00 PM PT:
[ ] Continue monitoring (lower priority now)
[ ] Thank hunter (if applicable)
[ ] Thank top supporters
[ ] Post Day 1 recap on Twitter/LinkedIn

9:00 PM - Midnight:
[ ] Respond to remaining comments (within 24 hours)
[ ] Compile launch day metrics
[ ] Analyze traffic and signup sources
[ ] Plan next day's follow-up
[ ] Finally rest!
```

### 3.6 Engagement Cadence

```
LAUNCH DAY CADENCE

Every 15 minutes (first 4 hours):
  - Refresh PH page
  - Check for new comments
  - Check for new upvotes
  - Respond to ALL comments immediately

Every 30 minutes (hours 4-12):
  - Check PH page
  - Respond to new comments
  - Post update on social media
  - Check analytics for signups

Every hour (hours 12-24):
  - Quick check
  - Respond to comments
  - Monitor server

Note: Engagement is the #1 factor for PH ranking.
The more you engage, the higher you rank.
```

## Section 5: Support & Infrastructure Preparation

### 5.1 Server Capacity

```
INFRASTRUCTURE CHECK

Before launch:
[ ] Expected traffic spike: [X]x normal
[ ] Server auto-scaling configured and tested
[ ] Database connection pool sized for spike
[ ] CDN configured for static assets
[ ] Load testing completed (simulate 10x normal traffic)
[ ] Monitoring and alerting configured
[ ] Backup plan if server goes down

During launch:
[ ] Monitor server load (every 5 minutes)
[ ] Monitor error rates
[ ] Monitor database connections
[ ] Monitor response times
[ ] Have SSH access ready for emergency
```

### 5.2 Customer Support Prep

```
SUPPORT PREPARATION

Before launch:
[ ] Knowledge base articles ready for common questions
[ ] Support auto-responses prepared
[ ] FAQ for Product Hunt users drafted
[ ] Support team/VA briefed and ready
[ ] Response time SLA: < 1 hour on launch day

During launch:
[ ] Monitor support queue continuously
[ ] Prioritize PH users (mention in the signup)
[ ] Respond to all inquiries within 1 hour
[ ] Escalate critical issues immediately

After launch:
[ ] Follow up with unresolved issues
[ ] Collect feedback from PH user conversations
[ ] Update FAQ based on real questions received
```

### 5.3 Signup Flow Optimization

```
PH OPTIMIZED SIGNUP FLOW

[ ] Frictionless signup (email only or Google OAuth)
[ ] No credit card required (for free trial)
[ ] Clear "PH user" acknowledgment
[ ] Redirect to onboarding, not generic dashboard
[ ] Special PH onboarding flow (shorter, faster to value)
[ ] Track PH source in analytics
[ ] Mark PH users in CRM for special treatment

PH user experience:
  Step 1: Click PH link -> Landing page with PH offer
  Step 2: Signup (email or Google) -> No CC
  Step 3: Quick onboarding (1-2 minutes to value)
  Step 4: "Magic moment" within first session
  Step 5: Upgrade prompt (with PH discount visible)
```

## Section 8: Post-Launch Optimization

### 8.1 If You Rank #1-3

```
CAPITALIZE ON THE WIN

Immediately:
  [ ] Add "Product Hunt #1 Product of the Day" badge
  [ ] Create case study from launch results
  [ ] Share win on all channels
  [ ] Press outreach (journalists cover PH winners)

  [ ] Create social proof section on website
  [ ] Collect testimonials from PH users
  [ ] Follow up with PH leads (highest intent)
```

### 8.2 If You Don't Rank Well

```
LEARN AND IMPROVE

Immediately:
  [ ] Analyze what went wrong
  [ ] Collect feedback from those who didn't upvote
  [ ] Fix any issues identified
  [ ] Plan strategy for next launch

Common reasons for low rankings:
  - Weak tagline/description
  - Poor hero image/video
  - Not enough pre-launch preparation
  - Low engagement during launch day
  - Wrong launch day (competing with big launches)
  - Product not ready / too buggy
  - Didn't respond to comments quickly enough
```

## Section 9: Product Hunt Metrics Benchmarks

### 9.1 Performance Benchmarks

```
Rank | Upvotes (typical) | Traffic | Notes
#1   | 500-2000+        | 20K-100K+ | Featured product
Top 3 | 300-800          | 10K-50K | Strong visibility
Top 5 | 200-500          | 5K-20K  | Good visibility
Top 10| 100-300          | 2K-10K  | Moderate

Conversion benchmarks:
  PH visitor -> signup: 3-10%
  PH signup -> activation: 40-60%
  PH signup -> paid: 5-15% (within 30 days)
  PH user LTV: Often higher than other channels
```

### 9.2 Community Size by Category

```
Categories with highest engagement:
  - Developer Tools (largest audience)
  - AI (fastest growing)
  - Productivity (consistent)
  - Marketing (B2B focused)
  - Design Tools

Categories with lower engagement:
  - Enterprise (too niche)
  - Hardware (less relevant to PH audience)
  - Non-English (PH is primarily English)
```

## Section 10: Common Mistakes to Avoid

```
TOP 10 PH LAUNCH MISTAKES

1. Launching on Monday or Friday (worst days)
2. Not preparing the community before launch
3. Poor tagline (unclear, too long, or generic)
4. Low-quality hero image (blurry, cluttered, no context)
5. Not responding to comments within 30 minutes (first 4 hours)
6. Launching without a hunter (when you could have found one)
7. No PH-exclusive offer or discount
8. Server can't handle the traffic spike
9. Launching when a major product is launching same day
10. Not following up with PH leads after launch

REMEDIES:
1. Launch Tuesday-Thursday
2. Build list of 100+ supporters before launch
3. Spend 5+ iterations on tagline
4. Use professional screenshots or GIF
5. Set aside full day for engagement
6. Identify and reach out to hunters 2-3 weeks before
7. Create meaningful PH exclusive offer
8. Load test and enable auto-scaling
9. Check PH calendar for competing launches
10. Have email sequence ready for post-launch follow-up
```
