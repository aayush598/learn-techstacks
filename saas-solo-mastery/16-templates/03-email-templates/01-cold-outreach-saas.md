# Cold Outreach Email Templates for SaaS (Partnerships, Press, Investors)

## Executive Summary

Cold email remains one of the most effective channels for solo SaaS founders. This guide provides tested templates for every type of SaaS outreach — partnerships, press, investors, potential hires, and strategic customers. Each template includes the strategy behind it, fill-in-the-blank structure, and best practices.

---

## Section 1: Cold Email Master Principles

### 1.1 The 5 Rules of SaaS Cold Email

**Rule 1: Research before you write**
- Read their blog, Twitter, LinkedIn
- Find something specific to reference
- Never send a generic email

**Rule 2: Keep it short**
- 50-150 words maximum
- 5 sentences or fewer
- One clear ask

**Rule 3: Focus on them, not you**
- Lead with how you can help them
- Mention your product only in context of their needs
- Use "you" and "your" more than "I" and "we"

**Rule 4: Make it easy to say yes**
- Specific, low-friction ask
- Clear next step
- No need for them to "figure out" what you want

**Rule 5: Follow up (but not too much)**
- Send 3-5 emails total (staggered)
- Each follow-up adds new value
- Last email is a "closing the loop" email

### 1.2 Cold Email Structure

```
Line 1: Personalization (something specific about them)
Line 2: Value proposition (how you can help them)
Line 3: Social proof (optional, but powerful)
Line 4: Specific ask (one clear request)
Line 5: Call to action (what you want them to do)
P.S.: Extra touch (optional, makes it feel human)
```

### 1.3 Follow-up Sequence Template

```
Email 1 (Day 0): Initial outreach
Email 2 (Day 3): Follow-up with new angle/value
Email 3 (Day 7): Follow-up with social proof
Email 4 (Day 14): Follow-up with specific offer
Email 5 (Day 30): "Closing the loop" email
```

## Section 2: Partnership Outreach Templates

### 2.1 Integration Partnership Outreach

**Strategy:** You want to partner with a larger platform that serves your target customers.

```
Subject: [Product] x [Partner] - Integration idea for mutual customers

Hi [Name],

I'm [Your Name], founder of [Your SaaS]. We help [target customer]
do [key outcome]. We've noticed that [X] of our customers also
use [Partner Product].

I think our products would work great together. [One sentence on
the integration benefit].

Would you be open to a 15-minute call to discuss a potential
integration? I can share what our mutual customers are asking for.

Best,
[Your Name]
Founder, [Company]
```

**Example:**
```
Subject: TaskFlow x Slack - Integration for better team coordination

Hi Sarah,

I'm Jane, founder of TaskFlow. We help remote teams automate
their project coordination. We've noticed that 80% of our
customers also use Slack, and many want automated standups
delivered directly into their Slack channels.

Would you be open to a 15-minute call to discuss a TaskFlow-Slack
integration? I can share the specific feedback our mutual
customers are asking for.

Best,
Jane Smith
Founder, TaskFlow
```

### 2.2 Distribution Partnership Outreach

**Strategy:** You want a partner to resell or refer your product.

```
Subject: Partnership idea - [Partner] x [Your Company]

Hi [Name],

I've been following [Partner]'s work with [target customer] and
I think there's an interesting partnership opportunity.

We've built [Your Product] which solves [problem] for [target].
Our customers love us (NPS: [X], [X]% retention).

Your customers who struggle with [related problem] would get
significant value from our solution, and it creates a new revenue
stream for you with our [X]% affiliate commission.

Would you have 15 minutes next week to explore this?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### 2.3 Affiliate/Referral Partner Outreach

```
Subject: Affiliate opportunity for [Partner]

Hi [Name],

I'm reaching out because your audience at [Partner/Publication/
Community] seems like a perfect fit for [Your Product].

We help [target customer] [key benefit], and our [X] customers
consistently tell us they found us through communities like yours.

We're launching an affiliate program with [X]% recurring commission
and would love to have you as a launch partner. It takes 5 minutes
to join.

Interested? Happy to hop on a quick call.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### 2.4 Strategic Partnership Outreach (Cold)

```
Subject: [Your Company] x [Partner]

Hi [Name],

My name is [Your Name] and I founded [Company]. We [what you do].

I believe there's a strategic fit between our companies:
- We share [target segment] as our customer

available for a brief call next week?

Best,
[Your Name]
[Link to your calendar]
```

## Section 3: Press/Media Outreach Templates

### 3.1 Product Launch Story Outreach

**Strategy:** Get your launch story covered in relevant publications.

```
Subject: [Company] launches [product] to [benefit]

Hi [Journalist Name],

I've been following your coverage of [topic/beat] at
[Publication], particularly your piece on [specific article].

We're launching [Your Product] on [date] — it helps [target
customer] [key benefit]. We're seeing strong early traction:
[X] signups in beta, [X]% MoM growth.

[Relevant hook that makes this newsworthy]:
- [Timely angle: ties to current event/trend]
- [Data angle: unique numbers or insights]
- [Human angle: founder story]

Would you be interested in an exclusive or early look before
our public launch?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

**Example:**
```
Subject: TaskFlow launches AI to eliminate status meetings

Hi Alex,

I've been following your coverage of remote work trends at
TechCrunch, particularly your piece on "meeting fatigue" last
month.

We're launching TaskFlow on March 15 — an AI-powered platform
that automates project coordination for remote teams. In beta,
we've saved teams an average of 6 hours/week in status meetings.

Three angles that might interest your readers:
- "AI eliminates the status meeting" (trend: AI in workplace)
- Our data shows 60% of employees dread Monday status meetings
- I left Slack to build this after seeing teams struggle with
  coordination overload

Would you be interested in an early look before launch?

Best,
Jane Smith
Founder, TaskFlow
```

### 3.2 Milestone/Traction Story Outreach

```
Subject: [Company] hits [milestone] — [data point if interesting]

Hi [Name],

We previously connected about [context/reference].

I wanted to share that [Company] just hit [milestone]:
- [Metric 1]: [Result]
- [Metric 2]: [Result]
- [Interesting data point]

This is notable because [why it matters in the broader context].

Would you be interested in covering this, or would you like to
chat about the trends we're seeing in our data?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### 3.3 Founder Story/Profile Outreach

```
Subject: The story behind [Company]: [Hook]

I founded [Company] after [personal experience that led to the
idea]. My background in [relevant experience] gave me a unique

Today, [Company] has [traction stat] and is helping [customers]

Would you be interested in hearing more about the journey?

Best,
[Your Name]

**Strategy:** Position yourself as a source for future stories.

```

I run [Company], a [category] serving [target]. We've been
tracking [metric/trend] across our customer base of [X] companies
and found something interesting:

[Specific, data-driven finding]

Would you be interested in seeing the full data? Happy to serve
as a source for future stories on [topic].

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## Section 4: Investor Outreach Templates

### 4.1 Cold Investor Email (Warm Intro Always Better, But...)

```
Subject: [Company] — [One-line description] — [$X MRR, X% MoM]

Hi [Investor Name],

I'm [Your Name], founder of [Company]. We help [target]
[outcome].

[1-2 sentences on what you do]

Current traction:
- MRR: $[X] (growing [X]% MoM)
- Customers: [X]
- NPS: [X]
- Churn: [X]%

We're different because [key differentiator].

We're raising a $[X] [Seed/A] round and looking for the right
partner. I'd love to share our deck and discuss.

Would you have 30 minutes this week or next?

Best,
[Your Name]
[Link to your calendar]
```

**Example:**
```
Subject: TaskFlow — AI project coordination for remote teams —
$52K MRR, 12% MoM

Hi Mark,

I'm Jane, founder of TaskFlow. We help remote engineering teams
eliminate status meeting overhead through AI-powered coordination.

Current traction:
- MRR: $52K (12% MoM)
- Customers: 187
- NPS: 67
- Monthly churn: 1.8%

We're different because we automate across existing tools (Slack,
Jira, Asana) rather than adding another tool to the stack.

We're raising a $3M Seed and looking for the right partner.
I'd love to share our deck and discuss.

Would you have 30 minutes this week or next?

Best,
Jane Smith
CEO, TaskFlow
```

### 4.2 Warm Investor Outreach (After Intro)

```
Subject: [Company] — thanks [Name] for the intro

Hi [Investor Name],

Thanks for taking the time to chat. As [Name] mentioned, we're
building [Company].

Quick overview:
- What we do: [1 sentence]
- Target customer: [1 sentence]
- Traction: $[X] MRR, [X]% MoM, [X] customers
- Round: $[X] [Seed/A]
- Use of funds: [1 sentence]

I'd love to share our deck and discuss further. Are you
available [day] or [day]?

Best,
[Your Name]
[Link to calendar]
```

### 4.3 Investor Update to Raise Interest

**Strategy:** Start sending monthly updates to target investors 3-6 months before raising.

```
Subject: [Company] — [Month] Update — $[X] MRR, [X]% MoM

Hi [Investor Name],

I'm adding you to our monthly updates as we're planning to
raise in [timeframe]. This is our current snapshot:

Key metrics:
- MRR: $[X] (up [X]% from last month)
- Customers: [X] (up [X]% from last month)
- Churn: [X]% (target: [X]%)

Top win: [One-sentence highlight]
Top challenge: [One-sentence challenge]

Full update below.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### 4.4 Investor Follow-up After Meeting

```
Subject: Great to meet — [Company] next steps

Hi [Investor Name],

Thanks again for the time today. I enjoyed our conversation
about [topic discussed].

As promised, here's our [deck / data room / financial model]:
[Link]

Key takeaways from our discussion:
- [Point of agreement/interest]
- [Question they asked you to think about]
- [Any follow-up items]

Happy to answer any additional questions. Looking forward to
exploring this further.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

**Step 1: LinkedIn Connection Request**
```

```

[Your Name]
```

### 8.2 Value-Driven Cold Email

```
Subject: [Specific value] for [Company]

Hi [Name],

I noticed [Company] is [specific observation about their
business].

We've helped similar companies [specific outcome with
numbers]. For example, [Reference Customer] achieved
[Result] with our platform.

I put together a [1-page brief / Loom video] showing how
we could achieve this for [Company]. Would you like to
see it?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

```
Subject: Closing the loop

Hi [Name],

I've reached out a few times about [Product] and haven't
heard back. I'll assume the timing isn't right.

If things change, feel free to reach out anytime. We're
here if [Company] ever needs help with [problem].

Best,
[Your Name]

P.S. If you're not the right person, would you mind
pointing me to the right person?
```

### 10.2 Deliverability Best Practices

```
- Use a custom domain (not Gmail directly)
- Warm up new domains (send 5-10/day for 2 weeks)
- Keep email copy < 150 words
- Use text-only (no images in cold emails)
- Avoid spam trigger words: "free," "limited time," "guaranteed"
- Include plain text unsubscribe link
- Don't buy email lists (they're always spammy)
- Use a dedicated sending domain if sending >100/day
- Monitor bounce rate (keep below 2%)
- Space out sends (don't blast 500 emails at once)
```

### 10.3 Tracking & Optimization

```
Metrics to track:
- Open rate: Target > 40% (cold), > 60% (warm)
- Click rate: Target > 5% (cold), > 20% (warm)
- Reply rate: Target > 5% (cold), > 30% (warm)
- Meeting booked rate: Target > 3% (cold), > 20% (warm)

A/B test:
- Subject lines (personalized vs. generic)
- Opening line (reference their work vs. reference commonality)
- Length (short vs. very short)
- Ask (call vs. email response vs. calendar link)
```

### 10.4 Personalization Research Framework

**Spend 5-10 minutes per prospect researching:**

```
Before writing:
  - Read their latest LinkedIn post
  - Check their company blog
  - Look at their Twitter/X profile
  - Search for recent mentions in press
  - Note any mutual connections

Find something specific to reference:
  - A recent article they wrote
  - A talk they gave
  - A product they launched
  - A challenge their company has publicly shared
  - A mutual connection or experience

If you can't find anything specific, find a common thread:
  - Same industry connection
  - Same location
  - Same alma mater
  - Shared interest visible on social media
```

```
SUBJECT LINES
[ ] Specific, not generic
[ ] < 10 words
[ ] Mentions company name if relevant
[ ] Avoids caps and exclamation marks

OPENING PARAGRAPH
[ ] Mentions something specific about them
[ ] Shows you did research
[ ] Relevant compliment or observation

BODY
[ ] Focuses on them, not you
[ ] One clear value proposition
[ ] Social proof or relevant data point
[ ] < 5 sentences total

CLOSING
[ ] One clear ask
[ ] Low-friction action
[ ] Specific call to action
[ ] Professional signature

FOLLOW-UP
[ ] First follow-up within 3 days
[ ] Adds new value each time
[ ] Maximum 4-5 total emails
[ ] Has a "close the loop" final email
```
