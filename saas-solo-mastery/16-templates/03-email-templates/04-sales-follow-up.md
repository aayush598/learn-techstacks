# Sales Follow-Up Email Sequence: Closed-Lost & Nurture Templates

## Executive Summary

Most sales happen after multiple touchpoints. Research shows 80% of sales require 5+ follow-ups, yet most salespeople give up after 1-2. This guide provides a complete sales follow-up sequence covering every stage of the sales process — from initial contact to closed-lost and everything in between.

---

## Section 1: Sales Follow-Up Philosophy

### 1.1 The Follow-Up Math

```
80% of sales require 5+ follow-ups
92% of salespeople give up after 4 "no"s
80% of sales happen between follow-up 5 and 12

The best follow-up strategy: Be persistent but valuable.
Each touchpoint should provide new value, not just "checking in."
```

### 1.2 Follow-Up Cadence

```
Hot lead (met, interested): Every 2-3 days
Warm lead (some interest): Every 5-7 days
Cold lead (initial outreach): Every 4-7 days
Closed-lost: Monthly for 3 months, then quarterly
Customer (post-sale): Bi-weekly for 1 month, then monthly
```

### 1.3 The Value-First Principle

Each follow-up must provide value:

```
Bad: "Just checking in..." (no value, annoying)
Good: "I found this article/case study/data you might find useful"
Better: "I noticed [specific update] — here's how it applies to you"
Best: "I implemented [something you suggested] and wanted to show you"
```

## Section 2: Initial Follow-Up (After First Contact)

### 2.1 Post-Meeting Follow-Up

**Send:** Within 2 hours of meeting
**Subject:** Great meeting — next steps

```
Hi [Name],

Thanks for the time today. I really enjoyed our
conversation about [topic discussed].

Here's a summary of what we discussed:

1. [Key point from meeting]
2. [Key point from meeting]
3. [Key point from meeting]

As promised:
- [Link to resource they requested]
- [Link to deck / proposal / pricing]
- [Next action item]

Next steps:
- [Action Item 1]: [Who does what by when]
- [Action Item 2]: [Who does what by when]
- [Action Item 3]: [Who does what by when]

Looking forward to [next step].

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### 2.2 Post-Demo Follow-Up

**Send:** Within 2 hours of demo
**Subject:** Thanks for the demo — [key takeaway]

```
Hi [Name],

Thanks for the excellent demo today. I was particularly
impressed by [specific thing from demo].

Here's what I'm sharing with my team:

1. [Key benefit 1 — specific to their use case]
2. [Key benefit 2 — specific to their use case]
3. [Key benefit 3 — specific to their use case]

Next steps:
1. I'll [action] by [date]
2. Your team can [action] by [date]
3. We'll reconvene on [date] to [decision]

Does that timeline work for you?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### 2.3 Proposal/Quote Follow-Up

**Send:** With proposal + 2-day follow-up
**Subject:** Proposal attached — [Company]

```
Hi [Name],

As promised, here's the proposal for [Product/Service].

Highlights:
- Scope: [What's included]
- Investment: $[X]/month (or total)
- Timeline: [Implementation timeline]
- ROI: [Expected return]

[Link to proposal document]

Let me know if you have any questions. I'll follow up
in a couple of days.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## Section 3: Standard Follow-Up Sequence

### 3.1 Follow-Up 1 (Day 3)

**Goal:** Re-engage after initial contact
**Subject:** Checking in — [Product] for [Use Case]

```
Hi [Name],

I wanted to circle back on my previous email about
[Product]. I know you're busy.

Quick question: Is [Problem] still a priority for
[Company]?

If timing isn't right, I understand. If it is, I'd
love to continue the conversation.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### 3.2 Follow-Up 2 (Day 7)

**Goal:** Add value with content
**Subject:** Thought you might find this useful

```
Hi [Name],

Came across this [article/report/case study] about
[Topic relevant to them] and thought of you.

[Link or attachment]

[1-2 sentence summary of why it's relevant to them]

Let me know if you'd like to discuss how this applies
to [Company].

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### 3.3 Follow-Up 3 (Day 14)

**Goal:** Social proof + re-engagement
**Subject:** [Customer Name] achieved [Result] with [Product]

```
Hi [Name],

I wanted to share a quick success story:

[Customer Name], a [company type] similar to yours,
was able to [Result] using [Product].

Before: [Their challenge]
After: [Their result with numbers]

[Quote from customer]

I think you could achieve similar results. Would you
be open to a brief call to explore?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### 3.4 Follow-Up 4 (Day 30)

**Goal:** Final attempt + close the loop
**Subject:** Closing the loop — [Product]

```
Hi [Name],

I've reached out a few times about [Product] and haven't
heard back. I'll assume the timing isn't right for now.

If anything changes, feel free to reach out. We're here
if [Company] ever needs help with [Problem].

Best,
[Your Name]

P.S. If you're not the right person for this, would you
mind pointing me to who I should talk to?
```

## Section 4: Multi-Threading Sequence

### 4.1 Reaching Other Stakeholders

**Strategy:** When one contact goes silent, reach out to others at the same company

```
Subject: [Product] for [Company] — reaching out

Hi [New Contact Name],

I've been speaking with [Original Contact] about how
[Product] can help [Company] with [Problem]. I wanted
to loop you in since [reason they're relevant].

Quick overview:
- [Company]: [Problem]
- [Product]: [Solution]
- [Expected result]: [ROI]

Would you be open to a brief call to discuss?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### 4.2 Champion Nurture

**Send:** Regular touchpoints with internal champion
**Subject:** Resources for your team discussion

```
Hi [Champion Name],

Thanks for pushing this forward internally. I wanted
to equip you with some resources for your team:

1. [1-page executive summary]
2. [ROI calculator / case study]
3. [Comparison vs. competitors]

Also, happy to join a call with your team to answer
questions directly. Let me know if that would help.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## Section 5: Objection Handling Follow-Up

### 5.1 Price Objection Follow-Up

```
Subject: Pricing options for [Company]

Hi [Name],

I understand budget is a concern. Let me share a few
options that might work:

Option 1: Annual billing
  Pay $[X]/month (billed annually) — save [X]%

Option 2: [Lower tier]
  Get started with our [Starter] plan at $[X]/month
  and upgrade when you're ready

Option 3: Custom
  Let's talk about what fits your budget. Reply and
  I'll see what I can do.

Any of these work for you?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### 5.2 Timing Objection Follow-Up

```
Subject: When's a better time to revisit?

Hi [Name],

I understand now isn't the right time. When would be
better to check in?

- [Suggested month/quarter]
- When [specific milestone/event]
- I'll reach out in [X] months

I'll set a reminder to follow up then. In the meantime,
if anything changes, feel free to reach out.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### 5.3 Competitor Objection Follow-Up

```
Subject: How we compare to [Competitor]

Hi [Name],

You mentioned you're also considering [Competitor].
Here's how we compare:

| Factor | [Us] | [Competitor] |
|--------|------|-------------|
| [Factor 1] | [Our advantage] | [Their weakness] |
| [Factor 2] | [Our advantage] | [Their weakness] |
| [Factor 3] | [Our advantage] | [Their weakness] |
| Pricing | $[X] | $[Y] |

I'd love to show you a side-by-side demo. Would
[day] or [day] work?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## Section 6: Proposal & Close Follow-Up

### 6.1 Post-Proposal Follow-Up

**Send:** 2 days after sending proposal
**Subject:** Do you have any questions on the proposal?

```
Hi [Name],

Following up on the proposal I sent on [date]. Have
you had a chance to review it?

I'm happy to:
- Clarify any part of the proposal
- Connect you with a reference customer
- Join a call with your stakeholders
- Adjust scope or pricing

What would be most helpful?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### 6.2 Proposal Extension Request

```
Subject: Extended proposal for [Company]

Hi [Name],

The proposal I sent is set to expire on [date]. If
you need more time, I can extend it for [X] days.

Let me know:
1. Do you need more time?
2. Are there questions I can answer?
3. Is there anything blocking a decision?

Just reply.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### 6.3 Closing Follow-Up

```
Subject: Ready to get started?

Hi [Name],

If everything looks good, we can get started today.

Next steps:
1. I'll send the order form (5 min to sign)
2. We'll schedule implementation (1 hour)
3. Your team will be live in [timeframe]

Ready to go? Reply "Yes" and I'll send everything over.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## Section 8: Closed-Lost Follow-Up

### 8.1 Post-Deal-No Follow-Up

**Send:** 1 day after losing deal
**Subject:** Thanks for considering [Product]

```
Hi [Name],

Thanks for letting me know your decision. I appreciate
you considering [Product].

If you don't mind sharing, I'd love to understand:
1. What was the deciding factor?
2. What could we have done better?
3. Who did you go with?

Your feedback helps us improve.

And if things change down the road, my door is always
open.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### 8.2 Competitor Win Follow-Up

```
Subject: If [Competitor] doesn't work out

Hi [Name],

I understand you went with [Competitor]. I hope it
works great for you.

If it doesn't, or if there's anything you miss from
[Product], I'm here. Reply anytime.

In the meantime, I'll check back in a few months to
see how things are going.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### 8.3 Long-Term Nurture for Closed-Lost

```
Hi [Name],

Hope you're doing well.

Just wanted to share some updates from [Product]:

- [New feature/improvement]
- [New customer result]
- [Industry news/insight]

No agenda — just wanted to stay on your radar in case
your needs change.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## Section 10: Sales Automation & Tracking

### 10.1 CRM Setup for Follow-Up

```
Stage-based follow-up automation:

Stage 1: New Lead
  - Immediate: Automated intro email
  - Day 3: Follow-up 1
  - Day 7: Follow-up 2 (value-add content)

Stage 2: Meeting Scheduled
  - Day of: Confirmation email (automated)
  - Post-meeting: Personal follow-up

Stage 3: Proposal Sent
  - Immediate: Proposal delivery notification
  - Day 2: Post-proposal follow-up
  - Day 7: Proposal reminder
  - Day 14: Extension offer

Stage 4: Negotiation
  - Personal (no automation)
  - Every 2-3 days

Stage 5: Closed Won
  - Immediate: Welcome email
  - Day 7: Onboarding check-in
  - Day 30: First month review

Stage 6: Closed Lost
  - Day 1: Feedback request
  - Month 1: Nurture start
  - Monthly: Value-add touchpoints
```

### 10.2 Tools for Follow-Up

```
CRM: HubSpot (free), Pipedrive, Salesforce
Email tracking: Mixmax, Streak, HubSpot Sales Hub
Sequences: Mailshake, Lemlist, Outreach
Calendar: Calendly, Chili Piper, Acuity
Doc signing: DocuSign, PandaDoc, HelloSign
```

### 10.3 Metrics to Track

```
Response rate: Target > 30% (how many reply)
Meeting rate: Target > 15% (how many book meetings)
Proposal rate: Target > 20% (how many get proposals)
Win rate: Target > 25% (how many proposals close)
Time to close: Track by deal size and source
Follow-ups to close: Track average number needed
```
