# Churn Prevention & Win-Back Email Templates

## Executive Summary

Churn is the silent killer of SaaS growth. While acquiring new customers is important, retaining existing ones is 5-10x more cost-effective. This guide provides complete email templates for preventing churn (proactive retention) and winning back lost customers (reactivation).

---

## Section 1: Churn Prevention Framework

### 1.1 The Churn Timeline

```
Before Churn (Prevention):
  - Health score drops → Automated intervention
  - Feature adoption low → Education sequence
  - Usage declines → Re-engagement
  - Support ticket spike → Proactive outreach

At Churn (Save):
  - Cancel flow (in-app) → Save attempt
  - Downgrade request → Save attempt
  - Payment failure → Dunning sequence

After Churn (Win-back):
  - Day 1: Cancel confirmation + feedback
  - Day 7: Win-back offer
  - Day 30: "We've improved" reactivation
  - Day 60: Final reactivation attempt
  - Day 90: "Sorry to lose you" final email
```

### 1.2 Churn Reasons & Responses

| Churn Reason | Prevention Tactic | Win-back Angle |
|-------------|-------------------|----------------|
| Too expensive | Offer discount, annual plan | New pricing tier, features |
| Not using enough | Education, onboarding improvement | New features, fresh start |
| Missing features | Feature request tracking, roadmap | New feature launched |
| Poor support | Faster response, self-help | Improved support |
| Competitor found | Competitive analysis, differentiation | New differentiator |
| Company closed | Diversify customer mix | N/A |
| Budget cuts | Flexible pricing, pause option | Temporarily free tier |
| Implementation failed | Better onboarding, success call | Free implementation help |

## Section 2: Proactive Churn Prevention Emails

### 2.1 Usage Decline Alert

**Trigger:** User hasn't logged in for 7 days
**Goal:** Re-engage before they forget about you

```
Subject: We miss you at [Product]

Hi [Name],

I noticed you haven't used [Product] in a week. Just
wanted to check in.

Is everything going well? Here are a few things you
might have missed:

- [New feature / improvement / popular use case]

Would a quick 5-minute refresher help? Just reply.

[Your Name]
Founder, [Company]
```

### 2.2 Feature Abandonment Alert

**Trigger:** User started but didn't complete a key workflow
**Goal:** Help them get to value

```
Subject: Help finishing [feature]?

Hi [Name],

I noticed you started setting up [feature] but didn't
finish. I wanted to make sure everything is working.

Here's a quick guide:
[Link to setup guide or 1-minute video]

The fastest way is:
1. [Step 1]
2. [Step 2]
3. [Step 3]

Or reply and I'll personally help you set it up.

[Your Name]
Founder, [Company]
```

### 2.3 Health Score Drop Alert

**Trigger:** Customer health score drops below threshold
**Goal:** Intervene before churn

```
Subject: [Name], is everything okay?

Hi [Name],

I'm reaching out because our system flagged that
[Product] might not be working perfectly for your
team right now.

Specifically, I noticed:
- [Issue 1: e.g., login frequency dropped]
- [Issue 2: e.g., fewer team members active]

I'd love to jump on a 15-minute call to troubleshoot.
No sales pitch — just helping you get value from
what you're already paying for.

Would [day] or [day] work?

[Your Name]
Founder, [Company]
```

### 2.4 Pre-Renewal Check-in

**Trigger:** 30 days before renewal/annual contract end
**Goal:** Proactively address concerns

```
Subject: Checking in before your renewal

Hi [Name],

Your [Product] subscription will renew on [date].
I wanted to check in before then:

1. Are you getting the value you expected?
2. Is there anything we could improve?
3. Are there features you'd like that we don't have?

If everything's great, you don't need to do anything
— your subscription will auto-renew.

If there's anything that could be better, I'd love to
hear about it. Reply to this email.

[Your Name]
Founder, [Company]
```

## Section 3: Cancellation Flow Emails

### 3.1 In-App Cancellation Prompt

**Shown when user clicks "Cancel Subscription" in-app**

```
Hold on! Before you go...

We'd love to make it right. Here are a few options:

Option 1: Pause your subscription instead
  Keep your data, pause billing for [X] months.
  [Pause button]

Option 2: Downgrade to a lower plan
  Keep access for less.
  [See lower plans button]

Option 3: 15-minute call with our founder
  We'll personally help you get unstuck.
  [Book call button]

Why are you leaving? [Dropdown with reasons]

Pricing / Not using it / Missing features / Other
```

### 3.2 Cancellation Confirmation Email

**Send:** Immediately after cancellation
**Goal:** Confirm + collect feedback

```
Subject: Confirming your [Product] cancellation

Hi [Name],

We're sorry to see you go. Your [Product] subscription
has been cancelled.

Here's what happens next:
- Your access ends on [date]
- Your data will be retained for [X] days
- You can reactivate anytime before then

If you have a moment, I'd love to understand why you
left. It helps us improve:

1. What was the main reason for cancelling?
2. What product were you hoping for but didn't find?
3. What could we have done differently?

Just reply to this email. I read every response.

Thank you for giving [Product] a chance.

[Your Name]
Founder, [Company]
```

### 3.3 Customer Feedback Collection

**Send:** 1 day after cancellation
**Goal:** Understand churn reason

```
Subject: Quick question about your cancellation

Hi [Name],

Thanks again for sharing your feedback earlier.

I have one more question: What solution are you using
instead of [Product]?

This helps me understand the competitive landscape
and make [Product] better.

Reply and I'll send you a [$X coffee/Amazon] gift card
as a thank you.

[Your Name]
Founder, [Company]
```

## Section 4: Win-Back Email Sequence

### 4.1 Win-Back Email 1 (Day 1 after cancellation)

**Send:** 1 day after cancellation
**Subject:** Your [Product] account is paused
**Goal:** Keep communication open

```
Hi [Name],

As a quick reminder, your [Product] data will be
available for the next [X] days if you change
your mind.

To reactivate:
[Reactivate link]

Your data will be exactly as you left it.

But if you're sure about leaving, no hard feelings.
I appreciate you giving [Product] a try.

[Your Name]
Founder, [Company]
```

### 4.2 Win-Back Email 2 (Day 7)

**Send:** 7 days after cancellation
**Subject:** We've made some improvements
**Goal:** Show progress, encourage return

```
Hi [Name],

Since you left [Product], we've been hard at work.
Here's what's new:

- [New feature 1]: [Brief benefit]
- [New feature 2]: [Brief benefit]
- [Improvement]: [Brief benefit]

I think you'd particularly like [specific feature
relevant to their use case].

I'd love to show you what's changed. No strings
attached — just a quick demo.

Or just reply "reactivate" and I'll give you [X]
days free to try the new features.

[Your Name]
Founder, [Company]
```

### 4.3 Win-Back Email 3 (Day 30)

**Send:** 30 days after cancellation
**Subject:** [Name], you're still in our system
**Goal:** Major re-engagement attempt

```
Hi [Name],

It's been a month since you left [Product]. I reached
out a few times but understand if you've moved on.

Before your data is permanently deleted in [X] days
(I wanted to make one more offer):

[Offer: e.g., 30 days free on our Pro plan, or 50%
off first 3 months, or free onboarding setup]

No strings. If [Product] doesn't work for you this
time, no hard feelings.

Just reply to claim this.

[Your Name]
Founder, [Company]
```

### 4.4 Win-Back Email 4 (Day 90)

**Send:** 90 days after cancellation
**Subject:** Final email from [Product]
**Goal:** Last attempt before data deletion

```
Hi [Name],

This is the last email I'll send about [Product].

Your data is about to be permanently deleted in [X]
days. If you need anything from your account, please
download it now.

[Download data link]

If you'd like to return, we'd love to have you back.
Reactivate here:
[Reactivate link]

Thank you for the time you spent with [Product].
If you ever need us in the future, we'll be here.

Wishing you all the best,

[Your Name]
Founder, [Company]
```

## Section 5: Downgrade Prevention & Management

### 5.1 Downgrade Save Attempt

**Trigger:** User requests downgrade to lower/free tier
**Goal:** Keep them on paid plan

```
Subject: Before you downgrade

Hi [Name],

You requested a downgrade to our [Free/Starter] plan.
Before I process that, I wanted to check:

Is it the price, or are you not getting enough value?

If it's price, here are some options:
- Switch to annual billing ([X]% off)
- Talk to me about a custom plan for your needs
- Pause instead of downgrade

If it's value, what's missing? I'd love a chance
to address it.

Just reply and let me know.

[Your Name]
Founder, [Company]
```

### 5.2 Downgrade Confirmation

**Send:** After downgrade is processed
**Goal:** Maintain relationship, encourage future upgrade

```
Subject: Your [Product] plan has been downgraded

Hi [Name],

Your plan has been downgraded to [Plan Name] as
requested.

What's included:
- [Features available on new plan]
- [Feature 2]
- [Feature 3]

What you'll lose access to:
- [Features no longer available]
- [Feature 2]
- [Feature 3]

If you ever want to upgrade again, just click here:
[Upgrade link]

We're still here to help. Reply anytime.

[Your Name]
Founder, [Company]
```

## Section 6: Payment Recovery (Dunning)

### 6.1 Dunning Email 1 (Immediate)

**Trigger:** Credit card declined
**Goal:** Quick fix

```
Subject: Payment issue with [Product]

Hi [Name],

We tried to process your [Product] payment of $[X]
but the transaction was declined.

This happens for a few reasons:
- Card expired or about to expire
- Insufficient funds
- Bank fraud prevention block

Please update your payment method:
[Update billing button]

Your account is still active for now. We'll retry
automatically in 3 days.

Need help? Reply to this email.

[Your Name]
[Company]
```

### 6.2 Dunning Email 2 (Day 3)

**Trigger:** 3 days after first failed attempt
**Goal:** Urgency

```
Subject: Reminder: Payment needed for [Product]

Hi [Name],

We've tried [X] times to process your [Product]
payment but haven't been successful.

If payment isn't received within [X] days, your
subscription may be downgraded.

Please update your payment method:
[Update billing button]

We don't want to interrupt your service!

[Your Name]
[Company]
```

### 6.3 Dunning Email 3 (Day 7)

**Trigger:** 7 days after first failure
**Goal:** Final notice

```
Subject: Your [Product] subscription has been paused

Hi [Name],

Since we couldn't process your payment, your
[Product] subscription has been paused.

Your data is safe. To reactivate, update your
payment method:

[Reactivate button]

If you'd like to cancel instead, let us know
by replying to this email.

[Your Name]
[Company]
```

## Section 7: Reactivation Offers & Incentives

**Send:** As part of win-back sequence
**Subject:** [Name], come back to [Product] on us

```

[Offer details:
  - 30 days free on Pro plan
  - 50% off first 3 months
  - Free onboarding call with me
  - etc.]

No commitment. If [Product] works for you, great.
If not, nothing lost.

To claim: [Reactivate with offer link]

This offer is valid until [date].

[Your Name]
Founder, [Company]
```

**Send:** After significant product update
**Goal:** Show improvement since they left

```

We've shipped [X] updates since you left [Product].
Here are the highlights:

1. [Major new feature]
2. [Major improvement]
3. [New integration]

We'd love to show you what's new. Would you be open
to a 10-minute demo?

Or just reactivate and try it yourself:
[Reactivate link]

[Your Name]
Founder, [Company]
```

  What % of churning customers are flagged before they cancel?

  Target: > 30%

## Quick Reference: Churn Prevention Playbook

```
BEFORE CHURN (Prevention):
[ ] Usage monitoring (weekly check)
[ ] Health scoring (automated)
[ ] Proactive outreach at-risk accounts
[ ] Pre-renewal check-in (30 days before)
[ ] Feature adoption tracking

AT CHURN (Save):
[ ] In-app save flow (multi-option)
[ ] Cancellation confirmation + feedback
[ ] Personal outreach for high-value accounts
[ ] Offer pause/downgrade before cancel

AFTER CHURN (Win-back):
[ ] Day 1: "Your data is safe" email
[ ] Day 7: "We've improved" email
[ ] Day 30: Offer-based email
[ ] Day 90: Final email before data deletion
[ ] Map reasons back to product improvements

DUNNING (Payment Recovery):
[ ] Immediate: "Payment failed" email
[ ] Day 3: "Reminder" email
[ ] Day 7: "Subscription paused" email
[ ] Day 14: "Final notice" email
[ ] Automate retry logic (3-5 attempts)
```

*Churn is inevitable — but it doesn't have to be fatal. A systematic approach to prevention, save, and win-back can recover 10-30% of seemingly lost customers. Every customer you save is worth 5-10 new ones in acquisition cost savings.*
