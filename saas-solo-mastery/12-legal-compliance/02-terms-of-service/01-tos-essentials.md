# Terms of Service Essentials for Solo SaaS Founders

## Why Your Terms of Service Matter

Your Terms of Service (ToS) is a legally binding contract between you and your users. It:
- Limits your liability (crucial for solo founders — you have no legal team)
- Sets expectations for how your service works
- Protects your intellectual property
- Defines what happens when things go wrong
- Governs dispute resolution

**The biggest mistake solo founders make:** Using a generic ToS template without understanding it, or copying a competitor's terms. Your ToS must be tailored to YOUR specific product and business model.

## What Every SaaS ToS Must Include

### 1. Acceptance of Terms

This section establishes that using your service means agreeing to the terms.

```
By accessing or using the Service, you agree to be bound by these Terms.
If you do not agree, you may not access or use the Service.

You represent that you are at least 18 years old (or the age of majority
in your jurisdiction) and have the legal capacity to enter into a binding
contract.

We may update these Terms at any time. We will notify you of material
changes via email or in-product notification. Continued use after changes
constitutes acceptance.
```

**Key considerations:**
- Include a "clickwrap" mechanism (user must check a box)
- Specify how you'll notify users of changes (email, in-app)
- Include the ability to terminate if user doesn't accept new terms
- Consider: "We will provide 30 days notice of material changes"

### 2. Description of Service

Clearly describe what your product does. This sets the scope of your obligation.

```
[Product Name] is a [brief description of your SaaS product].
We grant you a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to use
the Service during your subscription period.

We may modify, suspend, or discontinue the Service (or any part thereof)
at any time with notice. We are not liable to you or any third party for
any modification, suspension, or discontinuation.
```

**Key considerations:**
- Don't over-promise. Describe what you DO, not what you WANT to be.
- Include the right to modify or discontinue (you need this flexibility as a small company)
- Reserve the right to implement rate limits or usage caps

### 3. User Accounts and Registration

```
You must create an account to use the Service. You agree to:
  → Provide accurate, current, and complete registration information
  → Maintain and update your account information
  → Keep your password confidential
  → Not share your account with others
  → Notify us immediately of any unauthorized access

You are responsible for all activity under your account.
We reserve the right to suspend or terminate accounts for violation
of these Terms.
```

**Key considerations:**
- Hold users responsible for their account security
- Give yourself the right to suspend accounts (for violations)
- Include rules about multiple accounts, bots, automated access
- Specify account ownership (especially for business accounts)

### 4. Payment Terms

```
Subscription fees are billed in advance on a monthly or annual basis.
Fees are non-refundable except as expressly stated in our Refund Policy.

We use a third-party payment processor (Stripe) to handle payments.
Your payment information is stored by Stripe, not by us.

Automatic renewal: Your subscription will automatically renew unless
you cancel before the renewal date. You authorize us to charge the
applicable fees to your payment method on file.

Late payment: If payment is not received within [5] days of the due date,
we may suspend access to the Service until payment is received.

Price changes: We may change our prices with [30] days notice. Price
changes will not apply to existing paid subscriptions until renewal.
```

**Key considerations:**
- Stripe handles PCI compliance, so note that
- Be clear about refunds (fewer refunds = fewer disputes)
- Include automatic renewal (standard for SaaS)
- Address late payments and suspensions
- Include price change flexibility (with notice)

### 5. Refund Policy

```
Monthly subscriptions: We do not provide refunds for partial months.
You may cancel at any time, and you will retain access until the end
of your current billing period.

Annual subscriptions: We offer a [30]-day money-back guarantee on
annual plans. After [30] days, refunds are prorated for the remaining
months.

All refunds are discretionary and may be granted on a case-by-case basis.
```

**Key considerations:**
- More generous refunds = lower chargeback rates
- Annual plans typically have a 30-day refund window
- Make it easy to cancel (reduces chargebacks)
- Use Stripe's Customer Portal for self-service cancellation

### 6. Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

```
You agree NOT to use the Service to:
  → Violate any law or regulation
  → Infringe any third-party rights (IP, privacy, etc.)
  → Distribute malware, viruses, or harmful code
  → Send spam, phishing communications, or unsolicited messages
  → Engage in any activity that interferes with the Service
  → Attempt to gain unauthorized access to our systems
  → Use the Service for any illegal or harmful purpose
  → Reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble the Service
  → Rent, lease, loan, sell, or resell the Service (unless expressly permitted)
  → Use the Service in a way that exceeds reasonable usage limits

We have the right to investigate and take appropriate action for violations,
including suspending or terminating accounts, removing content, and
reporting to law enforcement.
```

**Key considerations:**
- Cover the basics: illegality, IP infringement, spam, interference
- Include specific prohibitions relevant to YOUR product
- Reserve the right to take action (you need this for abuse handling)
- Consider industry-specific restrictions (HIPAA, financial data)

### 7. User Content and Data

```
You retain ownership of all content and data you submit to the Service
("User Content"). You grant us a worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive
license to access, use, process, copy, distribute, perform, and display
your User Content solely as necessary to provide the Service.

You represent and warrant that:
  → You own your User Content or have the necessary licenses/rights
  → Your User Content does not violate these Terms
  → You have obtained any necessary consents (e.g., for personal data)

We do not pre-screen User Content but have the right to remove it for
violations of these Terms.

We take reasonable measures to protect your data but are not responsible
for data loss. You should maintain backups of your important data.
```

**Key considerations:**
- Users retain ownership (this is standard and expected)
- You need a license to their data to operate the service (processing, storing, etc.)
- Limit the license to "necessary to provide the Service" (not marketing!)
- Include data backup disclaimer
- Address what happens to data upon termination

### 8. Data Handling and Privacy

```
Our collection and use of personal data is governed by our Privacy Policy
and Data Processing Agreement (if applicable), which are incorporated
into these Terms by reference.

[Link to Privacy Policy]
[Link to DPA if applicable]
```

**Key considerations:**
- Always link to your Privacy Policy
- If you process EU data, reference your DPA
- If you have a separate cookie policy, link to it

### 9. Intellectual Property Rights

```
The Service (excluding User Content) is owned by [Company Name] and
is protected by intellectual property laws. This includes the software,
code, design, trademarks, logos, and all related intellectual property.

We grant you a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable
license to use the Service during your subscription. This license does
not include the right to:
  → Copy, modify, or create derivative works of the Service
  → Sell, sublicense, or distribute the Service
  → Remove any copyright or proprietary notices
```

**Key considerations:**
- Make it clear that YOU own the software, not the user
- The user has a license, not ownership
- Protect your trademarks, branding, and IP
- Don't give more rights than necessary

### 10. Confidentiality

```
"Confidential Information" includes non-public information about the
Service, business plans, customer data, pricing, and technical
information.

Each party agrees to:
  → Use Confidential Information only for purposes of these Terms
  → Not disclose Confidential Information to third parties
  → Protect Confidential Information with reasonable care

This obligation survives termination for [2] years.
Not confidential: Public information, independently developed information,
information received from a third party without restriction.
```

**Key considerations:**
- Protect your business secrets (pricing, architecture, strategies)
- User data is also confidential
- Include standard exceptions (public info, independently developed)

### 11. Third-Party Services

```
The Service may integrate with third-party services (e.g., payment
processors, analytics providers, hosting services). We are not
responsible for the availability, accuracy, or security of these
third-party services.

Your use of third-party services is subject to their terms and policies.
We do not endorse any third-party services.
```

**Key considerations:**
- Disclaim responsibility for third-party services you integrate with
- If your service depends on Stripe, AWS, etc., make it clear you're not liable for THEIR outages
- Address API integrations and marketplace connections

### 12. Disclaimer of Warranties

**THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT SECTION FOR LIABILITY PROTECTION.**

```
THE SERVICE IS PROVIDED "AS IS" AND "AS AVAILABLE" WITHOUT WARRANTIES
OF ANY KIND, WHETHER EXPRESS OR IMPLIED.

TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, WE DISCLAIM ALL WARRANTIES,
INCLUDING BUT NOT LIMITED TO:
  → MERCHANTABILITY
  → FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE
  → NON-INFRINGEMENT
  → ACCURACY OR RELIABILITY OF THE SERVICE
  → UNINTERRUPTED OR ERROR-FREE OPERATION
  → VIRUSES OR OTHER HARMFUL COMPONENTS

We do not warrant that:
  → The Service will meet your requirements
  → The Service will be uninterrupted, timely, secure, or error-free
  → The results from using the Service will be accurate or reliable
  → The quality of the Service will meet your expectations
```

**Key considerations:**
- "As is" and "as available" are the magic words
- Disclaim ALL implied warranties (as permitted by law)
- Some jurisdictions (EU, consumer protection laws) don't allow full disclaimer
- THIS IS WHY YOU NEED THE UPTIME SLA SEPARATELY (if you offer one)

### 13. Limitation of Liability

**THE SECOND MOST IMPORTANT SECTION.**

```
TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, IN NO EVENT SHALL [COMPANY NAME]
BE LIABLE FOR:
  → INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES
  → LOSS OF PROFITS, REVENUE, DATA, OR BUSINESS OPPORTUNITIES
  → COST OF PROCUREMENT OF SUBSTITUTE SERVICES
  → DAMAGES BASED ON INTERRUPTION OF SERVICE OR LOSS OF DATA

OUR TOTAL LIABILITY FOR ANY CLAIMS ARISING FROM THESE TERMS SHALL NOT
EXCEED THE AMOUNT YOU HAVE PAID US IN THE [12] MONTHS PRECEDING THE
CLAIM, OR [$100], WHICHEVER IS GREATER.
```

**Key considerations:**
- Cap your liability at subscription fees paid (standard for SaaS)
- Or cap at a nominal amount like $100 (common for free tiers)
- Exclude consequential damages (this prevents a user from suing for their lost business revenue)
- Some jurisdictions prohibit liability caps for certain claims (personal injury, fraud)
- Consider if you need higher caps for enterprise customers (they may negotiate)

### 14. Indemnification

```
You agree to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless [Company Name],
its officers, employees, and agents from any claims, damages, losses,
liabilities, and expenses (including legal fees) arising from:
  → Your use of the Service
  → Your violation of these Terms
  → Your violation of any law or third-party rights
  → Your User Content

We reserve the right to assume the exclusive defense and control of any
matter subject to indemnification, at your expense.
```

**Key considerations:**
- Users indemnify YOU for their misuse
- If a user uses your SaaS to do something illegal, you're protected
- Standard in most SaaS ToS

### 15. Termination

```
Either party may terminate this agreement:
  → At any time, for any reason, with [30] days written notice
  → Immediately for breach of these Terms
  → Immediately if the other party becomes insolvent or bankrupt

Upon termination:
  → Your access to the Service will be revoked
  → We may delete your data after [30] days (grace period)
  → You must pay all fees due up to termination

Survival: Sections [list: Payment, IP, Limitation of Liability,
Indemnification, Confidentiality, Dispute Resolution] survive termination.
```

**Key considerations:**
- Grace period for data export (30 days is standard)
- You can delete data after the grace period (save storage costs)
- Critical sections survive termination (liability, IP, confidentialty)
- Right to terminate for convenience (you need this)

### 16. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

```
These Terms shall be governed by the laws of [State], without regard
to its conflict of laws principles.

Informal resolution: Before filing any claim, the parties agree to
attempt to resolve the dispute informally by contacting the other party.
If the dispute cannot be resolved within [30] days, either party may
pursue arbitration or litigation.

Arbitration: Any dispute arising from these Terms shall be resolved by
binding arbitration under the rules of the American Arbitration Association.
The arbitration shall be held in [City, State].

Class action waiver: All claims must be brought in the parties' individual
capacity, not as a plaintiff or class member in any class action.

Jury trial waiver: Both parties waive any right to a jury trial.
```

**Key considerations:**
- Choose your home state law (most favorable to you)
- Arbitration is faster and cheaper than court (good for solo founders)
- Class action waiver is critical (prevents mass lawsuits)
- Informal resolution first (often resolves without legal fees)
- Some states (California) restrict arbitration clauses

### 17. Force Majeure

```
Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform its obligations
under these Terms if such failure is caused by events beyond the party's
reasonable control, including but not limited to: acts of God, natural
disasters, war, terrorism, government actions, pandemics, strikes,
internet outages, and third-party service provider failures.
```

**Key considerations:**
- Protects you from liability for things you can't control
- Include internet outages and third-party provider failures (AWS, etc.)
- Both parties get protection (fairness)

### 18. Miscellaneous

```
Entire Agreement: These Terms constitute the entire agreement between
the parties regarding the Service.

Severability: If any provision is found to be unenforceable, the
remaining provisions remain in effect.

Waiver: Failure to enforce any right does not constitute a waiver.

Assignment: You may not assign these Terms without our consent. We may
assign these Terms in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of
assets.

No Agency: These Terms do not create an agency, partnership, or joint
venture relationship.

Notices: Notices shall be sent to the email address on file or to the
address listed on our website.

Export Controls: You agree to comply with all applicable export laws.

Electronic Signature: These Terms may be executed electronically.
```

## SaaS-Specific Clauses

Beyond the standard ToS sections, SaaS products need additional clauses:

### SLA (Service Level Agreement)

If you offer uptime guarantees (99.9% etc.), include them in the ToS or as a separate document:

```
Service Level Commitment: We strive for 99.9% uptime, calculated monthly.

Exclusions: Scheduled maintenance, emergency maintenance, force majeure,
acts of third parties (including AWS and Stripe).

Credits: If uptime falls below 99.9%, you may request a service credit:
  → < 99.9%: 5% of monthly fee
  → < 99.0%: 10% of monthly fee
  → < 95.0%: 25% of monthly fee

Credit cap: Credits shall not exceed [25-50]% of the monthly fee.
Credits are the sole remedy for downtime.
```

**Solo founder note:** Be VERY careful about SLAs. You are a team of one. 99.9% uptime means 43 minutes of downtime per month. If you can't guarantee that, don't offer an SLA. Consider no SLA for early stage, or offer a "best effort" approach.

### Data Ownership and Portability

Users increasingly expect clear data rights:

```
Your Data: You retain all rights to your data. The license you grant us
is limited to providing the Service.

Data Portability: Upon termination, you may export your data using our
built-in export tools. We will provide data in a standard format (CSV,
JSON) within [30] days of request.

Data Deletion: After the [30]-day grace period following termination,
we will delete your data from our systems. Residual copies may remain
in backups for up to [90] days before being overwritten.
```

### API Terms

If you offer an API, include specific terms:

```
API Rate Limits: [Number] requests per [time period]. Excessive usage
may result in temporary or permanent rate limiting.

API Key Responsibility: You are responsible for the security of your
API keys. Report compromised keys immediately.

API Changes: We may modify or deprecate API endpoints with [30] days
notice. We will make reasonable efforts to maintain backward compatibility.

Prohibited API Uses:
  → Exceeding rate limits
  → Using the API for competitive analysis
  → Repackaging the API for resale
  → Automated scraping beyond documented limits
```

### Beta Features and Early Access

```
Beta Features: We may offer beta, preview, or early access features.
These are provided "AS IS" with no warranty or SLA commitment.

Beta may be modified or discontinued at any time without notice.

Beta features may have limited or no support.

Confidentiality: Beta features may be confidential until general availability.
```

## Free vs Paid Tier Differences

Your ToS may differ between free and paid users:

| Clause | Free Tier | Paid Tier |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| Support | Community/best effort | Email support, SLA (if offered) |
| Data retention | 30 days after account deletion | 90 days after cancellation |
| API rate limits | Lower limits | Higher limits |
| Storage limits | Limited | Higher limits |
| Service modifications | Can change anytime | Notice required |
| Liability cap | $100 or 1 month fees | Fees paid in last 12 months |

**Tip:** Consider having a separate "Supplemental Terms" for paid plans rather than two completely different ToS documents.

## Where to Get Your ToS

### Don't Copy Competitors

- You don't know if their ToS is legally sound
- Their business model may differ from yours
- You may accidentally plagiarize (copyright violation)
- You miss clauses specific to YOUR product

## Common ToS Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It's Dangerous | Fix |
|---------|-------------------|-----|
| **Copying a competitor's ToS** | Copyright violation, doesn't fit your business | Use a reputable template service |
| **No refund policy** | Ambiguity leads to disputes, chargebacks | Be explicit and clear |
| **As-is without liability cap** | You could be sued for ANY amount | Cap liability at fees paid |
| **No class action waiver** | You could face a class-action lawsuit | Include class action waiver |
| **No data deletion timeline** | Data hoarding liability, privacy compliance | Specify retention and deletion periods |
| **Can't modify terms** | You're stuck with the same terms forever | Include right to modify with notice |
| **No termination for convenience** | You must keep serving violating users | You must be able to terminate any account |
| **Warranties you can't meet** | If you promise "uninterrupted service," you must deliver | Describe service realistically |
| **No governing law clause** | You could be sued under any state's law | Choose your home state |
| **Not reviewed by a lawyer** | You don't know what you don't know | At least get a lawyer to review |

## ToS Checklist for Solo Founders

Before launching, ensure your ToS covers:

```
General:
  ☐ Introduction and acceptance
  ☐ Description of service (accurate, not over-promising)
  ☐ Eligibility (age, legal capacity)

Accounts:
  ☐ Registration requirements
  ☐ Account responsibility (user is responsible)
  ☐ Right to suspend/terminate accounts

Payments:
  ☐ Fees and billing (when, how much)
  ☐ Refund policy (clear terms)
  ☐ Automatic renewal
  ☐ Late payment handling
  ☐ Price changes (with notice)

Usage:
  ☐ Acceptable use (what's prohibited)
  ☐ User content and data
  ☐ API terms (if applicable)
  ☐ Beta features (if applicable)

IP:
  ☐ Company ownership of the Service
  ☐ User license (limited, revocable)
  ☐ User data ownership and license

Liability:
  ☐ Disclaimer of warranties (AS-IS)
  ☐ Limitation of liability (cap on damages)
  ☐ Indemnification (user protects you)
  ☐ Force majeure

Disputes:
  ☐ Governing law
  ☐ Dispute resolution (arbitration vs court)
  ☐ Class action waiver
  ☐ Jury trial waiver

Termination:
  ☐ Termination rights (both parties)
  ☐ Effect of termination (data, payments)
  ☐ Data retention and deletion

Other:
  ☐ Privacy Policy reference (linked)
  ☐ DPA reference (if processing EU data)
  ☐ Entire agreement clause
  ☐ Severability
  ☐ Assignment rights
  ☐ Modification of terms (with notice)
  ☐ Contact information
```

## When to Upgrade Your ToS

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| $0-1k MRR | Use a free template (Avodocs, Rocket Lawyer) |
| $1-10k MRR | Switch to a paid template service (Termly, Iubenda) |
| $10k+ MRR | Have a lawyer review your ToS |
| Enterprise customers | Custom ToS that addresses enterprise concerns |
| EU expansion | Add GDPR-specific clauses |
| Raising funding | Lawyer-drafted ToS (investors will ask) |
| Lawsuits or disputes | Immediate lawyer review |
