## Why Paid Acquisition for Solo Founders

Paid acquisition is the fastest way to get users. Unlike SEO and content marketing, which take months to compound, paid ads can drive traffic and signups within hours of launching a campaign.

For solo founders, paid acquisition should not be your primary channel initially. But when used strategically, it can:
- Validate demand for your product quickly
- Fill gaps while SEO and content marketing compound
- Retarget visitors who didn't convert organically
- Accelerate growth in specific segments or use cases

## The Solo Founder's Paid Acquisition Mindset

### The 90-Day Rule

Don't start paid ads until you have 90 days of runway for testing. Paid acquisition is an experiment, not a guarantee. Assume you'll lose money for the first 3 months while learning what works.

### The Unit Economics Test

Before scaling any paid channel, ensure:
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) < 1/3 of LTV**
- **Payback period < 6 months**
- **Gross margin > 70%** (typical for SaaS)

Calculate your target CAC:
`Target CAC = (Average Monthly Revenue Per User × 12 × Gross Margin) / 3`

If you can't acquire customers below this CAC, pause ads and fix your funnel or product first.

### Channels Are Experiments

Treat every channel as an experiment:
- Budget per experiment: $500-1,000
- Timeline: 30 days minimum
- Success metric: CAC below target
- Kill criteria: 3x above target CAC after 30 days

Of every 5 ad experiments you run:
- 2-3 will fail completely
- 1-2 will break even
- 1 will scale

## When to Start Paid Ads

### Prerequisites Before Paid Ads

- [ ] Product-market fit validated (at least 10 paying customers without ads)
- [ ] Conversion funnel optimized (landing page → signup → activation)
- [ ] Tracking fully implemented (pixel, events, goals)
- [ ] $1,000+ monthly budget available for testing
- [ ] 90 days of runway to support testing
- [ ] At least 2-3 organic acquisition channels working already

### Signs You're Ready

1. **Organic channels are maxed**: You're getting all the organic traffic you can
2. **You know your ICP precisely**: Who buys, why they buy, what they search for
3. **Your conversion rate is stable**: 2%+ free trial conversion, 20%+ trial-to-paid
4. **You have budget**: $1,000+/month minimum for meaningful testing

## Google Ads for SaaS

### Why Google Ads for SaaS

Google Ads captures intent. People searching for solutions to their problems are actively looking for what you offer. This intent makes Google Ads the highest-converting paid channel for SaaS.

### Campaign Types for SaaS

**Search Campaigns (Highest Intent)**
- Target keywords people search when looking for solutions
- Best for: Bottom-of-funnel keywords, competitor keywords
- Example keywords: "project management software," "Asana alternative," "best task management tool"

**Display Campaigns (Retargeting)**
- Show banner ads on websites across Google's network
- Best for: Retargeting website visitors, brand awareness
- Targeting: Remarketing lists, topic targeting

**YouTube Campaigns (Video)**
- Run video ads before, during, or after YouTube videos
- Best for: Product demos, tutorials, brand awareness
- Targeting: In-market audiences, custom intent, remarketing

**Performance Max (Multi-Channel)**
- Google's automated campaign that runs across all channels
- Best for: When you have conversion data and creative assets
- Note: Less control, more automated optimization

### Google Search Campaign Structure

```
Campaign: SaaS Product Search
├── Ad Group 1: Brand Keywords
│   ├── Keyword: [product name]
│   ├── Keyword: [product name] pricing
│   ├── Keyword: [product name] login
│   └── Ads: Brand-specific ad copy
│
├── Ad Group 2: Competitor Keywords
│   ├── Keyword: [competitor] alternative
│   ├── Keyword: [competitor] vs
│   ├── Keyword: switch from [competitor]
│   └── Ads: Comparison ad copy
│
├── Ad Group 3: Problem Keywords
│   ├── Keyword: [problem] solution
│   ├── Keyword: how to [solve problem]
│   ├── Keyword: best way to [achieve goal]
│   └── Ads: Problem-aware ad copy
│
└── Ad Group 4: Category Keywords
    ├── Keyword: [category] software
    ├── Keyword: [category] tool
    ├── Keyword: best [category]
    └── Ads: Category-specific ad copy
```

### Keyword Strategy for Google Ads

**Match Types for SaaS**

| Match Type | Example | Matches | Use Case |
|-----------|---------|---------|----------|
| Exact | [project management software] | Exactly "project management software" | Converting, low volume |
| Phrase | "project management software" | "best project management software", etc. | Balanced |
| Broad (Modified) | +project +management +software | Any order, close variants | Discovery, high volume |

**Negative Keywords (Add Immediately)**

Add these as negative keywords from day one:
- free (unless you have a free tier)
- job / jobs / career
- internship
- course / training / tutorial
- download (for non-downloadable SaaS)
- open source
- template (unless you sell templates)

### Google Ads Budget Strategy

| Budget Level | Strategy | Expected Results |
|-------------|----------|-----------------|
| $500/month | 1 campaign, 3-5 ad groups | Data collection, 10-20 clicks/day |
| $1,000/month | 2 campaigns, 10 ad groups | Optimization possible, 20-40 clicks/day |
| $3,000+/month | Full funnel, multiple campaigns | Scaling, 60+ clicks/day |

### Google Ads Optimization Checklist

- [ ] All campaigns have conversion tracking
- [ ] Landing pages match ad copy and keywords
- [ ] Negative keywords list comprehensive
- [ ] Ad extensions active (sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets)
- [ ] Responsive search ads with 10+ headlines and descriptions
- [ ] Device bid adjustments (decrease mobile if low converting)
- [ ] Location targeting correct
- [ ] Schedule ads for business hours
- [ ] Quality Score 7+ for top keywords

## LinkedIn Ads for SaaS

### Why LinkedIn Ads

LinkedIn has the most powerful B2B targeting options. You can target by job title, company size, industry, seniority, and more. For B2B SaaS with a defined ICP, LinkedIn Ads can be highly effective.

### Targeting Options

**Profile Targeting**
- Job title: "CTO," "VP of Engineering," "Product Manager"
- Job function: "Engineering," "Product," "Marketing"
- Seniority: "Director," "VP," "C-Suite"
- Company size: 11-50, 51-200, 201-500
- Industry: "Software," "Financial Services," "Healthcare"

**Audience Targeting**
- Retargeting: Website visitors, engagement retargeting
- Matched audiences: Email list targeting, account targeting
- Lookalike audiences: Based on your customers

**When LinkedIn Works Best**
- High-value B2B products ($50+/month)
- Well-defined ICP with specific job titles
- Products that require executive buy-in
- Long sales cycles (multiple touches needed)

### LinkedIn Ad Formats

**Sponsored Content (Single Image)**
- Appears in the feed
- Best for: Case studies, blog posts, product announcements
- Specs: 1200×627px, 600 char max

**Sponsored Content (Video)**
- Auto-plays in feed
- Best for: Product demos, founder stories, testimonials
- Specs: 3:4 or 1:1 for mobile, 3 minutes max

**Text Ads**
- Appears in sidebar
- Best for: Low-cost brand awareness
- Specs: 50 char headline, 100 char description

**Message Ads**
- Appears as LinkedIn messages
- Best for: High-intent conversion, event invites
- Note: Higher CPM but higher conversion

**Lead Gen Forms**
- Embedded in Sponsored Content
- User submits LinkedIn profile data
- Best for: Content downloads, demo requests, newsletter signups

### LinkedIn Ads Budget

LinkedIn is the most expensive ad platform. Minimum effective budgets:
- **Testing**: $1,000-2,000/month
- **Scaling**: $3,000-5,000/month

- Average CPC: $5-10
- Average CPM: $50-80
- Average CPL (lead form): $50-150

### LinkedIn Ads Optimization

1. **Narrow targeting**: Small, precise audiences outperform broad ones
2. **Creative refresh every 2 weeks**: Feed fatigue sets in fast
3. **Test Lead Gen Forms**: They typically have lower CPL than landing pages
4. **Retarget engagement**: Target people who watched 50%+ of your video
5. **Use Matched Audiences**: Upload your customer email list for lookalike targeting

## Twitter/X Ads for SaaS

### Why Twitter Ads

Twitter Ads are less expensive than LinkedIn and can be effective for:
- Building an audience around building in public
- Promoting content and threads
- Retargeting engaged users
- Product launches and announcements

### Twitter Ad Formats

**Promoted Tweets**
- Your tweet shown to targeted users
- Best for: Content promotion, product announcements
- Targeting: Followers of competitors, keyword targeting, interest targeting

**Follower Ads**
- Accounts recommended to users
- Best for: Building follower base
- Note: Expensive and low ROI for direct conversion

**Trend Takeover**
- Your brand on trending topics list
- Best for: Large launches and events
- Note: Very expensive ($100k+)

### Twitter Ads Budget

- **Testing**: $500-1,000/month
- **Scaling**: $1,000-3,000/month
- Average CPC: $0.50-2.00
- Average Engagement Rate: 1-3%

### Twitter Ads Strategy for Solo Founders

1. **Promote your best organic content**: Identify top-performing organic tweets, put $5-10/day behind them
2. **Target competitor followers**: "Followers of [competitor]" is a powerful targeting option
3. **Thread promotion**: Promote threads that convert to signups
4. **Retarget engagement**: Target users who engaged with your tweets or profile
5. **Newsletter promotion**: Twitter is great for growing an email list

## Retargeting Strategies for Solo Founders

### Why Retargeting Matters

Most first-time visitors won't convert. Retargeting keeps your product top-of-mind and brings them back. For SaaS, retargeting can increase conversion rates by 50-100%.

### Retargeting Segments

**Segment 1: All Website Visitors**
- Show: Brand awareness ads, value propositions
- Frequency: 3-5 impressions/week
- Goal: Brand recall

**Segment 2: Blog Readers**
- Show: Related content, content upgrades, newsletter signup
- Frequency: 5-7 impressions/week
- Goal: Email capture

**Segment 3: Pricing Page Visitors**
- Show: Testimonials, case studies, limited-time offers
- Frequency: 7-10 impressions/week
- Goal: Trial signup

**Segment 4: Trial Users (Not Converted)**
- Show: Feature highlights, onboarding tips, success stories
- Frequency: 10+ impressions/week
- Goal: Paid conversion

**Segment 5: Past Customers (Churned)**
- Show: New features, improvements, "we've changed" ads
- Frequency: 3-5 impressions/week
- Goal: Win-back

### Retargeting Platform Setup

1. Install the pixel/tag on ALL pages (not just marketing pages)
2. Set up events for key actions:
   - Page viewed (blog, pricing, signup, app)
   - Trial started
   - Feature used
   - Payment made
   - Churn event
3. Create audiences for each segment
4. Set frequency caps to avoid ad fatigue
5. Create segment-specific ad creative

### Retargeting Ad Examples

**Blog reader retargeting:**
"Learn 5 more strategies in our complete guide. [Link]"

**Pricing page retargeting:**
"Start your free trial — no credit card required. [Link]"

**Trial user retargeting:**
"Most users see results in 2 weeks. Here's what [Customer] achieved: [Testimonial]"

## Ad Creative for B2B SaaS

### Writing Ad Copy That Converts

**Structure for Search Ads:**
```
Headline 1: [Benefit] — [Product Name]
Headline 2: [Specific Outcome]
Headline 3: [Social Proof/Offer]
Description 1: [Problem] + [Solution] + [Key Feature]
Description 2: [Proof] + [CTA]
```

**Structure for Social Ads:**
```
Hook (first line): "Struggling with [Problem]?"
Body (2-3 sentences): How [Product] solves it
Proof: "Join [X] teams already using [Product]"
CTA: "Start free trial →"
```

### Ad Copy Templates

**Template 1: Problem-Aware**
```
Headline: Stop [Problem] with [Product]
Description: [Problem] costing you time/money? [Product] helps [audience] [solution]. [Key feature]. Try free.
```

**Template 2: Comparison/Alternative**
```
Headline: [Competitor] Alternative — [Product]
Description: Looking for a [competitor] alternative? [Product] offers [differentiator] at [price]. [Proof]. Start free.
```

**Template 3: Social Proof**
```
Headline: Join [X] Teams Using [Product]
Description: [Customer type] use [Product] to [outcome]. [Feature] + [Feature]. Start your free trial today.
```

### Visual Creative Guidelines

**SaaS ad best practices:**
1. Show your product UI (not stock photos)
2. Use screenshots of specific features, not the whole dashboard
3. Include a clear CTA button
4. Use brand colors consistently
5. Keep text overlay minimal (under 20% of image)
6. Test video vs. static images
7. A/B test constantly

**Landing page matching:**
- Ad headline = Page headline (message match)
- Ad CTA = Page CTA
- Visual style consistent between ad and page

## Budget Allocation Framework

### The 70/20/10 Rule

- **70%**: Proven channels (Google Search, retargeting)
- **20%**: Emerging channels (LinkedIn, Twitter, new ad formats)
- **10%**: Experimental channels (new platforms, creative approaches)

### Budget by Stage

| Stage | Monthly Budget | Allocation |
|-------|---------------|------------|
| Testing (Month 1-3) | $1,000-2,000 | 50% Google, 30% LinkedIn, 20% Retargeting |
| Optimization (Month 4-6) | $2,000-5,000 | 40% Google, 30% LinkedIn, 20% Retargeting, 10% Twitter |
| Scaling (Month 7+) | $5,000+ | Optimize based on CAC, scale best channels |

### When to Scale

Increase budget by 20-30% per week when:
- CAC is under target
- Conversion rate is stable
- Impression share is below 80%
- You have 50+ conversions in the last 30 days

Pause scaling when:
- CAC increases by 20%+
- Conversion rate drops by 20%+
- Impression share exceeds 90% (diminishing returns)

## Tracking and Attribution

### Setting Up Conversion Tracking

**Google Ads:**
1. Install Google Ads conversion tracking tag on all pages
2. Set up conversion actions for: Signup, Trial start, Payment, Demo request
3. Import Google Analytics goals as conversions

**LinkedIn:**
1. Install Insight Tag on all pages
2. Set up conversion tracking for key events
3. Create matched audiences from website visitors

**Twitter:**
1. Install Twitter universal website tag
2. Set up conversion events
3. Create remarketing audiences

### Attribution Model for Solo Founders

For solo founders, use a simple attribution approach:

**Primary: Last-click**
- Which channel got the last click before conversion
- Simple, easy to understand
- Gives credit to bottom-of-funnel channels

**Secondary: Assisted conversions**
- Which channels contributed at any point in the journey
- In Google Analytics: Assisted Conversions report
- Important for understanding channel interplay

**Don't use**: First-click attribution (ignores retargeting and closing channels)

### UTM Parameter Strategy

Standardize your UTM parameters:
```
?utm_source=[platform]&utm_medium=[ad type]&utm_campaign=[campaign]&utm_content=[ad variant]

Example:
?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=competitor_conquest&utm_content=asana_v1
```

This lets you track performance in Google Analytics and attribute conversions correctly.

## Scaling What Works

### The Scaling Framework

1. **Find winning campaigns**: Identify campaigns with CAC below target
2. **Increase budget gradually**: 20-30% increase per week
3. **Expand keywords/audiences**: Add new keywords or audience segments
4. **Copy winning structures**: Apply what works to new campaigns
5. **Add new channels**: When Google is maxed, test LinkedIn. When LinkedIn is maxed, test Twitter.

### Signs You Found Product-Channel Fit

- CAC is stable or decreasing as you spend more
- Conversion rate is consistent
- ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) is 3x+ (LTV:CAC 3:1)
- 50+ conversions from the channel

### When to Permanently Pause

Pause a campaign when:
- CAC exceeds LTV after 60 days of optimization
- Conversion rate is below 0.5% after 500 clicks
- Average position is 4+ (Google) after 1,000 impressions
- You've spent 3x budget target with no improvement

## Conclusion

Paid acquisition is a powerful channel for solo founders, but it requires discipline and a data-driven approach. Start with Google Search for intent-driven traffic, add retargeting to improve conversion rates, and test LinkedIn and Twitter as your budget allows.

The key is to never spend money you can't afford to lose. Treat every dollar as an investment in learning what works. When you find a channel that hits your CAC targets, scale it methodically. When a channel doesn't work, kill it quickly and move on.

Remember: paid acquisition accelerates growth but doesn't create it. Your product, landing pages, and conversion funnel must be strong before ads can work. Invest in organic channels first, then layer paid acquisition on top for maximum leverage.
